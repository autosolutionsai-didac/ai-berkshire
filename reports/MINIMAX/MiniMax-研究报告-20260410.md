## 3-5 Year Outlook

**In 3 years (2029)**:
- Very likely still among the leading independent AI startups in China (a "surviving top three" alongside Moonshot AI and Zhipu)
- Talkie has a good chance of reaching the global top three in AI social, with revenue of $500-800 million
- The open platform API could overtake consumer products as the primary revenue source

**In 5 years (2031)**:
- Optimistic case: becomes "a Chinese hybrid of Character.AI + ElevenLabs + Runway"
- Base case: a global AI platform company, with multimodal capability forming a moat
- Pessimistic case: steamrolled or acquired by a large tech company
