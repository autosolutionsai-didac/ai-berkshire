## I. Company Overview

MiniMax (Xiyu Technology) was founded in December 2021 by Yan Junjie, former Vice President of SenseTime, and is one of China's leading general large-model companies. Core products include:
- **Talkie**: an AI social/companion app with rapid overseas user growth
- **Hailuo AI / Xingye**: domestic AI assistant and roleplay apps
- **Hailuo AI (video)**: AI video generation
- **MiniMax API**: model-as-a-service (MaaS) for enterprise customers

The company listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in early 2025 under ticker 9922.HK.

**Key points:**

> **Buffett's-lens summary**: "Never invest in a business you cannot understand." The pace of technological evolution and shifting competitive landscape in the large-model AI industry make the path to long-term profitability highly uncertain. MiniMax is an interesting technology company, but falls far short of Buffett's investment criteria. From a financial and valuation standpoint, it does not currently offer investment appeal. We recommend continuing to monitor the durability of its revenue growth, the trend in narrowing losses, and user-retention data, and waiting for more financial reports and a more reasonable valuation before making a judgment.
