# Adobe Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Dimensions analyzed: earnings quality, capital allocation, intrinsic value, margin of safety
> Data as of: May 2026 | Based on the FY2025 annual report and Q1 FY2026 financial results

---

## I. Financial Overview: A Printing Press

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | Q1 FY2026 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|-----------|
| Total revenue | $19.41B | $21.40B | $23.77B | $6.40B |
| Revenue growth | 10% | 10% | 11% | 12% |
| GAAP operating income | - | - | $8.71B | - |
| Non-GAAP operating income | - | - | $10.99B | - |

| ROIC | ~24% | ~26% | 33-41%* | - |

*Note: ROIC figures vary by calculation methodology — GuruFocus reports 26%, ValuSense reports 33%, and some sources report 41%.

**Key observations:**

1. **Revenue growth is accelerating**: from 10% in FY2023-2024 to 11% in FY2025, and further to 12% in Q1 FY2026. That's a rare feat for a company with a $24 billion revenue base.
2. **Margins are exceptionally high and stable**: a 29.5% GAAP net margin and a 42% free-cash-flow margin are financial hallmarks of a top-tier software company.
3. **Cash-flow quality is excellent**: FY2025 operating cash flow was $10.03 billion, free cash flow was approximately $10.3 billion, and the conversion rate from net income to cash flow exceeded 100% — meaning profits are "real money."

## II. The AI Path to Higher ARR

Adobe's AI monetization path is already taking shape:

**Current contribution:**
- Firefly direct revenue is roughly $400 million (2024-2025 cumulative) — still small relative to the $24 billion total base.
- AI-related ARR has grown more than 3x year over year, though the absolute figure has not been separately disclosed.
- Generative-credit consumption has grown 45%+ quarter over quarter.

**Three monetization paths:**
1. **Price-increase bundling**: packaging AI features into higher-priced Pro plans ($69.99 vs. $54.99/month), monetizing through plan upgrades.
2. **Consumption-based revenue**: generative-credit packs ($19.99 for 4,000 credits), an incremental revenue stream layered on top of subscriptions.
3. **Enterprise-tier services**: Firefly Foundry offers custom brand-model training, a high-value-add service aimed at large enterprises.

**My estimate**: if AI-related revenue reaches 10-15% of total revenue (i.e., $3.0-4.5 billion) within the next three years, it would meaningfully lift Adobe's growth trajectory. Given the rapid growth in credit consumption and enterprise-customer adoption, this target appears achievable.

## III. Capital Allocation: Management Eating Its Own Cooking

**$25 billion buyback program (announced April 2026):**
- This is Adobe's largest buyback authorization in its history, running through April 2030.
- At roughly 25% of the current market cap (~$98.9 billion), the $25 billion figure reflects management putting real money behind its confidence in the company's value.
- At the current annual free cash flow run-rate of approximately $10.3 billion, Adobe has ample capacity to complete this buyback without taking on debt.

**EPS-accretion effect of the buyback:**
If Adobe repurchases $25 billion of stock over four years (assuming an average buyback price of $280), it would retire roughly 89 million shares — about 20% of shares currently outstanding. Even if revenue growth holds at 10%, EPS growth would reach 12-15%.

**No-dividend policy:**
Adobe pays no dividend, returning all capital via buybacks. For a high-ROIC growth company, this is a sound capital-allocation strategy.

## IV. Valuation Analysis: A Historically Undervalued Range

**Current valuation snapshot (May 2026):**
| Metric | Value |
|------|------|
| Market cap | ~$98.9 billion |
| Share price | ~$247 (down roughly 30% since the start of 2026) |
| P/E (GAAP) | ~19x (based on FY2025 net income of $7.13B) |
| P/E (Non-GAAP) | ~11x (based on Non-GAAP net income of $8.94B) |
| Price/Free cash flow | ~9.6x (based on TTM FCF of $10.3B) |
| Free cash flow yield | ~10.4% |
| EV/EBITDA | ~9-10x (estimated) |

**Historical comparison:**
Adobe's average forward P/E over the past five years has been in the 30-40x range. The current 19x GAAP P/E (11x Non-GAAP P/E) is the lowest level in nearly a decade. The valuation implies the market is assuming either that Adobe's growth will slow substantially, or that AI will disrupt its business model.

**Intrinsic value estimate (conservative DCF):**
- Assuming a 10% revenue CAGR over the next five years (consistent with history)
- Free cash flow margin held at 42%
- Terminal growth of 3%, discount rate of 10%
- This yields an intrinsic value of roughly **$350-400 per share**
- At the current price of $247, the implied margin of safety is roughly **30-40%**

**A more optimistic scenario**: if AI monetization pushes revenue growth up to 12-13%, intrinsic value could reach $400-450 per share.

## V. Buffett's Perspective — Summary

> "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."

Adobe's current situation goes a step further: **this is a wonderful company available at a cheap price.**

- A 10% free-cash-flow yield is highly attractive for a software monopoly with 26%+ ROIC and 10-12% growth.
- The $25 billion buyback will keep boosting EPS for years to come.
- The main risks are the CEO transition and AI-competition uncertainty, but the financial fundamentals are exceptionally solid.

**Valuation rating: the current price offers a high margin of safety — a case of "buying a good company at a good price."**
