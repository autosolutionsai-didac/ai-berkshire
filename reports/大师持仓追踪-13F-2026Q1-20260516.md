## 10. Cross-Fund Trend Summary

### 1. AI value chain: infrastructure over application layer

Every fund is re-evaluating its AI investment logic: **hardware offers more certainty than software.**

- **Added**: Micron, Marvell, Core Scientific, Nvidia (at some funds)
- **Exited**: Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow, Cloudflare, GoDaddy
- **Disagreement**: Nvidia (Bridgewater added vs. Tepper trimmed vs. Burry shorted)

### 2. Alphabet becomes the biggest point of disagreement

| Action | Fund | Rationale |
|------|------|------|
| +225% | Berkshire | Abel is bullish on the AI + search moat |
| +29% | Tepper | Value of AI infrastructure |
| -95% | Ackman | Risk of search advertising being disrupted by AI |

### 3. Energy/gold: broadly added across funds

Oaktree's top two holdings are energy names, Burry made a new buy in Halliburton, and Bridgewater added to a gold miner. The logic: AI data-center power demand plus geopolitical inflation.

### 4. China stocks: single names -> ETFs -> exit

- Tepper: Alibaba -33%, but KWEB sharply added (rotating single stock into ETF)
- Oaktree: exited Alibaba entirely
- Bridgewater: added to "emerging markets ex-China" ETFs

### 5. Airlines: broad exits vs. a new Berkshire buy

- Tepper and Oaktree both exited American Airlines/United Airlines entirely
- Berkshire, by contrast, opened a new $2.6 billion position in Delta Air Lines
- The core disagreement: what Abel sees in Delta is brand premium and pricing power

### 6. Beaten-down value stocks: the contrarian hunters move in

Burry's style is the most extreme — Pfizer, Lululemon, and MercadoLibre are all quality companies down 50%+ from their highs. Tepper has also been building positions in cyclical-trough names such as Whirlpool.

## 11. Implications for Investment Decisions

**If you're a value investor**:
- The holdings shared by Li Lu and Duan Yongping (Alphabet, Pinduoduo, Berkshire Hathaway, Occidental Petroleum) remain core allocations
- Li Lu's new "financial data infrastructure" theme (SPGI + MCO + MSCI) is worth studying
- Duan Yongping's shift from energy (Shenhua) to consumer names (Pop Mart + Moutai) is worth watching

**If you're focused on the AI theme**:
- Consensus: AI infrastructure (semiconductors/data centers) over the AI application layer (SaaS)
- Disagreement: has Nvidia already peaked? Is Alphabet's AI story a tailwind or a headwind?
- Non-consensus view: Burry avoids AI entirely, betting instead on traditional industries the AI narrative has overlooked

**If you hold China stocks**:
- The trend among large funds: reduce single-stock exposure, keep exposure via ETFs, or exit entirely
- But Duan Yongping and Li Lu remain heavily invested in China names (Pinduoduo + Tencent + Alibaba)
