### 5.2 Accounts Receivable "Doubling Explosion" (the Most Critical Contrarian Signal)
- In Q3 2025, receivables + notes receivable at 20 A-share liquor companies totaled **RMB 77.56 billion**
- Up **+100.41%** YoY (doubled)
- = liquor makers being forced to "sell on credit" — **a marker of credit-driven sales**
- Resembles the classic pattern seen in the final stage before the 2013-2014 bottom
- **The accounts-receivable inflection is the industry's last-to-arrive bottom signal**

### 5.3 First Large-Scale Supply-Side Clearing in the Industry
- Above-scale enterprises numbered **989** in 2024 → fell to **887** in H1 2025 (down more than 100 in a year)
- Listed liquor companies saw a **net decrease of 1,701 distributors** in H1 2025 (the first net decrease since 2020)
- 53% of distributors chose to "prioritize cash flow first"
- = **the first clear supply-side contraction since 2014-2015** — a leading bottom indicator

## 9. Identifying Four "False Signals" (to Avoid Misjudgment)

1. **Prepayments rise but accounts receivable explode** = forced channel-stuffing (exactly what happened in Q3 2025)
2. **Sell-through up 5% but inventory still rising** = shipments exceed consumption; inventory is simply moving from producer to distributor
3. **A Moutai price hike ≠ bottom-up industry recovery** = it is a deliberate volume-control, price-support action
4. **A broad 30% drop in inventory** = the base was high in 2024; the absolute level is still 3-4 months (not yet healthy)

## 14. Three Core Conclusions

### Conclusion 1: Stock-Price Bottom in Q3-Q4 2026, Fundamentals Bottom in Q4 2026-Q1 2027, Cycle Exit by 2028
The four analysts, working independently, arrived at highly consistent timing forecasts:
- Valuation level: current PE of 17x, 5th percentile — **bottom already triggered**
- Sentiment level: still needs **6-9 more months for time to substitute for magnitude**
- Accounts-receivable growth falling back from +100% to +20% is the last-to-arrive bottom signal
- **Q3-Q4 2026 is the best contrarian (left-side) entry window**

### Conclusion 2: This Cycle Is a Double Overlay of "2012 + 1990s Japan," Not a Repeat of 2012
- Government consumption has been essentially cleared out (vs. a 40% starting share in 2012)
- But the **demographic inflection + rising abstention among post-90s/00s generations + absent wealth effect** are new variables that were not present in 2012
- **A V-shaped rebound is not possible**; a U-shaped or even L-shaped bottoming process is more likely
- Watch for the tail risk of a "30-year Japanification" (20-25% probability)

### Conclusion 3: Must Shift from "Sector Beta" to "Structural Alpha" — K-Shaped Divergence Will Replace Broad Bull Markets
- Total industry volume has already peaked (the loss of more than 100 above-scale enterprises in 2024 is hard evidence)
- The next 10 years will bring **structural clearing**, not a "tide going out and coming back in"
- **Optimal strategy**: build contrarian positions in **Moutai + Luzhou Laojiao + Fenjiu**, and stay away from Wuliangye (already hit by scandal) and Yanghe (expensive PE)
- Takeaway: **baijiu is not a sector-beta opportunity, it is a structural-alpha opportunity** — only Moutai has Dassai-like potential
