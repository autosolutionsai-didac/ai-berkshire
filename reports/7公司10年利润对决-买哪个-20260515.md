## One-Sentence Conclusion

> **If you can only buy one: Tencent.** Not because it's the cheapest (Pinduoduo is cheaper), and not because it grows the fastest (Pop Mart grows faster), but because it scores highest overall across the three dimensions of "certainty × growth × valuation." Profit will most likely grow 2.6x over the next 10 years, its moat is the widest, and the current share price sits near its 52-week low.

## Discussion by Tier

### Tier One: High Certainty + Reasonable Return

**Tencent** — the best all-around choice
- Annualized return ~11.6%, relatively high certainty
- Widest moat (WeChat's social graph is irreplaceable)
- Current share price HK$460, only 3% above its 52-week low; analyst target price HK$725 (+58%)
- Risks: return on AI spending, and regulation
- **Best for: long-term investors who want to sleep at night**

### Tier Two: High Return but High Uncertainty

**Alibaba** — the highest return on paper
- Annualized return ~14.3%, the best-looking number
- But FCF has already turned negative, ROE is only 8%, and integration risk is high
- Cloud is the only bright spot; Taobao/Tmall is losing share
- **Best for: those who believe Joe Tsai and Eddie Wu can rebuild Alibaba**

**Meituan** — a bottom-turnaround option
- Currently loss-making, but if the competitive landscape stabilizes by 2027 and profit recovers, the return could be substantial
- Instant retail is a genuine growth track
- Risks: a pincer squeeze from Taobao Flash Sale and Douyin, a permanent step-down in margins
- **Best for: contrarian investors willing to buy while the company is losing money, betting that competition will ease**

**Pop Mart** — the fastest grower, but the least certain
- 2025 profit grew 309%, 10-year weighted return ~11%
- But IP life cycles are unpredictable, and LABUBU has already cooled off
- "The next Disney" or "the next Crocs" — no one can answer that 10 years in advance
- **Best for: investors willing to accept high volatility in exchange for high growth, who can judge consumer trends**

### Tier Three: Cheap for a Reason

**Pinduoduo** — the cheapest
- Cash-adjusted P/E of just 3.1x, FCF yield of 15.8% — the most attractive numbers on paper
- But growth has fallen off a cliff (+59% → +10%), with the core platform growing only 2.3% annualized
- Temu is a political option: double if it wins, back to square one if it loses
- $60.4 billion in cash sitting idle, with no dividends or buybacks, is a core governance problem
- **Best for: those who believe Temu's geopolitics will work out and can accept management not returning capital to shareholders**

**Moutai** — the most stable, but the most expensive
- Highest certainty, but a cash-adjusted P/E of 18.7x plus growth of only 2.7% works out to an annualized return of about 6%
- In a low-interest-rate environment, 6% isn't bad, but it offers little edge over bank wealth-management products
- **Best for: asset allocators seeking absolute safety, not chasing high returns**

**Kuaishou** — no visible growth
- Core platform growth has stalled (Q1 was only +2.3%), and 10-year profit is essentially flat
- Kling AI is the only variable, but the market has already discounted it in the valuation
- **Not recommended: unless you have an independent view on Kling AI, the same money is better spent on Tencent**

## Key Reminders

1. **A 10-year forecast is fundamentally a bet.** The value of this report lies not in precisely predicting profit figures, but in understanding each company's growth logic and risk structure.
2. **Certainty matters more than the return rate.** Alibaba's headline annualized 14% looks the highest, but its certainty is only "medium" — if the pessimistic scenario plays out, your money might not grow at all over 10 years.
3. **All seven companies are trading near their 52-week lows.** This either means the market has handed you an opportunity amid panic, or it means the market knows some bad news you don't. Stay humble.
4. **AI is the biggest shared variable.** Five of the seven companies (Tencent, Alibaba, Pinduoduo, Kuaishou, Meituan) will have their 10-year profits affected by AI — AI could either enhance their efficiency or disrupt their business models.
