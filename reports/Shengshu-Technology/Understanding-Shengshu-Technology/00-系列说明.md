## Series positioning

Shengshu Technology is not a company that has "already been understood" — it is **the most controversial independent startup** in China's AI video-generation race.

- The bulls say: a top-tier Tsinghua team, Vidu ranked No. 2 globally, and a Series B led by Alibaba Cloud at a valuation of over **$2 billion** — this is China's AI-video national team.
- The bears say: ARR of only **$20 million** against a $2 billion valuation, a **25x** revenue gap versus Kling, and ByteDance's **200 billion yuan** in capital expenditure — an independent startup won't survive three years.

**Both camps are looking at the same company — one sees a technology pioneer, the other sees a cash-burning machine.**

What this series sets out to do is **lay out the facts first, then let you judge for yourself whether this company is worth watching** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Series writing principles

This series follows these strict rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no presumption of bullish or bearish
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **Unreliable data is cut rather than kept** — Shengshu Technology is a private company, and its core figures on revenue, profit, and the like are all unaudited; this series uses only the most credible sources and does not pad in false precision
4. **Every estimate is explicitly labeled "estimate"** — to prevent manufacturing false certainty
5. **This does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article

---

## An important reminder

Shengshu Technology is a **private company**. That means:

- All financial data comes from the company's own disclosures or media reports, **unaudited by any third party**
- Funding valuations are VC pricing, not market pricing, with a significant liquidity discount
- Management is under no statutory obligation to disclose information

This series is based on public-information analysis, and its confidence level is inherently lower than research on a listed company. **Please keep "the AI analysis's confidence level" separate from "the certainty of the business's underlying nature."**
