## Part Two: Likelihood of success on the foundation-model route

### 2.1 Arguments supporting success (🟢 bullish)

#### 2.1.1 Parent company's "Bailing foundation model" accumulation lets Ant Digital "borrow the model"

- **2026-02-13**: Ant Group open-sourced Ring-2.5-1T, the world's first trillion-parameter reasoning model with a hybrid linear architecture ([QbitAI](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/02/379431.html))
- **2026-04**: Ling-2.6-1T open-sourced, trillion-parameter scale "benchmarked against GPT-5.4" (Ant's internal claim, [ITBear](https://www.itbear.com.cn/html/2026-04/1316923.html))
- **2026-02-11**: Ming-Flash-Omni 2.0 omni-modal model open-sourced, exceeding Gemini 2.5 Pro on some metrics ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.cn/article/d9TEFiU7kq8EKCIodTmI))
- **The Bailing family has released 18 models** (disclosed at the 2025-11 World Internet Conference), spanning the full range from 10B to 1T

**Implication**: Ant Digital does not need to train a foundation model from scratch — it can directly reuse Bailing's open-source checkpoints and fine-tune them with proprietary financial data, **saving over 80% of pretraining cost**.

#### 2.1.2 Exclusivity of financial data and scenarios

- The scale of financial behavioral data accumulated by **Alipay + MYbank + Ant International** is rare domestically
- **Customer coverage**: 100% of state-owned/joint-stock banks + 60%+ of regional commercial banks + 13,000 enterprise end clients ([36Kr](https://36kr.com/p/3699806105972614))
- **Agentar-Fin-R1 training data**: tens of billions of tokens of specialized financial corpus + a taxonomy of "6 major categories, 66 subcategories" of financial tasks, covering the full landscape of banking, securities, insurance, funds, and trusts ([Sohu](https://m.sohu.com/a/919019605_120181749))

#### 2.1.3 Continued parent-company backing

- Ant Group's 2024 net profit was 38.3 billion yuan (+61%), 2025 Q2 net profit was 7.6 billion yuan (+1.9x)
- Ranked 5th on the 2025 Hurun Global Unicorn List with a valuation of 635 billion yuan
- The Group explicitly stated it would "step up incentives for 'pioneering contributions' to AI" (Han Xinyi's company-wide letter, 2026-02-02)

#### 2.1.4 B-side customer base and lighthouse cases

- **Bank of Ningbo × Agentar KBase** ([Xinhua, 2025-10](http://www.news.cn/tech/20251030/01c228e382514af39f92ebc1bb99fda1/c.html)):
  - Complex Q&A accuracy rose from 68% to 91%
  - Response speed reached sub-100-millisecond level
  - Content recommendation accuracy up 35%, recall rate up 40%
  - Selected as a "2025 International Standard Excellent Case in Financial Applications"
- **Bank of Ningbo bill recognition** (from the 4/17 report): error rate fell from 0.8% to 0.05%, saving 30 million yuan annually
- **Financial and energy clients have already put the Bailing Enterprise Edition into production** ([Tencent News, 2026-02](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260220A021BD00))

#### 2.1.5 Hong Kong stablecoin + RWA + Web3 narrative premium

- Ant Digital established an overseas headquarters in Hong Kong in 2025-04
- The "Two Chains, One Bridge" initiative has already landed RWA cases such as GCL and Newocean
- **But**: the first batch of Hong Kong stablecoin licenses on 2026-04-10 included only 2 licenses (HSBC and Chek Point), and Ant was not among them — **this narrative premium has been significantly discounted** ([Sina Finance, 2026-04-12](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-04-12/doc-inhuetnq0871424.shtml))

### 2.2 Arguments against success (🔴 bearish)

#### 2.2.1 Accelerating commoditization of general foundation models

- **DeepSeek V4 open-sourced (2026-04-24)** ([Guancha](https://www.guancha.cn/economy/2026_04_24_814819.shtml); [21jingji](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260426/herald/05fee61739e3aa4cb240e8063303a948.html)):
  - V4-Flash priced at 1 yuan/million input tokens, 2 yuan/million output tokens
  - Cache-hit input tokens priced at only 0.025 yuan/million tokens (**effectively free**)
  - 1-million-token context is now standard
  - **"Domestic open-source Agent capability leads the field"** — directly squeezing Ant Digital Agentar's selling point
- **Qwen 3.5 (2026-02, Chinese New Year)** ([QbitAI, 2026-02](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/02/380433.html)):
  - 397B parameters, surpassing Gemini 3
  - Priced as low as 0.8 yuan per million tokens
  - **In 2026-03, Alibaba unified its AI branding under "Qwen"** — strengthening the general foundation model's "national team" status

**Conclusion**: **On general foundation-model capability, Ant Bailing shows no clear technical lead over DeepSeek/Qwen** — Bailing's "trillion-parameter MoE" and "training on domestic chips" are no longer scarce labels in 2026.

#### 2.2.2 Internal competition within the same corporate group

- **Alibaba Tongyi (an affiliate)**: shares a group affiliation with Ant Digital, but Tongyi directly sells a "Qwen + Alibaba Cloud" bundled offering to financial clients, competing internally with Ant Digital
- **China Merchants Bank case**: CMB chose **Tongyi's large model** as its primary partner ([Alibaba Cloud Developer](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1654755)), not Ant Digital — **a real-world example of affiliate-level internal competition**
- **Bailing belongs to the Group, not Ant Digital**: Ant Group also runs "Afu" and "Linggguang," two C-side AI applications that consume Group resources; Ant Digital's AI-resource priority within the Group **is not necessarily high**

#### 2.2.3 Uncertainty over compute delivery and team size

- Ant Digital has not disclosed an independent GPU cluster; "self-developed large models" heavily depend on Group compute
- The size of the "Large Model Technology Innovation Department" is undisclosed, **estimated at the hundred-person scale** (🔴), showing a clear gap versus the thousand-person-scale teams at DeepSeek, Qwen, and Doubao
- Industry rumors that "actual compute delivered was only one-quarter of the original plan" (source undisclosed, 🔴 low confidence)

#### 2.2.4 No precedent of success for "To-B spun-off tech subsidiaries" in the Chinese market

- **OneConnect (Ping An)**: listed on NYSE in 2019, then its share price fell from $10 to below $2, delisted in 2024
- **JD Digits**: withdrew its 2020 STAR Market IPO application
- **Lufax**: listed on NYSE in 2020, market cap fell from a peak of $47 billion to under $5 billion
- **Conclusion**: spun-off fintech subsidiaries have gone **0-for-3** in the China/Hong Kong markets; for Ant Digital to break through, it needs to find a differentiated path distinct from these three

#### 2.2.5 The question of who holds decision-making power at financial clients

- **Large banks lean toward self-development**: China Construction Bank's "Ark Project" ([53AI](https://www.53ai.com/news/AIjinrong/2025060973819.html)) chose the path of "open-source base + financial-data fine-tuning," and had already adapted 16 versions of general large models by the end of 2024 — **not dependent on Ant Digital**
- **China Merchants Bank**: "not building a general model, but building an industry-specific model for finance" ([STCN](https://stcn.com/article/detail/1443956.html)), choosing a dual track of Tongyi plus in-house development
- **China UnionPay**: privately deployed DeepSeek-V4 in 2026-04, running on Huawei Ascend compute ([NetEase](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KRM64L9S0514R9OJ.html)) — **bypassing all commercial large-model vendors**
- **Bank of Jiangsu**: became one of the first banks to deploy DeepSeek-VL2 + DeepSeek-R1 locally in 2025-02 ([NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2025-02-05/3742346.html))

**Conclusion**: Financial clients' purchasing decisions are shifting from "buying a large model" to "using an open-source base plus RAG plus fine-tuning ourselves." What Ant Digital is selling is no longer a scarce good.

#### 2.2.6 The cautionary tale of BloombergGPT

- **$10M invested, 50 billion parameters** for a vertical financial LLM
- **Two weeks after GPT-4's release**, BloombergGPT was surpassed by GPT-4 on nearly every financial benchmark ([Belitsoft](https://belitsoft.com/bloomberggpt); [Beancount](https://beancount.io/bean-labs/research-logs/2026/05/05/bloomberggpt-large-language-model-finance))
- **Core lesson**: "When frontier progress is fast enough, scale matters more than specialization"
- **JPMorgan's choice**: abandoned self-developed foundation models, building the LLM Suite **as a portal that calls OpenAI** ([CNBC, 2024-08](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/08/09/jpmorgan-chase-ai-artificial-intelligence-assistant-chatgpt-openai.html))

### 2.3 Probability assessment

**Defining "success"**: within 5 years (by 2031), building a "market-recognized foundation model" — meaning that in the bidding/procurement processes of To-B financial clients, Ant Digital's own large model becomes the **preferred or top backup choice**, rather than "one option among many required."

| Path definition | 5-year success probability (estimate) | Rationale |
|---|:---:|---|
| Self-developed general foundation model, competing head-on with DeepSeek/Qwen/Doubao | **<5%** | Comprehensive disadvantage in compute, talent, cost, and ecosystem; the BloombergGPT lesson |
| Based on Bailing's open-source base, building a "vertical financial foundation model" that becomes #1 in its niche | **30-40%** | Agentar-Fin-R1 already surpasses DeepSeek-R1 on financial benchmarks, but client decision-making power is shifting |
| Agent platform + long memory + toolchain combination achieving PMF (not competing directly on the foundation model) | **45-60%** | Agentar already topped BIRD-SQL, holds CAICT's highest-level certification, and has the Bank of Ningbo lighthouse effect |
| A "supporting role" in IT integration, monetized via services + data + compliance | **65-80%** | This is the "financial-grade IT services provider" path, already validated by Hundsun, Sunyard, and CS&S |

**Overall assessment**:
- Probability of success on the strict definition of "the foundation-model route succeeding": **<10%**
- Probability of success on the loose definition of "the AI business succeeding": **45-60%**
- But under the loose definition, "success" essentially means Ant Digital is a **"financial-grade AI services provider"** rather than a **"foundation-model company"** — this is the key question of species classification

### 3.3 Counter-signals against PMF

#### 3.3.1 Topping the BIRD-SQL benchmark ≠ commercial PMF

- In 2025-12, Agentar-Scale-SQL topped BIRD-SQL with 92% accuracy ([QbitAI](https://www.qbitai.com/2025/12/361042.html))
- **But**: BIRD-SQL is an academic benchmark, and the bridge between it and commercial willingness to pay **has not been validated by data**
- Analogy: BloombergGPT was also SOTA on FinEval at the time, **yet still failed commercially**

#### 3.3.2 The decision-making mechanism at financial clients

- **Compliance first**: the central bank and banking regulators impose extremely strong requirements for "independent and controllable" financial AI → large banks lean toward in-house development
- **China Construction Bank's Ark Project** ([53AI](https://www.53ai.com/news/AIjinrong/2025060973819.html)): **"chose a trillion-parameter open-source general large model and built it in-house through pretraining, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning,"** having adapted 16 versions by the end of 2024 — **no need for Ant Digital**
- **China Merchants Bank × Tongyi**: CMB partners directly with Alibaba Cloud, using Tongyi's large model as its base while developing its own industry layer ([Alibaba Cloud Developer](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1654755)) — **Ant Digital is bypassed**

#### 3.3.3 Limited paying capacity of small and mid-sized banks

- **DeepSeek V4 pricing**: 0.025 yuan per million input tokens (cache hit) — small and mid-sized banks can simply call the API directly, with **no willingness to pay for a "vertical financial foundation model"**
- **Bank of Jiangsu** became one of the first to deploy DeepSeek-VL2 + DeepSeek-R1 locally — already established a complete methodology
- **China UnionPay** privately deployed DeepSeek-V4 + Huawei Ascend — **a mature domestic open-source base has already arrived**

#### 3.3.4 Limited substitution rate for industry-specific large models

- **Industry consensus** ([Sina, 2026](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7857201856_1d45362c001904gpdi.html)): "No longer pursuing an 'omnipotent model,' but instead adopting the mainstream deployment path of 'open-source base + private-data fine-tuning + RAG augmentation,' bringing the cost of use down to roughly one-fifth that of general large models"
- This means clients **do not need a "vertical financial foundation model" as a standalone product**, but rather need an integrated solution of **"open-source base + fine-tuning tools + RAG + agent toolchain + services"**

**Counter-conclusion**: Clients' real pain point is not at the "foundation-model capability" layer, but at the "toolchain + deployment + compliance + service" layer.

- **Path A (self-developed general LLM)** has no realistic feasibility

## Part Four: Differentiated-value analysis

### 4.1 Benchmarking matrix: Ant Digital vs. general foundation-model vendors

### 6.3 Clear answers on differentiated value
