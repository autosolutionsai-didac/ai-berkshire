# Leader of a Small Track, Challenger in a Big Track

> *Understanding Ant Digital Technologies* series · Part 03 · Competitive landscape
> Reading time: approximately 13 minutes

## Ant Digital Technologies' competitive position

Being No. 1 in a given track and that No. 1 position actually being worth something are two different things.

The last piece dissected how Ant Digital Technologies makes money. This piece answers a sharper question: in every track where it makes money, can that No. 1 position hold? Who are the rivals, where do the threats come from, and is the moat real or an illusion? Here's the conclusion up front, argued point by point below:

> **Ant Digital Technologies' competitive position is "the leader of a small track, the challenger in a big track, a survivor caught in a pincer between two fronts."**

---

## The competitive landscape across the five product lines

### 1. AntChain (blockchain BaaS): No. 1 commercially, but the "national team" is closing in

AntChain's BaaS market share of **26.5%** has ranked No. 1 for 4 straight years (source: IDC), which looks like a secure position. But the threat isn't from commercial rivals — it's at the **policy level**.

| Rival | Share/position | Core strength | Threat level |
|---------|----------|---------|---------|
| **Chang'an Chain (ChainMaker)** | No. 1 in usage rate per CAICT | "National team" pedigree (led by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology + Tsinghua University) + dedicated chips + fully self-controllable | Extremely high |
| Tencent TBaaS | No. 2 at 18.8% | No. 1 in government blockchain at 23.3%; tens of thousands of financial clients | High |
| WeBank's FISCO BCOS | No. 1 open-source ecosystem | 5,000+ institutions / 100,000+ developers | Medium |
| BSN | Infrastructure layer | National-level blockchain infrastructure | Medium |

**Key judgment**: In the domestic market, AntChain faces the structural risk of "national-team substitution" — in government and state-owned-enterprise scenarios, Chang'an Chain is eating into AntChain's share on the strength of its "fully self-controllable" label. AntChain's advantage lies more in **commercialization and institutional-grade applications** than in policy-driven procurement.

**Overseas is AntChain's real battlefield**. Ant Digital Technologies' push into RWA tokenization — the Jovay Layer 2 public chain, the DT Tokenization Suite, the Anvita platform — targets the global market. But overseas, the rival becomes **R3 Corda** (**$17 billion** in on-chain assets, already partnered with Solana, source: R3's official statements).

**Counterargument**: AntChain's 5,000+ blockchain patents, the most of any company globally, is a genuine technical moat. In consortium-chain scenarios for financial institutions, AntChain's performance, stability, and compliance certifications remain the strongest in China.

### 2. Morse privacy computing: an outright leader, but the market itself is the biggest enemy

Morse's **36.7%** market share is far ahead of the No. 2 player. But as noted in the last piece — **the entire market is only about 1 billion yuan.**

| Rival | Share/position | Core strength | Threat level |
|---------|----------|---------|---------|
| Lanxiang Zhilian | No. 2 per IDC | Fastest growth at 84.1% | High |
| Huakong Qingjiao | Top five | The only vendor with an MPC production system live at a major bank; Tsinghua academic pedigree | High |
| WeBank's FATE | No. 1 in ecosystem influence | The world's largest open-source federated-learning framework (5,600+ GitHub stars) | Medium |

**Morse's problem isn't "who might beat it" — it's "can this track ever get bigger."**

In 2024, the whole industry saw only 1 financing deal and 4 newly registered companies — this track is undergoing a brutal shakeout. Whether it can break through its ceiling on the back of the large-model era's demand for federated training and data-element marketization policy is an existential question for Morse.

**Counterargument**: Data-element marketization reform is advancing, with 10 cities including Hangzhou building privacy-preserving computing platforms. If policy dividends materialize, the privacy-computing market could leap from 1 billion to 10 billion yuan. Morse's first-mover advantage as the leader would become enormous leverage once the market takes off.

### 3. AntShield (intelligent risk control): an overlooked moat

AntShield may be Ant Digital Technologies' **least-discussed but deepest-moated** product line.

- IDC ranks it **No. 1** in China's retail-credit intelligent risk-control market with a **15.7%** share (source: IDC, May 2025)
- Forrester ranks it **No. 1** among APAC anti-fraud solutions
- China's retail-credit risk-control market was worth approximately **65.4 billion yuan** in 2024

| Rival | Core strength | Threat level |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Tongdun Technology | Purely independent third party + 10,000+ clients, though its founder has been prosecuted | High |
| Bangsun Technology | Real-time computing engine "Liu Cube"; pursuing a second IPO attempt | Medium |
| Bingjian Technology | Enterprise credit reporting + overseas expansion | Medium |

**Why AntShield's moat runs deepest**: The core of risk control is **data**. More than a decade of anti-fraud data accumulated by Ant Group across Alipay, Huabei, and MYbank, plus the risk labels AntShield has accumulated in live deployments across hundreds of financial institutions, form a data moat that's extremely hard for rivals to replicate.

Large models are "evolutionarily reshaping" the risk-control track rather than disrupting it — the stronger the model's capability, the more it actually depends on data and real-world context, which **favors AntShield, the deepest data accumulator**.

**Counterargument**: The compliance boundary around data is a real risk. How much of Alipay's transaction data can legally be shared with Ant Digital Technologies? Tightening enforcement of the Personal Information Protection Law could narrow this data pipeline.

### 4. Agentar (AI agents): the fiercest battlefield

AI agents are Ant Digital Technologies' **fastest-growing but most brutally contested** product line.

First, Ant Digital Technologies' scorecard:

| Achievement | Data | Source |
|------|------|------|
| No. 1 globally on BIRD-SQL | 92% accuracy, No. 1 on the global leaderboard | QbitAI, Dec 2025 |
| CAICT's top-tier certification | Level-5 Trusted AI | CAICT, Oct 2025 |
| Financial reasoning benchmark | Agentar-Fin-R1 beats DeepSeek-R1 on multiple financial benchmarks | Sina Finance, Jul 2025 |
| Enterprise agent platform award | "Comprehensive Pick" | IT Home, Apr 2026 |

Not a bad scorecard. But look at the rivals:

**Ant Digital Technologies faces not a single competitor, but a "pincer attack from both directions":**

**Pressing down from above are the foundation-model vendors** — DeepSeek V4 went open source (April 2026) with an input price of **1 yuan per million tokens** and just **0.025 yuan per million tokens** on cache hits, with agent capability described as "leading among domestic open-source models." Qwen 3.5 (February 2026), with 397 billion parameters, surpasses Gemini 3, at prices as low as **0.8 yuan** per million tokens. As general-purpose foundation-model capability becomes commoditized and prices approach zero, Agentar's room for differentiation at the model layer is being squeezed hard.

**Pressing up from below are the financial-IT vendors** — Hundsun Technologies holds a monopoly-like position in securities IT, with LightGPT trained on 400 billion tokens of financial data; Baidu AI Cloud has the No. 1 financial-LLM market share at 12.2%; Huawei Cloud's Pangu 5.5 already covers six major banks.

**An even bigger threat comes from clients building in-house**:
- China Construction Bank's "Ark Program": chose an open-source foundation and built in-house, having already adapted **16 versions** of general-purpose LLMs by end of 2024, **independent of Ant Digital Technologies**
- China Merchants Bank: chose **Alibaba's Tongyi model** as its primary partner, **not Ant Digital Technologies**
- China UnionPay: in April 2026, deployed **DeepSeek-V4** privately, running on Huawei Ascend compute

**One-line verdict**: Agentar's value hinges on whether financial scenarios are special and complex enough that a general-purpose solution can never fully replace it. If the answer is "yes," Agentar has a moat; if "no," Agentar is just a financial AI integrator.

**Counterargument**: Ant Digital Technologies offers something in financial agents that a general-purpose LLM vendor can't — **a backstop**. General-model vendors treat the sale as "job done once the model ships"; Ant Digital Technologies offers a service commitment of "pay for performance, and I'll cover you if something goes wrong." In a financial industry with extremely high compliance requirements, that kind of service model carries genuine customer value.

## Two structural threats

Beyond the rivals in each individual track, Ant Digital Technologies also faces two **cross-track structural threats**.

### Structural threat one: Huawei Cloud eating upward across the full stack

Huawei Cloud is Ant Digital Technologies' biggest potential threat — not because it leads in any single track, but because it has **full-stack capability**.

| Huawei Cloud's position in the financial industry | Data |
|---------------------------|------|
| Share of financial self-built cloud | No. 1 at **28.9%** (source: IDC) |
| Client coverage | Six major banks + all joint-stock banks |
| Strategic direction | Extending from IaaS across the full stack into PaaS |

Huawei Cloud's "one cloud" strategy means: small and mid-sized banks may increasingly favor a "one-stop solution" (where Huawei Cloud has the edge) over a "best-of-breed combination" (where Ant Digital Technologies has the edge). Large banks currently still lean toward the latter — but if that preference shifts, Ant Digital Technologies' middleware and AI businesses will take a direct hit.

### Structural threat two: DeepSeek's open-source push eroding the value of the AI application layer

DeepSeek V4's (April 2026) pricing — **0.025 yuan per million tokens** on cache hits — is **essentially free**.

The impact on Ant Digital Technologies' AI business runs deep:

- **Large banks can build in-house directly on open-source models**, without needing Ant Digital Technologies' Agentar platform
- **Small and mid-sized banks can call ultra-cheap APIs directly**, without needing to buy a "financial vertical foundation model"
- Agentar's value is being compressed from "model + platform + service" down to a **pure service layer**

**But on the other hand**: the cheaper and more commoditized the model, the greater the demand for "who's going to help me actually use this model well." That is precisely the opportunity behind Ant Digital Technologies' positioning as a "financial-grade AI service provider."

## Testing the moat: real or illusory

Putting the competitive landscape of all five lines together, how deep is Ant Digital Technologies' moat really?

| Moat type | Score | Core evidence | Durability |
|-----------|------|---------|--------|
| **Switching costs** | High | SOFAStack deeply embedded in 130+ financial institutions; once Agentar is rolled out bank-wide at Bank of Ningbo, switching costs become extremely high | Medium-high |
| **Brand mindshare** | High | "Validated by Alipay" is a top-tier endorsement in the financial industry | Medium-high |
| **Regulatory licenses** | Medium-high | Financial-grade privacy-computing certifications, Hong Kong stablecoin application, first batch of private participants in the Ensemble Sandbox | Widening |
| **Data moat** | Medium | AntShield's anti-fraud data, tens of millions of asset records on AntChain — but Alipay's core data belongs to the parent group | Stable |
| **Network effects** | Low | Network effects are inherently weak in enterprise services. Blockchain/RWA has weak network effects but they haven't yet formed | Widening slowly |

**Overall moat rating: medium-high (3.4/5).**

Ant Digital Technologies' moat **does exist**, but it's different from Tencent's WeChat (an almost irreplaceable social-network effect) or Pinduoduo's algorithm-plus-user-base combination — Ant Digital Technologies' moat looks more like **Hundsun Technologies-style "embedded switching cost."**

This implies:
1. Existing customers rarely churn (high switching costs = high retention)
2. But acquiring new customers is also costly (no network effect to accelerate acquisition)
3. The depth of the moat depends on how deeply embedded the customer is — the deeper, the safer

**Counterargument**: Strip out OceanBase and the Bailing large model, and Ant Digital Technologies' own moat sits mainly in blockchain patents + SOFAStack switching costs + brand mindshare. These moats are real, but not as deep as OceanBase's engineering moat (over a decade of accumulated C++ engineering). Ant Digital Technologies looks more like a combination of "brand premium + domain understanding + service capability" than "absolute technical leadership."

## What is Ant Digital Technologies' real competitive advantage?

After dissecting all five lines and every rival, the answer is actually clear:

**Ant Digital Technologies' real competitive advantage isn't technology — it's "doing the dirty, unglamorous work of financial scenarios."**

- Technical moats can be caught up to — Huawei has full-stack capability, Zhipu has its own large models
- Client relationships can be poached — Huawei already has infrastructure footholds at the six major banks, Tencent has tens of thousands of financial clients
- What's genuinely hard for anyone to replicate is Ant Digital Technologies' **decade-plus of financial-scenario engineering capability built on the Alipay ecosystem** — 66 categories of financial agent use cases, CAICT Level-5 certification, industry-specific data training for its financial reasoning models, and battle-tested validation under extreme conditions like high concurrency, high availability, and fund-safety requirements

An analogy: **Ant Digital Technologies isn't the best chef (its foundation-model capability isn't the strongest), and it isn't the best ingredient supplier (it doesn't have the most compute) — but it may be the restaurant manager who best understands the financial industry's "diner's palate": knowing exactly how a dish needs to be adjusted, which diner is allergic to salt, and which dish can never be allowed to go wrong.**

This combination of scenario understanding, compliance backstopping, and private-deployment capability may, in an era where general-purpose foundation models are becoming commodities, turn out to be the most valuable form of differentiation.

## Optimal strategic positioning

Based on the analysis above, the most rational strategic positioning for Ant Digital Technologies is:

**Don't try to be a general-purpose platform (it can't beat Alibaba Cloud or Huawei Cloud) — be the deepest vertical solution provider in the financial industry.**

The core bet is: **the financial industry is special and complex enough that a general-purpose solution can never fully replace it.**

RWA/Web3 is the only path out of the "small-track leader" trap — if the Jovay public-chain ecosystem matures, stablecoin licenses are secured, and the global RWA market takes off, Ant Digital Technologies has a shot at leaping from "No. 1 in a small track" to an early mover in a large, emerging track.

But this is an "option" highly dependent on external variables (regulation, market timing), not a path with certainty.
