## I. One-Line Conclusion

> Ant Digital Technologies (Ant DT) is the core vehicle through which Ant Group monetizes the spillover of its technology capabilities, holding the No. 1 position in blockchain BaaS, privacy computing, and financial middleware sub-segments, with roughly 5.5 billion yuan in 2024 revenue and having reached breakeven. Note, however: OceanBase (the distributed database) now operates independently and is not part of Ant DT. Once OceanBase is stripped out, Ant DT's core assets are AntChain + privacy computing + SOFAStack + AI + ZOLOZ, sitting in tracks that are relatively small in scale. A reasonable valuation range is 30-50 billion yuan (RMB) (USD 4.1-6.9 billion), with commercialization still to be proven.

## VI. Summary by Dimension

### Business model (business-decoder)
1. **Essence**: The productized spillover of Ant Group's decade-plus of financial-grade technology; "battle-tested by Alipay" is the core trust endorsement
2. **"Pay-for-performance" model**: Lowers the decision threshold for smaller banks, but converts revenue from a fixed license fee into a variable tied to business outcomes
3. **RWA / Web3**: Connects 15 million new-energy devices + a pending stablecoin license + the Jovay public chain, pointing toward a complete "asset tokenization + payment + trading" closed loop
4. **Moderate flywheel strength**: Strong lock-in among financial customers, but dependent on a single industry; cross-industry network effects have not yet formed

### Financials & valuation (financial-detective)
1. **Revenue trajectory**: From RMB 1.1 billion in the 2020 prospectus to approximately RMB 5.5 billion in 2024, roughly a 5x increase over 4 years
2. **Only ~4% of the group total**: Ant Group's total revenue is approximately RMB 135 billion; the "technology content" is far lower than the external narrative suggests
3. **Ant Group's profit growth is driven by cost compression**: total revenue may still be declining (RMB 135 billion vs. RMB 180 billion in 2022); the strategic importance of the technology business as a "second growth curve" stands out
4. **Weighted valuation midpoint of approximately RMB 53 billion (approximately USD 7.3 billion)**, with a margin-of-safety entry point at RMB 30-40 billion

### Industry & competition (competitive-mapper)
1. **Ant DT's own tracks are relatively small**: the BaaS market is ~RMB 1.9 billion, privacy computing ~RMB 1.0 billion; although Ant DT holds the No. 1 spot, the ceiling is limited
2. **Blockchain competition**: AntChain leads BaaS with 26.5% share, but Tencent's TrustSQL (Zhixin Chain) and ChainMaker (Chang'an Chain) are close behind; the RWA track is at a very early stage with an unknown scale for commercialization
3. **AI is a red ocean**: the Bailing large model has less brand recognition than Qwen or DeepSeek; vertical financial AI is a differentiating direction, but the market is only just getting started
4. **Note**: the competitive landscape in the database track (OceanBase vs. TDSQL vs. GaussDB) belongs to OceanBase's independent business and does not directly concern Ant DT

### Risk & governance (risk-governance-analyst)
1. **Financial holding company license unapproved for 5 years**: the biggest obstacle to Ant Group's overall listing, affecting Ant DT via brand association
2. **AI core talent turmoil**: two AI vice presidents (Wang Xiaohang and Xu Peng) departed one after another within six months, exposing internal disagreement over the AI roadmap
3. **"No controlling shareholder" governance**: Jack Ma's voting rights have fallen to 6.2%, with 10 members of management exercising voting rights in a dispersed manner — decision-making efficiency is in question
4. **Ant International's Hong Kong IPO may come first**: Ant DT's independent listing is unlikely before 2027-2028 at the earliest

### Technology & IP (tech-ip-analyst)
1. **Ant Group's group-wide R&D spend is RMB 23.45 billion/year**, but Ant DT's independent share is unclear (Ant DT, OceanBase, and Ant International all share this spend)
2. **Ant DT's own technology highlights**: 5,000+ blockchain patents (No. 1 globally), trusted-AI patents No. 1 globally (2 consecutive years), the DTVM smart-contract virtual machine (JIT engine performance improved roughly 30x), and the SecretFlow open-source privacy-computing framework (2,700+ GitHub stars)
3. **On AI**: Ant DT operates at the AI application layer (the Agentar agent platform, financial reasoning models); the underlying Bailing large model belongs to Ant Group, not Ant DT
4. **Note**: OceanBase's engineering moat (the TPC-C world record, 14 years of C++ accumulation) and the Bailing large model (trillion-parameter scale, IMO gold-medal-level performance) do not belong to Ant DT. Ant DT's own technology moat sits primarily in blockchain + privacy computing + the SOFAStack middleware

### Alternative data signals (signal-miner)
1. **The three-step "spin-off + outside shareholders + new CFO"**: systematically preparing subsidiaries for IPO, with OceanBase and Ant International the most likely to go first
2. **RWA is the most undervalued piece**: 15 million devices on-chain + a pending stablecoin license + the Jovay public chain's mainnet launch in Q3 2025 (note: the Bright Smart Securities acquisition was carried out at the Ant Group level, not as a direct Ant DT asset)
3. **Anomalous signals**: the CFO change contradicts the IPO denials; OceanBase is deliberately "de-Ant-ifying"; RWA revenue is deliberately undisclosed (either the scale is still small, or there are compliance considerations)
4. **Glassdoor scores are on the low side**: Ant Group overall scores 3.6/5, work-life balance only 2.7/5, with a 43% recommend rate — Ant DT-specific data is unavailable; this is reference only

## VII. Assessment of True Value

### Judgment on the nature of the business

**In one line**: Ant DT sells a bundled solution of "blockchain infrastructure + privacy computing + financial middleware + AI applications," with the Ant brand endorsement and financial-industry know-how as its core selling points.

**Certainty assessment**: Medium. The core tracks (BaaS / privacy computing) are relatively small in scale and growth is slowing; RWA / Web3 is a big story but at a very early stage; the AI application layer depends on the group's foundation model. Certainty is lower than OceanBase's (whose demand is driven by the more assured Xinchuang domestic-substitution trend).

**Duan Yongping-style judgment**: Is this "the right business"?
- The right industry: fintech infrastructure, a long runway with thick snow (i.e., a long-term compounding opportunity)
- The right starting point: a decade of technology accumulated and proven within the Alipay ecosystem
- But still to be verified: whether it can keep acquiring customers and innovating once independent of the parent company's halo effect

## VIII. Investment Thesis (Bull vs. Bear)

### 🟢 Bull case

1. **Indirect beneficiary of the Xinchuang window**: Document No. 79 is accelerating digital transformation at financial institutions, and Ant DT's middleware (SOFAStack / mPaaS) is an indirect beneficiary of the Xinchuang (domestic IT substitution) wave (note: the direct beneficiaries are database vendors such as OceanBase, not Ant DT) (source: Xinchuang white paper 🟢)
2. **5x growth over 4 years plus a breakeven inflection point**: from RMB 1.1 billion in 2020 to approximately RMB 5.5 billion in 2024, with 2025E growth of 50%+ and "decent profit," entering a harvest phase (source: CEO disclosures + prospectus comparison 🟡)
3. **Blockchain / privacy-computing technology leadership**: 5,000+ blockchain patents (No. 1 globally), trusted-AI patents No. 1 globally, the DTVM virtual machine's JIT engine delivering roughly 30x better performance — the technology accumulation is real (source: white paper + open-source data 🟢)
4. **Compounded advantage of being No. 1 across multiple tracks**: 26.5% in BaaS + 35.3% in privacy computing + No. 1 in financial PaaS, with significant cross-sell potential (source: IDC report 🟢) (note: being No. 1 in financial databases belongs to OceanBase and is excluded)
5. **RWA / Web3 option value**: the global tokenizable-asset opportunity is enormous (note: industry forecasts vary widely, and the amount actually tokenized to date remains small); AntChain's first-mover position in Hong Kong (stablecoin license application + the Jovay public chain) is a potential breakout point (source: industry forecasts 🟡)
6. **Internationalization as a growth pole**: overseas revenue grew 300% year-on-year in 2022-2023, covering 24 countries and 13,000+ customers, with ZOLOZ leading in Southeast Asian eKYC (source: official disclosures 🟡)

### 🔴 Bear case

1. **Financial holding company license unapproved for 5 years**: the biggest obstacle to Ant Group's overall listing, with brand-association effects weighing on Ant DT's customer expansion and IPO progress (source: PBOC announcements 🟢)
2. **Revenue quality in question**: the share from related-party transactions is unclear — how much revenue comes from within the Ant ecosystem? Can growth be sustained once independent? (source: analytical inference 🔴)
3. **The value of being "No. 1 in a small track"**: the BaaS market is only ~RMB 1.9 billion and privacy computing only ~RMB 1.0 billion; Ant DT's combined tracks total under RMB 3 billion, a limited ceiling (source: IDC 🟢)
4. **Late-mover disadvantage in AI**: two AI vice presidents departed within six months; the Bailing large model has less brand recognition than Qwen or DeepSeek; the CEO admits it is "moving slower than others" (source: media reports 🟢)
5. **Competition from large incumbents**: Tencent's TrustSQL is close behind in BaaS (No. 2 in the market); Huawei and Tencent both have full-stack capability in privacy computing and AI; Ant DT lacks an absolute monopoly (source: competitive analysis 🟡)
6. **Unclear exit path**: Ant DT's independent listing is unlikely before 2027-2028 at the earliest; it cannot trade separately in the secondary market, and liquidity is very poor (source: CEO remarks 🟡)
7. **Parent-company drag risk**: Ant Group's total revenue may still be on a declining trajectory (~RMB 135 billion vs. RMB 180 billion in 2022), with regulatory pressure continuing (source: financial estimates 🔴)

### ⚖️ Which side is more persuasive?

**The bear case is somewhat stronger** — once OceanBase and the Bailing large model are stripped out, the tracks Ant DT itself occupies (BaaS + privacy computing + middleware) are relatively small in market size, and the AI application layer faces intense competition while depending on the group's foundation model. RWA / Web3 is the only direction with truly large upside, but it is at a very early stage today and its revenue contribution is likely negligible.

The bull case's core support lies in **RWA option value and internationalization growth** — if the Hong Kong stablecoin license is approved and the Jovay public-chain ecosystem matures, Ant DT could leap from "No. 1 in a small track" to a first mover in an emerging track. But this is a high-uncertainty bet.

## XIV. Summary

Ant DT is the vehicle through which Ant Group monetizes the spillover of its technology capabilities, leading in the blockchain BaaS (No. 1 at 26.5%) and privacy-computing (No. 1 at 35.3%) sub-segments, with roughly RMB 5.5 billion in 2024 revenue and having reached breakeven. But one must recognize clearly: **neither OceanBase (the distributed database) nor the Bailing large model belongs to Ant DT** — the former now operates independently, and the latter is an Ant Group-level asset. Once these two are stripped out, Ant DT's own core assets are AntChain + Morse privacy computing + the SOFAStack middleware + the Agentar AI application + ZOLOZ overseas eKYC, in tracks that are relatively small in scale (BaaS plus privacy computing together total under a RMB 3 billion market).

Ant DT faces four layers of uncertainty: **first**, revenue independence — the related-party-transaction share is unclear; **second**, a limited track ceiling — the core tracks are small in scale, and RWA / Web3 is the only direction with large upside, but at a very early stage; **third**, AI dependency — the AI application layer depends on the group's Bailing model, with no proprietary foundation model of its own; **fourth**, the exit path — an independent listing is unlikely before 2027-2028 at the earliest.

On valuation, after stripping out OceanBase and Bailing, we revise the range down to **RMB 30-50 billion (USD 4.1-6.9 billion)**. The current implied valuation of approximately RMB 38 billion sits in the lower-middle portion of the reasonable range.

**Final recommendation: Hold / wait-and-see.** Wait for three trigger conditions: ① Ant DT completes independent financing (a clear valuation anchor), ② the related-party-transaction share is disclosed and below 40% (verifying revenue independence), ③ RWA business revenue contribution becomes quantifiable (verifying the growth story).
