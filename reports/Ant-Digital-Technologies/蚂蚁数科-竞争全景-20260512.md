# Ant Digital Technologies Competitive Landscape Research Report

> **Research date**: May 12, 2026
> **Research method**: 7 agents conducting parallel research (competitors across 5 product lines + cloud-provider threats + large-model-vendor threats)
> **Research subject**: Ant Digital Technologies (excluding OceanBase, excluding the Bailing large model)

---

## I. Core Conclusion

Ant Digital Technologies' competitive position can be summed up in one sentence: **"the leader in small tracks, the chaser in large tracks, a survivor caught in a pincer movement."**

- It leads in sub-segments such as blockchain BaaS (26.5%, No. 1), privacy computing (36.7%, No. 1), and financial anti-fraud (No. 1 in Asia-Pacific) — but these tracks together add up to a market size of less than RMB 4 billion
- In the high-growth, large-scale AI agent track (projected at RMB 19.3 billion by 2030), it faces a four-way siege from Baidu, Tencent, Huawei, and Hundsun Technologies
- Structural threats come from two directions: Huawei Cloud encroaching upward from IaaS into a full PaaS stack, and large-model vendors squeezing downward from the model layer into the application layer

## IV. Cloud-Provider Threat Assessment

| Cloud provider | Threat rating | Core rationale |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Huawei Cloud** | ★★★★ | No. 1 in the financial private-cloud market at 28.9% share; clients include all six major banks plus every joint-stock bank; is extending from IaaS into a full PaaS stack. Brand trust ("domestically secure and controllable") is extremely strong in the financial industry |
| **Tencent Cloud** | ★★★ | No. 2 in BaaS (18.8%), No. 1 in government blockchain (23.3%), tens of thousands of financial clients, but less depth than Ant Digital Technologies in financial middleware/privacy computing |
| **Alibaba Cloud** | ★★ | Part of the same Alibaba ecosystem; SOFAStack is sold through Alibaba Cloud's channels. The main risk is internal overlap between Bailian and Agentar (there are reportedly six agent platforms within the Alibaba Group) |
| **AWS** | ★ | Its presence in China's financial market keeps declining; its major Greater China accounts are "wavering" |
| **Azure** | ★★ | Leads globally in enterprise AI, but is constrained in China's financial market by data sovereignty and domestic-substitution requirements |

**Core takeaway**: The biggest threat Ant Digital Technologies faces isn't direct competition from any single cloud vendor — it's two structural risks: (1) Huawei Cloud encroaching upward into the PaaS layer with its full stack; and (2) financial-institution clients choosing between a "one-stop cloud solution" and a "best-of-breed" combination. Large banks currently still lean toward best-of-breed (favoring Ant Digital Technologies), but small and mid-sized banks may lean more toward a one-stop solution (where Huawei Cloud has the advantage).

## V. Large-Model Vendor Threat Assessment

| Large-model vendor | Threat rating | Core rationale |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Alibaba Cloud Bailian** | ★★★★ | The most dangerous form of competition is from within the same ecosystem — Bailian is positioned as general-purpose but is penetrating finance, competing with Agentar for the same client base |
| **DeepSeek** | ★★★★½ | Not a direct competitor, but it **undermines the value of the AI application layer from below** — 20+ banks have already deployed it, and major banks are building their own systems on the open-source model, bypassing Agentar |
| **Zhipu AI** | ★★★½ | A direct rival (market cap of RMB 347 billion after listing; financial-agent revenue up 248.8%), with the advantage of a proprietary foundation model |
| **Baichuan AI** | ★ | Has already exited the financial track and pivoted to medical AI |
| **Moonshot AI / MiniMax** | ★½ | Focused on the consumer market; lacks a compliance framework for the financial industry |
| **OpenAI/Anthropic** | ★★ (for ZOLOZ's overseas expansion) | Not entering China's financial market, but empowering local competitors overseas |

**Core takeaway**: What Ant Digital Technologies faces isn't being "steamrolled by large models" — it's **"the value layer being squeezed"**: from above by large-model vendors (Bailian/Zhipu) extending from the model layer into the application layer, and from below by open-source models (DeepSeek) letting major banks build in-house and bypass the middle layer. The "model-as-platform" trend holds true for standardized use cases but not for deep financial use cases. Agentar's value depends on whether financial scenarios are specialized and complex enough that a general-purpose solution can never fully replace it.

## VI. The Ultimate Verdict on the Competitive Landscape

### What is Ant Digital Technologies' real competitive advantage?

**It's not technology — it's "doing the dirty, hard work of financial use cases."**

- Technology barriers (patents/algorithms) can be caught up to — Huawei has full-stack capability, Zhipu has a proprietary large model
- Client relationships can be poached — Huawei already holds infrastructure at the six major banks, Tencent has tens of thousands of financial clients
- What is genuinely hard for Ant Digital Technologies to replicate is: the **financial-scenario engineering capability** accumulated over a decade-plus within the Alipay system — 66 categories of financial AI-agent use cases, CAICT Level-5 certification, industry-data training for financial reasoning models, and real-world validation in extreme scenarios like high concurrency, high availability, and fund security

### The Three Biggest Competitive Risks

1. **Huawei Cloud pushing its full stack upward** (probability: high / impact: high): Huawei Cloud already holds the commanding heights of financial IaaS; if it continues extending into PaaS/the application layer, its "one cloud" strategy and client relationships could gradually erode Ant Digital Technologies' market
2. **DeepSeek's open-source models eroding the value of the AI application layer** (probability: high / impact: medium-high): major banks are building in-house capability on open-source models, and Agentar's positioning as a "financial AI platform" risks being squeezed down to that of an "integration service provider"
3. **A ceiling on the core tracks** (probability: high / impact: medium): BaaS (~RMB 1.9 billion) + privacy computing (~RMB 1 billion) + AntShield's share of the security/risk-control market together add up to a core market of less than RMB 4 billion — not enough to support a premium-valuation narrative

### Ant Digital Technologies' Best Strategic Positioning

**Don't build a general-purpose platform (it can't beat Alibaba Cloud/Huawei Cloud/Zhipu) — become the deepest vertical solution provider in the financial industry.** The core bet: the financial industry is specialized and complex enough that a general-purpose solution can never fully replace it. RWA/Web3 is the only way out of the "small-track leader" trap — if the Hong Kong stablecoin + Jovay public-chain ecosystem takes shape, Ant Digital Technologies has a chance to leap from "No. 1 in a small track" to a first-mover in an emerging large track.
