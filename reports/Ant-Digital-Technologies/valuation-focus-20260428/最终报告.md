### Key Bottleneck

> **The Hong Kong Stock Exchange has never approved an independent IPO by a subsidiary whose unlisted parent has not itself listed.** This is the single hardest constraint on Digital Technologies' IPO timing.

This means: **either Ant Group's overall IPO or Ant International's IPO going first** is a precondition (not a sufficient condition) for Digital Technologies' independent IPO. Whichever of the two happens first, plus a 12-18-month observation period, roughly determines the earliest possible date for Digital Technologies to file.

## Part Three: Total Share Capital (Q3)

### Core Conclusion: The Concept of "Total Share Capital" Does Not Currently Exist

Ant Digital Technologies' operating entity, "**Antchain (Shanghai) Digital Technology Co., Ltd.**," is currently a **limited liability company**; the only publicly disclosed figure is "registered capital of RMB 2.107 billion" — **there is no concept of "shares."** Discussing "total share capital of X shares" is a **false premise**.

| Dimension | Current state | IPO entity (future) |
|---|---|---|
| Legal form | Limited liability company | Joint-stock company (proposed Hong Kong-listing holding company, possibly Cayman/BVI) |
| Capital measurement | Registered capital of RMB 2.107 billion | Total share capital (to be issued) |
| Shareholder | Antchain International, 100% (presumed to be Ant Group's offshore holding shell) | Controlling shareholder + Pre-IPO investors + employee stock ownership + public |
| Employee stock-ownership platform | ❌ Not established in the business registry | ✅ Must be established (per Hong Kong convention) |

### Reconstructing the Current State (Business-Registry Level)

- Wholly owned by **Antchain International Company Limited** (presumed to be Ant Group's offshore holding layer)
- **No outside financial investors have been brought in**
- **No employee stock-ownership limited partnership has been established** — this is **1-2 years behind OceanBase** (OceanBase completed its equity restructuring in June 2024, adding 35 new shareholders, including Yunfeng Capital, Greenland, E Fund, Sequoia, and others)

### The Group's 1.4% Mapping ≠ Digital Technologies' Share Capital

> **Important clarification**: the "1.4% mapping to Digital Technologies" under the parent company's "one-split-into-four" split is an **internal accounting split of the Group's equity-incentive program** (applying to equity granted before 2024-03-31). Departing employees are subject to **mandatory cash buyback**, and this is **not directly linked** to Digital Technologies' own share-capital table. This is a separate system from "the incentive pool Digital Technologies will set up on its own in the future."

### Layer B: The "Digital Technologies Portion" Within an SER

**Accounting basis** (per the one-split-into-four 1.4% mapping):
- Digital Technologies' portion of each SER ≈ 108.21 × (1.4% ÷ 93.4%) ≈ **RMB 1.62/SER**

**Market-based correction** (using Digital Technologies' market-based valuation of RMB 52 billion vs. the Group's RMB 571 billion):
- Correction factor = RMB 52 billion / (RMB 571 billion × 1.4%) ≈ 6.5x
- Market-based "Digital Technologies portion of each SER" ≈ **RMB 10.5/SER**

> **The accounting basis undervalues by roughly 6-7x relative to the market-based basis** — this is the core commercial logic behind departing employees' protests against the mandatory buyback in March 2025.

## Part Five: Core Tensions and Blind Spots

### Three Major Valuation Tensions

1. **A 15x gap between accounting and market-based valuation**: the employee-equity basis implies RMB 8 billion, while the market-based neutral figure is RMB 52 billion — a 6.5x gap; using the parent-company back-solve's upper bound of RMB 122 billion, the gap widens to 15x. Which does the market actually believe?
2. **Hong Kong tech-stock re-rating vs. absence from the large-IPO reserve list**: tech-stock valuations are rising, but Ant-affiliated entities are absent from Hong Kong's 2026 large-IPO reserve list — Digital Technologies may not be able to capture the dividend of that re-rating.
3. **AI narrative strengthening vs. an untransparent revenue mix**: establishing a Foundation Model Innovation Department and topping the BIRD leaderboard make a good story, but **absolute third-party revenue is still only RMB 6-9 billion** — whether the narrative premium can be sustained is the key question.

## Part Seven: Overall Verdict

### Valuation Conclusion

> **Ant Digital Technologies' fair valuation range as of 2026/04/28: RMB 40-68 billion (median RMB 52 billion)**
>
> Revised down from 4/17's lower bound, mainly because the first batch of Hong Kong stablecoin licenses excluding Ant knocked out the optimistic narrative. The RMB 150-200 billion figure circulating in the market remains significantly overstated, and the employee-equity basis of RMB 8 billion remains significantly understated — the neutral range in between is the most credible.

### IPO Timing Conclusion

> **Baseline: file 2028 H2 / list 2029 Q2-Q4, with only a 50% probability of an independent IPO within 5 years**
>
> The absence from Hong Kong's large-IPO reserve list, the lack of any successful precedent among spun-off fintech subsidiaries, and the hard constraint that the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has never approved an "unlisted parent's spun-off subsidiary" IPO all compound one another. The probability of full success is 5-10%, and the expected IRR is roughly -5.7%/year.

### Total Share Capital Conclusion

> **"Total share capital" is currently a false premise; the correct statement is "registered capital of RMB 2.107 billion"**
>
> 100% held by Ant Group (via the offshore holding layer), with no outside investors brought in and no employee stock-ownership platform established. At IPO, total share capital is neutrally estimated at 8.0-12.0 billion shares, with the incentive pool at 8-12%.

### Per-Share Value Conclusion

> **The three layers must be kept separate: Layer A at RMB 24.7 per yuan of registered capital, Layer B at RMB 1.62/SER (accounting basis), and Layer C at an HKD 6-30 implied offer price**
>
> The "book value → IPO-tradeable" multiplier is roughly 8-10x, but realizing it requires strict preconditions: (a) the IPO must succeed; (b) the Group must design a mechanism for active employees to share in Digital Technologies' independent-IPO gains (not currently promised); and (c) liquidity discounts combine to a 0.5-0.6x haircut.

### Investment Recommendation

| Investor type | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **PE/VC (Pre-IPO round)** | **Cautious** | Lack of a valuation anchor + no precedent for outside financing; strict anti-dilution protection is required |
| **Secondary-market transfer** | **Watch and wait** | Liquidity is extremely poor; the transfer price signal for Ant Group's overall secondary transactions is more worth tracking |
| **Post-IPO purchase** | **Watch and wait** | Wait for at least 1-2 quarters of earnings verification; be alert to the "1.4% mapping" being repriced by the market |
| **Catalysts to watch** | **Continue tracking** | Parent-company IPO restart / Ant International pricing / second batch of stablecoin licenses / related-party-transaction disclosure |
