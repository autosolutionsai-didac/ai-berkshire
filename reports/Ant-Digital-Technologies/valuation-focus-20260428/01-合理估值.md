## 1. Valuation Anchor Update: What Happened in 11 Days

### 1.1 Bearish: Hong Kong's first batch of stablecoin licenses announced, Ant not included 🟢

**Facts** (April 10, 2026, Hong Kong Monetary Authority announcement):
- The Hong Kong Monetary Authority received 36 stablecoin issuer license applications in total, and **only 2 licenses were granted in the first batch**
- Licenses granted to: (1) **Anchorpoint Financial** — a consortium of Standard Chartered, HKT, and Animoca Brands; (2) **HSBC**
- **Major mainland Chinese players including Ant International and JD Coin Chain were not approved**
- Sources: [Sina Finance, 2026-04-12](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-04-12/doc-inhuetnq0871424.shtml), [HKMA announcement](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/chi/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/04/20260410-4/)

**Valuation implications**:
- The 4/17 valuation's "bullish factor 1" — "if the Hong Kong stablecoin license is granted, the valuation multiple for the B+ business could be revised up 3-5x" — **has directly fallen through**
- Signs of the license delay had already appeared since the second half of 2025 (FT reported that Ant/JD had paused their stablecoin plans), and this was confirmed on 4/10
- The probability of the "stablecoin × Ant Digital Technologies" optimistic scenario has fallen from a prior estimate of 30% to **<10%**
- Note the distinction: the entity that did not get approved is "Ant International"; Ant Digital Technologies has always pursued the RWA route ("two chains, one bridge"), and stablecoin is not a core revenue business, so **the direct impact on revenue is limited**, but **the narrative premium has been wiped out**

**Counterpoint**:
- The Monetary Authority granting only 2 licenses does not mean a "permanent rejection" — a second batch could open in the second half of 2026 through 2027
- Ant Digital Technologies' core business is RWA tokenization (already deployed with GCL and Landsea for roughly RMB 100 million in cross-border financing across two deals), and stablecoin is only a supplementary angle
- According to a [techwalker.com](https://www.techwalker.com/2024/1101/3161033.shtml) report, the RWA cross-border compliance cycle in 2026 is expected to compress from 45 days to 7 days, with technology costs down 40% — a tailwind for Ant Digital Technologies' RWA business

### 1.2 Neutral: No April progress on Ant International's Hong Kong IPO 🟡

**Facts**:
- As of 4/28, **there are no reports of Ant International formally filing a Hong Kong prospectus**
- The tender offer deadline for the Yuanta Securities (Hong Kong) acquisition was extended to March 25, 2026 (after 4/17), but search results do not clearly confirm completion
- Source: [Caizhongshe, 2025-10](https://m.caizhongshe.cn/article-4580693459642313888.html)
- Multiple analyses still cite the May 2025 "valuation target of USD 30-40 billion," with no update

**Valuation implications**:
- **If Ant International successfully IPOs within 2026** (still the base case, only the timeline has been pushed back), it would provide a "sibling effect" (referencing the 10-30% premium seen in Tencent/China Literature and Alibaba/Cainiao spin-off cases)
- **If it drags into 2027**, Ant Digital Technologies' IPO timeline will automatically be pushed back as well (market logic: parent not yet listed + sibling not yet listed → Digital Technologies' standalone valuation lacks an anchor)
- Current uncertainty is unchanged from the 4/17 assessment

### 1.3 Bullish: Hong Kong tech stock valuation recovery 🟢

**Facts** (April 2026):
- The Hang Seng Tech Index has risen a cumulative 8.85% since April, retaking the 5,000-point level on 4/22
- Current trailing P/E is 22.54x, at the 29.19th percentile since listing (still relatively low)
- Kingdee International (00268.HK) is up 66% year-to-date, with a market cap of roughly HKD 50-60 billion (approximately RMB 45-55 billion); latest P/S ≈ 6.1x ([Orient Securities research report](https://reportify-1252068037.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/media/production/s_ff32a78c_ff32a78c8d2864158be545bfddb895e6.pdf))
- Hundsun Technologies (600570.SH) closed at RMB 26.71 on 4/20; 2025 net profit of RMB 1.231 billion (+18%), with institutions giving a 2026 target P/E of 31-60x and a market cap of roughly RMB 50-60 billion
- Source: [Sina Finance, 2026-04-22](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-04-22/doc-inhvkyvy4215552.shtml)

**Valuation implications**:
- Hong Kong comparable-company **P/S multiples have edged upward**: Kingdee has moved from 8x on 4/17 to a current forward 6.1x, but with a higher absolute market cap (indicating the market has raised its revenue growth expectations)
- The P/S anchor for Ant Digital Technologies is revised **modestly upward by 10-15%**

### 1.4 Bullish: Continued progress on Ant Digital Technologies' business front 🟡

**Facts**:
- February 4, 2026: Ant Digital Technologies CEO Zhao Wenbiao issued an all-staff letter announcing the establishment of a "Large Model Technology Innovation Department," focused on to-B foundation large models and industry-specific models ([Sina Tech](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/digi/2026-02-04/doc-inhkpzpi4319173.shtml))

**Valuation implications**:

### Method 4: Parent-Company Back-Solve (Updated)

**Bottom-up** (based on the 2025 Hurun figure of RMB 635 billion):
- Ant International IPO valuation anchor (conservative-to-optimistic): USD 8-24 billion = RMB 58-174 billion (source: [Caproasia](https://www.caproasia.com/2025/05/20/alibaba-fintech-ant-group-plans-hong-kong-ipo-for-ant-international-with-reports-of-valuation-between-8-billion-to-24-billion/))
- But Ant International's 2024 revenue was RMB 27 billion, net profit RMB 2.2 billion; at PE 15-20x = RMB 33-44 billion; at PS 6-10x (payments company) = RMB 162-270 billion. **Take the median of approximately RMB 200 billion**
- Digital finance core business (including Alipay, MYbank, and consumer finance): the primary source of net profit, **approximately RMB 330-380 billion** (based on 2024 net profit of RMB 38.3 billion × PE 9-10)
- OceanBase: **estimated at RMB 30-50 billion** 🔴 (no external anchor)
- Investment assets + net cash + other: **approximately RMB 50 billion**
- Back-solving for Ant Digital Technologies: **RMB 635 billion − RMB 200 billion − RMB 350 billion − RMB 40 billion − RMB 50 billion ≈ −RMB 5 billion** (a negative value, indicating the parent-company valuation is already fully accounted for elsewhere)

**Top-down**:
- If Ant International is valued at a **conservative USD 10 billion (~RMB 73 billion)**: RMB 635 billion − RMB 73 billion − RMB 350 billion − RMB 40 billion − RMB 50 billion = **RMB 122 billion** left for Digital Technologies — this is the **theoretical upper bound**
- If Ant International is valued at a **neutral USD 20 billion (~RMB 146 billion)**: remaining **RMB 49 billion**
- If Ant International is valued at an **optimistic USD 30 billion (~RMB 218 billion)**: remaining **−RMB 23 billion** (impossible, indicating that the RMB 635 billion Hurun figure implies essentially zero value for Digital Technologies)

> **Conclusion**: The parent-company back-solve gives a Digital Technologies valuation range of **RMB 0 to 122 billion**, an extremely wide range reflecting the uncertainty in the parent-company valuation anchor itself.
> **Confidence**: 🟡 Medium.
> **Key insight**: The RMB 635 billion Hurun figure for Ant International + Digital Technologies + OceanBase combined implies approximately **RMB 50-250 billion** in aggregate, with **Digital Technologies getting at most 20-30% of that**, i.e., RMB 10-75 billion.

### Counterpoints (Let the Data Speak)

> Buffett: "Price is what you pay. Value is what you get."
> Duan Yongping: "If you don't understand it, don't touch it. Ant Digital Technologies has no external market-based pricing — every valuation here is a projection. What you're essentially buying is a 'hope.'"

**Why the valuation may be too high**:
1. The internal accounting mapping that assigns Digital Technologies 1.4% = RMB 8.8 billion is "hard data"; the marketized valuation of RMB 52 billion is a "story"
2. Related-party transactions of 30-40% have not yet been strictly disclosed in a prospectus; once disclosed, the valuation multiple could be compressed 30-50%
3. Chinese B2B SaaS valuations overall have trended down over the past three years (Kingdee's Hong Kong shares have retraced over 50% from their 2021 peak, with only partial recovery in early 2026)
4. Ant Digital Technologies has not yet produced a flagship "non-Ant-ecosystem" customer case study; genuine third-party revenue remains a black box

**Why the valuation may be too low**:
1. Hong Kong tech valuations overall remain at a historically low 29th percentile (Hang Seng Tech P/E of 22.5x), leaving room for valuation recovery
2. Ant Digital Technologies holds a 26.5% BaaS market share (No. 1 for four consecutive years) + the No. 1 privacy-computing market share + an exclusive path into Hong Kong RWA
3. The parent company's 2024 net profit was RMB 38.3 billion, with 2025 Q2 net profit of RMB 7.6 billion (+1.9x), giving it continued capacity to provide funding
4. A globally scarce AI + B2B SaaS asset, where the AI narrative could produce a short-term valuation premium
5. If a "sibling effect" forms with Ant International's IPO, the market could award Digital Technologies a 10-30% secondary-market premium
