## Summary of analysis by dimension

### I. Business-model analysis (Duan Yongping's perspective)

**Core findings**:

1. **The loss of pricing power is the most fatal signal.** ASP has fallen from RMB 330,000 to RMB 250,000; the flagship L9 was discounted by RMB 20,000 immediately upon launch; full-year 2026 vehicle gross-margin guidance is just 15%. The "pricing power" Duan Yongping values most highly is systematically disappearing.

2. **The moat was shallow to begin with and is being eroded.** The differentiation of "range extension + large-space SUV + fridge/TV/sofa-on-wheels" was once a moat, but all three dimensions are being closed — range extension is no longer scarce (AITO and Xiaomi have followed suit), the family-SUV segment is under siege (AITO M6/M7/M9 target it precisely), and configuration differentiation has vanished (it's now the industry standard).

3. **The product flywheel is slowing.** The front half of the loop (product definition → product strength → user satisfaction) is still turning, but the back half (word of mouth → brand premium → repeat purchase) has broken. i6 succeeded on volume but pulled down brand positioning.

4. **The pure-EV transition is "volume up, price down."** The i6 accounted for nearly 60% of deliveries in March 2026 (about 24,000 units), proving pure-EV capability — but ASP and gross margin declined in tandem. Li Auto has been forced from "high-margin range extension" to "low-margin pure-EV red ocean."

5. **Duan Yongping's comment**: this is not a "good business." Car manufacturing is inherently a hard business; Li Auto once found a sweet niche within that hard business, but that niche is now being eaten away.

### II. Financial & valuation analysis (Buffett's perspective)

**Core findings**:

1. **The negative operating profit is the most central danger signal.** 2025 operating profit was -RMB 521 million; the RMB 1.1 billion in net profit was propped up entirely by investment income (RMB 1.919 billion) — essentially "living off savings" to maintain a profitable-looking bottom line.

2. **Cash-flow deterioration is stark.** Free cash flow swung from +RMB 44.2 billion in 2023 to -RMB 12.8 billion in 2025 — from "cash cow" to "cash burner" in two years.

3. **RMB 101.2 billion in cash reserves is the biggest placebo.** Even burning cash at RMB 12.8 billion a year, the company could theoretically sustain this for 8 years. This gives the company "the right to make mistakes," but cash is just ammunition — what matters is whether management can use it efficiently.

4. **The valuation offers no clear margin of safety.** P/S of about 1.0x is on the low side among the new forces, but profitability is deteriorating rather than improving, and future earnings predictability is extremely low. Three-scenario valuation: optimistic $14-20, neutral $9-15, pessimistic $10-12 (current price: $18.5).

5. **Buffett's 8-point checklist assessment**: simple and understandable (pass), durable competitive advantage (fail), excellent management (to be observed), stable earnings (fail), high ROE (fail), abundant free cash flow (fail), reasonable valuation (borderline), margin of safety (fail). **5 items fail.**

### III. Industry & competition analysis (Munger's perspective)

**Core findings**:

1. **Competitive position has materially deteriorated.** From new-force sales leader in 2023 to the only company with negative growth (-18.8%) in 2025, with its ranking falling to fourth. In January 2026 it was overtaken by both AITO (Huawei) and Xiaomi.

2. **Competitor threat assessment**:

| Competitor | Threat level | Core threat |
|--------|---------|---------|
| AITO / Huawei | **Extremely high** | Fully overlapping positioning + superior autonomous driving + brand pull |
| Xiaomi Motors | **High** | RMB 200,000-300,000 pure-EV SUVs + traffic/attention advantage + planning a range-extended SUV |
| BYD | **Medium-high** | Aggressive premiumization (Q1 +60%) + scale cost advantage |
| Leapmotor | **Medium** | Value pricing + scale growth (+103%) |
| XPeng | **Medium** | Leading VLA 2.0 autonomous driving + overlapping products |

3. **The range-extender moat is narrowing.** Among new-force players, the pure-EV-to-range-extender mix shifted from 49%:51% to 74%:26% — pure-EVs now dominate. Seven multinational automakers are rushing to launch range-extended vehicles in 2026; range extension is no longer Li Auto's exclusive label.

4. **The industry has entered a zero-sum contest.** With NEV penetration past 50%, the nature of competition has shifted from "taking share from gasoline cars" to "new-energy players fighting each other for the same pool."

5. **Munger-style summary**: "Li Auto is sliding from a 'good business' with clear differentiation toward an 'ordinary business' fighting it out in a red ocean."

### IV. Risk & management assessment (Li Lu's perspective)

**Core findings**:

1. **Management stability is severely lacking.** Between August 2025 and early 2026, at least 8 core executives departed, and the autonomous-driving team — seen as the core future competitive edge — has undergone something close to a full rebuild.

2. **Li Xiang is a founder with strong learning ability but unsteady judgment.** The range-extender route (a major success) versus MEGA (a serious failure) versus bringing in a Huawei-background management team (a poor fit that was ultimately let go entirely) — the frequent strategic zigzagging is unsettling.

3. **Governance is highly concentrated.** Li Xiang holds 21.9% of equity but controls 68.9% of voting power, meaning the company's fate is entirely tied to the founder's personal judgment. Wang Xing (the second-largest shareholder) has continued to reduce his stake, casting doubt on his confidence.

4. **10-year outlook**: successful transition (25% probability), shrink-to-survive (45%), displaced or acquired (30%).

5. **Li Lu's perspective comment**: Li Auto is not without a chance to turn things around, but it is currently in the most difficult stage of its strategic transition, and the level of certainty is not enough to support a large position.

## Investment thesis (bull vs. bear)

### Bull case

1. **RMB 100 billion cash shield**: RMB 101.2 billion in cash reserves, the most ample among the new forces, providing a 2-3 year window for strategic adjustment
2. **i6 validates pure-EV hit potential**: 100,000 units rolled off the line in 6.5 months, proving the team can still build a hit
3. **In-house chip breakthrough**: the Mach M100 (1,280 TOPS, 5nm automotive-grade) could rebuild a technical moat if mass production succeeds
4. **2026 as a major product year**: the new L9 has launched, the i9/i8 arrive in the second half, and the L7/L6 refresh is imminent
5. **Valuation has already pulled back sharply**: P/S of ~1.0x, P/B of ~1.7x, down over 60% from historical highs — pessimistic expectations are partly priced in
6. **Overseas market upside**: 2026 overseas expansion into the Middle East, Latin America, and Europe, with the international L9 launching in Q3
7. **$1 billion buyback program**: management backing its confidence in the company's value with real capital

### Bear case

1. **Operating profit has turned negative**: the core business isn't profitable, and the profitable-looking bottom line is sustained by investment income — earnings quality is very poor
2. **Cash flow has deteriorated across the board**: free cash flow swung from +RMB 44.2 billion to -RMB 12.8 billion, burning over RMB 10 billion in cash a year
3. **Pricing power keeps eroding**: ASP fell from RMB 330,000 to RMB 250,000, the flagship was discounted immediately at launch, and gross-margin guidance is just 15%
4. **The competitive landscape is deteriorating irreversibly**: Huawei, Xiaomi, and BYD are not going away, and the moat is being systematically eroded
5. **The management team has been substantially overhauled**: 8 core executives have departed, the autonomous-driving team is being rebuilt, and execution risk is significant
6. **The range-extender window is narrowing**: pure-EVs already account for 74% of the mix, and the structural opportunity from range extension is disappearing
7. **The founder's judgment has been unsteady**: the MEGA failure, repeated organizational upheaval, and frequent strategic zigzagging

## Summary

The essence of Li Auto's 5.18 sell-off is **a reasonable repricing of systemic fundamental deterioration**, not a short-term reaction to a news event. Taking the combined view of the four masters' investment frameworks:

Li Auto was once the best business among the new forces — differentiated range-extender technology, precise targeting of family users, the industry's highest gross margins and strongest profitability. But in 2024-2025, all of this systematically unraveled under the assault of giants like Huawei, Xiaomi, and BYD. Operating profit turning negative, cash flow deteriorating, sales declining across the entire model lineup, 8 core executives departing — these are not cyclical fluctuations, but the result of a fundamental shift in the competitive landscape.

The company's biggest trump card is its RMB 101.2 billion cash reserve and Li Xiang's ability to learn and self-correct. This means Li Auto won't disappear in the near term, and it retains a chance to turn things around. But "able to survive" and "worth investing in" are two different things. Until operating profit turns positive again and the moat is rebuilt (in-house chips / autonomous driving forming genuine differentiation), Li Auto does not meet value-investing buy criteria.

**Holding RMB 100 billion in cash while its earnings power disappears — it doesn't lack the ability to survive, but it lacks proof it can once again earn money efficiently. Until then, it's not a bet worth making.**
