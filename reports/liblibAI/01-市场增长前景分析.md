## 1. Market definition and boundaries

### 1.1 LiblibAI's market positioning

LiblibAI is not a single-dimension product but a composite spanning three layers of value:

| Layer | Market definition | LiblibAI's role | Comparable companies |
|------|---------|---------------|---------|
| Infrastructure layer | AI model community and distribution platform | Model supermarket — aggregating 500,000+ LoRA models, providing cloud-based training and inference | Civitai, Hugging Face |
| Application tool layer | AI image/video generation tools | One-stop creation platform — text-to-image, image-to-image, video generation | Midjourney, Jimeng AI |
| Solution layer | AI design Agent/SaaS | Overseas product Lovart — a full-chain Agent for designers | Canva AI, Figma AI |

**Core judgment**: LiblibAI's fundamental positioning is an **aggregation platform for AI visual content generation**, and its business model is closer to a hybrid of "app store + cloud computing" — one hand connects to model supply (the open-source ecosystem), the other to creative demand (designers, e-commerce, short video), monetizing through compute services and value-added features.

### 1.2 Position in the value chain

```

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

LiblibAI sits in the **midstream aggregation layer** of the value chain — it owns neither the underlying foundation models nor direct access to end-customer business scenarios, but it builds unique connective value through model aggregation, community network effects, and workflow tools.

### 1.3 Broad market vs. core serviceable market

- **Total addressable market (TAM)**: the global generative AI content creation market (spanning text, image, video, and audio)
- **Serviceable available market (SAM)**: the global AI visual content generation market (image + video + design)
- **Serviceable obtainable market (SOM)**: China's AI visual creation platform market + the overseas AI design Agent market

## 4. Market life-cycle assessment

### 4.1 Position on the S-curve

**Judgment: currently in the early growth stage of the S-curve, at roughly a 10-20% penetration level.**

- China's generative AI user penetration has reached 36.5%, but paid penetration remains very low
- Year-over-year growth in AIGC segment usage time of 176.7% is a typical characteristic of the growth stage
- Commercialization remains in "phase one" (2024-2027 product-deployment period)
- The AI image generation market's CAGR is about 17-30%; AI video's CAGR is about 46%

### 4.2 Historical analogies

| Analogous market | Comparable stage | Key similarities | Key differences |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------|
| Mobile internet (2010-2012) | Penetration rising from 10% to 30% | User explosion, tool-type apps leading, landscape undetermined | AI monetization path is more uncertain |
| SaaS (2012-2015) | Transition period from freemium to paid | PMF validated but scaled profitability not yet reached | AI substitution effect is stronger |
| Cloud computing (2008-2012) | AWS's early days | Pay-as-you-go, platform-layer value emerging | AI demand elasticity is greater |

### 4.3 Key inflection points

**Could accelerate growth**: video-generation quality reaching "Hollywood-grade" (expected 2026-2027); mainstream e-commerce fully adopting AI production pipelines; another order-of-magnitude drop in inference costs; the emergence of a killer Agent application

**Could slow growth**: significant regulatory tightening; foundation-model capability growth slowing; a wave of copyright litigation; Big Tech's free tools squeezing the middle-layer platforms

## 5. Forecast of market structure evolution

### 5.1 Future competitive landscape

**Judgment: this will not be winner-take-all; a "2+N" oligopoly structure will form.**

| Layer | Competitive structure | Main players | Share of value |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Foundation-model layer | 3-5 oligopolists | Stability/BFL, Midjourney/Adobe, ByteDance/Alibaba/Kuaishou | 30-40% |
| Aggregation-platform layer | 2-3 regional leaders | LiblibAI (China), Civitai (overseas), Hugging Face | 15-25% |
| Application/Agent layer | Long-tail and fragmented | Lovart, Canva AI, Figma AI, and vertical-scenario tools | 30-40% |
| Infrastructure layer | Oligopoly | Alibaba Cloud, AWS, Nvidia/Google TPU | 10-15% |

### 5.2 Evolution of midstream-layer value

**Reinforcing factors**: rising model complexity (increasing the value of aggregation/curation); community network effects; compliance barriers; demand for multimodal integration

**Squeeze risks**: Big Tech entering directly; foundation-model companies integrating upstream-downstream; design-SaaS giants disrupting from above with superior resources

**Conclusion**: the value of the midstream layer will **strengthen first and then face pressure** over the next 2-3 years. LiblibAI's key challenge is whether it can complete the transition from "model supermarket" to "irreplaceable creative infrastructure" within the window of opportunity.

---

## Overall conclusion

The AI visual content generation market that LiblibAI operates in is in the early growth stage of the S-curve, and the market's overall size is expected to expand 5-7x over the next five years. Backed by the network effects of its community of 500,000+ models and its domestic duopoly position, LiblibAI holds a definite advantage within the current window of opportunity. But the long-term value of the midstream aggregation layer hinges on whether it can upgrade from a "model supermarket" to irreplaceable creative infrastructure — this is the core variable for the investment judgment.
