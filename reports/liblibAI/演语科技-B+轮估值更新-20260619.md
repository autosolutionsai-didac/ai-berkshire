# Evoken Technology (Evoken) Post-Series-B+ Valuation Update

> Analysis date: June 19, 2026
> Trigger event: A ~$300 million Series B+ round, with post-money valuation exceeding $2 billion
> Information-richness rating: **Grade B (fairly well-informed)** — an unlisted company; core data comes from the company's own official disclosures and cross-validated across multiple financial media outlets, with no audited financial statements
> This report does not constitute investment advice.

---

## One-Sentence Conclusion

> Evoken Technology's ARR rocketed from ~$100 million to $300 million in just 6 months, with LibTV's explosive growth redefining the company's growth curve. The Series B+ valuation of $2 billion is only 6.7x current ARR — a severe discount relative to comparable companies globally. But the 3,000% growth rate is not sustainable, the competitive landscape for AI video is far from settled, and "revenue quality" and "profit margin" remain two key unanswered questions. A reasonable valuation range: **$4-6 billion** (based on year-end 2026 ARR), with upside contingent on whether LibTV can transition from an explosive-growth phase into steady-state growth.

## Key Changes from the Previous Valuation Report

The previous report (June 2/15, 2026) was based on Series B data, when combined ARR was approximately $50-60 million and the valuation was about $1 billion. **Three months later, nearly every core figure has shifted by an order of magnitude:**

| Dimension | Old (Series B) | New (Series B+) | Magnitude of Change |
|------|------------|------------|---------|
| ARR | ~$50-60 million | $300 million | **~5-6x** |
| Valuation | ~$1 billion | >$2 billion | **2x+** |
| Product lines | LiblibAI + Lovart + Xingliu | LiblibAI + LibTV + Xingliu | LibTV becomes the core engine |
| Revenue mix | Overseas Lovart-led | LibTV-led growth | Engine switch |
| Funding stage | Series B | Series B+ | Investor roster expanded significantly |

**Key finding: ARR grew 5-6x, but the valuation grew only 2x, compressing the valuation multiple from ~17-19x down to ~6.7x.** This may reflect the market's caution about growth sustainability, or it may simply be a function of funding timing (ARR had not yet reached $300 million while the Series B+ was being negotiated).

**Analysis:** LiblibAI's core value lies not in direct monetization but in providing China's largest AI creator ecosystem. Its 30 million users and 500,000 original models form a network effect that competitors cannot easily replicate in the short term. However, it should be noted that domestic ARPU is extremely low (historically about RMB 50/month), limiting direct monetization capability. It functions more as a "customer-acquisition gateway," funneling traffic to LibTV and Xingliu.

### 2. LibTV — The Current Growth Engine

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Launch date | March 2026 |
| Positioning | AI video creation platform |
| Target customers | Short-drama production teams, film/TV production companies, advertising agencies, brand clients |
| Customers served | Nearly 1,000 professional teams |
| Peak daily revenue, first month | Over $1 million |
| May revenue vs. first month | 13x+ |
| Revenue model | Usage-based pricing (inferred, for professional production use cases) |

**Analysis:** LibTV is the centerpiece of this funding round's narrative. Revenue growing 13x within two months implies a month-over-month growth rate of roughly 260%. This explosive growth stems from precisely targeting the "AI short-drama/video production" segment — a B2B use case with clear budgets and high repeat-purchase rates.

**But the key questions are:**
- Is this truly "ARR," or is it "consumption-based revenue"? Short-drama teams pay per project, which may lack the "recurring" characteristics of traditional SaaS
- First-month daily revenue exceeding $1 million → first-month monthly revenue of roughly several million to $10 million → May revenue of roughly tens of millions to over $100 million → annualized revenue of roughly several hundred million dollars. This is consistent with the overall $300 million ARR figure, suggesting **LibTV likely already contributes the majority of ARR**
- The short-drama industry itself is in an overcapacity warning period (AI short dramas accounted for 95%+ of the category in Q1 2026); a slowdown in industry growth would directly affect LibTV

### 3. Xingliu — Next-Generation Design Agent

## Key Risks (Not to Be Ignored)

### 1. Revenue Quality in Question ★★★★ (High Risk)

The company discloses figures as "ARR," but LibTV's revenue looks more like **consumption-based/project-based revenue** than a traditional SaaS subscription. Short-drama teams pay according to project needs and may not renew once a project ends. If actual repeat-purchase rates fall below expectations, the "annualized" framing of ARR would overstate true revenue capacity.

**Missing data:** monthly retention rate, net revenue retention (NRR), and customer churn rate are all undisclosed.

### 2. Sustainability of LibTV's Growth ★★★★ (High Risk)

- AI short dramas accounted for 95%+ of all short dramas released in China in Q1 2026; the short-drama industry itself is already showing "overcapacity" warning signs
- LibTV's explosive growth from zero includes a substantial one-time "industry migration" dividend — existing teams trying AI tools for the first time
- Once penetration reaches a certain level, the growth rate is bound to slow substantially
- Reference data: the AI short-drama market was ~RMB 19 billion in 2025 and is projected at ~RMB 30-52 billion in 2026, but growth has already slowed from 276% to ~60-170%

### 3. Competitive Landscape Far From Settled ★★★☆ (Medium-High Risk)

| Competitor | Threat Level | Analysis |
|--------|---------|------|
| ByteDance (Jimeng / CapCut) | Extremely high | Integrated traffic + tools + distribution; CapCut is the natural entry point for video creators |
| Kuaishou (Kling AI) | High | Already has AI video capability, deeply tied to the short-drama industry chain |
| Baidu (ERNIE ViLG) | Medium | Strong model capability but weak consumer-facing product execution |
| Meitu | Medium | Publicly listed company with a large design-tool user base |
| Runway / Pika / Kling | Medium | Overseas competition; would be a direct benchmark if Evoken expands abroad |

**Core issue:** ByteDance's Jimeng + CapCut combination has a full-chain advantage (generation → editing → distribution). Once it pushes into B2B video production, the threat to LibTV is structural.

### 4. Margins and Cash Burn ★★★ (Medium Risk)

- GPU inference costs are estimated at ~35-45% of revenue (per the previous report's estimate), with gross margins significantly below the 75%+ typical of traditional SaaS
- The company has not disclosed its profitability status; given the current pace of expansion, it is presumed to still be loss-making
- Cumulative funding is about $500 million, but GPU costs and customer-acquisition costs during this high-growth period could burn through cash quickly
- **If positive cash flow is not achieved before 2027, a Series C round may become necessary**

### 5. China Market ARPU Ceiling ★★☆ (Medium Risk)

Historical data shows domestic AI tool ARPU is only 1/5 to 1/25 of overseas levels. Even with a leading user base, if the company cannot open overseas markets or raise domestic willingness to pay, its revenue ceiling will remain far lower than comparable overseas companies.

### 6. Regulatory Risk ★★ (Medium-Low Risk)

AI-generated content in China faces multidimensional regulation covering content moderation, copyright ownership, and deepfakes. The short-drama industry itself also carries content-compliance requirements. Tightening policy could affect the industry's growth rate.

## Positive Factors

### 1. Consecutive Multi-Product PMF Validation ★★★★★

In the AI application layer, companies that can consecutively validate product-market fit across multiple products are extremely rare. Within 3 years, Evoken Technology has validated three business lines in succession — LiblibAI (community), Lovart/Xingliu (design agent), and LibTV (video production). This ability to "repeatedly succeed at building new products" is itself a moat.

### 2. Founding Team Execution ★★★★★

Chen Mian, born in 1992, became ByteDance's youngest-ever Product 4-1 (Global Head of CapCut Commercialization) at age 28; Zhang Qizhi was deeply involved in taking CapCut from 0 to 1. The team brings deep ByteDance-system experience in both product execution and commercialization for video tools.

### 3. Ecosystem Flywheel Effect ★★★★

LiblibAI's 30 million users and 500,000 models form a data flywheel: better assets and models attract more creators → more creators generate more content → this feeds asset foundations and user traffic into LibTV and Xingliu. This kind of cross-product ecosystem synergy is something single-tool companies lack.

### 4. Signaling Value of the Investor Roster ★★★★

Sequoia China, Tencent, Ant Group, Granite Asia, Shunwei — this is a "collective vote" from top-tier institutions. Tencent's participation in particular may signal distribution support from the WeChat / Channels ecosystem.

### 5. Market Timing ★★★★

AI video production is moving from the "demo showcase" stage into the "production tool" stage, and LibTV has landed squarely in this window. First-mover advantage matters especially in B2B — once embedded in a customer's workflow, switching costs rise significantly.

## Valuation Conclusion and Investor Perspective

### For Series B+ Investors

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Entry price | $2 billion valuation / $300 million ARR = 6.7x, **highly attractive** |
| Margin of safety | High. Even if growth slows sharply, an 8-10x ARR multiple would still support a $2.4-3.0 billion valuation |
| Upside | If year-end-2026 ARR reaches $600 million at a 10x multiple, valuation would be $6 billion — **a 3x return on this round** |
| Downside risk | LibTV growth collapses + intensifying competition + failure to reach profitability → valuation could fall back to $1.5-2.0 billion |

### For Potential Next-Round (Series C) Investors

If a Series C round occurs in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027:
- Expected entry valuation: $5-8 billion (depending on the ARR trajectory)
- Risk-reward is less favorable than the Series B+ round, but there is still room
- **Key metrics to watch:** LibTV's monthly retention rate, NRR, gross margin, and overseas expansion progress

### Comparison with Duan Yongping's Investment Framework

> "Buying a stock is buying a company, and buying a company is buying the discounted value of its future cash flows."

**Evoken Technology is not currently the kind of company Duan Yongping would buy:**
- It has not demonstrated an ability to make money (profit undisclosed, likely still loss-making)
- Its moat is still under construction (a community ecosystem exists but is not deep, and technical barriers are not high)
- Growth depends heavily on an industry window (the AI short-drama boom) rather than predictable recurring revenue
- The competitive landscape is unsettled (ByteDance / Kuaishou could crush it at any time)

**But it is worth continued observation:** if by 2027 LibTV can prove out its retention and margin metrics while overseas business gains traction, this company could move from "high growth, unprofitable" into a "growth + profitability" stage, at which point the valuation logic would change entirely.
