DeepSeek, with a 200-person team and a $5.6M training cost, produced a model approaching GPT-4 level — the highest per-person efficiency of any AI lab globally — triggering the "DeepSeek shock" that reshaped the valuation logic of the entire AI industry.

If DeepSeek-V4 successfully validates frontier performance on domestic chips, and completes a $20B+ standalone financing round within the next 12-18 months, that would mark High-Flyer's complete transformation from a "quant private fund" into a global "AI + quant" technology-finance company. This is an asset worth tracking closely while waiting for the best entry point.
