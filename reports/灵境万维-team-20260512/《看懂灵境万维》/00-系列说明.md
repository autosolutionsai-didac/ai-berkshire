> 4 articles, taking apart an AI comic-drama startup layer by layer to reveal its true structure.
>
> Total reading time for the series: about 40 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series positioning

LingJing Wanwei is not a company that is "widely discussed" — it's one of the startups in the emerging AI comic-drama track that **capital has embraced but that has not yet been seriously scrutinized**.

- The bull case says: the AI comic-drama track is projected at **22.0-31.4 billion yuan** in market size for 2026; LingJing raised three rounds in one year, has **20 million yuan** in monthly revenue, and its breakout hit "Doomsday Supermarket" earned **12 million yuan** in five days — first-rate execution
- The bear case says: **90% of industry companies are losing money**, the hit rate is only **0.12%**, large platforms have entered across the board, and the founder's background is opaque — this is "content gambling"

**Both sides are looking at the same company, yet reach completely different conclusions.**

What this series aims to do is **first lay out clearly "what it is," then let you judge "how to see it" for yourself** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Writing principles

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no presumption of bullish or bearish
2. **Every core judgment carries a counterargument** — letting readers weigh it themselves
3. **Unreliable data would rather be cut** — LingJing Wanwei is an unlisted company with extremely scarce financial data; this series uses only data cross-validated across multiple sources, and does not force in false precision
4. **All estimated values are labeled "estimated"** — startup information asymmetry is severe, so speculation and fact are strictly separated
5. **Does not constitute investment advice** — every episode ends with a disclaimer
