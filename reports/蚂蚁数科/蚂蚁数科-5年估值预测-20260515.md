**Key findings**:
1. Ant Digital Technologies' current core tracks (BaaS + privacy computing) together represent only a ~3-billion-yuan market, leaving limited headroom.
2. RWA tokenization is the largest incremental opportunity, but forecasts vary enormously (2 trillion to 16 trillion USD), and the actual pace of real-world adoption is highly uncertain.
3. The AI-agent and intelligent-risk-control markets are large but fiercely competitive; Ant Digital Technologies needs to establish differentiation within the financial vertical.
4. The financial-middleware market is growing steadily and serves as the revenue ballast.

### 4.2 Five growth engines

**Engine 1: AI commercialization (fastest growth, highest uncertainty)**
- Signed large-model product agreements with 30 financial institutions in H1 2025
- The Agentar platform already covers 100+ agent solutions
- Formed the Large Model Technology Innovation Department in February 2026 to coordinate ToB commercialization of Bailing
- Per-customer contract value could rise from the hundreds of thousands into the millions of yuan
- Risk: Bailing's brand recognition trails Qwen/DeepSeek; the AI talent pool has already seen two rounds of VP-level departures

**Engine 2: RWA/Web3 (biggest blue-sky potential, longest payoff horizon)**
- The plan to tokenize 60 billion yuan (8.4 billion USD) of new-energy assets — if a 2-5% platform fee is charged, potential annual revenue of 1.2-3.0 billion yuan
- The Jovay public chain plus the DT Tokenization Suite form a complete infrastructure stack
- A Hong Kong international headquarters plus a Dubai presence provide twin pivot points for overseas expansion
- But global on-chain RWA volume currently stands at only 30 billion USD, and the commercialization model remains unproven
- Risks: regulatory shifts, insufficient liquidity for tokenized assets, an unclear fee model

**Engine 3: Deepening financial-client relationships (most certain, moderate growth)**
- Already covers 100% of state-owned/joint-stock banks, with penetration expanding into regional banks
- The financial and new-energy sectors grew 100% in 2025
- Cross-selling (from middleware to risk control to AI to blockchain) raises per-customer contract value
- Risks: tightening IT budgets at financial institutions, competition from major tech firms

**Engine 4: Overseas expansion (twin engines of ZOLOZ and RWA)**
- ZOLOZ covers 14 countries and 740 million users, with clients including leading wallets such as GCash, TNG Digital, Dana, and TrueMoney
- The global eKYC market has an 18% CAGR, growing from 13.7 billion USD to 63 billion USD (by 2033)
- Overseas revenue grew 300% YoY in 2022-2023 (though off a small base)
- Risks: geopolitics, local competition

**Engine 5: Privacy computing + the data-element economy (policy-driven)**
- Ranked No. 1 in China's privacy-computing market for three consecutive years (35.3% share)
- Market-oriented reform of the "data element" economy continues to advance, with privacy-preserving computing platforms being built in 10 cities including Hangzhou
- Risks: the track's growth rate may fall short of expectations, and the transition from project-based to SaaS business models is slow

4. Hundsun Technologies' 8.3x PS is a reasonable anchor for a mature financial-IT company.
5. Ant Digital Technologies' fair PS multiple should sit **between Hundsun's 8x and Qulian's 30x**, depending on the credibility of its AI + RWA growth story.

### 8.3 The biggest unknowns

1. **Related-party transaction share** (information gap): This is the single largest drag on valuation confidence. If related-party transactions exceed 50%, it implies that Ant Digital Technologies' true independent growth capability is overstated, and all valuations would need a 20-30% discount.

2. **Validation of the RWA business model** (business gap): The fee structure for the 60-billion-yuan asset-tokenization plan is unclear. Is it a 2-5% platform fee? A technical-service fee? A transaction commission? The revenue implications differ enormously across these models.

3. **Ownership and cost allocation of the Bailing large model** (structural gap): Ant Digital Technologies uses Ant Group's Bailing large model for AI commercialization, but Bailing belongs to Ant Group. How does Ant Digital Technologies pay for this? Is the related-party pricing fair? This directly affects Ant Digital Technologies' true profit margin.

1. **Ant Digital Technologies is a scarce asset within China's fintech infrastructure** — No. 1 in both BaaS and privacy computing, 100% coverage of state-owned banks, and SOFAStack deeply embedded in 130+ financial institutions; switching costs form a genuine moat.

### 10.3 Final judgment
