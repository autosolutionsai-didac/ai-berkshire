# "Understanding Ant Digital Technologies" Series Overview

> Four main articles that peel back Ant Digital Technologies layer by layer to reveal its true structure.
>
> Estimated reading time for the series: about 50 minutes; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series Positioning

Ant Digital Technologies is not a company that has been "widely studied" — it is one of the companies in China's tech sector that is **frequently mentioned but rarely understood**.

- The bulls say: it is the core vehicle for Ant Group's technology spillover, the No. 1 player in both blockchain and privacy computing, and one of the few players globally that combines AI + blockchain + privacy computing + regulatory licenses
- The bears say: it is a leader in a small niche, its related-party transactions are a black box, spun-off fintech subsidiaries have no precedent of a successful listing, and its AI depends on the group's Bailing large language model

**Both camps are looking at the same company, but on the question of "what species of company Ant Digital Technologies actually is," their answers are completely different**.

What this series sets out to do is **first make clear what the company actually is** — not hand you a ready-made conclusion on whether it's worth paying attention to.

## Series Writing Principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — with no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — letting readers weigh it for themselves
3. **Unreliable data is cut rather than kept** — only the most credible sources are used; no false precision is forced in
4. **Estimated figures are explicitly labeled "estimated" or "inferred"** — unverified data is never dressed up as fact
5. **Does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article

---

## How This Differs from "Understanding Pinduoduo" and "Understanding Tencent"

This is the first time the series has researched **an unlisted company**.

If you're used to the Tencent series' style of analysis, precise down to the decimal point — lower your expectations. Ant Digital Technologies has not even disclosed its revenue publicly, its core financial data has not been independently audited or verified, and the confidence level of the available information is only medium-to-low (about 55%). This series is more like **"making out shapes in the fog"** than "examining details under a magnifying glass."
