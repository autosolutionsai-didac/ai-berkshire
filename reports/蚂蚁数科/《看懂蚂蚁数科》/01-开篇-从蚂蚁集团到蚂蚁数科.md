## A Company Whose Valuation Spans 25x, From RMB 8 Billion to RMB 200 Billion

In March 2024, Ant Group completed its "split into four," breaking the entire group into four independently operated companies. Three of them — Alipay, Ant International, and OceanBase — each have a clear frame of reference within their respective tracks. Only the fourth leaves people struggling to pin down what it actually is: it's called Ant Digital Technologies.

It shows up at blockchain conferences, on AI-agent benchmark leaderboards, at Hong Kong's tokenization summit, and even at tech summits in Cannes, France. Depending on which figure you use, this company's valuation ranges from **RMB 8 billion** to **RMB 200 billion** — a span of **25x**. Ask the people around you, and you'll likely get three kinds of answers:

- "It's Ant Group's tech business, I think?"
- "The blockchain one"
- "Not really sure — is it connected to Alipay?"

None of the three answers are wrong, exactly, but none are precise either. The first thing this series sets out to do is spell out exactly what Ant Digital Technologies is.

## Where Ant Digital Technologies Came From

To understand Ant Digital Technologies, you first have to understand Ant Group's "split into four."

**In March 2024**, Ant Group completed a major restructuring, splitting the group into four independently operated segments:

| Segment | Core business | CEO |
|------|---------|-----|
| **Ant Group (the parent entity)** | Alipay + digital finance (consumer credit, wealth management, insurance, etc.) | Han Xinyi |
| **Ant International** | Cross-border payments (WorldFirst/Antom/Alipay+) | Yang Peng |
| **OceanBase** | Distributed database | Yang Bing |
| **Ant Digital Technologies** | Enterprise-grade AI + blockchain + cloud middleware + privacy computing | Zhao Wenbiao |

Each of the four companies has its own independent board, its own CEO, and operates independently — but **all four are 100% owned by Ant Group**, with no outside financial investors brought in.

**The essence of Ant Digital Technologies**: it packages the financial-grade technology capabilities (blockchain, privacy computing, risk control, middleware, AI) that Ant Group spent more than a decade honing across Alipay, MYbank, and Zhima Credit, and sells them as products to external enterprise clients.

To use an analogy: if Ant Group is a Michelin-starred restaurant, Ant Digital Technologies is the business of packaging up that restaurant's kitchen equipment, ingredient supply chain, and chef-training system, and selling them to anyone else who wants to open a restaurant.

## What Ant Digital Technologies "Is" — Three Key Facts

### Fact One: It's an Enterprise Services Company, Not a Consumer Internet Company

Ant Digital Technologies' clients are **enterprises and institutions** — banks, insurance companies, energy companies, government agencies — not ordinary consumers.

Unlike Alipay, which has **1 billion** users, it doesn't have a consumer user base — but it covers **13,000+** enterprise clients, **300+** partners, and **24** countries and regions (source: Ant Digital Technologies Dubai Summit, April 2025).

The more telling client profile: **100%** of state-owned joint-stock banks + **60%+** of regional commercial banks + hundreds of financial institutions (source: Ant Digital Technologies at the Bund Summit, September 2025).

**Counterpoint**: the figure of 13,000 clients may include a large number of low-paying or even free trial accounts. The number of genuinely high-value paying clients (annual contract value in the tens of millions of yuan or more) may only be in the dozens.

### Fact Two: It Doesn't Build Foundation Models — It Builds "Financial-Grade AI Services"

This is the point people most often get confused about.

Ant Group does have its own foundation large models — the **Bailing family** (three series: Ling/Ring/Ming, with parameter counts ranging from tens of billions to trillions). But the Bailing large models belong to **Ant Group's Bailing foundation-model team**, not to Ant Digital Technologies.

So what does Ant Digital Technologies actually do? **It layers four incremental capabilities on top of the open-source Bailing foundation**:

| Layer | What Ant Digital Technologies does | Example |
|------|----------------|------|
| Vertical fine-tuning | Trains financial reasoning models on financial-industry data | Agentar-Fin-R1 (fine-tuned on Qwen3, outperforming DeepSeek-R1 on financial benchmarks) |
| Agent platform | Provides enterprise-grade agent development tools | The Agentar platform (holds CAICT's top-level certification) |
| Efficiency optimization | Optimizes token consumption for enterprise use cases | LingDT-2.6-flash (cuts token consumption by **90%**) |
| Compliance loop | Financial-grade security + private deployment + auditing | The Bank of Ningbo's bank-wide rollout case |

**In one line**: Ant Digital Technologies isn't "a company that builds large models" — it's "a company that turns large models into products financial institutions are willing to trust."

**Counterpoint**: this "integrate + fine-tune + service" model is, at its core, highly dependent on the group's Bailing team. If Bailing falls behind in its competition with Qwen and DeepSeek, Ant Digital Technologies' AI products would be affected as well.

### Fact Three: In Terms of Scale, It's Still Just "Small Change" Within Ant Group

A set of numbers to give you a sense of scale:

| Metric | Ant Group overall | Ant Digital Technologies (estimated) | Ant Digital's share |
|------|------------|-----------------|---------|
| 2024 revenue | Approx. **RMB 135 billion** (estimate, based on Alibaba's equity-method accounting) | Approx. **RMB 5.5 billion** (estimate, based on CEO statements + IPO-prospectus baseline) | **~4%** |
| 2024 net profit | **RMB 38.3 billion** (source: Alibaba's financial report, May 2025) | Just reached break-even (source: CEO at the Bund Summit 2025) | Nearly zero |
| Valuation | Approx. **RMB 635 billion** (source: Hurun Global Unicorn List 2025) | Approx. **RMB 30-50 billion** (estimate) | **~5-8%** |

**Ant Digital Technologies accounts for only about 4% of the group's revenue.** This means that even if Ant Digital Technologies' revenue doubled, its contribution to Ant Group as a whole would still be very limited.

But looked at the other way — Ant Group's **RMB 38.3 billion** in 2024 net profit gives it ample resources to keep funding Ant Digital Technologies' R&D. The group's overall R&D spending of **RMB 23.45 billion** per year (source: ESG report) is both Ant Digital Technologies' biggest backer and its biggest dependency.

## What Ant Digital Technologies "Is Not" — Three Common Misconceptions

### Misconception One: Ant Digital Technologies = AntChain

AntChain (blockchain BaaS) is just **one of Ant Digital Technologies' five product lines**. Its complete portfolio is "ABC" — AI + Blockchain + Cloud — plus privacy computing and overseas eKYC:

```
Ant Digital Technologies
├── A: AI services (Agentar agent platform, financial reasoning models, AntShield risk control)
├── B: Blockchain services (AntChain BaaS, RWA tokenization, Jovay public chain, ZAN)
├── C: Cloud middleware (SOFAStack, mPaaS)
├── Privacy computing (MORSE)
└── Overseas (ZOLOZ eKYC identity verification)
```

Looking only at blockchain is like mistaking the elephant's trunk for the whole elephant.

### Misconception Two: Ant Digital Technologies = OceanBase

OceanBase (distributed database) is often confused with Ant Digital Technologies, but they are **parallel sister companies**, not parent and child. OceanBase became independently operated in 2024, with its own CEO (Yang Bing), its own client base (a TPC-C world record, 100 bank clients), and is independently preparing for an IPO.

**OceanBase's numbers (18.3% share of the financial database market, the TPC-C world record, etc.) do not belong to Ant Digital Technologies.** Many analyses mistakenly attribute OceanBase's achievements to Ant Digital Technologies — a serious misattribution.

### Misconception Three: Ant Digital Technologies = Ant Group's Technology Department

Ant Digital Technologies is not an internal technical-support department of the group. It has its own clients, its own revenue, and its own income statement. CEO Zhao Wenbiao said at the 2025 Bund Summit: "We broke even last year (2024)," and "this year's growth rate should reach roughly 50% or more."

It's a company **with its own independent business objectives** — it just happens to still be 100% owned by Ant Group for now, with no outside investors and no public listing.
