- At the level of business-registry disclosure, Ant Digital Technologies is currently **100%-controlled by Ant Group** (not held directly — see the pathway below), and **no employee stock-ownership limited partnership has been established in the business registry**. Employee incentives are implemented only at the parent-company level via a SER/RSU "1.4% mapping," and are **not accounting-linked** to the equity of the Ant Digital Technologies entity itself.
- After mapping the parent company's pre-2024-03-31 granted equity as "Group 93.4% / International 4.8% / Ant Digital 1.4% / OB 0.4%," the Ant Digital Technologies portion corresponds to roughly **8 billion yuan** in the Group's valuation pool (estimated at 1.4% of the Group's 571 billion yuan valuation) — but this **is not Ant Digital Technologies' own share capital**; it is an internal equity split of the Group's own equity-incentive program, and **departing employees are subject to mandatory cash buyback**, which in effect means the Group pays cash to "redeem" this portion of the incentive.
- What will actually determine Ant Digital Technologies' total share capital at IPO is a **future sequence of actions**: (1) Ant Group moving the Ant Digital Technologies equity down into the intended IPO entity; (2) bringing in outside Pre-IPO investors; (3) setting up an employee stock-ownership platform (limited partnership or a Cayman ESOP); (4) converting the whole entity into a joint-stock company; (5) issuing new shares in the IPO. Each step will reshape the share capital.
- Estimated total-share-capital range at IPO (highly dependent on structuring choices): **conservative 4.0-6.0 billion shares / neutral 8.0-12.0 billion shares / optimistic 15.0-20.0 billion shares**. This is derived by working backward from the typical per-share offer price range for Hong Kong-listed tech companies (HKD 5-15) and a valuation range (USD 5.0-10.0 billion).
- Estimated size of the employee incentive pool: following Hong Kong-listed tech-stock convention of **8-12%** of post-issuance total share capital, corresponding to an incentive value of **4.0-8.0 billion yuan** for Ant Digital Technologies (note: this is Ant Digital Technologies' own incentive scheme, a separate system from the Group's "one-split-into-four 1.4% mapping").

## 1. Clarifying the Concept: Registered Capital ≠ Total Share Capital

### 1.1 Current State of the Company's Organizational Form

**Antchain (Shanghai) Digital Technology Co., Ltd.** is currently a **limited liability company**, not a joint-stock company.
- Source: National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, Qichacha, and Qichamao business-registry data [1][2][6]
- Date established: June 2, 2017 [6]
- Current legal representative: ZHAO WENBIAO, who took over from JIANG GUOFEI GEOFF in May 2024 [1][6]
- Registered capital: 2.107 billion yuan, increased from 100 million yuan to 2.107 billion yuan (business-registry change in May 2024) [1][3][6]

### 1.2 The Accounting Difference Between "Registered Capital" and "Total Share Capital"

| Dimension | Limited liability company (current Ant Digital Technologies) | Joint-stock company (IPO entity) |
|---|---|---|
| Capital measurement unit | "Registered capital" (yuan) | "Share capital" (shares × par value per share) |
| Form of contribution | Cash / assets in kind / intellectual property | Converted into shares |
| Transfer rules | Requires 2/3 shareholder-meeting approval + right of first refusal | Freely transferable (outside lock-up period) |
| Shareholder cap | ≤50 people | No cap |
| Governing law | PRC Company Law, Chapter 2, Section 2 | PRC Company Law, Chapter 2, Section 3 and Chapter 5 |

**Conclusion**: As of today (2026-04-28), discussing "Ant Digital Technologies' total share capital of X shares" is a **false premise**. The correct statement is "registered capital of 2.107 billion yuan." The concept of "total share capital" **only exists once the company has converted to a joint-stock company**.

### 1.3 The Evolution of Share Capital from "Limited Company → Joint-Stock Company → IPO"

Per A-share/H-share convention, an IPO typically involves three actions:

1. **Overall conversion**: converting the limited company's net assets into shares of a joint-stock company. The common conversion ratio is 1 yuan of registered capital to 1 share or 0.5 shares, but tech companies commonly convert net assets at a ratio of 1:N, with N chosen by the company (often 1 yuan of registered capital converted into 3-10 shares)
2. **Pre-IPO financing**: bringing in outside investors, usually via a new share issuance or transfer of existing shares
3. **IPO new-share issuance**: the Hong Kong minimum public float is 25% (companies with a market cap ≥HKD 10 billion may apply to reduce this to 15%), meaning the IPO stage adds 10-25% of total share capital

**Reference examples**:
- **Hundsun Technologies**: A-share total share capital of 2.007 billion shares (2024 annual report), registered capital of approximately 1.897 billion yuan (1 yuan of registered capital corresponds to slightly more than 1 share)
- **Kingdee International** (H-share 0268): total share capital of approximately 3.5 billion shares
- **Ping An OneConnect**: issued approximately 1.15 billion shares at H-share IPO
- **JD Digits**: once disclosed a draft A-share STAR Market prospectus, with post-issuance total share capital of approximately 3.0 billion shares (per the draft prospectus, which was later withdrawn)

### 2.2 Inferring the Internal Meaning of the 2.107 Billion Yuan

Three interpretations, each with supporting evidence:

**Interpretation A: Pure capital replenishment** (relatively high probability)
- The full 2.107 billion yuan comes from Ant Group's internal funds
- Purpose: to meet minimum capital requirements for fintech businesses (blockchain, stablecoin, and cross-border RWA businesses face regulatory capital-base floors)
- **Unrelated** to employee incentives

**Interpretation B: Includes a reserve for the employee incentive pool** (moderate probability)
- A portion (e.g., 10-20%, or 200-400 million yuan) is reserved as "unissued share capital" for a future employee stock-ownership platform
- To be converted into shares once vesting conditions are triggered
- But as of April 2026, **no employee stock-ownership platform has appeared in the business registry**; if such a reserve exists, it remains in an "untriggered, unconverted" state

**Interpretation C: Includes an undisclosed small Pre-IPO investment** (lower probability)
- Media reports use the phrase "strategic investment," leaving room for this interpretation
- But if there were an outside investor, the business registry would normally show it
- Most likely not the case

**Most defensible conclusion**: the 2.107 billion yuan of registered capital is currently **simply 100% contributed by Ant Group**, and employee incentives are **not reflected at the level of the entity's own share capital**.

## 3. Reverse-Engineering the Accounting Meaning of the Parent Company's "One-Split-Into-Four, 1.4% Mapping"

### 3.1 Estimating the Group's Total Unit Count

**Known facts**:
- Ant Group's July 2023 buyback plan valued the company at 567.1 billion yuan [10]
- Pre-split SER pre-tax price of 115.85 yuan per unit [4]
- Implied total number of Group SER units ≈ 567.1 billion ÷ 115.85 yuan ≈ **approximately 4.895 billion units** (estimate)

**Cross-verification**:
- Ant Group's 2020 A-share prospectus disclosed post-issuance total share capital of **no less than 30.039 billion shares** [9]
- SER is measured as an "economic interest right"; **1 SER unit ≠ 1 share** — the two are converted via a valuation model
- The prospectus disclosed that the employee stock-ownership platforms (Junhan and Junao) together hold a post-issuance stake of ≥40%, corresponding to roughly 12 billion shares
- Assuming the SER units granted to employees within the incentive pool amount to roughly 4.0-5.0 billion units (one SER unit typically corresponds to 1-3 shares of economic value), this is consistent in order of magnitude with the roughly 4.9 billion unit estimate above

### 3.2 Three Possible Interpretations of Ant Digital Technologies' 1.4% Mapping

**Interpretation A: Group valuation of 571 billion yuan × 1.4% = 8 billion yuan (Ant Digital Technologies' implied valuation basis)**
- This treats Ant Digital Technologies as one asset of the Group, allocated at "accounting fair value"
- 8 billion yuan corresponds to roughly 1x P/S on Ant Digital Technologies' **6-8 billion yuan of estimated third-party revenue**, a relatively low valuation
- This is the **simplest and most common interpretation**

**Interpretation B: 1.4% is "the conversion ratio to be given to legacy employees at future IPO"**
- Meaning: when Ant Digital Technologies IPOs, the 1.4% × Ant Digital Technologies' IPO-time valuation in equity is "compensated" to legacy employees who were granted incentives before March 31, 2024
- This interpretation **has already been disproven**: per Sina Finance's reporting [4], departing employees' claim on this 1.4% is subject to **mandatory cash buyback**, not equity — that is, the Group pays cash to lock in this benefit, **it is not tied to Ant Digital Technologies' IPO**
- Whether the "mapping" portion for still-employed staff moves in lockstep with an Ant Digital Technologies IPO is not made explicit in the reporting, though it does mention that this will "mainly be determined by how Ant Digital Technologies develops after becoming independent," leaving room for ambiguity

**Interpretation C: 1.4% is the share of Ant Digital Technologies' own share capital already allocated to the employee incentive pool (a misreading)**
- This is an **incorrect interpretation**. The denominator of the 1.4% is the Group's equity-incentive instruments (SER + RSU), not Ant Digital Technologies' own total share capital
- Ant Digital Technologies' actual "own incentive pool" is a separate system, whose size has not been disclosed through public channels

### 3.3 The Real Implications for IPO Valuation

**Key judgment**:
1. The 1.4% mapping is fundamentally an **internal accounting asset split within the Group**, and is **not tightly bound** to Ant Digital Technologies' actual share-capital structure at IPO
2. Departing employees have already been cash-redeemed; the **future upside for Ant Digital Technologies' IPO lies only in**:
   - The portion of SER/RSU mapping held by currently employed staff (how this is compensated remains a variable if Ant Digital Technologies IPOs in the future)
   - A **new incentive system launched independently by Ant Digital Technologies itself** (new grants to current employees)
3. This means that at Ant Digital Technologies' IPO, **legacy employees' 1.4% is essentially unrelated to the company's share-capital table**, but the share of new incentive grants to current employees will become a new variable

## 5. Estimating the Size of the Employee Incentive Pool

### 5.1 Common Ranges for Incentive Pools at Hong Kong-Listed Tech Companies

Per public regulatory rules and materials compiled by firms such as Deheng Law Offices [11]:
- A-share listed companies: cumulative incentives capped at 10% of total share capital (a hard regulatory constraint)
- Non-state-owned Hong Kong-listed companies: no hard ratio limit, but market convention caps it at 15%
- State-owned tech enterprises: large enterprises ≤5%, medium enterprises ≤10%, small/micro enterprises ≤30%

Ant Digital Technologies is not a state-owned enterprise; following Hong Kong-listed tech-stock convention, its **incentive pool is estimated at 5-15%**, with a median of roughly 10%.

### 5.2 Reverse-Engineering the Size of Ant Digital Technologies' Incentive Pool

**Valuation-anchored method**:

| Valuation basis | Total valuation (estimate) | Incentive pool share | Incentive pool value (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee-equity basis | 8 billion yuan | 10% | 800 million yuan |
| Parent-company reverse-engineered basis | 15 billion yuan | 10% | 1.5 billion yuan |
| Neutral market-based valuation | 50 billion yuan | 10% | 5 billion yuan |
| Optimistic market-based valuation | 80 billion yuan | 10% | 8 billion yuan |

**Per-capita incentive reverse-engineering** (referencing Ant Group's own figures):
- Ant Group's 2020 prospectus disclosed an incentive scheme of approximately 137.7 billion yuan for approximately 16,000 employees, or roughly 8.6 million yuan per person [12]
- Ant Digital Technologies is estimated to have 3,000-5,000 employees (per existing estimates) [derived from the parent company's 2024 ESG report figure of 36,559 employees]
- Assuming a per-capita incentive value of 1.5-3.0 million yuan (priced lower than the mature parent Group, reflecting Ant Digital Technologies' start-up stage), the total comes to **4.5-15.0 billion yuan**
- This order of magnitude is broadly consistent with the "10% of total valuation" reverse-engineering above

**Conclusion**: Ant Digital Technologies' employee incentive pool at IPO is estimated to have a fair value of **4.0-8.0 billion yuan** (neutral case), corresponding to a **8-12% share of total share capital**.

## 6. Core Uncertainties

Key data that cannot be found in public sources are listed explicitly below (these are the pieces of the jigsaw puzzle needed to determine Ant Digital Technologies' true share-capital structure):

1. **The place of registration and the full ownership chain of Antchain International Company Limited**
   - Presumed to be in the BVI or Cayman Islands, but business-registry materials do not fully disclose this
   - If the intermediate chain from Ant Group → Antchain International → Antchain (Shanghai) Digital Technology includes an employee stock-ownership platform or outside minority shareholders, this piece cannot identify it

2. **The specific details of the 2.107 billion yuan capital increase**
   - Was the capital increase a single injection or made in installments?
   - Was the contribution in cash, in technology valued as a contribution, or a combination?
   - Has an employee stock-ownership platform already contributed capital without being fully reflected in the business registry?

3. **The specific form of Ant Digital Technologies' own equity-incentive instruments**
   - Are they options, RSUs, restricted shares, or phantom equity?
   - The exercise price, vesting cadence, and total pool size are all undisclosed
   - The amount granted, the amount vested, and the amount not yet granted are all undisclosed

4. **The final structure of the intended IPO entity**
   - Will it be H-shares (the domestic joint-stock company issuing H-shares directly) or a red-chip structure (an offshore company holding the domestic operating entity)?
   - The two have different implications for the share-capital structure: H-shares retain the domestic entity's share capital, while a red-chip structure requires rebuilding an offshore holding layer
   - Whether a VIE structure is unwound and whether the stablecoin business is spun off into a subsidiary would have a major impact

5. **Whether the Pre-IPO round will bring in outside investors, and at what valuation**
   - By analogy to OceanBase, it could bring in 15-30 institutional shareholders, but the specific list and valuation are unknown
   - Bringing in outside investors would significantly change the share-capital table and would anchor the IPO valuation

6. **How the Group's legacy-employee 1.4% equity-incentive portion is ultimately settled at Ant Digital Technologies' IPO**
   - Departing employees have already been cash-redeemed in full (clear)
   - Will the "mapping" portion for currently employed staff be converted into Ant Digital Technologies shares at Ant Digital Technologies' IPO, and at what ratio? This is highly sensitive and undisclosed information

7. **The overall share-conversion ratio**
   - How many shares does 1 yuan of registered capital convert into? This directly determines the absolute size of total share capital at IPO
   - There is no mandatory rule in Hong Kong convention; the company can choose freely, but this will affect the per-share offer price and liquidity

8. **Hong Kong IPO public float requirements**
   - The standard minimum is 25%, which can be reduced to 15% for companies with a market cap ≥HKD 10 billion
   - If Ant Digital Technologies is valued at USD 6.0-8.0 billion (approximately 50.0-65.0 billion yuan, well above HKD 10 billion), it could apply for the 15% threshold
   - This directly determines the number of shares issued in the IPO tranche
