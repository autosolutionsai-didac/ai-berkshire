### How Might Buffett View This?

> "I would rather be approximately right than precisely wrong." Ant Digital Technologies' "approximate rightness" is that it holds the #1 position in several sub-markets, sits behind the Ant ecosystem, and benefits from two major trends — AI and domestic substitution. But the "precise risk" is that Chinese B2B tech companies as a group struggle to turn a profit overall, and the valuation case depends heavily on an eventual IPO to be realized.
>
> Buffett would most likely say: "I can't figure out what this company's earnings power will actually look like in five years, so I'll pass."
