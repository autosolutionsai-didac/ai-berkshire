## I. Management assessment

### 1.1 Core management

**Ant Digital Technologies CEO: Zhao Wenbiao**
- Holds dual doctoral degrees from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Rutgers University, with 120+ technology patents
- Formerly President of Ant Group's Big Security Business Group; became Ant Digital Technologies' first CEO in March 2024
- Core strength lies in security technology and risk control; has proposed the "ABC" strategy
- **Assessment**: Technically grounded and product-savvy, but lacks a public track record of independently running a To-B business over the long term. This looks like an internal rotational appointment within Ant.

**Ant Digital Technologies Chairman: Ni Xingjun**
- Senior Vice President of Ant Group and Chair of its Technology Strategy Committee; former CTO of Ant
- Also serves as Chairman of OceanBase
- **Assessment**: A veteran figure, but his attention is split between Ant Digital Technologies and OceanBase.

**Former head: Jiang Guofei (departed)**
- Former President of Ant Digital Technologies, later joined China Electronics Corporation as Chief Scientist
- Laid the technical foundation for Ant's blockchain business
- **Assessment**: The departure of a founding-era leader is a signal of core talent-retention risk.

**Ant Group Chairman: Jing Xiandong (Eric Jing)**
- Champions "collective navigation" over "single-helmsman" leadership; management team skewing younger
- In 2023, proposed the three strategic pillars of "AI First, Alipay's dual-flywheel, and accelerated globalization"
- Key decisions: open-sourcing OceanBase (2021, correct call), spinning off Ant Digital Technologies (2024, right direction)
- **Limitation**: His political sensitivity was questioned during the 2020 IPO suspension incident

**Ant Group CEO: Han Xinyi** (took over in March 2025)
- Liu Zheng (formerly Cainiao's CFO) joined as Ant's CFO, which the market has read as a signal of IPO preparation

## II. Regulatory risk

### 2.1 Ant Group's rectification status
- **Completed**: Paid a 7.123 billion yuan fine in July 2023; special rectification is complete, and the company has entered a state of routine, normalized regulation
- **Not yet completed**: Its financial holding company license application is still pending; the restructuring plan for its personal credit-reporting business is still being adjusted
- **Impact on Ant Digital Technologies**: Since operating independently, its direct link to Ant Group's financial rectification has weakened, but brand perception and resource allocation are still indirectly affected
- **Impact level: Medium | Probability: partially realized already, medium remaining**

### 2.2 Data Security Law / Personal Information Protection Law
- The Regulations on the Administration of Network Data Security took effect January 1, 2025, imposing stricter requirements on platforms with 10 million+ monthly active users
- Ant Digital Technologies' security-technology products handle large volumes of personal identity-verification data, making it a regulatory focus area
- **Impact level: Medium-high | Probability: high (already being enforced)**

### 2.3 Domestic-substitution ("Xinchuang") policy (positive impact)
- Document No. 79 requires 100% domestic-substitution for state-owned enterprises' IT systems by 2027
- OceanBase, as a leading domestic distributed database, stands to benefit directly
- However, its private-enterprise background may face restrictions in some government scenarios
- **Net impact: Positive | Certainty: medium-high**

## IV. Business risk

### 4.1 Dependence on related-party transactions (the biggest risk)
- Ant Digital Technologies was spun out of Ant Group, and its share of internal clients is estimated to have been extremely high in its early days
- The revenue split between internal and external clients has not been disclosed — this is the key metric for judging independent commercialization capability
- If Ant Group's internal contribution exceeds 50% of revenue, the logic behind an independent valuation becomes questionable
- **Impact level: High | Probability: high (a relatively high share of related-party transactions almost certainly exists)**

### 4.2 Technology-path risk

**Can blockchain achieve large-scale commercial use?**
- The BaaS market is only 1.89 billion yuan — an extremely small scale
- RWA is the newest direction (charging-station RWA at 100 million yuan, Brazilian solar green-certificate RWA at 220 million Brazilian reais), but the scale remains small
- Honest assessment: product-market fit for enterprise-grade blockchain applications is still being tested
- **Impact level: High | Probability: medium**

**Can the AI business sustain growth?**
- A new "Large Model Technology Innovation Department" has been established, launching Agentar
- Competition in enterprise AI is extremely intense (Baidu, Alibaba, Huawei, and ByteDance are all in the game)
- **Impact level: Medium-high | Probability: medium**

### 4.3 International-expansion risk
- Overseas revenue grew 300%, but off a small base; main markets are Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Brazil
- Geopolitical risk, localization competition, and compliance costs
- **Impact level: Medium | Probability: medium**

## V. Governance-structure risk

### 5.1 Ownership structure
- In January 2023, Jack Ma relinquished actual control, with his voting rights falling from 53.46% to 6.2%
- 10 individual shareholders now independently exercise their voting rights separately
- Positive: eliminates the political risk of "single-helmsman" control; negative: may weaken the efficiency of strategic decision-making

### 5.2 Independent listing plans
- Ant International is already widely reported to be preparing for a Hong Kong listing
- Ant Digital Technologies' CEO said in September 2024 that there is "no specific timetable"
- OceanBase completed a funding round in 2024 with new shareholders significantly overlapping with Ant Group's, which is read as a pre-IPO signal
- **Assessment**: Ant Digital Technologies' listing is most likely to happen in 2027-2028 at the earliest

### 5.3 Related-party transactions and conflicts of interest
- Extensive technical and business collaboration exists among Ant Digital Technologies, Ant International, and OceanBase
- The fairness of transfer pricing and profit allocation will need careful handling ahead of any listing
- **Impact level: Medium-high | Structurally present**

## VI. Long-term certainty analysis (Li Lu's lens)

### What will Ant Digital Technologies look like ten years from now?

| Scenario | Probability | Description |
|------|------|------|
| Optimistic | 25% | Becomes China's leading enterprise digital-technology platform, annual revenue of 30-50 billion yuan, successfully listed, 30%+ overseas |
| Base case | 40% | A mid-sized technology service provider, annual revenue of 15-25 billion yuan, unable to break through the major platforms' dominance in AI and cloud services |
| Pessimistic | 25% | Blockchain commercialization falls short of expectations, AI is marginalized, and the company becomes little more than Ant Group's internal IT provider |
| Extreme negative | 10% | A sudden regulatory-policy shift, or a major risk event at the Ant Group level |

### What could disrupt its business model?

1. **AI-native applications reshaping enterprise services**: AI agents directly replacing traditional SaaS/PaaS
2. **Public-chain ecosystems maturing**: enterprises may no longer need consortium-chain/BaaS services
3. **Big-tech dimensional strikes**: Huawei and Alibaba Cloud bundling sales on the strength of stronger ecosystems
4. **A regulatory black swan**: a fresh round of regulatory action targeting Ant Group

### Is it moving in the correct direction of "civilizational evolution"?

**Partly yes, partly uncertain.**

Correct direction: digital transformation is irreversible; enterprise AI-enablement is a certain direction; domestic-substitution ("Xinchuang") represents a structural opportunity.

Uncertain direction: whether blockchain is a "necessity" or merely "icing on the cake" in enterprise applications remains undecided; the regulatory outlook for RWA/stablecoins in China is unclear.

## Overall conclusion

**Ant Digital Technologies currently does not meet the "certainty" bar required by Li Lu's investment framework.**

1. **Not sufficiently understood** (Grade-C information): unlisted, no public financial statements, core operating data remains opaque
2. **Not sufficiently certain**: the commercial prospects of blockchain are uncertain, the AI competitive landscape is uncertain, and the degree of dependence on related-party transactions is uncertain
3. **Management is capable but not exceptional**: strong technical capability, but lacking a proven track record of independently running a To-B business over the long term

**Recommendation**: Keep tracking, but don't rush to place a bet. Key indicators to watch:
- Changes in the share of revenue from external clients
- Whether overseas revenue share reaches 20%+
- Whether the RWA/stablecoin business can break through the 1 billion yuan scale
- Whether an IPO process begins (at which point a prospectus would disclose detailed data)

**Combined risk and management score: ★★★☆☆**
