## Top 5 Detailed Assessment

### 1. Tencent (700.HK) — Confirmed

**10-year certainty: Very high**

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Moat direction | Widening — WeChat's social graph of 1.43 billion users is irreplaceable; AI is more likely to reinforce WeChat than to replace it |
| Management | Capital allocation has shifted from "poor" to "excellent" — annual buybacks of HK$80 billion, exiting the JD.com and Meituan stakes, and focusing on AI |
| Biggest risk | The succession question around Pony Ma (age 55, will be 65 in 10 years); the structural discount on China ADRs |
| 10-year return | Bull case 22% / base case 14-15% / bear case 6-7%, probability-weighted ~13% |

### 2. Mastercard (MA) — Confirmed

**10-year certainty: Very high**

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Moat direction | Widening — a dual engine of global payment network effects and value-added services (41% of revenue, growing 23%) |
| Biggest risk resolved | The CBDC threat has been disproven (China has abandoned the digital-cash route); UPI mainly affects domestic small-value payments, leaving the cross-border moat intact |
| Antitrust | The $38 billion settlement has received preliminary approval; the fee restrictions are manageable and are offset by value-added services |
| 10-year return | Bull case 14.7% / base case 10.4% / bear case 4.2%, probability-weighted ~10% |

### 3. Moutai (600519) — Confirmed (conditional)

**10-year certainty: High**

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Moat direction | Stable — cultural monopoly plus pricing power; the Feitian wholesale price (RMB 1,640) still exceeds the retail price (RMB 1,539) |
| Consumption-tax risk | Reform would cut profit by 7-17%, but Moutai can pass part of this on to consumers |
| Management | Four leadership changes in five years is a common ailment of state-owned enterprises, but the fact that "anyone can run it well" precisely shows the moat lies in the product, not the management |
| 10-year return | Bull case 9.7% / neutral case 5.4% / bear case 1.1%. **Conditional: wait for PE to fall back below 17x before buying** |

### 4. Progressive (PGR) — Confirmed (conditional)

**10-year certainty: High**

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Moat direction | Stable to widening — a data-driven pricing flywheel, 40% ROE, and 40 years of underwriting discipline |
| Autonomous driving | Within 10 years, penetration will reach at most 20-30%, shrinking the market by 10-15%; Progressive has time to adapt |
| Cyclicality | The current 87% loss ratio is a product of the hard market; once it normalizes back to 92-94%, EPS could fall from 19 to 14-16 |
| 10-year return | Bull case 15% / neutral case 6.7% / bear case 0.5%, probability-weighted ~7%. **Conditional: monitor whether the loss ratio exceeds 96% for consecutive periods** |

### 5. Booking (BKNG) — Reinstated (replacing NetEase)

**10-year certainty: Medium-high**

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Moat direction | Stable — supply-side aggregation of 28 million listings plus global payments-compliance infrastructure, which AI cannot replicate in the short term |
| AI threat | Overpriced by the market — Booking's moat lies on the supply side (hotel integration), not the demand side (search) |
| Management | Highly disciplined — steady buybacks, no ill-advised acquisitions, and steadily improving margins |
| 10-year return | The 21x PE is a historic low (10-year median of 31.6x); base case is 12-15% annualized |

## Common Traits of the Top 5

1. **The moat widens over time rather than narrows** — the social graph (Tencent), the payment network (MA), the cultural monopoly (Moutai), the data flywheel (PGR), supply-side aggregation (BKNG)
2. **The bear case doesn't lose money** — under the worst assumptions, every one of them still delivers a positive 10-year return
3. **Not dependent on a single technology, so not vulnerable to disruption** — the moats come from user habits, culture, or institutional lock-in
4. **Management can win "without being especially brilliant"** — the business itself is good enough that it doesn't need a genius CEO to sustain it
5. **Geographic diversification** — 2 Chinese companies plus 3 US companies, a natural hedge against geopolitical risk
