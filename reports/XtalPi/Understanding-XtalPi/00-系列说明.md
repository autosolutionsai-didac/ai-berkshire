>
> The series takes about 50 minutes to read; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series positioning

XtalPi is **one of the most controversial AI-concept stocks in the Hong Kong market**.

- The bulls say: AI transforming drug discovery is a civilization-level trend, XtalPi is a globally leading AI + robotics drug discovery platform, and the DoveTree **$6 billion** mega-deal plus Eli Lilly's **$345 million** renewal prove the platform's value
- The bears say: PS is **~40x**, the company raised **HK$6 billion** through three share placements in under two years since listing, **45%** of revenue depends on a single customer, and no AI-designed drug has ever successfully reached market globally

**Both camps are looking at the same company but reaching opposite conclusions**. The core disagreement isn't about the data — it's about judging what stage AI drug discovery is actually at.

What this series aims to do is **lay out the facts clearly and explain the risks plainly** — so you can judge for yourself whether this company is worth following.

## Writing principles

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **No fabricated data** — much of the AI drug discovery industry's data is not publicly disclosed; this series uses only credible sources, and all estimates are labeled "estimate"
4. **No stock price predictions** — a framework is provided, not an answer
5. **Not investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article
