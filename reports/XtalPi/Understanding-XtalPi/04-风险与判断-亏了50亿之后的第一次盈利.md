Every risk below carries a probability and impact assessment — the probability itself is a subjective judgment, for reference only.

### Which Risks Deserve the Most Attention

If you can only watch three:

**First is DoveTree.** This partnership contributed nearly half of FY2025 revenue. Whether subsequent milestone payments arrive on schedule will directly determine the trajectory of 2026-2027 results.

**Second is dilution.** With 3 share placements plus 1 convertible bond issuance, if the convertible bond fully converts (conversion price HK$13.85), total share count will increase by approximately **207 million shares** (4.81%). While the company still needs substantial capital, dilution may well continue.

**Third is industry validation.** The global AI drug discovery industry needs a "moonshot moment" — the first AI-designed drug to successfully launch. Until that moment arrives, the entire sector rests on faith. The most optimistic forecast has a drug entering regulatory approval around 2027 (such as Generate Biomedicines' GB-0895), but uncertainty remains high.

## Profit Sustainability: The Most Critical Question

In 2025, XtalPi's adjusted net profit was **258 million yuan** — the company's first profit in its 10-year history.

How much substance does this number really have? Breaking it down:

| Revenue component | Amount (RMB millions) | Nature |
|---------|:--:|------|
| DoveTree upfront payment | ~360 | **One-time**, not repeatable |
| Drug discovery (excluding DoveTree) | ~180 (estimate) | Recurring, up roughly +74% YoY |
| AI4S Smart Solutions | 265 | Recurring, +63% YoY |
| **Total** | **803** | — |

If we strip out the one-time DoveTree upfront payment of 360 million yuan, recurring revenue is approximately **440 million yuan**, up roughly **65%** YoY — the growth rate is still solid, but based on this estimate, **whether the company can be profitable excluding the large order remains uncertain**.

The reason: FY2024 recurring revenue was approximately 266 million yuan, with a loss of 457 million yuan. FY2025 recurring revenue was approximately 440 million yuan, an increase of 170 million yuan. Given the rigidity of operating expenses (R&D headcount, robot maintenance, etc.), the 170 million yuan of incremental revenue may just barely cover costs — or may not.

**Core conclusion**: FY2025's profitability is most likely the combined result of "large-order boost + early signs of operating leverage." If DoveTree's subsequent payments arrive on schedule and the recurring business keeps growing, profit sustainability can gradually be established. If the large order stalls, a return to losses is possible.

## Shareholder Dilution: An Account That Must Be Settled

Less than two years after listing, XtalPi's share capital has expanded aggressively:

| Time | Event | Amount raised | Dilution ratio |
|------|------|:--:|:--:|
| June 2024 | IPO | — | Baseline |
| January 2025 | First share placement | ~HK$2 billion | ~7% |
| April 2025 | Second share placement | ~HK$2 billion | ~6% |

| January 2026 | Zero-coupon convertible bond | HK$2.866 billion | +4.81% if converted |
| **Cumulative** | — | **~HK$9 billion** | **~18-23% (if the convertible bond converts)** |

**Data sources**: company announcements, Sina Finance, Eastmoney

This means: if you bought at IPO and have held through today, your equity stake has already been diluted by roughly **one-fifth**.

**To be fair**: the company used this capital to build up its cash reserves (currently **7 billion yuan+**), providing ammunition for future development. At a stage when the company still needs heavy investment, raising capital is not in itself "wrong" — but every raise dilutes per-share value for shareholders.

**A warning sign to watch for**: if the company continues large-scale fundraising while the share price is depressed (say, placing shares below HK$8), that would indicate management is safeguarding company operations at the expense of existing shareholders' interests.

Counter-argument: frequent fundraising suggests the company is still at a stage that needs transfusions of capital, which is itself a risk. A truly good business model should be able to self-fund. XtalPi's FY2025 free cash flow situation needs more data to verify.

## Valuation: Is a 40x P/S Really Expensive?

### Peer Comparison

| Company | Market cap | Revenue (TTM) | P/S | Profit status | Gross margin |
|------|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| XtalPi | ~$4.5 billion | ~$110 million | **~40x** | First profit | ~70% |

| Recursion | ~$1.9 billion | Minimal | — | Loss-making | — |
| WuXi AppTec | ~$30 billion | ~$5.5 billion | **~5x** | Profitable | ~38% |

**Data sources**: Yahoo Finance, StockAnalysis (May 2026)

A straightforward question: **XtalPi's revenue is only half of Schrodinger's, but its market cap is 4.6x higher. Why?**

Possible explanations:
1. XtalPi is the only profitable listed AI pharma company
2. The DoveTree mega-deal is being read by the market as "platform value validation"
3. Hong Kong equities value AI-concept stocks under a different valuation framework than US equities
4. XtalPi's "physics + AI + robotics" narrative has more imaginative appeal than a pure-software company

**But another interpretation**: XtalPi's 40x P/S is itself bubble pricing. Schrodinger has 20 years of accumulated computational-chemistry expertise, revenue more than double XtalPi's, yet trades at only 3.5x P/S. Applying the same standard to value XtalPi, a reasonable P/S would be in the 5-15x range — corresponding to a share price of **HK$1-3**.

Both interpretations have merit. **The key is which one you believe**:

- If you believe XtalPi is "the next AWS-level drug R&D platform," 40x P/S isn't expensive
- If you believe XtalPi is just "a small CRO company with $100 million in revenue," 40x P/S is absurd

### Reverse DCF: What Does the Current Share Price Imply?

At the current market cap of approximately HK$35.3 billion, after subtracting net cash of approximately HK$7.3 billion, enterprise value is approximately **HK$28 billion**.

Assuming a 25x P/E in 10 years and a 12% discount rate:

> Net profit required in 10 years = HK$28 billion / 25 x (1.12^10) = HK$28 billion / 25 x 3.11 = **approximately HK$3.5 billion (approximately RMB 3.2 billion)**

This means: growing from FY2025's **258 million yuan** adjusted net profit to **3.2 billion yuan** — a compound annual growth rate of roughly **29%**, sustained for 10 years.

**This growth expectation is not theoretically impossible**, but it requires:
- DoveTree and other large deals continuing to deliver on milestone payments
- New large customer partnerships of a similar scale
- AI4S successfully breaking into new industries
- No major competitive setbacks
- No further large-scale shareholder dilution

Whether all these conditions can be met simultaneously — you'll have to judge for yourself.

## Comparison with Historical Analog Companies

| Analog | Similarities | Outcome | Implications for XtalPi |
|------|--------|------|------------|
| **Illumina (gene sequencing)** | Technology platform + heavy upfront investment + long-term payoff | Became the industry's dominant player after 15 years | If AI pharma works out, XtalPi could become a platform-level company |
| **Palantir (data platform)** | Technology platform + government mega-clients + long-term losses | Crashed 80% post-IPO, later rebounded on the AI narrative | Volatility is the norm; the outcome ultimately depends on whether the product can keep landing large customers |
| **Schrodinger (computational chemistry)** | AI drug discovery + high valuation | Share price fell 83%, from a high of $100 to $17 | The "high valuation → low valuation" path for AI pharma already has a precedent |
| **BGI Genomics (genomics)** | Scientist-founded + platform concept + Chinese company | Fell from 300 yuan to 40 yuan | The gap between concept and reality can be large |

**The most instructive analog is Illumina.** Founded in 1998, listed in 2000, it barely made money for its first 10 years, but kept building technological moats and eventually became the absolute dominant player in gene sequencing in the 2010s, with a market cap that once exceeded $70 billion.

If the AI pharma sector's evolution follows a path similar to gene sequencing — **direction is certain, but realization takes 10-15 years** — then the core question for evaluating XtalPi today isn't "how much money can it make this year," but "will it still be one of the most important platforms in this field 10 years from now."

Counter-argument: Illumina's success is survivorship bias. Many other genomics companies died during the same period. And gene sequencing has clear physical barriers (instruments + consumables), whereas AI pharma's moat is softer and more easily closed by competitors.
