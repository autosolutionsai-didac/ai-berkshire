## Margin of Safety Assessment

### Favorable Factors

1. **Extreme valuation compression**: P/E compressed from 40x to 9x, with a large amount of pessimism already priced in
2. **ROIC still strong**: a 30% ROIC at the current valuation implies an extremely high return on capital
3. **No debt pressure**: healthy balance sheet, no repayment risk
4. **Buyback capacity**: the company continues to repurchase stock, which is more effective when undervalued

### Unfavorable Factors

1. **Growth slowdown is a fact**: FY2026 guidance is only 2%-4% revenue growth, with EPS set to decline further
2. **Management vacuum**: the new CEO doesn't start until September, leaving a short-term lack of strategic clarity
3. **Tariff uncertainty**: the ongoing impact on margins is hard to quantify

### Conclusion Under the Buffett Framework

Applying Buffett's standard of "will this company be better or worse in 10 years than it is now":

- **Brand**: Lululemon's brand still has deep roots in the global athletic lifestyle space, and it will most likely be stronger in 10 years
- **Industry**: the long-term trend of rising athleisure penetration has high certainty
- **Current valuation**: buying a consumer company with a 30% ROIC and 58% gross margin at 9x P/E provides a fairly ample margin of safety even if growth slows

**The core judgment from the Buffett lens**: buying at the current price offers a very attractive risk-reward ratio under the neutral scenario. The key is having the patience to wait for the management transition to complete and for growth to reaccelerate. Even under the most pessimistic scenario, the downside is limited (~15%), while the upside under the optimistic scenario is enormous (170%+). This is an **asymmetric bet**.
