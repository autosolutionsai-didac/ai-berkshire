### 1.1 Chen Lei (CEO / Chairman of the Board)

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|

### 1.2 Colin Huang (Founder)

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| **Shareholding** | ~25.4%, largest shareholder |

| **Risk point** | Founder is distanced from management; lacks a "guiding figure" should a major crisis occur |

### 1.3 Management team integrity — **major concern**

**Employee treatment disputes (severe):**
- December 2020: a 22-year-old female employee died suddenly after working overtime
- Monthly working hours of 300-380 (12-15 hours per day)
- The company fired an employee for filming a colleague being carried away by ambulance
- Departments forced to choose between "996" and "11-11-6" schedules

**Merchant relations disputes (severe):**
- July 2024: over 200 Temu merchants gathered at the Guangzhou headquarters to protest
- The "refund-only" policy was subject to regulatory summons
- The platform uses extreme measures to squeeze merchant prices

**Li Lu's perspective**: Pinduoduo's extreme squeeze on employees and merchants, while improving short-term efficiency, **severely damages the long-term health of the business ecosystem**.

### 1.4 Capital allocation capability — **disappointing**

| Metric | Current status |
|------|------|
| Dividend | None paid |
| Buyback | Buyback yield of 0% |
| Cash reserves | ~$54.0 billion, extremely abundant |
| Capital return | Zero return. Sitting on massive cash but not returning it to shareholders |

## IX. Li Lu's Overall Conclusion

### Three fundamental problems

**First, management culture harbors deep-seated concerns.** Li Lu places extreme emphasis on "treating stakeholders well." Pinduoduo's extreme squeeze on employees and merchants is efficient in the short term but **severely damages the long-term health of the business ecosystem**.

**Second, the foundation of Temu's business model has been shaken.** The elimination of de minimis is not a "one-time shock" but a structural shift. The pivot to local warehousing means competing head-on with Amazon in its strongest domain.

**Third, capital allocation capability is disappointing.** $54.0 billion in cash, a PE of 10.7x, yet zero buybacks and zero dividends. Either management lacks confidence, or it lacks any sense of shareholder returns.

### Positive factors

- The domestic main platform remains first-tier
- Duoduo Grocery is approaching a near-monopoly position
- $54.0 billion in cash provides a substantial margin of safety
- The C2M model is difficult to replicate

### Final judgment

**Risk level: high risk (4.01/5.00)**

**Li Lu's likely stance**: "This is an interesting company at a very cheap price, but I'm not sure what it will look like in 10 years. When certainty is insufficient, no price is cheap enough to constitute an adequate margin of safety. I'll keep watching, but I won't act for now."
