# PDD's 2025 Profit Decline: A Deliberate Choice or a Forced Outcome?

**Research date: May 15, 2026 | Three agents in parallel**

---

## One-line conclusion

> **Roughly 60% deliberate, 40% forced. Core earning power has not weakened — if anything, it is strengthening.** Reported net profit came in at RMB 99.3 billion (-12%), but after stripping out deliberate investment and the Temu tariff shock, normalized profit is roughly RMB 120-140 billion — actually higher than 2024's RMB 112.4 billion. PDD is choosing not to let all of its earnings show up on the income statement — it is trading certain short-term profit for an uncertain long-term ecosystem.

---

## Where did the profit "disappear" to?

### Reported figures

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Revenue | RMB 393.8 billion | RMB 431.8 billion | +10% |
| Gross margin | 60.9% | 56.3% | **-4.6 percentage points** |
| Marketing expense | RMB 111.3 billion | RMB 125.3 billion | +13% |
| R&D expense | RMB 12.7 billion | RMB 16.5 billion | **+30%** |
| Operating profit | RMB 108.8 billion | RMB 94.6 billion | -13% |
| Net profit | RMB 112.4 billion | RMB 99.3 billion | **-12%** |

Profit fell by roughly RMB 13 billion. Revenue is rising (+10%) while profit is falling (-12%) — the money was eaten up by expenses and costs.

### Where the money went

| Expense item | Increase | Share of profit decline | Deliberate/forced |
|--------|---------|-------------|----------|
| Marketing expense increase | +RMB 14 billion | Largest item | ~60% deliberate (merchant fee rebates + consumer subsidies), ~40% forced (Temu upkeep + competitive defense) |
| R&D expense increase | +RMB 3.8 billion | Medium | **Fully deliberate** — the AI recommendation engine, Temu's tech infrastructure, merchant governance systems |
| Gross margin decline | -4.6 percentage points | Large | Mostly forced — Temu's shift from air to sea freight raised logistics costs, plus extra tariff costs |
| Administrative expense | -RMB 0.9 billion (decrease) | Positive | Strong cost control |

## Main platform vs. Temu: who's dragging things down?

PDD does not disclose this breakdown, but according to investment-bank segmentation models:

| Segment | 2024 revenue | Profit/(loss) | Margin |
|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Main platform (domestic e-commerce)** | ~RMB 244.2 billion | **~RMB 150 billion** | **~61%** |
| **Temu (globalization)** | ~RMB 119.6 billion | **~-RMB 25.3 billion** | -21% |
| **Duoduo Maicai (community group-buying grocery business)** | ~RMB 30 billion | Close to breakeven | ~0% |
| **Consolidated** | RMB 393.8 billion | RMB 112.4 billion | 28.5% |

**The main platform is a money-printing machine with a 61% margin.** The consolidated margin is only 28.5%, entirely because of Temu's RMB 25.3 billion loss.

Temu's loss trend: an RMB 58-65 billion loss in 2023 → narrowed to ~RMB 25.3 billion in 2024 → continued to narrow in 2025 (loss ratio of roughly -8%) → targeting breakeven in the US/European market by Q4 2026. The direction is right, but the Temu tariff shock (US daily active users collapsed 58%, forced shift from air to sea freight) did add to the burden in 2025.

## Deliberate vs. forced: roughly 60% vs. 40%

### Deliberate factors (~60% of the profit decline)

**1. Merchant fee reductions — the largest deliberate investment**
- The "RMB 100 billion support" program: has cumulatively covered 2.7 million-plus merchants
- Q2 alone saw RMB 11 billion in promotion-fee rebates, saving the average merchant RMB 180,000 a year
- Deposit requirements were cut 50-90% across categories including large appliances
- Substantial cuts to commission rates

**2. Increased R&D investment (+RMB 3.8 billion, +30%)**
- AI recommendation system upgrades
- Temu's technical infrastructure
- Merchant operations and governance systems
- Entirely a deliberate, long-term-oriented choice

**3. Consumer-side subsidies**
- "RMB 10 billion consumer vouchers," "RMB 10 billion doubled subsidies"
- More than 3.76 million orders in a single day during the June 18 shopping festival
- Platform-direct subsidies offsetting the government subsidy program's weaker reach into 3C electronics and appliances

**4. Preparation for "New Pinmm"**
- Increased Q4 investment in preparation for the March 2026 launch

**Key evidence — expense pacing was fully controllable**:

| Quarter | Marketing expense | YoY | Interpretation |
|------|---------|------|------|
| Q1 | RMB 33.4 billion | **+43%** | A concentrated, heavy bet |
| Q2 | RMB 27.2 billion | **+4.5%** | Clearly pulled back |
| Q3 | RMB 30.3 billion | Manageable | Resumed, but not out of control |
| Q4 | RMB 34.4 billion | Year-end step-up | Paving the way for "New Pinmm" |

**Able to both ramp up and pull back — this is not runaway spending.** If this spending were being forced by competition, Q2 could not have suddenly dropped from +43% to +4.5%.

### Forced factors (~40% of the profit decline)

**1. The Temu tariff shock — the largest forced factor**
- The US eliminated the $800 de minimis exemption in May 2025
- Temu's daily active users collapsed 58%
- Air-freight share fell from 85% to 2%, shifting almost entirely to sea freight
- Roughly $15 million a day in subsidies to keep prices low
- The shift to a semi-managed model lowered commission rates, and transaction-service revenue growth collapsed from +108% to +9%

**2. Douyin competition**
- Douyin e-commerce GMV growth exceeded 30%, mounting a direct assault on the low-price, white-label segment
- Some categories were forced to increase subsidy intensity

**3. Merchant friction forcing the company's hand**
- Accumulated merchant resentment over the "refund-only" policy (16,000 complaints on the Heimao complaint platform, merchants surrounding headquarters)
- Mandatory refund-only was forced to be dropped in April 2025 — not entirely voluntary

**4. Government subsidy program skewed toward 3C electronics and appliances**
- A category where PDD is weak, forcing it to dig into its own pocket to compensate

## True earning power: normalized profit is actually higher

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 reported | 2025 normalized (estimate) |
|------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Net profit | RMB 112.4 billion | RMB 99.3 billion | **~RMB 120-140 billion** |
| YoY | — | -12% | **+7% to +25%** |

Normalization method: reported profit of RMB 99.3 billion + the increase in deliberate investment (merchant rebates + consumer subsidies, ~RMB 20-30 billion) + Temu's extra tariff-related costs (~RMB 10 billion) - one-off gain adjustments ≈ RMB 120-140 billion.

**Core earning power has not just held up — it is actually improving.** Management chose to reinvest both incremental profit and part of its existing profit back into ecosystem-building.

## What did management say?

### Timeline — a warning issued half a year in advance, not an after-the-fact excuse

| Time | Speaker | Core statement |
|------|--------|---------|
| August 2024 | Lei Chen | "A decline in profitability is inevitable"; "profit should not be used as long-term guidance" |
| August 2024 | Jiazhen Zhao | "Sacrificing short-term profit is necessary — management has reached consensus on this" |
| May 2025 | Lei Chen | "The RMB 100 billion support program is not just a slogan" |
| August 2025 | Jiazhen Zhao | "We don't believe this quarter's profit is sustainable" |
| November 2025 | Jiazhen Zhao | "We may be at a stage-specific disadvantage relative to some peers" |
| March 2026 | Jiazhen Zhao | "Margin volatility will be the norm"; "rebuild another PDD in three years" |

August 2024 (the Q2 earnings call) was the key turning point. Management gave advance warning two quarters before profit actually declined — if this were purely reactive, it could not have been predicted this precisely this far ahead.

Shares plunged 28.5% that day, wiping out $55 billion in market value. But **Duan Yongping sold puts to buy the dip on that very day and kept adding afterward.** All three major investment banks (Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Citi) maintained buy ratings.

### A signal worth watching closely

Jiazhen Zhao said something in the Q3 call worth flagging: "Relative to some peers, we may be at a stage-specific disadvantage for quite some time."

This does not sound like something said by a manager fully in control. It suggests: **the subsidies and concessions are not purely a matter of confident choice — there is also anxiety about losing competitive position.**

## RMB 422.3 billion in cash: confidence to invest, or a governance flaw?

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Cash + short-term investments | RMB 422.3 billion ($60.4 billion) |
| Share of market cap | 42.5% |
| Dividends since IPO | 0 |
| Buybacks since IPO | 0 |
| Capex | Only RMB 960 million a year (extremely asset-light) |

Management says it is not paying dividends or buying back stock because the company is "still in an investment phase." The RMB 100 billion three-year plan for "New Pinmm" genuinely requires capital.

But even after investing RMB 100 billion in "New Pinmm," RMB 320 billion would remain. **The absence of dividends more likely reflects management's indifference to shareholder returns than a purely strategic necessity.** This is the core governance issue that has long suppressed PDD's valuation.

---

## Final judgment

**PDD's 2025 profit decline is, in essence, a "forced acceleration of a deliberate transformation."**

Management recognized as early as mid-2024 that "high profit extracted by squeezing merchants is unsustainable" and mapped out a path of deliberate concessions and ecosystem rebuilding. But external factors — the tariff shock, Douyin's competition, merchant friction — forced the transformation to unfold faster and harder than planned.

| Dimension | Judgment |
|------|------|
| Deliberate vs. forced | **~60% deliberate, ~40% forced** |
| Core earning power | **Not weakened — normalized profit is actually growing** |
| Main platform margin | **~61%, extremely healthy** |
| Expense controllability | **Can be ramped up and pulled back — not runaway spending** |
| Management intent | **Advance warning, consistent messaging, backed by capital — fairly credible** |
| Governance concern | **RMB 420 billion in cash not returned to shareholders — the biggest demerit** |
| Next validation point | **2027-2028: whether "New Pinmm" delivers, whether Temu turns profitable** |
