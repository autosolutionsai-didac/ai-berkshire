## Series Positioning

Pinduoduo is not a company that has been "under-researched" — it is one of the Chinese ADRs that has been **researched the most yet remains the most divisive**.

- The bulls say: PE of 6.3x (ex-cash), ROE of 31%, net cash of 422.3 billion yuan — cheap to an extreme
- The bears say: management is completely opaque, the 422.3 billion yuan in cash isn't being returned to shareholders, and Temu's political risk could go to zero at any time

**Both camps are looking at the same company, yet arrive at completely opposite conclusions.** This is extremely rare among A-shares / Hong Kong stocks / Chinese ADRs.

What this series aims to do is **first lay out clearly "what it is," then let you judge for yourself "whether it's worth buying"** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Series Writing Principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Data first, then logic, then conclusion** — no presumption of bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment is accompanied by the counter-argument** — letting readers weigh it themselves
3. **Unreliable data would rather be omitted** — many of Pinduoduo's key metrics (Temu GMV, core-platform MAU breakdown, etc.) have never been publicly disclosed; this series only anchors on the most credible sources available and does not force false precision
4. **No "probability-weighted expected return" calculations** — assigning probability weights to scenarios is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **Does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every chapter

## Comparison With "Understanding Tencent"

The 8-part "Understanding Tencent" series has already been published. Comparing the two series:

| Dimension | Tencent | Pinduoduo |
|------|------|--------|
| Asset quality | High quality + extremely deep moat + high certainty | Extreme financial efficiency + moderate moat + low certainty |
| Valuation | 14.5x PE, reasonable to cheap | 10.5x PE / 6.3x ex-cash, **extremely cheap** |
| Management transparency | High (detailed earnings reports, generous buybacks) | Low (no earnings calls, no buybacks, no dividends) |
| Risk structure | Regulatory tail risk + US-China relations | Temu political gamble + VIE structure + management trust |
| **Suitable investor** | Steady type who can "sleep soundly" | Value-arbitrage type who can tolerate high uncertainty |

If you read the Tencent series and thought "that makes a lot of sense," pay attention to one thing when reading the Pinduoduo series — Pinduoduo's "cheapness" comes at a cost, and that cost is "opacity."
