# Pinduoduo Domestic Main Site — 10-Year Profit Forecast Model

**Research Date**: May 15, 2026
**Research Scope**: Pinduoduo domestic main site only (excludes Temu, excludes Duoduo Maicai community group buying)
**Core Question**: Will main-site net profit in 2036 be higher or lower than in 2025? By how much, specifically?
**Data Sources**: PDD Holdings financial reports, Wall Street CN, brokerage research report breakdowns, Taotian (Taobao & Tmall Group) / Amazon / Coupang / Rakuten financial reports
**Confidence Level**: Medium (the main site is far more predictable than Temu, but ten years out still carries considerable uncertainty)

---

## One-Line Conclusion

> **Three-scenario forecast for Pinduoduo main-site net profit in 2036: bear case approx. 90.0 billion yuan, base case approx. 135.0 billion yuan, bull case approx. 185.0 billion yuan. Against an estimated 2025 main-site net profit of approximately 105.0 billion yuan, the base case implies about 29% growth over ten years — only 2.6% annualized. The main site is a mature money-printing machine, but its room to grow is limited. The core tension: GMV growth is slowing to the low single digits, the take rate is already near its ceiling, and margins remain under sustained pressure from the "100 Billion Support" program.**

**Trend assessment**:

- There is roughly 1.0-1.5 percentage points of further upside (Kaiyuan Securities projects 5.5-6.0% by 2026), but the "100 Billion Support" program's commission-cut policy is pushing in the opposite direction

### 5.2 Upside Risks (Factors That Could Make the Forecast Too Conservative)

1. **Buyback initiation**: If PDD uses half of its $60.4 billion cash pile for buybacks, EPS accretion of 20%+ would follow, boosting shareholder returns even if profit stays flat
2. **Advertising product innovation**: AI-driven smart ad placement, video-feed advertising, and similar tools could push the take rate to 7%+ (compare Douyin's 8-10%)
3. **Deepening lower-tier market penetration**: China still has approximately 300 million people with below-average e-commerce penetration
4. **Industrial-cluster upgrading**: shifting from "white-label OEM" to "industrial-cluster brands" could lift average order value by 15-20%
5. **Duoduo Maicai synergy**: community group buying drives traffic to the main site, boosting user stickiness and purchase frequency

### 5.3 Downside Risks (Factors That Could Make the Forecast Too Optimistic)

1. **Continued erosion by Douyin e-commerce**: Douyin has compressed into 5 years what took Alibaba 20 years; if its GMV reaches RMB 8 trillion by 2030, it will directly squeeze PDD's space
2. **AI agents disrupting traffic distribution**: once AI shopping agents mature in 2028-2030, PDD's "recommendation algorithm + low-price mindshare" moat is weakened
3. **The rise of instant retail**: 30-minute-delivery experiences such as Meituan Shangou and JD Miaoda are eating into traditional e-commerce share, and PDD is absent from this race
4. **The "100 Billion Support" program becoming permanent**: if commission cuts/subsidies become a long-term competitive strategy, operating margin could permanently decline by 5-10 percentage points
5. **Regulatory risk**: antitrust, data security, and user privacy protection rules are tightening
6. **Continued consumption downgrading**: China's slowing economic growth causes the overall e-commerce market to underperform expectations

### 5.4 The Biggest "Known Unknown"

**Is the "100 Billion Support" program a short-term concession or a long-term strategic transformation?**

- If it is a short-term concession (similar to Taotian's 2023-2024 "return to the user" strategy): margins recover after 2028, making the base/bull cases more likely
- If it is a long-term strategic transformation (shifting from "profit maximization" to "ecosystem health"): margins permanently decline to 30-33%, making the bear case more likely
- Management's exact words: "strive to rebuild another Pinduoduo within three years" — the meaning is ambiguous; it could refer to doubling GMV (requiring heavy investment), or it could refer to restructuring the ecosystem
