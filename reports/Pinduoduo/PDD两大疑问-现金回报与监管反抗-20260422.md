3. He believes Colin Huang will eventually do the right thing (with no specific timeline)

**Investment Implications**:
- **Valuing PDD's $60.4 billion in cash at a 50-70% discount** is the rational approach
- **Don't treat Duan Yongping's large position as a signal that "PDD will pay dividends"** — he's betting on Colin Huang's character, not on capital allocation discipline
