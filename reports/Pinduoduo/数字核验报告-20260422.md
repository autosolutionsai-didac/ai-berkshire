## VII. Explanation of major discrepancies

### Discrepancy 1: Q4 segment figure errors (most critical)

The research report's stated Q4 online marketing figure of 57.0 billion (+17%) and Q4 transaction services figure of 53.6 billion (+33%) are actually **2024 Q4** historical figures, not 2025 Q4. The actual 2025 Q4 data:
- Online marketing services: **60.01 billion yuan** (+5% YoY, a significant slowdown)
- Transaction services: **63.90 billion yuan** (+19% YoY)
- Total: **123.91 billion yuan** (fully consistent with total Q4 revenue)

This error not only causes the figures to fail to reconcile, but also obscures an important trend: **2025 Q4 online marketing growth slowed sharply to just 5%**, a significant deceleration from 2024 Q4's +17%, and a signal that deserves close attention.

### Discrepancy 2: 2024 net income basis confusion

"2024 net income 122.0 billion yuan" — the GAAP basis is approximately 112.4 billion yuan, and the Non-GAAP (adjusted) basis is 122.3 billion yuan. The report should specify which basis it's using.

### Discrepancy 3: 2025 China online retail sales

The report forecasts "16.9 trillion (+9.1%)," while the National Bureau of Statistics has already published an actual 2025 figure of 15.97 trillion (+8.6%) — the actual growth rate came in lower than forecast.

### Discrepancy 4: RMB/USD conversion of the cash figure

Cash of 422.3 billion yuan ÷ 7.1 ≈ $59.5B; ÷ 7.0 ≈ $60.3B. The differing figures of $59.5B, $60.0B, and $60.4B all stem from different exchange-rate assumptions (7.0-7.1), which are all reasonable conversions, not a data contradiction.
