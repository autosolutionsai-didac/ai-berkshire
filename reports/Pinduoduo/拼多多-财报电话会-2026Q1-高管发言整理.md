## Key Takeaways (Read This Part First)

### One-Sentence Summary

**Management repeatedly emphasized two key themes throughout the call — "supply-chain investment" and "self-operated brands" — while calmly accepting near-term profit declines and refusing to give growth guidance. The core message: "we are not chasing short-term results; building supply-chain capability comes first."**

## Part One: Executive Opening Remarks

### Zhao Jiazhen (Executive Director/Co-CEO)

> The following is the full text of Zhao Jiazhen's opening remarks:

Hello everyone, I'm Zhao Jiazhen. Thank you for joining PDD's first-quarter 2026 earnings call.

2026 marks the beginning of Pinduoduo's second decade and a pivotal year for a company-wide overhaul of our organizational culture, centered on high-quality development.

**On organizational restructuring:**

Since the start of this year, we have been driving a profound restructuring of our internal organizational management around safety compliance and social responsibility. Teams have been instructed to treat safety, compliance, and social responsibility as the baseline requirement for all work. We are committed to genuinely strengthening our sense of responsibility as a platform enterprise.

**On the new self-operated brand venture:**

The company has entered a new phase of its three-year "build another Pinduoduo" strategy. Our self-operated brand business is progressing steadily, and we are doubling down on supply-chain investment.

This past March, we registered a dedicated company in Chang'an and formally launched our self-operated brand business, with an initial capital injection of RMB 15 billion and a plan to invest RMB 100 billion over the coming years. The goal is to systematically incubate a group of globally recognized brands.

**On the 10 Billion Subsidy Program / 100 Billion Support Program:**

In the first quarter, the 10 Billion Subsidy program continued to sustain a high level of investment, launching a series of initiatives including the "Duoduo Quality Goods" agricultural upgrade program and next-generation supply-chain capabilities. We deployed planting guidance, logistics support, and processing empowerment programs in regions including Guangxi, Hainan, and Sichuan.

**On building industrial belts:**

Manufacturing industrial belts are shifting from homogeneous competition toward an R&D model centered on consumer demand. In the lighting industry, for example, supply-chain optimization has cut logistics costs per order from RMB 40-50 down to around RMB 10. Merchant order volumes in western provinces grew more than 30% year over year.

**On rural free-shipping:**

Since the start of this year, we have been pushing full speed ahead on the "express delivery to every village" initiative, with early results already showing. As of March, direct-to-village express coverage had exceeded 70%. Transit warehouses now handle nearly 10,000 orders a day on average, creating substantial local employment in county and township economies.

Our teams are upgrading village-level pickup points into multifunctional digital rural service hubs, combining package pickup/drop-off with first-mile logistics for agricultural products moving to market.

**On platform governance:**

In the first quarter, the platform rolled out more than 20 food-safety initiatives, including compliance reviews, food-advertising audits, and enhanced monitoring. We also tightened live-streaming compliance checks and upgraded our food-safety reporting channels.

**Closing remarks:**

Early-stage challenges are unavoidable, but these very difficulties are what drive us to reinvent ourselves. The current management team will confront these challenges head-on.

### Chen Lei (Chairman/Co-CEO)

> The following is the full text of Chen Lei's opening remarks:

**On the three-year strategy:**

At last year's shareholder meeting, we formally announced our three-year "build another Pinduoduo" strategy, focusing the company's strategic priorities on supply-chain investment.

**On progress in self-operated brands:**

In the first quarter, our self-operated brand business made solid progress. Our teams went deep into industrial belts across categories, driving the integration of high-quality supply-chain resources. We are working closely with merchants and manufacturers to co-develop branded products, aiming to strengthen our supply-chain mid-platform capabilities and drive industrial transformation and upgrading.

**On lessons from the 10 Billion Subsidy program:**

The results of the 10 Billion Subsidy program show that brand development is the next major opportunity in supply-chain upgrading, which gives us confidence in pursuing the self-operated brand model. The program's results have provided valuable first-hand experience for future investment direction, improved the experience for both merchants and consumers, made agricultural supply chains more efficient and resilient, and extended free-shipping service into remote areas.

**On brand development goals:**

Through the self-operated brand model, our goal is to systematically incubate a group of globally recognized brands, thereby driving the transformation and upgrading of the entire supply chain.

**On industrial transformation:**

Merchants are leveraging the platform's support to build brands, shifting their product mix from "broad but generic" to "focused and premium," and moving from standardized production to demand-driven customization. OEM factories are beginning to invest in R&D, technology, brand-building, and channel distribution.

Brand development is the next major opportunity in supply-chain upgrading.

**On agricultural innovation:**

We recently held the finals of the "Duoduo Agricultural Research Technology Competition," now in its sixth edition. This competition has evolved from an industry contest into an integrated platform for cultivating the next generation of agricultural research talent, contributing to agricultural modernization.

**On organizational development:**

We have identified our own shortcomings and taken corrective action. We are pushing forward with an overhaul of internal management, strengthening compliance awareness, and laying the foundation for the company's long-term healthy development.

**On our investment commitment:**

2026 marks a new starting point for Pinduoduo's next decade. With a deep sense of responsibility and mission, we firmly believe that staying true to our founding purpose is the foundation of everything we do. We will resolutely increase investment, advance the self-operated brand business, and deepen our supply-chain commitment.

### Li Jiong (Group CFO)

> The following is the full text of Li Jiong's financial results presentation:

**Revenue overview:**

Total revenue in the first quarter grew 11% year over year to RMB 106.2 billion, driven mainly by growth in transaction services revenue.

**Revenue breakdown:**

| Item | 2026 Q1 | 2025 Q1 | YoY Change |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Online marketing services and other revenue | RMB 49.9 billion | RMB 48.7 billion | +2.5% |
| Transaction services revenue | RMB 56.3 billion | — | +20% |
| Total revenue | RMB 106.2 billion | ~RMB 95.7 billion | +11% |

**Cost analysis:**

Total cost of revenue grew 15% from RMB 40.9 billion in 2025 Q1 to RMB 46.9 billion, driven mainly by increased fulfillment expenses, bandwidth and server costs, and payment processing fees.

**Expenses (Non-GAAP basis):**

| Expense Item | 2026 Q1 | % of Revenue | 2025 Q1 % of Revenue |
|----------|--------|---------|---------------|
| Sales and marketing expenses | RMB 33.4 billion | 31% | 34% |
| R&D expenses | RMB 4.0 billion | — | +32% YoY |
| General and administrative expenses | RMB 872 million | — | RMB 735 million |
| Total operating expenses | RMB 38.3 billion | 36% | 38% |

**Profitability metrics:**

| Metric | 2026 Q1 | 2025 Q1 | Change |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| GAAP operating profit | RMB 19.6 billion | RMB 16.1 billion | +22% |
| Non-GAAP operating profit | RMB 21.1 billion | RMB 18.3 billion | — |
| Non-GAAP operating margin | 20% | 19% | +1pt |
| GAAP net profit attributable to shareholders | RMB 12.5 billion | RMB 14.7 billion | -15% |
| Non-GAAP net profit attributable to shareholders | RMB 14.1 billion | RMB 16.9 billion | -17% |
| Non-GAAP diluted earnings per ADS | RMB 9.51 | RMB 11.41 | -17% |

**Explanation for the profit decline:**

Operating profit grew 22% year over year, yet net profit actually fell 15%. The gap comes mainly from non-operating items — interest income and investment gains swung from positive to negative — which, combined with other changes in income and expense, together produced a negative impact of more than RMB 6.0 billion.

**Cash flow and balance sheet:**

- Operating cash flow: RMB 16.4 billion (RMB 15.5 billion in the same period last year)
- As of March 31, 2026, cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments totaled: **RMB 436.1 billion**

## Part Two: Analyst Q&A

### Q1: Citi's Alicia Yap

**Question one:** Pinduoduo has always positioned itself as an e-commerce platform. What's the core rationale behind launching a self-operated brand business now? Does this signal a major shift in the company's overall strategy?

**Question two:** Third-party data shows Pinduoduo's overseas business has achieved solid user growth. What will be the focus of the overseas business going forward?

---

**Zhao Jiazhen's answer (self-operated brand strategy):**

Since the start of this year, we have been driving a profound restructuring of our organization and internal management around safety compliance and social responsibility.

In the process, we have come to see with increasing clarity that today's supply chains face real challenges — many excellent manufacturers, constrained by talent, information, and scale, have not yet completed the transition to branding.

As a platform, we have both the responsibility and the capability to deepen our supply-chain investment and provide solutions to this industry-wide problem. By establishing a dedicated company, we are consolidating platform resources to advance this new self-operated brand model.

Through the self-operated brand model, the platform takes on greater responsibility and risk, freeing our industrial partners to focus on high-quality production.

We believe this is a necessary step to push the platform, and the entire e-commerce ecosystem, into the next stage of high-quality development. Leveraging the platform's scale advantages, we will take on more risk while sharing the returns with our supply-chain manufacturers, giving them certainty.

In a complex and fast-changing market environment, the platform must take on greater responsibility and deepen its operations.

---

**Chen Lei's answer (overseas business):**

After nearly three years of development, our overseas business has earned the support of consumers around the world. The key is to return to the fundamentals of e-commerce — that is, supply-chain capability.

Our work will proceed along two strategic directions:

**First, advancing the integration and optimization of the supply chain to eliminate end-to-end bottlenecks.** We will actively bring in high-quality merchants and ecosystem partners.

**Second, deepening the development of the self-operated brand model.** Brands are particularly critical to reinforcing consumer mindshare.

Competition in e-commerce is fierce, and switching costs for consumers are low. The true driver of durable, sustainable competitive advantage lies in the supply-chain capabilities that are often invisible to the outside world.

Supply-chain investment is a long-term, systemic undertaking. Whatever challenges we face, we will stay true to our founding purpose and continue driving deep transformation.

---

### Q2: Goldman Sachs' Ronald Keung

**Question one:** Regarding the three-year, RMB 100 billion self-operated brand investment plan — which areas will this investment be allocated to? When will it show up financially? How should we evaluate the incremental growth potential this strategy brings?

**Question two:** Against a backdrop of rising consumption and increasing penetration of low-price goods, why did online marketing services revenue growth slow in the first quarter? (Note: ad revenue grew only 2.5% year over year)

---

**Zhao Jiazhen's answer (investment direction):**

Brand building involves a whole set of capabilities — from product design, standard-setting, and manufacturing to quality control, warehousing and logistics, legal compliance, and customer service — and every link requires long-term, patient investment.

Many small and mid-sized manufacturers in the industrial belts are very good at large-scale production and cost control, but because they lack branding capability, they have not been able to climb the value chain.

By establishing a dedicated company, we are using the platform's advantages in technology, scale, and organization to build foundational capabilities and help our supply-chain partners develop brands.

Under the self-operated brand model, we provide the supply chain with certainty of sales volume, giving manufacturers the confidence to invest in R&D and process innovation, substantially lowering their costs and risks.

We believe this is the right strategic direction. We use certainty to empower the supply chain, creating a win-win outcome — manufacturers can break free from homogeneous competition and focus on quality upgrades, while consumers can enjoy high-quality products at reasonable prices.

Looking back at our history — from early improvements to agricultural supply chains, to Duoduo Grocery later on, to the overseas business, to the 10 Billion Subsidy program — **if we are to fundamentally solve the problem of homogeneous competition faced by merchants on the platform, we must take on greater responsibility and drive deeper supply-chain integration. This is a path we cannot avoid.**

This year opens a new decade of high-quality development. We will use this as an opportunity to reshape our organization and internal management. As more supply-chain initiatives roll out, we believe there is an opportunity to build another Pinduoduo within the next three years.

---

**Zhao Jiazhen's answer (slowdown in advertising revenue growth):**

There is still enormous potential in the online retail market, and there's a great deal more we can do. The industry has entered a critical phase of high-quality development. The only way to achieve sustainable, healthy industry growth is to take the necessary steps to deeply empower the supply chain.

Our teams are upgrading village-level pickup points into multifunctional digital rural service hubs, combining package pickup/drop-off with first-mile logistics for agricultural products moving to market. By giving farmers a one-stop solution for packing and shipping, we help high-quality agricultural products reach a wider market.

On the logistics side, our teams have brought more consumers in remote areas into the free-shipping zone. Through concrete supply-chain improvements, logistics costs in some remote areas have fallen by around 80%. Many merchants have saved millions of yuan in logistics fees.

These are just a few examples. Looking ahead, we will continue to roll out more concrete supply-chain projects.

### Q3: Bank of America's Joyce Ju

**Question one:** How does the company assess the impact of new models (livestream e-commerce, instant retail, etc.) on the competitive landscape of the industry? Are there plans to expand into these areas?

**Question two:** Sales and marketing expense ratio ticked up slightly in the first quarter, and overall margins have been volatile. What's the outlook for margins going forward? What is a reasonable expectation for a steady-state margin level?

---

**Zhao Jiazhen's answer (new business models):**

Compared with traditional retail, switching costs in e-commerce are much lower, which drives faster industry evolution and more intense competition. Management is also closely watching the development of new technologies. (The core still comes down to) product selection, competitive pricing, and better service.

**Competition between different e-commerce platforms or business models, at bottom, is always a competition of underlying supply-chain capability.**

That is precisely why, at the end of last year, we set a clear group strategy — focusing and doubling down on supply-chain investment.

Under this strategy, we are steadily advancing our various supply-chain initiatives, particularly the self-operated brand business. Dedicated teams are already working in major industrial belts, accelerating the integration of high-quality supply-chain resources and building out the framework for self-operated brands.

We are working closely with manufacturers to design and develop branded products tailored to different markets, while also driving the transformation and upgrading of the supply chain.

**The period ahead is a critical window for our supply-chain investment, and we will push the self-operated brand plan forward at full speed.** We will also respond to the evolving competitive landscape by continually creating unique value for the supply-chain ecosystem.

---

**Li Jiong's answer (margin outlook):**

Our goal is the long-term, sustainable growth of the platform's intrinsic value. Due to seasonal factors, some quarter-to-quarter volatility in the financial data is normal.

**The platform's long-term value fundamentally depends on the value we create for consumers and the broader ecosystem.**

That is why we remain firmly committed to investing in supply-chain development — whether it's the self-operated brand strategy, express delivery to every village, or next-generation supply-chain capabilities — these are all high-impact projects capable of unlocking long-term value and new industry growth. We will continue to invest with resolve.

**We will not over-focus on short-term financial performance; instead, we prioritize the healthy development of the platform ecosystem and the accumulation and reinforcement of supply-chain capability.** The durable competitive advantage built up over the long term will determine the trajectory of the platform's intrinsic value.
