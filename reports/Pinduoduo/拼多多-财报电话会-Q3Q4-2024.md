### 1.2 Lei Chen's Remarks

**Quote (official English press release):**
> "Our focus remained on driving the high-quality development of the platforms."

> "We are committed to investing consistently and patiently in our platform ecosystem to deliver impactful results over the long run."

**Quote (Chinese-language call, source: Sina Finance transcript):**
> "Over the coming quarters, we will remain firmly committed to long-term investment, continuing to double down on ecosystem building initiatives such as 'new-quality supply' and 'e-commerce goes west,' driving the platform and the broader industry toward accelerated high-quality development."

> "Over the past quarter, we kept increasing our commitment to agricultural R&D and ecosystem building, continuing to give back to society, agriculture, and industry."

---

### 1.3 Jiazhen Zhao's Remarks

**Quote (official English press release):**
> "In the third quarter, we stepped up the investments in our platform ecosystem through merchant support policies and trust and safety updates. High-quality merchants are encouraged to further strengthen their product and service offerings, thereby fostering a virtuous cycle."

**Quote (Chinese-language call, source: Sina Finance transcript):**
> "We are fully prepared for a protracted contest; changes in the external competitive environment will not affect our pace."

> "Constrained by the natural trajectory of business development, our growth rate will not be a linear extrapolation over the long term either, so in this environment we consider the slowdown in revenue growth inevitable."

> "We have made substantial investments in the merchant ecosystem, vigorously supporting high-quality merchants, and rolled out the 'RMB 10 billion fee reduction' program — a package of new measures including service-fee rebates, lower deposit requirements, waived logistics transfer fees, and upgraded after-sales service for merchants."

**Paraphrase (source: Sina Finance / Tencent News reporting):**
- Acknowledged that the overall competitive environment in e-commerce "remains very intense"
- Admitted candidly that "the team has shown some shortcomings in capability in responding to certain changes"

---

### 1.4 Jun Liu's Remarks

**Quote (official English press release):**
> "Our topline growth further moderated quarter-on-quarter amid intensified competition and ongoing external challenges."

**Quote (Chinese-language call, source: Sina Finance transcript):**
> "Looking further out, our profitability may fluctuate somewhat, or even decline."

---

### 1.5 Key Strategic Initiatives in Q3 (Official Announcements)

**"RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" Program:**
- Rolled out RMB 10 billion in fee reductions, benefiting more than 10 million merchants
- Specific measures: service-fee rebates, lower deposit requirements, waived logistics transfer fees, upgraded after-sales service for merchants

**"E-commerce Goes West":**
- Eliminated the logistics transfer fees merchants pay for orders shipped to remote western regions, with the platform absorbing the cost in full
- Officially stated to have driven double-digit growth in orders from western regions

**"New-Quality Merchant Support Program":**
- Committed an RMB 10 billion resource package
- Established a dedicated team reaching into 100 agricultural regions and industrial clusters
- Focused support on merchants with product and technology innovation capabilities

**Trust and Safety Investment:**
- Expanded investment in trust and safety
- Strengthened compliance systems and merchant compliance vetting

### 2.3 Jiazhen Zhao's Remarks

**Quote (official English press release):**
> "Leveraging our digital capabilities, we continued to give back to consumers and support quality merchants."

**Quote (Chinese-language call, source: Sina Finance transcript):**
> "In the current e-commerce environment, deep transformation on the supply side has become urgent. The investments we are making today for the healthy development of merchants and the industry will, over the long term — through protection of merchant rights and interests, improved merchant services and satisfaction, and richer new-quality supply — further improve the consumer shopping experience, thereby driving the healthy development of the platform ecosystem."

> "The high-quality development strategy we have pursued for the long term has now entered a harvest period. The synergy between the platform and the industry ecosystem is gradually being unlocked and continues to deliver inclusive value to both supply and demand. This further strengthens our resolve to invest in ecosystem building."

> "The e-commerce industry is gradually shifting toward an emphasis on quality service and trustworthy innovation. Simply judging a company's performance by short-term returns is no longer well suited to the company's current stage of development. As a company of the new era operating globally, we will actively guide innovation and take on greater social responsibility in the regions where our business operates."

> "Going forward, we will continue to maintain long-term, high-quality investment on the consumer side."

> "The company's profit margin will continue to show ups and downs across different quarters going forward, and this will be the new normal."

**Paraphrase (source: Sina Finance reporting):**
- Acknowledged that "competition in the e-commerce industry today is indeed intense, and that is a fact for any platform"

---

### 2.4 Jun Liu's Remarks

**Quote (official English press release):**
> "Looking ahead, we will continue to prioritize investments in the platform ecosystem as the cornerstone of our long-term value creation strategy."

**Quote (Chinese-language call, source: Sina Finance transcript):**
> "Uncertainty in the external environment together with intense industry competition weighed on the company's revenue growth in the short term, while the stepped-up investment in 'merchant support' and 'platform ecosystem building' also caused fluctuations in the company's profitability."

---

### 2.5 Key Strategic Initiatives in Q4 (Official Announcements)

**Continuation and Upgrade of the "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" Program:**
- Extended the RMB 10 billion fee-reduction program from Q3
- Further lowered merchants' operating costs

**Continued Advancement of "E-commerce Goes West":**
- The platform continued to absorb logistics transfer fees for remote western regions in full
- Officially stated that orders from western regions continued to achieve double-digit growth

**Consumer-Side Investment:**
- Upgraded the "Super Double Subsidy" campaign
- Rolled out promotional mechanisms such as "RMB 10 billion consumer vouchers"

**Merchant Rights Protection:**
- Introduced new mechanisms such as the "Merchant Protection Council"
- Strengthened merchant rights-protection mechanisms
