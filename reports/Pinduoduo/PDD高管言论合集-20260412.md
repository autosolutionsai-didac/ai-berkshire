## 1. 2024 Q2 Earnings Call (Most Controversial)

**Background**: On August 26, 2024, PDD released its 2024 Q2 earnings, showing revenue growth of 85.65% year over year (RMB 97.060 billion) — seemingly strong. But executive remarks on the earnings call triggered a single-day stock plunge of 28%, the largest single-day drop since the company's IPO.

Source: [Sina Finance Q2 earnings call report](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2024-08-27/doc-inckzssr4270380.shtml) | [21jingji.com report](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20240827/herald/302c471b5270c1f7fd451f62672e16e3.html) | [Sina Finance Q&A transcript](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2024-08-27/doc-inckzwym7453062.shtml)

### Lei Chen (Co-Chairman and CEO)

> "We have said many times that the profit growth of recent quarters should not be taken as long-term guidance — it is the result of a mismatch between business investment and the financial reporting cycle."

> "Looking ahead, our profitability may fluctuate in the near term, but the direction is that it will gradually trend downward."

> "Our business currently faces intense competition and changing external factors, which will inevitably bring volatility to our business and slow our revenue growth."

> "We are entering a new phase of steady transformation and high-quality development, which requires patient investment in the platform's long-term health."

> **(In response to an investor question about buybacks/dividends)** "Now is not the right time for share buybacks or dividends. And for the foreseeable next few years, we don't see the need for it either."

> "In the face of a complex environment, we will focus on our core strengths and continuously improve our supply chain capabilities."

### Jiazhen Zhao (Co-CEO)

> "High revenue growth is not sustainable."

> "Merchants are our important partners in serving consumers together."

> "We will substantially reduce transaction fees for quality merchants, which we expect will lower costs by RMB 10 billion over the coming years."

**Market reaction**: After the earnings call, PDD shares fell 28.51% in a single day, wiping out roughly $55 billion in market value.

## 2. 2024 Q3 Earnings Call

Source: [Wall Street CN — full Q3 earnings call transcript](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3759650) | [Wall Street CN — Q3 analysis](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3759650)

### Lei Chen

> "As always, we prioritize long-term value over short-term results."

> "Against a backdrop of intense industry competition, a complex global environment, and our ongoing investment in the ecosystem, our quarterly profitability will fluctuate and is inherently difficult to predict."

### Jiazhen Zhao

> "This quarter's profit should not be used as guidance for what comes next — we don't rule out continued volatility in performance over the coming quarters."

> "The platform is willing to sacrifice its own profit to create broader room for the platform ecosystem to develop — this is our long-term investment."

> "We choose to view competition with a long-term perspective, proactively increasing investment even if it means sacrificing some short-term profit, in order to create more possibilities for the healthy growth of the ecosystem and its future development."

> "Competition around new business formats in e-commerce continues to intensify."

> "Relative to short-term results, we place greater emphasis on investments that can bring long-term returns to the platform ecosystem. This is also why we are willing to sacrifice profit and persist in feeding it back into the ecosystem over the long term."

> "Going forward we will continue to increase investment, creating room for merchants to grow and building a healthier platform ecosystem over the long run."

## 3. 2025 Q4 Earnings Call (Most Recent)

**Background**: The 2025 Q4 earnings were released in March 2026, alongside the announcement of the "New Pinmm" strategy.

Source: [Eastmoney — "New Pinmm" report](https://caifuhao.eastmoney.com/news/20260326161308588913060) | [Lejucaijing — Jiazhen Zhao quotes](https://m.lejucaijing.com/news-7308743743728448931.html) | [Sina Tech report](https://finance.sina.cn/tech/2026-03-31/detail-inhswpmy2063689.d.html)

### Jiazhen Zhao (Co-Chairman and Co-CEO)

> "Over the past three years, Temu has expanded to more than 90 countries worldwide — three years to cover the same ground that took PDD's domestic e-commerce business ten years."

> "In the next phase, building on Temu's platform operating model, the company will go all-in on the 'New Pinmm' in-house brand model, and through this model continue to double down on China's supply chain, build higher manufacturing standards, and incubate a group of brands with international influence."

> "The company is not trying to diversify — it is trying to focus more, on the high-quality development of the supply chain. Through this core lever, we aim to remake the platform and drive a value leap across the ecosystem."

> "We believe there is an opportunity to build another PDD over the next three years."

> **(On evaluating short-term returns)** "Simply evaluating the company's performance by short-term returns is no longer well suited to the company's current stage of development."

> "Between short-term financial performance and the platform ecosystem's long-term value, the company will resolutely choose the latter — so margins will continue to fluctuate from quarter to quarter, and this will be the new normal."

### Lei Chen (Co-Chairman and Co-CEO)

> "We believe that corporate governance and internal talent development have not kept pace with business growth, and in many places we are stretched thin. Combined with the fast-changing international environment, we believe it is necessary to carry out a systematic rebuild of our organizational culture and corporate governance."

> "Regulation will lay a good foundation for the company to enter its next phase, and will also point the way for the company amid a rapidly changing international environment."

> "I'd suggest not focusing too much on any single quarter's margin figure, and instead paying more attention to the high-quality development of our platform ecosystem — only a healthy platform ecosystem and a strong supply chain can deliver sustainable long-term growth in intrinsic value."

> "For some time to come, the development focus of the international business will remain on investment, without directly affecting the consumer shopping experience."

## 5. Fact-Check: Straight Talk or Media Exaggeration?

This section fact-checks, one by one, remarks that were widely reported as "shocking."

### 5.1 "Profit will gradually trend downward"

**[Fact]** Lei Chen did say, at the 2024 Q2 earnings call, that "the direction is that it will gradually trend downward" — this quote is on the full record, and multiple media outlets cite it consistently.

**[Fact]** This remark directly triggered the -28.51% single-day decline, a verifiable market event.

**[Room for interpretation]** Whether this remark is "shocking" depends on the standard applied: from a transparency standpoint, this is a rare case of proactively lowering profit guidance; from a long-term standpoint, it may have been an attempt to manage investor expectations ahead of time.

---

### 5.2 "No buybacks or dividends for the next few years"

**[Fact]** Lei Chen did say this at the Q2 2024 earnings call; the original quote was: "Now is not the right time for share buybacks or dividends. And for the foreseeable next few years, we don't see the need for it either."

**[Fact]** At the time, the company held more than RMB 284.9 billion in cash, with operating cash flow of RMB 43.793 billion. Ample cash reserves coexisting with "no need for buybacks" is at the core of investor controversy.

---

### 5.3 "Three years to cover ten years' ground" (on Temu)

**[Fact]** Jiazhen Zhao's original quote at the 2025 Q4 earnings call was "three years to cover the same ground that took PDD's domestic e-commerce business ten years," referring to Temu's expansion into 90-plus countries within three years.

**[Note]** This statement describes Temu's expansion speed, not a profit or profitability claim, which cannot be verified. Coverage of 90-plus countries is a verifiable business fact.

---

### 5.4 "Simply evaluating performance by short-term returns is no longer well suited"

**[Fact]** Jiazhen Zhao did say this at the 2025 Q4 earnings call; it has been independently cited by multiple media outlets with consistent wording.

**[Context]** This remark came against a backdrop of slowing profit growth and pressure on the share price — sensitive timing.

---

### Overall assessment

| Remark | On the record? | Media exaggeration? |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Profit entering a downward trend | ✅ On record | No — the original statement itself was already a rare disclosure |
| No buybacks or dividends for the next few years | ✅ On record | No — the original is clear, and given the scale of cash on hand it is indeed rare |
| High growth is unsustainable | ✅ On record | No — the CEO publicly acknowledged this as proactive disclosure |
| Three years to cover ten years' ground | ✅ On record | Some simplification — the original refers to the number of markets covered, not profitability data |
| Short-term returns no longer an appropriate yardstick | ✅ On record | No, but understanding it requires reading the full context |

**Conclusion**: all of the above remarks are on the record and were not fabricated by the media. The controversy lies in this: management proactively signaled negative expectations while the share price and results were under pressure — the opposite of the market norm (which favors good news). Whether this is telling it straight, or management deliberately talking down expectations and managing valuation, cannot be determined from public information alone.
