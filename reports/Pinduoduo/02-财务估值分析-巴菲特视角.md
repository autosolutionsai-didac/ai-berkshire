| 2021 | 939.5 | +61% | ~13 | Turned profitable | ~69 |
| 2022 | ~1,306 | +28% | ~46 | +254% | ~83 |
| 2023 | 2,476.4 | +84% | ~85 | +85% | ~148 |

| 2025 | 4,318.5 | +10% | ~136 (RMB 99.4bn) | **-12%** | ~135 (RMB 94.6bn, **-13%**) |

**Key findings**:
- Explosive growth from 2021-2024, roughly 43% CAGR over four years
- **Clear inflection point in 2025**: revenue growth decelerated sharply to 10%, and net profit declined for the first time, down 12%
- Main driver of the profit decline: a sharp rise in sales & marketing and R&D expenses tied to Temu's global expansion
