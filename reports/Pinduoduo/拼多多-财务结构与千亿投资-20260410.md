### 1.2 The nature of the two businesses

**Online marketing services (advertising)**: merchants place ads on Pinduoduo/Temu to gain traffic. This is "traffic monetization" with an extremely high gross margin (estimated at 80%+), similar in business model to Baidu's search advertising.

**Transaction services (commissions)**: the platform takes a commission on every transaction. The 108% surge in 2024 was mainly due to commission recognition under Temu's fully-managed model. Growth collapsed to 9% in 2025, reflecting slowing Temu growth plus fee waivers to merchants under the 100-billion-yuan support program.

**Key takeaway**: the two businesses each account for roughly 50% of revenue — a balanced structure. Advertising is the profit cash cow; transaction services is the growth engine.

## IV. A Detailed Look at the 100-Billion-Yuan Investment Program

### 4.1 Basic facts

- **Announced**: early April 2025
- **Scale**: over **100 billion yuan** invested over the next three years (not 50 billion — a genuine 100-billion-yuan-scale commitment)
- **Official positioning**: building "a win-win-win commercial ecosystem for users, merchants, and the platform"

### 4.2 Where is the money going? Three directions

**Direction one: agricultural supply chain — "Duo Duo Specialty Products"**
- Deep investment in over a hundred agricultural specialty regions nationwide (Lianjiang abalone, Meizhou pomelo, Yunnan coffee, Guizhou prickly pear, Zhaotong potatoes, etc.)
- Investment in agricultural product standardization, grading and packaging, cold-chain storage, and direct-from-origin logistics
- Goal: cut the farm-to-table loss rate for agricultural products from 30% to below 10%

**Direction two: logistics reaching lower-tier areas — "E-commerce Goes West"**
- Waiving logistics transfer fees in western regions
- Breaking down logistics barriers between counties and townships
- Extending "free-shipping zone" coverage to remote administrative villages
- Goal: connecting China's last "logistics islands" to the national e-commerce network

**Direction three: merchant ecosystem — "new-quality supply" + "10-billion-yuan rebate"**
- 10 billion yuan in merchant coupons
- Waivers on multiple service fees, lower deposit and withdrawal thresholds
- Promoting supply-chain branding and quality upgrades
- Easing the operating burden on small and midsize merchants, offsetting the negative impact of the "refund-only" policy

### 4.3 What happens once this is built out?

**Short-term impact (2025, already happened)**:
- Profit directly compressed: operating profit -13%, Q1 margin fell from 29% to 17%
- Merchant sentiment improved: the 100-billion-yuan support program was seen as a response to the "refund-only" controversy
- Share price volatility: negative short-term market reaction, but the stock rebounded after Q4 profit recovery

**Medium-term impact (2026-2027, expected)**:

| Effect | Specific manifestation | Financial impact |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Merchant retention/growth | More quality merchants onboarding, richer SKU selection | Faster ad revenue growth (more merchants → higher bidding-based ad revenue) |
| Higher average order value | "New-quality supply" drives product-quality upgrades, users willing to spend more | Transaction-service commission growth |
| Agricultural GMV breakout | Supply-chain efficiency gains → lower prices/better quality → higher repeat purchase | Agricultural products become a new growth driver |
| Deeper penetration of lower-tier markets | Users in remote western regions activated | Both user count and purchase frequency rise |
| Widening gap versus competitors | JD.com/Taobao struggle to replicate Pinduoduo's depth of investment in agriculture and lower-tier markets | Market share further consolidated |

**Long-term impact (2027+, outlook)**:
- If the 100-billion-yuan investment succeeds, Pinduoduo will upgrade from "low-price e-commerce" to "China's agriculture + lower-tier-market infrastructure"
- Once built, the agricultural supply chain is a very deep moat — logistics networks, cold chain, and origin relationships all require years of accumulation
- Analogy: JD.com's self-built logistics was questioned by the market at the time, but once built it became a core barrier. Pinduoduo's 100-billion-yuan investment follows similar logic

### 4.4 One-year results (March 2026 data)
