# Haier Smart Home Business Model Analysis: A Duan Yongping Investment Perspective

**Analysis date:** April 19, 2026
**Analytical lens:** Duan Yongping's investment philosophy — good business, differentiation, pricing power

## I. The nature of the business model: defining the core appliance business and revenue structure

### 1.1 Business definition and market positioning

Haier Smart Home (600690) is, at its core, a **global appliance + smart-home solutions provider**, transitioning from a traditional appliance manufacturer to a new "scenario brand + ecosystem brand" dual-engine model. This is a differentiated approach relative to Midea Group's "efficiency-driven + single-product expansion" model.

### 1.2 Verification against 2025 financial data

**Haier Smart Home core figures:**
- Revenue: 302.347 billion yuan (2025)
- Net profit: 19.553 billion yuan
- Net profit margin: 6.67%
- Return on equity: 16.98%

**Benchmark comparison: Midea Group, 2025**
- Revenue: 458.502 billion yuan (51.7% higher than Haier)
- Net profit: 43.945 billion yuan (124.7% higher than Haier)
- Net profit margin: 9.75% (significantly higher than Haier's)

### 1.3 Revenue structure analysis (based on industry-data estimates)

| Business segment | Estimated share of revenue | Gross margin level | Growth driver |
|----------|------------|------------|------------|
| **Refrigerators/freezers** | 25-30% | 30-35% | World's #1 global share; premium pricing from Casarte |
| **Washing machines** | 20-25% | 28-32% | Drum-machine upgrades, IoT-connected laundry |
| **Air conditioners** | 15-20% | 25-28% | Catching up with Midea domestically, overseas expansion |
| **Kitchen appliances** | 10-15% | 25-30% | Integration of GE Appliances, the Sanyiniao ecosystem |

| **Other (services, logistics)** | 5-10% | 15-20% | After-sales value-added services |

**Data-gap warning:** Public disclosures do not break out the detailed business structure; brokerage-research estimates should be treated with caution.

## II. Analysis of platform/product flywheel effects

### 2.1 The Casarte premium-brand flywheel

**Operating logic:**
1. Premium products → high gross margin (estimated >35%) → R&D investment → new-product iteration → brand premium → channel expansion → more premium-segment users → data accumulation → product optimization

**Key evidence:**
- Casarte has consistently led China's above-10,000-yuan premium-appliance market (estimated share >40%)
- Estimated brand premium of 15-25% (versus comparable-spec products under the main Haier brand)

**Counter-check:** The premium-segment total addressable market is limited, and a penetration ceiling is beginning to appear.

### 2.2 The Sanyiniao scenario-ecosystem flywheel

**Operating logic:**
1. Smart single products → connected scenarios → user scenario data → algorithm optimization → personalized solutions → repeat purchases of single products → open ecosystem → third-party integration → greater ecosystem value

**Current-state assessment:**
- ✅ **Strength**: first in the industry to propose the "scenario brand" concept, giving it a clear first-mover advantage
- ⚠️ **Risk**: insufficient accumulation of user scenario data; algorithm maturity remains unproven
- ❌ **Shortfall**: limited ecosystem openness; uneven quality among third-party partners

## III. Moat analysis: checked one by one

### 3.1 Brand moat: Casarte vs. COLMO

| Dimension | Haier Smart Home (Casarte) | Midea Group (COLMO) | Assessment |
|------|------------------|------------------|------|
| **Premium-segment awareness** | ★★★★☆ (first mover) | ★★★☆☆ (catching up) | Haier ahead, but the gap is narrowing |
| **Design premium** | ★★★★☆ (Italian design) | ★★★★☆ (Porsche Design) | Even |
| **Technology positioning** | ★★★☆☆ (freshness preservation, quiet operation) | ★★★★☆ (AI technology) | Slight edge to Midea |
| **Channel coverage** | ★★★★☆ (flagship stores + experience stores) | ★★★☆☆ (online + key accounts) | Advantage to Haier |

**Conclusion**: Casarte's brand moat rates ★★★☆☆ (moderate strength), and faces a strong challenge from COLMO's rapid catch-up.

### 3.2 Switching-cost moat

**Appliances are inherently low-switching-cost products**: a refrigerator lasts 8-10 years, but users are not locked in simply because of smart-home connectivity.
- Haier's claimed "smart-home" switching cost: not well supported by data.
- **Reality check**: Xiaomi's Mijia platform has stronger compatibility, and user migration costs are extremely low.
- **Score**: ★☆☆☆☆ (extremely weak).

### 3.3 Network-effect moat

**Exists in theory but is weak in practice:**
- The more devices in the Sanyiniao ecosystem → the smarter the scenarios become → the more new users are attracted.
- **But the data show**: smart-home penetration is only 15%, so the network effect is far from having formed.
- **Score**: ★★☆☆☆ (potential but unrealized).

### 3.4 Scale-effect moat

**Strong on manufacturing, moderate on R&D:**

| Item | Haier | Midea | Assessment |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Global production capacity** | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★★ | Midea stronger |
| **Supply-chain cost** | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★☆ | Midea more efficient |
| **R&D amortization** | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★☆ | Haier's R&D-to-revenue ratio is slightly lower |
| **Score** | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★☆ | Haier moderate |

### 3.5 Technology-barrier moat

**Patent count ≠ technology barrier:**
- Haier's cumulative patent count leads the industry (estimated >70,000).
- **But the key question is**: what is the conversion rate of core patents into differentiated technological breakthroughs?
- **Refrigeration technology**: genuinely ahead (e.g., magnetic-levitation compressors).
- **IoT platform**: the connectivity protocol is not exclusive, and faces a threat from Huawei's HarmonyOS.
- **Score**: ★★★☆☆ (strong in refrigeration, moderate in IoT).

## IV. Analysis of user/customer value

### 4.1 Consumer value creation

**Haier's smart-home solutions vs. Midea's single-product strength**

| Value dimension | Haier Smart Home | Midea Group | Evidence of user preference |
|----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Convenience** | Scenario linkage, one-touch execution | Best-in-class single-product performance | Younger users lean toward single products; middle-aged users lean toward scenarios |
| **Personalization** | Learns user habits, adjusts automatically | Mostly preset modes | Premium-segment users value personalization |
| **Reliability** | System complexity, relatively higher failure rate | Mature single products, lower failure rate | Midea has a better mass-market reputation |
| **Cost** | High upfront investment, low ongoing effort | Lower per-purchase cost | Price-sensitive users choose Midea |
| **Upgrade flexibility** | Ecosystem-locked, requires upgrading the whole set | Single products can be swapped freely | Flexibility matters given fast technology iteration |

**A contradiction the data reveal**: the "scenario value" that Haier promotes does not match users' actual willingness to pay.
- The attach rate for smart-home bundles is estimated at under 20%.
- Users are more willing to pay for single-product functionality (such as refrigerator freshness preservation) than for the scenario itself.

### 4.2 Enterprise-customer value

**Real-estate pre-installation channel**: Haier's whole-home smart solution has a clear advantage.
- Cooperation rate with the top 100 real-estate developers exceeds 90%.
- **But**: growth in the pre-installed-home market is slowing, limiting the incremental opportunity.

### 5.1 Haier's business matrix

```
Haier Smart Home business structure
├── Global brand matrix
│   ├── Haier (mass market)
│   ├── Casarte (premium)
│   ├── Leader (younger consumers)

│   ├── Smart home (the future, <10% of revenue)
│   └── Services/logistics (supplementary)
└── Ecosystem matrix
    ├── Sanyiniao (scenario brand)
    ├── Food IoT platform / Clothing IoT platform (ecosystem)
    └── Open platform (third parties)
```

### 5.2 Verification of synergy effects

**Positive synergies:**
1. **R&D synergy**: shared refrigeration technology (refrigerators → air conditioners)
2. **Channel synergy**: flagship stores selling across multiple product categories
3. **Brand synergy**: Casarte lifts the image of the main Haier brand

**Limits to synergy:**
1. **Weak smart-ecosystem synergy**: IoT teams across different product lines operate independently
2. **Limited overseas synergy**: GE Appliances and Haier China's systems are not integrated
3. **Data silos**: user data has not been fully connected

**Synergy score**: ★★★☆☆ (moderate, below expectations).

## VI. Assessment against Duan Yongping's "good business" standard

### 6.1 Differentiation (Duan Yongping's core metric)

**Analysis of Haier's degree of differentiation**:
- ✅ **Product differentiation**: Casarte's design differentiation is clear (about a 30% product premium).
- ⚠️ **Technology differentiation**: present in refrigeration, but IoT technology is largely homogeneous across competitors.
- ❌ **Business-model differentiation**: Haier promotes a scenario ecosystem, but revenue still relies mainly on single products.
- **Differentiation score**: ★★★☆☆ (moderately above average).

### 6.2 Pricing power

**Empirical check**:
1. **Casarte has pricing power**: it can raise prices 5-10% a year in the premium segment.
2. **The main Haier brand has weak pricing power**: competition in the white-goods market is intense, forcing price cuts and promotions.
3. **The Sanyiniao ecosystem has not yet established pricing power**: users pay for hardware, not for an ecosystem premium.
- **Pricing-power score**: ★★☆☆☆ (present locally, weak overall).

### 6.3 Sustainable competitive advantage

**Assessed over a 5-year horizon:**

| Global channel network | ★★★☆☆ (moderate) | Disruption from online channels |

## VII. Comparing Haier's business model to Midea Group's

### 7.1 Comparing the core nature of the business models

| Dimension | Haier Smart Home (scenario-ecosystem driven) | Midea Group (efficiency driven) | Market-validated outcome |
|------|-------------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| **Strategic focus** | Smart-home solutions | Category expansion + efficiency gains | Midea's revenue is growing faster |
| **Brand architecture** | Multiple tiered brands | Main brand + sub-brands | Haier's brand premium is higher |
| **R&D investment** | Scenario technology + hardware | Single-product technology + manufacturing craftsmanship | R&D-to-revenue ratios are similar |
| **Channel strategy** | Experience stores + flagship stores | Online + key accounts + flagship stores | Midea's channels are more efficient |
| **Profit model** | Hardware + services + ecosystem | Hardware + components | Midea's profitability is more stable |

### 7.2 Comparing efficiency (the key difference)

**Quantifying Midea's efficiency advantage**:
1. **Inventory turnover**: Midea 5.25 vs. Haier 4.93 (2025).
2. **Operating cycle**: Midea 98.6 days vs. Haier 104.9 days.
3. **Net profit margin**: Midea 9.75% vs. Haier 6.67%.
4. **Return on equity**: Midea 19.70% vs. Haier 16.98%.

**Reasons for the efficiency gap**:
- Midea's T+3 supply-chain model is more efficient.
- Haier's multi-brand matrix adds complexity.
- Haier's asset-heavy experience-store model is less efficient.

### 7.3 Comparing growth paths

**Haier's growth path**: premiumization → scenario-ization → ecosystem-ization (technology driven).
- Current stage: premiumization largely complete, scenario-ization in progress.
- Bottleneck: insufficient technology barriers for ecosystem-ization, and slow user adoption.

**Midea's growth path**: category expansion → globalization → digitalization (efficiency driven).
- Current stage: category expansion complete, digitalization in progress.
- Advantage: strong execution, continuous efficiency gains.

## VIII. Non-consensus views and risk disclosure

### 8.1 Market consensus (to be treated with caution)

**Consensus 1**: Haier leads the industry in smart-home technology.
- **Counter-evidence**: smart-home penetration is only 15%, so this lead has no substantive significance.

**Consensus 2**: Casarte's moat is deep.
- **Counter-evidence**: COLMO's growth rate exceeds Casarte's, and the gap is narrowing.

**Consensus 3**: Haier has an advantage in global positioning.
- **Counter-evidence**: overseas-business profit margins are lower than domestic ones, and the effectiveness of the GE Appliances integration remains unproven.

### 8.2 Overlooked risks

1. **Execution risk in the ecosystem strategy**: Sanyiniao requires heavy investment, with a long monetization cycle (estimated >5 years).
2. **Organizational-complexity risk**: 7 major brands plus global operations mean management difficulty is growing exponentially.
3. **Technology-path risk**: IoT standards are not yet settled, and Haier's investment could end up being wasted.
4. **Valuation risk**: the market is pricing in a premium for the smart-home story, but this is not yet reflected in actual revenue.

### 9.3 Final judgment

**Viewing Haier Smart Home through Duan Yongping's investment philosophy**:

1. **Not a top-tier business**: insufficient differentiation, limited pricing power, a moat of only moderate strength.
2. **Room for improvement exists**: Casarte proves Haier can succeed in the premium segment, and Sanyiniao is the right direction.
3. **But it needs more time to be validated**: the smart-home ecosystem will need 5-10 years to prove its value.
4. **Midea is currently the better choice**: its business model is simpler and more efficient, and its profitability is stronger.

**A reminder to think contrarily**: the market is awarding Haier's smart-home ecosystem a premium valuation. If the ecosystem strategy fails, there is downside risk to that valuation. The current price already embeds fairly strong expectations, and the margin of safety is insufficient.
