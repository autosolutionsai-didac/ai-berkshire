## 1. Core Judgment

**Moat strength: High (data center inference) / Medium (edge) / Low-to-medium (China)**. NVIDIA still holds a **60-75% share** of the AI inference market (FY2026 data center revenue of $194B, roughly 86% share of the overall market), but this is starting to diverge from training (>90%). **The deepest moat comes from 18 years of software-stack accumulation across CUDA + TensorRT-LLM + NIM** — on identical hardware, the software stack alone contributes a 2.6-10x performance gap. Over the next three years NVIDIA faces a three-front squeeze: AMD's MI355X overtaking on TCO, hyperscaler-custom ASICs (Maia/TPU/Trainium/MTIA) ramping at a 44.6% growth rate, and Huawei Ascend climbing from 35% to 50%+ share in China. **We expect NVIDIA's inference share to fall to 50-55% by 2030, while absolute revenue still doubles**. The $20B acquisition of Groq (December 2025) is a key defensive move against the ASIC threat.

## 3. NVIDIA's Inference Software Stack (the deepest moat)

### TensorRT (2017)
General-purpose GPU inference optimizer with FP8/FP4 quantization, kernel fusion, and dynamic shapes. 5-10x faster than native PyTorch.

### TensorRT-LLM (2023-09)
Purpose-built for LLMs: in-flight batching, paged KV cache, speculative decoding, multi-GPU tensor/pipeline parallelism. Supports 100+ mainstream models.

### Triton Inference Server
Unified serving across multiple models and frameworks (PyTorch/TF/ONNX/TRT-LLM/vLLM).

### NIM (NVIDIA Inference Microservices, 2024-03)
**The most important recent product**: containerized, one-line deployment, bundling TensorRT/TensorRT-LLM/vLLM/SGLang across multiple backends.
- **2025-12 data**: NIM running Llama 3.1 8B on H100 hits 1,201 tokens/s, versus 613 tokens/s bare-metal (a 2.6x improvement)
- Available on AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure
- Enterprise customers: Lowe's, Siemens, Box, Cohesity, Dropbox, NetApp, Hippocratic AI, Glean

**The moat logic**: CUDA → TensorRT → TensorRT-LLM → NIM forms an ever-ascending "performance + ease of use" flywheel — **on identical H100 hardware, NVIDIA's software stack delivers 2-3x more effective compute than competitors**.

## 4. Five Dimensions of NVIDIA's Inference Moat

### 4.1 Hardware performance lead (medium moat)
- B200 vs MI300X: 9 PFLOPS vs 5.2 PFLOPS on FP8 (1.7x)
- GB200 NVL72 vs any competitor: 72-GPU full NVLink interconnect, 30x H100 trillion-parameter inference
- But AMD's MI355X has already matched B200 on FP8/FP4

### 4.2 Software-stack maturity (the deepest moat)
- TensorRT has 18 years of iteration; TensorRT-LLM shipped 30+ versions in 13 months
- vLLM CI data: AMD ROCm's pass rate on vLLM tests rose from 37% in November 2025 to 93% in January 2026
- At batch size 1-4 (low latency), H100 + TRT-LLM delivers 20-30% higher throughput than MI300X + vLLM

### 4.3 Model ecosystem (high moat)
Hugging Face defaults to NVIDIA, and every leading open-source model launches with its optimizations on NVIDIA first.

### 4.4 Customer inertia + installed base (high moat)
5M+ Hopper and 1M+ Blackwell GPUs deployed globally. **Switching cost**: migrating an inference workload worth tens of millions of dollars a year to ROCm typically takes 6-12 months with a 10-30% performance loss.

### 4.5 Supply-chain advantage (medium moat)
Priority allocation on TSMC's 4N/3nm nodes, dual-sourced HBM3E/HBM4 from SK Hynix and Samsung, and 70%+ of global CoWoS capacity.

## 11. Investment Implications (Buffett's lens)

**Moat depth ranking**: software ecosystem > customer inertia > model ecosystem > hardware performance > supply chain. **The software stack is the true bedrock of NVIDIA's valuation** — its hardware lead runs 1-2 years, but its software lead runs 3-5 years.

**Risk points**:
1. Hyperscaler ASICs will ramp heavily in 2027-2028
2. If AMD's MI400 series pushes ROCm/vLLM to full price parity with TRT-LLM
3. The China market is essentially lost for good
4. Pricing elasticity is already visible: B200 discounts are showing up in cloud customer contracts

**Conclusion**: NVIDIA's position as the "leader in AI inference infrastructure" should hold at least through 2028-2029. **Vera Rubin / Rubin Ultra is the key thing to watch** — if Rubin Ultra lands on schedule in H2 2027 and performs as expected, the moat can be extended another 3 years.
