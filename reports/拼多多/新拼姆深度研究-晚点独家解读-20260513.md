## Part One: Key Information From LatePost's Exclusive Article

### 1. What New PinMu Actually Does

**Core definition**: a "private-label retailer" model combining a Pinduoduo house brand, contract manufacturing from big-name factories, and sales through Temu's overseas channel.

**A key correction**: New PinMu is not an upgrade to the domestic e-commerce business — its products **are sold only in the US and Europe** and are not listed domestically.

Specifics:
- The platform creates its own house brands and contracts manufacturing out to well-known factories such as Bosideng, Youngor, and Camel
- The platform provides detailed product specifications, and factories manufacture to those instructions
- **Buyout / exclusive distribution**: the platform sets its own pricing and marketing, and the goods may not be resold to third parties
- Settlement structure: raw-material cost + a processing-fee rebate + a sales rebate
- Suppliers' gross margin runs roughly 10%-20%

### 2. Category Selection and Pricing

**Categories selected**: apparel, home goods, and outdoor gear (apparel accounts for about 40% of Temu's GMV, home goods about 20%)

**Sub-categories**: down jackets, shell jackets, shirts, dress trousers, and the like, expected to launch in Q3

**Categories ruled out**: food (shelf life), beauty (compliance certification), and consumer electronics (complex technical standards)

**Pricing strategy**:
- Retail price is roughly 4-5x production cost
- Priced at 50-70% of comparable big-brand products, or even lower
- Positioning: "the best value at a given quality level" — not the cheapest option, but a trustworthy, mid-tier quality choice

### 3. Temu "Gets Heavier"

| Dimension | Temu Before | New PinMu Model |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Product sourcing | Merchants supply independently | Platform creates its own brand, buys out and distributes exclusively |
| Quality control | Platform spot-checks | Platform designates factories and provides product specifications |
| Inventory risk | Borne by merchants | **Borne by the platform** |
| Pricing | Set by merchants | **Set by the platform** |
| Branding | Unbranded / white-label | **Platform's own house brand** |
| Price band | Mostly under $10 | More items priced above $20 |

Background: after the US eliminated the small-parcel duty-free exemption (de minimis), the volume of sub-$10 items dropped sharply, forcing Temu to move upmarket.

### 4. Team and Organization

- **An independent team of about 100 people**; most Pinduoduo/Temu employees don't even know it exists
- The lead goes by the internal alias **"Beimo,"** a former Tencent product manager whose main prior achievement was Kuaituantuan (GMV holding steady above RMB 300 billion+)
- Multiple teams compete under a **"horse race" system**
- Currently at **version 1.0**, expected to take 1-2 years to mature before gradually expanding into more categories
- Internal positioning: "a necessary investment for Temu's continued growth, not yet another standalone venture"

### 5. Key Differences From Shein

| Dimension | New PinMu | Shein |
|------|--------|-------|
| Approach | Doesn't chase fashion trends | Fast fashion |
| Lead time | 15-30 days | 7 days |
| Per-SKU order volume | 10,000+ units | A few hundred units |
| Quality-control method | Upfront vetting + after-the-fact penalties | Deploys teams on-site at factories |
| Compliance certification | Factories must handle it themselves (takes 2-3 months) | Handled by the platform |

### 6. Management Mechanisms

- Suppliers are managed under a **points system**, with order volume adjusted — and suppliers eliminated — based on their point totals
- Carrying forward a rule from Colin Huang: "everyone starts with a credit line like a credit card — you build up your limit only through sustained delivery, and a single mistake on something critical wipes the limit to zero"
- A single annual contract can run into the tens of millions of RMB, or even over RMB 100 million

## Part Two: Business Model Analysis (Duan Yongping's Lens)

### Core Judgment: 3.15 / 5

**The positives:**

1. **The business-model logic is clear**: the New PinMu model = an online Costco (radically curated SKUs + quality endorsement + value-driven pricing). Duan Yongping himself has said it "will be quite formidable in 10 years"
2. **Scale effects are the strongest moat**: 20,000 employees generate RMB 430 billion in revenue — the best per-employee productivity in the industry
3. **The value created for manufacturing clusters is real**: it gives white-label factories a path to branding, which carries significant social value

**The negatives:**

1. **Brand is a conditional asset**: it's an asset in lower-tier markets but a liability in the context of brand-driven consumption upgrading. New PinMu is trying to redefine "cheap" as "great value," but that will take more than five years
2. **Switching costs are extremely low**: neither users nor merchants face a strong lock-in mechanism, making this the weakest link in the moat
3. **The pricing-power paradox**: the platform has pricing power over merchants (it collects commissions) but none over consumers (it must keep prices low). The good businesses Duan Yongping invests in — Moutai, Apple — can raise prices without losing customers; Pinduoduo is exactly the opposite

**Duan Yongping's own characterization**: Pinduoduo is a "venture bet" rather than a "value investment" — less certain than Apple or Moutai, but potentially offering better odds.

**Core Management Issues**:
1. Colin Huang has been out of day-to-day management for four years, and the co-CEO structure's effectiveness remains untested
2. Zhao Jiazhen was personally fined RMB 6.94 million over the "ghost takeaway" case — a sign of serious compliance shortfalls in the corporate culture
3. A violent law-enforcement obstruction incident: an enforcement officer was deliberately injured by having his hand caught in a door, fracturing his left index finger — a major negative signal about management character
4. RMB 420 billion in cash, zero dividends, zero buybacks, with management explicitly stating it "will not return capital to shareholders for the foreseeable future"

**ESG rating**: MSCI CCC (the lowest in the industry), versus AA for JD.com and Baidu, and BBB for Alibaba

**Three Irreversible Risks**:
1. Entrenched brand perception: the "Pinduoduo = cheap counterfeits" association is extremely hard to reverse
2. Structural global trade barriers: the elimination of de minimis is irreversible
3. A management trust crisis: violent obstruction of law enforcement, zero shareholder returns, and a lack of information transparency compounding one another

### Strategic Assessment of New PinMu

**What New PinMu really is**: not Pinduoduo's "second growth curve," but Temu's forced upgrade under tariff barriers — a shift from "low-price white-label direct mail" to "localized, mid-tier house brands."

**Probability-of-Success Assessment**:

| Favorable Factors | Unfavorable Factors |
|---------|---------|
| China's supply chain still holds a manufacturing-cost advantage | Platforms running their own house brands is a poor genetic fit (neither JD's Jingzao nor NetEase Yanxuan succeeded) |
| Manufacturing capability from big-name factories such as Bosideng | Quality control relies on "upfront vetting + after-the-fact penalties" rather than on-site teams, creating significant quality risk |
| Temu already has a distribution network across 90 countries | Tariffs and compliance costs eat into the price advantage |
| $75.0 billion in cash is ample for trial and error | A 15-30 day lead time, versus just 7 days for Shein |
| A hundred-person elite team plus a "horse race" system | The team is too small, and its ability to scale across categories is unproven |

### Investment Thesis

**Bull Case:**
1. Ex-net-cash, the core business trades at just 5x P/E — extremely undervalued
2. Temu's profitability inflection is within sight (Goldman Sachs forecasts $3.2 billion in profit by 2027)
3. If New PinMu makes the "online Costco" model work, the business model would undergo a qualitative shift
4. Profitability remains the strongest in the industry (23% net margin, 30% ROE)
5. $75.0 billion in net cash provides a very thick safety cushion
6. Duan Yongping holds a position and has said it will be "quite formidable in 10 years"

**Bear Case:**
1. Growth collapsed from 59% to 10%, and net profit fell for the first time
2. Violent obstruction of law enforcement plus a CCC ESG rating raise doubts about governance quality
3. Douyin e-commerce is growing 30%+ and could surpass Pinduoduo's GMV within 1-2 years
4. The elimination of de minimis is a structural shock to Temu
5. Zero dividends, zero buybacks, and under the VIE structure, shareholders' actual "claim" on the cash is in doubt
6. New PinMu faces multiple challenges: a poor organizational fit, quality-control risk, and lead-time constraints
