## Core open questions

### Question 1: Does PDD's moat genuinely exist? Will it still be there in 5 years?

**The contradiction in the current view:**
- PDD's differentiated features (group buying, the 10-Billion-Yuan Subsidy program, direct-from-farm produce) have been fully copied by competitors
- The deepest moat (WeChat's social graph) belongs to Tencent, not to PDD
- The low-price mindshare moat requires continuous "feeding" (subsidies) — would it collapse if subsidies stopped?
- User loyalty is weak, and switching costs are close to zero

**Questions that need research:**
- [ ] PDD's user repeat-purchase and retention data, compared against Taobao and JD.com
- [ ] Is there a formal agreement governing the WeChat-PDD relationship? Does Tencent hold a stake in PDD?
- [ ] Changes in PDD's penetration in lower-tier markets over the past 3 years
- [ ] How deep is the actual moat in the agricultural supply chain — how long would it take competitors to catch up?
- [ ] Is Douyin e-commerce's growth eating into PDD's market share? Specific data needed

### Question 2: When can Temu become profitable? Under what conditions?

**Currently known:**
- Operating loss of approximately 32 billion yuan in 2024, approximately 50 billion yuan in 2025
- The European market achieved partial profitability in 2025
- The U.S. market has been hit hard by tariffs; the original model (duty-free plus direct air-mail shipping) no longer holds
- UBS forecast: quarterly breakeven by end of 2026, roughly 6% margin by 2029

**Questions that need research:**
- [ ] What are the unit economics of the semi-managed model versus the fully-managed model respectively? Which has better profitability prospects?
- [ ] Progress on building overseas warehouses — which countries currently have warehouses, and at what scale?
- [ ] Latest developments on U.S. tariffs — is there any possibility the 145% tariff gets reduced? Status of U.S.-China negotiations
- [ ] Specific reasons for European profitability — is it due to lower logistics costs, or a more favorable competitive landscape?
- [ ] What is SHEIN's profitability model — can Temu draw on it as a reference? Is SHEIN actually profitable?
- [ ] In which countries/regions is Temu's model closest to sustainable profitability?

---

### Question 3: When can New PDD Manufacturing succeed? What are the markers of success?

**Currently known:**
- Phase one: 15 billion yuan; 100 billion yuan over three years; established in Shanghai
- Goal: integrate PDD and Temu's supply chains, incubate Chinese self-owned brands aimed at the global market
- Analogy: upgrading from Yiwu-style white-label goods to Anker/DJI-level brands

**Core uncertainties:**
- PDD has no track record or genes for brand-building
- Going from white-label to branded took Anker 10 years and DJI 8 years
- Channel conflict: self-owned brands would compete against third-party merchants on the platform

**Questions that need research:**
- [ ] Which specific product categories does New PDD Manufacturing plan to pursue? Are there any already-announced category directions?
- [ ] What is New PDD Manufacturing's team composition — transferred from within PDD, or externally recruited brand/consumer-goods talent?
- [ ] Case studies of successful Chinese brands going global: what is the common path shared by Anker, DJI, and Shokz?
- [ ] Research on user perception of Temu — how do overseas users perceive the Temu brand? Is an upgrade even possible?
- [ ] How is the initial 15-billion-yuan phase-one funding being allocated — the split between supply-chain investment, brand marketing, and overseas warehouses?
