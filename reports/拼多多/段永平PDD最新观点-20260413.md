## 1. The Shift in Tone (Most Critical)

> "I never talked with Colin Huang about Pinduoduo, so I never really understood it well. AI is really something — it can watch videos like this, saving so much time, and suddenly I could roughly get it."

**Key information**:
- Duan Yongping **candidly admits** that his understanding of PDD's business model has long been **incomplete** (in contrast to his deep understanding of Apple and Tencent)
- In late 2025, using AI to watch Colin Huang's 2016-2019 interview videos, he "suddenly could roughly get it" — the tone shifted from "never really understood" to "gradually understanding"
- **Counterpoint**: Holding a massive PDD position while admitting he "still doesn't fully understand it" is itself a risk signal

## 2. Deep Endorsement of "New Pinmu" (the platform's private-label brand)

### March 27, 2026 — Xueqiu, verbatim
> "I think it's a natural progression for Pinduoduo to build its own online brand. They know exactly what sells in volume. They can take the highest-volume items and perfect them — both good quality and, once you strip out every layer of middlemen, a lower price to the end user. They only need to pick a handful of representative products to build their own brand around; they don't need every product to carry their own label. This is essentially the Costco playbook. If Pinduoduo's online brand ends up with only around 4,000 SKUs, it will be a formidable force in 10 years."

**Core judgment**:
- **Costco-model analogy** — roughly 4,000 curated SKUs, high value-for-money, cutting out middlemen
- **Time horizon: 10 years** — a classic Duan Yongping-style long-term view
- **Strategic positioning**: from "transaction platform" to "standard-setter" to "brand dominator"
- This marks a key shift in his repositioning of PDD from an "efficiency company" to a "brand company"

## 3. Attitude Toward No Dividends, No Buybacks

### March 29, 2026 — Xueqiu, verbatim (in reply to a critic)
> "Why are you worrying about this for nothing, or rather, why should they worry about it for a short-term trader like you? It's already been said as clearly as it can be on the conference call — read it carefully, and whether you get it or not is on you."

**Interpretation**:
- Slightly sarcastic, implying management already explained the use of cash clearly on the earnings call
- Frames investors who care about dividends and buybacks as "short-term traders"
- **Implicit message**: Duan Yongping accepts that PDD is putting its $60.4 billion in cash toward strategic investment (the RMB 10-billion-yuan-scale support program, Temu, "New Pinmu") rather than returning it to shareholders

**Counter-argument**:
- This "management already explained it, go read it yourself" response actually dodges the core question of "why not buy back stock"
- PDD's earnings calls have **not** contained an explicit commitment to a future capital-return plan
- Duan Yongping's explanation reads more as "self-rationalization" than as something grounded in fact

## 4. Attitude Toward Temu Tariff Risk

### March 5, 2025 — Xueqiu, verbatim
> "Raising tariffs is really just a disguised tax hike on ordinary people. Nothing else about it really matters."

**Interpretation**:
- A relaxed tone, framed from the consumer-burden angle
- But this comment came in March 2025, **before tariffs were actually escalated**
- The subsequent supply-chain pivot (from May 2025 onward, with the share of domestically shipped orders rising to ~90%) suggests **the actual impact was larger than Duan Yongping's initial judgment**
- **Risk**: Duan Yongping's read on Temu tariff risk may have been overly naive

## 9. Implications for the User's Decision

### Anchoring psychology
- Duan Yongping's cost basis is $100-110, and the current price is $100.87 → **following his lead now puts you at essentially the same cost basis**
- But note: Duan Yongping's portfolio allocation differs from yours — he also holds large positions in Apple, Kweichow Moutai, Tencent, and others
- **Don't simply build a heavy position just because "Duan Yongping bought it"** — his risk tolerance is not the same as yours

### Key questions you must answer for yourself
Duan Yongping hasn't resolved these — you need to answer them yourself:
1. **Will "New Pinmu" really become like Costco in 10 years?** — this is his core thesis, but it remains unverified
2. **Will the $60.4 billion in cash ultimately reach shareholders?** (given the VIE structure and taxes) — he sidesteps this question
3. **How large will the final impact of Temu tariffs turn out to be?** — his read may be too optimistic
4. **Is PDD's moat wide enough to withstand competition from Douyin/Kuaishou/Temu-Amazon?** — he himself admits it isn't wide enough

### Duan Yongping-style weighing of pros and cons
- **Pros**: Trustworthy management + clear long-term strategy + ample cash + reasonable valuation
- **Cons**: Even Duan Yongping doesn't fully understand the business model + he admits the moat isn't wide enough + his Temu tariff read may be wrong

### Conclusion
Duan Yongping's rating for PDD is "**trust-driven long-term holding**," not "**conviction-driven heavy position**." If your decision principle strictly requires "only buy what you understand," PDD may not meet your bar; if you can accept a position "partly resting on trust in management," you can follow his lead.
