### 4.1 Business Model (Duan Yongping Perspective)

**Domestic core platform** (85-90% probability of persisting over 10 years):
- 2025 GMV ≈ RMB 4.7 trillion, market share of approximately 19-22%, locked in a three-way contest with Taobao/Tmall and Douyin
- Estimated core platform operating margin of 35-40% (asset-light, no self-owned inventory)
- The take rate still has 1-1.5 percentage points of upside room, but is already close to its ceiling

**Duoduo Maicai** (Pinduoduo's community group-buying arm): marginal impact, best viewed as an option
- After Meituan Select exited in July 2025, monthly GMV grew +340% YoY

**Temu** (the biggest variable and biggest uncertainty over the next 10 years):
- 2025 GMV ~$90B, global MAU 416 million

**Duan Yongping's judgment**: PDD is a business that is "hard to think through ten years out" — the core platform resembles "a slightly lesser Coca-Cola," while Temu resembles "an internet casino."

### 4.3 Industry Landscape (Munger Perspective, supplemented by the team lead)

**China's Three-Way E-commerce Landscape (2025)**:
| Platform | Market Share | 2025 GMV (est.) | Growth Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taobao/Tmall (Alibaba) | 31% | RMB 8.3 trillion | Single digits |

**Key industry variables (10-year horizon)**:
1. **Content commerce eating into shelf-based e-commerce**: Douyin covered in 5 years what took Alibaba 20 years, and will continue eroding PDD's exclusive claim on the "low-price" mental positioning over the next 10 years
2. **Instant retail's rise**: the market reached RMB 3 trillion in 2025, with Meituan Instant Shopping's peak daily order volume hitting 120 million — **PDD is absent from this race**; Duoduo Maicai only began testing self-built warehouses in 2025
3. **AI agents disrupting recommendation algorithms**: ChatGPT already drives 20%+ of referral traffic for Walmart, 15% for Target, and 10% for eBay. The three major protocols — OpenAI's ACP, Anthropic's MCP, and Google's UCP — are taking shape in 2025-2026. **PDD's biggest moat, "low-price positioning plus recommendation algorithm," is being directly stripped away by AI-agent price comparison** — the platform is reduced from "decision-maker" to mere "fulfillment channel"
4. **Cross-border e-commerce pivots to Europe**: Temu, hit by US tariffs, has shifted toward Europe (>40% of GMV), but the EU will also eliminate its duty-free threshold in July 2026, plus potential DSA compliance fines (an estimated $3-6 billion)

**Analogous lessons**:
- The 20-year evolution of Walmart and Amazon in the US: shelf-based e-commerce doesn't disappear, but its growth settles into single digits
- Temu's path-risk is analogous to TikTok's: geopolitical variables dominate the commercial outcome

**A Munger-style inversion**: what would cause Pinduoduo's 2036 profit to shrink sharply? The answer is three independent threats acting at once: Douyin continuing to capture mindshare, AI agents rewriting traffic distribution, and Temu being dismantled in English-speaking markets. If any two of these occur, the bearish scenario becomes the base case.

## 5. Investment Thesis (Bull vs. Bear)

### 🟢 Bull Case (2036 profit → RMB 300 billion)

1. **Valuation already prices in the worst-case scenario**: ex-cash EV/E is only 6.2x, implying the market believes Temu is effectively dead
2. **A cash fortress of $60.4 billion**: 42.5% of market cap, providing very strong downside protection
3. **Successful semi-managed transition**: Temu is shifting from a "loss-making cash-burning machine" to an "asset-light platform," validated in the European market
4. **The domestic core platform remains a cash machine**: even in the bearish scenario, the core platform's RMB 70 billion in profit remains stable
5. **Not paying dividends is actually capital ammunition**: Colin Huang is preserving $60 billion+ in cash to leave room for Temu's future expansion and to hedge political risk
6. **Colin Huang's individual capability is exceptional**: evident in the foresight behind Temu's overseas expansion decision
7. **Duoduo Maicai's exclusive profitability**: the only nationwide player left standing after the brutal community group-buying wars

### 🔴 Bear Case (2036 profit → RMB 60 billion)

1. **Temu is fundamentally a political option, not a commercial asset**: 35% probability of being banned in the US
2. **The domestic growth myth has ended**: 2025 net profit declined 12%, the first such decline
3. **Management integrity flaws**: the April 2026 violent-resistance incident, opaque disclosure, refusal to buy back shares
4. **AI agents disrupting recommendation algorithms**: PDD has almost no AI positioning
5. **Irreversible erosion from Douyin e-commerce**: it covered in 5 years what took Alibaba 20 years
6. **PDD is absent from instant retail**: shelf-based e-commerce will inevitably cede some use cases over the next 10 years
7. **Colin Huang has stayed out of the public eye for 5 years**: actual control has become decoupled from public accountability, a governance risk
8. **RMB 284.9 billion in cash left untouched**: either the company doesn't care about shareholders, or it is extremely pessimistic about the future

## 8. Summary

**The most honest answer to the question "what will Pinduoduo's annual profit be in 2036" is: anywhere from RMB 60 billion to RMB 300 billion is possible, with a neutral forecast of RMB 170 billion.**

This 5x forecast range is not a failure of analytical rigor but a reflection of the structure of the question itself — Temu, a business accounting for 35-40% of current revenue, will have its fate over the next 10 years determined largely by US presidential executive orders, the intensity of EU DSA enforcement, and UFLPA entity-list decisions, all of which are political variables that commercial analysis cannot predict.

Excluding Temu and looking only at **the domestic core platform**: 2036 profit will most likely be RMB 70-190 billion, with a neutral estimate of RMB 120 billion — this portion of the business can genuinely be "thought through for ten years." At the current market cap of $142 billion, subtracting $60.4 billion in cash leaves $81.6 billion, against the core platform's forward profit of $17 billion (RMB 120 billion) — **implying a P/E of roughly 5x**, cheap at the level of a Buffett-style "cigar butt."

**Final judgment**:
- **If you can accept the worst-case scenario of "Temu going to zero"** — PDD at its current price is a cigar-butt worth buying
- **If you're looking for a core holding you can "sleep soundly with for 10 years"** — PDD does not meet the bar, because governance flaws and Temu uncertainty are unhedgeable tail risks
- **If you're betting on Temu's success** — this is a potential 10x return opportunity, but it is fundamentally a macro-and-political gamble, not Buffett-style investing

**The real ticking time bomb is not Temu**, but **where the $60.4 billion USD in cash will be allocated**. If Colin Huang continues refusing buybacks/dividends over the next 10 years, that cash is worth close to nothing to minority shareholders; if large-scale buybacks are launched, PDD's IRR could immediately rise 3-5 percentage points to 10-12%/year. **This is the single most important signal to track over the next 12-24 months.**
