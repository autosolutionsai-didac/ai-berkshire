### 1.4 "Reading Russell: Happiness and the Greed for Freedom"

> "Ever since middle school, I had imagined myself becoming a scientist, a statesman, an entrepreneur, or something along those lines."

In this piece, Colin Huang cites Bertrand Russell's "The Conquest of Happiness," emphasizing that rational judgment is superior to inherited bias, and stressing the importance of shifting one's attention from self-improvement toward outward interests.

### 1.6 "The Uncertainty of Love — Buddhism, Quantum Mechanics, Logic, and AI"

> "The world and life as a whole are unknowable — or at least cannot be measured precisely; they are subject to uncertainty."

### 1.8 "More Market, or More Planning — Some Thoughts on Supply-Side Reform"

(Published: September 22, 2017)

Colin Huang analyzes three categories of supply-side change, focusing in particular on "changes originating from the aggregation of front-end demand."

> Although traditional e-commerce made distribution-side information transparent, it "changed little for traditional manufacturing factories," which still depended on bulk orders from offline supermarkets and retailers.

Source: [The Code to Becoming China's Richest Man, Hidden in Colin Huang's Nine Articles — Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20240814A075QA00)

### 1.9 "Turning 'Capitalism' Upside Down"

(The final article on the WeChat account, 2017)

> "From these joint orders, factories gained a certainty of demand that they did not originally have."

In this piece, Colin Huang proposes the concept of "reverse insurance": consumers pool demand in advance (paying a small deposit), giving factories a guaranteed order, which in turn makes factories willing to offer a discount — a win-win for both buyers and sellers.

> "He [Buffett] knows clearly that money is not the goal. On the one hand, he enjoys the pleasure that the game of capital brings him; on the other, he has wisely donated the vast majority of his money to Bill Gates, who is younger than him, trusting Gates to carry out the redistribution that wealth ought to undergo."

Source: [A Brilliant Article Reflecting Pinduoduo Founder Colin Huang's Thinking: Turning Capitalism Upside Down — Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/352488291) | [Colin Huang Interprets Buffett: Turning Capitalism Upside Down — CSDN](https://blog.csdn.net/j2IaYU7Y/article/details/84801270)

## 2. Letters to Shareholders (2018–2021)

### 2.1 2018 Letter to Shareholders (Released with the IPO Prospectus, June 30, 2018)

> "Pinduoduo is not a traditional company. It was born at a time when everyone believed the e-commerce landscape was already settled, that the history books had already been written. In just three short years, it brought together more than 300 million users and over a million sellers to jointly build a new shopping model."

> "We hope Pinduoduo will be a public institution that exists to create value for the broadest possible base of users. It should not be a tool for showcasing any individual's ability, nor should it carry too much of any individual's personal imprint."

> "Pinduoduo will be a company that is bold enough to invest in the future and that takes the long view."

> "In the IPO prospectus, I solemnly wrote that we would aim to be a combination of Costco and Disney — and I certainly wasn't joking."

Colin Huang explains the five layers of meaning behind the value of "Benfen" (staying true to one's proper duty):
1. Be honest and trustworthy — become a person worthy of trust
2. Fulfill your own duties fully, regardless of what others are doing
3. Shut out external pressures, return to your original intent, and focus on doing what you should do
4. Don't take advantage of others, even when you could
5. When problems arise, look to yourself first for responsibility

Source: [Revisiting Colin Huang's Three Shareholder Letters: What Is Pinduoduo's "New World of the Future" — Jiemian News](https://www.jiemian.com/article/4304561.html) | [Interpreting Pinduoduo's Colin Huang: A Shareholder Letter Full of Philosophy and Poetry — GeekPark](https://www.geekpark.net/news/258959)

---

### 2.2 2019 Letter to Shareholders (Released April 25, 2019)

**Theme: Three Defining Features of the New E-Commerce**

> "The single biggest feature of the new e-commerce is 'inclusiveness.'"

> "The second feature of the new e-commerce is 'people first.' The new e-commerce no longer treats living, breathing people as mere traffic, and no longer turns the business model into wholesale trading of traffic."

> "The third feature of the new e-commerce is 'greater openness.'"

In the letter, Colin Huang opposes monopolistic competition and advocates for cooperation and competition that coexist and are oriented toward incremental innovation, arguing that "long-term exclusivity is bound to be broken."

Source: [Revisiting Colin Huang's Three Shareholder Letters: What Is Pinduoduo's "New World of the Future" — Jiemian News](https://www.jiemian.com/article/4304561.html)

---

### 2.3 2020 Letter to Shareholders (Released April 25, 2020, covering fiscal year 2019)

**Theme: The Pandemic, the Philosophy of Time, and the New World**

> "This is a once-in-a-century, extraordinary moment — and yet perhaps it is also nothing more than an utterly ordinary instant in the long river of history."

> "The boundary between the virtual world and the physical world has blurred as never before; we are beginning to see (not merely imagine) a new world arriving."

> "No matter how stubbornly we crave symmetry and eternity, time is forever generating every manner of asymmetry, irreversibility, and death in this world."

> "Time most likely should not be a reversible parameter in an equation; it is more like an irreversible vector."

> "Just as in human society, no matter how independent each individual may be, we all define our own existence — in both the real and the virtual world — through our interconnections with one another."

> "Any attempt to exploit others' hardship or take advantage of loopholes for one's own benefit looks extraordinarily foolish in the face of time."

> "We need to prove that our generation moves with the times and is different from those that came before."

> "For this reason, we will invest in the future with even greater resolve, and work hard to build the new world that lies ahead."

Source: [Interpreting Pinduoduo's Colin Huang: A Shareholder Letter Full of Philosophy and Poetry — GeekPark](https://www.geekpark.net/news/258959)

### 2.4 2021 Letter to Shareholders and Public Letter on Resignation as Chairman (March 17, 2021)

**Theme: Stepping Down as Chairman, Handing Over Power, and Exploring Science**

> "At the board meeting that just concluded, the directors approved my request to step down as chairman of Pinduoduo. Lei Chen will take over as chairman effective immediately, while continuing to serve as CEO."

> "Once I no longer hold the position of chairman or any management role at Pinduoduo, my 1:10 super-voting rights will also lapse. The voting rights attached to the shares under my name will be entrusted to Pinduoduo's board of directors to be decided by vote, and I personally commit that the shares under my name will remain locked up and will not be sold for the next three years."

> "Pinduoduo was still like a child just starting elementary school; yet now, though only three years have passed, Pinduoduo already seems like a teenager entering adolescence."

> "If we are to ensure its high-speed, high-quality development ten years from now, then certain explorations are already timely today. As the founder, stepping outside the company to feel out the stones on the road ten years ahead may make me the more suitable person for the job."

> "The increasingly fierce, even distorted competition within the industry has made me realize that this traditional form of competition, oriented mainly around scale and efficiency, has its own unavoidable problems. To change it, we must act on deeper, more fundamental issues, and seek answers in core technology and its underlying theory."

> "Becoming a true scientist is perhaps no longer very possible, but if I make the effort to combine the chemistry I loved most in middle school, the computer science I studied in university, and the management skills I've learned on the job... maybe I can still create something interesting."

> "I want to do some research in the fields of food science and life science... I may not become a scientist, but perhaps I'll have the chance to become an assistant to the (great) scientists of the future — and that too would be a very happy thing."

> "And now, in the blink of an eye, I have already passed the age of forty; becoming a true scientist is perhaps no longer very possible."

Source: [41-Year-Old Colin Huang Steps Down as Pinduoduo Chairman: I Want to Go Feel Out the Stones on the Road Ten Years From Now — Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/2021-03-17/doc-ikkntiam4156163.shtml) | [Colin Huang Steps Down as Pinduoduo Chairman: Turning to Basic Science Research — The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_11748812)

## 4. Thematic Quotes

### 4.1 On "Consumption Downgrade"

> "I don't like that term." (in response to being labeled with "consumption downgrade")

> "When it comes to material consumption, no one voluntarily downgrades. Everyone's the same — if you don't have a flush toilet, you want one."

> "It's like someone who never used paper towels before — once they start using them, that's an upgrade for them too."

Source: [We Combed Through 130,000 Words of Colin Huang's Interviews and Public Letters — Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/money/smjj/smdt/2021-03-27/doc-ikkntiam9247812.shtml)

---

### 4.2 On AI and Technology

> "Most technology today is centralized AI, which emphasizes efficiency. Distributed AI, by contrast, has no central neuron — it puts people into the loop, where every person is part of the neurons and influences the entire neural network."

> "This is very interesting — it's not just about using technology to improve efficiency, but about using technology to amplify each person's individuality. It lets people connect more deeply, gives them better ways to express themselves, and lets each person's individuality influence more things."

Source: [A Compilation of 17 Pieces — Pinduoduo Founder Colin Huang's Own Writings, Speeches, and Interview Transcripts, Series 3 — Woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/it/5722492.html)

---

### 4.3 On Entrepreneurship and Life

> "Starting a business is like playing golf — in both cases, you're competing with yourself. The scenario you face each time may differ, but the basic motion of the swing never changes. What's required is keeping a calm, ordinary state of mind and making the motion more standardized."

> "As you get older, nobody can keep starting businesses for their whole life."

> "Money is a tool, not a goal."

Source: [We Combed Through 130,000 Words of Colin Huang's Interviews and Public Letters — Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/money/smjj/smdt/2021-03-27/doc-ikkntiam9247812.shtml)
