# PDD Holdings (Pinduoduo) Business Model Deep-Dive Report
**——Duan Yongping's Investment Perspective | April 2026**

---

## I. The Essence of the Business Model: Defining the Core Business and Breaking Down the Revenue Structure

### 1.1 The Core Business in One Sentence

At its core, Pinduoduo is **a traffic allocator for China's manufacturing capacity** — using algorithms to efficiently match a vast, fragmented supply base (white-label factories) with price-sensitive demand, and monetizing through advertising fees and transaction commissions. Temu is the global replication of this same capability.

### 1.2 Revenue Structure Breakdown (FY2025)

| Revenue Category | Amount (RMB billion) | Share | YoY Growth | Description |
|---------|-------------------|------|---------|------|
| Online marketing services | 217.78 | 50.4% | +10% | Merchant bid-based advertising (CPC/CPM), search promotion |
| Transaction services | 214.06 | 49.6% | +9% | Main-site commissions + Duoduo Grocery + Temu commissions/markup |
| **Total** | **431.85** | **100%** | **+10%** | |

**Key findings:**
- Transaction services' share of revenue rose from roughly 30% in 2022 to 49.6% in 2025, reflecting **the scaling up of Temu and Duoduo Grocery**
- Online marketing services growth slowed to 10% (versus +28% in 2024), as domestic main-site advertising monetization approaches its ceiling
- Full-year 2025 non-GAAP net profit was 107.3 billion yuan, down 12% year-on-year — **the first time profit growth has turned negative**

### 1.3 Implied Temu Revenue Breakdown (Estimated)

PDD does not disclose Temu's revenue separately, but based on industry data it can be estimated as follows:

| Metric | 2024 Estimate | 2025 Estimate |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Temu GMV | ~$70.8 billion | ~$92.5 billion |
| Temu revenue (commissions + markup) | ~$20-25 billion | ~$30 billion+ |
| Share of PDD total revenue | ~35-40% | ~40-45% |

> **Non-consensus view**: the market tends to frame PDD as a "domestic e-commerce + overseas expansion" dual-engine story, but based on the revenue structure, Temu may already contribute more than 40% of revenue. PDD's valuation logic is shifting from "a Chinese e-commerce platform" to "a global supply-chain platform," and its risk exposure is likewise expanding to encompass global geopolitics.

**Business model essence score: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

## II. The Platform Flywheel Effect

### 2.1 Pinduoduo's Domestic Main-Site Flywheel

```
Low-price white-label goods → attracts price-sensitive users → massive order volume → thin-margin factories willing to cut prices further
→ even lower prices → more users → more merchants join the platform → more advertising revenue → subsidies sustain low prices
```

**Core driver of the flywheel: price** — not brand, experience, or service.

### 2.2 The Temu Flywheel

```
China's ultra-low-cost supply chain → Temu prices far below overseas competitors → social-sharing virality + massive marketing spend drive user acquisition
→ huge order volumes feed back into the supply chain → unit logistics costs fall as scale grows → semi-managed model/local warehousing shortens delivery times
→ repeat-purchase rates rise
```

### 2.3 Fundamental Differences from the Taobao/JD.com Flywheels

| Dimension | Pinduoduo | Taobao/Tmall | JD.com |
|------|--------|----------|------|
| **Core flywheel driver** | Extreme low price | Product breadth + brand | Logistics experience + quality trust |
| **Merchant relationship** | Platform holds pricing power; merchants are passive | Brands operate autonomously | Mix of self-operated and marketplace (POP) |
| **Source of user stickiness** | Price dependence | Brand loyalty + habit | Logistics certainty |
| **Flywheel's fragile point** | Deteriorating merchant ecosystem, race-to-the-bottom pricing | Traffic leaking to short video | Rigid cost structure |
| **Algorithmic logic** | Recommendation-led, weak search | Search and recommendation given equal weight | Search-led |

**Key distinction**: Pinduoduo's flywheel is **supply-side driven** — algorithms push factory output directly to users. It is extremely efficient in the "standardized products + white-label" segment, but is inherently weak in the "non-standardized + branded" segment.

**Flywheel effect score: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

## III. Moat Analysis — Verified One by One

### 3.1 Verifying the Five Classic Moat Types

| Moat type | Rating | Verdict | Detailed analysis |
|-----------|------|------|---------|
| **Brand** | ★★☆☆☆ | **False moat** | "Cheap" is not a brand premium — it's a brand discount. Users don't pay more just because the name is "Pinduoduo." |
| **Switching costs** | ★★☆☆☆ | **False moat** | Switching costs on e-commerce platforms are extremely low. Users have Taobao, Pinduoduo, and Douyin installed simultaneously; merchants operating on multiple platforms is the norm. |
| **Network effects** | ★★★☆☆ | **Weak moat** | Network effects on e-commerce platforms are inherently weaker than on social platforms — goods are homogeneous, and comparison shopping across platforms is rational behavior. The "team purchase" model has become vestigial. |
| **Scale effects** | ★★★★☆ | **Genuine moat** | The supply-chain bargaining power, logistics scale effects, and algorithmic data advantages that come from massive order volume are real. Duoduo Grocery's scale advantage in community group-buying (GMV of nearly 300 billion yuan) is a concrete manifestation of a competitive barrier. |
| **Technology barrier** | ★★★☆☆ | **Weak moat** | Recommendation algorithms and C2M capability offer some barrier, but they are not irreplicable. The real technological edge lies in **organizational efficiency** — 20,000 employees generating over 400 billion yuan in revenue. |

### 3.2 A "Hidden Moat" the Market Overlooks

**Supply-chain lock-in effect**: A large number of small and mid-sized factories have already reorganized their production lines around Pinduoduo's rules. The cost for these factories to switch to other platforms is not zero — product design, pricing strategy, and inventory management have all been "customized" for Pinduoduo.

### 3.3 A "Moat" the Market Overrates

**"Low-price mindshare" is not the same as a moat:**
1. Any competitor can wage a price war
2. Low-price mindshare limits category expansion and average order value growth
3. Once every platform is racing toward the bottom on price, low price stops being a point of differentiation

**Overall moat score: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

## IV. Analysis of Value Created for Users and Customers

### 4.1 Distinct Value Delivered to Each Stakeholder

| Stakeholder | Distinct value | Pain points/limitations |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Consumers** | Extreme value for money; the certainty of "no need to comparison-shop" low prices; direct access to farm produce; shopping access for lower-tier markets | High uncertainty over quality; poor after-sales experience; limited selection of branded goods |
| **White-label/factory merchants** | Direct-to-consumer, cutting out middlemen; massive order volume raises capacity utilization; Temu opens overseas markets | Razor-thin margins; total dependence on algorithmic traffic with no owned customer base; the mandatory "refund-only" policy has previously badly hurt merchants' interests |
| **Branded merchants** | Incremental users (lower-tier markets); a channel for clearing inventory | Damage to brand positioning; disruption of pricing structure |
| **Supply chain/logistics** | Certainty from scaled order volume | Extreme price pressure squeezes margins to the bone |

### 4.2 Key Shift: The 2025-2026 Turn in the Merchant Ecosystem

- **Abolishing mandatory "refund-only"**: merchants regain autonomy to handle after-sales
- **From tens of billions in fee waivers to hundreds of billions in support**: lowering merchants' operating costs
- **Establishment of a Merchant Rights Protection Committee**: appeal success rate up roughly 30%
- **March 2026 launch of "New Duoduo Mama"**: an initial 15 billion yuan to help factories build their own brands

> **Non-consensus judgment**: the deeper logic here is that Pinduoduo has come to recognize that **pure low-price race-to-the-bottom competition is unsustainable**. "New Duoduo Mama" is, at its core, an attempt by Pinduoduo to upgrade itself from a "traffic allocator" into a "supply-chain brand incubator" — a Chinese version of Costco's private-label (Kirkland) model.

**User/customer value score: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

## V. Business Portfolio and Synergy Effects

### 5.1 Positioning of the Three Core Businesses

| Business | Positioning | Estimated 2025 scale | Profitability status | Strategic role |
|------|------|--------------|---------|---------|
| **Main site** | Domestic e-commerce platform | GMV ~50 trillion yuan, revenue ~250 billion yuan | Highly profitable (net margin 30%+) | Profit cow |
| **Duoduo Grocery** | Community group-buying/near-field retail | GMV ~300 billion yuan | Profitable in some regions, slightly loss-making overall | High-frequency entry point + lower-tier market penetration |
| **Temu** | Global cross-border e-commerce | GMV ~$92.5 billion, revenue ~$30 billion+ | Loss-making, expected to turn profitable in 2026 | Growth engine + supply-chain internationalization |

### 5.2 Synergy Matrix

| Synergy dimension | Main site → Temu | Main site → Duoduo Grocery | Temu → main site | Duoduo Grocery → main site |
|---------|----------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| **Supply chain** | White-label factories going overseas directly (the core synergy) | Shared logistics network and warehousing | Reverse-customization demand feedback | Fresh-produce supply-chain capability feeds back |
| **Traffic** | Weak (standalone app) | Embedded entry point within the main app | Weak | High-frequency usage drives low-frequency usage |
| **Technology** | Reuse of recommendation algorithms | Reuse of fulfillment algorithms | Overseas data enriches algorithms | Last-mile delivery capability |
| **Merchants** | One-click overseas expansion (New Duoduo Mama) | Shared local suppliers | Overseas demand feeds back into domestic production | Weak |

**Strongest synergy**: the supply-chain synergy between the main site and Temu. The production capacity and flexible-manufacturing capability that Chinese white-label factories have built up through Pinduoduo's main site can migrate to Temu at close to zero cost.

**Weakest synergy**: there is almost no synergy running from Temu back to the main site.

**Business portfolio score: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

## VI. Evaluating PDD Against Duan Yongping's "Good Business" Criteria

### 6.1 Testing Duan Yongping's Core Criteria One by One

| Criterion | Score | Analysis |
|------|------|------|
| **Doing the right thing** | ★★★★☆ | Abolishing refund-only, hundreds of billions in merchant support, launching New Duoduo Mama — actively correcting past problems |
| **Degree of differentiation** | ★★★☆☆ | Differentiation lies in "extreme efficiency" rather than "a unique product." As every platform races toward low prices, that differentiation is narrowing |
| **Pricing power** | ★★★☆☆ | See the detailed analysis below |
| **Sustainable competitive advantage** | ★★★☆☆ | The supply-chain efficiency advantage is sustainable, but faces erosion from Douyin's e-commerce push and the structural risk of tariff policy |
| **Corporate culture/management** | ★★★★★ | Duan Yongping has explicitly said he "trusts Pinduoduo's culture and team." Generating 400 billion yuan in revenue with 20,000 employees is a stunning feat of organizational efficiency |
| **Simple and understandable** | ★★★★☆ | The e-commerce platform model itself is simple, but Temu's global operations add complexity |

### 6.2 A Deeper Look at Pricing Power

**Platform's pricing power over merchants: ★★★★☆ (strong)**
- The platform controls traffic allocation; merchants have no owned customer base and must accept the platform's rules
- But overexercising this pricing power has already caused the merchant ecosystem to deteriorate

**Consumers' bargaining power over the platform: ★★★★☆ (strong)**
- Consumers face virtually zero switching costs and are extremely price-sensitive
- Pinduoduo **has no pricing power over the end consumer**

**The paradox of pricing power**: strong pricing power upstream (over merchants), none downstream (over consumers). Profit comes from compressing the spread between the two ends, not from a brand premium.

> **The key judgment from Duan Yongping's perspective**: the core reason Duan Yongping does not hold a large position in Pinduoduo — **Pinduoduo earns money from efficiency, not from brand**. Efficiency can be caught up with; a brand can almost never be replicated.

## VII. Overall Assessment and Conclusion

### Summary of Scores Across Dimensions

| Analysis dimension | Score | One-sentence conclusion |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Business model essence | ★★★★☆ | An asset-light platform plus supply-chain efficiency — an excellent model, though Temu adds asset-heavy characteristics |
| Flywheel effect | ★★★★☆ | The domestic flywheel is self-sustaining but decelerating; the Temu flywheel still needs marketing spend to drive it |
| Moat | ★★★☆☆ | Scale effects are a genuine moat; brand and network effects are false moats |
| User/customer value | ★★★★☆ | Strong value for consumers; merchant value is in the process of being repaired |
| Business synergy | ★★★★☆ | Supply-chain synergy is the core highlight |
| Duan Yongping's "good business" criteria | ★★★☆☆ | Driven by efficiency rather than brand, and lacking end-consumer pricing power |

### Overall score: ★★★★☆ (3.7/5)

**Pinduoduo is a "good but not great" business.** It has extreme organizational efficiency and powerful supply-chain integration capability, but lacks brand pricing power and its moat remains narrow. **The biggest variable is "New Duoduo Mama" — can it shift from "earning money on efficiency" to "earning money on brand"?** That is the question most worth tracking over the next three to five years.
