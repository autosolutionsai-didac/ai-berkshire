# PDD Holdings Earnings Call Executive Commentary Notes
## Q3 2025 (2025-11-18) & Q4 2025 (2026-03-25)

> Compiled: 2026-04-13

## I. Q3 2025 Earnings Call (November 18, 2025)

**Executives present:**
- Lei Chen, Co-Chairman and Co-CEO
- Jiazhen Zhao, Co-Chairman and Co-CEO
- Xinyi Lin, Head of Investor Relations (attending on behalf of Jun Liu, who was on medical leave)

> **Note**: Vice President of Finance Jun Liu was on medical leave and did not attend in person; Xinyi Lin read his prepared written statement on his behalf.

### 1.2 Lei Chen's Remarks

**Quotes (from the English-language call transcript):**
> "Short-term stock price fluctuation has never been our focus. And rather, our focus remains on building long-lasting intrinsic value by doing the right thing and creating value for consumers."

> "It is with our firm commitment to high-quality development, we embark on the next decade towards our vision of Costco plus Disney."

> "We will continue to uphold the principles that have guided us over the past decade."

**Paraphrase (source: Seeking Alpha Transcript):**
- Emphasized the company's commitment across every market to "sustainable development and compliance with local regulations"
- Continuing to increase investment in trust, safety, and product compliance

---

### 1.3 Jiazhen Zhao's Remarks

**Quote (from the English-language call transcript):**
> "Over the past 10 years, we have remained committed to creating value for our users and growing alongside our merchants. We have strived to drive the industry to become more open and have delivered incremental value to the society. At this 10-year juncture, we will continue to step up our efforts to give back to the supply side and the demand side as part of our efforts to drive industry upgrades and deliver more savings and more fun to the general public."

**Paraphrase (source: Insider Monkey Transcript):**
- The company chooses "to view competition through a long-term lens"
- Will continue investing in the ecosystem even if it suppresses short-term margins
- Operating margin declined from a peak of roughly 27% to roughly 25%, characterized as a deliberate choice rather than a forced outcome

---

### 1.4 Jun Liu's Remarks (written statement, read by Xinyi Lin)

**Quote (from the written statement):**
> "As we roll out greater merchant support initiatives and ecosystem investments, financial results may continue to fluctuate from quarter to quarter."

**Paraphrase**: Explicitly cautioned investors that this quarter's profit figures **should not be taken as guidance for future performance**.

---

### 1.5 Key Strategic Initiatives, Q3 2025 (Official Disclosures)

**"10 Billion Merchant Support" program continues:**
- The RMB 100 billion merchant support program plus RMB 10 billion in fee reductions continues to be implemented
- Management explicitly stated this is a long-term investment that will not be withdrawn under short-term profit pressure

**Strong growth in the agriculture segment:**
- Agricultural product sales grew **47%** year-over-year in the first half of 2025
- The number of merchants born after 2000 grew **30%** year-over-year
- Branding initiatives for agricultural products such as "Duoduo Fresh" continue to advance

**Zero mentions of AI:**
- AI was not mentioned once during the entire call — extremely unusual among leading tech companies
- Management appeared to deliberately avoid hot topics unrelated to the current quarter's results

**Margin decline openly acknowledged:**
- Operating margin declined from roughly 27% to roughly 25%
- Management characterized this as a deliberate strategic choice, not a passive response

### 2.2 Jiazhen Zhao's Remarks

**Quote (from the English-language call transcript, on "building another Pinduoduo" — the single most significant statement of this call):**
> "At the last shareholders meeting, we announced our all-out efforts in investing in the transformation of the supply chain and in building another Pinduoduo. This remains our duty and primary focus."

> "2026 marks PDD's 11th year and a new starting point. As we head into our next decade, we are starting afresh and moving forward with an all in attitude and a persistent focus on execution. We will dedicate more talent and resources to deepen our investments in the supply chain, accelerating its upgrade and transformation. We believe that in the next three years we will have the opportunity to build another Pinduoduo."

**Quote (on margin volatility):**
> "Given continued strategic investment and a volatile macro environment, quarterly profit margins may continue to fluctuate and that this volatility could become a normal condition."

**Quote (characterizing 2025 strategically):**
> "2025 marked the year in which we made our largest investment in high-quality development."

**Quote (a rare instance of self-critical governance commentary):**
> "Our governance and talent development have not kept pace with the rapid expansion of our business."

**Paraphrase (source: Investing.com / Yahoo Finance):**
- China's e-commerce industry has entered a phase of "intensified competition and slower growth"
- The company is choosing to increase investment rather than pull back, viewing the present as a window to "do the right thing"

---

### 2.3 Lei Chen's Remarks

**Quote (from the English-language call transcript, on regulatory scrutiny):**
> "Over the past period, we have indeed received inquiries from regulatory authorities as our global business grew rapidly and reached a meaningful scale in different countries. It is understandable that this has drawn surprise, concern and closer scrutiny. However, our management believes that the current regulatory scrutiny will lay a solid foundation for our next stage of growth and will also provide direction for iterating our model amid the rapidly evolving international political and regulatory landscape."

**Quote (on the long-term strategy for the international business):**
> "Since the launch of our global business, we have consistently focused on the long term, building on our deep roots in the supply chain, we are committed to achieving sustainable development in each market and creating real value for consumers."

**Paraphrase (source: Benzinga Transcript):**
- Explicitly stated that "compliance is the baseline"
- For the first time, publicly acknowledged having received inquiries from regulators in multiple countries, and characterized this as a positive development — arguing that regulatory clarity will lay the foundation for the next stage of growth

---

### 2.4 Jiong Li's Remarks (Finance Director)

**Paraphrase (source: Insider Monkey Transcript):**
- Reiterated that FY2025 full-year operating cash flow exceeded RMB 106.9 billion, with cash reserves reaching RMB 422.3 billion
- Noted that FY2025 cost growth (approximately +23%) far outpaced revenue growth (+10%), driven by deliberate strategic investment
- Made no commitment to a timeline for margin recovery

---

### 2.5 Key Strategic Initiatives, Q4 2025 (Official Disclosures)

**"Building another Pinduoduo":**
- PDD **publicly disclosed a three-year strategic target for Temu for the first time in its history**: to build an international business matching the scale of domestic Pinduoduo within three years
- Jiazhen Zhao had previewed this strategy at the annual shareholders meeting; the Q4 call formally confirmed it

**All-in bet on the supply chain:**
- Moving away from a traffic-distribution logic toward "the platform creating incremental value across the entire supply chain"
- Specifics: branding of industrial clusters such as Yiwu accessories and Pinghu down jackets; standardization of agricultural products such as Anyue lemons and Pu'er coffee; village-level logistics last-mile deployment across more than 10 provinces

**Response to tariff shocks (Temu):**
- The US eliminated the "de minimis" duty-free exemption for shipments under $800, directly impacting Temu's low-price cross-border model
- Response measures: rolling out a "semi-managed" model in which merchants share overseas delivery costs; increasing the number of sellers using overseas local warehousing

**Margin pressure becomes the new normal:**
- Jiazhen Zhao characterized margin volatility as the "new normal"
- No commitment was made on any timeline for margin rebound; the RMB 100 billion supply-chain investment is a three-year plan

## III. Core Themes Compared Across the Two Calls

### Theme 1: Growth and Margins

| Metric | Q3 2025 (2025/11/18) | Q4 2025 (2026/03/25) |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Revenue growth | +9% YoY | +12% YoY (quarter); +10% full year |
| Non-GAAP operating margin | approx. 25% | approx. 24% (Q4); approx. 24% full year |
| Net income growth | +17% (GAAP net income) | -11% (Q4 GAAP net income); -12% full year |
| Margin guidance | "May continue to fluctuate quarter to quarter" | Confirmed as the "new normal" |

### Theme 2: Strategic Focus

| Dimension | Q3 2025 | Q4 2025 |
|------|---------|---------|
| Core message | "Long-termism, not focused on short-term share price" | "Building another Pinduoduo in three years" |
| Domestic ecosystem | 10 billion merchant support program + agricultural branding | Supply-chain transformation + village-level logistics |
| International business | Compliance and sustainable development | First public acknowledgment of regulatory inquiries; semi-managed model |
| AI mentions | **Zero mentions** | None (a stark contrast with peer tech companies over the same period) |

### Theme 3: Personnel-Change Signals

| Dimension | Q3 2025 | Q4 2025 |
|------|---------|---------|
| Jun Liu (Finance VP) attendance | No (medical leave, written statement) | No (Jiong Li substituted) |
| Finance spokesperson | Xinyi Lin (IR, reading the written statement) | Jiong Li (Finance Director) |
| Signal | Liu's absence was a first occurrence, considered unusual | Two consecutive absences, drawing market attention |

### Theme 4: Evolution of Tone on Competition

| Time | Official statement |
|------|---------|
| Q3 2025 | Chose to "view competition through a long-term lens," unswayed by external changes |
| Q4 2025 | Openly acknowledged the industry has entered a phase of "intensified competition and slower growth"; self-critical about governance failing to keep pace with business expansion |
