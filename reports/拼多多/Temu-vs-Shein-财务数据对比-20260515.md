> Data as of May 2026. Shein is a private company; most of its data comes from media reports, leaked investor documents, and third-party analyst estimates.
> Confidence labels: **High** = listed-company filings/official disclosure; **Medium** = cross-validated across multiple independent sources; **Low** = single source or analyst speculation

## I. Temu (PDD Holdings' overseas business)

### 1. GMV, revenue, and losses

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 (known/estimated) |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| GMV | $18 billion | $55-71 billion | Q1 $13.2 billion, Q2 $14.5 billion (full-year estimate $60-70 billion) |
| Revenue (actual platform revenue) | Not disclosed separately | Not disclosed separately (PDD total revenue $54 billion) | Not disclosed separately |
| Loss | $8-9 billion | Loss narrowing (specific figure not disclosed) | Targeting break-even by end of 2025 (but delayed by tariff impact) |

**Confidence:** GMV data is **Medium** (cross-validated across multiple third-party sources, but definitions are inconsistent, with a wide range of $55 billion-$71 billion); loss data is **Low** (PDD does not disclose Temu's profit and loss separately)

**Important note:** The GMV vs. revenue definitions differ significantly.
- The $71 billion figure cited by some sources is on a GMV basis, but it may include the full value of goods resold by Temu under the fully-managed model
- Bernstein estimates Temu's overall markup rate at roughly 35% of GMV (about 40% under the fully-managed model), meaning actual platform revenue is far below GMV
- PDD's total FY2024 revenue was $54 billion, of which Temu's revenue share is estimated at 30-40%

### 2. Profitability timeline

Temu's path to profitability has three phases:
- **Phase 1 (September 2022 - end of 2024):** Loss-making period, strategic cash burn to acquire customers
- **Phase 2 (2025):** Targeting break-even, but the U.S. tariff impact has pushed break-even in the U.S. market to 2026
- **Phase 3 (from 2026):** Targeting full profitability

**Confidence: Medium** (from multiple industry analyst reports, but the 2025 tariff shock has introduced uncertainty into the timeline)

Key variable: U.S. tariffs on Chinese goods eliminated the de minimis exemption, directly pushing up Temu's logistics and compliance costs; losses in the U.S. market widened in Q2 2025.

EU GMV in 2024 was approximately $10-12 billion, projected to exceed $15 billion in 2025. Temu is estimated to have had Europe overtake the U.S. as its largest market by Q4 2024.

### 4. Take rate

Temu's monetization model differs fundamentally from traditional platforms (such as Amazon or PDD's domestic marketplace):

| Model | Commission/markup rate | Description |
|------|------------|------|
| **Fully Managed** | ~40% markup | Merchant supply price + Temu adds a 40% markup before selling |
| **Semi-Managed** | 8-15% commission | Merchants set their own prices and handle shipping; Temu takes a commission |
| **Overall average** | ~35% markup (on a GMV basis) | Bernstein estimate |

**Confidence: Medium** (investment bank research such as Bernstein)

For comparison: PDD's domestic marketplace take rate is about 4.2% (FY23). Temu's 35% markup exists fundamentally because it operates a "buy-and-resell" model rather than a pure platform model.

## IV. Key assumptions to flag for the 5-year forecast

Based on the data above, the core variables to watch for a 5-year forecast:

### Temu
1. **Quantifying the tariff impact:** How much has the per-order cost increased since the de minimis exemption was eliminated? Will it fundamentally change the business model?
2. **Regional-shift capacity:** Can growth in Europe/emerging markets offset the shrinkage in the U.S.?
3. **Profitability inflection point:** The pace of shifting from fully-managed to semi-managed determines the margin trajectory
4. **Ceiling on PDD's parent-company subsidy:** How long can PDD's $15.4 billion in annual profit sustain Temu's losses?

### Shein
1. **Whether the IPO can be completed:** Is a valuation anchored at $30-50 billion reasonable?
2. **Pace of marketplace share increase:** The impact on margins as 3P rises from 24% to 40%+
3. **Category-expansion execution:** Execution capability in expanding from fashion apparel into all categories
4. **Regulatory risk:** France has already shown signals of restrictions on Shein

### Shared variables
- Direction of U.S.-China trade policy
- The ceiling of global consumer acceptance of "ultra-low-price Chinese e-commerce"
- The intensity of competition and the zero-sum battle for market share between the two
