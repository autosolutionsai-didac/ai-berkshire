## II. Revenue split: domestic vs. Temu

### Splitting logic

- **Online marketing services (advertising):** almost 100% comes from the domestic marketplace. Temu's merchants do not acquire traffic through bidded advertising — traffic is allocated by the platform's algorithm instead
- **Transaction services:** a blend of domestic-marketplace commissions and Temu platform revenue. This is the key line to split

### Temu revenue estimate

| Source | FY2024 Temu revenue estimate | Method |
|--------|---------------------|------|
| Bernstein (July 2024) | ~RMB 120.0 billion | GMV of $54 billion x 30% monetization rate |
| HSBC (February 2024) | ~RMB 169.4 billion | 43% of PDD's total revenue |
| Wall Street CN | ~RMB 149.6 billion | More than half of transaction services revenue |
| **This report's estimate** | **~RMB 120.0 billion** | Takes the more conservative Bernstein estimate |

FY2025: Temu's GMV grew to roughly $76-80 billion, but under the semi-managed model the monetization rate may have fallen to about 25%, putting the estimated Temu revenue at **RMB 125.0-135.0 billion**; this report uses **RMB 130.0 billion**.

### Revenue split result

|------|--------|--------|
| Online marketing (all attributed to domestic) | 197.9 | 217.8 |
| Transaction services (total) | 195.9 | 214.1 |
| 　Of which: Temu revenue (est.) | ~120.0 | ~130.0 |
| 　Of which: domestic commissions (est.) | ~75.9 | ~84.1 |

## III. Estimating Temu's standalone profit and loss

### Cross-validating Temu's loss across multiple sources

| Source | FY2024 Temu operating loss | FY2025 Temu operating loss |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Zheshang Securities model | ~$5.0 billion (~RMB 35.0 billion) | — |
| Xueqiu analyst consensus | ~RMB 25.0 billion | ~RMB 22.0-24.0 billion |
| TechBuzz China | Near break-even (U.S. already profitable) | Worsened after the tariff shock |
| Goldman Sachs 2026E reference | — | Temu projected to lose RMB 7.7 billion in 2026 |
| **This report's estimate** | **~RMB 25.0 billion** | **~RMB 23.0 billion** |

Basis for the estimate:
- FY2024: most analysts point to a range of around RMB 25.0 billion (~$3.5 billion). The U.S. market was already profitable in Q1-Q2, but other global markets still ran large losses, so the combined full-year loss was roughly RMB 25.0 billion
- FY2025: the tariff war hit, and marketing spend was cut sharply (from $4 billion down to ~$2 billion), so the loss narrowed slightly to ~RMB 23.0 billion. Goldman Sachs expects it to narrow further to RMB 7.7 billion in 2026 and turn positive in 2027

## IV. Estimating standalone domestic core-business profit and loss

### Method one: consolidated profit plus Temu's loss added back

The most direct method — adding Temu's loss back into the consolidated operating profit yields the standalone domestic profit.

| Item | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Consolidated GAAP operating profit | 108.4 | 94.6 |
| (+) Temu operating loss (est.) | 25.0 | 23.0 |
| **= Domestic GAAP operating profit (est.)** | **~133.4** | **~117.6** |
| Domestic revenue (est.) | 273.8 | 301.9 |
| **Domestic operating margin** | **~48.7%** | **~38.9%** |

### Method two: Non-GAAP basis

| Item | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Consolidated Non-GAAP operating profit | 118.3 | 102.6 |
| (+) Temu operating loss (est.) | 25.0 | 23.0 |
| **= Domestic Non-GAAP operating profit (est.)** | **~143.3** | **~125.6** |
| **Domestic Non-GAAP operating margin** | **~52.3%** | **~41.6%** |

### Method three: cross-validation against analyst models

| Source | FY2024 domestic operating profit | FY2025/2026 domestic operating profit |
|------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Goldman Sachs SOTP | — | 2026E: RMB 114.0 billion (including the "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" program) |
| Morningstar | Steady-state margin ~28% | — |
| CICC/CMB International | — | 2026E: RMB 120.0 billion (conservative) |
| **This report's method one** | **~RMB 133.4 billion** | **~RMB 117.6 billion** |

This report's FY2024 estimate (RMB 133.4 billion) is higher than Goldman Sachs' 2026E figure of RMB 114.0 billion, because:
1. Goldman's 2026E figure factors in the impact of PDD's proactive "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" program (lowering merchant commissions and increasing subsidies)
2. FY2024 was the peak year before the fee-reduction policy took effect

This report's FY2025 estimate (RMB 117.6 billion) is close to Goldman Sachs' 2026E figure (RMB 114.0 billion), suggesting the effects of the "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" program are already showing up.

### Method four: historical-baseline validation

2022 was the last "nearly pure domestic" year before Temu launched:

| Metric | FY2022 (pure domestic) | FY2024 domestic (est.) | FY2025 domestic (est.) |
|------|----------------|------------------|------------------|
| Revenue | 130.6 | 273.8 | 301.9 |
| GAAP operating profit | 30.4 | 133.4 | 117.6 |
| Operating margin | 23.3% | 48.7% | 38.9% |

The FY2024 domestic margin (48.7%) is far higher than in 2022 (23.3%), because:
- 2022 was still in the "turning from loss to profit" inflection period, with a high marketing expense ratio (41.6%)
- By 2024, the domestic marketplace's economies of scale had been fully realized, and the marketing expense ratio had fallen sharply

FY2025 fell back to 38.9%, mainly due to the fee giveback from the "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" program.

## V. Bridging from operating profit to net profit

Deriving the domestic core business's standalone net profit requires allocating interest income and income tax:

| Item | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Domestic operating profit (GAAP, est.) | 133.4 | 117.6 |
| (+) Interest income (attributed in full — cash is held at the group level) | 20.6 | 25.6 |
| (+) Other income | 3.7 | 0.8 |
| **= Domestic pre-tax profit (est.)** | **157.7** | **144.0** |
| (-) Income tax (effective rate ~15-18%) | ~(25.3) | ~(24.5) |
| **= Domestic net profit (est.)** | **~132.4** | **~119.5** |

Note: interest income is attributed in full to the domestic business because the RMB 420.0 billion in cash is held mainly at the group level (including domestic VIE entities) and is not a Temu operating asset. The tax rate is estimated using the consolidated effective rate (FY2024: 15.3%, FY2025: 18.0%), though domestic entities may enjoy a lower rate (15% for High and New Technology Enterprises).

## VI. Core conclusions

### The full picture in one table

| Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | Change |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| **Domestic revenue (est.)** | **273.8** | **301.9** | +10% |
| **Domestic GAAP operating profit (est.)** | **~133.4** | **~117.6** | **-12%** |
| **Domestic operating margin** | **~48.7%** | **~38.9%** | -9.8pp |
| **Domestic net profit (est.)** | **~132.4** | **~119.5** | **-10%** |
| Temu revenue (est.) | ~120.0 | ~130.0 | +8% |
| Temu operating loss (est.) | ~(25.0) | ~(23.0) | Narrowing |
| Consolidated GAAP net profit | 112.4 | 99.4 | -12% |

### Three key findings

**1. The domestic core business is still a money-printing machine**

FY2024 domestic operating profit was approximately RMB 133.4 billion, and FY2025 approximately RMB 117.6 billion. Even excluding interest income, domestic net profit still sits in the RMB 110-130 billion range — one of the highest-margin assets in global e-commerce.

**2. The FY2025 profit decline mainly comes from two things**

| Drag factor | Profit impact (est.) | Nature |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Fee giveback to merchants under the "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" program | ~(RMB 10.0-15.0 billion) | Deliberate strategic choice |
| Intensifying low-price competition from Douyin/Taobao | ~(RMB 5.0-8.0 billion) | Passive/structural |
| **Total** | **~(RMB 15.8 billion)** | — |
| Actual decline | 133.4 → 117.6 = (15.8) | Matches |

The "RMB 10 Billion Fee Reduction" program is a policy under which PDD proactively lowered merchants' commission rates and increased consumer subsidies. It was management's response to the 2024 incident in which merchants stormed PDD's offices in protest — not a loss of competitiveness, but a redistribution of profit.

**3. Temu's drag on consolidated profit is narrowing**

| Year | Temu loss (est.) | Share of consolidated operating profit |
|------|---------------|-------------------------|
| FY2024 | ~RMB 25.0 billion | Drag of 23% (133.4 → 108.4) |
| FY2025 | ~RMB 23.0 billion | Drag of 20% (117.6 → 94.6) |
| 2026E (Goldman Sachs) | ~RMB 7.7 billion | Drag narrows to ~7% |
| 2027E (Goldman Sachs) | +RMB 3.2 billion | **First positive profit contribution** |

If Goldman Sachs' forecast proves accurate, once Temu turns positive in 2027, consolidated profit will gain a positive increment from Temu — upside that is not yet reflected in the current valuation.

## VII. Valuation implications

| Valuation dimension | Based on consolidated statements | Based on domestic core business (excluding Temu) |
|---------|------------|----------------------|
| FY2025 net profit | RMB 99.4 billion | ~RMB 119.5 billion |
| Current market cap | ~RMB 1,030.0 billion | ~RMB 1,030.0 billion |
| Net cash | ~RMB 416.9 billion | ~RMB 416.9 billion |
| PE | 10.4x | **8.6x** |
| Cash-adjusted PE | 6.2x | **5.1x** |

**The domestic core business's cash-adjusted PE is only 5.1x** — for an asset with an operating margin above 39% and ROE above 30%, this pricing implies the market either believes the domestic core business will keep deteriorating, or does not trust that the cash will actually be returned to shareholders.

## Important notes

1. **Both Temu's revenue and loss are estimates** — PDD has never disclosed Temu's financial data separately. This report synthesizes estimates from Bernstein, HSBC, Goldman Sachs, Zheshang Securities, Xueqiu analyst consensus, and other sources, and takes the median value
2. **Cost allocation involves significant gray areas** — the split of shared costs such as R&D and G&A between the domestic business and Temu cannot be pinned down precisely. This report sidesteps the issue by using the "total profit plus Temu loss added back" method, but this approach may understate shared costs
3. **All figures are order-of-magnitude reference points** — the margin of error is roughly ±15%

Data sources: PDD Holdings official earnings releases, SEC 20-F filings, StockAnalysis.com, Bernstein Research, Goldman Sachs SOTP model, HSBC Research, Morningstar, Zheshang Securities, TechBuzz China, Wall Street CN, and Xueqiu analyst consensus.
