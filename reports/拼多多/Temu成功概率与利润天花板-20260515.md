**Research date: May 15, 2026 | Core question: If Temu succeeds, how much would it earn per year, and how likely is that?**

---

## I. Direct Answer

### Temu's annual profit if it succeeds

| Degree of success | GMV | Annual operating profit | Annual net profit | Equivalent to |
|---------|-----|-----------|---------|--------|
| Modest success | $50 billion | $3 billion | $2.5 billion | 1/4 of the main platform |
| Moderate success | $150 billion | **$12 billion** | $10 billion | **rebuilding a second main platform** |
| Major success | $300 billion+ | **$30 billion** | $25 billion | 2.5x the main platform |

**The decisive factor for the profit ceiling is advertising revenue**. After commissions are eaten up by logistics and subsidies, margins are thin, but the marginal cost of advertising is near zero. Pinduoduo's main platform monetizes ads at 4.6% of GMV, and that is the core source of its profit. If Temu can get ad revenue to 4% of GMV, profit could reach $15-30 billion; if it can't build that out, the ceiling is only $3-5 billion.

### Probability of success

| Scenario | Probability | GMV | Annual profit |
|------|------|-----|--------|
| Major success (top 3 globally) | 5-8% | $300 billion+ | $30 billion |
| Moderate success (profitable) | 25-30% | $100-300 billion | $12 billion |
| Modest success (breakeven) | 35-40% | $50-100 billion | $3 billion |
| Failure / major contraction | 25-30% | <$50 billion | $0 |
| **Probability-weighted** | — | — | **~$5.1 billion/year** |

**The most likely outcome is "modest success" (35-40% probability)**: Temu survives but struggles to become a major win.

## IV. Detailed Analysis of Success Probability

### Favorable factors

1. **Pinduoduo's execution capability**: extremely fast strategic adjustment from tariff shock to countermeasures, with rapid iteration on semi-managed Y1/Y2 models
2. **Chinese supply chain cost advantage**: even with added tariffs, some categories retain price competitiveness
3. **Multi-market global footprint**: setbacks in the US are offset by Europe and Latin America
4. **Parent company's 420 billion yuan cash pile**: can keep funding losses for years
5. **Semi-managed model opens up local merchant ecosystems**: shifting from "China goods mover" to a multi-country, multi-source platform

### Unfavorable factors

1. **US tariffs (the biggest blow)**: de minimis elimination plus 145% tariffs have undercut Temu's core US value proposition
2. **EU regulation**: the EU has already found Temu in violation of the DSA, with fines potentially reaching 6% of global revenue
3. **Quality ceiling**: the platform model is inherently unfriendly to quality control, limiting brand upgrades
4. **Competitive encirclement**: squeezed on multiple fronts by Amazon Haul, Shein, and TikTok Shop
5. **Uncontrollable geopolitics**: every escalation in US-China relations could bring new restrictions

### Key variable

**Tariffs are the biggest variable**. A $5 item with a 54% tariff becomes $7.70, and the final retail price could double. Whether Temu can recover in the US depends on how fast its share of local-warehouse fulfillment can rise from 25% to 50%+ (which would take 12-18 months).

### Historical reference

| Company | Outcome | Lesson for Temu |
|------|------|-------------|
| Wish | Collapsed from $2.5 billion in revenue to being sold for $170 million | Temu has parent-company funding and won't repeat this fate |
| Shein | Successfully built a brand and supply chain | Temu is a platform model and cannot replicate a vertical supply chain |
| AliExpress | 10+ years of operation, consistently lukewarm | Temu's growth burst has far exceeded this, but long term it may revert to a similar position |

## VI. Core Conclusion

### One-sentence summary

> Temu's most likely fate is "modest success" — GMV of $50-100 billion, breakeven or slightly profitable, at 35-40% probability. Moderate success (annual profit of $12 billion, effectively rebuilding a second main platform) has a probability of roughly 25-30%. But even if Temu fails completely, the value of Pinduoduo's main platform plus net cash still far exceeds the current $96 share price. This is an asymmetric bet where "win big, lose small."
