## I. Clearly missing topics

### 1. Duoduo Grocery (Duoduo Maicai) deep dive (importance: high)

The "sole dominant player" structure in community group buying is a major competitive-landscape shift in 2025-2026:

- Meituan Select has exited, JD's Jingxi Pinpin has been marginalized, and Taocaicai has scaled back — Duoduo Grocery has become the only nationwide player
- 2025 GMV approached 300 billion yuan, exceeding the combined 2024 GMV of Duoduo Grocery and Meituan Select
- Daily order volume surged 50% after Meituan Select's exit, with all 72 warehouses nationwide running at full capacity
- Duoduo Grocery has temporarily shelved instant retail and set aside short-term profitability goals, focusing entirely on capturing the market vacuum left by Meituan's exit
- Growth of 20-30% is expected over the next several years; the addressable market could exceed 500 billion yuan, with profit potentially reaching tens of billions or even 10 billion+ scale

**Existing files**: the "New Business Deep Dive" (2026-04-10) touches on Duoduo Grocery as a sub-topic, but lacks a standalone, in-depth breakdown of the dramatic shift in competitive landscape, profit outlook, and valuation contribution.

### 2. Free rural delivery / last-mile logistics strategy (importance: medium-high)

A core component of the 100-billion-yuan support program, with major implications for PDD's penetration of lower-tier markets:

- Piloted in Q4 2025, full rollout in 2026
- Model: merchants ship to a county-level transfer warehouse → third-party logistics delivers to village pickup points → the platform fully absorbs the second-leg shipping cost
- Responds to the Central No. 1 Document's guidance to "deepen express delivery into villages"
- County-level transfer warehouse + village pickup point networks have already been built in multiple regions nationwide (e.g., 71 sites in Fufeng County, Shaanxi)
- Rural online retail sales exceeded 3 trillion yuan for the first time in 2025
- Two-way value: connects the "last mile" for industrial goods going to rural areas + the "first mile" for agricultural products going to market

**Existing files**: none.

### 3. Hong Kong secondary listing progress tracker (importance: medium-high)

Multiple research files mention "awaiting a Hong Kong listing" as a key catalyst, but there is no progress tracker:

- PDD has switched Hong Kong auditors, engaging EY Hong Kong to review its financial statements (replacing its Beijing auditor), suggesting it is advancing toward a Hong Kong listing
- Goldman Sachs estimates 27 China-concept stocks (combined market cap of 1.35 trillion yuan) are eligible for a dual-primary or secondary listing in Hong Kong, with PDD among them
- A Hong Kong listing would allow ADRs to be converted into Hong Kong shares, hedging against U.S. delisting risk
- The Hong Kong Stock Exchange is conducting a listing-reform consultation covering weighted voting rights, secondary listings, and other regime changes
- No official timetable yet

**Existing files**: no standalone file. Briefly mentioned in the buy-risk panorama analysis.

### 4. 100-Billion Supermarket / instant-retail push (importance: medium)

PDD's first genuine foray into instant retail:

- Internal testing of "100-Billion Supermarket" began in early 2026, strengthening its position in fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG)
- Adopts an "asset-light + crowdsourced delivery" strategy, reusing community-group-buying grid warehouses to reduce upfront investment
- Piloting self-built forward warehouses in tier-1 cities such as Shanghai, with plans to expand gradually
- Strong competitors: JD Supermarket users +18% / order volume +20%; Meituan's Xiaoxiang forward warehouses cover 30+ cities; Alibaba's multi-pronged supermarket portfolio
- Given the heavy losses other players suffered in the food-delivery price war, PDD has explicitly ruled out restaurant food delivery, focusing instead on non-dining instant delivery

**Existing files**: none.
