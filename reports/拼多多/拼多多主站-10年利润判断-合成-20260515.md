# Pinduoduo's domestic main platform: will profit be higher or lower 10 years from now?

**Research date: May 15, 2026 | Four-agent parallel research**
**Core question: stripping out Temu and Duoduo Grocery, looking only at the domestic main platform, will profit in 2036 be higher or lower than in 2025?**

---

## Conclusion

> **Most likely somewhat higher, but don't celebrate.** In the base-case scenario, the main platform's 2036 net profit is approximately 135 billion yuan, 29% higher than the roughly 105 billion yuan in 2025 — that sounds fine, but the 10-year annualized growth rate is only 2.3%. This is a money-printing machine that is still running, just running slower and slower. On a probability-weighted basis, profit is about 28% higher than today. In the pessimistic scenario (25% probability), profit is actually 14% lower than today.

## Consensus and disagreement across the four perspectives

### Consensus (high confidence)

1. **The main platform's golden growth phase has ended.** Revenue growth fell from +59% to +10% in 2025 — a structural inflection point, not a cyclical fluctuation.
2. **Margins will decline structurally.** Repairing the merchant ecosystem (ending "refund-only," the 100-billion-yuan support program), New Pinmu's self-operated model dragging down margins, and rising subsidies from intensifying competition — three pressures that are not reversible.
3. **The "low-price mindset" will not disappear within 10 years.** China's roughly 600 million consumers earning under 1,000 yuan a month are the core base, and slower economic growth actually reinforces demand for low prices.
4. **The main platform won't collapse, but it is shifting from "growth" to "value."** Compare with Taotian: an EBITA margin of 43.6% with nearly zero growth over three years — this is the fate of mature platform e-commerce, and PDD is still 3-5 years from that "steady state."

### Disagreements (the most valuable part of the analysis)

| Point of disagreement | Optimistic view | Pessimistic view | Judgment |
|--------|---------|---------|------|
| **Is AI agent shopping a threat or an opportunity for Pinduoduo?** | AI price comparison will preferentially recommend the lowest price → Pinduoduo benefits | AI bypasses the platform app → traffic-allocation power is hollowed out | **A double-edged sword, but Pinduoduo's R&D spend is only 3.45% of revenue, leaving it under-prepared** |
| **How much will margins fall?** | Down 5 percentage points (40%→35%) | Down 10 percentage points (40%→30%) | **Take the midpoint of 7-8 percentage points, leaning toward the more cautious estimate** |
| **Is the 100-billion-yuan "New Pinmu" investment worth it?** | Successfully builds self-operated brand capability, raising the ceiling | Self-operated margins are far lower than pure platform margins, dragging down overall profitability | **The outcome is unknown, but management's "all-in bet" itself is a risk signal** |
| **Will merchants leave en masse?** | Already stabilizing, thanks to the 100-billion-yuan support program + ending "refund-only" | Tax normalization + margins at their limit → mass exits | **Stable in the short term; the long term depends on the pace of tax policy** |

## Five decisive variables

Ranked by their impact on 10-year profit, from largest to smallest:

### 1. AI agent shopping (impact magnitude: ±20-30% of profit)

**This is the only variable that could flip the conclusion's direction.**

Current signals: Amazon has already launched its Alexa shopping agent, OpenAI has integrated Stripe payments, and Qwen is now fully integrated with Taobao. McKinsey forecasts that agent commerce will drive $3-5 trillion in global transactions by 2030.

Impact on Pinduoduo: users no longer "browse" Pinduoduo themselves but let an AI act on their behalf → ad-bidding-based ranking becomes ineffective → the core monetization mechanism is hollowed out.

Mitigating factors: Pinduoduo's white-label goods lack standardized SKUs, making it hard for AI to compare prices precisely across platforms; "lowest price across the internet" itself could become AI's preferred recommendation criterion.

**Judgment**: limited impact before 2030; from 2030-2036, AI could divert 5-10% of e-commerce traffic entry points. But with R&D spending of only 3.45% of revenue, Pinduoduo is significantly underprepared.

### 2. Douyin's low-price competition (impact magnitude: -10-15% of profit)

Douyin's 2025 GMV has already surpassed Pinduoduo's (4.4 trillion vs. 4 trillion), and it has made "price competitiveness" its top strategic priority; white-label beauty brands on Douyin have already surpassed 100 million yuan in sales. Douyin's average daily usage time of 120 minutes vs. Pinduoduo's under 30 minutes — time spent is traffic, and traffic is merchants and ad budgets.

**Judgment**: Douyin will not displace Pinduoduo's low-price mindset (Walmart was never displaced by Amazon), but it will continue to erode its share. It is highly likely that Pinduoduo's share falls from 19% to 14-16% within 10 years.

### 3. Merchant ecosystem (impact magnitude: ±10-15% of profit)

The "refund-only" era has ended and the 100-billion-yuan support program is rolling out, but merchant margins have already been squeezed to their limit. Tax normalization (new 2026 VAT rules) is a new source of pressure.

**Judgment**: management is doing the right thing (repairing the ecosystem), but at the cost of actively compressing margins. This is "short-term pain for long-term gain" — margins decline over 3 years in exchange for 10 years of supply-side stability.

### 4. Instant-retail substitution (impact magnitude: -5-8% of profit)

Instant retail is expected to exceed 1 trillion yuan in 2026, with daily-necessities standard goods migrating from "next-day delivery" to "30-minute delivery." Pinduoduo is entirely absent from this race.

**Judgment**: this will chip away at Pinduoduo's GMV in the daily-necessities category, but won't affect non-instant categories like apparel and agricultural products. The direction of impact is certain but the magnitude is limited.

### 5. Demographics (impact magnitude: -5-10% of profit)

The user base has peaked and is beginning a slow decline (MAU from 700 million to 600-650 million), and the core consuming population (ages 25-55) continues to shrink. Penetration among older users is rising, but their ARPU is low.

**Judgment**: a slow-moving variable, but the direction is clear, and 10 years is enough time for the impact to be felt.

## Taotian as a mirror of the endgame

Taotian's present may well be Pinduoduo's main platform's future. Comparison:

| Metric | Taotian 2025 | Pinduoduo main platform, 2036 forecast |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| GMV | ~8.3 trillion | ~6.5-7 trillion |
| Revenue growth | Low single digits | Low single digits |
| EBITA margin | ~44% | ~30-35% |
| Profit growth | Near zero for three years | 2-3% annualized |
| Positioning | Full-category platform | Low-price + white-label platform |

Taotian's EBITA margin of 44% with nearly zero growth over three years — this is the fate of mature marketplace e-commerce. Pinduoduo's main platform currently has a higher margin (~40%), but is converging toward this "steady state." The difference is that Pinduoduo's main-platform margin will ultimately land below Taotian's (weaker brand-premium capability, lower average order value).

---

## Final judgment

**Pinduoduo's domestic main-platform profit will be higher than today 10 years from now, but only about 30% higher — an annualized rate of just 2.3%.**

Confidence in this conclusion is medium — the main source of uncertainty is AI agent shopping. If AI shopping becomes mainstream around 2030, the conclusion could flip from "somewhat higher" to "somewhat lower."

For investors:
- The main platform is a stable cash machine that will not collapse
- But the growth narrative has ended, and its valuation should shift from "growth" to "value"
- Nearly all of PDD's overall investment variance comes from Temu — the main platform only provides a floor, not upside
- At the current P/E of 10.5x and ex-cash EV/E of 6.2x, even valuing only the main platform, it is not expensive — the market is already pricing in Temu going to zero
