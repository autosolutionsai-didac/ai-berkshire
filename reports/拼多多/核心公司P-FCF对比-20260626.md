## Key Findings

1. **Chinese ADRs are far cheaper overall** — Pinduoduo, JD.com, and NetEase all trade around 10x P/FCF, while comparable US companies generally trade above 30x, a roughly 3x valuation gap
2. **Capex is the biggest distorting factor** — Amazon, Google, Tesla, BYD, and Xiaomi all have P/FCF figures severely distorted by massive AI/manufacturing capex; looking at this metric alone can lead to misjudgment
3. **The companies with the best cash-flow quality** — NetEase (38% FCF margin), Pinduoduo, Visa, and Mastercard convert nearly all of their profit into real cash
4. **From a historical-percentile perspective** — Nvidia, despite trading at 43x, is below its historical median, indicating that cash-flow growth has outpaced the share price; Tencent and Alibaba are both slightly above their historical medians
5. **Pinduoduo's position** — At 10.7x P/FCF, it ranks 4th lowest among all the companies covered here; its EV/FCF of 5.0x (net of cash) is even more extreme, reflecting the market's deep skepticism about the sustainability of its growth
