## IX. Three core conclusions

### Conclusion 1: Duan Yongping's PDD holding is a textbook case of "five years of observation + two years of full conviction"

- First took notice in 2018 → didn't formally build a position until 2023 (**a five-year wait**)
- Only added aggressively after the August 2024 crash (**waited for the moment of peak fear among others**)
- Currently holds 11.56 million shares, but the position is **roughly at breakeven on paper** (approximately $1.15-1.27 billion in cost vs. $1.156 billion in market value)
- This is not a case of "already made a lot of money, so just letting it sit" — it is **holding driven purely by conviction**

### Conclusion 2: Duan Yongping has never reduced his position, yet he does not recommend that others buy it

- H&H's 13F: from Q3 2023 to the present, **100% buying, zero selling**
- Duan Yongping himself has repeatedly emphasized: "I've never recommended Pinduoduo to anyone"
- This contrast — "buying it myself but not recommending it" — demonstrates that Duan Yongping himself **is not certain whether PDD meets his own standard of staying within his circle of competence**; he is simply taking on this uncertainty based on personal trust in Colin Huang

### Conclusion 3: Duan Yongping's current PDD position is a pure bet on Colin Huang's philosophy, not on any secondary consideration

Based on a revised judgment from earlier research:
- **This is not a bet on "broad shareholder benefit"** (Colin Huang donating his personal shares cannot excuse the company's decision not to pay dividends)
- **This is not a bet on the market value rising** (market value is just market sentiment)
- **It is a bet that**: PDD's intrinsic value will grow fast enough + the $30 billion in cash sitting on the balance sheet will be effectively deployed over the next 5-10 years + the market will eventually recognize the intrinsic value

**Duan Yongping has expressed this bet with $1.15-1.27 billion of real capital** — that's 7.5% of the H&H portfolio, **not a small amount, but also not an all-in bet**.
