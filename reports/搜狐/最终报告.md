## 0. One-Sentence Core Conclusion

> **Sohu is an A-grade cigar butt plus a C-grade company. Market cap of $410M is below net cash of $1,164M (a 2.84x discount, i.e. buying a dollar for forty cents), and EV is negative $385M (the market is paying you to take the business off its hands). But three of the four analysts — Duan Yongping, Li Lu, and Munger — all say "don't buy" (a classic value trap); only the Graham perspective says "would buy." In 2025, Charles Zhang already returned $352M via a special dividend plus buyback (= 86% of market cap), marking a pivotal shift from "hoarder" to "value release." Fundamentally this is a bet on "take-private arbitrage," an event-driven opportunity — not a compounding investment built on a good business, a good company, and a good price.**

## III. Summary of Analysis by Dimension

### 3.1 Business model (Duan Yongping perspective) → 2 stars, Pass

**Core findings**:
1. **The true business structure**: Sohu is no longer an "internet portal" — it is "a *Tian Long Ba Bu* games company wearing a media disguise, plus a cash-management box": Changyou 86.6%, media advertising 10.3%, other 3.1%
2. **A single IP holds the whole company hostage**: *The New Tian Long Ba Bu* alone contributes 60% of total revenue and 69% of games revenue. A single PC-client game IP from 2007 is propping up the entire listed company
3. **Charles Zhang's personal IP as an anti-moat**: his physics lectures have gone viral, yet marketing services revenue fell 18% in 2025 — **the hotter Charles Zhang's personal IP gets, the more Sohu's advertising shrinks**. This proves two things: (1) his personal traffic isn't being monetized by the company; and (2) "founder as the company's sole brand" functions as an anti-moat
4. **The key counter-question**: if Sohu shut down today, what impact would it have? **Almost none.** Users, advertisers, and the industry could migrate away at zero cost — **this means it is no longer a going concern, but effectively a liquidation asset**

### 3.2 Financials and valuation (Buffett/Graham perspective) → 4 stars, A-grade cigar butt

**Core findings**:
1. **Extreme net-cash discount**: market cap of $410M vs. net cash of $1,164M, a 2.84x discount — **one of the most extreme cases in global capital markets**
2. **Negative enterprise value**: EV = -$385M, the market is paying you to take the entire operating business off its hands
3. **Management shifting from hoarder to value-releaser**: $352M returned in 2025 (= 86% of market cap), a major pivot for Charles Zhang
4. **A triple catalyst**: buyback (completed $150M) + dividend ($202M special dividend) + a take-private option (5-10% probability)
5. **A-grade cigar-butt checklist, 5 of 6 criteria met**: market cap < net cash (yes) + cash > 50% of total assets (yes) + extremely low debt (yes) + core business cash flow positive (yes) + major shareholder buying back stock (yes) + ongoing but manageable losses (marginal)
6. **Three-scenario valuation**: bear $8 (-49%) / base $25 (+59%) / bull $30-32 (+91% to +104%), expected value $20.80 (+32%)

### 3.3 Industry and competition (Munger's "invert, always invert" perspective) → 1 star, an almost certain loss

**Core findings**:
1. **All three core businesses are in "structural death," not "cyclical decline"**:
   - Portal: under triple assault from algorithmic recommendation (Toutiao), social distribution (WeChat), and short video (Douyin)
   - Long-form video: Sohu Video has effectively exited the competition (vs. iQiyi/Youku/Tencent Video/Bilibili)
   - Games: Changyou already went private and delisted in 2020
2. **A 200x market-cap gap versus NetEase**: both companies started from the same point (founded the same year, both originating as portals, both listed overseas) — 25 years later, the market caps differ by 200x. NetEase found a second-curve business in gaming; Sohu's three attempts at a second curve (Sogou / video / Changyou) all failed
3. **Internet advertising share is <0.1%**: a rounding-error-level share within an $850 billion market
4. **No exception among former overseas portal kings**: Yahoo and AOL were liquidated by Apollo private equity, Sina went private, MySpace went to zero — Sohu is the last portal-identity company in China still independently listed
5. **Munger-style judgment**: Sohu is standing in a place that will die; the only decision left for retail shareholders is what posture to leave in

### 3.4 Risk and management (Li Lu perspective) → Grade C-

**Core findings**:
1. **Charles Zhang's strategic instincts rate D grade**: he missed e-commerce, mobile social, search (Sogou already sold off), short video, livestream e-commerce, long-form video, and large AI models — **an almost perfectly inverse indicator**
2. **Integrity B+, everything else C/D**: integrity is a bright spot, but strategy, capital allocation, and succession-readiness are all weak
3. **Take-private probability 60-70%** (within 3-5 years): referencing both the Changyou precedent (68%-premium take-private in 2020) and the Sina precedent (going-private delisting in 2020), the financial feasibility of "using Sohu's own money to buy Sohu" is entirely manageable
4. **No successor**: a 62-year-old CEO who also holds the chairman role, with zero succession plan — a major deduction under Li Lu's "century-old enterprise" framework
5. **Li Lu-style ultimate verdict**: **would not buy Sohu, 100%** — a classic "value trap"; cheapness is not a reason to buy, greatness is

## IV. Investment Thesis (Bull vs. Bear)

### Bull Case (5 points)

1. **Extreme net-cash discount**: market cap is below net cash / 3, cash per share of $44.65 versus a share price of $15.72
2. **Negative enterprise value of -$385M**: buying the stock is effectively "the market paying you $385M to take the business off its hands"
3. **Major 2025 capital return**: $352M in dividends plus buybacks = 86% of market cap, proof that management has pivoted from hoarding to releasing value
4. **A take-private option**: referencing the Changyou precedent's 60%+ premium, with a 5-10% probability
5. **Downside is protected**: the net-cash pile forms a natural floor; a drop below $22 could actually be an opportunity to add to the position

### Bear Case (5 points)

1. **All three core businesses are in structural death**: portal, video, and games have all been left behind by the times, with no possibility of revival
2. **Hostage to a single IP**: 60% of revenue comes from a PC-client game launched in 2007, and IP has a finite lifespan
3. **Cash trapped within the onshore VIE**: most of the $1,200M is held in RMB, and dividends/repatriation are subject to foreign-exchange controls plus a 10% withholding tax
4. **The founder's personal IP is an anti-moat**: Charles Zhang is 62, the hotter his personal IP burns the more advertising shrinks, and there is no successor
5. **Retail investors could be squeezed out in a take-private**: referencing the Sina take-private precedent (in which retail shareholders were forced out), the outcome might be a low-price buyout rather than a return to fair value

## VIII. Summary

Sohu is a **philosophical test case for value investing**: are you willing to pay $1 for $2.84 of net cash, plus a negative-value shrinking business, plus an honest CEO who isn't skilled at capital allocation but has begun returning capital to shareholders?

If you follow Graham — this is a textbook cigar butt, and you **would buy**.
If you follow Buffett / Duan Yongping / Munger / Li Lu — this is a **classic value trap**, because the moat, the compounding potential, and management quality all fail to make the grade.

**My final judgment (synthesizing all four analysts)**: Sohu is **suited as a 3-5% satellite position for event-driven arbitrage**, not as a long-term compounding holding. **The core bet is that Charles Zhang launches a take-private within 3-5 years** (following the Changyou path, at a 60-100% premium). If the bet fails, the net cash on the books provides downside protection (adding to the position below $22 is even better); if the bet succeeds, the return is 1.5-2x.

But **do not, under any circumstances, treat Sohu as "the next NetEase" or "a portal in revival"** — that would be a cognitive error. **Sohu is a shroud draped over a pile of gold, waiting for an undertaker, not a doctor.**
