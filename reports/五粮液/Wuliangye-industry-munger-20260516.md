## One-Sentence Core Judgment

**The baijiu industry is experiencing a historic inflection point from "volume contraction + price increase → volume and price both contracting." Production has fallen 70% over 8 years, yet industry profits are concentrating in the top 5 companies (CR5 revenue share exceeds 80%). But Wuliangye's "thousand-yuan price tier second place" ecological position is being squeezed from two sides—above by Kweichow Moutai's dimension reduction attack, below by Shanxi Fenjiu/Luzhou Laojiao aggressively seizing share. This is not cyclical difficulty but structural reordering.**

> *Munger says:* "In a shrinking market, being number one allows you to survive well, being number two becomes dangerous, and being number three or lower is essentially waiting to die."

## 1. Industry Panorama: A Business Experiencing "Volume Contraction + Price Increase"

### 1.1 Market Scale: Revenue Still Growing, But Entirely Supported by Prices

| Metric | 2020 | 2022 | 2024 | Change |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Number of enterprises above scale | 1040 | 963 | 989 | -5% |
| Total production (10,000 kiloliters) | 740 | 671 | 414.5 | **-44%** |
| Sales revenue (100 million yuan) | 5836 | 6626 | 7963.8 | +36% |
| Total profit (100 million yuan) | 1585 | 2201 | 2508.7 | +58% |

**Data Sources**: National Bureau of Statistics, China Alcohol Industry Association

**Core Contradiction**: Production fell 44% over 4 years, yet revenue increased 36%. This demonstrates the entire industry is "trading volume for price"—selling less but at higher prices. Profit growth rate (+58%) far exceeds revenue growth rate (+36%), indicating profits are accelerating concentration in premium products and leading enterprises.

But by 2025, this game finally couldn't be played anymore—19 listed baijiu companies' total revenue declined 18.13% year-over-year, marking the arrival of an era of volume and price contraction.

Two stages:
- **2016-2020 (Cliff Period)**: Eight Provisions deepened + low-end capacity eliminated, production halved
- **2021-2025 (Continued Bottom Exploration)**: Aging population + young people refusing baijiu + higher environmental thresholds, decline continues

> *Munger says:* "When total industry volume is contracting, you're not participating in a growth game—you're participating in an elimination tournament."

## 2. Industry Cycle: Adjustment Enters Third Year, Inventory Clearing Shows Clear Differentiation

### 2.1 Current Cycle Positioning

This round of baijiu downcycle began in 2024 Q2 and has now entered the third year. Compared with the four historical cycles:

| Dimension | 2012-2015 Cycle | **2024-2026/27 Current Cycle** |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Trigger Factors | Eight Provisions + phthalate crisis | Alcohol restriction + consumption downgrade + population inflection point |
| Government consumption share | 40%→5% | 5%→lower (marginal impact limited) |
| Population Trend | Tail of dividend | **Population inflection point passed 2022** |
| Demand Substitution | Government→commercial (available substitution) | **Commercial + consumer both contracting (no substitution)** |
| Macro Environment | Economic stabilization and rebound | L-shaped bottom + real estate decline |

**Core Judgment**: This cycle is harder to exit than 2012-2015 because it lacks "substitute growth." In 2012, government consumption was lost but commercial consumption picked up; this time both commercial and consumer demand are contracting—who will take over?

### 2.2 Inventory Clearance Progress: Top Players Cleared, Middle Market Blocked

| Category | Inventory Status (2026 Q1) | Estimated Clearance Time |
|------|---------------------|------------|
| Feitian Kweichow Moutai | Near safe inventory levels, some distributors have zero inventory | **Essentially complete** |
| Wuliangye/Guojiao 1573 | 1-2 months inventory, down 30%+ after Spring Festival | 2026 Q2 |
| Sub-premium (300-800) | 3-4 months inventory, still significant pressure | 2026 Q2-Q3 |
| Regional Brands | Over 70% of enterprises with high inventory | 2026 second half+ |

**Data Sources**: March 2026 alcoholic beverage merchant survey—over 80% of merchants report some inventory pressure relief, average decrease exceeds 30%

**But on the other hand**—most distributors believe that marginal inventory improvement does not signal an industry inflection point. Channel inventory presents "structural blockage" characteristics: top tier cleared, middle tier congested, bottom tier submerged.

### 2.3 Key Micro Signal Tracking

Based on previous baijiu cycle research (2026-05-03), only 3 of 10 bottom signals are triggered (30%), core bottlenecks:
- Accounts receivable: 2025 Q3 industry accounts receivable + notes receivable totaled 77.56 billion yuan, doubled year-over-year—baijiu enterprises are "selling on credit"
- Thousand-yuan price tier: Puwan and Guojiao 1573 all trade inverted, none above ex-factory price
- Distributor payment rate: approximately 70%, 20 percentage points away from the healthy 90% level

**Conclusion**: Inventory is improving, but far from completely cleared. Industry fundamental bottom estimated at 2026 Q4-2027 Q1.

## 4. Price Tier Competition: Wuliangye's "Sandwich Cookie" Dilemma

### 4.1 Ultra-Premium (2000+): Kweichow Moutai Alone and Unchallenged

| Metric | Feitian Kweichow Moutai (April 2026) |
|------|-------------------|
| Ex-factory price | 1269 yuan (after adjustment March 31, 2026) |
| Self-operated retail price | 1539 yuan |
| Case wholesale price | 1700-1730 yuan |
| Loose bottle wholesale price | 1565-1580 yuan |

Feitian Kweichow Moutai remains the only baijiu where "wholesale price exceeds retail price"—it's a financial product, not a consumer good. Even though it briefly fell below 1500 yuan at the industry's lowest in early 2026, it quickly rebounded to the 1700 yuan range after the March price increase.

**Kweichow Moutai's Moat Data**:
- Market share 19.47%, nearly 2 times that of second-place Wuliangye
- 2025 revenue declined only 1.2%, equivalent to "traversing the cycle" against the industry's -18% background
- Brand premium: same baijiu, Kweichow Moutai ex-factory price 1269 yuan, wholesale price 1700 yuan—channel profit of 430 yuan/bottle

> *Munger says:* "Kweichow Moutai possesses a type of 'psychological monopoly.' When Chinese people need a bottle of alcohol to express respect, there is no substitute."

### 4.2 Premium (800-1500): Puwan and Guojiao 1573 Fully Inverted, Combat Fierce

| Product | Ex-factory Price (yuan) | 2024 Wholesale Price | Early 2026 Wholesale Price | Inversion Magnitude |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|---------|
| 8th Generation Puwan | ~1019 | ~950 | **880-920** | **-10% to -14%** |
| Guojiao 1573 | ~980 | ~880 | **750-800** | **-18% to -23%** |
| Mengyue Blue M9 | ~1000 | ~950 | **Drops ranging from tens to hundreds** | Opaque |

**Data Sources**: Alcohol industry media, distributor channel research

**Key Findings:**

1. **Puwan wholesale price breaks below 900 yuan**: Degraded from a thousand-yuan product to an "800-yuan tier" product, brand momentum severely depleted
2. **Guojiao 1573 Even Worse**: Wholesale price 750-800 yuan, already in the sub-premium price tier while maintaining premium product "dreams"
3. **2025 Anomaly: Guojiao wholesale price temporarily exceeding Puwan**—indicating their "status" has blended together; consumers and distributors no longer accept as self-evident that Puwan should be more expensive than Guojiao
4. **Mengyue Blue M9**: Priced at thousand yuan, but market recognition and sales velocity weaker than Puwan and Guojiao

**Munger's "Ecological Position" Analysis**:
- Kweichow Moutai occupies "ultra-premium social currency" position—irreplaceable
- Puwan once occupied "thousand-yuan business standard configuration" position—being eroded
- Guojiao 1573 wants to seize Puwan's position—but its own brand strength is also declining
- Wholesale price gap between the three shifted from "Kweichow Moutai>>Puwan>Guojiao" to "Kweichow Moutai>>Puwan≈Guojiao"—premium tier differentiation is disappearing

### 4.3 Sub-Premium (300-800): Most Bloody Battlefield

Sub-premium is the most fiercely competitive price range among all tiers, dubbed the "sandwich cookie"—premium products above pushing down, mass market products below pushing up.

| Brand/Product | Price Range | 2025-2026 Dynamics |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| Qinghua Fenjiu 20 | 400-500 | Promotional price down over 150 yuan, relying on banquet volume |
| Redflower Langjiu 10/15 | 300-500 | Banquet flagship, 2024 revenue approached billions |
| Shede | 400-600 | New product 980 yuan attacking premium, low-end 88-358 yuan targeting mass market |
| Shuijingfang | 400-600 | Split "Shuijingfang" (sub-premium) + "First Fang" (premium) |
| Jiannanchun | 400-500 | Banquet king, but growth slowing |

**Core Characteristics**:
- Group-purchase-dominated sub-premium/premium baijiu sales **universally declining 30%-50%**
- Government group purchases **nearly halved**, commercial group purchases down over 20%
- 500-800 yuan price segment **faces maximum survival pressure**—this is where Puwan and Guojiao wholesale prices have "crashed" into

> *Munger says:* "In a shrinking market, price warfare is the stupidest strategy, but also the strategy everyone will adopt."

### 4.4 Price Tier Competition Summary: Who's Stealing from Whom?

```
Ultra-Premium (2000+): Kweichow Moutai monopoly, moat unfathomably deep
      ↓ Kweichow Moutai wholesale price fluctuates, but remains 1500+
      ↓
Premium (800-1500): Puwan vs Guojiao ← mutually trampling
      ↓ Wholesale price drops to 800-900, encroaching downward on sub-premium
      ↓
Sub-Premium (300-800): ← fallen products + internal tier competition
      ↓ Shanxi Fenjiu/Langjiu/Jiannanchun melee
      ↓
Mass Market (100-300): Most rigid demand, actually stabilizes first
```

**Wuliangye's Specific Dilemma**:
- Puwan **cannot reach upward to Kweichow Moutai**: brand momentum gap too large, lacks financial attributes
- Puwan **being caught upward by Guojiao**: wholesale price gap narrowing from 200+ to near parity
- Puwan **being overtaken by Shanxi Fenjiu via curved overtaking**: Shanxi Fenjiu with light aroma differentiation + banquet channel, revenue scale already approaching Wuliangye

## 5. Consumer Trends: Long-term Structural Change, Irreversible

### 5.1 Do Young People Really Not Drink Baijiu?

| Data | Source |
|------|------|
| Among consumers under 30, baijiu accounts for only 8% of alcohol consumption | Industry research |
| 25-35 age group baijiu drinking frequency declined 22% year-over-year | United Credit Research Report |
| Young consumers prefer "slight buzz" over "inebriation" | Multiple sources |
| Low-alcohol products, craft beer, fruit wine categories growing at high speed | Market data |

**Conclusion**: Young people haven't "abandoned" baijiu, but baijiu's share of their alcohol consumption is extremely low. They're not "anti-baijiu," but rather "don't need baijiu"—social occasions have changed (camping, girlfriend gatherings, solo light buzz), baijiu's "cheers culture" doesn't fit.

**But on the other hand**—when these young people enter the 30-40 age commercial socializing stage, some will "return" to baijiu. The question is: what's the return rate? Currently no reliable data, but based on Japanese experience, the return rate is declining by generation.

### 5.2 Impact of Consumption Downgrade

Impact of consumption downgrade on baijiu is stratified:

| Price Tier | Impact Level | Logic |
|--------|---------|------|
| Ultra-Premium (Kweichow Moutai) | ★★ Low | Social currency attribute, essential need |
| Premium (Puwan/Guojiao) | ★★★★ High | Commercial consumption frequency down, downgrading |
| Sub-Premium | ★★★★★ Highest | Squeezed from both sides |
| Mass Market | ★★★ Medium | Demand relatively rigid, stabilizes first |

**Most Counter-intuitive Finding**: Consumption downgrade actually has minimal impact on mass market baijiu. What's truly "eliminated" is the middle ground—the 800-1500 yuan commercial drinking occasion is shrinking; some demand downgrades to 300-500 yuan, some disappears entirely.

### 5.3 Core Consumer Group Shrinking

30-59 year-old consumers are baijiu's core group. Per UN population forecast:
- 2022: 665 million
- 2035: 569 million
- Compound annual decline rate: -1%

This means baijiu's "base market" is naturally shrinking by 1% annually. Combined with young people's preference shift for alcohol, actual shrinking speed may be faster.

## 6. Policy Environment: Alcohol Restrictions Have Limited Impact, But Emotional Damage Significant

### 6.1 Policy Evolution

| Timeline | Policy | Impact |
|------|------|------|
| 2012 | Eight Provisions | Government consumption share down from 40%→5%, industry devastated |
| 2017 | Local alcohol restriction escalated | Further compressed official consumption |
| May 2025 | Revised limits on three public expenses | Explicitly prohibits high-end tobacco/alcohol at official events |

### 6.2 Actual Impact Assessment

**Direct Impact Limited**:
- Government consumption represents less than 5% of baijiu market, Kweichow Moutai's government channel sales less than 1%
- The "de-government" from the 2012 Eight Provisions has essentially been completed
- The 2025 new regulations are more "restatement" than "escalation"

**Indirect Impact Not Negligible**:
- **Demonstration Effect**: Local layers amplify enforcement, some regions expand to SOEs and government agencies
- **Emotional Impact**: May 2025 alcohol restriction announcement caused baijiu sector stocks to plummet
- **Spillover in Commercial Consumption**: Enterprises dealing with government proactively lower alcohol specifications

**But on the other hand**—alcohol restrictions pushed baijiu consumption from "government-driven" to "commercial + personal-driven" structural transformation. Long-term, this transformation has essentially completed; future policy's marginal impact will diminish.

## 7. Endgame Thinking: Will Baijiu Follow Japan's Sake Path?

### 7.1 Japan's Sake: A Cautionary Tale

| Metric | Japanese Sake | Chinese Baijiu |
|------|---------|---------|
| Consumption trend | 2004-2022 CAGR -3% | 2016-2024 Production CAGR -13.7% |
| Premiumization | Ginjo/Junmai 2004-2022 CAGR +2% | Premium share 81%→83% |
| Premium share | 26%→37% (+11pp) | Ultra-premium 35% (2024) |
| Population factors | Aging + low birth rates | 2022 population inflection point + accelerating aging |
| Young people preference | Shifting to beer/spirits/RTD | Shifting to low-alcohol/craft beer/fruit wine |

### 7.2 Similarities and Differences

**Similarities (Pessimistic Signals)**:
- Continuous volume contraction: Chinese baijiu declining speed (-13.7%/year) even faster than Japanese sake (-3%/year)
- Premiumization is the mirror of "volume contraction": selling less, only can sell premium
- Generational replacement: Under-30 baijiu consumption only 8% of alcohol consumption, trend irreversible
- Population structure shift: 30-59 age core consumer group shrinking -1%/year

**Differences (Relatively Optimistic)**:
- Cultural embedment depth: Baijiu's position in Chinese culture (marriages, deaths, business socializing, holiday gifts) far exceeds sake's position in Japanese culture
- Brand concentration higher: Kweichow Moutai's "psychological monopoly" degree far exceeds any Japanese sake brand
- Market scale base: China's 800 billion+ yuan baijiu market, even with 20-30% volume reduction, remains enormous
- Consumer upgrade space: Chinese per capita baijiu consumption volume still has structural upgrade space

### 7.3 Endgame Judgment

**Baijiu will not follow Japan's sake path of "complete decline," but will follow "Japan's whisky" path—continuous volume contraction, leading brands growing against the trend, tail tier mass death.**

Specific predictions:
- **Volume**: Next 10 years production may contract another 30-40% (from 4 million to approximately 2.5 million kiloliters)
- **Revenue**: Industry total revenue may remain stable or slightly increase (trading volume for price)
- **Profit**: Further concentrating toward CR3 (Kweichow Moutai, Wuliangye, Shanxi Fenjiu)
- **Landscape**: Eventually forming "1 super (Kweichow Moutai) + 3 strong (Wuliangye/Shanxi Fenjiu/Luzhou Laojiao) + N regional champions" endgame

**Implications for Wuliangye**:
- Wuliangye will likely survive to the endgame—brand, channel, scale all sufficient
- But the "eternal second place" position is no longer secure—Shanxi Fenjiu threat is real
- Key variable is whether it can find balance between "de-Kweichow Moutai-fication" and "protecting thousand-yuan price tier"
