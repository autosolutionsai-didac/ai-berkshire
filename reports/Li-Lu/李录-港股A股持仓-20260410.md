## VII. Li Lu's Investment Philosophy as Reflected in His Holdings

### 7.1 Portfolio Characteristics and Investment Style

**Extreme concentration + extremely long holding periods:** Li Lu held BYD from 2002 all the way to his first divestment in 2021—a full 19 years. He held PSBC for three and a half years after buying it at the end of 2020, "without selling a single share." This kind of patience is nearly unmatched in the global hedge fund industry.

**Buying during crises:** Every one of Li Lu's major position builds has come during a moment of crisis:
- 2002: BYD had just listed, and market interest was cold
- 2008: financial crisis, BYD collapsed, and Li Lu added heavily
- Q1 2020: bought Bank of America during the pandemic crash
- End of 2020: PSBC was at a historic valuation low
- 2023: bought East West Bancorp during the Silicon Valley Bank crisis
- Q2 2025: built a heavy position in PDD Holdings amid a Chinese internet-sector downturn

> Li Lu: "Macro turmoil is precisely the moment when value investing shows its advantage."

**Balanced allocation between China and the U.S.:** roughly 50% in Hong Kong/A-shares and 50% in U.S. equities. This is a portfolio that only an investor who truly understands both China and the U.S. could build—combining high-growth Chinese names (BYD, PSBC) with high-quality U.S. blue chips (Alphabet, Bank of America, Berkshire).
