## I. The Core Campaign: BYD (the Most Successful Investment)

This is the most celebrated chapter of Li Lu's investing career, and a textbook case of value investing.

### Full Timeline

| Time | Event | Details |
|------|------|------|
| **July 2002** | BYD lists in Hong Kong; Li Lu makes his first purchase | BYD's H-share IPO price was approximately HK$10.95, then pulled back. Li Lu built his position at around HK$8, becoming a cornerstone investor with a stake of about 1.67% |
| **2003** | Introduces BYD to Munger | After an initial extended conversation with Munger on Thanksgiving, Li Lu began recommending BYD to Munger. Munger was not interested at first |
| **2005** | Annual report shows a holding of 8.9975 million H-shares | About 1.67% of BYD's H-shares |
| **2008** | Substantially increases the position to 52,204,200 shares | About 2.55% of BYD's total share capital. The same year saw the global financial crisis, during which BYD's stock crashed |
| **September 2008** | Facilitates Buffett's investment in BYD | After Li Lu's sustained efforts to persuade him, Munger finally "pounded the table" to get Buffett to buy in. Berkshire Hathaway's MidAmerican Energy bought 225 million shares (a 10% stake) at HK$8/share, an investment of HK$1.8 billion |
| **2010–2020** | Long-term holding, no change | BYD's stock swung wildly (surging to HK$88 in 2009 before falling back to HK$14), but Li Lu held throughout |
| **July 2021** | First reduction | Reduced the position in two tranches totaling 10,771,500 shares, cashing out more than HK$2.439 billion. Remaining holding after the reduction: approximately 73,650,600 shares |
| **2021–2022** | Continued gradual reduction | Reduced the position incrementally as BYD's stock rose from over HK$200 to over HK$300 |

### Estimated Returns

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Cost basis | Approximately HK$8–15/share (accumulated over many years in batches); total cost estimated at no more than HK$1.2 billion |
| Peak position value | With BYD's share price peaking at around HK$340, the position was worth more than HK$25 billion |
| Realized gains | More than HK$18 billion cashed out through reductions |
| Investment return | **More than 15x** on cost basis; **more than 40x** if measured from the initial HK$8 purchase |
| Holding period | **Approximately 20 years** (2002–2022) |

### Investment Rationale

Li Lu has repeatedly stated his views on BYD publicly:

"BYD is a company with a genuine engineering culture. Wang Chuanfu himself is an engineer by training, and the execution ability of its hundred thousand engineers is its core competitive advantage."

"When I first bought BYD, what I valued was its accumulated expertise in battery technology and its vertical integration capability. Its later moves into automobiles and new energy were natural extensions of that capability."

Munger's assessment: "BYD is one of the most remarkable companies I've ever seen."

Buffett later said: "In making investment decisions, Charlie has pounded the table for me to buy, buy, buy only twice." BYD was one of those two times.

## V. Summary of Investment Style

### The Shift in Style Around 2018

| Dimension | Before 2018 | After 2018 |
|------|---------|---------|
| Market focus | **Primarily China concept stocks** (Baidu, Sina, BYD) | **Shifted to US blue chips** (Alphabet, Bank of America, Berkshire Hathaway) |
| Position concentration | High | **Extremely high** (top 3 account for 83%+) |
| Turnover frequency | Relatively high (averaging 15 months) | Extremely low (almost no turnover) |
| Investment type | Technology + Chinese consumer | US technology platforms + financials |

### Core Traits

1. **Extreme concentration**: Typically holds only 5–8 stocks, with the top 3 accounting for more than 80%
2. **Long-term holding**: Once convinced, will hold for 20 years without selling (BYD)
3. **High win rate**: 11 of 13 publicly disclosed holdings were profitable, an 84.6% win rate
4. **Decisive loss-cutting**: Walks away without hesitation when wrong (PDD Holdings first attempt, Alibaba)
5. **Acting during crises**: Added to BYD during the financial crisis, bought Bank of America during the pandemic, bought East West Bancorp during the SVB crisis
6. **No management fee**: Only earns money when he makes money for investors

### Li Lu's Own Stated Investment Principles

"Long-term research, the right timing, the right price."

"We fish where the fish are. You don't need to understand every company — only a select few, truly."

"The best investment is one you never need to sell."
