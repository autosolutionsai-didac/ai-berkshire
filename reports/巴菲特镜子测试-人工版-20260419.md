### ▍Team Research Answer: The Math of Long-Term Returns Under High ROE + High P/B (2026-04-19, AI-team addition)

> **This section is AI-produced research.** Two parallel agents independently researched the question from two angles — historical valuation ranges, and ROE sustainability plus long-term return math — and the findings were then synthesized.

#### One-sentence conclusion
**Moutai's valuation is "reasonable but not cheap" — the implied long-term annualized return is 8-10% per year, about a third lower than the intuitive read of "32% ROE."** This isn't a mispricing; it's the mathematical result of a high payout ratio combined with limited reinvestment opportunity. The current P/E of 21x sits at the 25th percentile of its historical range — that looks low, but it isn't extreme.

#### Key finding 1: ROE ≠ long-term return (the core math)

Long-term return formula = ROE × (1 − payout ratio) + payout ratio × (1/P/E) + change in P/E

| Scenario | ROE | Payout ratio | P/E change (5 years) | **Annualized return** |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Bull | 34% | 50% | 21x → 25x | **15.4%** |
| Base | 32% | 52% | 21x → 20x | **9.8%** |
| Bear | 28% | 55% | 21x → 15x | **2.1%** |

Historical backtest validation:

| Entry year | Entry P/E | Holding period | Annualized return |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 2014 (anti-corruption low) | 8x | 10 years | **+13.5%** ⭐ |
| 2018 (median valuation) | 25x | 5 years | **-1.5%** ❌ |
| 2021 (bubble peak) | 50x | 4 years | **-24%/year** 💥 |
| 2026 (present) | 21x | 5-year expectation | **+4.5% to +10%** ⚠️ |

#### Key finding 2: Growth is genuinely slowing

- Q3 2025 single-quarter growth was **0.48%** (a 10-year low)
- Management guidance was cut from 15% to 9%
- Wholesale price down 40%, inventory up 27%

#### Key finding 3: A multi-angle derivation of a reasonable P/E center

| Lens | Implied P/E center | Note |
|------|:---:|------|

> Could AI enable other studios to reach Tencent's level of game production, eroding its competitive edge?

### ▍Team Research Answer (2026-04-19, AI-team addition)

> **This section is AI-produced research.** Four parallel agents independently researched the question from four angles — the current state of the technology, counterfactual case studies, Tencent's own response, and the economics of distribution — with the findings synthesized by AI. #### One-sentence conclusion
**For Tencent's gaming business, AI is an "asymmetric benefit" — it strengthens the moat rather than eroding it.** What AI democratizes is "production capability" (which accounts for ≤30% of what makes a game succeed), while the moat is really built on distribution/traffic/social/IP/live-ops (≥70%) — none of which AI can weaken, and all of which become **more valuable** as production itself gets democratized.

#### Key findings across four angles

| Angle | Key finding | Supporting data |
|------|---------|---------|
| **1. Current state of AI technology** | AI has "largely solved" art/code/QA (saving 25-50% of effort), but **actual development timelines have not shortened** — the time saved gets absorbed by rising expectations for graphics and content | 83.3% of developers use AI for art; GitHub Copilot boosts coding output by +55%; but AAA game cycles remain 4-7 years (GDC 2025) |
| **2. Small-studio counterfactuals** | The success of Palworld/Black Myth: Wukong/Manor Lords **was not driven by AI** — Palworld's CEO has publicly rejected AI use; the real drivers are "creativity + time + publishing support" | Steam released 18,000 new games in 2024, of which only 0.5% were profitable; the top 100 titles captured 80% of revenue |
| **3. Industry concentration** | **Concentration is rising, not falling** — the CR2 (Tencent + NetEase) share of China's mobile-gaming market rose from 61% in 2022 to 78% in 2025; technology democratization has repeatedly reinforced the dominance of large incumbents (mobile gaming, short video, livestreaming) | Tencent alone accounts for about 50% of China's gaming market; WeChat's 1.25 billion-user distribution power cannot be replicated by AI |
| **4. Tencent's own AI investment** | Its three-layer tool stack — Hunyuan + GiiNEX + ACE — has been deployed across 100+ projects; 2025 capex is projected at RMB 107 billion, and international game revenue has hit new highs for four straight quarters | GiiNEX cuts scene-generation time from 5 days to 25 minutes; AI teammates in PUBG Mobile boost user stickiness; game revenue grew +22% in 2025 (versus a 10-12% industry average) |

#### Key insight: a structural rebuild of the moat

Old-moat weighting (manual-production era) vs. new-moat weighting (AI era, projected):

| Element | Weight in the manual era | Weight in the AI era | Implication for Tencent |
|------|:----------:|:----------:|---|
| Production quality | 40-50% | **10-15% ↓** | This gap is flattened by AI, so Tencent loses relative advantage here |
| Distribution / traffic / social | 30-40% | **50-60% ↑** | **Tencent's strongest moat becomes even more valuable** |
| IP / live-ops | 10-20% | 25-35% ↑ | Tencent's 8-year IP accumulation in Honor of Kings/PUBG Mobile is amplified |

#### Evidence of "the strong get stronger": Tencent's asymmetric gains in the AI era

1. **Toolchain-integration advantage**: 100+ projects share GiiNEX/Hunyuan/ACE, an efficiency of scale that competitors (miHoYo with separate in-house teams per unit, NetEase with independently operating labs) lack
2. **Data flywheel**: Tencent's gaming data can feed back into training its own models — something DeepSeek/Doubao cannot replicate
3. **Cash-flow resilience**: RMB 107 billion of capex will not strain Tencent; startups cannot keep pace
4. **Social-graph lock-in**: Honor of Kings' "all my friends are playing it" effect is a moat AI simply cannot substitute for

#### Historical analogy (the most important counter-narrative)

**The prediction that "technology democratization leads to industry fragmentation" has repeatedly been proven false**:
- 2010-2015, mobile gaming took off (democratized by Unity/Cocos) → the industry instead consolidated, with Tencent/NetEase/miHoYo capturing 70%+
- 2016-2017, short video had an extremely low technical barrier → Douyin + Kuaishou captured 90%+
- 2015-2024, UE5 democratized AAA-quality art → AAA production is still dominated by major studios
- **The AI era is very likely to repeat the same script**

#### Risks that remain (to be monitored)

| Risk | Probability | Signal to watch |
|------|:---:|---|
| An "AI-native" game genre emerges that Tencent cannot produce | Low-to-medium | Watch for breakthroughs like "AI interactive narrative" from 2027 onward |
| ByteDance's Nuverse studio uses Doubao + Douyin traffic to produce a breakout hit | Medium | Progress of ByteDance's gaming unit's new titles in 2025-2026 |
