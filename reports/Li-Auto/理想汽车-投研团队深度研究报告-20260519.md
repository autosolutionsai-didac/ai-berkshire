**Research date**: May 19, 2026
**Research method**: Four-dimension parallel analysis (business model / financial valuation / industry competition / risk & management)
**Information richness rating**: Grade A (long listing history, broad brokerage coverage) — research emphasis placed on contrarian checks and non-consensus perspectives

---

## I. One-sentence conclusion

> Li Auto is a company with strong product-definition capability and a hundred-billion-yuan cash safety cushion, but it is currently caught in a triple bind: "the extended-range dividend is fading, the pure-EV transition is unproven, and profitability is collapsing." The current valuation already reflects a large amount of pessimism (net cash equals 64% of market cap), but the deteriorating fundamental trend has not yet bottomed out — 2026 could see the company's first annual loss since listing. The core question investors must answer is: is the profit decline a temporary pain of a product-generation transition, or a structural decline following a permanent shift in the competitive landscape?

## IV. Summary of analysis by dimension

### 4.1 Business model dimension

1. **Revenue structure is highly concentrated**: vehicle sales account for >95% of revenue; no meaningful recurring revenue stream has yet been established — the business is still essentially "a car-selling company"
2. **The flywheel is clearly slowing**: the extended-range SUV flywheel was spinning strongly in 2023, but in 2025 Seres overtook Li Auto's extended-range market share (Seres's range-extender share jumped to first place at 37.5%), and differentiation has disappeared
3. **Moat composite rating "moderately weak"**: brand recognition is strong but pricing power is weakening (average selling price per vehicle down 30% over three years); switching costs are weak, network effects are absent, and technical barriers are low
4. **High replicability of user value proposition**: "fridge, TV, big sofa" has become an industry standard, with Aito, Leapmotor, and Xiaomi all having followed suit
5. **Non-consensus view**: the core crisis is not whether the pure-EV push will succeed, but that the extended-range base business is collapsing — in 2025, extended-range L-series delivery volumes declined year-on-year, with both volume and price falling

### 4.2 Financial valuation dimension

1. **Inverted-V profit curve**: net profit went from RMB 11.7 billion in 2023 → RMB 8.0 billion in 2024 → RMB 1.1 billion in 2025, with 2026 Q1 guidance projecting a loss exceeding RMB 1 billion
2. **Cash flow collapse**: free cash flow went from RMB 44.2 billion in 2023 → RMB 8.2 billion in 2024 → approximately -RMB 2 billion in 2025 — an extremely unfavorable trend
3. **The balance sheet is the biggest bright spot**: RMB 101.2 billion in cash fully covers all liabilities, with net cash of $12.6 billion equal to 64% of market cap
4. **Valuation is low but for a reason**: P/S <1x, EV of only $7 billion (excluding net cash, the market prices the business at only $7 billion) — but the low valuation reflects fundamental deterioration rather than market mispricing
5. **DCF neutral scenario**: value per ADS is approximately $20.95, close to the current share price of $18.51; the margin of safety comes mainly from cash rather than business quality

### 4.3 Industry competition dimension

1. **Li Auto fell out of the new-force top 2 in 2025**: delivered 406,000 units (-18.8%), overtaken by Xpeng (429,000 units, +126%) and Xiaomi (~410,000 units, +204%)
2. **The extended-range route has clearly turned**: in the second half of 2025, extended-range model sales began to decline year-on-year, and the spread of 800V fast charging is eroding the core advantage of extended-range powertrains
3. **Huawei/Aito is the most direct threat (5/5)**: the Aito M7/M8/M9 compete head-to-head with Li Auto's L-series; in 2025, Harmony Intelligent Mobility delivered 589,000 units, already well ahead of Li Auto
4. **The product matrix is "drifting downward"**: the L6 accounts for over 40% of total sales; the i6 starts at RMB 249,800; continuously falling ASP is eroding gross margin
5. **Missing autonomous-driving label**: Huawei ADS and Xpeng XNGP have established leading brand recognition; Li Auto lacks a distinctive label in the autonomous-driving race

### 4.4 Risk and management dimension

1. **Core executives have left en masse**: 8 core executives departed within a year; the "three pillars" of the autonomous-driving team all left, with CTO Wang Kai and autonomous-driving head Lang Xianpeng departing in succession — this is the least reversible risk
2. **Li Xiang's credibility is in question**: sales targets have been missed by a wide margin for two consecutive years (62% attainment rate in 2024, 63.5% in 2025) — either chronic over-optimism or deliberate overpromising
3. **The embodied-intelligence strategy is aggressive**: opening a second front in humanoid robotics before the pure-EV business has even found its footing; Li Xiang has claimed "2026 is the last year to get on board with in-car AI" — FOMO-driven decisions are usually not good decisions
4. **Governance-structure risk**: under the dual-class share structure, Li Xiang controls 68.9% of voting rights with only 21.9% equity, with no effective checks
5. **Risk matrix ranks core talent loss first**: loss of core talent (already happened, hard to reverse) > uncertainty of the pure-EV transition > Huawei taking market share

## V. Investment thesis

### Bullish case

1. **Net cash of $12.6 billion equals 64% of market cap** — excluding cash, the market prices the auto business at only $7 billion; for a company with RMB 110 billion in revenue, that's an EV/Revenue of only 0.35x
2. **The only new force to have been profitable for three consecutive years** — profits have shrunk sharply, but Li Auto is still ahead of NIO (loss of RMB 22.4 billion) and Xpeng (loss of RMB 5.8 billion)
3. **A product-refresh cycle is approaching** — the L-series refresh and new L9 launch in the second half of 2026 could restart growth if successful
4. **NPS of 79.1, first in the industry** — user reputation remains strong; brand mindshare has not collapsed
5. **$1 billion buyback plan** — management is putting real money behind its confidence
6. **The pure-EV i6 has already shown improvement** — monthly deliveries of 24,000 units; gross margin is under pressure but this proves Li Auto's pure-EV push isn't a total failure
7. **R&D spend remains high (RMB 11.3 billion/year)** — building capability in autonomous driving and pure-EV technology

### Bearish case

1. **The extended-range base business is collapsing** — in 2025, both volume and price for extended-range L-series models fell, and the core flywheel has stalled
2. **The profit trend is extremely poor** — net profit has plunged 90% over two years, with 2026 highly likely to turn into a loss
3. **Free cash flow has already turned negative** — 2025 FCF was approximately -RMB 2 billion, and cash burn is accelerating
4. **Core executives have left en masse** — the autonomous-driving team has been nearly wiped out; the resulting decline in organizational capability will take 2-3 years to fully show up
5. **Competitors are surrounding the company on all sides** — Huawei, Xiaomi, and BYD are squeezing from three directions, and all are growing rapidly
6. **The embodied-intelligence strategy is diverting resources** — opening a second front while the core business hasn't stabilized adds uncertainty
7. **Li Xiang's decision quality has been volatile** — the MEGA failure, organizational expansion followed by contraction, and repeated missed targets suggest insufficient decision-making stability

## VIII. Summary

Li Auto is a textbook case of "a great balance sheet paired with a bad trend." The hundred-billion-yuan cash pile provides an extremely thick safety cushion, ensuring the company won't go under and giving the current valuation a degree of downside protection (net cash of $11.87/ADS). But a safety cushion alone isn't enough — the fading extended-range dividend, the collapsing profitability, the departure of the core team, and intensifying competition on all fronts are fundamental trends that have not yet bottomed out. The market performance of the second-half-2026 L-series refresh and the pure-EV i8/i9 will determine whether Li Auto is experiencing "a rebound after a temporary trough" or "the start of a structural decline." Until that inflection point is confirmed, this is a name with an asymmetric risk-reward profile — the upside depends on a highly uncertain successful transition, while the downside risk, though cushioned by cash, could see profitability continue to deteriorate.

## AI research limitations disclaimer

1. **Information timeliness**: this report is based on public information as of May 2026; some 2025 figures are estimates
2. **Inherent AI bias**: under Grade-A information richness, AI tends to summarize market consensus rather than produce genuinely non-consensus insight
3. **Quantitative analysis limitations**: the DCF valuation is highly sensitive to baseline assumptions; the three-scenario valuation range is extremely wide ($16-$32), limiting practical usefulness
4. **Unverifiable information**: management's true intentions, internal organizational climate, and undisclosed strategic adjustments cannot be obtained from public information
5. **Fast-changing competitive dynamics**: the competitive landscape of the NEV industry shifts every quarter; the competitive analysis in this report may need reassessment within 3-6 months
