>
> The series takes about 60 minutes to read; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series positioning

Li Auto is the Chinese new-force automaker that **turned profitable earliest**, and also one of the new forces with the **largest decline** over the past two years.

- The bulls say: net cash of **RMB 101.2 billion** is 64% of market cap; excluding cash, the market is pricing the business at only **$4.9 billion** — absurdly cheap
- The bears say: operating profit has turned negative, the extended-range dividend has vanished, core executives have left in a mass exodus, and the competitive landscape is deteriorating irreversibly

**Both camps are looking at the same company — one sees a safety cushion backed by hundreds of billions in cash, the other sees an automaker whose earning power is disappearing.**

What this series sets out to do is **lay out the facts first, then let you judge for yourself whether this is a "temporary trough" or a "structural decline."**

## Series writing principles

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — let readers weigh it themselves
3. **No fabricated data** — every figure is sourced; estimates are labeled "estimate"
4. **No probability-weighted expected returns** — assigning weights to scenarios is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **Not investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article
