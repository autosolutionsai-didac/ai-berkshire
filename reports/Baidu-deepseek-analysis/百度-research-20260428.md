## Statement of AI research limitations

Baidu has been publicly listed for 20+ years, with highly abundant information and broad sell-side coverage. But **more information does not equal more certainty**:
- AI new-business revenue figures come from the company's own classification; auditors have not independently verified the "AI revenue" definition
- Apollo Go has never separately disclosed a full income statement — "unit economics breakeven per city" excludes R&D amortization and headquarters overhead
- The competitive landscape for AI large models is changing extremely fast (on a month-to-month basis); the conclusions in this report could change materially within 3-6 months
- The analysis from this report's four agents may be unconsciously influenced by the market's "Baidu AI transformation" narrative

**Certainty comes from the business model itself, not from the volume of information.** Baidu is at the peak of uncertainty in its "old-engine-to-new-engine transition" — any forecast of Baidu's free cash flow 10 years out should be treated as highly speculative.

---

## 1. One-line conclusion

> Baidu is a "transition option stock" — cushioned by $33 billion in net cash, holding a full-stack AI technology option, while being dragged down by rapidly shrinking search advertising. A Non-GAAP P/E of 16x looks cheap on the surface, but negative free cash flow, uncertain management execution, and an irreversible decline in the core business mean the current valuation lacks a margin of safety. Aggressive investors could hold a small position as an AI option; conservative and moderate investors should stay on the sidelines and wait for two clear signals: FCF turning positive and AI revenue exceeding 50% of the mix.

## 4. Summary analysis by dimension

### 4.1 Business model (Duan Yongping's perspective) — ★★

**The 3 most important findings:**

1. **The search-advertising flywheel is disintegrating**: Market share fell from 86.82% in 2021 to 55.85% in May 2024 (Statista), with revenue declining for six consecutive quarters. This is not a cyclical problem — it is a structural disruption of the search category by AI.

2. **AI is a "life swap," not a "transformation"**: Alibaba's and Tencent's AI "empowers" their core businesses; Baidu's AI "disrupts" its own core business. AI search results reduce ad placements — the better the AI works, the harder ads are to sell. This is a uniquely Baidu "left hand fighting right hand" dilemma.

3. **Baidu fails every one of Duan Yongping's standards**: no Baidu business line has pricing power (search CPC is declining, AI cloud faces price wars), none has irreplaceability (ByteDance/DeepSeek are eroding its AI moat), and sustainable competitive advantage is unconfirmed.

> Duan Yongping: "If you cannot roughly estimate a company's free cash flow over the next 10 years, you should not invest in it."

### 4.2 Financials & valuation (Buffett's perspective) — ★★

**The 3 most important findings:**

1. **Profit cliff in 2025**: GAAP net income of RMB 5.6 billion (-76%), with RMB 16.2 billion in core asset impairment. But Non-GAAP net income still came in at RMB 18.9 billion — was the impairment a one-off? If there is uncertainty about the recoverability of AI assets (cloud infrastructure, the autonomous-driving platform), further impairment could recur.

2. **FCF turned negative for the first time ever**: Free cash flow was -RMB 15.1 billion, operating cash flow was -RMB 3.0 billion, and capex rose 49% to RMB 12.1 billion. At the current burn rate, the $33 billion cash pile will shrink materially within 2-3 years.

3. **Three-scenario valuation**: Optimistic (15% growth / 20x P/E) → $245 (+90%); neutral (5% growth / 15x P/E) → $140 (+9%); pessimistic (-5% growth / 10x P/E) → $69 (-46%). Hard-asset floor value is $107-112; the current price of $128.71 is only about 17% above that floor.

### 4.3 Industry & competition (Munger's perspective) — ★★

**The 3 most important findings:**

1. **The consumer AI entry point is already lost**: Doubao MAU of 345 million vs. Ernie Bot MAU of 4.79 million — of Baidu App's 679 million monthly active users, only 0.7% convert into AI-assistant users. On the "AI reshaping search" battlefield, Baidu has lost the battle for the consumer entry point.

2. **Search is being dismembered from multiple directions**: ByteDance (Doubao + Douyin Search) → Tencent (Weixin Search + Yuanbao) → DeepSeek (89% domestic market share of the open-source ecosystem) → Xiaohongshu (guide/how-to search) — every rival is chipping away at Baidu's search traffic.

3. **AI cloud share is small but growing fast**: AI cloud market share is only 6.1% (Omdia, 1H25), but Baidu has ranked #1 in large-model bid-winning for two consecutive years, serving 65% of central state-owned enterprises and every systemically important bank. Its enterprise-side capabilities are underappreciated by the market.

### 4.4 Risk & management (Li Lu's perspective) — ★★★

**The 3 most important findings:**

1. **Robin Li: an excellent technology visionary, not an excellent CEO**: Technical judgment ★★★★, execution judgment ★★, capital allocation ★★. History shows repeated failures outside his circle of competence — O2O (RMB 20 billion burned), 91 Wireless ($1.9 billion written down to zero), and Jidu Auto (collapsed in 2024).

2. **Long-term certainty has a floor but is not solid**: The most likely scenario (50% probability) — Baidu becomes a second-tier AI leader, with a stable enterprise cloud business, regionally profitable autonomous driving, but unable to reclaim the consumer entry point. The worst-case scenario (30%) — Baidu becomes "the Yahoo of the AI era."

3. **Three underappreciated catalysts**: the Kunlun chip IPO (P800 shipments of 60,000 units, orders exceeding 200,000 units), Apollo Go's global expansion (Dubai/London/Uber partnership), and the AI-conversion potential of 679 million dormant monthly active users (a conversion rate of just 0.7% implies enormous headroom).

## 5. Investment thesis — Bull vs Bear

### 🟢 Bull case

1. **$33 billion in net cash provides a hard floor**: Net cash after deducting all liabilities is about $18 billion, or 41% of market cap, limiting downside
2. **Full-stack AI capability unmatched in China**: Kunlun chips + PaddlePaddle + Ernie + Qianfan + Apollo — the only company covering the full stack from chips to applications
3. **Enterprise AI cloud is underappreciated**: Ranked #1 in large-model bid-winning for two consecutive years, serving 65% of central SOEs, 800+ financial institutions, and every systemically important bank
4. **Apollo Go is a genuine option**: #1 in global robotaxi scale (20 million+ rides), RT6 costs just 1/7th of Waymo's, with substantive breakthroughs in Dubai and London
5. **AI revenue is already accelerating the replacement of traditional revenue**: AI new-business revenue +48%, with its share of total revenue rising from ~20% in 2023 to ~31% in 2025
6. **Shareholder returns arriving for the first time**: First-ever dividend in 2026 plus a $5 billion buyback (equivalent to 11% of market cap)
7. **679 million monthly active users are a massive dormant asset**: Converting even 10% of users to high-frequency AI users (68 million) would be enough to reverse the consumer narrative

### 🔴 Bear case

1. **The core cash cow is in irreversible decline**: Search advertising revenue has fallen for six consecutive quarters; AI search is a structural disruption to the traditional bid-ranking ad model
2. **2025 free cash flow of -RMB 15.1 billion**: Operating cash flow turned negative; if this persists for 2-3 years, the net-cash safety cushion will be consumed rapidly
3. **The battle for the consumer AI entry point is already lost**: Doubao's 345 million MAU vs. Ernie Bot's 4.79 million MAU — a 72x gap
4. **Management execution is a real weakness**: History repeatedly shows Robin Li "sees the future" but "cannot get there"
5. **AI cloud gross margin undisclosed**: Growth may be coming at the cost of losses, and faces a fierce chase from Volcano Engine (ByteDance Cloud, 14.8%)
6. **A Non-GAAP P/E of 16x may not be the bottom**: If operations keep deteriorating, a falling E will inflate the P/E — "cheap" is a moving target
7. **Governance concerns**: Dual-class shares give absolute control via 59% voting power, yet decision-making mistakes keep recurring with no effective checks

## 8. Summary

Baidu is the most complex investment case in the Chinese internet sector — not because it is hard to understand, but because it sits at the peak of uncertainty in the handover between its "old self" and its "new self."

**The good**: $33 billion in net cash provides a real, hard floor; its full-stack AI capability (Kunlun chips → PaddlePaddle → Ernie → Qianfan → Apollo) is unmatched in China; its global leadership in autonomous driving is significantly underappreciated by the market. If the AI transformation succeeds, the current valuation will look extremely cheap.

**The bad**: Core search advertising is being irreversibly dismembered by multiple forces in the AI era; free cash flow turning negative in 2025 means the company is using its cash reserves to subsidize the transition; management's historical record of "seeing the future but being unable to get there" makes it hard to trust them with capital.

**The central contradiction**: Monetizing Baidu's biggest moat (full-stack AI technology) depends precisely on destroying its biggest cash cow (search advertising). This is a race against time — the new AI engine must reach escape velocity before the old engine runs out of fuel. So far, the old engine's deceleration (-14%) is outpacing the new engine's acceleration (+48% off too small a base).

Munger said: "Invert, always invert." The biggest risk in investing in Baidu is not that AI fails — AI capability is already an established fact. The biggest risk is that **even if the AI technology succeeds, the profit could still be eroded by competition and commercialization difficulties, ultimately "winning on technology, losing on the investment."**

**Final recommendation: wait and see. Two things would change this judgment — FCF turning positive, or Ernie Bot MAU breaking 50 million. Until then, Baidu is an option with a floor, not an investment with a value anchor.**
