# Nanya Technology Investment Research Report

**Research date: May 12, 2026 | Ticker: 2408.TW | #3 in the low-valuation screen**

---

## One-line conclusion

> Nanya Technology is "a mediocre business that caught a lucky break" — the PE of 6.3x used in the screen is stale data; the current forward PE is about 7.1x. The Q1 2026 profit explosion, with a 53% net margin, comes entirely from the DDR4 supply vacuum left after the three memory giants shifted production to HBM, not from any competitive advantage of its own. The US$2.5 billion strategic investment (Kioxia/SanDisk/Solidigm/Cisco) provides a 2-3 year demand cushion, but over the long run this remains a small DRAM maker with 3% market share and no moat.

## Key analysis

### The truth behind the "low PE"

A PE of 6.3x is stale data calculated from 2025 EPS of NT$2.13. As it stands today:
- TTM PE is ~24.5x (which includes the low-profit period)
- Forward PE is ~7.1x (based on 2026E EPS of NT$42.55)
- Nanya Technology is in the **early upswing** of a DRAM super-cycle, not the tail end

### The structural opportunity: the "siphon effect" of the three giants' capacity

Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron are shifting wafer capacity en masse to HBM (which consumes three times the wafer area per GB), causing DDR4/LPDDR4 supply to shrink sharply. As one of the few makers still producing legacy DRAM in volume, Nanya Technology has unexpectedly become a source of "capacity everyone is fighting over."

### The US$2.5 billion strategic investment is the key variable

The private placement completed in March 2026 had a rare lineup — NAND makers (Kioxia/SanDisk/Solidigm) and a networking giant (Cisco) all bought equity stakes in a DRAM maker, which is essentially about **locking in supply security**, paired with long-term supply agreements. This gives Nanya Technology 2-3 years of demand certainty.
