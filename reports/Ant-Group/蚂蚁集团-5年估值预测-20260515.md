## 2. Seven-Dimension Business Deep Scan

### 2.1 Payments Business: Stable Second Place, Slowly Declining Share

**Current state**:
- Alipay's domestic mobile payment share fell from 45.7% (2024, by value) to 36.2% (Q1 2025, by transaction count), while WeChat Pay rose to 59.7%
- Monthly active users approximately 890 million; "Tap to Pay" NFC users reached 200 million
- Payment fee rates continue to decline into the 0.2-0.4% range; cumulative fee reductions over 3 years exceed RMB 21.0 billion
- The duopoly structure remains stable (CR2 > 94%); Douyin Pay/UnionPay Cloud QuickPass remain second tier

**5-year outlook**:
- Payments itself is accelerating toward becoming "infrastructure," with margins narrowing
- "Tap to Pay" is a defensive innovation that can stop the bleeding but cannot reverse the trend
- Digital RMB (e-CNY 2.0) began paying interest on deposits on January 1, 2026, and has expanded cross-border reach to 50 countries; the direct impact on Alipay is limited (M0 vs. M1/M2 are different tiers), though the medium-term effect is to accelerate the "utility-ization" of payments

**Competitive assessment**: Alipay has shifted from a "profit center" to a "traffic gateway." Over the next 5 years, its share is expected to stabilize in the 31-36% range, with revenue growth of 2-3% per year.

### 2.2 Credit Business: Scale Has Peaked, Profit Stable

**Current state**:
- Ant Consumer Finance had assets of RMB 312.3 billion at year-end 2025, revenue of RMB 21.6 billion, and net profit of RMB 3.1 billion (essentially flat versus 2024)
- Huabei/Jiebei's outstanding loan balance is approximately RMB 2.1 trillion (including joint loans with partner banks); the on-balance-sheet portion is approximately RMB 300 billion (near the 10x leverage cap)
- User base of approximately 400 million, with average loan size per user declining

**Regulatory developments**:
- New rules issued by eight government departments in April 2026 adjust the bundling of payments and credit; Huabei usage frequency could decline 30-40%
- The interest rate cap discussion has moved from 24% down toward 20%, the biggest variable for the industry
- Ant Consumer Finance was fined RMB 1.4 million in March 2025 (inadequate corporate governance, insufficient independence of risk controls)

**5-year outlook**: The credit business has entered a mature phase, with growth dependent on capital injections to expand leverage. The neutral expectation is that Ant Consumer Finance plus MYbank combined net profit grows from RMB 6.2 billion to RMB 8.0-10.0 billion (5-year CAGR of 5-10%).

### 2.3 Internationalization: The Largest Growth Engine

**Current state**:
- Ant International's 2024 revenue was RMB 27.0 billion ($3.0 billion), with net profit of approximately RMB 2.2 billion
- 2025 revenue grew 20-25% to approximately $3.7 billion
- Alipay+ covers 100+ countries and regions, 90 million+ merchants, and connects 40 international payment partners and 1.8 billion user accounts
- WorldFirst covers cross-border e-commerce B2B collections and payments, with 38,000+ merchants
- Antom provides merchant-side acquiring services

**IPO progress**:
- Since May 2025, Ant Group has been in discussions with regulators regarding a possible Hong Kong IPO for Ant International; "there are currently no policy obstacles"
- No formal filing has yet been made, and there is no confirmed timeline
- Bloomberg Intelligence valuation range: $8.0-24.0 billion (wide divergence)
- Some analysts have given Ant International a valuation of RMB 200.0 billion ($27.8 billion)

**5-year outlook**:
- Ant International is Ant Group's most certain growth engine over the next 5 years
- The neutral expectation is revenue CAGR of 20-25% (from $3.7 billion → $9.0-10.0 billion), with net margin gradually rising to 10-12%
- The Saudi central bank's partnership with Alipay+ on cross-border QR code payments (launching 2026), and continued penetration into emerging markets
- Stablecoin license: Ant International plans to apply once Hong Kong's stablecoin ordinance takes effect in August 2025

**Risks**: geopolitical risk (a TikTok-style ban), competition from local digital wallets in various countries, fragmented cross-border regulation.

### 2.4 AI Strategy: Heavy Investment, Monetization Still Unproven

**Current state**:
- CEO Han Xinyi has set an "AI First" strategy (three flagship applications: AI financial steward, AI health steward, AI life steward)
- The Bailing (Ling) large-model family has released 18 models spanning language (Ling series), reasoning (Ring series), and multimodal (Ming series), all fully open-sourced
- Ring-1T is the world's first open-source trillion-parameter reasoning model (released October 2025)
- The AI health steward "AQ" receives over 10 million user queries per day on average, having served 140 million users cumulatively
- The "Zhixiaobao" AI agent is integrated into Alipay's lifestyle-service scenarios
- R&D spending was RMB 23.45 billion per year (2024), with AI-related spending's share continuing to rise

**5-year outlook**:
- AI investment is suppressing profit in the short term (the primary reason for slowing profit growth in 2025)
- In the medium term (2028-2030), AI is expected to achieve scaled monetization across three scenarios: risk control, smart investment advisory, and health management
- Optimistic scenario: AI-related revenue reaches RMB 20.0-30.0 billion by 2031, contributing net profit of RMB 3.0-5.0 billion
- Pessimistic scenario: AI investment continues to run at a loss, dragging down net profit by RMB 3.0-5.0 billion per year

**Key risk**: "Whether AI agents bypass Alipay" — if a user tells an AI agent "book me a flight," Alipay could be relegated from "user gateway" to "backend pipe," with fee rates compressed and stickiness lost. Ant is building a defensive position through "Tap to Pay" plus Lingguang plus AQ.

### 2.5 Wealth Management and Insurtech

- Ant Fund's H1 2025 revenue was RMB 9.3 billion (+22.5%), with net profit of RMB 430 million (+361%), benefiting from the A-share bull market
- Ant Insurance (an internet insurance intermediary) holds the #1 market position by scale
- Combined, the two segments can steadily contribute incremental net profit of RMB 2.0-4.0 billion per year over the next 5 years

### 2.6 Regulatory Environment

**Completed/in progress**:
- The financial-business rectification was largely completed in July 2023, with the RMB 7.123 billion fine paid
- Jack Ma's voting rights fell from 53.46% to 6.2%, with 10 members of management gaining voting rights
- The financial holding company license remains unapproved (the central bank has to date approved only 3 central-SOE-backed financial holding companies); progress is stalled

**5-year forecast**:
- The financial holding company license is likely to be granted before Ant International's IPO (70% probability)
- Regular, routine regulation will be the norm, with a low probability (80%) of a large-scale new rectification round
- 30% probability of a new round of anti-monopoly investigation into payments

### 2.7 Other Developments

- In April 2025, Ant spent HKD 2.8 billion to acquire Hong Kong's Bright Smart Securities, entering the Hong Kong securities market
- The OceanBase database continues to commercialize, having served 100+ global fintech clients

## 9. Investment Judgment

### 9.1 Valuation Assessment

Based on a current valuation of approximately RMB 700-750 billion:
- **Neutral 5-year return of +47%, annualized 8.0%** — beats the risk-free rate, slightly below long-run stock-market returns
- **Probability-weighted 5-year return of +37%, annualized 6.5%** — pulled down by the 25% pessimistic-scenario probability
- **Current P/E of approximately 18-20x** — neither especially cheap nor expensive for a company growing at 7-8%

### 9.2 The Core Tension

Ant Group's core tension today is the **timing mismatch between "short-term profit suppressed by AI investment" and "long-term growth dependent on AI and internationalization monetization."** If AI and internationalization deliver by 2028-2029, the current valuation is undervalued; if delivery is delayed or fails, the current valuation is overvalued.

### 9.3 The "Buffett Price"

Requiring a 10% annualized return plus a 20% margin of safety:
- 2031 neutral valuation of RMB 1.10 trillion / (1.10^5) / 0.80 = **~RMB 550.0 billion**
- That is, the current valuation would need to be below RMB 550.0 billion to qualify as a "safe price"
- The current RMB 700-750 billion **does not meet the Buffett standard**
