**Proposition under review**: Is it true that "Ant Group raised the value of employee stock options"?

## 0. One-line bottom line

**No official announcement or authoritative media report of "raising employee option value" was found. If anything, the counter-evidence is stronger: Alibaba's FY2026 Q3 results show its share of Ant's profit/loss plunged 93% year-over-year (a fair-value decline), and in March 2025 the 1-for-4 share split repricing cut the SER price from RMB 115.85 to RMB 108.21. The most likely "source of the rumor" is the dividend resolution passed at Ant's 2026-04-24 shareholders' meeting — employees' entitlement to dividend proceeds appears to have been misread as "options being revalued upward."**

## 4. Most likely source of the rumor

### 4.1 Leading candidate: the 2026-04-24 shareholders' meeting dividend resolution

**Original account** (from [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/2026-04-24/doc-inhvqwea4734051.shtml)):
> Ant Group's shareholders' meeting passed a profit-distribution resolution to pay a dividend to all shareholders. **Employees holding equity incentive instruments will also share in the corresponding economic benefit of the dividend.** This marks the fifth dividend since Ant's market-based financing began, with cumulative payouts exceeding RMB 50 billion.

**Why this is easily misread**:

| What actually happened | What it is easily mistaken for |
|---|---|
| Employees holding equity incentive instruments **receive a dividend** (a one-time cash distribution) | "Option value raised" |
| This is the **5th dividend** (a continuation of existing policy, not a new one) | "Options finally went up" |
| **No mention** of any adjustment to the SER unit price or buyback price | But word of mouth easily distorts this |

## 8. Practical recommendations for readers

### 8.1 Current assessment
- **Based on currently available public data, the rumor that "Ant raised employee option value" does not hold up**
- **The most recent official action with a concrete number was a decrease** (March 2025, SER RMB 115.85 → RMB 108.21)
- **Likely source of the rumor**: the 2026-04-24 dividend resolution

### 8.2 How to tell future rumors apart
**Signs of a genuine signal**:
- An official announcement (Ant / Alibaba HKEX / SEC)
- Simultaneous reporting by multiple mainstream financial media
- A specific number provided (e.g., "SER unit price 108 → XX")
- A specific date and document reference provided

**Signs of a false signal**:
- A single public-account "exclusive scoop"
- Vague phrasing like "I heard" or "reportedly"
- A number with no date, or a date with no number
- No corresponding discussion at all on internal platforms like Maimai

### 8.3 Recommendations for conservative decision-making
When evaluating options in the Ant family of companies (Ant Group / Ant Digital Technologies / OceanBase):
- **Current authoritative baseline**: SER unit price RMB 108.21, overall valuation RMB 567.1 billion
- **Do not** incorporate the optimistic assumption of a "rumored increase"
- **Liquidity discount**: unlisted options should carry a 30-50% discount
- **Key verification window**: Alibaba's annual report on 2026-05-13
