## 0. One-line core conclusion

> **Ant Group's neutral-case 2036 net profit is forecast at roughly RMB 90 billion, with a probability-weighted expected value of about RMB 85 billion, an optimistic ceiling of RMB 150 billion, and a pessimistic floor of RMB 30 billion. The implied 12-year CAGR is roughly 7-9% — this is not a high-growth stock but a "Chinese CMB + Visa option" — a steady cash cow plus a globalization option plus AI uncertainty.**

### 3.3 What users care about most: "AI agents vs. Alipay"

**This is the most underappreciated "silent tsunami" from Li Lu's vantage point**:
- **Stripe released ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol) in late 2025**; ChatGPT Instant Checkout is already live
- **The Stripe Link wallet** already supports AI agents paying on a user's behalf (virtual cards + user authorization)
- China's counterpart: Ant's "Tap-to-Pay," "Lingguang," and "AFU" — Ant is going all-in defensively
- **The core threat**: a user tells an AI agent "book me a flight," and Alipay slips from being the "user's entry point" to a "behind-the-scenes pipe"
- **The result**: take-rate compression + loss of stickiness = **a double blow**

**Judgment**: This is the most certain downside risk over the next 10 years — **whether Ant can defend its position as the "user entry point" determines the gap between RMB 130 billion and RMB 53 billion**.

## 6. Investment judgment (by how the position is held)

### 6.1 As a "free option" attached to a BABA (Alibaba) position

Alibaba owns 33% of Ant. At Ant's neutral-case 2036 valuation of RMB 1.62 trillion, 33% = **RMB 534.6 billion ≈ $74 billion USD** = roughly 28% of Alibaba's current market cap.

**Conclusion**: Alibaba shareholders **already get this Ant option for free** — there's no need to bet on it separately.

### 6.2 Intrinsic value as Ant employee stock options (SER)

- Current SER unit price is RMB 108.21 (after the 1-for-4 split in March 2025)
- If Ant's 2036 valuation reaches RMB 1.62 trillion (neutral case), the unit price would rise to **~RMB 187** (+73% over 10 years, 5.7% annualized)
- If it reaches the optimistic RMB 2.6 trillion, the unit price would be **~RMB 300** (+177% over 10 years, 10.8% annualized)
- If it stays at the pessimistic RMB 636 billion (flat), the unit price would be **~RMB 108** (0% growth over 10 years)

**For employees**: The expected annualized return on SER value falls between 5.7% and 10% — **far below the optimistic hope of "doubling."**

### 6.3 As a standalone investment

**Not recommended as a concentrated standalone bet on Ant**:
- Expected annualized return of 6.4% < the CSI 300's long-run average of 8%
- 30% downside probability plus a 10-12 year lock-up
- Only suitable as a **"growth option" sized at 5-10% of a portfolio**

**The genuinely attractive entry point**: a valuation below RMB 1.5 trillion (P/E of 10x on a forward RMB 85 billion) — only then does a 50% margin of safety make the discussion worthwhile.

| Peer companies' 10-year CAGR | **High** (from public annual reports) |

| Specific 2036 net-profit figure | **Low** (inherent uncertainty in any 10-year forecast) |

### An honest statement of disagreement among the three roles

- The **financial modeler** puts the neutral case at RMB 75.4 billion (conservative, using a 7% CAGR)
- The **industry analyst** puts the neutral case at RMB 110 billion (optimistic, using a normalized RMB 35 billion base and a 10% CAGR)
- The **risk assessor** puts the expected value at RMB 77 billion (incorporating a 27% weight on pessimistic-plus-black-swan scenarios)
- **Team-lead synthesis**: taking the median of RMB 90 billion as the neutral case, but readers should understand that **anywhere in the RMB 60-130 billion range is reasonable**

### Limitations of AI-driven investment research

The essence of a 10-year forecast is **stacking up every uncertain event over the next decade** — the evolution of CBDC, AI agents' business models, the geopolitical landscape, and regulatory posture — and the actual path of any single variable could push the forecast off by 50%+. **The real value of this report is not the specific number, but the analytical framework, the key variables, and the probability distribution.**
