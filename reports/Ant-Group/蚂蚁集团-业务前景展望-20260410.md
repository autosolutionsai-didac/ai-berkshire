# Ant Group: 3-5 Year Business Outlook by Segment

**Report date: April 10, 2026**
**Methodology: Four-agent parallel research (Payments + Credit / Wealth Management + Insurance / Internationalization / AI Innovation)**

---

## One-line summary

> Ant Group's six business lines form a pattern of "two certain-growth engines (internationalization, wealth management) + two steady defensive businesses (payments, credit) + two high-elasticity bets (AI health, financial AI agents)." Five years from now, Ant Group will have transformed from a "payments company" into an "international fintech + AI health platform."

## I. Payments: A solid #2, shifting from "profit center" to "traffic gateway"

### Core judgment

Alipay's position in payments has stabilized from "the former king" into "the permanent runner-up." Payments itself is increasingly becoming "infrastructure," and profit margins keep narrowing. "Tap-to-Pay" can stop the bleeding but can't turn the tables.

### Key data and forecasts

| Metric | 2025 status | 2028 forecast | 2030 forecast |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Market share | 36.2% | 33-36% | 31-35% |
| MAU | 890 million | 900-930 million | 900-950 million |
| "Tap-to-Pay" users | 200 million | 400-500 million | 600-700 million |
| Share of offline transaction count | ~20% | 22-25% | 23-28% |
| Industry ranking | #2 | #2 | #2 |

### Three-scenario analysis

| Scenario | Probability | 2030 share | Drivers |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Optimistic | 25% | 35-37% | Tap-to-Pay becomes mainstream; content-driven traffic acquisition succeeds |
| Baseline | 50% | 32-34% | Status quo holds; share drifts down slowly |
| Pessimistic | 25% | 29-31% | WeChat follows with NFC, Douyin rises, digital yuan siphons off volume |

### Assessment of competitive threats

| Competitor | Threat level | Time horizon | Core logic |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| WeChat Pay | Extremely high | Ongoing | Its social, high-frequency entry point is unassailable; accounts for 80% of offline transaction count |
| Douyin Pay | Medium | 3-5 years | Registered capital surged 20x to RMB 3.15 billion, but still ranked 9th |
| Digital yuan (e-CNY) | Low-to-medium → medium | 5+ years | Mostly coexistence for now, but it is accelerating the "publicization" of payments |

### Payment take-rate trend

Take rates have fallen steadily from 0.6% to a 0.2-0.4% range. Over the past three years, Alipay has cut fees by more than RMB 21 billion. Payments is increasingly behaving like public infrastructure, and the profit focus must shift toward value-added services.

## II. Credit tech: Scale has plateaued, the business-model transition is complete

### Core judgment

Ant Consumer Finance's own-book lending has already hit its capital ceiling (RMB 301.5 billion against a cap of ~RMB 300 billion); future growth depends on capital injections plus expanding joint lending. The interest-rate cap falling from 24% to 20% is the single biggest industry variable. The technology-service-fee model is healthier but "more ordinary."

### Ant Consumer Finance vs. WeBank

| Metric | Ant Consumer Finance (Huabei/Jiebei) | WeBank (Weilidai) | MYbank |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Total assets | RMB 313.7 billion | RMB 580 billion+ | ~RMB 470 billion |
| Revenue | RMB 15.2 billion | RMB 38.1 billion | RMB 21.3 billion |
| Net profit | RMB 3.05 billion | RMB 10.9 billion | RMB 3.17 billion |
| NPL ratio | 1.78% | 1.44% | 2.30% |
| Users served | 400 million+ | 70 million | 68 million |

**Ant Consumer Finance has the largest user base but its profitability lags WeBank by far**, mainly because 98% of its lending capital comes from partner banks, leaving Ant with only about a 1% technology-service fee.

### Key forecasts

**Ant Consumer Finance:**

| Metric | 2024 | 2028 baseline | 2030 baseline |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Revenue | RMB 15.2 billion | RMB 18-22 billion | RMB 20-28 billion |
| Net profit | RMB 3.05 billion | RMB 4-6 billion | RMB 5-8 billion |
| Own-book loan balance | RMB 301.5 billion | RMB 350-450 billion (requires capital injection) | RMB 400-550 billion |
| NPL ratio | 1.78% | 1.6-2.0% | 1.5-1.9% |
| Industry ranking (licensed consumer-finance companies) | #1 by scale | #1 by scale | #1 by scale |

**MYbank:**

| Metric | 2024 | 2028 baseline | 2030 baseline |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Revenue | RMB 21.3 billion | RMB 23-26 billion | RMB 25-30 billion |
| Net profit | RMB 3.17 billion | RMB 3-4.5 billion | RMB 3.5-5.5 billion |
| NPL ratio | 2.30% | 1.8-2.2% | 1.6-2.0% |

### Key risks

- The interest-rate cap fell from 24% to 20% (effective December 2025) and could be cut further to 18%
- New rules on loan-facilitation require banks to strengthen independent risk control, pressuring technology-service-fee rates
- The NPL ratio has risen for three straight years (1.69% → 1.74% → 1.78%) and could exceed 2% in an economic downturn

## III. Wealth-management tech: The most certain growth engine

### Core judgment

Ant Fund is the **undisputed leader** in internet wealth management. 2025 revenue reached RMB 20.2 billion (+24%), non-money-market AUM reached RMB 1.57 trillion (more than 50% ahead of the #2 player, China Merchants Bank), and equity-fund AUM broke through RMB 1 trillion for the first time. Even in a fee-cutting environment, it can still "make up for price with volume," making it Ant's most certain growth engine.

### Competitive landscape

| Platform | Non-money-market AUM (2025 H1) | Core user base | Competitive strategy |
|------|---------------------|-----------|---------|
| **Ant Fund** | **RMB 1.57 trillion (#1)** | Mostly Gen Z (58%) | Inclusive access + scale + AI |
| China Merchants Bank | RMB 1.04 trillion (#2) | Mass-affluent/high-net-worth | Premium service + offline branches |
| Tiantian Fund | RMB 637.4 billion (#3) | Active traders | Content community |
| Tencent LiCaiTong | Undisclosed (#4-5) | Age 35+, higher net worth | Careful curation + high ARPU |

Tiantian Fund (East Money) has seen revenue decline for three straight years through 2024, and the gap with Ant has widened sharply.

### Key forecasts

| Metric | 2025 actual | 2028 baseline | 2030 baseline |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Ant Fund revenue | RMB 20.2 billion | RMB 28-31 billion | RMB 38-42 billion |
| Ant Fund net profit | RMB 1.085 billion | RMB 1.8-2.2 billion | RMB 3-3.8 billion |
| Non-money-market AUM | RMB 1.6 trillion | RMB 2.2-2.6 trillion | RMB 3.5-4.0 trillion |
| Yu'ebao balance | ~RMB 790 billion | RMB 600-700 billion | RMB 500-650 billion (shrinking) |
| Industry ranking | #1 across all categories | #1 across all categories | #1 across all categories |
| Non-money-market share | ~15% | 18-22% | 20-25% |

### Key variables

- **Positive**: An A-share bull market would sharply boost AUM and fund-distribution revenue
- **Negative**: The third phase of fee reform (effective January 1, 2026) will cut industry-wide distribution fees by 34%
- **AI advisory**: Ant Fortune has launched three AI assistants; within 2-3 years these will become the key pillar of user retention
- **Yu'ebao**: Its 7-day annualized yield has already fallen below 1%, shifting it from a "wealth-management product" back into a "spare-change management tool," and its balance will shrink gradually

## IV. Insurance tech: High growth headroom, but the profit model is still unproven

### Core judgment

Ant Insurance ranks first among the top-100 insurance intermediaries with RMB 6.7 billion in revenue, but its net margin is only 3.6%. Internet insurance penetration is below 10% (versus 40%+ for fund distribution), leaving huge growth headroom, but the "filing-and-execution alignment" rule is compressing commission rates, and the profit model has not yet fully proven out.

### Competitive landscape

| Platform | Positioning | User base | Core strength |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| **Ant Insurance** | Comprehensive distribution platform | **600 million+** | Traffic + use cases + brand |
| Zhong An Insurance | Licensed internet insurer | — | Underwriting capacity + tech licensing |
| WeSure (Tencent) | Curated boutique storefront | 100 million+ | WeChat ecosystem |
| Waterdrop Insurance | Insurance + health | — | Lower-tier markets |

### Key forecasts

| Metric | 2023 actual | 2028 baseline | 2030 baseline |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Ant Insurance revenue | RMB 6.659 billion | RMB 9-11 billion | RMB 14-17 billion |
| Ant Insurance net profit | RMB 241 million | RMB 0.6-1 billion | RMB 1.2-1.8 billion |
| Total internet insurance premiums | RMB 494.9 billion | RMB 800 billion-1 trillion | RMB 1.5-2.8 trillion |
| Industry ranking | #1 internet insurance intermediary | #1 | #1 |

### Key variables

- **The "filing-and-execution alignment" rule**: first-year commissions have been cut in half; 2024-2026 is a digestion period, after which compliant leading platforms should benefit
- **The 2025 edition of the Haoyibao medical plan**: 70 million users, now covering international/VIP wards and private hospitals
- **Scenario-based insurance**: return-shipping insurance was Ant's original innovation, but a high return rate creates significant claims pressure

## V. Internationalization: The biggest growth engine, with an endgame of "Visa of Asia-Pacific"

### Core judgment

Ant International is Ant Group's fastest-growing segment — RMB 27 billion in 2024 revenue (20% of the total) — and plans an independent listing in Hong Kong. Its endgame is not "a global PayPal" but **a "Visa of Asia-Pacific" — the underlying interoperability infrastructure for digital payments across the Asia-Pacific region**.

## VI. AI and innovation: AFU is the most likely second growth curve

Ant does not lead in overall AI scale (it trails ByteDance, Alibaba Cloud, and Tencent), but it holds leading positions in three vertical tracks: **financial AI, health AI, and agentic payments**. AFU (Ant's AI health app) is the most likely candidate for a second growth curve, while Ant Digital Technologies (OceanBase + financial AI) is the more certain revenue contributor.

### Evolution of business mix

```
2024 (current)                     2030 (5-year baseline)
┌─────────────────────┐          ┌─────────────────────┐
│ Payments   ~25-28%  │          │ International ~30%  │ ← Biggest growth
│ Credit tech ~40%    │    →     │ Credit tech   ~25%  │
│ Wealth mgmt ~12-15% │          │ Wealth mgmt   ~17%  │ ← Steady growth
│ International ~20%  │          │ Payments      ~12%  │ ← Declining share
│ Insurance/AI  ~5%   │          │ Insurance+AI  ~16%  │ ← Emerging force
└─────────────────────┘          └─────────────────────┘
```

### Ranking of segments by 3-5 year growth speed

| Rank | Business | Annual growth (baseline) | Driver |
|------|------|-------------|--------|
| 1 | AI and innovation | 40%+ | AFU passing 100 million users, financial AI going live, OceanBase's domestic-substitution push |
| 2 | Internationalization | 25% | Alipay+ network expansion, WorldFirst cross-border e-commerce, IPO |
| 3 | Wealth-management tech | 18-20% | Continued expansion of non-money-market AUM, making up for price with volume |
| 4 | Insurance tech | 15-20% | Rising penetration (from <10% to 20%), after the filing-alignment digestion period |
| 5 | Credit tech | 6-10% | Needs capital injections to break through the ceiling; interest-rate cap remains a constraint |
| 6 | Payments | 2-3% | Mature stage, share drifting down slowly |

### Overall industry-position assessment in 5 years

| Business | Current position | Position in 5 years | Trend |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Payments | #2 (36.2%) | #2 (31-35%) | Holding steady but share slipping slightly |
| Consumer credit (licensed consumer-finance) | #1 by scale | #1 by scale | Steady |
| Internet wealth management / fund distribution | #1 (commanding lead) | #1 (lead widening) | Strengthening |
| Internet insurance intermediary | #1 | #1 | Steady |
| Asia-Pacific cross-border payments | Top 3 | #1 | Major improvement |
| Financial AI | #1 | #1 | Strengthening |
| AI health | #1 (AFU) | #1-2 | Depends on monetization |

### Final judgment

**In 5 years, Ant Group will no longer be a "Chinese payments company" but an "Asia-Pacific fintech infrastructure + AI health platform" company.** Internationalization and AI are the two engines driving this transformation; wealth-management tech provides steady profit growth; payments and credit provide the cash-flow base.

The biggest upside risk is that AFU finds a "medical + pharmaceutical + insurance" monetization loop, unlocking a trillion-plus-RMB health market; the biggest downside risk is that the financial holding-company license remains stalled indefinitely while geopolitics worsens, stalling both internationalization and the IPO.
