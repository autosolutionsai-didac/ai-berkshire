> "The standard for a good business: you can picture what it will look like ten years from now, it has a moat, and management is trustworthy." — Duan Yongping

**1. The Essence of the Business Model**

Gpixel's business is **the design and sale of high-end CMOS image sensors**, operating under a fabless (no in-house fab) model. The company only does chip design, outsources manufacturing to foundries, and then sells the finished products to B2B customers such as industrial equipment makers, scientific instrument makers, and medical device manufacturers.

This is a classic **technology-driven business**:
- **High gross margin** (67%) shows the product has genuine pricing power

**3. The Double-Edged Sword of the Fabless Model — Li Lu's Deep Dive**

**Advantages**:
- Asset-light, high ROE, no need to spend billions of dollars building fabs
- Focused on design, with high talent efficiency
- Can choose whichever foundry process best fits the need

**Fatal Disadvantages**:
- **Manufacturing is uncontrollable**: When foundry capacity is tight, large customers can crowd it out, and when sanctions hit, it cannot save itself
- **Process know-how leakage**: Process expertise accumulated by the foundry could theoretically serve competitors as well
- **Cost opacity**: When the foundry raises prices, it has little bargaining power (Gpixel's scale is too small)

The prospectus discloses that Gpixel is "highly dependent on overseas foundries," which is a **structural risk** in the current geopolitical environment. Even absent direct sanctions, foundries may proactively limit cooperation out of compliance concerns.

- Although remediation has been carried out, these issues reflect a governance structure that is not fully clear

**Li Lu's likely take**: "The two things that worry me most: first, under a chip-sanctions regime, the fabless model effectively hands your fate to someone else; second, for a company spun off from a state research institute, the risks of related-party transactions and related benefit transfers need long-term monitoring.
