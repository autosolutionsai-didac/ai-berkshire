# "Understanding Ant Group" Series Overview

> Five main articles that peel back Ant Group layer by layer, revealing the true structure of this company after its regulatory overhaul.
>
> The series takes about 75 minutes to read in total; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series positioning

Ant Group is **the most unusual company** in China's internet world — it once came within two days of what would have been the largest IPO in history, only to have it halted overnight.

- October 2020: valued at **RMB 2.1 trillion**, on the verge of the world's largest-ever IPO
- November 2020: the IPO was suspended, kicking off three years of regulatory rectification
- July 2023: paid a **RMB 7.123 billion fine** and repurchased employee shares at a valuation of roughly **RMB 567.1 billion** — a 73% evaporation in valuation
- 2025: net profit plunged from RMB 38.3 billion to roughly **RMB 15.3 billion**, while R&D spending hit a record high of **RMB 28.98 billion**
- Since May 2025: media reports say Ant International plans an independent listing in Hong Kong; as of May 2026, no filing has been made with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, and the group as a whole remains unlisted

**Five years on, what has changed at Ant — and what hasn't? What is the post-overhaul Ant actually worth?**

This series sets out to **pull apart Ant before and after the overhaul and lay its real structure bare** — rather than handing you a ready-made verdict on whether it's worth investing in.

## Series writing principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** — no presumption of a bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment is paired with a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it for themselves
3. **Ant is an unlisted company, so some figures are estimates** — anything estimated is labeled "est." or "unofficial," never dressed up as precise
4. **No "probability-weighted expected return" calculations** — assigning weights to scenarios is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **This does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article

## Data sources

This series draws on:
- Alibaba's FY2025-2026 financial reports (Ant's profit inferred via the equity method)
- Ant Group's 2020 IPO prospectus and historical public disclosures
- Annual reports of subsidiaries such as Ant Consumer Finance, MYbank, and Ant Fund
- The Hurun Global Unicorn List and UpMarket secondary-market data
- Third-party data from QuestMobile, iResearch, and others
- Internal ai-berkshire project research drafts (cross-validated across multiple independent reports)

All third-party data is clearly attributed to its source; estimates and inferred figures are labeled "est." or "unofficial."
