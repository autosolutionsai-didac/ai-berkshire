# Tencent / Meituan / Pinduoduo: 5-Year Share Price Correlation Analysis

> **Report date**: 2026-05-01
> **Data window**: 2021-05-03 ~ 2026-04-30 (261 weeks total)
> **Data source**: Yahoo Finance weekly closing prices (0700.HK / 3690.HK / PDD)
> **Method**: ISO-week alignment, Pearson correlation (price level + weekly log returns), rolling phase-by-phase correlation, timing gap between peaks and troughs

---

## I. Core Conclusions (Quick Read)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Do the peaks and troughs occur at the same time?** | **No — completely out of sync** |
| Largest gap between the three peaks | **1,561 days** (Tencent Sep-2025 vs. Meituan Jun-2021, a 4.3-year gap) |
| Largest gap between the three troughs | **693 days** (Meituan Jan-2024 vs. PDD Mar-2022, a 1.9-year gap) |
| Weekly-return correlation | Moderate-to-strong (**0.48 ~ 0.68**) |
| Price-level correlation | **Almost uncorrelated** (-0.30 ~ +0.36) |
| 5-year cumulative change | Tencent **-15%** / Meituan **-71%** / PDD **-26%** |
| Correlation shift post-2025 | **Falls sharply** (down to as low as +0.17) → market begins pricing each name on its own fundamentals |

**One-line summary**: **The three companies move together in the short term on a shared "China-concept-stock sentiment" (weekly correlation of 0.5-0.7), but over the long run each follows its own independent trajectory, with peaks and troughs completely out of alignment.**

## IV. Correlation Analysis

### 4.1 Price-Level Correlation (Long-Run Co-Movement)

| Pair | Pearson r | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Tencent vs. Meituan | **+0.09** | **Essentially uncorrelated** |
| Tencent vs. PDD | +0.36 | Weakly correlated |
| Meituan vs. PDD | **-0.30** | **Negatively correlated** (move in opposite directions) |

**Implication**: over the long run, the "price levels" of the three companies are nearly independent of one another. This is because, over 5 years, each has followed a completely different growth trajectory — Tencent fell then recovered, Meituan declined steadily, and PDD traced an inverted V.

> **Do not be misled by "price correlation"**: this figure is dominated mainly by long-term trend and does not reflect short-term co-movement. The "return correlation" below is the more accurate measure.

### 4.2 Weekly Return Correlation (Short-Term Co-Movement, More Central)

| Pair | Pearson r | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Tencent vs. Meituan** | **+0.68** | **Strongly correlated** — both large-cap Hong Kong stocks, traded by the same pool of capital |
| Tencent vs. PDD | +0.53 | Moderately correlated |
| Meituan vs. PDD | +0.48 | Moderately correlated |

**Implication**: at the weekly level, the three stocks do show significant co-movement — an expression of the "China-concept-stock beta." When a macro risk hits (the Fed, US-China relations, regulation), they fall together; when sentiment improves, they rise together. But Tencent and Meituan (**both Hong Kong-listed**) show a markedly higher correlation than any cross-market pairing.

### 4.3 Phase-by-Phase Weekly Return Correlation (How Correlation Has Shifted)

| Phase | Period | Tencent-Meituan | Tencent-PDD | Meituan-PDD | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | 2021-05 ~ 2022-05 | **+0.81** | +0.65 | +0.68 | **Sold off together by regulation** — the highest correlation |
| Phase 2 | 2022-05 ~ 2023-05 | +0.70 | +0.52 | +0.57 | China-concept stocks recovering as a group |
| Phase 3 | 2023-05 ~ 2024-05 | +0.66 | +0.43 | +0.22 | PDD breaks out on its own thanks to Temu |
| Phase 4 | 2024-05 ~ 2025-05 | +0.71 | +0.55 | +0.37 | Divergence between Hong Kong and US listings |
| **Phase 5** | 2025-05 ~ 2026-04 | **+0.32** | +0.37 | **+0.17** | **Correlation falls sharply, independent pricing** |

**Key trend**: **correlation has been declining steadily over the 5 years** — the Meituan-PDD figure in particular falls from +0.68 to +0.17, essentially independent.
- Early on: driven by a shared "regulatory panic" → high correlation
- Later: each company's own fundamentals begin to dominate → low correlation
- This is **exactly the shift a fundamentals-driven investor most wants to see**

## V. Why Are the Peaks and Troughs So Out of Sync?

### 5.1 The Logic Behind Meituan's 2021 Peak → Its Steady Decline

```
2021-06: The platform-economy antitrust storm hits — the sharpest immediate blow
2022:    Community group-buying cash burn + double hit from dining-sector lockdowns
2023:    Douyin's full-scale assault on local-services food delivery
2024-01: Overseas Keeta investment not yet paying off → new low
2024-2026: Slow recovery, but no new growth story
```
Meituan's core conflict: **its core food-delivery/in-store business growth has peaked + Douyin is taking its market share + the overseas story has not yet been proven out**. This is fundamentals deteriorating alongside intensifying competition — **with no "double-upside" catalyst anywhere in sight**.

### 5.2 The Logic Behind PDD's 2024-05 Peak → Its Subsequent Decline

```
2022-03: The lowest point for China-concept stocks (Russia-Ukraine war + SEC delisting risk) → $32
2022-2024: Temu's global breakout + continued domestic growth → rises all the way to $158
2024-05: Q1 earnings beat expectations → peak
2024-08: Q2 earnings — management warns of margin compression → single-day -28%
2025-2026: Trump tariffs + elimination of the $800 de minimis exemption → Temu's model comes under pressure
```
PDD's core conflict: **Temu's success has been so great that it has drawn US tariff retaliation — its business model is now being pulled into the regulatory crosshairs**.

### 5.3 The Logic Behind Tencent's 2025-09 Peak → Its Subsequent Decline

```
2022-10: China-concept-stock low + Hong Kong liquidity drought → HK$191
2023-2024: Large-scale buybacks + Video Accounts monetization + gaming recovery → slow repair
2025-09: AI-narrative peak + spillover excitement from DeepSeek → HK$673
2025-Q4: AI spending doubling + concerns over reduced buybacks → decline
2026-Q1: Tax-hike rumors + Agentic AI falling short of expectations → continued grinding decline
```
Tencent's core conflict: **heavy AI investment plus reduced buybacks are making the market nervous about capital allocation in the short term**, even though the underlying fundamentals (WeChat, gaming, advertising) have not deteriorated.

### 5.4 The Three Companies' "Fundamental Drivers" Are Completely Different

| Company | Primary driver | Current position |
|---|---|---|
| Tencent | WeChat ecosystem, gaming, AI | Digesting the AI investment cycle |
| Meituan | Food-delivery competition, overseas expansion, margins | Defending its core business |
| PDD | Temu's global expansion, tariffs | Facing tariff uncertainty |

**This is precisely why the peaks and troughs are out of sync**: when Meituan was being hit by regulation (2021), Tencent was not yet doing so badly; when PDD was hit by the SEC (2022), Meituan was still trading above 100; when Tencent was rallying on AI (2025), Meituan was already lying flat at the bottom.

## VI. Implications for Investment Decisions

### 6.1 The Three Are Not "Substitutes" — They Are "Complements"

If you build a portfolio of all three:
- **You will not gain diversification benefits** (because weekly returns are correlated at 0.5-0.7)
- **But you will gain exposure to different fundamentals** (fully out-of-sync peaks and troughs show each has its own independent story)
- **In essence, this is a combination of "China-concept-stock beta plus three independent alphas"**

### 6.2 How to Choose Among the Three?

| Dimension | Tencent | Meituan | PDD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-year cumulative decline | -15% | -71% | -26% |
| Current distance from high | -30% | -75% | -37% |
| Moat | Deepest | Moderate (threatened by Douyin) | Shallow but fast-growing |
| Direction of fundamentals | Stable + AI optionality | Defensive, searching for a new growth story | Tariff uncertainty |
| Valuation (reference) | Core-business PE ~12 | PE ~15 | PE ~10 |
| **Value-investing lens** | **First choice** | Turnaround candidate | High-risk, high-reward |

### 6.3 Key Takeaway: Wait for "Everything to Fall Together"

**The best buying opportunity comes when the correlation across all three approaches 0.9** — that signals indiscriminate panic selling with no regard for fundamentals (as in 2022-10). The current phase (Phase 5, correlation down to 0.17-0.37) shows the market is already pricing them apart by fundamentals — **this is not a panic moment, and the right approach is to select whichever name has the strongest fundamentals**.

## VII. Statistical Notes

### 7.1 Data Completeness

- Overlapping observation weeks: **261 weeks**
- Spans 5 full calendar years
- Data source: Yahoo Finance's public weekly API
- Alignment method: ISO week (to resolve timestamp offsets between Hong Kong and US market data)

### 7.2 Limitations

1. **Price-level correlation is dominated by long-term trend**, so a low value does not mean there is "no short-term co-movement"
2. **Pearson correlation assumes a linear relationship**, and may not adequately capture extreme events (black swans)
3. **No currency adjustment was made**: Tencent/Meituan are priced in HKD, PDD in USD — but since the analysis is based on returns, the currency effect is limited
4. **The 5-year window happens to span a complete "regulation → recovery → divergence" cycle**, and the conclusions may not hold for other cycles

### 7.3 Further Analysis That Could Be Done

- Rolling 12-week correlation (to observe dynamic changes)
- Regression against the Hang Seng Tech Index / China-concept-stock ETFs (to extract beta and alpha)
- Event-driven analysis (the impact of regulatory milestones, earnings releases, and AI-related events)

## VIII. Conclusion

> **The three companies move together in the short term on a shared "China-concept-stock sentiment" (weekly return correlation of 0.48-0.68), but are entirely independent over the long run — their 5-year highs span 4.3 years, their 5-year lows span 1.9 years, and their 5-year cumulative performance differs by as much as 55 percentage points.**
>
> **This means:**
> - **You cannot treat the three as "the same thing"** — they have entirely different fundamental drivers
> - **If your goal is to diversify away China-concept-stock beta risk, a three-name portfolio does little to help** (the beta is shared)
> - **But if your goal is to cover different sub-sectors of the Chinese internet, a three-name portfolio makes sense** (the alpha is independent)
> - **The current phase (2025-2026), with correlation falling sharply, is a good window for fundamentals-driven investors** — the market is no longer moving in lockstep without regard for fundamentals
