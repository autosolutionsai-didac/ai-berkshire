# Micron Technology (MU) In-Depth Investment Research Report

**Research date: May 12, 2026 | Share price: $789.35 | Market cap: approx. $889B**

---

## One-Line Conclusion

Micron is at the peak of an AI-driven "memory super-cycle." Its HBM business is transforming it from a cyclical commodity company into a high-barrier AI infrastructure supplier, but current valuation (TTM PE of 35x, PB of 10-12x) already fully reflects optimistic expectations. History shows that cyclicality in the memory industry has never truly been eliminated — investors must judge between the "this time is different" narrative and the iron law of mean reversion.

## Dimension One: Business Model Analysis (Duan Yongping's Perspective)

### What Has HBM Changed?
- **High degree of customization**: co-designed with specific GPUs, with a 6-12 month certification cycle
- **Demand exceeds supply**: currently able to meet only 50-66% of core customers' demand
- **Long-term-contract model**: price and volume locked in via long-term agreements

### What HBM Has Not Changed
- Product performance across the three suppliers is converging
- Price competition will return once supply and demand rebalance
- At its core, it is still stacked and packaged DRAM

### Assessment Against Duan Yongping's Standard
- High gross margin but unstable (swinging from 81% to negative)
- Classic asset-heavy business (FY2026 CapEx >$25.0 billion)
- Pricing power partially improved but not permanent
- **Conclusion: not a "good business," but HBM has moved it from "bad" to "okay"**

## Dimension Three: Industry and Competition Analysis (Munger's Perspective)

### The Big-Three Structure

| Company | DRAM Market Share | HBM Market Share |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Samsung | ~40% | ~22% |
| SK Hynix | ~35% | ~57% |
| Micron | ~25% | ~21% |

### HBM Competition
- SK Hynix holds a commanding lead (57% share)
- Samsung is expanding HBM capacity by 50% in 2026, to 250,000 wafers per month
- Micron ranks second/third, but leads on technology speed (>11Gbps)

### Chinese Competitors
- **CXMT**: monthly capacity expanded from 100,000 to 290,000 wafers, pressuring the low end of DRAM
- **YMTC**: NAND shipment share has reached 13%
- Key constraint: no HBM production capability

### Non-Consensus View
The market may be underestimating the long-term memory demand driven by AI inference. Inference deployment scale could be more than 10x that of training, and the HBM demand cycle could be longer than expected.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (7 points)
1. The AI super-cycle is real — Q3 guidance of $33.5 billion in a single quarter exceeds any historical full year
2. HBM demand will exceed supply through 2027
3. HBM4 technology leads on speed
4. Forward PE is only 7.7x (FY2027E)
5. Strong balance sheet (net debt near zero)
6. Excellent management
7. China risk has already been absorbed (revenue share down to 7%)

### Bear Case (7 points)
1. The memory cycle has never disappeared — every "this time is different" claim over 50 years has been disproven
2. PB of 10-12x is a historical high
3. Samsung is expanding HBM capacity by 50%
4. $25.0 billion+ in annual capex creates downside risk on the way down the cycle
5. Chinese manufacturers are rising in low-end DRAM/NAND
6. AI capex cycle is uncertain
7. CEO selling at highs
