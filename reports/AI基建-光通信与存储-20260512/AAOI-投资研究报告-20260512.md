## One-Sentence Conclusion

AAOI is a small, vertically integrated manufacturer riding the AI optical-module wave, with revenue growing explosively (from $455.7 million in 2025 to guidance of $1.1 billion in 2026). But the company has a long history of losses, negative cash flow, heavy insider selling, and severe share dilution (66% in one year), while its valuation is already stretched to an extreme (TTM P/S of 31.9x) and two short-sellers — Citron and Culper — have both publicly targeted the stock. This is a textbook high-volatility speculative play, not a value investment.

## Key Analysis Points

### Not a good business (Duan Yongping's standard)
- 29 years since founding, never sustainably profitable
- Extremely weak pricing power (a B2B business facing hyperscale customers with strong bargaining leverage)
- Extremely capital-intensive (2025 CapEx equaled 45.9% of revenue)
- Squeezed from both sides competitively: Coherent and Lumentum above, Innolight below

### 800G revenue is overstated
- In Q1 2026, 800G revenue was just $4.6 million (3% of total revenue)
- The bulk of data-center revenue is still legacy 100G and 400G product
- The explosive-growth phase for 800G has not truly arrived yet

### Short-seller allegations
- **Culper Research**: questions the authenticity of the 800G capacity and customer relationships
- **Citron**: calls the valuation "crazy," saying it prices "commodity economics at a monopoly premium"
