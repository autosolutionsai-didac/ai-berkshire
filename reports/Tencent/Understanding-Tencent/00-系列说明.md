## Why We Wrote This Series

Tencent is one of China's largest companies by market cap, one of the most covered by institutions, and one of the most researched — but it's also one of the most misunderstood.

- Some treat it as a **social media company** — ignoring that games are actually its biggest profit driver;
- Some treat it as a **gaming company** — ignoring the network effects of the WeChat ecosystem;
- Some treat it as an **internet platform** — ignoring the RMB 1 trillion in equity assets sitting on its balance sheet;
- Some treat it as an **AI laggard** — ignoring its "AI in everything" push across advertising, entry points, and e-commerce scenarios;
- And others treat it as a **pure value stock** — ignoring its RMB 180 billion in annual free cash flow and RMB 120 billion in shareholder returns.

Each perspective is correct on its own — it's only by layering them together that you get the real Tencent. A single 5,000-word piece can't fit it all in, and cramming it in would make it unreadable. So we've split it into **8 episodes**, each focused on one axis, which together add up to a complete picture of Tencent.
