## 0. Four-sentence summary (answering the user's core questions)

### Q1: What is the current market value of all of Tencent's investments?
**Carrying value totals RMB 1,035.8 billion (approximately USD 144.0 billion)**: fair value of listed investments of RMB 672.7 billion + carrying value of unlisted investments of RMB 363.1 billion. If unlisted investments were revalued at fair value (+30-50%), the **total would be approximately RMB 1,140-1,220 billion**. This represents **24% (carrying value) / 27-29% (fair value)** of Tencent's total market capitalization (HKD 4.67 trillion).

### Q2: How much cash does it currently have?
**Total cash of RMB 494.9 billion** (+19% year-over-year):
- Cash and cash equivalents: RMB 189.2 billion
- Time deposits: RMB 194.6 billion
- Short-term highly liquid investments: RMB 111.1 billion
- Less interest-bearing debt of RMB 277.5 billion = **net cash position of RMB 107.1 billion**

### Q3: What are the total assets and liabilities?
- **Total assets: RMB 2,039.0 billion** (+14.5%)
- **Total liabilities: RMB 797.9 billion**
- **Shareholders' equity: RMB 1,241.1 billion** (attributable to owners: RMB 1,154.2 billion + non-controlling interests: RMB 86.9 billion)
- Debt-to-asset ratio of **39.1%** (one of the healthiest among Chinese internet companies)

### Q4: How is the investment market value reflected on the balance sheet?
**Under IFRS 9, four classifications apply, and market value is reflected in entirely different ways**:
- **Controlled subsidiaries** (Riot/Supercell/Miniclip) → consolidated financial statements (no separate "investment" line item exists)
- **Associates** (Kuaishou/Bilibili/ZhongAn, etc.) → equity method, "investments in associates" line item (**does not reflect market value**)
- **FVTPL** (most holdings such as Pinduoduo/JD.com/Meituan post-dividend/Spotify/Snap) → "financial assets" (**fair value changes flow directly through the income statement**)
- **FVOCI** (a small number of strategic equity stakes) → "other financial assets" (fair value changes flow to OCI, **not through the income statement**)

**This is why Tencent's GAAP net profit fluctuates sharply while its Non-GAAP profit is comparatively stable** — the investment classification determines how market-value swings flow into the income statement.

## 3. How is investment market value reflected on the balance sheet? (the core accounting mechanism)

### 3.1 Four classifications of financial assets under IFRS 9

| Classification | Qualifying condition | Balance sheet location | Market-value change pathway | Tencent's application |
|------|---------|---------------|------------|---------|
| **Controlling consolidation** | Control (>50%) | Fully consolidated, non-controlling interest disclosed | N/A (revenue/profit consolidated) | Riot, Supercell (Halti 51.2%), Miniclip, Funcom |
| **Equity method (IAS 28)** | Significant influence (20-50% or board seat) | "Investments in associates" | **Does not reflect market value! Share of profit/loss recognized as stake % × net profit** | Kuaishou, Bilibili, ZhongAn, BOSS Zhipin, WeBank 30%, Futu |
| **FVTPL (fair value through profit or loss)** | Default for anything not meeting other conditions | "Financial assets (current/non-current)" | **Fair value changes flow directly through the income statement** | Pinduoduo, JD.com (post dividend), Meituan (post dividend), Spotify, Snap, most unlisted holdings |
| **FVOCI (fair value through other comprehensive income)** | Irrevocably designated strategic equity stakes | "Other financial assets" | Fair value changes flow to OCI, **never recycled to profit or loss** | A small number of strategic holdings |
| **Amortized cost** | Held to collect contractual cash flows | Cash/bonds | Does not reflect market value | Cash management, time deposits |

### 3.2 Key insight: the equity method does not reflect market value!

**The Meituan case** (Tencent held a 17% stake before the 2022 distribution):
- Meituan's share price fell from HKD 460 to HKD 60 (-87%) between 2021 and 2022
- Yet Tencent's financial statements **only reflected a "share of Meituan's losses"** (proportional to the 17% stake), not the collapse in market value
- The gap between Tencent's "carrying value" and "market value" of Meituan reached hundreds of billions of RMB
- This is exactly why the 2022-11 distribution **triggered a one-time gain/loss on disposal** (fair value minus equity-method carrying value)

### 3.3 The direct pass-through of FVTPL

**The Pinduoduo case** (Tencent holds ~14%, classified as FVTPL):
- Every 50% rise in Pinduoduo's share price → Tencent's income statement gains +X (RMB 100M), directly pre-tax
- A 50% decline → Tencent's income statement loses -X (RMB 100M)
- **This is the primary driver of the sharp swings in Tencent's GAAP net profit in 2021-2022**

### 3.4 The root of the GAAP vs. Non-GAAP divergence

| Year | GAAP net profit (RMB billion) | Non-GAAP net profit (RMB billion) | Reason for the difference |
|------|------------------|--------------------|---------|
| 2020 | +58.3 billion investment gain | 130.0 billion | GAAP exceeded Non-GAAP |
| 2021 | 224.8 (includes one-time gain from JD.com distribution) | 123.7 | One-time items |
| 2022 | 188.2 (includes gain from Sea Limited reduction) | 115.6 | One-time gains offset fair-value losses |
| 2023 | 115.2 | 157.7 | Investment recovery not fully reflected |
| **2024** | **194.0 (+68%)** | **222.7 (+41%)** | Investment recovery + JD.com/Meituan dividend reclassification |
| **2025** | **224.8** | **estimated ~250.0** | Continued recovery |

**Lesson**: **when evaluating Tencent, one must separate "operating profit" from "investment gains/losses"** — simplistically applying "GAAP net profit × PE" severely distorts the valuation.

### 3.5 Accounting treatment for in-kind dividend distributions (a very special case)

**JD.com distributed 2021-12, Meituan distributed 2022-11**:
1. Dividend payable is measured at fair value on the declaration date (IFRIC 17)
2. **Fair value minus equity-method carrying value = one-time gain on disposal recognized through profit or loss**
3. Once the post-distribution stake falls below 20%, the remaining portion is **reclassified from the equity method to FVTPL**
4. It is then re-measured at fair value again, with the difference flowing through profit or loss

**Result**: Tencent used the distributions to "**unlock**" market-value appreciation that had been "hidden" in the portfolio (equity-method carrying value below market value), while simultaneously paying shareholders an in-kind dividend (equivalent to USD 13.0 billion in JD.com shares + USD 20.4 billion in Meituan shares).

## 9. Core conclusions

1. **Tencent is the dual champion of "healthiest balance sheet" and "largest investment portfolio" among Chinese internet companies**: net cash of RMB 107.1 billion + investment portfolio at fair value of RMB 1.14 trillion (approximately 27-29% of total market cap).

2. **The Q4 2025 pullback in the investment portfolio (from RMB 1.14 trillion to RMB 1.04 trillion)** reflects: the Q4 pullback in Hong Kong/US-listed Chinese ADRs + Tencent's active reduction of stakes + capital being diverted to AI capex.

3. **The core difference between GAAP and Non-GAAP lies in "fair value changes on investments"**: the investment classification determines how market-value swings flow into the income statement. **A simple P/E cannot be used to evaluate Tencent** — a sum-of-the-parts (SOTP) approach (core business + investment portfolio) is required.

4. **The "China's Berkshire Hathaway" narrative is taking hold**: from 2024-2026, cumulative buybacks plus dividends have exceeded HKD 200 billion, marking a shift from "expansion" to "returns."

5. **The next shift in asset structure**: AI capex (RMB 79.2 billion in 2025, planned to double in 2026) + rapid PP&E growth → a transition from an "asset-light ecosystem company" toward a "moderately asset-heavy AI company."
