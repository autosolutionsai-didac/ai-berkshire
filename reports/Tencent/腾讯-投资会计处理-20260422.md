# Tencent (0700.HK) Investment Portfolio — Deep Dive into IFRS Accounting Treatment

**ai-berkshire Tencent Investment Accounting Specialist | 2026-04-22**

## 1. Core conclusion

The accounting treatment of Tencent's investment portfolio is **extremely complex and highly fragmented**. Controlled subsidiaries (Riot, Supercell) are fully consolidated; associates (Kuaishou, Bilibili, BOSS Zhipin, etc.) are recognized under the equity method, booking a share of net profit in proportion to ownership without reflecting market-value swings; the vast majority of non-associate listed investments (Pinduoduo, Spotify, Snap, etc.) are classified as **FVTPL**, so fair-value changes hit the income statement directly; and a small number of strategic equity stakes are designated as **FVOCI**, insulated from the income statement. **This structure causes Tencent's GAAP net profit to swing enormously** (down roughly 47% year over year in 2021, falling again in 2022, then rebounding sharply in 2023-2024), while adjusted net profit (Non-GAAP) is comparatively stable — **this is the first-principles key to understanding Tencent's income statement.**

## 2. The 4 classifications of financial assets under IFRS 9

### A. Measured at amortized cost
- Condition: business model is "held to collect contractual cash flows" + passes the SPPI test
- Typical examples: accounts receivable, bonds held to maturity
- **Market-value fluctuations are not reflected during the holding period**
- **Tencent's application**: large-scale cash management, structured deposits

### B. Fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI)
**Equity instrument variant (irrevocable designation)**:
- Fair-value changes are recognized entirely in OCI and **never recycle to the income statement**
- On disposal, cumulative OCI changes can only be transferred to retained earnings
- **Tencent's application**: a small number of long-term strategic equity stakes

### C. Fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL)
- Condition: the default classification for assets that don't meet the above conditions
- Fair-value changes flow **entirely and directly into the current period's income statement**
- **Tencent's application**: **the bulk of the investment portfolio** — listed holdings such as Pinduoduo, Kuaishou, Sea, Spotify, Snap, Tesla, and others; most unlisted investments

### D. Equity method (IAS 28)
- Condition: significant influence (20-50% ownership + board representation)
- Investment income recognized as **ownership percentage × investee's net profit**
- **Market-value fluctuations are not reflected at all**
- Impairment testing per IAS 36 (when recoverable amount < carrying value)
- **Tencent's application**: Kuaishou, Bilibili, BOSS Zhipin, ZhongAn, WeBank (30%), Futu, and others

## 3. Classification framework for Tencent's investment portfolio

| Investment type | Accounting classification | Balance-sheet location | Income-statement impact | Is market-value fluctuation reflected? |
|---------|---------|--------------|---------|---------------|
| Controlled subsidiaries (Riot, Supercell (consolidated), Miniclip, Funcom) | Consolidated financial statements | Revenue/costs fully consolidated | Direct impact | N/A |
| Associates (Kuaishou, Bilibili, BOSS Zhipin, WeBank, Futu) | Equity method (IAS 28) | "Investments in associates" | Share of profit/loss in proportion to ownership | **No** |
| Joint ventures | Equity method | "Investments in joint ventures" | Share of profit/loss | **No** |
| Non-associate listed investments (Pinduoduo, Spotify, Snap, residual Sea stake) | FVTPL | Financial assets | Fair-value changes + dividends flow fully into P&L | **Yes (directly)** |
| Unlisted minority stakes (numerous VC/PE holdings) | Mostly FVTPL | Long-term financial assets | Valuation remarks flow into P&L | **Yes (indirectly)** |
| A small number of strategic equity stakes | FVOCI | Other financial assets | Only dividends flow into P&L | **Yes, but not through P&L** |

**Key insight**: Tencent's disclosed "fair value of investment portfolio" was approximately RMB 1,035.8 billion at year-end 2025. **Associates are recorded at book value, not market value**, so the book-value basis significantly understates true market value.

## 4. Specific treatment under the equity method

### Kuaishou case (Tencent holds ~17-19%)

**Annual process**:
1. Opening-of-year carrying value: RMB X (in hundreds of millions)
2. Share of Kuaishou's net profit for the year: 18% × Kuaishou's net profit → recognized as Tencent's "share of profits of associates"
3. Kuaishou dividend distribution: Tencent receives cash → carrying value is reduced
4. Kuaishou share-price movements: **not reflected in Tencent's financial statements**
5. Impairment test: if market value remains significantly below carrying value (a decline of 30%+ sustained over 12+ months), impairment is required

**Meituan case (before the 2022 dividend distribution, 17% stake)**:
- From 2021 to 2022, Meituan's share price fell from HKD 460 to HKD 60 (-87%), but Tencent's financial statements **reflected only its share of losses**, not the collapse in market value
- The gap between carrying value and market value reached hundreds of billions of RMB
- This is why the November 2022 dividend distribution triggered a **one-off, material gain/loss** (fair value minus equity-method carrying value)

### JD.com case (before the December 2021 dividend announcement, 17% stake)
- After the distribution of 958 million shares (USD 16.4 billion) was completed in March 2022, the stake fell from 17% to 2.3%
- **Loss of significant influence → reclassified from an associate to FVTPL**
- Remeasured at fair value as of the date significant influence was lost, **with the difference flowing through the income statement as a one-off item**

## 5. Specific treatment under FVTPL

### Pinduoduo case (~14% stake, FVTPL)

**Annual process**:
1. Beginning-of-year fair value: RMB X (in hundreds of millions), based on the prior year-end closing price
2. Share-price movements during the period: remeasured quarterly/annually, with the change flowing directly into "other gains, net"
3. Dividends received: recognized as "investment income"
4. Year-end fair value: based on the closing price as of the balance-sheet date

**Intuition with numbers**:
- Pinduoduo up 50% → Tencent's income statement +0.5X (in hundreds of millions RMB)
- Pinduoduo down 50% → Tencent's income statement -0.5X (in hundreds of millions RMB)

**This is the main reason behind the sharp swings in Tencent's net profit in 2021-2022**

### FVTPL valuation of unlisted investments
- Valued using the most recent financing round (Level 2) or DCF/comparables (Level 3)
- The cooling of private-market valuations in 2022-2023 directly hit Tencent's income statement

## 6. Historical income-statement impact of fair-value changes

### 2021: The inflection year for investments
- "Other gains, net" included substantial FVTPL impairments and revaluations
- **GAAP net profit attributable to shareholders: RMB 224.8 billion** (including a large one-off gain from the disposal of JD.com shares)
- **Non-GAAP net profit: RMB 123.7 billion** (single-digit growth)
- The difference lies almost entirely in "changes in fair value of investments plus impairments"

### 2022: Deep losses
- The China-concept-stock crisis, plus Meituan's share price falling 80% (though not directly reflected under the equity method), plus broad-based FVTPL declines
- **GAAP net profit: RMB 188.2 billion** (including a one-off gain of USD 16.0 billion from paring down the Sea stake)
- **Non-GAAP net profit: RMB 115.6 billion** (-7% year over year)

### 2023: Investment recovery
- **GAAP net profit: RMB 115.2 billion** (-39% year over year, due to a high 2022 base)
- **Non-GAAP net profit: RMB 157.7 billion** (+36% year over year)

### 2024: Recovery plus dividend/divestment
- The JD.com/Meituan dividend distributions triggered reclassification gains/losses (now complete)
- The FVTPL portfolio recovered broadly (Pinduoduo and Spotify performed strongly)
- **GAAP net profit: RMB 194.0 billion (+68%)**
- **Non-GAAP net profit: RMB 222.7 billion (+41%)**

### 2025
- **GAAP net profit: RMB 224.8 billion**
- **Non-GAAP net profit: approximately RMB 250.0 billion** (estimate)

## 7. Accounting treatment of dividend distributions/divestments

### December 2021 in-kind distribution of JD.com A shares

**Structure**: Tencent distributed 958 million JD.com shares (USD 16.4 billion) to shareholders on a pro rata basis.

**Accounting steps**:
1. **At declaration**: the dividend payable is measured at fair value (IFRIC 17)
2. **The difference**: fair value minus equity-method carrying value = **a one-off disposal gain recognized in the income statement**
3. **Actual distribution**: offset against the dividend payable and shareholders' equity
4. **Reclassification of the remaining stake**: the residual 2.3% stake lost significant influence → reclassified from the equity method to FVTPL, with the difference flowing into the income statement
5. **Subsequently**: the residual JD.com stake is carried at FVTPL, with share-price movements flowing directly into the income statement

### November 2022 distribution of Meituan shares
- Similar mechanism
- Distributed 958 million shares (USD 20.4 billion)
- Stake fell from 17% to ~1.5%

**These two distributions were an important contributor to the jump in Tencent's GAAP net profit in 2024**

## 8. Full consolidation of subsidiaries

**Riot Games, Miniclip, Funcom, Techland (newly acquired in 2024), and others**:
- These are not "investments" but **subsidiaries**
- Fully consolidated under IFRS 10
- Non-controlling interests are presented separately

**Supercell (51.2%-controlled via Halti S.A.)**:
- Through Halti, Tencent's effective economic interest in Supercell is approximately 43%
- Halti was consolidated in 2023
- Supercell's revenue and profit are fully consolidated into Tencent's financial statements

## 9. "Operating profit" vs. "GAAP net profit"

**Tencent's three-tier profit framework**:

1. **Operating profit**: revenue minus cost of revenue minus selling/administrative/R&D expenses, plus or minus other operating gains. **Excludes fair-value changes in investments**
2. **GAAP net profit attributable to shareholders**: the full IFRS net profit, including fair-value changes in investments, share of profits/losses of associates, gains/losses on disposals, impairments, share-based compensation, and amortization of intangible assets
3. **Non-GAAP (adjusted) net profit**: GAAP net profit with the following excluded:
   - Net fair-value changes in investments
   - Gains/losses on disposal of investments
   - Impairment provisions
   - Share-based compensation expenses (SBC)
   - Amortization of intangible assets
   - Related income-tax effects

### Why the distinction matters
- **GAAP swings sharply**: investments make up a large share
- **Non-GAAP reflects operating performance**
- Both Tencent's management and sell-side analysts treat Non-GAAP as the core metric

### 2024 figures compared
- GAAP net profit: RMB 194.0 billion
- Non-GAAP net profit: RMB 222.7 billion
- Difference: share-based compensation of RMB 30+ billion, plus amortization of intangible assets and some impairments

## 10. Which metric should investors watch?

| Perspective | Metric to watch |
|------|---------|
| Operating quality | Non-GAAP net profit + operating profit |
| Total return | GAAP net profit + OCI + the true market value of associates |
| Buffett's "look-through earnings" | Operating profit + the proportionate share of net profit from major associates/FVTPL holdings |
| Cash-flow perspective | Operating cash flow + cash inflows from investment disposals |

**ai-berkshire's preference**:
- **Core-business valuation**: Non-GAAP operating profit × a reasonable multiple
- **Investment-portfolio valuation**: a separate sum-of-the-parts approach (associates at market value, FVTPL at fair value, unlisted holdings at book value × a discount)
- **Adding the two together** yields net asset value (NAV)
- **Avoid the simplistic "GAAP net profit × P/E"** valuation approach, since GAAP is heavily distorted by investment-related swings
