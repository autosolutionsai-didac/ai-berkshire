### Clarifying common misconceptions

- **"Tencent-affiliated" ≠ "Tencent-controlled"**: media often label every company Tencent has ever invested in as "Tencent-affiliated," but in fact **the vast majority are only 5-15% financial holdings** — Tencent neither participates in their operations nor can unilaterally sell down its stake.
- **"Tencent empire" ≠ "Tencent's financial statements"**: only 6 wholly controlled companies are consolidated, plus a portion of associates share proportionately — the market-value swings of other companies **do not directly affect Tencent's financial statements** (except for the FVTPL category).
- **The linguistic trap of "control vs. stake"**: the Supercell case is the classic example — it is commonly said online that "Tencent acquired 84.3% of Supercell," but **this is Halti's stake in Supercell**; Tencent's indirect economic interest in Supercell is only 41.7%.

## 4. Timeline of key historical changes (revised edition)

### 2008-2020: Expansion phase
- **2008** Invested in Riot Games (started as a minority stake)
- 2011-02 Took a controlling **92.78%** stake in Riot Games
- 2014-03 Invested in JD.com for a 15% stake (USD 215 million cash + e-commerce assets)
- **2015-12** Acquired Riot Games outright (remaining shares)
- 2016-06 **A Tencent-led consortium** (including SoftBank and others) acquired 84.3% of Supercell for USD 8.6 billion; Tencent itself contributed approximately $3.0-4.0 billion
- 2017 Increased Epic Games stake to a historical high of ~40%
- 2019-10 Tencent raised its voting rights in Halti to 51.2% via convertible notes
- 2019-05 Acquired Sharkmob outright
- 2020-09 Completed the full acquisition of Funcom ($148M)

### 2021-2023: Contraction and dividend-distribution phase
- **2021-12-23 Distributed JD.com shares** (17% → 2.3%, valued at USD 16.4 billion)
- **2022-01-04 Reduced Sea Limited stake** (USD 3.0 billion, 21.3% → 18.7%)
- **During 2022-2023** gradually exited Tesla (had invested 5% in 2017)
- **2022-11-16 Distributed Meituan shares** (17% → 1.5%, valued at USD ~20.4 billion)

### 2024-2026: Buybacks + in-kind dividends + AI redeployment
- **2024-01** Completed the **67% majority stake** acquisition of Techland (USD 1.6 billion)
- **2024-12** The DOJ required Tencent to give up its unilateral board-appointment rights at Epic Games
- Repurchased HKD 112 billion in 2024 (a historical high)
- **2025-03** Increased Kadokawa stake (6.86% → 7.97%)
- **2025-03** Signed an agreement to invest €1.16 billion in Ubisoft's subsidiary Vantage Studios
- 2025 Reduced Reddit stake (down to 4.17%)
- 2025 Reduced BOSS Zhipin stake (further reduced by $200M in 2026-01, down to 9.2%)
- **2025-11-21** Vantage Studios investment completed (final stake 26.32%)
- **2025-12** Moonshot Series C completed (IDG led with $500M, Tencent participated as follow-on, valuation $4.3B)
- Full-year 2025: buybacks of 80 billion + dividends of 41 billion = HKD 121 billion in total shareholder returns
- **2026-Q1** MiniMax / Zhipu IPO on the Hong Kong exchange

### Current risks in 2026
- **2026-03-04** Media reported the Trump administration is discussing a CFIUS case — whether to require Tencent to divest its US/global holdings in Riot Games (100%) / Epic Games (~28-35%) / Supercell (Halti 51.2%)
- Status: **still at the discussion stage, no decision made**; Treasury leans toward retention plus data mitigation; DOJ leans toward forced divestiture
- Note: **this is a CFIUS case** (led by Treasury), which is a **separate review** from the DoD's 1260H "Chinese military company" list (Tencent was added in 2025-01 and is contesting it)
- Equity at stake: approximately USD 30-35 billion (Riot $22B + Supercell indirect $6.25B + Epic $7B)
