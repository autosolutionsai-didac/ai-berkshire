## Core Highlights

**Games empire goes global**: Overseas game revenue reached RMB 77.4 billion (+33%), surpassing USD 10 billion. Supercell (84%-controlled) posted 2024 revenue of EUR 2.8 billion (+77%). Fourteen evergreen titles each generate over RMB 4 billion in annual gross receipts. Domestic CR2 reached 78% (Tencent 50%+, NetEase 28%).

**Advertising is the strongest growth engine**: Video Accounts advertising was up 60% YoY in Q4. WeChat's ecosystem "social - content - advertising - transaction" closed loop has taken shape. Within 5 years it could rise from #3 to #2, with market share of 18-20%.

**Video Accounts e-commerce**: GMV of RMB 200-250 billion (+92%), 85% of merchants are unbranded ("white-label"). Within 5 years it could become China's fourth-largest e-commerce platform, with GMV exceeding RMB 1 trillion.

**Profit quality keeps improving**: Gross margin rising from 53% to 60%, net margin 30%+. Adjusted net profit is projected at ~RMB 430.0 billion in 2030.

## Tencent's Profile 5 Years From Now

**A global tech giant with revenue past RMB 1 trillion and net profit exceeding RMB 400 billion.**

- A globalized games empire: overseas revenue share 40%+, a genuine global #1
- WeChat ecosystem monetization goes deeper: advertising nearing RMB 300 billion, Video Accounts e-commerce GMV in the trillions
- An AI-driven efficiency machine: AI permeating every business line, annual R&D plus capex exceeding RMB 200 billion
- Industrial synergy from a trillion-yuan investment portfolio
- Maintaining global/China #1 position in games, social, and payments
