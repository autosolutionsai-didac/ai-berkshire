| Metric | Data |
|---|---|
| Launch date | 2015 |

| Global MAU | **260 million** (highest in the world) |

## VI. Moat Analysis (Seven Layers of Defense)

### Layer 1: Traffic Gateway
- **WeChat's 1.4 billion DAU + QQ's 500 million+ MAU**
- Any new game can reach players nationwide within 24 hours
- This is an asset that NetEase, miHoYo, and ByteDance cannot buy their way into

### Layer 2: Channels and Distribution
- Yingyongbao (Android channel) + WeChat Mini Games + WeGame (PC)
- In 2025, WeChat Mini Games negotiated its revenue share with Apple down to **15%** (versus the usual 30%)
- Best-in-industry channel bargaining power

### Layer 3: IP and Capital Deployment
- 100+ global game-company investments/acquisitions
- The "big three" — Riot (League of Legends, Valorant) + Supercell (Clash) + Epic (Fortnite)
- This reflects the **irreplicability of 20 years of capital accumulation**

### Layer 4: Evergreen Operating Capability
- The operating SOPs behind the 14 evergreen games have been proven out
- Data-driven update cadence, event design, and monetization-point design
- **CrossFire at 18 years and Honor of Kings at 11 years are both still hitting new highs — unique in the industry**

### Layer 5: Esports Ecosystem
- KPL (Honor of Kings Pro League) + LPL (League of Legends Pro League) + VCT (Valorant Champions Tour) + PEL (Peacekeeper Elite League)
- Esports is gaming's "professional sports" — it extends a product's lifecycle by 10+ years
- This is NetEase's weakest area

### Layer 6: AI + Technology
- **Hunyuan** (Tencent's proprietary large language model)
- **Hunyuan 3D Studio** (3D asset generation, 100x efficiency gain)
- AI NPCs, AI teammates (Peacekeeper Elite has already integrated DeepSeek)
- An internal AI engine serves all studios

### Layer 7: Organizational Capability (Horse-Racing + Fault Tolerance)
- The four major studios racing each other + NExT's trial-and-error + autonomy for overseas holdings
- Strategic redundancy: multiple bets within the same category
- This is something ByteDance's "winner-take-all" model cannot replicate — ByteDance Nuverse's continued retreat is the counter-example

## VIII. How AI Is Reshaping the Games Business (Medium-Term Catalyst)

### 8.1 Content Production Side (Cost Reduction)
- Art assets: Hunyuan 3D Studio boosts 3D asset generation efficiency by **100x**
- Level design: AI auto-generates levels, storylines, and dialogue
- Localization: AI-driven multilingual translation accelerates overseas expansion
- **Expected impact**: game development costs fall 30-50%, content output per unit of time rises 5-10x

### 8.2 Gameplay Side (Experience Enhancement)
- AI NPCs: NPC behavior tailored to each individual player
- AI teammates: fill in match experience when player counts are low (Peacekeeper Elite already uses DeepSeek)
- AI coaching: dynamically adjusts difficulty to player skill level
- Dynamic storylines: generate content in real time based on player choices

### 8.3 UGC Ecosystem (Disruptive)
- DreamStar + Oasis Genesis (Peacekeeper Elite's UGC mode)
- Lets players use AI tools to create their own levels/gameplay
- **Goal**: replicate the Roblox / Fortnite Creative model
- Once proven out, UGC content-creation costs approach zero, potentially extending evergreen lifespans by another 10 years

### 8.4 Business Model Side
- AI-personalized push notifications: higher paying-conversion rates
- AI-driven player-care systems: churn warnings + win-back

- Core-business implied valuation ≈ RMB 2.57 trillion
- Games contribute about **40-50%** of core-business revenue and **45-55%** of profit

### 11.3 A Value-Investing Verdict

**Duan Yongping/Li Lu's "good business" checklist**:
- ✅ Strong brands/IP (Honor of Kings, League of Legends, CrossFire, Clash)
- ✅ Network effects (social + gaming)
- ✅ Switching costs (accounts, friends, sunk equipment/gear)
- ✅ Economies of scale (marginally declining operating costs)
- ✅ Enduring demand (gaming is a permanent form of entertainment)

- ✅ Management's capital-allocation skill (repeatedly demonstrated)

- ⚠️ Uncertain innovation cycle (AI is both an opportunity and a wild card)

## XIII. Final Verdict for Investors

### Three Core Conclusions

**1. The games business is both the steadiest cash cow and the largest AI option within Tencent's core business**
- 14 evergreen games = a stable base of **RMB 70-100 billion** in cash flow
- AI-driven transformation + an overseas breakout = the growth engine for the next five years
- Taken together, these two things are something no other game company can replicate

**2. The market is severely underpricing the games business right now**
- Doubling AI spend has been read as "bad news," but gaming is one of AI's biggest monetization scenarios
- The market has not fully priced in overseas revenue growth of +33%
- Delta Force's +29x growth shows Tencent's ability to launch new hits hasn't faded

**3. Doubling games-business revenue within five years is a high-probability outcome**
- Domestic: RMB 164.2 billion → RMB 240 billion (5% CAGR)
- Overseas: RMB 77.4 billion → RMB 240 billion (25% CAGR)
- Total revenue: RMB 241.6 billion → RMB 480 billion
- As gross margin rises and AI cuts costs, profit could grow 2.5-3x

### Bottom Line

> **When you buy Tencent at 12x P/E, you get not only a nearly free RMB 1 trillion investment portfolio, but also a nearly free gaming juggernaut — one whose revenue is highly likely to double over the next five years and whose moat is the deepest in the world.**
