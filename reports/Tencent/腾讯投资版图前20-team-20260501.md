## I. Executive Summary

Tencent's investment portfolio (excluding internal IEG studios), measured on a look-through economic equity basis, totals **approximately USD 125-140 billion**, broken down as:
- **Gaming**: USD ~43.5 billion (Riot/Epic/Supercell account for 84%)
- **US, European, and Korean equities**: USD ~53.5 billion (PDD/Spotify/Sea/UMG are the top four)
- **Hong Kong and Japanese equities**: USD ~25 billion (Meituan/Kuaishou are the core holdings)
- **Unlisted non-gaming**: USD ~26.5 billion (ByteDance/WeBank are the largest)

**Key findings**:
1. **Riot Games ($23B equity value) has overtaken ByteDance to become Tencent's single largest holding** — this diverges from mainstream perception
2. **Tencent's equity stake in ByteDance is approximately $9.35B, far below the erroneous "$50 billion" valuation** ($50 billion would imply a 9.1% stake, more than double the historical peak)
3. **Tencent's top 4 holdings (Riot/PDD/ByteDance/Spotify) together are worth $64B+ in equity value, accounting for more than 50% of the total investment portfolio**
4. **Systematic divestment of non-strategic holdings in 2022-2024** (JD.com/Meituan/ZhongAn/Weimob), while **maintaining or increasing stakes in gaming IP holdings such as Riot/Epic/Supercell/Krafton**

## IV. Key Views by Holding Category

### 4.1 By Accounting Treatment (User Question: Is It Consolidated?)

| Category | Holdings | Does Profit/Loss Flow into Tencent's Income Statement? | Form |
|------|------|----------------------|------|
| **Fully consolidated (subsidiaries)** | Riot, Miniclip, Funcom, Sharkmob, Grinding Gear, China Literature, WeSure, Halti tier | Yes — revenue/profit/loss fully consolidated | Control >50% |
| **Associates (equity method)** | Epic, Supercell, WeBank, Kuaishou, Tongcheng Travel, Krafton (under some bases) | Partial — only "share of associates' profit/loss" line included | Significant influence, 20-50% |
| **Financial assets, FVOCI** | Krafton, Kakao Games, some long-term holdings | No — fair value changes flow only through equity | Long-term strategic holding |
| **Financial assets, FVTPL** | PDD, Sea, Spotify, UMG, ByteDance, Snap, Bilibili, Meituan, JD.com, AI startups, etc. | Partial — flows through the income statement but reflects market-value fluctuation, not operating profit | Financial investment <20% |

### 4.2 Income Statement Presentation

```
Tencent GAAP net profit = Core operating profit (games + ads + FBS + WeChat)
                        + Consolidated subsidiary profit (Riot/China Literature/Halti etc.)
                        + Share of associates' profit/loss (Epic/WeBank/Kuaishou/Krafton etc., pro-rata)
                        + Fair value change on investments (PDD/Sea/ByteDance/AI startups market-value swings) ⭐
                        - Impairment losses
                        + Gain on disposal of investments (realized gains on sales)
```

⭐ This line item (fair value changes) is the core driver of the large gap between Tencent's GAAP net profit and Non-IFRS net profit. **Non-IFRS strips this out**, since it is non-operating, non-cash market-value fluctuation.

### 4.3 By Strategic Nature

**Gaming strategic holdings (USD 43.5B)**:

## V. Notes on Corrections to Disputed Data

### 5.1 The Three Competing Figures for Meituan's Ownership: "17% vs 6.4% vs 1.5%"

The HK/A-share agent's "steady 17%" figure is **incorrect** — that reflects data from **before** the 2022 special dividend.

**Precise history**:
- 2022-11-16: Tencent announced a special dividend distributing 958 million Meituan Class B shares to shareholders, worth approximately HKD 159.4 billion (approximately $20.4B)
- **Before the dividend**: Tencent held approximately 1.54 billion Meituan shares = **17.04%**
- **After the dividend**: Tencent retained approximately 586 million shares = **approximately 6.4%** (of total share capital)
- **Some 2024 reports**: use a "further reduced after the dividend" basis, approximately 1.5%

**This report's base case**: 6.4% (the most authoritative disclosure), equity value approximately **$4.1B**
- Upper-bound basis (17%): $11.0B (incorrect, already distributed)
- Lower-bound basis (1.5%): $1.0B (would reflect further divestment, but not publicly disclosed)

### 5.2 Krafton's Valuation: "$14.5B vs $8.9B"

- Gaming agent's estimate: $14.5B (did not use primary-source data)
- US-equities agent's verification: $8.9B (KRX market cap, primary-source data as of 2026-04-24)
- **The US-equities agent's figure is adopted: $8.9B / equity value $1.2B**

### 5.3 Whether Activision "Is Still in Tencent's Portfolio"

## VI. Several Surprising Findings

### 6.1 Riot Games' Single Equity Value Exceeds the Combined Value of ByteDance + Spotify + UMG
- Riot at $23B alone vs. ByteDance ($9.35B) + Spotify ($9.29B) + UMG ($7.7B) = $26.34B
- Under current estimates, **Riot accounts for approximately 18% of Tencent's entire investment portfolio**
- This came from roughly $400M in total investment by Tencent in 2011 — **a 58x return**

### 6.2 PDD Is Tencent's Largest US-Equity Holding and Has Not Been Divested
- PDD equity value of $22.48B vs. JD.com $1.0B, Meituan $4.1B, Sea $8.6B
- This contrasts with the clean-sweep, dividend-driven exits from JD.com (2.3%), Meituan (6.4%), and Sea (reduced in 2022)
- **Retaining PDD is a strategic choice** — likely because PDD's internationalization (Temu) has potential synergy with WeChat Pay and the broader ecosystem

### 6.3 AI Large-Model Investment Scale Is Surprisingly Small
- The combined equity value of Zhipu + Moonshot AI + MiniMax + Baichuan + Yuanxiang is only **~$1.3-1.4B**
- Far below Tencent's own capex on its in-house Hunyuan model (annual capex of RMB 18 billion+)
- Reflects that Tencent's AI investment is a "financial bet" rather than "strategic leadership"

### 6.4 Education-Sector Holdings (Yuanfudao/Zuoyebang) Carry a Book Equity Value of $1.7B but May Be Severely Overstated in Reality
- Valuations shrank sharply after the "double reduction" education policy
- Whether Tencent has taken an impairment on its books is not disclosed
- True realizable value could be below 30% of book value

## VII. The Questions Users Care About Most Regarding the Top 20

### Q1: How much is Tencent's investment portfolio actually worth?
- **On a look-through economic equity basis**: USD 125-140 billion (median ~$133 billion)
- **Per Tencent's financial disclosures**: "Financial assets measured at fair value" of approximately RMB 800-900 billion ($110-130B)
- The two bases are close, with the main difference arising from valuation gaps on wholly-owned gaming assets (such as Riot)

### Q2: Whose profits flow into Tencent's financial statements?
- **Fully consolidated** (subsidiary level): all profit from Riot/China Literature/Miniclip, etc.
- **Pro-rata** (equity method): Epic, WeBank, Supercell, Kuaishou, etc. — only a single line reflecting the pro-rata share of net profit
- **Market-value fluctuation** (FVTPL): PDD, Sea, Spotify, ByteDance, etc. — market-value changes flow through the income statement but are not operating profit
- **Look to Non-IFRS to assess the core business**: Non-IFRS strips out investment-related noise

### Q3: Which holdings represent a "strategic core" Tencent will not sell?
- Absolute core: Riot, Epic, Supercell, Miniclip, China Literature (integrated into the business, will never be sold)
- Ecosystem core: WeBank, Spotify (music-rights strategy), UMG (same rationale)
- Possible further divestment: JD.com (already reduced), Meituan (already reduced), ZhongAn (already reduced), Weimob (already reduced), ByteDance (gradual)
- Financial investments: PDD, Sea, Snap, Bilibili, education-sector holdings, AI startups — disposed of flexibly based on market conditions

### Q4: How much lies beyond the Top 20?
- Beyond rank 11-20 there is a large "long tail" of holdings, each with an equity value of $0.1-1.0B, totaling approximately $10-15B
- Including: ZhongAn, Reddit, JD.com, Roblox, Kadokawa, Kakao Games, Kingsoft, WeRide, Momenta, AI startups, residual value of education-sector holdings, autonomous-driving/robotics startups, and more

## VIII. Data Limitations Statement

1. **Tencent is only required to disclose holdings above a 5% stake**; positions below 5% (such as Roblox, Reddit, AI startups) carry low confidence
2. **Unlisted valuations are inherently opaque**: exact ownership percentages for individual holdings are never disclosed; figures are range estimates
3. **Round valuations ≠ fair value**: primary-market valuations often include liquidation preferences and ratchet clauses, and the gap between book value and true realizable value can reach 30-50%
4. **ByteDance's ownership range is an estimate**: the exact figure is known only to Tencent's finance department and ByteDance's shareholder registry
5. **Education-sector valuations**: there is no active market following the "double reduction" policy, and whether Tencent has taken impairments is unknown
6. **Share-price volatility**: the data in this table reflects a snapshot as of 2026-05-01; monthly fluctuation can reach ±10%

## IX. Research Methodology Retrospective

Results from this exercise using a four-role parallel investment research team:

### Confidence Differences Across Roles
- 1st place: **US-equities holdings agent** — 22 WebSearch calls, primary-source data (13G/PR/financial reports), highest confidence
- 2nd place: **HK/A-share holdings agent** — 31 WebSearch calls, but **the 17% Meituan figure was incorrect** (used pre-dividend data)

- 3rd place (tie): **Unlisted strategic-investment agent** — did not use WebSearch, estimated from historical financing data

### The Value of Cross-Report Verification
- Krafton's valuation differed by $5.6B across two reports ($14.5B vs. $8.9B), corrected to $8.9B through cross-verification
- Meituan's ownership was listed as 17% in the HK/A-share report, but corrected to 6.4% through historical verification
- **Without cross-verification, incorrect figures would have been written into the final report**

2. Verify every disputed figure against at least 3 independent sources
3. Be honest about uncertainty ranges rather than forcing a precise number
