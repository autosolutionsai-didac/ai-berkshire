> Total reading time for the series: approx. 50 minutes; recommended to read in order.

---

## Series positioning

NetEase is not an obscure company — it is one of the most profitable, yet least seriously discussed, companies in Chinese internet.

- The bulls say: a 33% net margin, 49.6 billion yuan of free cash flow, 163.5 billion yuan of net cash equal to 31% of market cap, and a forward P/E of only 12x — absurdly undervalued
- The bears say: 82% of revenue from gaming is too concentrated, overseas revenue of just 10% lags far behind Tencent's, Marvel Rivals' concurrent users plunged 85%, and seven overseas studios have closed — the growth story doesn't hold up

**Both sides have data to back them up, yet reach opposite conclusions.** That tells us the issue isn't who's right and who's wrong — it's that each side is looking at a different facet of the same company.

This series sets out to **lay every facet out clearly** — so you can judge for yourself whether it's worth buying, rather than having a conclusion handed to you.

## Writing principles for this series

This series adheres to the following hard rules:

1. **Data first, then logic, then conclusion** — no presumption of bullish or bearish
2. **Every core judgment is paired with a counterpoint** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **Written in plain, direct prose** — no jargon-mixing
4. **No "I think" or "I feel"** — use "the data shows" or "the evidence indicates" instead
5. **No name-dropping of any investing masters** — except for the anonymized "a well-known investor," labeled as such
6. **Does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article

## Data sources

This series draws on:
- NetEase's 2024-2025 annual and quarterly financial reports
