## III. A Buffett-style examination

### 3.1 The "true worth" of the revenue growth

> "If a tree grew from 1 meter to 150 meters in two years, the question you should ask isn't 'how tall can it get?' — it's 'is this normal?'"

**Breaking down the 155x growth:**
- Founded in May 2022; only began shipping at scale in 2023 — the base was extremely low
- 2023 revenue was just RMB 58.3 million (about $8 million) — the size of a mid-sized installer
- The growth figure that actually matters is the 2024-to-2025 increase of 5.8x
- **A low base effect dominates the growth story — one shouldn't be dazzled by "155x"**

### 3.2 50% gross margin — too good to be true?

**A 50.1% gross margin is extremely rare in the solar-plus-storage industry, and the reasons need to be unpacked:**

| Factor | Impact | Sustainability |
|------|------|---------|
| 5-in-1 integration premium | Users are willing to pay a premium for a one-stop solution | Moderate — premium will narrow as competitors catch up |
| Channel pricing strategy | Sold through distributors into premium markets (Europe, US, Australia) | Moderate — price wars have already spread overseas from China |
| Early-market windfall | Few competitors in stackable solar-plus-storage units | Low — Huawei, GoodWe, and others are catching up fast |
| Software value-add | SigenOS raises the product's added value | High — software moats are relatively durable |
| Emerging scale effects | The Nantong factory has ramped up, lowering production costs | High — will keep benefiting as scale expands |

**Buffett would say:** "A 50% gross margin isn't the problem — the problem is how long you can hold onto it. In Chinese manufacturing, a fat margin is like honey — it draws every bear in the forest."

### 3.3 Cash flow analysis

**Key concerns disclosed in the prospectus:**
- As of September 30, 2024, total liabilities were RMB 744 million, current liabilities RMB 702 million
- Cash and cash equivalents were only RMB 294 million
- **IPO proceeds of HKD 4.19 billion (~RMB 3.68 billion) will substantially improve the balance sheet**
- But working-capital needs (inventory, receivables) are large during a high-growth phase

### 3.4 Margin of safety assessment

**Buffett's margin-of-safety test:**

| Scenario | Assumptions | Valuation |
|------|------|------|
| **Optimistic** | 2026 revenue RMB 18 billion, net margin 35%, 35x P/E | Market cap HKD 220.5 billion (+176%) |
| **Neutral** | 2026 revenue RMB 15 billion, net margin 30%, 25x P/E | Market cap HKD 127.7 billion (+60%) |
| **Pessimistic** | 2026 revenue RMB 12 billion, net margin 20%, 15x P/E | Market cap HKD 40.9 billion (-49%) |

**Margin-of-safety judgment: the neutral scenario offers 60% upside, but the pessimistic scenario implies a nearly 50% drawdown. For a company that's only 3 years old, this risk-reward profile isn't especially compelling.**

## IV. Buffett's overall verdict

### Rating: ★★★☆ 3.5/5

**Strengths:**
- Astonishing growth rate — already delivered RMB 2.9 billion in net profit in 2025
- A 50% gross margin and 36% net margin stand head and shoulders above the industry
- IPO P/E of ~24x isn't outlandish for a high-growth company
- The balance sheet will improve substantially after the IPO proceeds come in

**Weaknesses:**
- The "155x growth" is mainly a base effect and shouldn't be extrapolated linearly
- A 50% gross margin will almost certainly be pulled down by competition — the only question is how fast
- Only 3 years old, with too small a financial track record to judge "normalized" profitability
- Operating cash flow and inventory management need continued monitoring
- The IPO valuation isn't cheap — there's no obvious "bargain" opportunity

**Buffett's likely conclusion:** "I don't invest in IPOs — never have. This company's growth truly is remarkable, but I'd need to see at least 3-5 years of stable operating data before I could assess its 'normalized' profitability and the durability of its moat. A 24x P/E for a company with only 3 years of history already prices in very high expectations from the market."
