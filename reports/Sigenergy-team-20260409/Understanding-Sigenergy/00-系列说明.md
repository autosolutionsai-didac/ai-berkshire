# "Understanding Sigenergy" Series Overview

> Four articles that take apart a company founded just four years ago whose share price doubled on its first day of trading, revealing its true structure.
>
> Series reading time: roughly 50 minutes; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series positioning

Sigenergy is not lacking for attention — 1,102x oversubscription, a 103% first-day pop, and a market cap that briefly topped **HKD 160 billion**. It was the hottest Hong Kong IPO of 2026, bar none.

But being hot and being understood are two different things.

- The bulls say: Huawei-lineage DNA + the world's number-one five-in-one product + a 50% gross margin + AI-enabled — this is the next CATL
- The bears say: only 1 profitable year out of 4 since founding, 93% of revenue riding on a single product, Huawei has already filed a patent-infringement lawsuit, and the share price has fallen from 669 to 519

**Both camps have factual backing, yet arrive at completely opposite conclusions.**

What this series aims to do is **first lay out clearly "what it is," then let you judge for yourself "whether it's worth buying"** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Writing principles

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **No fabricated data** — data is sourced; estimated figures are labeled "estimate"
4. **Not investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article
