## Hangzhou Key Companies (7 Detailed Profiles)

### 1. Unitree Robotics ★★★★✦ (4.5 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | 2016; founder Wang Xingxing (born in the 1990s, formerly of DJI) |
| Products | Quadruped robots (Go/B series) + humanoid robots (H1/G1) |
| Funding | 10 rounds; IPO valuation of RMB 42 billion; plans to raise RMB 4.2 billion on the STAR Market |
| Financials | **2025 revenue of RMB 1.7 billion (+335%), net profit of RMB 600 million (+674%), gross margin of 60.3%** |
| Competition | vs. AgiBot (domestic), Tesla Optimus (global) |

**Investment verdict**: The most certain play in China's robotics sector. The combination of profitability, high growth, and high gross margin is exceedingly rare in the global robotics industry. The RMB 42 billion valuation implies roughly a 70x P/E, which is on the expensive side, but the high growth rate can absorb it. Core risk: large-scale application scenarios for humanoid robots have not yet materialized.

### 2. DeepSeek ★★★★ (4 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | July 2023; founder Liang Wenfeng (helm of High-Flyer Quant) |
| Products | DeepSeek family of large language models (V2/V3/R1/V4), open source |
| Funding | First external funding round of RMB 50 billion; valuation of RMB 350 billion; the National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund is in talks to lead the round |
| Team | **Only 139 people**, with world-class talent density |
| Competition | Benchmarked globally against OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic |

**Investment verdict**: A "national fate" caliber asset. A tiny team built a world-class model at extremely low cost, and its technical strength has been internationally validated. Core risks: the fragility of a 139-person team (5 core members have already been poached), an unclear commercialization path (how to monetize an open-source model), and Liang Wenfeng's absolute personal control. High risk, high reward — better suited to strategic capital than financial investment.

### 3. XtalPi ★★★★ (4 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | 2015, by a trio of MIT quantum-physics scholars |
| Products | AI-driven drug discovery platform, serving 17 of the world's top 20 pharmaceutical companies |
| Market cap | Hong Kong-listed (2228.HK) |
| Financials | **2025 revenue of RMB 800 million (+201%), net profit of RMB 258 million (first-ever profit)** |
| Highlight | Renewed a USD 345 million contract with Eli Lilly; cash reserves of RMB 7.07 billion |

**Investment verdict**: The safest play in the AI drug-discovery sector. Already profitable, with major pharma orders and ample cash. Insufficient Hong Kong-market liquidity is the main risk.

### 4. AgiBot ★★★✦ (3.5 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | 2023; CTO Peng Zhihui ("Zhihuijun," a former Huawei "Genius Youth" program member), CEO Deng Taihua (former Huawei vice president) |
| Products | Yuanzheng/Jingling/Lingxi humanoid robots; GO-1 embodied foundation model |
| Funding | 10+ rounds in two years; valuation of RMB 15 billion; plans a Hong Kong IPO in Q3 2026 |
| Financials | **2025 revenue of RMB 1.05 billion; shipments of 5,168 units (nearly 40% of global shipments)** |

**Investment verdict**: Astonishing growth rate but aggressive cash burn (raising capital while simultaneously investing in 15 startups). The RMB 15 billion valuation implies a 15x P/S, which is on the expensive side given the company is not yet profitable. Over-reliance on Peng Zhihui's personal IP is a latent risk.

### 5. ShengShu Technology (Vidu) ★★★✦ (3.5 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | 2023, incubated by a Tsinghua-affiliated team together with Ant Group and Baidu Ventures |
| Products | Vidu AI video-generation model, ranked No. 1 in China and No. 2 globally |
| Funding | Series B of nearly RMB 2 billion; valuation over USD 2 billion (RMB 15 billion) |
| Financials | ARR surpassed USD 20 million; users and revenue both grew more than 10x |

**Investment verdict**: Technology ranked among the global top two, but the USD 2 billion valuation is set against less than RMB 200 million in ARR — an extremely high P/S. A 70-person team is at a clear scale disadvantage against big-tech incumbents. A high-beta, high-volatility play.

### 6. DEEP Robotics ★★★ (3 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | 2017; founder Zhu Qiuguo (associate professor at Zhejiang University) |

**Investment verdict**: A hidden champion in quadruped robotics, with an extremely high share of the industrial market. However, the quadruped market has limited headroom, and the humanoid product line is only just getting started.

### 7. Unisound ★★✦ (2.5 stars)

| Dimension | Data |
|------|------|
| Founded | 2012; founder Huang Wei (PhD from the University of Science and Technology of China) |

## AI Chip Valuation Bubble Warning

| Company | Market cap | 2025 revenue | P/S multiple | Profit/loss | Verdict |
|------|------|-----------|--------|------|------|
| Cambricon | RMB 700 billion+ | RMB 6.5 billion | ~110x | First-ever profit | Sector leader but extremely expensive valuation |
| Moore Threads | RMB 300 billion+ | RMB 1.5 billion | **200x+** | Loss-making | Most severely sentiment-driven |
| MetaX | RMB 300 billion | RMB 1.6-1.7 billion | **180x+** | Loss-making | Single-product line, weakest fundamentals |
| Biren Technology | HKD 100 billion | RMB 1 billion | **100x** | Loss-making | Mass production of the BR20X is the make-or-break factor |

**The four GPU companies together have a combined market cap of over RMB 1.3 trillion, against combined revenue of only about RMB 10.6 billion; of the four, only Cambricon is profitable, with roughly RMB 2.1 billion in profit.** P/S multiples of 100-200x far exceed the global semiconductor industry average (10-30x). This is a collective bubble driven by "Xinchuang" (domestic IT substitution) policy and domestic-substitution sentiment, not by underlying fundamentals.

## Investment Strategy Recommendations

### If You Could Only Pick 3

1. **Unitree Robotics** (certainty) — Already profitable + high growth; subscribe to its STAR Market IPO
2. **Horizon Robotics** (margin of safety) — Strongest certainty of domestic substitution in ADAS chips
3. **Moonshot AI / DeepSeek** (upside potential) — Sector leaders in large language models, high risk-reward

### To Avoid

1. **01.AI** — Clearly falling behind, likely to be acquired
2. **Yitu Technology** — Irreversible marginalization
3. **MetaX** — Weakest fundamentals among AI chip companies, P/S of 180x+

### Waiting for the Right Entry Point

1. **Zhipu AI** — Solid fundamentals, but a HKD 400 billion market cap implies a 70x P/S; wait for a pullback
2. **MiniMax** — Overseas revenue is exceptionally rare, but a HKD 200 billion market cap implies a 350x P/S; wait for a pullback
3. **Cambricon** — Leadership position established, but a 110x P/S; commercial demand beyond "Xinchuang" procurement needs to be validated
