| Vice Chairman | **Bao Zuben** | Assisting the second-generation succession transition | Retained |

**Key finding — second-generation succession:**

- General Manager Pan Jian has a technical background (senior engineer, State Council allowance recipient), which **can offset Jiang Lei's lack of industry experience**
- **Management transition risk:** In March 2025, both the chairman and general manager were replaced simultaneously — a major personnel change

## 9. Li Lu's Perspective: Final Conclusion

**Positive factors:**
1. Excellent shareholder returns: cumulative payout ratio of 77.77%, dividend yield of ~5%, among the highest in the industry
2. Healthy governance structure: zero share pledges, ORG Packaging's strategic stake, well-regulated related-party transactions
3. Strict financial discipline: manageable debt ratio, conservative capital allocation
4. Environmental risk overworried by the market: core products are not in the plastic-ban catalog

**Negative factors:**
1. Considerable uncertainty from the 2025 second-generation succession (the chairwoman has no industry experience)
2. Revenue grew but profit fell in 2025, and cash flow deteriorated
3. Fierce industry competition, weak pricing power
4. Long-term transition pressure from environmental substitution persists

**Li Lu-style conclusion:**
> "The best investment is one that lets you sleep at night."

Yongxin Co. offers two things that let you "sleep at night" (high dividend yield + zero pledges), but also two things that might keep you "up at night" (unproven new management + long-term industry transition pressure). At the current level of PE ~16x and dividend yield ~5%, it can serve as a "bond-like" allocation, but it lacks a growth catalyst.

**Recommendation: wait for the full fiscal-year reports of 2026-2027 to verify the new management's performance. If the Jiang Lei + Pan Jian combination can deliver stable results, the current price offers allocation value; if performance continues to deteriorate, a reassessment will be needed.**
