## Decision framework

**Standard for "understood"**: able to reasonably estimate the company's net profit range 5 and 10 years out, without relying on "trusting the story."

**Conditions for a concentrated position**:
1. Understood (meets the standard above)
2. The moat will not be fundamentally destroyed within 5-10 years
3. The current valuation offers a margin of safety

**Exclusion conditions** (if any one is met, do not select):
- Profits 5 years out are entirely unpredictable
- The moat depends on an external party (e.g., PDD's dependence on Tencent's WeChat)
- The business model requires continuous, large capital investment just to sustain itself
