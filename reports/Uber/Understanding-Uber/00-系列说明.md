# "Understanding Uber" Series Overview

> 4 articles, unpacking Uber's transformation from "king of losses" to "profit machine."
>
> The series takes about 50 minutes to read; recommended reading order applies.

---

## Series Positioning

Uber is one of the most controversial technology companies in global capital markets.

- The bulls say: FCF of **$9.8 billion**, growth of 18%, a P/E of just 13x, an unassailable 76% share of the ride-hailing market — the valuation is clearly too low
- The bears say: once autonomous driving matures, Uber's role as "middleman" becomes worthless; regulation could reclassify drivers as employees at any time, cutting margins in half

**Both camps are looking at the same company. The core of their disagreement comes down to a single word: autonomous driving.**

What this series sets out to do is **first lay out clearly what kind of business Uber actually is, then let you judge for yourself whether the company is worth holding** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Series Writing Principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Data first, then logic, then conclusion** — no predetermined bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **Data is sourced** — all key figures come from Uber's earnings filings, SEC filings, and credible third parties
4. **No "probability-weighted expected returns"** — assigning weights to scenarios is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **This does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article
