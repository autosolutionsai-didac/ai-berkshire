## One-Sentence Core Judgment

**Wuliangye is a "good business" but not a "great business." It has brand, moat, and cash flow, but lacks Kweichow Moutai's "consumer mindshare monopoly + irreplaceability"—to quote Duan Yongping, Wuliangye's differentiation is insufficiently strong, pricing power has been disproven in weak cycles, and moat is narrowing slowly rather than widening.**

## 1. Business Model Essence: Core Business Definition

### 1.1 What Business Is Wuliangye Really In?

**Essential Definition**: Brand franchise for premium strong aroma type baijiu. The core asset is brand awareness of the three characters "Wuliangye"—commanding the largest share in the 800-1,200 yuan price segment, with quality barriers formed by 600 years of ancient cellar pools and strong aroma type fermentation techniques.

Duan Yongping's first question about business judgment: "If you're a consumer, why would you buy it?"

Answer: In business banquets, wedding banquets, and gift-giving scenarios, Wuliangye is "the best choice below Kweichow Moutai"—it has brand prestige while costing half as much as Kweichow Moutai. Consumers buy not the alcohol itself, but the **social currency face value**.

**But here lies a fatal qualifier—"below Kweichow Moutai." This positioning is both Wuliangye's safety zone and its ceiling.**

## 2. Price System: Relationship Between Ex-Factory Price, Wholesale Price, and Retail Price

### 2.1 8th Generation Puwan Price System (2025-2026)

```
ex-factory price (nominal) → invoice price (actual) → wholesale price (market) → retail price (terminal/end-user)
  1019 yuan              900 yuan           790-850 yuan          820-950 yuan
                        (from 2026)        (fluctuating)         (significant channel/scenario variation)
```

| Price Point | Amount (yuan/bottle) | Notes |
|---------|-------------|------|
| ex-factory price (nominal) | 1019 | Unchanged since 2024 |
| distributor invoice price | 900 | From 2026 onwards, includes 119 yuan/bottle subsidy |
| distributor actual cost | ~800-850 | After stacking rebates and expense support |
| market wholesale price (2026Q1) | ~790-850 | Fluctuation range, slight rebound around Spring Festival |
| e-commerce platform price | 820-860 | JD.com/Tmall includes platform subsidies |
| suggested retail price | 1399 | Nominal only |

### 2.2 Price Inversion: The Core Problem

**Magnitude of Inversion**: Distributors purchase at 900 yuan invoice price (approximately 800-850 yuan after stacking expenses), while market wholesale price is 790-850 yuan.

- **Severe inversion period (mid-2025)**: wholesale price dropped to around 810 yuan, distributor purchase cost about 920 yuan → **loss of approximately 70-110 yuan per bottle**
- **After invoice price reduction (from 2026)**: wholesale price 850 yuan vs invoice price 900 yuan → inversion narrows to ~50 yuan
- **Accounting for rebates**: actual cost ~800-850 yuan vs wholesale price 850 yuan → essentially break-even or minimal profit

**Duan Yongping's Pricing Power Test**: "Anything that can increase in price demonstrates pricing power."

Wuliangye in 2025-2026 not only failed to raise prices, but was **forced to lower invoice prices**—the first time in a decade. The previous price cut dates back to 2014 during the last major baijiu adjustment cycle.

**Positive Argument**: Wuliangye's price reduction was a strategic choice—narrowing the gap between ex-factory and wholesale prices to protect channel profitability is a "short-term concession, long-term channel consolidation" strategy.

**Counterargument Test**: If consumers truly believed Wuliangye is worth 1019 yuan, why has wholesale price consistently stayed above 800 yuan? The essence of pricing power is not what price you set, but whether **the market is willing to pay your asking price**. The market is voting with its feet—Wuliangye currently lacks pricing power above 1000 yuan.

### 2.3 Comparison with Kweichow Moutai Wholesale Price

| Product | ex-factory price | wholesale price | Premium/Inversion |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| Feitian Kweichow Moutai | 1169 yuan | ~1500 yuan (end of 2025) | **Premium of ~28%** |
| 8th Generation Puwan | 1019→900 yuan | ~850 yuan | **Inversion of ~6%** |

The difference is stark: Kweichow Moutai has positive premium, Wuliangye has negative inversion. This is the dividing line between "true pricing power" and "false pricing power."

Duan Yongping once said: "I know many friends who drink baijiu, but many drink only Kweichow Moutai among baijius."—this is the ultimate manifestation of consumer mindshare monopoly.

### 3.3 Consumer Mindshare Analysis

**Kweichow Moutai's Mindshare Position**:
- "China's best baijiu"
- "Social hard currency"—giving Kweichow Moutai needs no explanation
- "Sole option"—consumers don't need to choose between Kweichow Moutai and other brands
- "Investment product/collectible"—has financial attribute backing

**Wuliangye's Mindshare Position**:
- "Best choice besides Kweichow Moutai"
- "Premium value"—substitute when can't obtain/afford Kweichow Moutai
- "High priority but not sole"—consumers compare Wuliangye vs Guojiao 1573
- "Wedding/business banquet safe bet"—correct but not impressive

**Duan Yongping's Differentiation Test**: "Differentiation is something customers need but competitors can't provide."

What is Wuliangye's differentiation?

- vs Kweichow Moutai: Wuliangye has no differentiation advantage, only price advantage
- vs Guojiao 1573: differentiation in broader brand awareness and better channel network
- vs Jiannanchun: differentiation in brand tier (thousand-yuan vs 500-yuan tier)

**Core Problem**: Wuliangye's differentiation mainly reflects in brand awareness within the "thousand-yuan strong aroma type" price tier, but this differentiation is being eaten by Guojiao 1573. If price moves above 1500 yuan, Wuliangye has almost no competitiveness.

| Period | Distributor Purchase Cost | Market Wholesale Price | Unit Margin |
|------|-------------|---------|---------|
| First half 2025 | ~920 yuan | ~850 yuan | **-70 yuan loss per bottle** |

| From 2026 (after invoice price reduction) | ~800-850 yuan | ~850 yuan | **0~+50 yuan micro-profit/break-even** |

**Channel Ecosystem Vicious Cycle**:
```
wholesale price inversion → distributor loss → eager to liquidate inventory → inventory flow increases → wholesale price further declines → more inversion
```

### 4.3 Inventory Flow Issues

Wuliangye has launched a "digital liquor certificate" tracing system, but inventory flow remains severe:
- E-commerce platform subsidy prices as low as 820-860 yuan
- Some offline channels even lower, reports mention extreme low prices of 640-750 yuan (including various discounts/clearance)
- Large supermarkets and e-commerce platforms become "price destroyers"

### 4.4 Channel Reform Measures

| Measure | Content | Effectiveness Assessment |
|------|------|---------|
| Invoice price reduction | 1019→900 yuan | Narrows inversion, but acknowledges "price cut" fact |
| Distributor cleanup | 90 fewer in 2025 | Optimizes channel, but means declining sales momentum |
| Direct sales expansion | Self-operated stores up to 200+ | Price control strengthened, but scale limited |
| E-commerce share increase | E-commerce revenue ~15% | Double-edged sword—online low prices undermine offline price system |
| Digital liquor certificate | Track flow | Effective technical means but difficult execution |

**Counterargument Test**: Wuliangye's channel reform is essentially "repaying past debts"—in past years, to pursue revenue growth, they pushed too much inventory to distributors; now they must spend much greater cost to absorb it. Kweichow Moutai never needs these measures—supply shortage.

## 6. Core Question: Is Moat Widening or Narrowing?

### 6.1 Evidence Moat is Narrowing

| Evidence | Details |
|------|------|
| **Pricing Power Loss** | First price reduction in a decade; wholesale price consistently below ex-factory; suggested retail price 1399 yuan purely nominal |
| **Brand Value Gap Widening** | Gap with Kweichow Moutai brand value from 1.5x to 2.2x (2025) |
| **Market Cap Gap Widening** | Gap with Kweichow Moutai from 3x to ~4.8x |
| **Selling Expenses Soaring** | 2024 selling expenses 106.92 billion yuan, year-over-year +37.15%—spending more and more to maintain sales, brand organic pull declining |
| **Competition Intensifying** | Guojiao 1573 continuously eating thousand-yuan tier; Jiannanchun rising in 500-800 yuan band |
| **Channel Health Deteriorating** | Distributor losses, inventory flow, inventory accumulation—healthy moats don't cause channel partners continuous losses |
| **Management Governance Collapse** | Two consecutive board chairmen under investigation; financial statement credibility severely damaged |
| **Lagging Youth Transition** | Kweichow Moutai's youth transition is natural brand momentum extension, Wuliangye in passive catch-up state |

### 6.2 Evidence Moat Still Exists

| Evidence | Details |
|------|------|
| **Thousand-Yuan Tier Leader** | 8th Generation Puwan still #1 in 800-1200 yuan range, no one can replace short-term |
| **Irreplaceable Ancient Cellars** | Ming Dynasty cellar pool complex 600 years continuous use, cellar age core barrier for strong aroma type |
| **Consumption Scenario Essential** | Business banquets and weddings still view Wuliangye as "safe bet" |
| **Scale Advantage** | Annual production 160,000 tons+ far exceeds competitors, cost advantage from scale |
| **Cash Reserves** | 138.5 billion yuan monetary funds (2025Q1), extremely high financial margin of safety |
| **Series Wine Growth** | Series wine revenue growth 11.79%, multi-product matrix forming |

### 6.3 Composite Judgment

**Data points to moat slowly narrowing.**

Key logical chain:
```
Baijiu industry decline → demand contraction → consumers becoming rational → brand loyalty declining (especially non-Kweichow Moutai brands)
→ Wuliangye facing more substitute competition → forced price cuts for volume → pricing power weakening → moat narrowing
```

But this is not "moat collapse"—rather "wide moat" contracting to "medium moat". Wuliangye's brand position in thousand-yuan strong aroma type still solid, just no longer "resting easy."

**But on the other hand**: Industry downturns best test real moat strength. If Wuliangye can complete channel cleanup, price system repair, management turnover through this cycle, moat could widen again post-cycle. Historically, Wuliangye faced similar 2014 deep adjustment cycle difficulties, eventually completing recovery in 3 years.

## 7. Ultimate Comparison with Kweichow Moutai: Will Wuliangye Always Be "Eternal Second"?

### 7.1 Historical Perspective: Wuliangye Once Was "Baijiu King"

| Period | Competitive Landscape |
|------|---------|
| 1994-2003 | **Wuliangye dominated**: Pricing exceeded Kweichow Moutai (2005 ex-factory price 70 yuan more), sales volume #1 |
| Around 2005 | Inflection point: Wuliangye crazy co-branding (100+ sub-brands), brand severely diluted |
| 2008 | Wuliangye first lost revenue crown |
| Post-2012 | Kweichow Moutai comprehensively overtook, gap continuously widening to today |

**Historical Lesson**: Wuliangye's decline not because product worsened, but management short-sightedness—used co-branding for short-term revenue, sacrificed brand "purity." Kweichow Moutai always stuck to "one product only" extreme focus.

### 7.2 Possibility of Catch-up Analysis

**Catch-up Arguments**:
1. Wuliangye production capacity (160,000 tons) far exceeds Kweichow Moutai (~50,000 tons), theoretically more growth space
2. If Classic Wuliangye series opens ultra-premium market (1500+), can break through upward
3. If management turnover improves governance, can release suppressed brand value

**Impossible Catch-up Arguments (Counterargument—more persuasive)**:

1. **Consumer Mindshare Already Solidified**: Kweichow Moutai="China's best baijiu" deeply rooted, advertising can't change. Duan Yongping said "many friends drink only Kweichow Moutai"—once formed, mindshare nearly irreversible
2. **Natural Technique Barrier**: Sauce aroma type longer fermentation cycle (5+ years), complex technique, natural production cap—this "scarcity" strong aroma type can't replicate. Wuliangye bigger capacity means less scarcity
3. **Financial Attribute Gap**: Kweichow Moutai has investment/collectible attributes (older = more valuable), Wuliangye doesn't. Gives Kweichow Moutai extra "financial moat"
4. **Price Anchoring Effect**: Kweichow Moutai at 2000+, Wuliangye at 800-1000. Once consumers form "Wuliangye = thousand-yuan baijiu" price perception, upward break extremely difficult
5. **10-Year Compound Growth Rate Gap**: Kweichow Moutai ~12%, Wuliangye only ~2%—gap expanding exponentially

**Conclusion: Probability of Wuliangye "surpassing Kweichow Moutai" is extremely low, nearly impossible.**

More realistic problem is not "can it catch Kweichow Moutai" but "can it keep #2 position." Guojiao 1573 increasingly fierce in thousand-yuan tier, Luzhou Laojiao management execution recognized superior to Wuliangye—if Wuliangye continues governance issues, #2 position not guaranteed.

### 7.3 "Eternal Second" Strategic Meaning

From investment angle, "eternal second" isn't necessarily bad. Duan Yongping investing Apple never cared if Apple's market share #1. Key question is:

**Can Wuliangye as "second" continuously earn money?**

Current answer is **yes, but earning power declining**:
- Normalized net profit from 32 billion peak possibly falling to 18-23 billion
- Even at 18 billion net profit, 15x PE = 270 billion yuan (vs current 376.8 billion, still has downside)
- Even at 23 billion net profit, 20x PE = 460 billion yuan (from current can have 20% upside)

## 8. 2025 Financial Correction Event: Special Risk Warning

### 8.1 Event Overview

April 30, 2026, Wuliangye released "Prior Period Accounting Error Correction Notice," providing retroactive adjustment of 2025 first three quarters data:

| Metric | Pre-Adjustment | Post-Adjustment | Adjustment Amount |
|------|--------|--------|--------|

| 2025 Full Year Net Profit | ~31.8 billion yuan (estimated) | 8.954 billion yuan | **-71.89%** |

### 8.2 Correction Substance

- **Company's Statement**: "Accounting error correction," based on conservatism principle adjusted revenue recognition timing
- **Market Interpretation**: Substance is shifting "inventory pushed to distributor" recognition to "actual end-user sales" recognition
- **Deeper Meaning**: Past 2-3 years revenue had large "advance recognition" component—shipment to distributor warehouse = sale, but may actually be accumulating in channel

### 8.3 Duan Yongping Perspective

> "Disloyal management will turn good business into bad."

This financial correction event exposes problems far exceeding finance technique level:
1. **How much of past years' high growth was real?** If "shipping to distributor=recognition" is long-term practice, 2022-2024 revenue all may have "water"
2. **How's new management integrity?** Huge bath + 2026Q1 high growth combo itself suspicious of "first dig hole, then claim credit"
3. **Systemic SOE governance risk**: Wuliangye 55% state-owned, two consecutive board chairmen under investigation—not personal issue but system issue

Using Duan Yongping's three standards for final judgment:

| **Reasonable Price** | Per normalized profit 18-23 billion, current PE 16-21x, not expensive but not cheap | ⚠️ Ambiguous |

**Final Judgment: Duan Yongping likely would not buy Wuliangye.**

Logic is simple—if investing in baijiu, why not buy Kweichow Moutai directly?

Duan Yongping bought Kweichow Moutai from 2012 onward, bought at 180 yuan and added at 120 yuan. His rationale was never "baijiu is good" but "Kweichow Moutai is good." In Duan Yongping's framework, only invest in the best player in an industry—when industry declines, only best survives well; when industry rises, best rises most.

**Wuliangye is a good business, but by Duan Yongping's standards, it's not good enough—differentiation not strong enough, pricing power not hard enough, corporate culture has problems. If there's obviously better choice in same industry (Kweichow Moutai), Wuliangye doesn't deserve investment.**

> Duan Yongping: "Investment doesn't need courage. If you need courage to make investment decisions, it means you don't understand the business well enough."

For Wuliangye, requires too much "courage" to assume management will improve, moat will widen again, pricing power will recover—every one of these assumptions carries uncertainty. But investing in Kweichow Moutai requires almost none of these assumptions.
