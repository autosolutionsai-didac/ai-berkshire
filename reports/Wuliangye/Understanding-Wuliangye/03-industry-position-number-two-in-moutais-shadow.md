## What's Happening in the Baijiu Industry?

In 2025, Shanxi Fenjiu's net profit of 12.25 billion yuan historically surpassed Wuliangye's 8.95 billion yuan. Outside Kweichow Moutai, the baijiu market landscape is being rewritten. The "Moutai-Wuliangye-Yanghe" structure became "Moutai-Fenjiu-Wuliangye" in just under three years.

To understand Wuliangye's industry position, we must first understand the industry itself—**the baijiu industry is experiencing a historic contraction.**

### Production: Fell Nearly 70% Over 8 Years

| Year | Production (10 Million Liters) | YoY |
|------|------------|------|
| 2016 | **1,358** (peak) | — |
| 2018 | 871 | -27.3% |
| 2020 | 741 | -5.8% |
| 2022 | 671 | -6.2% |
| 2023 | 449 | -33.1% |
| 2024 | **414** | -1.8% |
| 2025 H1 | 192 | -5.8% |

*Data Source: National Bureau of Statistics*

From 135.8 million liters to 41.4 million liters—**8 consecutive years of decline, reducing industry scale to less than one-third.**

Yet what about revenue? 2024 baijiu industry sales revenue was **796.4 billion yuan**, up **36%** from 2020's 583.6 billion yuan. Production fell 44%, revenue rose 36%.

**This shows the entire industry is "trading quantity for price"—selling less but at higher prices. Profit is accelerating toward high-end products and leading enterprises.**

However, this game finally stopped working in 2025. The combined revenue of 19 baijiu listed companies fell by **18.13%** year-on-year (Sina Finance). The era of volume-price contraction has arrived.

### Concentration: Oligopoly Accelerating

| Metric | 2015 | 2020 | 2024 | Trend |
|--------|------|------|------|------|
| CR5 (Top 5 Revenue Share) | <20% | ~30% | **45%** | Rapid concentration |
| CR10 | ~30% | ~40% | **~60%** | Oligopoly |
| Listed Companies CR3 | — | — | **65.4%** | Three giants dominant |

*Data Source: Qianzhan Industry Research, Company Financial Reports*

The baijiu industry is transitioning from "100 flowers blooming" to "oligopoly monopoly." **The story is basically that of the top five companies.**

*Data Source: Company 2025 Annual Reports*

**Three Major Signals:**

**First, Shanxi Fenjiu's historic breakthrough.** Full year 2025 revenue growth of 7.5%, expanding against market contraction. 2025 Q3 single-quarter revenue of 8.96 billion temporarily surpassed Wuliangye's 8.17 billion for that quarter. Net profit of **12.25 billion**, surpassing Wuliangye's **8.95 billion** for the first time.

Note that Wuliangye's 2025 data is severely distorted by accounting adjustments—on 2024's normal basis (31.9 billion profit), Wuliangye still significantly leads Fenjiu. But the trend is clear: **Fenjiu is catching up, Wuliangye is losing momentum.**

**Second, the former "Moutai-Wuliangye-Yanghe" structure is becoming "Moutai-Fenjiu-Wuliangye."** Yanghe is falling fastest, Fenjiu rising most sharply. The "absolute dominance" of aroma-style baijiu is being broken by light-aroma-style.

**Third, the industry's "Moutai galloping alone, others struggling" polarization is intensifying.** Moutai only declined 1.2%, while the industry average fell 18%—Moutai isn't "surviving the cycle," it's "eating other's share" within the cycle.

## Price Band Warfare: Wuliangye's "Sandwich Cookie" Dilemma

### Ultra-Premium (2,000+ yuan): Moutai Monopoly, Unchallenged

| Metric | Feitian Kweichow Moutai |
|--------|---------|
| Factory Price | 1,269 yuan (after March 2026 adjustment) |
| Original Case Wholesale Price | **1,700-1,730 yuan** |
| Single Bottle Wholesale Price | **1,565-1,580 yuan** |
| Channel Profit Per Bottle | **~400 yuan** |

*Data Source: Sichuan Observation, Paileet News*

Feitian Kweichow Moutai is the only baijiu with "wholesale price exceeding factory price"—it's a financial product, not just a consumer good. Distributors scramble for allocations.

### Premium (800-1,500 yuan): Standard Wuliangye and Luzhou Laojiao All in Inversion

| Product | Factory Price | Wholesale Price (Early 2026) | Inversion Magnitude |
|--------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Eighth-Gen Standard Wuliangye | 1,019→900 yuan | **880-920 yuan** | **-10% to -14%** |
| Luzhou Laojiao 1573 | ~980 yuan | **750-800 yuan** | **-18% to -23%** |
| Blue Flower Langjiu | 1,099 yuan | 630 yuan | -43% |
| Junpin Xijiu | 1,498 yuan | 588 yuan | **-61%** |

*Data Source: Sina Finance, 21st Century Business Herald, Channel Research*

**Key Findings**:

1. No thousand-yuan price range product stands above factory price—all in inversion
2. The wholesale price gap between standard Wuliangye and Luzhou Laojiao narrowed from historical 200 yuan to near parity—consumers and distributors no longer feel standard Wuliangye is "naturally more expensive" than Laojiao
3. Lower price range products (Blue Flower Langjiu, Xijiu) have worse inversion—premium baijiu is "collapsing downward"

### Premium-Accessible (300-800 yuan): Most Brutal Battlefield

Premium-accessible is called "sandwich cookie"—premium baijiu squeezing from above, mass-market baijiu upgrading from below.

- Group purchase-focused premium baijiu sales **universally down 30-50%**
- Government group purchases **nearly halved**
- 500-800 yuan price segment faces maximum survival pressure—precisely the range where standard Wuliangye and Laojiao "slide down" into

### Wuliangye's Specific Dilemma

```
       ↑ Can't reach up to Moutai: brand momentum gap too large, no financial attributes
       |
  Wuliangye ← Being matched by Laojiao on sides: wholesale price gap shrinking to near parity
       |
       ↓ Being outflanked by Fenjiu below: light-aroma differentiation + banquet channels
```

**Wuliangye is experiencing "three-way pressure": upward Moutai is unreachable, sideways Laojiao pressing close, downward Fenjiu outflanking.**

## Wuliangye vs. Kweichow Moutai: "Eternal Second Place"?

### Historical Perspective: Wuliangye Was Once the "Baijiu King"

Many don't know this history—Wuliangye wasn't "always second."

| Period | Competitive Landscape |
|--------|---------|
| 1994-2003 | **Wuliangye dominated**: factory price 70 yuan more expensive than Moutai, top sales |
| Around 2005 | Turning point: Wuliangye went crazy with sub-brands (100+), brand severely diluted |
| 2008 | Wuliangye painfully lost revenue crown for the first time |
| 2012 onwards | Moutai completely overtakes, gap continuously widens to today |

*Data Source: Sina Finance, Industry Historical Records*

**Wuliangye's decline wasn't because the product worsened, but due to management myopia—using sub-branding strategy to exchange long-term brand "purity" for short-term revenue.** Over 100 sub-brands diluted the value of "Wuliangye" three characters, while Moutai consistently maintained "one product only" extreme focus.

### Possibility of Catching Kweichow Moutai

**Arguments for Catching Up**:
1. Wuliangye's capacity (**1.6 million tons**) far exceeds Moutai's (~500,000 tons), theoretically more growth room
2. If the Classic Wuliangye series opens the ultra-premium market (1,500+ yuan), could break upward
3. After management renewal, if governance improves, could unlock suppressed brand value

**Counter-Evidence (More Convincing)**:
1. **Consumer mindset is ossified**—the understanding "Moutai = China's best baijiu" is deeply ingrained, not changeable by spending on advertising
2. **Natural barriers from production differences**—sauce-style fermentation cycle long (5+ years), high process complexity, naturally constrained capacity. Aroma-style lacks this "scarcity," Wuliangye's larger capacity actually makes it less scarce
3. **Financial attributes gap**—Moutai has investment/collectible value (more valuable over time), Wuliangye hardly any. Moutai has an extra layer of "financial moat"
4. **Price anchoring effect**—consumers formed the price perception "Wuliangye = thousand-yuan baijiu," upward breakthrough is very difficult
5. **CAGR gap**—past decade Moutai approximately **12%**, Wuliangye only **2%**, gap widening exponentially

**Conclusion: Probability of Wuliangye surpassing Moutai is extremely low.**

The more realistic question isn't "can it catch Moutai," but rather—

## More Realistic Threats: Is Second Place Still Secure?

### Shanxi Fenjiu: The True Challenger

Shanxi Fenjiu's 2025 performance was remarkable:

| Metric | Wuliangye (2025 Adjusted) | Shanxi Fenjiu (2025) |
|--------|---------------------|----------------|
| Revenue | 40.5 billion | **38.7 billion** |
| Net Profit | 8.95 billion | **12.25 billion** |
| YoY Growth | -54.6% | **+7.5%** |

*Data Source: Company 2025 Annual Reports*

Wuliangye's 2025 data is severely distorted by accounting adjustments, making direct comparison unfair. But even on 2024's normal basis (89.2 billion vs ~36 billion), Fenjiu's catching-up speed can't be ignored—Fenjiu's 5-year revenue CAGR is about **20%**, Wuliangye's same period about **7%**.

**Fenjiu's Differentiation Strategy**:
- Light-aroma vs. aroma-style—product differentiation, avoiding direct competition with Wuliangye
- Deep cultivation of banquet channels—rapid penetration growth in wedding banquets, birthday celebrations, etc.
- Flexible price bands—Blue Flower series covers 300-1,000 yuan, precisely positions premium-accessible

**However**—Fenjiu's brand ceiling is currently still below Wuliangye's. In the pure premium market above 1,000 yuan, Fenjiu's awareness and share far lag Wuliangye's. Fenjiu's surpassing is more "mid-tier rise" than "premium replacement."

### Luzhou Laojiao: Close Combat

Luzhou Laojiao's 1573 is Wuliangye's direct competitor in the thousand-yuan price band.

| Comparison Dimension | Wuliangye | Luzhou Laojiao |
|---------|--------|---------|
| 2025 Net Profit | 8.95 billion (adjusted) | **10.8 billion** |
| PE | ~11x | ~15x |
| Accounting Cleanliness | **C+** (blowup) | **A-** (clean) |
| Wholesale Price Recovery | Still in inversion | **Already recovered to ~990 yuan** |
| Accounts Receivable | Exploded **163%** | **Nearly zero** |

*Data Source: Company Annual Reports, Channel Research*

**A Notable Fact**: Luzhou Laojiao proactively cut supply and controlled volume in 2025; 1573's wholesale price recovered from a low of 875 yuan to 990 yuan. While Wuliangye's standard Wuliangye remains in inversion.

**Positive View**: Wuliangye's brand momentum, channel scale, capacity advantages still far exceed Luzhou Laojiao's. On a 30-year horizon, Wuliangye remains the strongest contender for "aroma-style champion."

**Negative View**: On a 1-2 year horizon, Luzhou Laojiao leads across channel health, financial report cleanliness, valuation safety. The market is voting with its feet—Laojiao's PE is higher than Wuliangye's, indicating the market trusts Laojiao more.

## Industry Endgame: Will Baijiu Follow Japanese Sake's Path?

### Japanese Sake's Cautionary Tale

| Metric | Japanese Sake | Chinese Baijiu |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Consumption Trend | 2004-2022 CAGR **-3%** | 2016-2024 Production CAGR **-13.7%** |
| Premiumization | Ginjo/Junmai CAGR +2% | Premium baijiu share continuously rising |
| Population Factor | Aging + low birth rate | **Population inflection point passed 2022** |
| Youth Preference | Shifting to beer/foreign liquor/RTD | Shifting to low-alcohol/craft/fruit wine |

*Data Source: Sina Finance, HSBC Qianhai*

**Similarities (Pessimistic Signals)**:
- Total volume continuously shrinking, Chinese baijiu decline rate (**-13.7%/year**) even faster than Japanese sake (-3%/year)
- Youth generational replacement irreversible—baijiu consumption among under-30s only **8%**
- Core consumer demographic (30-59) shrinking at **-1%/year**

**Differences (Relatively Optimistic)**:
- Baijiu's embedding in Chinese culture (weddings, funerals, business social) far exceeds sake's place in Japan
- Higher brand concentration—Moutai's "psychological monopoly" degree far exceeds any Japanese sake brand
- Market scale 800+ billion yuan, even with 20-30% contraction still substantial

### Endgame Assessment

**Baijiu won't follow Japanese sake's "comprehensive decline" path, more likely following "Japanese whisky's" path—total volume continuously shrinking, leading brands growing against trend, tail end mass death.**

Predictions:
- **Total volume**: Future 10 years production may contract another **30-40%**
- **Revenue**: Industry total revenue may remain stable (trading quantity for price)
- **Structure**: Final form "**1 super (Moutai) + 3 strong (Wuliangye/Fenjiu/Luzhou Laojiao) + N regional kings**"

### Implications for Wuliangye

1. Wuliangye will likely survive to endgame—brand, channels, scale all sufficient
2. But "eternal second place" position no longer secure—Fenjiu threat is real
3. Key variable is whether it can complete channel purging and governance repair this cycle

## Industry Cycle: Where's the Bottom?

### Current Cycle Positioning

This baijiu downturn began in 2024 Q2, lasting about 2 years through May 2026.

Compared to the previous cycle:

| Dimension | 2012-2015 Cycle | 2024-2026/27 Current |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Triggering Factors | Eight-Point Regulation + plasticizer scandal | Alcohol ban + consumption downgrade + population inflection |
| Government Spending Impact | Fell from 40% to 5% | Limited marginal impact (already below 5%) |
| Demand Replacement | Commercial consumption stepped in | **Commercial + consumer both contracting, no replacement** |
| Recovery Duration | ~3 years | Expected longer |

*Data Source: Industry Research Reports, United Credit*

**This round is harder to overcome than 2012-2015—lacking "replacement growth."** 2012 lost government spending, commercial stepped in. This time commercial and consumer both contract, who steps in?

### Inventory Reduction Progress

| Category | Inventory Status (2026 Q1) | Estimated Clearance Time |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| Feitian Kweichow Moutai | Near safe inventory | **Essentially complete** |
| Standard Wuliangye/Luzhou Laojiao | 1-2 months, down 30%+ after CNY | 2026 Q2 |
| Premium-Accessible | 3-4 months, still high pressure | 2026 Q2-Q3 |
| Regional Brands | Over 70% inventory elevated | 2026 H2+ |

*Data Source: East Finance Channel Research*

**Inventory improving but far from cleared. Industry fundamental bottom expected 2026 Q4-2027 Q1.**
