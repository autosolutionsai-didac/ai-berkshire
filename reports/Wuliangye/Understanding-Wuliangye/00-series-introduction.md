## Series Positioning

Wuliangye is not a company lacking attention—it is the **most controversial, most information-confused** stock in the A-share baijiu sector.

- Bull case: PE **11x**, dividend yield **5.6%**, CNY 127.4 billion cash on balance sheet, the baijiu printing machine was wrongly crushed
- Bear case: two consecutive board chairmen fell from power, CNY 30.3 billion revenue inflated, moat narrowing, cheap for a reason

**Both sides are looking at the same company but reaching completely opposite conclusions.** Worse, both can pull out hard data to support their judgment.

What this series does is **clarify "what it is" and clarify "why it's cheap"**—so you can make your own judgment on "whether it's worth buying."

## Writing Principles

1. **Present data first, then logic, then conclusion**—no predetermined bull or bear bias
2. **Every core judgment includes counter-viewpoint**—let readers weigh both sides
3. **Unreliable data is better deleted**—Wuliangye's 2025 annual report has data gaps due to accounting adjustments; this series clearly marks which data are reliable and which are questionable
4. **Does not constitute investment recommendation**—all essays include a disclaimer at the end
