---

## Series Positioning

Unitree Robotics is not a company that "no one pays attention to" — after its robot dance went viral at the 2025 Spring Festival Gala, it became the company in China's robotics race with **the highest visibility but the shallowest understanding**.

- The bulls say: world's No. 1 in humanoid robot shipments, 60% gross margin, 335% annual growth — this is "the DJI of robotics"
- The bears say: **1.7 billion yuan** in revenue can't support a **42 billion yuan** valuation, 73% of revenue depends on research and education, the AI brain is second-rate, and it's finished the moment Tesla arrives

**Each side sees half the truth.** What this series sets out to do is first make clear "what it is," and then let you judge for yourself "whether it's worth investing in" — rather than reaching a conclusion for you.

## Series Writing Principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Data first, then logic, then conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — letting readers weigh it themselves
3. **Unreliable data is better left out** — many key figures for Unitree Robotics (humanoid-robot GMV by customer, breakdown of overseas revenue by country, etc.) are not fully disclosed; this series anchors only on the prospectus and the most credible sources, without forcing in unsupported figures
4. **No "probability-weighted expected return" calculations** — assigning weights to scenarios is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **Does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article
