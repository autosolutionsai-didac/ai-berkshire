## Series positioning

Tencent Music isn't an obscure, unwatched company — among Chinese ADRs, it's one of the names that is **badly overlooked yet whose data is deeply contradictory**.

- The bulls say: profit has grown for years running, net cash of 38 billion yuan equals 40% of market cap, and cash-adjusted PE is only about 6x — absurdly cheap
- The bears say: monthly active users have declined for 16 straight quarters, Qishui Music is attacking fiercely, and the stock has crashed 68% over 52 weeks — the market isn't stupid

**Both sides have a point, yet they reach opposite conclusions.** That tells us the issue isn't who's right and who's wrong — it's that each side is looking at a different facet of the same company.

What this series sets out to do is **lay every facet out clearly** — so you can judge for yourself whether it's worth buying, rather than having a conclusion handed to you.

## Series writing principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, and only then the conclusion** — no presetting a bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **Written in genuine, natural prose** — no mixing in fragments of another language
4. **No "I think" or "I feel"** — use "the data shows" or "the evidence indicates" instead
5. **Unreliable data is dropped rather than used** — much of the data on Qishui Music is not publicly disclosed, so only the most credible sources are used
6. **Not investment advice** — every article ends with a disclaimer
