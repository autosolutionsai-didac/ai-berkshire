## 8. Summary

Tencent is one of the most certain long-term investment targets in the Chinese internet. WeChat's 1.4 billion MAU social network effect forms a nearly insurmountable moat — every challenger over the past decade has failed. Management has demonstrated commitment to shareholder value with a HK$112 billion buyback (the largest in Hong Kong stock market history). Gross margin surging from 42% to 56%, combined with 22% annualized EPS growth, shows this is not just a good company, but one that keeps getting better.

The main concerns center on AI: the 5.5x gap between Yuanbao and Doubao shows Tencent isn't evolving fast enough in the AI application layer. But WeChat's social relationship graph provides the strongest possible setting for AI deployment — the key question is whether management can strike the right balance between its "restraint" culture and "aggressive" competition.

A Non-IFRS P/E of 16x, a SOTP valuation of HK$582 (vs. the current HK$505), combined with 5x upside room in Weixin Channels ad monetization and ongoing buyback support, make the current price a textbook opportunity to "buy a great company at a reasonable price." Duan Yongping's RMB 36 billion position, which he calls "not for sale," is the best footnote to this thesis.
