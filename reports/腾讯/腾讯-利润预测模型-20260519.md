## Forecasting Framework

```
Non-IFRS net profit = Σ(segment revenue) × Non-IFRS net margin
Segment revenue = f(user base × ARPU, market growth rate, share change)
Margin = f(business-mix shift, pace of AI investment, scale effects)
EPS = net profit ÷ (total shares outstanding − cumulative buyback cancellations)
```

Breakdown of core variables:

| Variable | Weight | Predictability | Current Direction |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Ad revenue growth | High | Medium-high | Accelerating (rising Video Accounts monetization rate) |
| Games revenue growth | High | Medium | Steady (dual engines of domestic + overseas) |
| FBS growth | Medium | Medium | Stable (cloud accelerating, payments slowing) |
| AI investment intensity | High | Low | Rising sharply (doubling in 2026 to over RMB 36.0 billion) |
| Pace of buyback cancellations | Medium | Medium | Slowing (capital redirected to AI) |
| Margin trend | High | Medium-high | Under short-term pressure (AI spend), expanding over the medium-to-long term |

## II. 5-Year Forecast (2031)

### Assumed Changes in Business Mix

By 2031, Tencent's business structure is assumed to change as follows:

1. **Advertising may become the largest revenue segment**: If Video Accounts monetization keeps accelerating, ad revenue could overtake value-added services
2. **AI products should start contributing revenue**: After five years of development, Yuanbao/Hunyuan/AI agents and the like should show commercialization results
3. **International games' share should rise further**: from the current 21% to over 30%
4. **Cumulative buyback-cancellation effects become significant**: cumulative cancellations of roughly 9-12% of share capital over five years

## III. 10-Year Forecast (2036)

### Framework for the 10-Year Forecast

Tencent's profit ten years out hinges on three core questions:

1. **Will WeChat still be China's ultimate traffic gateway?** (determines the ceiling for advertising + ecosystem revenue)
2. **Has AI investment created a new profit source?** (determines the incremental profit)
3. **Can the international business become a second growth pole?** (determines the durability of growth)

### The 10-Year Durability of WeChat's Moat

**Supporting evidence**:
- WeChat is an OS-level application (payments + social + content + services), so switching costs are extremely high
- Analogy: Facebook 2016→2026, MAU grew from 1.8 billion to 3.3 billion, ARPU rose from $15 to $60+, and it has not been disrupted to date
- WeChat's position in the AGI era: AI agents will most likely "run on top of WeChat" rather than "bypass WeChat"

**Counter-risks**:
- Younger users migrating to new platforms (though every "WeChat killer" over the past decade has failed)
- Regulator-mandated breakup (extremely low but non-zero probability)

**Judgment**: The probability that WeChat remains China's top traffic gateway ten years from now is > 90%

## IV. Sensitivity Analysis

### AI Investment Is the Biggest Variable

Tencent's 2026 AI investment is projected to exceed RMB 36.0 billion (double the RMB 18.0 billion in 2025), with a "significant increase" expected in the second half. This variable has an enormous impact on profit:

| AI Investment Scenario | Average Annual Spend | Impact on Net Profit | Likelihood of Returns |
|-----------|---------|------------|-----------|
| Conservative spending | RMB 30.0 billion/year | -RMB 20.0 billion/year (net of enablement benefits) | Low risk, low return |
| **Current pace** | **RMB 40.0-50.0 billion/year** | **-RMB 25.0 billion/year** | **Medium risk, medium return** |
| Aggressive spending | RMB 70.0 billion+/year | -RMB 45.0 billion/year | High risk, high return |

**The two faces of AI investment**:
- **AI that's already making money** (Layer 1): higher ad efficiency — of the +20% ad growth in Q1 2026, roughly 5-8pct is attributable to AI optimization. On a RMB 145.0 billion advertising base, AI contributes an incremental RMB 7.0-12.0 billion in ad revenue each year
- **AI that's still burning cash** (Layer 2): Yuanbao MAU is only 57 million (one-sixth of Doubao's 340 million). The Hunyuan large model holds under 5% share of the MaaS market

**Core judgment**: Layer 1 of AI investment (enabling existing businesses) has confirmed, steadily rising returns; Layer 2 (standalone AI products) needs 2-3 years to be validated. If Layer 2 shows no significant payoff before 2028, management may adjust strategy.

## V. Core Conclusions

### One-Line Verdict

**Tencent is China's most predictable profit-growth machine. On a probability-weighted basis, the EPS CAGR is ~12% over 3 years, ~12% over 5 years, and ~10% over 10 years; adding a 2-3% dividend yield gives a total return of roughly 14-15%/year. The core risk lies not in the business itself but in the pace of AI investment and geopolitics.**
