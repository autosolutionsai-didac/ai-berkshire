## 0. Four-Sentence Answers to the User's Core Questions

### Q: How exactly is the $144.0 billion reflected on the balance sheet?
**A "mixed-basis" disclosure**: the listed portion (US$93.5 billion) is carried at fair value, and the unlisted portion (US$50.5 billion) at carrying value. What actually appears on the balance sheet — the **carrying-value total — is only US$100.0 billion** (RMB 717.5 billion: associates RMB 300.0 billion + joint ventures RMB 7.5 billion + non-current FVTPL RMB 245.0 billion + FVOCI RMB 165.0 billion). The remaining US$44.0 billion is the appreciation in the market value of listed associates disclosed in the footnotes (**not reflected in the carrying value**).

### Q: Is it fully reflected?
**Answer: formally adequate, substantively insufficient**. The disclosure-transparency score is **15/25 (60%, below average)**, notably weaker than Berkshire's 10/10, SoftBank's 7/10, and Prosus's 6/10. It is IFRS-compliant, but Tencent **does not disclose line-by-line** the fair value of associates, the specific list of FVTPL/FVOCI holdings, or the Level 3 valuation assumptions.

### Q: How much hidden value is there?
**RMB 250.0-350.0 billion (US$35.0-48.5 billion)**, with a neutral estimate of **RMB 300.0 billion (US$42.0 billion)**:
- Associates (the equity method doesn't reflect market value): **US$18.5-41.0 billion** (mainly Kuaishou/WeBank/Futu)
- Unlisted investments (carrying value is conservative relative to fair value): **US$11.0-17.5 billion** (ByteDance is the biggest variable)

### Q: What does this mean for valuation?
**SOTP total of US$945.5-1,068.5 billion**, a **56-77% discount** (median 65%) to the current market cap of US$604.0 billion (HKD 4.67 trillion). But a **20-30% holding-company discount** must be deducted — the true gap should be seen as a **more modest 30-50% undervaluation**. **Dividend-in-specie distributions are the only catalyst for unlocking hidden value** (already validated by the 2021 JD.com and 2022 Meituan distributions).

### 2.2 Top 3 sources of hidden value
1. **WeBank (30% unlisted)**: +RMB 20.0-45.0 billion
2. **Kuaishou (17%)**: +RMB 15.0-33.0 billion
3. **Futu (23-28%)**: +RMB 14.0-20.0 billion

### 2.3 Why does carrying value stay below market value for so long?
1. **Mechanics of the equity method**: carrying value = initial cost + cumulative share of profit/loss - dividends received ± OCI. If an associate is loss-making, its carrying value grows slowly (Kuaishou only turned profitable in 2024)
2. **China-concept discount**: after 2022 the valuation center for Hong Kong/US-listed China names shifted down; when market value falls, carrying value is written down accordingly, but when market value rises, carrying value doesn't adjust upward in step
3. **Unlisted associates (WeBank, WeDoctor)**: with no market price to reference, this severely understates highly profitable financial assets

3. **Easy to mark down, hard to mark up**: SHEIN's IPO has stalled, WeDoctor's valuation has pulled back
4. **Tax conservatism**: an overly high carrying value would trigger deferred income tax

## 5. SOTP Valuation Correction (Key Pricing Framework)

### 5.1 Three-part valuation

**① Core-business valuation (excluding investments)**:
- Non-GAAP net profit of ~RMB 250.0 billion in 2025
- A reasonable PE of **22-25x** (WeChat ecosystem moat + high-ROE gaming + stable dividends)
- Core-business market value = **RMB 5,500.0-6,250.0 billion (US$765.0-868.0 billion)**

**② True fair value of the investment portfolio**:
- Carrying value of RMB 1,035.8 billion + neutral-case hidden value of RMB 300.0 billion
- **True fair value = RMB 1,335.8 billion (US$185.5 billion)**

**③ Net cash**:
- Total cash of RMB 494.9 billion - interest-bearing debt of RMB 277.5 billion = **RMB 107.1 billion (US$15.0 billion)**

### 5.3 But a "holding-company discount" must be deducted

**Why does the market not fully re-rate this even though the carrying value is understated?**
1. Liquidity discount (unlisted holdings are hard to monetize)
2. Agency costs (management may not monetize in shareholders' best interest)
3. Tax friction (monetizing triggers tax liabilities)
4. Information asymmetry (Tencent's 5/10 disclosure score amplifies the discount)
5. Group management-fee burden

**Tencent's reasonable holding-company discount: 20-30%** (vs. Prosus's 30-40%, SoftBank's 40-50%)

**Post-discount true target value: US$670.0-750.0 billion** (**still +11-24% upside from current levels**)

## 6. Dividend-in-Specie Distributions Are the Core Tool for "Unlocking Value"

### 6.1 The value of historical precedents
- **December 2021 JD.com distribution**: a one-off distribution worth US$16.4 billion
- **November 2022 Meituan distribution**: a one-off distribution worth US$19.8-20.4 billion
- **Mechanism**: distributing an asset that's understated under the equity method to shareholders at market value
- **One-time realization of hidden value** + reclassification of the remaining stake as FVTPL

### 6.2 Why there's been no large-scale dividend-in-specie in 2024-2026
- The remaining Meituan (1.5%) and JD.com (2.3%) stakes are now too small to be worth distributing
- The strategic focus has shifted to **buybacks** (HKD 112.0 billion in 2024, HKD 80.0 billion in 2025)
- Buybacks plus cash dividends combine for a shareholder return yield of about **5-6%**

## 7. Investment Implications

### 7.1 Tencent cannot be valued with a simple PE multiple
- GAAP net profit is heavily affected by fair-value changes in the investments (contributing tens of billions of RMB in 2025)
- **A SOTP must be done**: core business × PE + investments at market value + cash - adjustments

### 7.2 Three layers of discount
Tencent's share price =
- Core-business PE discount (China-concept policy discount)
- + investment portfolio's carrying-value understatement
- + holding-company discount (20-30%)

**1-2 of these three layers of discount can be unlocked through dividend-in-specie distributions, buybacks, and improved transparency**.

### 7.3 The Q4 2025 pullback is an opportunity
- The investment portfolio fell from a Q3 high of RMB 1,146.0 billion → RMB 1,035.8 billion in Q4 (-10%)
- Tencent's share price pulled back over the same period
- **Buying window**: at a low point for China-concept stocks + ahead of a dividend-distribution catalyst + during an accelerating buyback period

### 7.4 Key signals to track
- **Quarterly 13F filings + HKEX disclosures**: changes in associate holdings
- **Interim/annual report footnotes**: changes in the investment portfolio's carrying value by classification
- **Management's earnings calls**: signals of dividend-distribution intent
- **The pace of Prosus's selldown**: technical pressure on the share price

## 8. Methodological Limitations and Data Confidence

### 8.1 Data confidence

| Judgment | Confidence |
|------|-------|
| A gap exists between carrying value and fair value | **High** (an inevitable consequence of IAS 28 accounting rules) |
| The specific gap figure of US$35.0-48.5 billion | **Medium** (depends on external estimates) |
| SOTP upside of 56-77% | **Medium** (depends on the core-business PE assumption) |
| Holding-company discount of 20-30% | **High** (evidenced by Prosus/SoftBank) |
| Future dividend-distribution catalyst | **Medium** (management's intent is unpredictable) |

### 8.2 Key data gaps
1. Tencent does not disclose associates' carrying values line-by-line
2. Tencent has never disclosed its specific stake in ByteDance (the biggest variable)
3. Its SHEIN stake is not disclosed
4. The list of designated FVOCI holdings is a complete black box
5. Level 3 valuation assumptions are not disclosed

### 8.3 Methodological limitations
- Subjectivity of Level 3 valuations
- Cumulative carrying value under the equity method must be tracked over many years; precise figures require reverse-engineering company by company
- Unlisted unicorn valuations (ByteDance especially) have doubled in 24 months, leaving carrying values badly lagging

## 9. Overall Conclusion (Final Word)

**Tencent's $144.0 billion investment portfolio at the end of 2025 is, on the balance sheet, "formally adequate but substantively insufficient"**:
- IFRS-compliant, but disclosure granularity scores only 5/10 (at the bottom of the peer group)
- The balance sheet shows only US$100.0 billion in carrying value (true fair value is US$185.5 billion)
- **Hidden value of US$35.0-48.5 billion (neutral estimate US$42.0 billion)**
- SOTP total of US$945.5-1,068.5 billion vs. the current US$604.0 billion
- **After deducting the holding-company discount, the true gap is still +11-24%**

**This is why value investors do a SOTP rather than a simple PE valuation** — Tencent "looks expensive (high PE)" but in reality "its hidden value hasn't been priced in." **Dividend-in-specie distributions are the only real catalyst for unlocking hidden value**, and Kuaishou/NIO are the holdings most likely to be distributed in the future.
