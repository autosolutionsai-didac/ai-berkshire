# Tencent (0700.HK) Investment Portfolio Valuation Report

**ai-berkshire Tencent Investment Portfolio Team | 2026-04-22**

## 1. Core Conclusion

As of 2025-12-31 (per Tencent's latest annual report), the fair value of Tencent's investment portfolio totals approximately **RMB 1,035.8 billion** (about **USD 144.0 billion**): of which **listed investments' fair value is RMB 672.7 billion (about USD 93.5 billion)**, and **unlisted investments' book value is RMB 363.1 billion (about USD 50.5 billion)**. Compared with RMB 1,146.0 billion as of 2025-09-30 (listed RMB 800.8 billion + unlisted RMB 345.2 billion), the listed portion pulled back approximately RMB 128.1 billion in Q4. This is equivalent to about **24%** of Tencent's current total market cap of roughly HKD 4.67 trillion.

## 4. Timeline of Major Divestments (Revised)

### Dec 2021 - March 2022: JD.com Dividend Distribution (Milestone)
- Distributed 958 million Class A JD.com shares (worth USD 16.4 billion / HKD 127.7 billion)
- Stake reduced from 17% to **2.3%**
- JD.com reclassified from associate (equity method) to FVTPL

### Nov 2022 - March 2023: Meituan Dividend Distribution
- Distributed 958 million Class B Meituan shares (worth USD 19.8 billion / HKD 159.4 billion)
- Stake reduced from 17% to **~1.5%**

### 2024-2025: More Modest Reductions
- No divestment on the scale of the JD.com/Meituan distributions occurred
- New investments dropped from 300+ per year to 20-40
- In 2025, "divestments exceeded new investments by over USD 1 billion"
- Capital shifted mainly toward AI capex (RMB 18.0 billion in 2025, doubling in 2026)

## 5. Prosus's Counter-Stake

- Prosus holds approximately **22.99%** of Tencent (as of April 2026)
- Launched an open-ended buyback program in June 2022: selling Tencent shares daily to fund buybacks of its own stock
- Cumulative reduction from 28.8% to 22.99% (down 5.8 percentage points)
- **In 2025, Tencent's HKD 80 billion in buybacks plus HKD 41 billion in dividends offset Prosus's selling pressure**
