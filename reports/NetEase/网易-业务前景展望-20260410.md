## Core highlights

**Gaming is the absolute lifeblood (82% of revenue)**:
- Fantasy Westward Journey has run for 22 years, with a concurrent-user record of 3.58 million — a super cash cow
- Marvel Rivals earned $136 million globally in its first month on the market, with 40 million cumulative players; 2025 was merely a "warm-up year" — **the biggest breakthrough in overseas expansion**
- Naraka: Bladepoint topped Steam's global bestseller list on its fourth anniversary
- Overseas revenue is only ~10% of the total — the single biggest growth lever

**The gap with Tencent's gaming business**:
| Metric | Tencent | NetEase | Ratio |
|------|------|------|------|
| 2025 gaming revenue | 241.6 billion yuan | 92.1 billion yuan | **2.6x** |
| Overseas gaming revenue | 77.4 billion yuan | ~9 billion yuan | **~8x** |
| Gap trend | — | — | **Widening** |

The structural reasons for the widening gap: Tencent has built a global footprint through acquisitions (Supercell/Riot/its Epic stake), while NetEase relies mainly on in-house development; Tencent also holds a near-monopoly on the mini-game market (a 53.5-billion-yuan market).

**Unless Marvel Rivals grows into a globally evergreen franchise earning tens of billions of yuan a year, the gap will keep widening.**

## What NetEase looks like five years from now

**"A high-margin gaming aristocrat" — excellent profitability and a solid moat, but growth lagging behind Tencent's.**

- Gaming still accounts for 80%+ of revenue; the company remains fundamentally a gaming company
- Overseas expansion is the biggest variable: if the Marvel Rivals franchise succeeds broadly, overseas share could rise from 10% to 20%
- Net margin remains above 30%, with ample free cash flow
- Youdao is the seed of a second growth engine, but its scale remains small
- The gap with Tencent may widen from 2.6x to roughly 3x, though absolute profit growth should still be sizable (annualized 8-10%)
