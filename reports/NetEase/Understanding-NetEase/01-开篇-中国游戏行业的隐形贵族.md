## A set of numbers that will make you look at NetEase differently

In 2025, NetEase turned in an exceptionally strong scorecard:

| Metric | 2025 figure | What it means |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Total revenue | 112.6 billion yuan | A hundred-billion-yuan-class internet company |
| Gaming revenue | 92.1 billion yuan (+11%) | China's second-largest gaming company |
| Non-GAAP net profit | 37.3 billion yuan (33% net margin) | For every 3 yuan earned, 1 yuan is net profit |
| Gross margin | 64.3% | Approaching luxury-goods-company territory |
| Free cash flow | ~49.6 billion yuan (capex of only 1.1 billion yuan) | Money earned barely needs to be reinvested |
| Net cash | 163.5 billion yuan | 31% of market capitalization |
| R&D spending | 17.7 billion yuan (15.7% of revenue) | Higher than the vast majority of Chinese internet companies |

A 33% net margin, 49.6 billion yuan of free cash flow, and only 1.1 billion yuan of capex — in plain terms: **NetEase is a machine that keeps "printing money" without needing to be "refueled."**

So what valuation does the market assign it?

| Valuation metric | Value | What it means |
|----------|------|----------|
| Share price | $113.61 | — |
| Market cap | $72.9 billion (about 530 billion yuan) | — |
| P/E (GAAP) | ~15x | Below the average for Chinese internet companies |
| P/E (Non-GAAP) | ~13x | Closer to actual earning power |
| Forward P/E | ~12.3x | Based on analyst consensus estimates |
| EV/EBITDA | 9.35x | Cheaper still once cash is excluded |
| P/FCF | 9.9x | Roughly 10 years of free cash flow buys the whole company |
| Net cash as % of market cap | 31% | Almost a third of the market cap is cash on the books |

In plain terms: **the market's pricing of NetEase amounts to handing you the 163.5 billion yuan of cash on the books at a 30% discount, while valuing the underlying business at under 10 years of profit.**

A company with a 33% net margin, 49.6 billion yuan of free cash flow, and still growing — priced at 13x P/E — either the market has spotted some serious problem, or the market has gotten it wrong.

Figuring out which is the goal of this series.

## Three common misconceptions about NetEase

Before digging in, let's dispel the three most widespread misconceptions.

### Misconception one: NetEase is just a gaming company

Most people's impression of NetEase begins and ends with Fantasy Westward Journey, Onmyoji, and Naraka: Bladepoint. "Isn't it just a game maker?"

That judgment isn't entirely wrong, but it isn't entirely right either.

Gaming does account for 82% of NetEase's total revenue and is absolutely the core. But focusing only on gaming means missing two other businesses that are undergoing a qualitative shift:

| Business | 2025 revenue | Key change |
|------|-----------|---------|
| NetEase Cloud Music | 7.76 billion yuan | Operating profit of 1.62 billion yuan, up 38.5% YoY — has just crossed the profitability inflection point |
| Youdao | 5.9 billion yuan (+5%) | AI subscription revenue of 400 million yuan (growth above 50%) — switching lanes |

Cloud Music went from "losing money year after year" to "earning 1.6 billion yuan a year." Youdao transformed from "online education" into "AI education." The two businesses together bring in 13.7 billion yuan in revenue — not a small figure for what would be, on its own, a standalone company.

**NetEase is indeed a gaming-centered company, but it is not merely "a gaming company."** A more accurate description: a content group with gaming as its engine and music and education as its two wings.

**Counterpoint**: Cloud Music and Youdao together account for only 18% of total revenue, and an even smaller share of total profit. At the end of the day, NetEase's fate still hinges on gaming — if gaming runs into trouble, the two wings can't save it.

### Misconception two: NetEase is Tencent's "little brother"

"Tencent is the leader, NetEase is the runner-up" — that's accurate in terms of industry ranking. But if that leads to the conclusion that "NetEase isn't worth studying on its own," that would be a serious mistake.

Consider this comparison:

| Metric | Tencent (gaming) | NetEase (gaming) | Gap |
|------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 2025 gaming revenue | 241.6 billion yuan | 92.1 billion yuan | 2.6x |
| Overseas gaming revenue | ~77.4 billion yuan | ~9 billion yuan (estimate) | ~8x |
| Mini-game market | 53.5 billion yuan, dominated by Tencent | Essentially absent | Effectively infinite |

By scale, Tencent is 2.6 times the size of NetEase. But scale isn't everything — look at another set of numbers:

| Metric | Tencent (overall) | NetEase |
|------|-----------|------|
| Non-GAAP net margin | ~28% (estimate) | **33%** |
| Gross margin | ~53% (estimate) | **64.3%** |
| Capex-to-revenue ratio | Relatively high | Only 1% (1.1 billion / 112.6 billion) |

**NetEase converts revenue into profit more efficiently than Tencent.** Its gross margin is 11 percentage points higher, its net margin 5 points higher, and its capital expenditure is nearly negligible by comparison.

To use an analogy: Tencent is a globalized internet empire, spanning social media, gaming, finance, and cloud computing. NetEase is more like a content-focused boutique workshop — smaller in scale, but with far higher margins and far more abundant cash flow.

**This isn't a "big brother, little brother" relationship — it's an "empire and aristocrat" relationship. The empire does everything; the aristocrat only does what's profitable.**

**Counterpoint**: A high margin could simply reflect underinvestment. Tencent has built a global moat through acquisitions of gaming studios worldwide (Supercell, Riot, its Epic stake). NetEase's higher margin comes partly from "spending less" — but spending less also means placing fewer bets, which could mean falling further behind over the long run.

### Misconception three: growing profit means everything is fine

2025 Non-GAAP net profit of 37.3 billion yuan represents solid growth. But growing profit doesn't mean there are no hidden problems.

At least three issues lurk behind the shiny profit figure:

**First, gaming revenue is highly concentrated.** 82% of revenue comes from gaming, and gaming revenue in turn depends heavily on a handful of long-running hit titles — Fantasy Westward Journey has been running for 23 years, alongside Naraka: Bladepoint and Eggy Party. Once a core title's lifecycle ends, where is the replacement?

**Second, overseas markets are nearly a blank slate.** Overseas revenue is only about 10% of the total, versus roughly 30% for Tencent. Seven overseas studios have been shut down — a sign that expanding abroad has proven far harder than expected. Whether Marvel Rivals can turn this around is the biggest variable.

**Third, NetEase is essentially absent from the 53.5-billion-yuan mini-game market**, a fast-growing segment monopolized by Tencent in 2025.

**Growing profit is a fact, but the sustainability of that growth depends on the answers to the three questions above.**

## Understanding NetEase requires answering four questions

If we had to sum up NetEase's current situation in one sentence:

> **An extremely profitable company standing at a growth crossroads — to the left, comfortable-zone slow growth; to the right, accelerated growth driven by overseas expansion and new titles.**

Understanding this company requires answering at least four questions:
