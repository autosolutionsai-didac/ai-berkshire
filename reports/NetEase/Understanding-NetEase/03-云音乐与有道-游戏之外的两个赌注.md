# Cloud Music and Youdao — Two Bets Beyond Gaming

> "Understanding NetEase" series · Article 03 · Non-core businesses
> Reading time: approx. 8 minutes

---

## A fact that often gets overlooked

Most people discussing NetEase focus entirely on gaming. That's understandable — the 92.1 billion yuan in gaming revenue accounts for 82% of the total, while Cloud Music and Youdao combined bring in only 13.7 billion yuan, and their profit contribution is smaller still.

But skipping straight past these two businesses means missing two important developments:

**First, Cloud Music has just crossed the profitability inflection point.** Going from years of losses to earning 1.6 billion yuan a year is not a change in degree — it's a change in kind. A internet platform that has proven it can turn a profit is a fundamentally different animal from one still burning cash.

**Second, Youdao is switching lanes.** It is moving from "online education" to "AI education" — AI subscription revenue has reached 400 million yuan and is growing at over 50%. In the still-nascent AI education track, Youdao is one of the few players already generating revenue.

The core question this article answers is: **are Cloud Music and Youdao merely icing on the cake, or a second growth engine?**

---

## I. NetEase Cloud Music: the literary youth who finally crossed into profitability

### Fundamentals

| Metric | 2025 figure | Note |
|------|-----------|------|
| Revenue | 7.76 billion yuan | NetEase's second-largest business |
| Operating profit | 1.62 billion yuan (+38.5%) | Stable profitability achieved |
| Operating margin | ~21% | Solid profit quality |
| Monthly active users (MAU) | ~150 million (estimate) | Third in the industry (behind QQ Music and Kugou) |
| Independent musicians | 1 million+ | The platform's biggest differentiating asset |

### What the profitability inflection point means

The history of NetEase Cloud Music is, in a sense, the story of a "literary youth" growing up and starting to make money.

In its early days, Cloud Music was known for its "sentimentality" — users left moving comments in the comment section, and independent musicians found their first listeners on the platform. But sentimentality doesn't pay the bills: high licensing costs, weak paid-conversion rates, and low operating efficiency kept the business in the red for years.

The turning point came in 2024-2025: more disciplined content licensing, an optimized membership system, and tighter control of operating expenses turned the margin from negative to positive. In 2025, operating profit reached 1.62 billion yuan, up 38.5% year over year.

**1.62 billion yuan is only 4% of NetEase's total profit of 37.3 billion yuan — not a large number. But what matters isn't today's absolute figure, it's the trend.** For a platform that has just proven it can be profitable, with margins improving rapidly, the slope of that curve carries more information than the absolute level.

### 1 million independent musicians: the most underrated moat

NetEase Cloud Music has amassed more than 1 million independent musicians — a figure that puts it well ahead of any other Chinese online music platform.

Why do independent musicians matter?

**First, licensing costs are extremely low.** The rights to independent musicians' works cost far less than works from the three major record labels (Universal, Sony, Warner). The more users stay on the platform because of independent music, the less the platform depends on "expensive licensed content."

**Second, they create differentiated content.** Songs on QQ Music are also on Qishui Music. But works that debut on NetEase Cloud Music from its 1 million independent musicians may be unavailable elsewhere — or available but without the comment-section culture that surrounds them here.

**Third, community stickiness.** Cloud Music's comment sections, curated playlists, and personal FM radio exist on other platforms too, but Cloud Music users clearly engage more deeply and feel a stronger emotional connection to these features. The web of interaction formed between 1 million independent musicians and users is difficult for other platforms to replicate.

**Counterpoint**: the commercial value of independent musicians may be limited. Of the 1 million musicians, likely fewer than 1% generate meaningful revenue. Most users' core need remains listening to mainstream music — which every platform offers. "Comment-section culture" is a nice-to-have, but it is hard to convert into profit.

### The Qishui Music threat

The biggest external pressure facing NetEase Cloud Music is the same one facing Tencent Music — ByteDance's Qishui Music (literally "Soda Music").

| Metric | NetEase Cloud Music | Qishui Music |
|------|----------|---------|
| MAU | ~150 million (estimate) | 156 million |
| Monthly card price | CNY 12-15 | CNY 8 |
| Core users | Literary youth, those born in the 1990s | Younger users, lower-tier cities |
| Content advantage | Independent musicians, comment culture | Douyin hit songs, short-video crossover |

**In March 2026, Qishui Music's MAU overtook NetEase Cloud Music's.** Falling from the industry's second position to third is not good news for Cloud Music.

But the overlap between Cloud Music's and Qishui Music's user bases may not be as large as it appears. Cloud Music's core users skew literary, favor independent music, and listen more deeply; Qishui Music's users lean more toward Douyin hit songs and lighter consumption. What the two are really competing for is the "middle ground" — users who listen to mainstream music but occasionally to independent artists too.

**Reasonable expectation**: Cloud Music's MAU is likely to stabilize between 120 and 150 million — unlikely to grow much further, but also unlikely to collapse. The priority isn't chasing MAU growth but continuing to raise the paid-conversion rate and revenue per user — a "small but refined" strategy.

## II. NetEase Youdao: an early contender in AI education

### Fundamentals

| Metric | 2025 figure | Note |
|------|-----------|------|
| Revenue | 5.9 billion yuan (+5%) | Growth is modest but the business is mid-transition |
| AI subscription revenue | 400 million yuan (growth over 50%) | The most noteworthy growth driver |
| Core products | Youdao Dictionary, Youdao Translate, Youdao Premium Courses | Spanning tools, learning, and AI |
| Large language model | The "Ziyue" LLM | A vertical model for the education domain |
| Profitability | Near breakeven (estimate) | Stable profitability not yet achieved |

### AI subscriptions: the real questions behind the 400-million-yuan figure

400 million yuan in AI subscription revenue, growing over 50% — the numbers look good on the surface. But a few questions deserve sober scrutiny.

**First, 400 million yuan is only 7% of total revenue of 5.9 billion yuan.** AI subscriptions are still a "young sprout" within Youdao's business, far from having grown into a mature tree.

**Second, how long can 50% growth be sustained?** AI education subscriptions are an entirely new category — how much users are willing to pay, and for how long, for "AI English tutoring," "AI homework help," or "AI translation" has no mature benchmark yet. Fast growth may simply reflect a low base.

**Third, the competitive landscape is entirely uncertain.** The AI education track isn't just Youdao's to run — iFlytek, TAL Education, and various large-model companies (Baidu's Ernie, Alibaba's Tongyi, ByteDance's Doubao) are all in the mix. Youdao's advantage lies in the combination of "education DNA plus AI capability" — but if general-purpose large language models become good enough at education, specialized education AI tools risk being marginalized.

### The Ziyue LLM: a possible differentiator

Youdao's Ziyue large language model focuses specifically on education scenarios — a "vertical" strategy.

The advantages are clear: education scenarios demand extremely high accuracy (a math problem can't be solved incorrectly), the ability to adapt to different students' levels (teaching to the individual), and familiarity with China's education and examination systems — areas where general-purpose large models tend to fall short.

But the disadvantages are equally clear: developing a vertical large model is not cheap, and its commercial ceiling may not be high. Ultimate willingness to pay for AI education depends on parents and students — and while Chinese parents' willingness to pay for education is generally high, willingness to pay specifically for "AI tools" remains unproven.

**Reasonable expectation**: Youdao has a first-mover advantage in AI education, and AI subscription revenue could grow to 1.0-1.5 billion yuan over the next three years. But for Youdao as a whole to achieve stable profitability and contribute meaningful profit may take more than five years.

**Counterpoint**: AI education could turn out to be a winner-take-all track — whoever has the best model and the most data captures most of the market. Youdao's Ziyue model may not maintain a durable technical lead over the general-purpose model giants. And with Youdao's revenue base of 5.9 billion yuan, against a backdrop of ever more capital-intensive AI investment, it may not be able to sustain high-intensity R&D spending indefinitely.

## III. What the two businesses have in common

Looking at Cloud Music and Youdao together reveals an interesting shared trait: **neither is large in scale, but each has a distinctive moat.**

| Dimension | NetEase Cloud Music | Youdao |
|------|----------|------|
| Revenue scale | 7.76 billion yuan | 5.9 billion yuan |
| Share of group revenue | 6.9% | 5.2% |
| Moat | 1 million independent musicians + community culture | Education DNA + Ziyue LLM + user base |
| Profitability status | Already profitable and improving fast | Near breakeven |
| Biggest risk | Qishui Music encroachment | Uncertain AI competitive landscape |
| Growth logic | Raising profit rather than scale | AI subscriptions scaling from small to large |

**The strategic value of these two businesses to the NetEase group lies not in how much profit they contribute today, but in reducing the company's single-point dependence on gaming.**

Gaming currently accounts for 82% of revenue; if Cloud Music's profit keeps growing and Youdao's AI business takes off, gaming's share could fall below 75% within three to five years. That wouldn't change NetEase's fundamental identity as a "gaming-first" company, but it would improve the company's degree of risk diversification.

## IV. Innovation and Other Businesses: strategic retrenchment

Beyond gaming, Cloud Music, and Youdao, NetEase also runs an "Innovation and Others" segment — including NetEase Mail, NetEase News, NetEase Yanxuan, and similar businesses.

This segment generated roughly 6.8 billion yuan in revenue in 2025, but it is in a phase of **strategic retrenchment** — the annualized growth rate is projected at -5% to -10%.

William Ding's strategy is unambiguous: **cut what isn't profitable, shrink what lacks competitiveness, and concentrate resources on gaming and AI.** This is rational capital allocation — rather than burning resources competing with e-commerce giants and content platforms in Yanxuan and news, it makes more sense to redirect money and people toward higher-margin areas.

## V. Summary: how good are these two bets?

To summarize this article's analysis in a few points:

1. **NetEase Cloud Music has crossed the profitability inflection point** — 1.62 billion yuan in operating profit, 38.5% growth, and a 21% margin all confirm this business can make money
2. **1 million independent musicians is the most underrated moat** — though the ability to monetize it still needs to be proven
3. **Qishui Music's MAU has already overtaken Cloud Music's** — but the overlap between their user bases may be smaller than expected, and a "small but refined" strategy is the more realistic path for Cloud Music
4. **Youdao's AI subscriptions are the most imaginative seed** — 400 million yuan in revenue, growth above 50%, but the base is small and the competitive landscape uncertain
5. **The two businesses combined generate 13.7 billion yuan in revenue and about 2 billion yuan in profit** — a "meaningful supplement" to the group, not a "decisive force"

**In one sentence: Cloud Music is a small vault that has already proven it can turn a profit, while Youdao is a seed that still needs watering. Neither is enough to change NetEase's fundamental identity as a gaming company, but together they make the company's fundamentals somewhat more robust.**
