## Important Notes

1. **Analyst coverage vs. quantitative models**: Morningstar has **direct analyst coverage** for most US- and Hong Kong-listed companies (high data quality); for A-shares/STAR Market names it mainly relies on a **quantitative rating model** (algorithm-generated, which may carry significant bias)

## III. Key Findings

### 1. Morningstar's March 2026 Moat Downgrades for Software Companies

In March 2026, Morningstar downgraded the moat rating of six software companies from "wide" to "narrow," on the grounds that AI (particularly agentic AI) could disrupt the traditional per-seat SaaS pricing model:

| Company | Moat Change | Fair Value Change | Current Discount |
|------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Adobe | Wide → Narrow | $560 → $380 (-32%) | -35% |
| Salesforce | Wide → Narrow | $300 → $280 (-7%) | -36% |
| ServiceNow | Wide → Narrow | $200 → $165 (-18%) | -38% |

**Notably, even after these substantial fair-value cuts, all three companies still trade significantly below their new fair values.** The market's sell-off has far outpaced Morningstar's downward revisions.

**Microsoft is the only software company to retain a "wide moat"** — Morningstar believes Microsoft stands to benefit regardless of how AI develops.

### 2. Hong Kong Internet Stocks Are Severely Undervalued

The fair values Morningstar analysts assign to Hong Kong-listed internet giants show that **almost all of these companies have upside of more than 50%**:
- Meituan +118%
- Alibaba +95%
- Xiaomi +86%
- Tencent +74%

This reflects an overall "China discount" in Hong Kong equities — companies of comparable quality are valued far lower in Hong Kong than in the US.

### 3. Three Companies Morningstar Considers Overvalued

| Company | Reason |
|------|------|
| **Micron (MU)** | 1-star rating; the $716 share price exceeds the $455 fair value by 57%. No moat, highly cyclical memory business |
| **China Mobile (0941.HK)** | Share price already exceeds Morningstar's fair value by 29%. Government objectives may not align with minority shareholder interests |
| **China Telecom (0728.HK)** | 1-star rating, no moat. Morningstar applies a substantial governance discount to H-shares |

### 4. Baidu's Moat Downgraded to "None"

In March 2026, Morningstar downgraded Baidu's moat directly from "wide" to "none" (skipping "narrow"), citing continued erosion of its search business by competitors such as Douyin. This is the most severe downgrade among all companies covered.
