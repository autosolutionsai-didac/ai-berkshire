## 0. Information-richness rating and AI research limitations

**AI researchability rating: Grade A (data-rich)**.

Shenhuo Co. has been listed for many years, and its annual reports, quarterly reports, exchange filings, market data, financial data feeds, industry policy materials, and comparable-company data are relatively abundant. Historical financials, revenue structure, capacity, and dividends can be cross-validated at the factual level with relatively high confidence.

However, abundant data does not equal high investment certainty. The company's core profit comes from the electrolytic-aluminum price spread, which is fundamentally driven by aluminum prices, alumina prices, electricity, coal, carbon costs, hydropower inflow, power curtailment, and policy constraints. The most common mistakes to avoid are:

1. Mechanically annualizing the high profit of Q1 2026;
2. Mistaking a "low P/E" for "low risk";
3. Mistaking "partial coal-power-aluminum integration" for "full integration";
4. Mistaking a low-cost cyclical asset for a compounding "great business" that can be held forever without further thought.

## 1. One-line conclusion

**Shenhuo Co. is an electrolytic-aluminum cyclical stock with a relatively favorable cost position, but it is not a top-tier "great business" in Duan Yongping's sense. The company enjoys cost advantages from its Xinjiang coal-power-aluminum operations and its Yunnan green-power aluminum operations, and its cash flow and dividends are currently solid; however, the current price of RMB 22.99 does not offer a sufficient margin of safety relative to neutral value. It is better suited as a cyclical watch-list candidate — worth a serious re-evaluation below RMB 18 or during a pessimistic phase of the cycle.**

## 4. Summary analysis by dimension

### 4.1 Business model and moat

1. **The nature of the business**: Shenhuo is neither a pure coal stock nor a fully integrated aluminum leader, but rather a cyclical resource stock combining "low-cost electrolytic-aluminum profit core + coal resource base + aluminum-foil processing extension."
2. **Core moat**: Not brand, customer stickiness, or network effects, but the scarcity of compliant electrolytic-aluminum capacity, the cost advantage of its Xinjiang coal-power-aluminum operations, and the low-carbon/location advantage of its Yunnan green-power aluminum operations.
3. **Integration needs a discount**: The Xinjiang base is relatively well integrated, but the Yunnan base depends on the grid and hydropower inflow, and alumina is still largely purchased externally; the company is not a fully closed loop from bauxite to alumina to electrolytic aluminum.
4. **Coal is not currently a strong moat**: 2025 coal gross margin was only 7.61%, and coal-mine asset impairments have been substantial — coal is more of a resource option and a historical base than a current strength.
5. **Duan Yongping's conclusion**: This is not a top-tier great business where "users can't do without it and it can keep raising prices"; it is better suited as a candidate good asset when the price is cheap enough and the cycle is well-positioned.

### 4.2 Financials and valuation

1. **Clear balance-sheet repair**: The debt-to-asset ratio fell from 73.13% in 2021 to 42.19% in 2025, and interest-bearing debt fell from approx. RMB 26.3 billion to approx. RMB 9.0 billion.
2. **Strong cash-flow quality**: 2025 operating cash flow was RMB 8.743 billion, and simplified free cash flow was approx. RMB 7.882 billion, comfortably covering dividends and buybacks.
3. **Profit is highly cyclical**: Net profit attributable to parent was RMB 7.578 billion in 2022, falling to RMB 4.005 billion in 2025; a cyclical peak should not be used to estimate perpetual value.
4. **Valuation is not expensive but not cheap enough**: 2025 static P/E is approx. 12.70x and P/B approx. 2.1x; for a resource-cyclical stock, this lacks a sufficiently deep discount.
5. **Three-scenario valuation**: Using 2025 EPS of RMB 1.81 as the base, the five-year-forward optimistic target is approx. RMB 27.3, the neutral target approx. RMB 18.1, and the pessimistic target approx. RMB 9.8. The current price is above the neutral value.

### 4.3 Industry and competition

1. **The supply side of the electrolytic-aluminum industry is relatively favorable**: China's electrolytic-aluminum sector is constrained by capacity-swap rules, energy-intensity controls, dual-carbon policy, and an approx. 45-million-tonne capacity ceiling, which raises the value of existing compliant capacity.
2. **Demand is not purely a growth story**: New-energy vehicles, solar, the grid, and packaging offset the downturn in real estate, but overall this remains a cyclical material, not a high-growth sector.
3. **The cost curve is what determines the winners**: Electricity and alumina are the key drivers of per-tonne aluminum cost. Shenhuo is strong on electricity, but weaker on alumina and full-chain integration.
4. **Positioning relative to comparable companies**: Hongqiao and Chalco are stronger in scale and full-chain integration; Yunnan Aluminium is stronger in green-power purity; Xinjiang Tianshan (Tianshan Aluminium) is stronger in alumina self-sufficiency; Nanshan Aluminium is stronger in high-end processing; Shenhuo is a second-tier, high-elasticity, low-cost name.
5. **Aluminum foil should not be over-rated**: The barriers to entry in aluminum foil/battery foil come from customer qualification, yield, processing fees, and capacity utilization — not simply from upstream primary-aluminum cost.

### 4.4 Risk and management

1. **State-owned governance is stable but warrants a discount**: The controlling shareholder is the Shangqiu Municipal State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, which provides relative stability; but the boundaries of related-party transactions, local industrial obligations, and minority-shareholder interests need ongoing tracking.
2. **Cash returns are fairly generous**: In 2025, the company distributed RMB 8 per 10 shares, a cash dividend of approx. RMB 1.787 billion, and conducted buybacks of approx. RMB 255 million for equity incentives.
3. **Related-party transaction rectification is a warning sign**: The annual report discloses that, per rectification requirements from an on-site inspection by the Henan Securities Regulatory Bureau, certain related-party transactions with the Shenhuo Group were terminated; this is not a disqualifying red flag, but it should be tracked as a governance line to watch.
4. **Core risk variables**: A decline in aluminum prices, a rebound in alumina prices, power curtailment/rising electricity prices in Yunnan, carbon costs on Xinjiang coal-power, continued coal-mine impairments, and safety/environmental incidents.
5. **Ten-year certainty is limited**: Low-cost aluminum capacity may hold long-term value, but carbon costs, recycled aluminum, real-estate demand, hydropower inflow, and policy will continue to reshape the profit pool.

## 5. Investment thesis: Bull vs. Bear

### 🟢 Bull case

1. **Supply-side discipline in electrolytic aluminum remains fairly strong**: The capacity ceiling and swap system constrain new supply, raising the value of existing compliant capacity.
2. **Shenhuo sits toward the left side of the cost curve**: The Xinjiang coal-power-aluminum operations and Yunnan green-power aluminum operations together give it a per-tonne cost advantage.
3. **The balance sheet has improved markedly**: Continuous deleveraging over the past several years has left financial risk far below 2021 levels.
4. **Cash flow and dividends are solid**: 2025 FCF of approx. RMB 7.882 billion comfortably covers the RMB 1.787 billion cash dividend.
5. **Q1 2026 shows profit elasticity**: Net profit attributable to parent of RMB 2.290 billion, up sharply YoY, indicates strong elasticity when aluminum prices/costs improve.
6. **Shareholder-return awareness stands out among cyclical stocks**: Dividends, buybacks, and equity incentives all reflect a degree of capital discipline.
7. **Low-carbon aluminum offers a long-term structural opportunity**: Yunnan green-power aluminum may capture incremental value from carbon-footprint considerations and customers' low-carbon procurement.

### 🔴 Bear case

1. **No active pricing power**: Electrolytic-aluminum and coal prices are largely set by the market — the company earns a price spread, not a brand premium.
2. **Q1 2026 should not be mechanically annualized**: The quarter's profit came from a high-prosperity window of rising aluminum prices and falling alumina prices, and cannot be treated as perpetual profit.
3. **Reliance on purchased alumina is a clear shortcoming**: The company is not fully integrated, and once alumina prices rebound, per-tonne aluminum profit will compress quickly.
4. **The Yunnan base is exposed to hydropower inflow and electricity-price risk**: The green-power aluminum advantage comes with the risk of dry-season curtailment, rising electricity prices, and output volatility.
5. **Xinjiang coal-power-aluminum carries carbon-cost risk**: The low-cost advantage stems from coal power, which is exposed long-term to dual-carbon policy, CBAM, green-power certification, and ESG-related discounting.
6. **The coal business is a drag and carries impairment risk**: 2025 coal gross margin was low, and multiple coal-mine impairments have exposed historical asset-quality issues.
7. **Insufficient margin of safety at the current price**: RMB 22.99 is above the neutral valuation of around RMB 18, leaving an uncomfortable risk-reward ratio.

## 9. Key open questions

1. **Q1 2026 profit bridge**: The contributions of aluminum prices, alumina prices, electricity prices, coal, hedging, and minority interests to profit need to be decomposed.
2. **Per-base cost curves**: More granular data is needed on electricity prices, alumina purchase prices, anode costs, logistics costs, and the equity-interest basis at the Xinjiang and Yunnan bases respectively.
3. **The tail end of coal-mine impairments**: The remaining book value, resumption-of-production assumptions, and future impairment potential at mines such as Hecheng, Damoling, Liuhe, and Xuehu need to be tracked.
4. **True ROIC of the aluminum-foil business**: Capacity utilization, customer qualification, yield, processing fees, and receivables turnover still need to be verified.
5. **Closing the loop on related-party-transaction rectification**: Ongoing tracking is needed to confirm whether transactions with the Shenhuo Group and related parties continue to decline and are priced on market terms.

---

## 10. Summary

Shenhuo Co.'s appeal lies in the fact that it is not an ordinary highly leveraged resource stock, but rather an electrolytic-aluminum cyclical name with a markedly repaired balance sheet, strong cash flow, generous dividends, and cost advantages from its Xinjiang coal-power-aluminum and Yunnan green-power aluminum operations. But its core weaknesses are equally clear: it has no active pricing power, it relies on purchased alumina, its Yunnan base is exposed to hydropower inflow and electricity-price risk, its Xinjiang base is constrained by carbon costs, and its coal assets may continue to be a drag. The current price of RMB 22.99 is not absurdly overvalued, but it lacks a margin of safety relative to the neutral value of around RMB 18. The most sensible strategy is to place it on a high-priority watch list and wait for a pessimistic phase of the cycle, a price pullback, or clearer evidence of sustained per-tonne aluminum profit before deciding whether to buy.
