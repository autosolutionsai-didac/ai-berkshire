## Series positioning

Gpixel is not a "hot" company — most investors have never heard the name. But it is the **only Chinese player that can compete with the global top three** in high-end industrial/scientific CMOS image sensors. It surged 75% on its first day of trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in April 2026, pushing its market cap to HKD 30.4 billion.

- The bull case says: global No. 3, China's only player, 67% gross margin, domestic substitution just getting started
- The bear case says: HKD 30 billion valuation for HKD 300 million in annual profit, a pond worth only $3.3 billion, and its manufacturing lifeline in someone else's hands

**Both sides have a point.** That is exactly why this company deserves serious study.

What this series aims to do is **first clearly establish "what this company is," and then let you judge for yourself whether it's worth buying** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Writing principles for this series

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **Unreliable data is better deleted than kept** — as a newly listed IPO, Gpixel has limited disclosed information, and we do not force false precision to fill gaps
4. **No "probability-weighted expected return" calculations** — assigning scenario weights is purely subjective and gives readers a false sense of precision
5. **This does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article
