# "Understanding Ping An Group" Series Overview

> 4 articles that peel back China's largest integrated financial conglomerate layer by layer to reveal its true structure.
>
> The series takes about 60 minutes to read; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series positioning

Ping An Group is one of the most contentious names in China's capital markets —

- The bulls say: PEV of **0.65x**, NBV growth of 30% for two consecutive years, a dividend yield of **4.8%**, 14 consecutive years of dividend growth — absurdly undervalued
- The bears say: the long-term decline in interest rates will inevitably cause a negative interest spread, the 54 billion yuan China Fortune Land Development blowup still hasn't been fully digested, and the succession question around the 68-year-old Ma Mingzhe remains unresolved

**Both camps are reading the same annual report and reaching completely opposite conclusions.**

This divergence is no accident. Ping An's complexity lies in the fact that it is simultaneously an insurer (subject to the interest-rate cycle), a bank (subject to the asset-quality cycle), a technology company (subject to losses during the investment phase), and also runs a healthcare and senior-care business. These four lines are interwoven, and no simple label can capture the whole.

What this series sets out to do is **first take the structure apart clearly, then let you judge for yourself whether it's worth buying** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Series writing principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then work through the logic, and only then reach a conclusion** — with no bullish or bearish stance decided in advance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it for themselves
3. **Unreliable data is deleted rather than kept** — insurance-company financials are extremely complex; estimated figures are labeled "estimate," and actuarial assumptions are labeled with their source
4. **No precise target price is given** — the three-scenario valuation only marks out a range, with no false precision
5. **This does not constitute investment advice** — every article ends with a disclaimer
