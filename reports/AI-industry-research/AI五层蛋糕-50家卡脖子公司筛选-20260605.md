# AI Five-Layer Cake: 50 bottleneck companies screened

> Screening date: June 5, 2026
> Screening criteria: bottleneck purity × irreplaceability × degree of market pricing × catalyst time window (each ★1-5)
> Goal: find bottleneck, overlooked investment candidates

---

## Screening criteria

| Dimension | Notes | Ideal state |
|------|------|----------|
| Bottleneck purity | Bottleneck business as share of revenue | >30%, higher is better |
| Irreplaceability | Sole/oligopoly supplier | 2+ years before an alternative emerges |
| Degree of market pricing | How much the stock price reflects the bottleneck thesis | Higher score = more undervalued |
| Catalyst time window | Near-term confirming events | Catalyst within 6 months |

## Bottom line

**The best AI investment opportunities aren't in the most popular companies — they're in the supply chain, in the hidden bottlenecks that are "doing the same thing but priced at half the valuation" or "sitting on a trillion-dollar supply chain but worth only a few billion dollars."**
