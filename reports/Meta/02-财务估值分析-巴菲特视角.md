# Meta Platforms Financial and Valuation Analysis

> Analytical lens: financial quality and intrinsic value (Buffett framework)
> Data as of: May 2026 | Based on Q1 2026 earnings

---

## I. Revenue and Profit Trends: A Powerful Money Printer

### Revenue

Meta's Q1 2026 revenue was $56.31 billion, up 33% year over year (29% on a constant-currency basis). Revenue CAGR over the past three years has reached 20%, which is exceptionally rare for a company with a trillion-dollar market cap.

Breaking down the growth drivers:
- **Ad impressions**: up 19% year over year, driven by increased user time spent and higher Reels ad load
- **Ad pricing**: up 12% year over year, as AI-driven precision targeting improved advertiser ROI and supported price increases
- **Q2 guidance**: $58-61 billion, implying full-year revenue could exceed $230 billion

Meta's full-year advertising revenue is expected to surpass Google's for the first time in 2026, reaching an estimated $243.46 billion, or 26.8% of the global digital advertising market. This is a historic inflection point.

### Profit

Q1 net profit was $26.8 billion, up 61% year over year, but that figure includes an $8.03 billion one-time tax benefit that needs to be excluded. Adjusted earnings per share were $7.31 (reported basis: $10.44).

Key profitability metrics:
| Metric | Q1 2026 | Note |
|------|---------|------|
| Operating profit | $22.9 billion | Operating margin of 41% |
| Net margin | 33.1% (adjusted ~26%) | Still top-tier even after excluding the tax benefit |
| ROIC | 35% | Return on invested capital far exceeds cost of capital |

## II. Capital Expenditure: An Unprecedented Bet on AI

The 2026 capex guidance has been raised from $115-135 billion to **$125-145 billion**, a figure that deserves careful scrutiny.

### Capex evolution
| Year | Capex | Note |
|------|----------|------|
| 2023 | ~$28 billion | Baseline level following the "year of efficiency" |
| 2024 | ~$39 billion | AI investment starts accelerating |
| 2025 | ~$72.2 billion | Large-scale buildout of AI infrastructure |
| 2026E | $125-145 billion | Historical peak, up roughly 80% year over year |

Spending is directed at power, land, and GPU compute, primarily to support large-scale AI model training and inference. Meta is simultaneously advancing its in-house MTIA chip to reduce long-term dependence on NVIDIA.

### The key question: where is the return?

Buffett's core standard for evaluating capital allocation is: **can every dollar of retained earnings create more than a dollar of market value?**

Supporting evidence:
- Q1 revenue growth of 33% already significantly outpaces cost growth, showing AI investment is producing returns
- The Advantage+ tool now processes more than $60 billion in annualized ad spend, directly driving revenue growth
- Management has committed that 2026 operating profit will exceed 2025's absolute level

Risk factors:
- 2026 expense growth will "significantly outpace" 2025's rate
- Zuckerberg's answer on ROI evidence for the $145 billion in AI spending was vague: "that's a very technical question"
- If the AI race escalates further, capex could climb even higher

## III. Free Cash Flow and Shareholder Returns

Meta's free-cash-flow generation is one of its greatest financial strengths:
- 2026 free cash flow is projected at roughly $114.2 billion (squeezed by capex, but outpaced by faster revenue growth)
- EV/FCF ratio of approximately 31.8x
- Continued share buybacks have reduced shares outstanding by about 1.45% over the past year

Even while investing heavily in AI, Meta continues to deliver strong cash returns. This is unusual among large historical tech companies — high capex is typically accompanied by a sharp decline in cash flow, but Meta's advertising growth is strong enough to cover the investment.

## IV. Valuation Analysis

### Current valuation level
| Metric | Value | Peer comparison |
|------|------|----------|
| Market cap | $1,593 billion | — |
| Trailing P/E | 22.2x | Peer average 29.1x |
| Forward P/E | 18.4x | Significantly below peers |
| P/E change since Q4 2025 | 27.5x → 20.5x | Valuation has already compressed noticeably |
| GF Value estimate | $787 | Current share price $601, a 23.6% discount |

### Valuation judgment

At a forward P/E of 18.4x, the implied earnings growth expectation is roughly 20%. Given Meta's three-year revenue CAGR of 20%, Q1 growth of 33%, and the large untapped monetization potential in Reels and WhatsApp, **the current valuation is not expensive — arguably even cheap**.

The contrast with peers is even sharper:
- Google (Alphabet): forward P/E of roughly 20x, but revenue growth of only 12%
- ByteDance, TikTok's parent: not publicly listed, but its implied valuation growth is slowing
- Snap: still struggling around breakeven

**Meta is offering significantly higher growth at a lower valuation than its peers.** This combination of "growth plus value" is very rare among large-cap tech stocks.
