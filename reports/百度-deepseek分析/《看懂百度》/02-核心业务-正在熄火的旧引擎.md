# Search Advertising: The Stalling Old Engine

> "Understanding Baidu" Series · Essay 02
> Reading time: about 10 minutes

---

## Baidu's Core Cash Cow: Online Marketing

**63 billion yuan in revenue, declining for 6 consecutive quarters, down 14% in 2025. This is the current state of Baidu's most important cash cow.**

Baidu's money-making logic is extremely simple: a user searches → Baidu shows an ad → the advertiser pays per click (CPC) → Baidu earns money. This model has supported Baidu's profits for the past 20 years, but it is visibly heading toward its end.

In 2025, online marketing (search advertising) still contributed **~62%** of Baidu Core's revenue (about 63 billion yuan). Look at the data:

| Year | Online Marketing Revenue (billion yuan) | YoY | Share of Core Revenue |
|------|-------------------|------|-----------|
| 2023 | ~75.0 | +8% | ~75% |
| 2024 | 73.0 | -3% | ~70% |
| 2025 | ~63.0 | **-14%** | ~62% |
| 2026Q1 | 16.0 | **-6%** | ~63% |

> Data sources: Baidu annual report, Xinhua, Wall Street CN.

**Six consecutive quarters of decline, with 2025 as a full year seeing accelerated deterioration (-14%).** In 2026Q1 the decline narrowed to -6%, but it's not yet clear whether this reflects a genuine trend improvement or seasonal fluctuation.

## Search Market Share: From 87% to 56%

Baidu search's decline didn't begin with AI — AI merely accelerated a process already underway.

According to Statista data:

| Period | Baidu Search Market Share |
|------|-------------|
| November 2021 | **86.82%** |
| December 2023 | ~65% |
| May 2024 | **55.85%** |
| 2025 (mobile) | ~65% (still strong on mobile) |
| 2025 (PC) | **~30%** (has lost PC dominance) |

> Data sources: Statista, Shoushai.com.

**31 percentage points of market share evaporated in 3 years.** This is not cyclical fluctuation — it is a structural migration.

Where did users go?

| Competitor | Search-Related Metric | Threat to Baidu |
|--------|------------|------------|
| Douyin | Average daily search volume of **5.3 billion** (mobile search share ~70%) | **Most lethal** — has taken over the "quick search" use case |
| Xiaohongshu | Average daily search volume of **600 million** (1/3 of its 300 million MAU search from the home feed) | Has taken over "guides / purchase-decision" searches |
| WeChat Search | MAU of **800 million+** (integrated with Yuanbao AI) | Has taken over "in-ecosystem search" |
| DeepSeek | MAU of **127 million** | Has taken over "deep Q&A" use cases |
| Doubao (ByteDance) | MAU of **345 million** | Has taken over the "AI assistant" use case |

> Data sources: QuestMobile (March 2026), TMTPost, Zhihu.

**Counter-argument**: "Baidu Search still has 679 million MAU and still holds a 65% share on mobile — it's nowhere near 'dead.' Search is an essential need that won't disappear, it has just become fragmented."

**Rebuttal**: "The frequency and duration of user searches on Baidu are both declining. Market share may be 65%, but daily search volume is only 300 million — Douyin alone has **17 times** Baidu's search volume. The habit of 'open Baidu and search it' is being replaced by 'open Douyin and search it,' especially among younger users."

## Advertising Monetization Efficiency: Volume and Price Both Falling

Search advertising revenue = search volume × ad load rate × cost-per-click (CPC).

Baidu's problem isn't just declining search volume — **three variables are deteriorating simultaneously**:

**1. Declining Search Volume**

Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and WeChat Search have diverted a huge amount of search demand; Baidu's average daily search volume has fallen from a peak of **6 billion+** to roughly **300 million** (sources: TMTPost, Zhihu).

**2. Ad Load Rate Under Pressure**

AI search (such as Ernie Bot integrated into Baidu Search) delivers more precise, more direct results — users get their answer without needing to click a link. This means **fewer ad-display opportunities**. Baidu's own AI search is, ironically, compressing its own ad inventory.

**3. Declining CPC**

Advertiser budgets are migrating toward Douyin and Xiaohongshu. In Q2 2025, Baidu's online marketing revenue fell **15%** YoY — the largest decline among all major advertising platforms.

**This is a concrete manifestation of "left hand fighting right hand": the better AI search performs, the more the traditional advertising model suffers.** This isn't a problem unique to Baidu — Google faces the same dilemma — but Baidu's substitute revenue streams (AI Cloud, subscriptions) are far less diversified than Google's.

## The Baidu App Ecosystem: The Real Value of 679 Million MAU

Baidu App's MAU of **679 million** looks large — trailing only WeChat and Douyin, putting it in the third tier of Chinese internet platforms.

But "MAU" and "user value" are two different things:

| Platform | MAU | Annual Revenue per User (est.) | User Stickiness |
|------|------|------------------|---------|
| WeChat | 1.3 billion+ | ~200 yuan | Extremely high (social lock-in) |
| Douyin | 750 million+ | ~400 yuan | Extremely high (content addiction) |
| Baidu App | 679 million | **~93 yuan** (63 billion / 679 million) | **Low-to-medium** (search-and-leave) |

> Note: Baidu's annual revenue per user is a rough estimate derived from online marketing revenue divided by MAU.

**Baidu App's problem isn't a lack of users — it's that users "search and leave"** — short dwell time, weak content consumption, and near-zero e-commerce conversion. Of its 679 million MAU, only **4.79 million** (0.7%) use Ernie Bot — meaning Baidu can't even convert its own search users into AI users.

**Counter-argument**: "A 0.7% conversion rate actually implies enormous upside. If Baidu can make the search experience fully AI-native (rather than launching a separate standalone app), the conversion potential of 679 million users is far greater than any competitor's."

**Rebuttal**: "The problem is that Baidu App's user profile skews toward 'search utility users' — they come to search, not to 'use AI.' Doubao's 345 million users, by contrast, are users actively seeking AI conversation — the intent is entirely different. There's a product-capability chasm between 'having users' and 'being able to convert them.'"

## How Much Moat Is Left?

Search advertising's traditional moat has three layers:

| Moat Layer | Status | Assessment |
|-----------|------|------|
| User habit ("got a question, Baidu it") | **Severely eroded** | Younger users have already shifted to searching on Douyin/Xiaohongshu |
| Advertiser budget inertia | **Moderately eroded** | Large clients are cutting Baidu budgets, but small and mid-size merchants still rely on it |
| Search technology barrier | **Partially intact** | AI search capability remains strong (Ernie 5.1's search leads domestically), but the technical barrier is falling |

**Overall assessment: Baidu Search's moat has gone from "very deep" to "shallow" — not gone, but no longer enough to stop revenue from continuing to decline.**

There's a key timing issue here: **search advertising is declining 14% a year while the new AI business is growing 48% a year — but the AI business's base (40 billion yuan) is only 63% the size of search advertising's (63 billion yuan). At the current pace, AI revenue won't fully make up for search advertising's losses until 2027-2028.**

The 1-2 years in between are Baidu's most dangerous "gap" window.

## Essay Summary

| Dimension | Conclusion |
|------|------|
| Search advertising revenue | Declined for 6 consecutive quarters, -14% in 2025; structural rather than cyclical |
| Search market share | Fell from 87% to 56%; still 65% on mobile, but daily search volume far below Douyin's |
| Advertising monetization efficiency | Volume and price both falling; AI search is compressing ad inventory |
| User value | 679 million MAU but only ~93 yuan annual revenue per user; low stickiness |
| Moat | Fallen from "very deep" to "shallow"; small/mid-size merchant inertia is the last line of defense |

**In one sentence: Baidu's old engine hasn't "stalled," but it's decelerating at 14% a year. The question isn't whether it stops — it's whether the new engine can get up to speed before it does.**
