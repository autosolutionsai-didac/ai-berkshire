## 1. Special Fact-Check: "Li Lu Knew Nothing About Moutai" — **A Misattribution**

### 1.1 Three Layers of Misattribution

| Layer | What actually happened | Version that circulated |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Pabrai said "I (myself) know nothing about Moutai"** | Became "Duan Yongping said" |
| 2 | **Duan Yongping replied "He doesn't understand Moutai at all"** | Became "Li Lu" |
| 3 | **Duan Yongping did not name anyone** | Became naming "Li Lu" |

### 1.2 Pabrai's Original Words (from the video Duan Yongping reposted)

> "A few years later, Li Lu called me and said: 'I'm selling my Moutai shares, and I think you should sell yours too.' I said: 'But God told me never to sell it.' ... He said: 'I think you should sell it and move on to invest in something else.' I thought about it and said: **'I know nothing about Moutai, I don't even drink, Li Lu knows everything.'** So I said: 'I've already made five or six times my money, I think I should listen to him.'"

**Note**: Pabrai was saying that **he himself** knew nothing about Moutai, and said **"Li Lu knows everything"** — the meaning is exactly the opposite!

### 1.3 Duan Yongping's original Xueqiu post from 2023-07-22

**Post link**: xueqiu.com/1247347556/256327549

**The full three layers of context**:
1. Outer layer: Duan Yongping reposted Pabrai's video (originally posted by @Honghuangli-ant)
2. User @Donghaizhifeng commented: "He also has a view that Moutai and Tencent have gotten too large in scale, and he's a bit worried about the law of large numbers. You could say **he doesn't understand Moutai the way Duan Yongping does**."
3. Duan Yongping replied: "**He doesn't understand Moutai at all, always going on about how trees don't grow to the sky**"

**Key judgment**:
- "He" very likely implicitly refers to Li Lu (since the discussion was about Li Lu's 2019 exit from Moutai)
- **But Duan Yongping did not name anyone**
- **The wording was "doesn't understand," not "knew nothing about"**
- "Trees don't grow to the sky" is a common phrase in the investment world (originating from a German proverb), also used by Buffett and Munger

### 1.4 Duan Yongping's **other** documented public comments about Li Lu

| Date | Original quote | Target |
|------|------|------|
| Undated/scattered | "Doesn't understand the downsides of leverage" | Commenting on Li Lu's use of leverage |
| Undated/scattered | "Buffett investing in BYD was just doing Munger a favor" | Indirect (implying Munger was influenced by Li Lu on BYD) |
| 2023-07-22 | "He doesn't understand Moutai at all" | Unnamed, very likely referring to Li Lu |
| Any time | **"Li Lu knew nothing about Moutai"** | **No evidence found** |

### 1.5 Li Lu has never responded
Li Lu never speaks on Xueqiu, Weibo, or in Chinese-language online communities. He communicates only through lectures at Peking University/Columbia and works such as *Civilization, Modernization, Value Investing, and China*. There is **no record of any public direct interaction** between the two men.

### 1.6 Methodological Lesson (Important)
- **"Quotes attributed to influencers"** must be traced back to the original post (with timestamp and link) before being used as evidence
- Pronouns in Xueqiu comment threads depend on context, and get easily distorted once separated from the original thread
- **It is better to say "the original source could not be found, treat with caution"** than to repeat a secondhand quotation

## 2. Comparison of Investment Styles

### 2.1 Commonalities (Four Major Ones)
| Commonality | Duan Yongping | Li Lu |
|--------|-------|------|
| Value-investing belief | Regards Buffett as mentor | Direct disciple of Munger |
| Long-term holding | 10+ years, "the best investments never need to be sold" | Held BYD for 20 years, GOOG for 5+ years |
| Concentrated holdings | Top 3 positions account for 80%+ | Top 3 positions account for 74.58% (GOOGL + BAC + PDD) |
| Circle-of-competence principle | "Only do what you understand" | "Fish where the fish are" |

### 2.2 Six Major Differences

#### Difference One: Path and Background
- **Duan Yongping**: Industry → investing (1989-2001 at Subor/BBK, retired in 2001) → "the soul of a businessman, the talent of a businessman"
- **Li Lu**: Academia/activism → finance (Columbia JD/MBA/MA, three degrees) → "the soul of a scholar, the talent of a businessman"

#### Difference Two: The Weight Given to Price
- **Duan Yongping**: More of a Fisher-style investor — "**it's fine if a good company is a bit expensive; don't touch a bad company no matter how cheap it is**." His purchases of Apple and Moutai were not at absolute lows.
- **Li Lu**: More of a Munger-Graham hybrid — **his 2019 exit from Moutai perfectly avoided the 2021-2023 decline**, showing a clearly higher sensitivity to valuation than Duan Yongping.

#### Difference Three: Extreme Concentration
- **Duan Yongping**: Apple accounts for 80%+ of his U.S. equity holdings (including indirect exposure to Apple via Berkshire)
- **Li Lu**: As of Q4 2025, the top 5 holdings account for 87.22% (across 9 stocks); **he doesn't dare put 80% into one stock as Duan does**, because of fiduciary duties to LPs

#### Difference Four: Turnover
- **Duan Yongping**: Nearly "buy and never sell" (NetEase for 25 years, Apple for 15 years, Moutai held 12 years without exiting)
- **Li Lu**: Both buys and sells (**cashed out HK$18 billion+ from trimming BYD in 2021-2022**, cut Apple by 58% in Q3 2024, exited PDD entirely in 2021 then rebuilt the position at 10x the size in Q2 2025)

#### Difference Five: Communication Style
- **Duan Yongping**: Posts as @Dadaowuxingwoyouxing on Xueqiu, with **9,491 posts** and 4 million followers — "teaching online"
- **Li Lu**: Low-profile, communicating only through lectures at Peking University/Columbia and a small number of works (*Civilization, Modernization, Value Investing, and China*), aimed at a professional audience

#### Difference Six: Attitude Toward "Selling"
- **Duan Yongping**: "Try not to sell good companies"
- **Li Lu**: **Three principles for selling** (fundamentals deteriorate / value is fully reflected / a better alternative exists) — highly disciplined
