### Finding One: All Six Companies Are Now Fully Priced

Not a single company currently offers a Buffett-standard margin of safety. This is not an isolated phenomenon but a systemic feature of overheated valuations across the entire AI infrastructure track.

### Finding Two: NVIDIA Is the "Kingmaker" of the Optical Communications Track

- NVIDIA has invested $2.0 billion in Coherent, signed a $2.0 billion strategic partnership with Lumentum, and signed a $3.2 billion long-term agreement with Corning
- **AAOI, which has not received NVIDIA strategic investment**, is clearly a tier below on competitiveness and certainty
- In the optical communications track, "inside NVIDIA's supply chain" versus "outside NVIDIA's supply chain" is the core dividing line

### Finding Three: Management Is Broadly Selling Into Strength

| Company | Management Selling Signal |
|------|-------------|
| GLW | CEO cashed out $250 million, executives collectively sold >$10 million |
| AAOI | 22 sales, 0 purchases over 6 months, totaling >$25 million |
| MU | CEO sold ~$21.5 million |
| WDC | CEO sold $8.2 million |

This is a signal worth heeding — when management across an entire industry is cashing out at highs, investors should stay level-headed.

### Finding Four: Chinese Competitors Are a Long-Term Structural Threat

- **Optical communications**: Innolight holds >40% share of 800G, with a 10-20% cost advantage
- **Storage**: ChangXin Memory produces 290,000 DRAM wafers per month, YMTC holds 13% NAND share
- Constrained in the short term by geopolitics, but will continue to erode the low end of the market over the medium term

## V. Investment Decision Matrix

### If Forced to Pick Just One

**Top choice: Micron Technology (MU)**
- Rationale: Stable position as one of storage's three oligopolists, HBM technology leadership, lowest forward PE, strongest balance sheet
- But must accept: extreme cyclicality of the storage industry, and the possibility that we are currently at a cycle peak
- Strategy: wait for a pullback into the $400-500 range before building a position

### If Choosing a Track ETF Rather Than a Single Stock

All four companies in the optical communications track (GLW/LITE/COHR/AAOI) are in valuation-bubble territory. If bullish on the AI optical communications track but uncertain which company ultimately wins, consider:
- Waiting for an overall pullback of 30-50% before diversifying across names
- Focusing on GLW (largest, most stable) and LITE (strongest technology)

### Clear Avoid

**AAOI**: Never profitable, management selling across the board, shorted by two short-selling firms, PS far above peers — meets none of the standards of value investing.

## VIII. Summary

These six companies span the "connectivity" (optical communications) and "memory" (storage) pillars of AI infrastructure, and all sit in the strong tailwind of the AI super-cycle. However, the market has already fully — in most cases excessively — priced in these positive factors.

**Core tension**: the certainty of AI-infrastructure demand versus the absence of a valuation margin of safety.

From a value-investing framework, no single company at the current juncture warrants an immediate full position. But several of these six companies (MU, GLW, LITE, COHR) are genuinely excellent businesses that deserve a place on a "Tier-1 watch list," awaiting a patient buying opportunity after a valuation pullback.

> Duan Yongping: "Don't do what you don't understand; margin of safety; circle of competence."
> Buffett: "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."
> Munger: "All intelligent investing is value investing."
> Li Lu: "Good investment opportunities often appear when everyone is fearful."

Market enthusiasm for AI infrastructure is currently at a peak. Genuine value-investing opportunities may not appear until the next market panic.
