But it operates in a track riddled with structural contradictions: scale is destined to be constrained (strategy-capacity bottleneck), alpha is destined to decay (more participants + improving market efficiency), and regulation is destined to tighten.

Ubiquant's three research labs need to convert their AI investment into verifiable performance advantages as soon as possible, or the criticism that "AI gains have been meager" will only grow louder.

Investing in Ubiquant is fundamentally not a bet that "it will get bigger" (scale is the enemy of performance), but a bet that "it can outlast peers by one more cycle, and earn for one more cycle, as the industry's tailwinds fade." Given the currently available information (the 2021 financing valuation may have contained a bubble, the alpha-decay trend is clear, and exit paths are narrow), the recommendation is to **wait and watch for the valuation to revert to a fair range**.
