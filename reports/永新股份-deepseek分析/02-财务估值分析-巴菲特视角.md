## 1. Revenue and Net Profit Trend Analysis

### 5-Year Revenue Trend (data source: Baostock/Yongxin Co. annual reports)

| Year | Revenue (billion yuan) | YoY growth | Net profit (billion yuan) | YoY growth | Net margin |
|------|-----------|---------|-------------|---------|-------|
| 2021 | 3.024 | +10.45% | 0.316 | +4.25% | 10.62% |
| 2022 | 3.304 | +9.27% | 0.363 | +14.85% | 11.16% |
| 2023 | 3.379 | +2.26% | 0.408 | +12.46% | 12.26% |
| 2024 | 3.525 | +4.34% | 0.468 | +14.63% | 13.37% |
| 2025 | 3.723 | **+5.61%** | 0.438 | **-6.33%** | **11.87%** |
| Q1 2026 | 0.940 | +10.81% | 0.091 | +2.06% | **9.78%** |

### Trend Analysis Conclusion

**Revenue growth: ★★★☆☆**
- 5-year revenue CAGR is about 5.32% — growth is moderate but a ceiling has emerged
- Growth was lowest in 2023 (2.26%), rebounding slightly to 5.61% in 2025
- Demand in the packaging industry is stable, but lacks explosive growth

**Net profit quality: ★★★☆☆ (trend turning negative)**
- Net profit growth hit a recent high of 14.63% in 2024, but declined **6.33% for the first time** in 2025
- Net profit excluding non-recurring items was 0.409 billion yuan (-7.17%), a steeper decline than headline net profit — non-recurring gains and losses had a cosmetic effect
- Net margin fell further to 9.78% in Q1 2026, a three-year low

**Buffett's focus:** "When profit declines while revenue grows, it is a classic sign that a company has lost its pricing power."

## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Profitability Metrics

### ROE Trend

| Year | ROE | Equity multiplier | Net margin | Asset turnover (implied) |
|------|-----|---------|-------|-----------------|
| 2021 | 15.90% | 1.39 | 10.62% | ~1.08 |
| 2022 | 17.01% | 1.54 | 11.16% | ~0.99 |
| 2023 | 17.72% | 1.56 | 12.26% | ~0.93 |
| 2024 | **19.18%** | 1.64 | 13.37% | ~0.87 |
| 2025 | **17.34%** | **1.71** | 11.87% | ~0.85 |

**ROE analysis (the metric Buffett values most):**
- ROE has exceeded 15% for five consecutive years, which looks excellent
- But it eased to 17.34% in 2025, and the earlier improvement **relied on leverage** (equity multiplier rose from 1.39 to 1.71)
- Asset turnover has declined steadily from 1.08 to 0.85 — operating efficiency is deteriorating
- DuPont analysis shows: ROE has been sustained mainly by net margin, but net margin declined in 2025

### Gross Margin and Net Margin

| Year | Gross margin | Net margin | Period expense ratio (implied) |
|------|--------|-------|-----------------|
| 2021 | 22.24% | 10.62% | ~11.6% |
| 2022 | 22.20% | 11.16% | ~11.0% |
| 2023 | **24.66%** | 12.26% | ~10.1% |
| 2024 | 23.78% | **13.37%** | ~10.5% |
| 2025 | 23.00% | 11.87% | ~11.2% |

**Gross margin has fluctuated in a 22-25% range**, reflecting a business that is sensitive to raw material prices. According to broker research: synthetic resins account for 58.53% of procurement cost, and every 10% rise in raw material prices lowers gross margin by about 7.9 percentage points. The company is a **price-taker** with no pricing power.

**Profitability composite rating: ★★★☆☆ (negative trend)**

## 6. Margin of Safety Assessment

### Three-Scenario Valuation

| Scenario | Assumption | Target price | Upside/downside | Probability |
|------|------|--------|----------|------|
| Optimistic | 8% growth, 20x PE | ~19.2 yuan | +68% | 15% |
| Neutral | 4% growth, 15x PE | ~12.5 yuan | +10% | 50% |
| **Conservative** | **0% growth, 10x PE** | **~7.2 yuan** | **-37%** | **35%** |

### Margin of Safety Assessment

- In the neutral scenario, the target price of 12.5 yuan versus the current 11.4 yuan offers only **10% upside**
- The conservative scenario implies 37% downside — an unfavorable risk-reward ratio
- **Risk-adjusted expected return is negative:** (68%×15%) + (10%×50%) + (-37%×35%) = 10.2% - 12.95% = **-2.75%**

**Margin of safety rating: ★★☆☆☆**
