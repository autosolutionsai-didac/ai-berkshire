# Ant Group + Ant Digital Technologies: What Will They Be Worth in 5 Years?

**Research date: May 15, 2026 | Four-agent parallel research**

---

## One-line conclusion

> Ant Group's probability-weighted valuation in 5 years (2031) is approximately RMB 1 trillion (currently about RMB 750 billion), an annualized return of about 6.5% — not a high-growth name, but "China's version of Visa plus an AI option." Ant Digital Technologies' weighted valuation is about RMB 72 billion (currently ~RMB 40 billion), an annualized return of about 10% — a small track but fast growth, with RWA and AI as the upside variables. **The biggest catalyst is Ant International's Hong Kong IPO (2026-2027); the biggest risk is the new Huabei regulations plus continued erosion of market share by WeChat Pay.**

### Three major uncertainties

1. **The share of related-party transactions is an information black box** — how much of Ant Digital Technologies' revenue comes from within Ant Group? Estimated at 30-50%, and it must be brought down to an acceptable level before any IPO.
2. **The RWA business model is unproven** — tokenizing RMB 60 billion of new-energy assets on-chain is a milestone, but the fee model (transaction fees / service fees / licensing) is unclear.
3. **Ownership of the Bailing large model is ambiguous** — Ant Digital Technologies' enterprise AI business depends on the group's Bailing model, raising doubts about its independence.

## Conclusion: how should we view this?

### Ant Group

**Positioning: a mature cash cow + a globalization option + AI uncertainty.**

At a current valuation of about US$88.0 billion, the probability-weighted valuation in 5 years is about US$143.0 billion (RMB 1.03 trillion), an annualized return of about 6.5%. Neither cheap nor expensive. The biggest upside catalyst is a successful Ant International IPO (which could lift the overall valuation by 15-35%); the biggest downside risk is a continued decline in payments share plus the stalled financial holding license.

If you are an Ant Group employee holding stock options: the options have value, but don't expect to get rich quick — an annualized 6-8% is a reasonable expectation.

### Ant Digital Technologies

**Positioning: a small but elegant enterprise-facing fintech business + an RWA option.**

At a current valuation of about RMB 40.0 billion, the weighted valuation in 5 years is about RMB 72.0 billion, an annualized return of about 10%. Faster growth than Ant Group as a whole, but with lower certainty. RWA and AI are two variables that could lift the ceiling, but the track is relatively small (BaaS ~RMB 1.9 billion / privacy computing ~RMB 1.0 billion), and dependence on related-party transactions is a hard flaw for any IPO.

If you are an Ant Digital Technologies employee holding stock options: the liquidity timeline is much longer than for Ant International (not before 2028), but if RWA scales up, the valuation upside is larger.

### The relationship between the two

Ant Digital Technologies is the vehicle through which Ant Group's technology capabilities spill over. In the short term (1-2 years) it still needs the parent company's financial support and brand endorsement; in the medium term (3-5 years), as the share of external revenue rises and profitability is achieved, it should gain the ability to survive independently. But "independence" and "going public" are two different things — an IPO requires bringing related-party transactions down to an acceptable level, which will take at least another 2-3 years.
