Jiang Fan's original remarks (Q4 earnings call):
> "Compared with the same period last year before the large-scale investment began in March, order volume on Taobao Flash Sale from January through March this year was 2.7 times that of the same period last year, with the non-food retail portion at 3 times last year's level."

### 3.2 Market Share

**By average daily order volume:**

| Source | Time | Meituan | Alibaba ecosystem | JD |
|------|------|------|--------|------|
| Hongcan Industry Research Institute | Q3 2025 (peak) | 46.9% | **42.8%** | ~10% |
| J.P. Morgan | November 2025 | 50% | **42%** | 8% |

**By GTV (gross transaction value):**

Meituan still holds roughly **60%+** GTV share, because its average order value is significantly higher than competitors' (about 1.5x). Orders above 15 yuan account for over two-thirds of GTV, and orders above 30 yuan account for about 70%.

**Key distinction:** By order count, Alibaba has nearly caught up to Meituan; by GTV, Meituan's lead remains clear. The reason is that Taobao Flash Sale's early growth was driven mainly by low-ticket beverage orders.

**Share comparison before the food-delivery war (end of 2024):** Meituan at roughly 65-70%, Ele.me at roughly 30-33%. Over the course of the year-long battle, Alibaba lifted its order-volume share from about 30% to about 42%.

**Evolution of per-order losses:**

|------|----------------|------|

| November 2025 | About 2 yuan | LatePost data |

Jiang Fan's original remarks (Q2 earnings call, Nov 2025):

> "Since April, while maintaining order volume, we have significantly improved unit economics by raising logistics fulfillment efficiency and optimizing order mix — particularly through a further increase in average order value."

By 2026, some merchants' commission rates had already been raised to 7.4%, and the commission portion for merchants using self-delivery had reached about 20%.

### 3.6 Subsidy Intensity and Its Retreat

**Subsidy scale:**
- Taobao Flash Sale announced a 50-billion-yuan subsidy program (mid-2025)
- The three platforms together spent 80-100 billion yuan in subsidies in Q2-Q3 2025
- Subsidy forms: large red-envelope coupons, limited-time free orders, official flat pricing, merchant commission waivers

**Regulatory crackdown:**
- August 1, 2025: the three platforms issued a joint statement on "regulating promotions"
- March 25, 2026: the Economic Daily published "The Food-Delivery War Should End," reposted by the State Administration for Market Regulation's official website
- April 2026: regulators summoned the platforms; large subsidies contracted noticeably
- April 17, 2026: the State Administration for Market Regulation issued the largest-ever fine in the food-safety domain (3.597 billion yuan)

**Adjustments after the subsidy retreat:**
- Commission rate raised from 6.4% to 7.4%
- Merchants' share of red-envelope costs increased (of a 9-yuan coupon, merchants now cover 8 yuan, the platform only 1 yuan)
- Non-beverage orders now exceed 75% of the total, and average order value (AOV) has kept rising sequentially

## IV. Management's Profitability Roadmap

Jiang Fan laid out a clear three-step plan on the Q4 earnings call:

| Time point | Target |
|---------|------|
| **End of FY2027** (March 2027) | Unit economics (UE) turn positive |
| **FY2028** | Instant retail GMV surpasses **1 trillion yuan** |
| **FY2029** | Instant retail business achieves **overall profitability** |

Jiang Fan's original remarks (Q4 earnings call):
> "We are confident of achieving positive unit economics by the end of fiscal 2027."
> "We remain committed to the goal of instant retail's overall transaction volume surpassing 1 trillion yuan in fiscal 2028."
> "Building on that scale, we expect the instant retail business segment to achieve overall profitability in fiscal 2029."

CFO Toby Xu (Q2 earnings call, Nov 2025):
> "This quarter marks the peak of investment in Taobao Flash Sale."

## V. Competitive Landscape

### Meituan Food Delivery

- Full-year 2025 revenue of 364.9 billion yuan (+8%), full-year net loss of 23.4 billion yuan (swung from profit to loss)
- Core local commerce operating loss of 6.9 billion yuan
- Maintains 60%+ GTV share, with a decisive advantage in the mid-to-high average-order-value full-meal market
- Goldman Sachs cut its long-term per-order profit estimate for Meituan from 1.5 yuan to 0.8 yuan

### JD Food Delivery

- Entered the market in February 2025, serving over 240 million users
- Share of about 7-8% (per J.P. Morgan); JD itself claims as high as 31% (during the 618 festival, though the methodology is questionable)
- New business segment full-year loss of 46.6 billion yuan
- Differentiation: full-time riders receive full social insurance and housing fund contributions (150,000 full-time riders), and Qixian Xiaochu operates in-house restaurants

### Douyin Food Delivery

- Has effectively exited the food-delivery competition
- In July 2025, officially stated it has "no plans to build its own food-delivery operation"
- Focused on in-store business (about 40% share vs. Meituan's 60%)
- "Douyin Instant Delivery" serves only select branded chains and does not participate in the delivery-speed arms race

## VI. AI and Food Delivery Integration

On May 11, 2026, the Qwen app was fully integrated with Taobao, letting users place food-delivery orders, shop, and pay directly through conversation within Qwen, without switching apps. This also covers in-car voice ordering, Qwen AI glasses ordering, and other multi-device scenarios.

Eddie Wu (Q4 earnings call):
> "We are at a critical inflection point in the evolution from conversational chatbots to autonomous AI agents."

## VII. Strict Separation of Fact and Opinion

### Confirmed facts (source: earnings report / earnings call / official disclosure)

1. FY2026 instant retail revenue of 78.52 billion yuan, up 47% year-over-year
2. Q4 instant retail revenue of 19.988 billion yuan, up 57% year-over-year
3. Q4 order volume 2.7x year-over-year, non-food portion 3x year-over-year
4. Ele.me has been rebranded as Taobao Flash Sale
5. Management has committed to positive UE by end of FY2027, 1-trillion-yuan GMV by FY2028, and overall profitability by FY2029
6. CFO explicitly stated Q2 was the investment peak
7. 88VIP membership of 62 million
8. Full-year capital expenditure of 126.063 billion yuan

### Investment bank estimates (not officially confirmed; watch for estimation bias)

1. Taobao Flash Sale's full-year FY2026 loss of about 86 billion yuan (Morgan Stanley)
2. Per-order loss falling from 4.7 yuan to about 1.5 yuan (cross-referenced across CICC, Goldman Sachs, LatePost, and other sources)
3. Order-volume share of about 42% (J.P. Morgan, November 2025)
4. Meituan's GTV share still above 60% (Goldman Sachs)

### Key uncertainties

1. **Can the post-subsidy-retreat share be held?** After regulators halted predatory subsidies, the retention rate of users acquired via low prices is unknown
2. **Will the UE-turnaround timeline hold?** Management has committed to end of FY2027, but this depends on how much commission rates can rise and how much delivery efficiency can improve
3. **Is the 1-trillion-yuan GMV target achievable?** FY2026 instant retail revenue was 78.5 billion yuan; GMV is larger in scale, but reaching 1 trillion still requires sustained high growth
4. **Will JD's food-delivery push be sustained?** Whether Richard Liu can keep investing after a 46.6-billion-yuan loss is the biggest variable
5. **How long can Alibaba absorb the losses?** It holds 520.8 billion yuan in cash, but free cash flow has already turned negative at -46.6 billion yuan; if AI and instant retail burn cash simultaneously, the pace of cash consumption bears watching
