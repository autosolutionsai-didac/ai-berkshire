## Alibaba Cloud Decides Whether Alibaba Is a "Value Stock" or a "Growth Stock"

The answer to this question lies with Alibaba Cloud.

The previous installment's conclusion: Taotian's share has fallen 14 percentage points over four years, and the flywheel has shifted from "self-reinforcing" to "propped up by external force." An Alibaba with only Taotian would be a slowly declining value stock — richly profitable but past peak growth, and ultimately destined to be discounted.

But Alibaba has another asset whose numbers stand out like those of a different company: FY2026 revenue growth of 34%, AI product revenue with 11 consecutive quarters of triple-digit growth, and an AI cloud market share of 35.8% that exceeds the combined share of the 2nd through 4th ranked players. This asset is called **Alibaba Cloud** — the **only segment in the group achieving both high-speed growth and expanding profitability simultaneously** — and it is the direction on which management has staked a 380-billion-yuan bet.

Whether Alibaba Cloud can pick up the baton from Taotian will decide whether Alibaba spends the next decade on a "Microsoft-style rebirth" path or an "IBM-style stagnation" path.

## II. How Is AI Driving Alibaba Cloud's Growth?

### 2.1 AI Revenue Has Become the Core Growth Engine

Revenue from Alibaba Cloud's AI-related products has now posted **11 consecutive quarters of triple-digit growth**.

FY2026 Q4 single-quarter AI product revenue was 8.971 billion yuan, an annualized 35.8 billion yuan — a figure that was close to zero just a year earlier. AI products crossed 30% of Alibaba Cloud's external revenue for the first time, meaning **1 out of every 3 yuan of cloud revenue now comes from AI**.

The growth is driven mainly by:
- **An explosion in AI inference demand**: once large-model applications go into production, inference compute demand runs tens of times higher than training demand
- **Qwen's open-source ecosystem driving cloud consumption**: Qwen has been downloaded over 600 million times globally, with 170,000+ derivative models — developers debugging and deploying these models on Alibaba Cloud generates compute consumption
- **Accelerating enterprise AI adoption**: the Bailian platform serves over 980,000 enterprises and developers

### 2.2 Qwen's Competitiveness

**Strengths:**
- Globally leading open-source ecosystem: ranked No. 1 on Hugging Face by download volume in Q1 2026, surpassing Meta's Llama
- The Qwen3 series comprises 8 open-source models covering the full range of sizes and modalities
- Advantages in enterprise/B2B deployment (leveraging Alibaba Cloud's customer base)

**Weaknesses:**
- A clear gap in consumer-facing MAU: the Qwen app has about 166 million MAU (per QuestMobile) versus Doubao's **345 million** MAU — a gap of more than 2x
- Average monthly usage frequency: Qwen at **19.8 times** versus Doubao at **54.8 times** — a 2.7x gap in user stickiness
- After the Spring Festival's "3-billion-yuan free-order" cash-burning marketing campaign, retention fell below 50% — users bought with subsidies are not real users

*Data sources: QuestMobile December 2025, ai-berkshire Qwen deep-dive report*

**Alibaba's strategy in AI is "open source for traffic, monetize on the cloud"** — the model is free to use, but the inference compute must be purchased through Alibaba Cloud. This logic mirrors AWS's playbook: you build applications with my tools, and the more successful your application, the more compute you buy.

**On the other hand**, ByteDance's Doubao advantage at the consumer application layer may transmit down to the cloud layer. In 2025, in the large-model API call market, ByteDance's Volcano Engine held a 46.4% share, with Alibaba Cloud in second place. If "more Doubao users → more developers using Volcano Engine → Volcano Engine's compute share rises" plays out, that would be the cloud-computing version of the "Douyin disrupts Taobao" pattern.

## III. The 380-Billion-Yuan AI Investment — Foresight or Bubble?

### 3.1 Scale of Investment

Eddie Wu committed: "Over the next three years (FY2026-FY2028), our investment in cloud and AI infrastructure will exceed the total of the past decade." The market estimates this figure at roughly **380 billion yuan**.

Eddie Wu has also hinted that "380 billion is a conservative figure."

Full-year FY2026 capital expenditure already reached **126.1 billion yuan** — burning through 126.1 billion yuan in a single year alone.

### 3.2 Is the Money Well Spent?

**The bull case (positive arguments):**

This is "offensive capital expenditure," similar to AWS's path during 2005-2015. AWS also burned cash for a solid decade back then, compressing profits to extremely low levels, in exchange for the 30%+ margins and trillion-dollar market-cap contribution that followed.

Supporting data:
- Alibaba Cloud's AI revenue is already beginning to materialize at an accelerating pace (annualized 35.8 billion yuan, 11 consecutive quarters of triple-digit growth)
- China's AI inference demand is genuinely exploding — token call volume is growing tens of times over, year after year
- Eddie Wu's 5-year target: cloud + AI commercialized annual revenue exceeding **$100 billion** (about 710 billion yuan)

**The bear case (negative arguments):**

- Joe Tsai himself has said "there is a bubble in AI data centers" — when a company's chairman issues a public warning like this, investors should not ignore it
- Alibaba Cloud's margin is only 9%, versus AWS's 36% — a 4x gap. If Alibaba Cloud never catches up to AWS's margin level, the 380-billion-yuan investment is simply piling up scale at a low return
- China's AI industry is in the middle of a price war — DeepSeek built a top-3 model using one-tenth the compute, proving that "stacking compute" is not necessarily a moat
- ByteDance's Volcano Engine, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud are all doubling down — a price war could compress everyone's margins

### 3.3 Key Verification Milestones

| Time | What needs to be seen |
|------|-----------|
| FY2027 (before March 2027) | Alibaba Cloud's external revenue growth holds at 30%+, AI revenue share exceeds 40% |
| FY2028 | Alibaba Cloud's annual revenue surpasses 250 billion yuan, EBITA margin rises to 12-15% |
| FY2030 | Whether there are signs that Eddie Wu's $100 billion target can be half-achieved ($50 billion) |

If these milestones are met one by one, Alibaba Cloud will become the Alibaba group's largest profit engine, and the 380-billion-yuan investment will have been worthwhile. If AI revenue growth falls below 15% and margin gets stuck at 12%, this money becomes a sunk cost.

## IV. Alibaba Cloud's Moat: Three Layers of Defense

### 4.1 First Layer — Scale and Customer Lock-in

- China IaaS market share of 26.8%, No. 1 for multiple consecutive years
- Extremely high data-migration costs for government and enterprise customers — once deployed on Alibaba Cloud, migrating to Huawei Cloud or Tencent Cloud can take months or even years
- Compliance advantage on state-owned sensitive data: Alibaba Cloud has deep accumulated experience in financial cloud and government cloud

### 4.2 Second Layer — AI Model Ecosystem

- Qwen open-source models downloaded 600 million times globally, with 170,000 derivative models
- The Bailian platform's 980,000 enterprises/developers form a closed loop of "model draws traffic → monetized on the cloud"
- The only cloud vendor in China that simultaneously owns "foundation large models + IaaS + downstream application entry points (Taobao/DingTalk/Amap/Alipay)"

### 4.3 Third Layer — Irreplaceability in the China Market

- US-China decoupling means foreign cloud providers cannot serve China's government and enterprise clients
- AWS China plus Azure China together hold only a 5-7% share, and are subject to geopolitical restrictions
- Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and CTYun together carve up most of the government/enterprise cloud market

**These three layers of defense make Alibaba Cloud the segment across all of Alibaba's businesses with the deepest — and still widening — moat.**

### 4.4 Threats to the Moat

But the moat is not without cracks:

- **ByteDance's Volcano Engine**: leveraging the Doubao large model's advantage at the AI application layer, it is pushing from consumer into enterprise. Its large-model API call share of 46.4% has already surpassed Alibaba Cloud. Volcano Engine's share was only 5-6% in 2024, and surged to 8-12% in 2025 — a remarkable growth rate
- **Huawei Cloud**: leveraging domestic-tech-substitution policy and government relationships, it has already surpassed Alibaba Cloud in hybrid cloud / government cloud
- **Carrier clouds** (China Telecom's CTYun, China Mobile, China Unicom): combined share exceeding 30%, rising fast on the strength of their infrastructure advantages

**Ten-year neutral forecast:**
- Alibaba Cloud's China share: 33-36% → **28-32%** (a modest decline but still No. 1)
- ByteDance Volcano Engine: 8-12% → **18-22%**
- Huawei Cloud: 18-21% → **22-26%**

The landscape will shift from "Alibaba Cloud dominance" to "a three-way split plus state-owned clouds."

## V. Is Eddie Wu's "China's AWS" Narrative Credible?

Eddie Wu's strategic vision is to turn Alibaba into "China's Amazon" — e-commerce as the cash cow, cloud as the growth engine and valuation anchor.

### 5.1 The Microsoft Success Path

The closest historical analogy is **Microsoft's 2014-2024 transformation**:

| Dimension | Microsoft | Alibaba |
|------|------|------|
| Leadership change | Nadella took over in 2014 | Eddie Wu took over in 2023 |
| Strategy | Mobile First → Cloud First | E-commerce empire → AI cloud + consumer |
| Businesses cut | Abandoned phones, shrank consumer business | Sold Intime, Sun Art Retail |
| Legacy-business cash flow | Office 365 brings in over $100 billion a year | Taotian's annual profit is ~200 billion yuan |
| Transformation outcome | Market cap rose from $300 billion to $3 trillion | ? |

### 5.2 The IBM Failure Path

But there are also failure cases — **IBM 2010-2024**:

| Dimension | IBM | Alibaba's risk |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Strategic direction | Bet on Watson AI + cloud | Bet on Qwen + Alibaba Cloud |
| Problem | Legacy business (mainframes) declined too fast; new business (Watson) execution was poor | Taotian's share keeps eroding; Qwen's consumer product is weaker than Doubao |
| Result | Revenue flat for a decade, market cap chronically underperformed the broader market | ? |

**The fork between the two paths is: can the legacy business's cash flow last until the new business turns profitable.**

Microsoft succeeded because Office 365's cash flow was extremely stable, buying Azure a 10-year transformation window. IBM failed because mainframe revenue declined too fast to sustain Watson's R&D spending.

For Alibaba: Taotian's annual profit is 200 billion yuan, and Alibaba Cloud's EBITA margin is slowly improving (9% → target of 18-20%). If Taotian can hold onto 30%+ share and sustain profit in the hundreds-of-billions range, it can give Alibaba Cloud a 5-8-year transformation window. If Taotian's share accelerates its decline below 25%, the cash cow falls first while Alibaba Cloud has not yet grown up — that is the IBM script.

### 5.3 A Neutral Judgment

**Probability that Alibaba Cloud becomes "China's AWS": about 40-50%.**

Optimistic path: EBITA margin rises to 18-20% within 5 years, annual revenue surpasses 400 billion yuan, and it becomes the group's largest profit engine.
Pessimistic path: a price war compresses margins, share is eroded by Volcano Engine, margin gets stuck at 12%, and it becomes "a decent but unremarkable business."

## VI. Core Conclusion: Alibaba Cloud's Ten-Year Profile

| Dimension | Current | Ten years from now (neutral forecast) |
|------|------|----------------|
| Annual revenue | 158.1 billion yuan | **400-730 billion yuan** |
| EBITA margin | ~9% | **18-20%** |
| Operating profit | ~14 billion yuan | **80-140 billion yuan** |
| Position in the group | Second-largest revenue source, thin margin | **A profit engine on par with Taotian** |
| Moat | Widening | China's AI cloud leader, global top 4 |

*Forecast sources: synthesis of Goldman Sachs, ai-berkshire's 10-year profit projection report*

By Duan Yongping's standard: **Alibaba Cloud is the closest thing to a "good business" among all of Alibaba's operations** — it has user stickiness, potential pricing power, economies of scale, and compounding effects.

But the premise of "good business" is that the ROIC inflection point arrives before 2030. If the 380-billion-yuan investment fails to yield a substantive improvement in margin, "the seed of a good business" will turn into "a capital black hole."

Goldman Sachs, in its sum-of-the-parts valuation, values Alibaba Cloud alone at about **$54 per share** — implying that this one segment alone is worth about 40% of Alibaba's current market cap. Whether the market accepts this valuation depends on whether AI revenue growth can be sustained over the coming quarters.
