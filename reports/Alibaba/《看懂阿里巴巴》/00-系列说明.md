# "Understanding Alibaba" Series Overview

> 5 main articles, peeling Alibaba apart layer by layer to reveal its real structure.
>
> The series takes about 75 minutes to read in total; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series Positioning

Alibaba is arguably the most discussed — and possibly the most deeply misunderstood — company in the history of the Chinese internet.

- The bulls say: revenue crossed one trillion, net cash of 389.0 billion, annual buybacks of $11.9 billion, Alibaba Cloud AI growth of 40% — this is a transformative giant severely underestimated by the market
- The bears say: e-commerce share down 14 percentage points over four years, Non-GAAP net profit growing essentially zero, free cash flow down 53%, fighting three wars at once — this is an empire in decline

**Both camps are looking at the same company, citing the same earnings report.** The difference is whether you view Alibaba as a whole, or break it apart and look at the pieces separately.

What this series does is **first break each piece apart clearly, then let you judge for yourself what the whole is worth** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Series Writing Principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — letting readers weigh it for themselves
3. **Unreliable data is deleted rather than used** — Alibaba's business is extremely complex, and different accounting bases can differ enormously; this series uses only the most credible sources
4. **No "probability-weighted expected returns"** — scenario weightings are purely subjective and give readers a false sense of precision
5. **This does not constitute investment advice** — a disclaimer appears at the end of every article

## Comparison with "Understanding Pinduoduo" and "Understanding Tencent"

| Dimension | Tencent | Pinduoduo | Alibaba |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| Asset quality | WeChat is irreplaceable + evergreen gaming | Extreme financial efficiency + an algorithmic advertising machine | An e-commerce cash cow + a cloud/AI option |
| Valuation | ~18x PE, reasonable | ~10.5x PE, extremely cheap | ~17x PE, 47% below its historical average |
| Management transparency | High | Low | Medium (Joe Tsai has publicly admitted mistakes, but business complexity is high) |
| Risk structure | Regulatory tail risk | Temu's political gamble + a trust discount | Fighting on three fronts + an AI capex gamble |
| **Core contradiction** | High growth certainty but valuation not extremely cheap | Extremely cheap but management is not transparent | **The most profitable business is weakening, while the most promising business hasn't grown up yet** |

If you read the Tencent series and felt "strong certainty," and read the Pinduoduo series and felt "cheap but hard to see clearly," then the keyword for Alibaba is — **transformation**. What you need to judge is: is this transformation worth waiting for?
