# Taotao E-Commerce — A Cash Cow Under Siege

> *Understanding Alibaba* series · Episode 02
> Reading time: about 15 minutes

---

## Contributing 90% of Profit, With a Margin to Rival Moutai — But Under Attack on Four Fronts

An e-commerce platform with a 44% EBITA margin — in the same league as Moutai.

That is today's Taotao (Taobao/Tmall): the true profit engine behind Alibaba's trillion-yuan revenue, the source of over 90% of the group's earnings. Without Taotao, Alibaba Cloud's 380-billion-yuan AI bet, instant retail's 86-billion-yuan annual loss, and FY2025's $16.5 billion buyback would all be castles built on sand.

But this machine is being pulled apart brick by brick. Losing 14 percentage points of e-commerce share in four years is not a single quarter's noise — it's a structural crack in the foundation. Understanding Taotao means understanding whether the foundation of Alibaba's entire investment story still holds.

---

## I. How Does Taotao Actually Make Money?

Many people assume Taobao/Tmall is a company that "sells things." It isn't.

**Taotao's core revenue comes from two sources:**

| Revenue type | FY2026 amount | Nature |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Customer management revenue (CMR, i.e., advertising) | ~300 billion+ yuan (estimate) | Merchants buy traffic by placing ads on Taobao |
| Commissions + technology service fees | — | Take a cut of GMV |

Taotao is fundamentally an **advertising company** — it holds no inventory and bears no logistics burden; it does exactly one thing: distributes consumer attention to merchants, who pay for it.

The upside of this model: **extremely high margins** (44% EBITA margin), **asset-light**, and **excellent cash flow**.

The downside: **it's entirely dependent on traffic**. When consumer attention shifts from Taobao to Douyin or Pinduoduo, the ad dollars follow.

## II. How Much Share Has Actually Been Lost?

This is the single most central question in the entire Alibaba investment thesis.

### 2.1 Four Years of Share Shifts

| Year | Taotao | Pinduoduo | Douyin + Kuaishou | JD |
|------|------|--------|---------|------|
| 2021 | **51.3%** | 15.9% | 9.2% | ~20% |
| 2022 | ~46% | ~18% | ~14% | ~18% |
| 2023 | 39.2% | ~20% | ~19.3% | ~17% |
| 2024 | **37.3%** | ~21% | **21.4%** | ~17.4% |
| 2026E | ~36.2% | ~22% | ~22.7% | ~16% |

*Source: QuestMobile 2025 Annual Report, China Merchants Securities (Jan 2026), Zheshang Securities forecast*

**Fourteen percentage points lost in four years — an average of 3.5 points per year.**

More critically, look at the trend: Douyin e-commerce's 2025 GMV was roughly 4.0-4.3 trillion yuan, still growing 25-30%. At a pace of closing the gap by roughly 900 billion yuan per year, **Douyin e-commerce's GMV is projected to catch up with Taotao's around 2028-2029.**

### 2.2 Why Was Share Lost?

It's not that Taobao got worse. It's that the "paradigm" of e-commerce changed.

**Paradigm one: search commerce (Taobao's home turf)**
Consumer has a clear need → opens Taobao to search → compares prices → places an order. Taobao dominated this paradigm for 15 years.

**Paradigm two: interest-driven commerce (Douyin's home turf)**
Consumer is scrolling short videos/livestreams → gets "planted" by content → makes an impulse purchase. Douyin's 800 million daily active users are essentially free traffic; Taobao's 300-400 million DAU means traffic must be bought.

**Paradigm three: value-for-money commerce (Pinduoduo's home turf)**
Consumer already knows what to buy → goes straight to Pinduoduo to find the lowest price. Pinduoduo's C2M model (connecting factories directly to consumers) achieves structurally low prices — not through subsidies, but through supply-chain efficiency.

Taobao's predicament: **it is not the first choice in any of the three paradigms.**

- Want it cheap? Check Pinduoduo first
- Want to be inspired by content? Open Douyin
- Want it fast? JD or Meituan Instant Shopping
- Want quality? Tmall has an edge, but JD is catching up too

This is what Duan Yongping means when he says "it's unclear where the moat is."

### 2.3 Has the Share Decline Bottomed Out?

**Evidence it has bottomed:**
- FY2026 Q4 CMR grew +8% on a comparable basis; merchants are still buying ads
- 88VIP membership surpassed **62 million** (double-digit growth), and high-value user loyalty is strengthening
- Taobao Flash Sale opened a new front in instant retail, with peak daily orders of **120 million**
- Douyin e-commerce's growth, though fast, will naturally slow as its base grows

**Evidence it hasn't bottomed:**
- Zheshang Securities forecasts share will continue falling to 36.2% in 2026
- Douyin e-commerce's shelf-scenario GMV share has risen to 40%, encroaching on Taobao's core "search shopping" territory
- FY2026 Q4 CMR grew only 1% year-over-year (unadjusted basis), showing that last year's technology-service-fee price hike dividend has already faded

**Assessment: the pace of decline is slowing, but it hasn't truly bottomed yet.** Going from losing 3-4 points a year to losing 1-2 points a year is "the bleeding slowing down," not "the bleeding stopped."

## III. The Truth Behind "Rising Monetization Rate"

The bulls' favorite story: even if GMV growth slows, raising the monetization rate (charging merchants a higher cut) can still grow revenue.

**Supporting data:**
- A new 0.6% basic software service fee was introduced in September 2024
- Penetration of the full-site promotion tool keeps rising
- FY2025 Q4 CMR grew +12%, far outpacing GMV growth

**But on the other hand:**

| Platform | 2024 Take Rate (monetization rate) |
|------|--------------------------|
| Pinduoduo | ~7.6% |
| Taotao | ~3.9% |

*Source: derived from company earnings reports*

Taotao's take rate is indeed lower than Pinduoduo's, implying room to raise it in theory. But price increases have a ceiling:

1. **Competitive constraint**: merchants operate across multiple platforms — when Taobao raises fees, merchants shift traffic to Pinduoduo/Douyin. The 0.6% technology service fee introduced in 2024 already drew merchant pushback
2. **Diminishing marginal returns**: FY2026 Q4 CMR grew only 1% year-over-year (+8% on a comparable basis), showing the price-hike dividend faded within a single year
3. **Core problem**: revenue growth driven by fee hikes is "extractive growth," not "flywheel growth." Over the long run it accelerates merchant outflow

Duan Yongping's test is simple: **a good business "spends less and collects more."** Taotao is indeed "collecting more" now, but "spending less" doesn't hold up at all — instant retail is losing 86 billion yuan a year, and the investment required to AI-overhaul Taobao has no end in sight.

## IV. Taobao Flash Sale: Lifeline or New Cash-Burning Black Hole?

In December 2025, Ele.me was formally rebranded "Taobao Flash Sale," positioned around "delivering everything in 30 minutes." This is one of Alibaba's biggest strategic bets of 2025-2026.

### 4.1 Achievements

| Metric | Data | Source |
|------|------|------|
| FY2026 full-year revenue | **78.5 billion yuan** (+47%) | Alibaba earnings report |
| Peak daily orders | **120 million** (August 2025) | Alibaba disclosure |
| Order-volume share | ~42-45% (near Meituan) | J.P. Morgan / Hongcan Research Institute |
| Q4 order growth y/y | **2.7x** | Jiang Fan's Q4 earnings call |
| 88VIP members | **62 million** | FY2026 Q4 |

### 4.2 The Cost

| Metric | Data | Source |
|------|------|------|
| FY2026 estimated loss | **~86 billion yuan** | Morgan Stanley |
| Loss per order (peak period) | ~4.7 yuan/order | CICC |
| Loss per order (latest) | ~1.5 yuan/order | Multiple sources |
| Combined cash burn across three platforms | **~173 billion yuan** | Sina Finance |

### 4.3 Management's Commitments

Jiang Fan laid out a clear three-step timeline on the FY2026 Q4 earnings call:

| Timeframe | Target |
|------|------|
| End of FY2027 | Unit economics (UE) turn positive |
| FY2028 | Instant retail GMV exceeds **1 trillion yuan** |
| FY2029 | Instant retail business becomes **profitable overall** |

### 4.4 Assessment: Opportunity or Trap?

**The bull case:**
- Instant retail reflects genuine consumption upgrading demand (delivering everything in 30 minutes); the market is projected to reach $126.7 billion by 2029 (source: GlobeNewswire)
- Taobao's main app, with 400 million+ DAU, feeds traffic to Flash Sale at an acquisition cost far below building it standalone
- Taobao Flash Sale has lifted Taobao's DAU by 20%, feeding traffic back to e-commerce

**The bear case:**
- An 86-billion-yuan annual loss; even if profitable by FY2029, cumulative losses could exceed 200 billion yuan
- Meituan has a ten-year delivery network and merchant relationships; by GTV, its share still exceeds 60%
- Regulators have already stepped in — in March 2026, Economic Daily published "The Delivery War Should End"
- User retention once subsidies fade is the biggest unknown
- Instant retail's per-order economics are inherently thin-margin — delivery costs of 6-7 yuan/order, commission rates of 5-7% — long-run net margin may be just 3-5%

**Most likely outcome: in ten years, Taobao Flash Sale captures a 25-30% share of instant retail, becoming the industry's number two. Profitable, but only marginally so.** Its strategic value exceeds its financial value — it prevents Meituan from using its local-services traffic to counterattack into far-field e-commerce.

## V. Is Taotao's "Flywheel" Still Spinning?

Taotao's classic flywheel: more merchants → more products → lower prices → more consumers → more merchants.

**Evidence the flywheel is being disrupted:**
- Low- and mid-tier merchants are leaving: Pinduoduo charges zero to onboard vs. Taobao's 0.6% technology service fee
- Cross-platform price comparison by consumers has become the norm; the overlap of users across all three platforms rose 19.9% year-over-year
- Douyin has built an entirely new wheel outside this flywheel (content planting the seed → impulse consumption → repeat purchase)

**Evidence the flywheel is still turning:**
- Tmall's flagship-store merchant ecosystem remains China's most mature; switching costs for branded merchants are high
- 88VIP's 62 million members lock in the mid-to-high-end consumer segment
- Taobao's SKU count remains the largest in the industry
- The full-site promotion tool shows merchants are still willing to spend money on Taotao

**Assessment: the flywheel isn't dead, but it has shifted from "self-accelerating" to "sustained by external force."** CMR growth is driven by fee hikes rather than organic GMV growth — that's "extractive" growth, not flywheel growth.

## VI. Core Conclusion: Taotao's Five-Year Profile

| Dimension | Current | Five years out (neutral forecast) |
|------|------|----------------|
| GMV share | ~37% | **28-32%** (continued decline, but decelerating) |
| Annual revenue | ~550 billion | **650-730 billion** (4-5% CAGR) |
| EBITA margin | ~44% (excluding Flash Sale) | **35-38%** (recovering after Flash Sale drag peaks) |
| Moat | Narrowing | Downgraded from "franchise" to "ordinary good business" |
| Positioning | Alibaba's profit engine | Still the largest profit source, but no longer the growth engine |

*Forecast sources: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and ai-berkshire's internal research working papers*

Taotao remains a remarkable money-making machine — a cash cow generating profit at the 200-billion-yuan level annually, top-tier even among global internet companies.

But it is no longer the monopoly platform where "everyone comes to me." It has become a composite of "branded e-commerce + mid-to-high-end consumption + instant retail" — differentiated, but with the differentiation shrinking.

For investors, the core question isn't "will Taotao die" — it won't. It's "can Taotao hold on long enough for Alibaba Cloud to take the baton as the new growth engine."
