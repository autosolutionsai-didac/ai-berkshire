# What Exactly Is Alibaba?

> "Understanding Alibaba" series · Part 01 · Introduction

## A Set of Numbers Nobody Can Quite Make Sense Of

On May 13, 2026, Alibaba delivered an earnings report that left everyone puzzled.

Revenue crossed the one-trillion-yuan mark for the first time — a rare milestone in the history of the Chinese internet. But in that same report: Non-GAAP net profit plunged 62%, free cash flow flipped from positive to a negative 46.6 billion, and the flagship e-commerce business's share has declined for four consecutive years.

Two questions split the market in half: is this a sick elephant under siege, or a giant "destroying itself to be reborn"? Before reading any analysis, let's put the raw numbers from both fiscal years on the table:

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2026 | Change |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| Total revenue | 996.4 billion yuan | **1,023.7 billion yuan** | +3% (**crossed one trillion for the first time**) |
| GAAP net profit | 126.0 billion yuan | **105.9 billion yuan** | **-16%** |
| Non-GAAP net profit | 158.1 billion yuan | **60.7 billion yuan** | **-62%** |
| Free cash flow | 73.9 billion yuan | **-46.6 billion yuan** | **Flipped from positive to negative** |
| Adjusted EBITA | 173.1 billion yuan | **76.4 billion yuan** | **-56%** |
| Full-year capital expenditure | ~89.6 billion yuan | **126.1 billion yuan** | +41% |
| Net cash | ~389.0 billion yuan | — | Still abundant |
| Cash and liquid investments | 597.1 billion yuan | **520.8 billion yuan** | -76.3 billion yuan |
| Shareholder returns (FY2025) | **$16.5 billion** ($11.9B buybacks + $4.6B dividends) | — | The most aggressive among Chinese ADRs |

*Data source: Alibaba FY2025/FY2026 earnings reports, SEC filings*

Put these numbers together and you see an extremely contradictory picture:

- **Revenue crossed one trillion** — but growth is only 3%
- **Non-GAAP net profit plunged 62%** — but not because the business got worse; management **deliberately** poured profit into AI infrastructure and instant retail
- **Free cash flow flipped negative** — but the company still has **520.8 billion yuan** sitting in cash
- **$11.9 billion was repurchased in FY2025** — but FY2026 Q4 posted a single-quarter operating loss of 848 million yuan, the first since 2021

**If you think Alibaba is declining — a 62% profit plunge is indeed alarming.**
**If you think Alibaba is being reborn — one trillion in revenue, 520 billion in cash, and 34% cloud growth are indeed full of promise.**

This series will not tell you which judgment is correct. It will tell you: **before you make that judgment, here is what you need to see clearly first.**

## Alibaba's "True Portrait"

The Alibaba of 2026 is no longer the "king of Chinese e-commerce" of 2020. It has changed.

Here is a table describing what it is today:

| Business segment | FY2026 revenue | Share | Growth | Profitability | One-line characterization |
|---------|-----------|------|------|---------|-----------|
| China e-commerce (Taotian + instant retail) | 554.2 billion | 54% | +9% | Contributes the vast majority of profit, but instant retail's heavy losses are a drag | A cash cow under siege |
| Alibaba Cloud Intelligence | 158.1 billion | 15% | **+34%** | EBITA margin ~9%, improving | The piece that looks most like a good business |
| International digital commerce (AIDC) | 144.2 billion | 14% | +9% | Near breakeven | From burning cash to stopping the bleeding |
| Other (Cainiao/digital media & entertainment/DingTalk, etc.) | 167.2 billion | 17% | -25% | Heavy losses (including the impact of divested businesses) | Focus, or baggage? |

*Data source: Alibaba FY2026 Q4 earnings report, May 13, 2026*

**Alibaba's true identity today is: a large conglomerate transitioning from an "e-commerce empire" to a dual-engine "AI Cloud + Consumption" company.**

The direction of the transformation is clear — focus on "e-commerce + cloud + AI," cutting non-core assets (Intime sold at a 9.3 billion loss, Sun Art Retail sold at a 13.0 billion loss).

But the cost of the transformation is also real — FY2026 profit collapsed, free cash flow turned negative, and three wars are being fought simultaneously.

## Three Common Misjudgments About Alibaba

### Misjudgment one: "Alibaba is an e-commerce company"

That was fine to say five years ago. Today it's no longer accurate.

Open the FY2026 earnings report, and Alibaba's revenue structure has fundamentally changed:

- **China e-commerce** (including instant retail): 54% — still the largest segment, but its share is declining
- **Alibaba Cloud**: 15% — growing 34%, with AI revenue now exceeding 30% of cloud's external revenue
- **International e-commerce**: 14% — AliExpress, Lazada, Trendyol
- **Other**: 17% — Cainiao, digital media & entertainment, DingTalk, etc.

Alibaba's current CEO, Eddie Wu, simultaneously serves as CEO of the group, of Taotian, and of Alibaba Cloud — the top position at the three most core businesses. His strategic narrative is to turn Alibaba into "China's Amazon": e-commerce as the cash cow, cloud as the growth engine.

**But on the other hand**: Alibaba Cloud's FY2026 revenue of 158.1 billion yuan is less than a fifth of AWS's (roughly $120 billion, or about 870 billion yuan). Its margin of 9% versus AWS's 36% is a huge gap. Whether the "China's Amazon" narrative holds up depends on whether Alibaba Cloud can raise its margin to 18-20% over the next five years — and that has not yet happened.

### Misjudgment two: "Alibaba's moat is deep"

That was true before 2020.

| Year | Taotian e-commerce share | Pinduoduo share | Douyin + Kuaishou share |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| 2021 | **51.3%** | 15.9% | 9.2% |
| 2024 | **37.3%** | 21.0% | 21.4% |

*Data source: QuestMobile, China Merchants Securities*

**Fourteen percentage points lost in four years.** Who took it?

- **Douyin e-commerce** (+12 percentage points): 800 million daily active users of free traffic vs. Taobao's 300-400 million daily active users of paid customer acquisition — a business-model-level mismatch
- **Pinduoduo** (+5 percentage points): an extreme low-price mindshare has already formed; "check Pinduoduo first for the price" has become a consumer habit

Alibaba Cloud's moat, meanwhile, is **widening** — its AI cloud market share of 35.8% exceeds the combined share of the #2 through #4 players (source: IDC 2025H1). But a narrowing e-commerce moat and a widening cloud moat **cannot simply be added together** — because e-commerce contributes over 90% of profit, while cloud is still only marginally profitable.

Duan Yongping said back in 2023: "I can't see through e-commerce, I don't know where the moat really is, and I don't know who will still be doing relatively well in ten years." He added to his Alibaba position in 2024, then cut it by more than 25% in 2025. He voted with his actions.

### Misjudgment three: "The profit collapse means Alibaba is done"

FY2026 Non-GAAP net profit plunged 62%, which looks alarming. But it needs to be unpacked:

| Reason for the profit decline | Estimated impact | Nature |
|-------------|---------|------|
| Instant retail (Taobao Flash Sale) cash burn | ~86.0 billion yuan in losses (Morgan Stanley estimate) | **Deliberate investment** |
| AI infrastructure capital expenditure | Full-year capex of 126.1 billion | **Deliberate investment** |
| Impact of divested businesses (Intime, Sun Art) | Revenue on a comparable basis actually grew 11% | One-time factor |

CFO Toby Xu explicitly said: **excluding the losses from instant retail, the China e-commerce group's EBITA would have achieved mid-single-digit growth.**

So the profit plunge isn't "the business getting worse" — it's management **deliberately choosing** to pour profit into two wars: AI infrastructure and instant retail.

**But that doesn't mean there's no risk.** Deliberate investment doesn't mean the investment was correct. A 380 billion yuan three-year AI investment plan, an 86.0 billion yuan annual loss in instant retail — what if that money burns without a return?

Joe Tsai himself has warned: "There is a bubble in AI data centers." When the chairman himself worries about a bubble while the company keeps increasing its investment — is that foresight, or is it recklessness?

## Understanding Alibaba Requires Answering a Few Core Questions

If you had to define today's Alibaba in one sentence:

> **Alibaba = an e-commerce cash cow (Taotian) under siege but still highly profitable + an AI cloud engine (Alibaba Cloud) that is accelerating but has not yet released its profits + several frontier businesses (AIDC, Flash Sale, Cainiao) that are either stopping the bleeding or still burning cash + 520 billion yuan in cash.**

To understand this company, you need to answer at least the following questions:

| Question | Which part of the series covers it |
|------|-------------------|
| Will Taotian's e-commerce share keep falling? How much longer can the cash cow hold up? | Part 02 |
| Can Alibaba Cloud become "China's AWS"? Is the 380 billion yuan investment worth it? | Part 03 |
| International e-commerce, instant retail, Cainiao — which are assets and which are baggage? | Part 04 |
| Is management trustworthy? Is the valuation actually cheap? | Part 05 · Finale |

Each question, taken alone, has had answers given by someone. But very few people have put them all together and measured them with the same ruler. That is precisely why the market's views on Alibaba are so divided — **the Alibaba you see depends on which piece of the puzzle you picked up.**

---

Before diving into each segment, keep a few orders of magnitude in mind:

- **Revenue at one trillion, but growth of only 3%** — big but not fast
- **Non-GAAP net profit of 60.7 billion, down 62%** — profit is disappearing, but it's management's deliberate choice
- **FY2025 shareholder returns of $16.5 billion** — $11.9 billion in buybacks + $4.6 billion in dividends, the most aggressive among Chinese ADRs
- **Alibaba Cloud revenue of 158.1 billion, growing 34%** — AI-driven acceleration, with Q4 single-quarter AI product revenue of 8.971 billion (annualized 35.8 billion)
- **Instant retail revenue of 78.5 billion, growing 47%** — high growth, but an estimated annual loss of 86.0 billion
- **Taotian e-commerce share ~37%** — down 14 percentage points over four years, still declining
- **PE of about 17x** — 47% below the historical average of 32.5x
- **Net cash of ~389.0 billion yuan** — plenty of ammunition on the balance sheet
- **380 billion yuan three-year AI investment plan** — the CEO called it "conservative"

*Data source: Alibaba FY2025/FY2026 earnings reports, Yahoo Finance, Investing.com*
