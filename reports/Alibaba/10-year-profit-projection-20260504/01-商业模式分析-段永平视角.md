# 01 Business Model Analysis — Duan Yongping's Perspective

> **Research subject**: Alibaba Group (9988.HK / BABA)
> **As of**: FY26Q3 (2025-12-31)
> **Report date**: 2026-05-04
> **Research question**: Will Alibaba's various business segments still be good businesses ten years from now (FY2036)? Which segments will remain the core of profit?

---

## 1. One-Sentence Core Judgment

**The data shows** that Alibaba will most likely still be a good company in ten years, but the good businesses may number only **2.5**: Alibaba Cloud (a core good business), Taotao (half a good business — pricing power is being eroded, but the moat holds), and AIDC/AliExpress (a potential good business, not yet proven). By Duan Yongping's standard of "spend less, collect more," local services, Cainiao, Digital Media & Entertainment, and other physical-retail businesses **do not constitute good businesses** — they are "necessary costs" that Alibaba must bear for ecosystem defense, not profit machines. The group as a whole has been reset from an "all-around platform company" into a capital-intensive AI infrastructure company driven by dual engines of "AI + cloud + e-commerce." **The nature of free cash flow is switching from the "money-printing machine" of the past decade to a "capex black hole" for the next five years** (a three-year, 380-billion-RMB capex plan has already been announced, and the CEO has hinted at even bigger plans).

## 3. Duan Yongping's "Ten-Year-Unchanged" Test (Segment by Segment)

### 3.1 Taotao Group

| Test question | Data/evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will users still need this business in 10 years? | China's retail e-commerce penetration is ~30%, still room for 5-10pct more growth; but consumers have already dispersed across Pinduoduo/Douyin/JD | **Yes**, but users do not exclusively depend on Taotao |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | 88VIP is near 60 million (FY26Q3), the mid-to-high spending cohort's mindshare remains; but Pinduoduo dominates the low-to-mid end, Douyin dominates content-driven planting, JD dominates self-operated 3C | **Partially**, differentiation exists only in mid-to-high-end physical goods |
| Trend in pricing power (monetization rate)? | Taotao take rate ~3.9% vs. Pinduoduo ~7.6% (2024 basis); FY26Q3 CMR grew only +1%, limited room to raise take rate further | **Weakening**, price wars have become the norm |
| What "unchanging things" are unique to Taotao? | (1) The Alipay-Taobao-Cainiao closed-loop ecosystem; (2) merchant ERP data accumulated across hundreds of thousands of brands; (3) 88VIP mindshare | Only the merchant-side moat is truly solid |

**Duan Yongping's take**: Downgraded from "an old-school Buffett-style franchise" to "an ordinary good business." It will still be around in 10 years, but "spend less, collect more" no longer holds — to defend its share, **the cash burn on Taobao Flash Sale and the never-ending investment in AI-overhauling the Taobao app will continue indefinitely**.

### 3.2 Cloud Intelligence Group (Alibaba Cloud)

| Test question | Data/evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it in 10 years? | China's cloud infrastructure market grew +26% YoY in Q4 2025 (Omdia), driven by AI agents; global hyperscaler capex will exceed $600 billion in 2026 | **Absolutely**, demand growth won't peak for 5-10 years |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen) has 300 million monthly active users (Feb 2026), leading China's AI cloud market, ahead of ByteDance/Huawei/Tencent; 2025 IaaS share of 32.8% (vs. 30.1% in 2024) | **Yes**, differentiation is strengthening |
| Pricing power? | AI inference demand is highly elastic and switching costs are high (data localization + API lock-in); but ByteDance's Volcano Engine is undercutting on price | Strong pricing power long-term, price war short-term |
| Unique "unchanging things"? | (1) The only Chinese cloud provider that simultaneously owns "foundation models + IaaS + downstream entry points (Taobao/DingTalk/Amap/Alipay)"; (2) state-sensitive-data compliance advantage; (3) physical decoupling from OpenAI/AWS makes it "irreplaceable" within China | Three layers of unique moat |

**Duan Yongping's take**: **This is the segment that most resembles a "good business" at Alibaba** — it has all four elements: user stickiness, pricing power, economies of scale, and compounding. But Duan Yongping would ask: will capex intensity (380 billion RMB over three years) permanently erode shareholder free cash flow? Looking at AWS's own trajectory — burning cash for the first 10 years, printing money for the next 10 — **the key variable is the ROIC inflection point around 2030**.

### 3.3 AIDC (International Digital Commerce)

| Test question | Data/evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it in 10 years? | Matching global small merchants with global consumers via cross-border e-commerce is a permanent need | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | AliExpress Choice's unit economics are improving in Europe + Latin America; Trendyol leads the Turkish market; but Lazada is fully overwhelmed by Shopee/TikTok Shop in Southeast Asia; Temu (Pinduoduo)/TikTok Shop form strong opposition in Europe/US | **Uncertain**, regional markets each fight their own battle, no global network effect |
| Pricing power? | The fully-managed model compresses merchant margins and caps the platform's take rate; logistics is the key differentiator (after Cainiao integration) | Neutral |
| Unique "unchanging things"? | The combination of Chinese manufacturing capacity plus global fulfillment (Cainiao logistics) is a combination few besides Temu possess | A single moat |

**Duan Yongping's take**: The 59% YoY narrowing of the FY26Q3 loss is a positive signal, but **"narrowing losses" does not equal "a good business."** In 10 years, the key question isn't whether Alibaba can turn a profit (it likely can) — it's whether it can hold a clear piece of territory amid the four-way melee of Lazada/Temu/Shopee/TikTok Shop. **This is "a segment that still needs 5 more years of observation," not an already-proven good business.**

### 3.4 Local Services Group (Taobao Flash Sale + Amap + Ele.me)

| Test question | Data/evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it in 10 years? | China's instant retail market is projected to reach $126.7 billion by 2029 (GlobeNewswire, April 2026); food delivery + instant retail are high-frequency essentials | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Meituan's order-volume share is 51% vs. Taobao Flash Sale's 42% (UBS, Feb 2026); short-term share gains rely on subsidies, long-term needs a rider network and merchant density | **Difficult** |
| Pricing power? | A duopoly price-war landscape; Alibaba's 2026 strategy explicitly states it will "chase the #1 spot at any cost," implying sustained capex and opex | **Extremely weak** |
| Unique "unchanging things"? | The integration of Amap's location data + Taobao traffic + Alipay payments — a low-cost traffic entry point Meituan lacks | Traffic-synergy advantage |

**Duan Yongping's take**: A textbook case of "a business you're forced into for defensive reasons." **This business will most likely still be ranked second in 10 years, and still be losing money or barely profitable** (referencing Meituan's long-run take rate of ~6-7% and single-digit net margins). "Spend less, collect more" does not hold at all here — **it should be treated as a defensive cost center, not a profit source**.

### 3.5 Cainiao

| Test question | Data/evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it in 10 years? | Cross-border logistics is an absolute necessity | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Strong in cross-border, but squeezed domestically by the Tongda network/JD Logistics/J&T; the IPO was withdrawn in 2025 and it has since been folded into AIDC | **Strong cross-border, weak domestic** |
| Pricing power? | Logistics is fundamentally a scale business, with gross margins of 5-10% and no pricing power | **Weak** |
| Unique "unchanging things"? | Alibaba's captive e-commerce order flow plus its global overseas-warehouse network | Moderate |

**Duan Yongping's take**: Logistics is not a good business (low gross margin, capital-intensive, no pricing power); Duan Yongping rarely invests in logistics companies. But as AIDC's "fulfillment backend" it is necessary — **it should be viewed as AIDC's "cost department," not an independent profit center**.

### 3.6 Digital Media & Entertainment (renamed Whale Entertainment)

| Test question | Data/evidence | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Will customers still need it in 10 years? | Entertainment content is a perpetual need | **Yes** |
| Can Alibaba maintain differentiation? | Youku ranks third/fourth in long video; iQIYI/Tencent Video/Bilibili have stronger content ecosystems; Alibaba Pictures has pivoted into Damai (live-event ticketing) | **Difficult** |
| Pricing power? | Long-video subscription fees are severely commoditized | **Weak** |
| Unique "unchanging things"? | Alibaba Pictures' film investment and distribution capability (some projects profitable); Damai's near-monopoly in live-event ticketing | Damai has a moat |

**Duan Yongping's take**: Content is a classic "not a good business" — hit-dependent on luck, no economies of scale. **FY25 full-year loss was still 550 million**; FY25Q4 turned profitable, but sustainability is questionable. In 10 years it will most likely be further divested or restructured.

### 3.7 Others (Freshippo, DingTalk, exited physical retail)

- **Intime and Sun Art have already been cleared out in 2024-2025** (exited at a combined loss of 22.5 billion) → from Duan Yongping's perspective, this is **correctly admitting a mistake**.
- **Freshippo**: multiple rounds of spin-off/sale rumors have already circulated; retail is fundamentally a low-margin, capital-heavy business.
- **DingTalk**: ARR exceeds $200 million (H1 FY2025), the leading SaaS platform in China; 700 million+ users, but monetization rate remains low. **DingTalk is a potentially good business**, but its scale relative to the Alibaba Group is too small (< 0.5% of revenue).

## 6. Key Judgments

### 6.1 Which 1-2 Segments Will Remain Alibaba's "Profit Core" in 10 Years?

**The evidence indicates that Alibaba's profit core in 10 years will be Alibaba Cloud (70% probability) plus Taotao (still contributing the base business)**:

- **Alibaba Cloud**: FY26Q3 revenue of 43.284 billion, +36%, EBITA +25% — this is the **only segment in the group simultaneously achieving "high growth + margin expansion."** China's cloud market is projected to reach 600+ billion dollars by 2030 (linear extrapolation from the current growth rate); if Alibaba maintains a 30% share, that implies revenue of $180+ billion, and at AWS's 25-30% EBITA margin, that could contribute $45-54 billion in EBITA — **a figure that would exceed the entire group's total profit for FY26**.
- **Taotao**: FY26Q3 CMR growth of only +1% shows growth has peaked, but as a "cash cow" it can still contribute 120-150 billion RMB in EBITA per year (referencing the FY25 scale), acting as the stabilizer for Alibaba's cash flow.

### 6.2 Which Segments Will Become "Dispensable" or "Loss-Making Black Holes"?

- **Digital Media & Entertainment (Whale Entertainment)**: dispensable; most likely to be spun off or exit the consolidated financials via an independent listing within 10 years.
- **Freshippo**: sale rumors have circulated for a long time without resolution, but its strategic standing is declining; may be divested over the long run.
- **Local services' "Taobao Flash Sale"**: **a loss-making black hole in the short term** (cash burn dragged down group EBITA in FY26Q3), but cannot be abandoned strategically; it is a "necessary loss."
- **Cainiao (viewed independently)**: downgraded from "independent IPO candidate" to "AIDC's fulfillment department"; its independent profitability is questionable.

### 6.3 Under Duan Yongping's "Spend Less, Collect More" Standard, Will Alibaba Still Be a Good Company in 10 Years?

**Supporting evidence:**
- Alibaba Cloud has AWS-like good-business characteristics (data, scale, technology, and ecosystem-distribution — a fourfold moat)
- 88VIP near 60 million shows mid-to-high-end user stickiness is still strengthening
- Free cash flow remains positive (though eroded by capex), and cash on the balance sheet is ample
- Duan Yongping increased his position in Alibaba (via H&H International) in Q4 2024, indicating his judgment that "the price already reflects most of the pessimism"
- Proactive divestiture of non-core assets (Intime, Sun Art) — management is evolving toward "focus"

**Contrary evidence:**
- "Spend less" doesn't hold at all: 380 billion RMB in three-year capex + Flash Sale cash burn + AIDC still losing money + AI foundation-model cash burn — **for the next 3-5 years, Alibaba is "a company that spends money," not "a company that collects money"**
- Taotao's take-rate increases are being capped by Pinduoduo/Douyin, and **the core good business's pricing power is weakening**
- Duan Yongping himself has said his "enthusiasm for Alibaba is lower than for Tencent" (based on business-model judgment)
- Fighting on multiple fronts (e-commerce, cloud, AI, local services, cross-border, logistics) violates Duan Yongping's preferred principle of "focus"

**Overall score (Duan Yongping's perspective)**:
- Current overall business quality of Alibaba: **3-3.5 / 5**
- Overall business quality of Alibaba in 10 years: **3.5-4 / 5** (contingent on whether Alibaba Cloud can deliver AWS-like profit realization)

**Core judgment**: **Alibaba will be a good company in 10 years, but only 2.5 good businesses will remain.** Looking at Alibaba Cloud alone, Duan Yongping would be willing to invest; but a discount needs to be applied for "the capital dissipated across other segments to support Alibaba Cloud's capex." This is why Goldman Sachs uses a sum-of-parts valuation (cloud segment alone at $54/share, group target price of $205) — the market has already accepted the characterization that "the group is a sum-of-the-parts story rather than a single good business as a whole."

## 7. Key Uncertainty Checklist

1. **When will Alibaba Cloud's capex hit its ROIC inflection point?** Whether the compute capacity from the 380-billion-RMB investment can convert into commensurate-scale profit by 2028-2030 is the single largest point of risk in the 10-year judgment. **Confidence: low** — no player across the US-China AI industry has yet proven this out.

2. **Will AI disrupt e-commerce search/recommendation?** Integrating Qwen into Taobao is Alibaba's offensive play, but if ChatGPT-style products (or ByteDance's Doubao) become the next generation's shopping entry point, Taotao's "proprietary traffic" moat could be bypassed. **Confidence: medium** — need to observe retention after Qwen app's monthly active users hit 300 million.

3. **When will Taobao Flash Sale's cash burn stop?** Alibaba's current "chase #1 at any cost" strategy could see local services lose 50-100 billion RMB over the next 2-3 years (referencing Meituan's/Pinduoduo's historical cash-burn patterns). **Confidence: medium-high** — cash burn will continue at least through 2027.

4. **AIDC's overseas geopolitical risk**: tightening tariffs/compliance on Temu/SHEIN/AliExpress in Europe (the 2024-2026 changes to the de minimis rule) could materially change the Choice model's economics. **Confidence: medium**.

5. **The GMV growth curve for Pinduoduo/Douyin e-commerce**: Pinduoduo's 2024 GMV was 5.1 trillion, Douyin's was 4.3 trillion+ and growing 30% — if the two combined continue taking 0.5-1 trillion of incremental share each year, room for Taotao's take rate to rise will be further foreclosed. **Confidence: high** (the trend is already established).

6. **Management stability**: CEO Eddie Wu's tenure and strategic continuity (in office since Sept 2023, ~2.5 years so far); whether Joe Tsai's leadership of the sum-of-parts restructuring path will be disrupted. **Confidence: medium** — management currently shows firm commitment to the "AI + e-commerce" dual-engine strategy.

7. **Shareholder-return policy**: while ramping up capex, Alibaba is still maintaining buybacks and dividends (FY26 buyback scale versus capex balance); if continued capex escalation forces buybacks to shrink, **from Duan Yongping's perspective, the "value returning to shareholders" pathway could be impaired**. **Confidence: medium**.
