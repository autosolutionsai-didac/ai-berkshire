# 02 Financial & Valuation Analysis — Buffett's Perspective (Core)

> **Research subject**: Alibaba Group (9988.HK / BABA)
> **As of**: FY26 Q3 (2025-12-31)
> **Report date**: 2026-05-04
> **Research question**: What is the neutral-case forecast for Alibaba Group's FY2036 annual profit? How does the current valuation compare with intrinsic value?

---

## 0. One-line core judgment

**The neutral-case forecast for Alibaba Group's FY2036 Non-GAAP net profit is approximately RMB 459.1 billion** (probability-weighted expected value approximately RMB 488.0 billion), which is **3.6x** the current FY25 net profit of RMB 126.0 billion. Discounting back at a 14x reasonable PE and a 10% discount rate implies an intrinsic value of approximately **RMB 2.55 trillion**, versus the current market cap (approximately RMB 2.31 trillion) — a **margin of safety of only 22%, moderately weak attractiveness**. The AI/cloud upside optionality is not fully priced in, but the near-term profit collapse risk has already been priced in by the market ahead of time.

## 2. 10-year revenue projection (by segment)

### 2.1 Taobao & Tmall Group (China e-commerce)

**Factual basis**: FY25 revenue RMB 449.8 billion, EBITA RMB 196.2 billion (margin 43.6%); FY26 Q3 EBITA -43% (heavy instant-retail investment).

**Core tensions**:
- Overall growth in China e-commerce has slowed to single digits, with Pinduoduo + Douyin e-commerce continuing to take share
- Limited room to raise the take rate further (the "software service fee" has already been implemented, and its marginal effect had already faded by FY26 Q3)
- Instant retail (Taobao Flash Sale, FY26 Q3 revenue RMB 20.8 billion, +56%) is a new growth driver but currently loss-making

**10-year CAGR assumptions**:

| Scenario | Assumption | CAGR | FY36 revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | 88VIP penetration + instant retail turns profitable + premiumization lifts take rate by +1pp | 6% | RMB 805.5 billion |
| **Neutral** | **Low single-digit GMV growth + slight take-rate increase** | **5%** | **RMB 732.8 billion** |
| Bear | Pinduoduo/Douyin continue to take share, CMR stagnates | 2% | RMB 548.3 billion |

### 2.2 Cloud Intelligence Group (the core AI story)

**Factual basis**: FY25 revenue RMB 118.0 billion (+11%), EBITA RMB 10.56 billion (margin 8.9%). FY26 Q3 quarterly revenue RMB 43.284 billion (+36%), EBITA margin 9.0%. AI revenue has posted 10 consecutive quarters of triple-digit growth.

**Scrutiny of Eddie Wu's "USD 100 billion in 5 years" target credibility**:
- The target covers MaaS + full-stack AI (B2B + B2C), requiring USD 100 billion ≈ RMB 710 billion by FY30
- Going from RMB 118.0 billion in FY25 to RMB 710 billion in FY30 requires a 5-year **CAGR of 43%** (tool-calculated)
- Current growth is 36%, with triple-digit AI growth continuing — the target is **aggressive but not impossible**
- Constraints: compute supply (H800 restrictions / Huawei Ascend), the pace of enterprise AI commercialization, price wars

**10-year CAGR, three scenarios**:

| Scenario | 5-year CAGR | 10-year CAGR | FY36 revenue (RMB 100M) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bull (Eddie Wu's target is achieved + 15% in the following 5 years) | 43% | 25% | 10,990 |
| **Neutral (30% for 5 years + 12% for the following 5 years)** | **30% / 12%** | **20%** | **7,306** |
| Bear (18% for 5 years + 8% for the following 5 years) | 18% / 8% | 13% | 4,774 |

> Comparable benchmark: AWS's FY25 revenue is approximately USD 120 billion (CAGR 18-20%); Azure's growth rate is 30%+. As China's AI leader with expansion into overseas ASEAN markets, Alibaba Cloud's **neutral 20% CAGR is reasonable**.

## 3. 10-year profit-margin projection

### 3.1 Alibaba Cloud's steady-state operating margin (the key question)

**Current**: FY25 EBITA margin 8.9%, rising to 9.0% in FY26 Q3.

**Cross-comparison**:
- AWS: FY25 operating margin 36-38%
- Azure: estimated 40-45% (including scale effects)
- Google Cloud: FY25 approximately 14% (still catching up)

**10-year steady-state forecast**:
- Bull: reaches 25% (between GCP and Azure, driven by AI-inference scale effects + high-margin MaaS)
- **Neutral: 18-20%** (public cloud share stabilizes + AI compute gross margin affected by domestic chip substitution)
- Bear: 12% (persistent price war + heavy Capex amortization burden)

### 3.2 Taobao & Tmall's steady-state margin

**Current**: FY25 EBITA margin 43.6% (CMR functions similarly to a pure profit take-rate).

- The drag from instant retail peaks in FY26-FY28 (reference: Meituan's food-delivery breakeven took 7 years)
- Long-term, the CMR business's take rate is unlikely to rise further (regulation + competition)
- Neutral assumption: steady-state EBITA margin of **35-38%** (excluding the instant-retail drag)

### 3.3 When does AIDC turn profitable

**Key inflection point**: the FY26 Q3 quarterly loss has already narrowed to RMB 2.0 billion (versus -RMB 5.0 billion in the same period last year).
- **FY28 (calendar year 2027) breakeven** is the base case (medium confidence)
- Steady-state EBITA margin: 5-8% (reference: Amazon International, Shopee)

### 3.4 Local services / instant retail

- Instant retail (Taobao Flash Sale), by reference to Meituan, will take 5-7 years to reach positive unit economics
- **FY29-FY30 overall breakeven** is the base case
- Steady-state margin 3-5%

## Core conclusions and investment recommendation

1. **The FY2036 neutral forecast for annual profit is RMB 459.1 billion** (approximately USD 63.1 billion), 3.6x current FY25 net profit
2. **The probability-weighted expected value is RMB 488.0 billion** — reaching RMB 716.2 billion under a more bullish scenario, and only RMB 213.1 billion under the bear scenario
3. **The current share price implies a premium of only 22% to intrinsic value**, short of Buffett's "clearly cheap" standard (which requires a 30% margin of safety)
4. **The key variable is Alibaba Cloud**: whether it can deliver on the USD 100 billion target by FY30, and whether it can raise its EBITA margin to 18-20%
5. **From Buffett's perspective**: Alibaba **is not a no-brainer** — if you believe in the AI story and management's execution capability, the "neutral-to-bullish" path is worth a 50%-probability bet; if you strictly require a margin of safety of 30%+, it would be advisable to build a position in tranches below HKD 100/USD 110

**Duan Yongping's supplementary perspective**: Alibaba's RMB 560.2 billion in cash + a business model approaching stable free cash flow + China internet's leading moat — this qualifies as a name that is "understandable and won't die," but **the odds at the current price are not extreme enough**. The FY26 profit collapse is the "admission ticket" being paid for FY28-FY36 AI dividends — if you believe the ticket will pay off, holding at the current price is reasonable; if you believe Chinese cloud cannot replicate AWS's profitability path, then 14x PE is still too expensive.
