## What Alibaba Looks Like Five Years From Now

**Transitioning from an "e-commerce empire" to an "AI-cloud-driven technology group."**

- Taobao & Tmall's share of revenue falls from 45% to ~35% — still the largest revenue source, but no longer the growth engine
- Alibaba Cloud's share rises from 12% to ~20%, becoming the core profit and valuation engine (analogous to AWS for Amazon)
- CEO target: cloud + AI external revenue exceeding $100 billion within 5 years
- Biggest risk: when Douyin e-commerce will catch up to Taobao & Tmall on GMV (expected 2028-2029), and the threat from ByteDance's Volcano Engine at the AI application layer
