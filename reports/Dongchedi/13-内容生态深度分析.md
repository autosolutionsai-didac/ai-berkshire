# Dongchedi Content Ecosystem: In-Depth Analysis

> Analysis date: May 2026

## I. Overview of the Content Ecosystem

Dongchedi's content ecosystem has gone through three evolutionary stages: from an "automotive information channel" to an "automotive content platform" to an "automotive transaction ecosystem." The core positioning of its content system is: **use content to connect users with transactions, and use content to drive automotive purchase decisions**.

## II. Creator Scale and Tiering System

### 2.1 Creator Scale Growth Trajectory

Dongchedi's creator ecosystem has gone through explosive growth:

- **2019**: Dongchehao (Dongchedi's creator account program) launched; the number of creators doubled in less than a year
- **2020**: Content publishing volume rose 4x, video publishing volume grew 5x, and live-streaming sessions grew 11x
- **2023**: After the four platforms (Dongchedi + Douyin + Toutiao + Xigua Video) merged their content ecosystems, creators reached 6.34 million
- **2025**: Creator scale reached approximately 7.5 million, up 78% year-over-year
- The number of creators earning monetized income grew 6x

### 2.2 Creator Tiering Structure

Dongchedi employs a clearly tiered operating strategy:

**Top-tier creators (exclusive-contract tier)**
- Scale: approximately 200-300 people
- Representatives: Auto Tianya (ranked No. 1 on Dongchehao's composite index, 900,000+ followers), Wanche Riji (Ten Thousand Cars Diary), Adam Huanglin Yi, and others
- Support mechanism: the "Star Program" incubates 100 top-tier exclusively-signed creators, opening up 10 nationwide test routes and 15 smart-driving professional test grounds
- Revenue sources: exclusive signing fees + traffic revenue share + commercial partnerships + priority platform resource allocation
- In 2024, the "Exclusive Studio" program signed nearly 30 well-known domestic and international automotive content creators in its first cohort

**Mid-tier creators (moderate-cooperation tier)**
- Scale: approximately 8,000-10,000 people (estimated)
- Support mechanism: access to 350 vehicle models for content use, with nearly 100 million yuan invested in incentives
- Revenue sources: content incentive funds + branded-content monetization + transaction commissions
- In 2024, more than 8,000 creators received help increasing their income

**Long-tail creators (light-cooperation tier)**
- Scale: hundreds of thousands to over a million
- Support mechanism: no follower-count threshold; any creator meeting Dongchehao's content-posting requirements can share in a 30 million yuan exclusive incentive fund
- Revenue sources: basic traffic revenue share + campaign rewards

**UGC users (spontaneous-creation tier)**
- Scale: approximately 2 million daily active posters (including the Douyin side)
- Characteristics: car-owner community sharing, usage experiences, car pick-up diaries, etc.
- No direct income, but forms the platform's foundational content base

### 2.3 Key Observations

The figure of 7.5 million creators includes the combined total across Douyin, Toutiao, Xigua Video, and other merged platforms. The actual scale of independent creators on the Dongchedi app itself is far smaller than this. This figure carries a degree of "scope inflation," but it also reflects the depth of internal resource integration within ByteDance's ecosystem.

## III. Analysis of Creator Revenue Models

### 3.1 Revenue Source Structure

Dongchedi's creator monetization system spans five main channels:

**(1) Platform traffic revenue share**
- Basic revenue share based on content views and engagement
- A new content traffic revenue-share mechanism launched in 2025
- Similar in logic to Douyin's mid-form video revenue-share program

**(2) Branded-content monetization (advertising partnerships)**
- Connects brands with creators through influencer-marketing products such as "Dongche Star Selection"
- Supports coordinated multi-channel placements with complementary content
- Provides placement-effectiveness guarantee mechanisms

**(3) Transaction commissions (CPS)**
- New-car lead commissions: purchase leads generated by creator content convert into commissions
- Used-car transaction commissions: creators earn commissions when users complete used-car transactions via their content
- The CPS commission model launched in 2025

**(4) Platform subsidies/incentive funds**
- The "Spotlight Program" 300 million yuan monetization fund
- Cash incentives for various themed content campaigns
- Guaranteed-minimum income for exclusively signed creators

**(5) E-commerce sales**
- Sales of automotive accessories and car-related merchandise
- Vehicle transaction commissions from livestream sales

### 3.2 Scale of Investment in Support Programs

| Time | Program Name | Scale of Investment |
|------|----------|----------|
| 2018 | Creation Program | Supported 100 content organizations, incubated 100 video IPs |
| 2019 | 2020 Creation Program | Aimed to build 50 creators with a million-plus followers ("Di Zao Jia") |
| 2020 | Creation Conference / Ecosystem Program | Goal of helping creators achieve 100 million yuan in combined income |
| 2023 | Navigator Program | Partnered with Douyin Auto, focused on supporting professional content |
| 2024 | Exclusive Studio Program | 800 million yuan in cash + 100 billion in traffic (including a 500 million yuan content fund + 300 million yuan monetization fund) |
| 2025 | Creator Support Program | 500 million yuan in cash + 150 billion in traffic + 10,000 vehicle-uses |

### 3.3 Assessment of Support-Program Effectiveness

**Positive results:**
- In 2024, over 7,000 vehicle-use instances were provided to creators cumulatively
- More than 20 offline events were held
- More than 8,000 creators received help increasing their income
- The number of creators earning monetized income grew 6x
- More than 100,000 creators saw traffic growth of over 70%

**Issues worth watching:**
- The support-program budget was reduced from 800 million yuan (2024) to 500 million yuan (2025), which may reflect the platform reassessing its return on investment
- Income concentration among top-tier creators is high, and transparent data on the actual income levels of mid- and long-tail creators is lacking
- "Helped more than 8,000 creators increase their income" represents a coverage rate of only 0.1% relative to the total of 7.5 million creators

## IV. Content Format Distribution: Short Video vs. Text-and-Image vs. Livestreaming

### 4.1 Evolution of Content Formats

Dongchedi's content formats have gone through three stages:

- **2017-2019 (text-and-image-dominant period)**: Emerged from Toutiao's automotive channel, centered on text-and-image information
- **2019-2022 (video transformation period)**: Short-video community launched; video publishing volume grew 5x, text-and-image content grew 4x, and live-streaming sessions grew 11x
- **2022-present (all-format integration period)**: Short video and livestreaming dominate, with text-and-image content serving as an informational supplement

### 4.2 Analysis of Each Format's Characteristics

**Short video**
- Currently the dominant format for content consumption
- Dongchedi pioneered the automotive short-video community
- Content types: quick vehicle reviews, driving tips, car-pickup shares, automotive humor, etc.
- After deep integration with Douyin content, the short-video ecosystem expanded further
- Estimated to account for the largest share of user consumption time (60-70%)

**Livestreaming**
- Major push starting in 2019; live-streaming sessions grew 11x in 2020
- Dongchedi has become China's largest automotive livestreaming platform (2020 data)
- Livestreaming's functional positioning: bringing users closer + transaction monetization
- During the dealer-host livestream challenge, livestream viewership grew 7.4x and average watch time grew 4x
- In the Chongqing pilot, 95% of 530 participating dealers used the commission-based version of the car-sales tool
- Livestreaming is transitioning from a "content arena" to a "transaction arena"

**Text-and-image**
- Still retained on vehicle model pages for spec configurations, owner reviews, professional reviews, and similar use cases
- Serves as a vehicle for in-depth content and tool-type information
- Incremental growth has shifted to video and livestreaming, but text-and-image content remains irreplaceable at the decision-making stage

### 4.3 Estimated Content Consumption Structure

The platform has not disclosed precise consumption-time shares by format, but based on industry trends and platform characteristics, the following is estimated:

| Content Format | Consumption Time Share (estimate) | Content Volume Share (estimate) |
|----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Short video | 55-65% | 60-70% |
| Livestreaming | 20-25% | 10-15% |
| Text-and-image | 10-20% | 20-25% |

## V. UGC / PGC / OGC Content Ratios

### 5.1 Definitions and Characteristics of the Three Content Types

**OGC (Occupationally Generated Content)**
- Content produced by Dongchedi's own in-house team: the real-world testing database (covering 4,000+ vehicle models and 1 million+ data points), winter/summer test programs, ADAS safety real-world tests, and similar
- Branded content from automaker-official accounts on the platform
- Positioning: the core asset that establishes platform credibility

**PGC (Professionally Generated Content)**
- Signed and partnered professional automotive influencers, car reviewers, and MCN organizations
- 200+ top-tier creators signed under the Exclusive Studio program
- Positioning: the main supply of in-depth content and professional reviews

**UGC (User Generated Content)**
- Ordinary car owners' pickup shares, usage experiences, and community interactions
- 2 million authors/users posting content daily (including the Douyin side)
- Positioning: the content foundation and source of community vitality

### 5.2 Estimated Ratios

The platform has not disclosed precise ratios; the following is an estimate based on public information:

| Type | Content Volume Share | Consumption Volume Share | Commercial Value Share |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| UGC | 70-80% | 30-40% | 10-15% |
| PGC | 15-25% | 40-50% | 50-60% |
| OGC | 3-5% | 15-20% | 25-35% |

### 5.3 Key Insight

Dongchedi's content system is a three-layer architecture of "professionalized OGC + diversified PGC + interactive UGC." Although OGC is small in volume, it carries the core role of establishing platform credibility (the real-world testing database, large-scale review programs, etc.); PGC is the main driver of content consumption and commercial monetization; UGC forms the base of community activity.

This structure differs markedly from purely entertainment-focused platforms (where UGC consumption dominates absolutely), reflecting the professional nature of automotive content — when making a purchase decision involving hundreds of thousands of yuan, users demand far higher content professionalism and credibility than for ordinary consumer goods.

## VI. Relationship with Douyin's Automotive Content

### 6.1 History of Integration

- **2017-2022**: Dongchedi operated as an independent app, relatively separate from Douyin's automotive content
- **July 2023**: Dongchedi announced the full integration of automotive content across Dongchedi, Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video, with Dongchedi serving as the overall operating entity coordinating resources across platforms
- **2025**: Integration deepened further, with jointly launched initiatives such as the Navigator Program and Creator Carnival events

### 6.2 Content Interoperability Mechanisms

**Interoperability aspects:**
- Creator account systems are interconnected: content from Dongchehao creators can be simultaneously distributed to Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video
- Traffic pool sharing: Dongchedi creators can gain traffic exposure on Douyin
- Data interoperability: users' automotive-interest behavior on Douyin can feed back into Dongchedi's recommendation system
- Marketing product interoperability: "Dongche Star Selection" supports coordinated cross-platform placements

**Differentiated positioning:**
- Douyin side: narrative-style reviews, car-owner lifestyle vlogs, trending-topic tie-ins — focused on "reach beyond the core audience" to raise brand awareness
- Dongchedi side: teardown reviews, technical breakdowns, comparative reviews — focused on "deep conversion" to build purchase trust
- The two sides combine to form a content matrix of "broad reach, deep conversion"

### 6.3 Degree of Creator Overlap

Creator overlap is fairly high. Dongchedi's core operating logic is "create once, distribute across multiple platforms":

- After integration, the total number of automotive creators covered across the four platforms was 6.34 million (2023), rising to 7.5 million (2025)
- Top-tier professional car reviewers are essentially active on both Douyin and Dongchedi simultaneously
- However, mid- and lower-tier creators may treat Douyin as their primary base, with Dongchedi serving as a secondary distribution channel

### 6.4 Strategic Significance of the Integration

This integration is fundamentally an internal resource consolidation within ByteDance's ecosystem, aimed at:
1. Using Douyin's traffic advantage to offset Dongchedi's shortfall in user scale
2. Using Dongchedi's vertical, specialized expertise to improve the conversion efficiency of Douyin's automotive content
3. Inflating overall reported metrics ahead of an IPO

**Potential risks:**
- Over-reliance on Douyin traffic — if ByteDance adjusts its internal traffic-allocation strategy, Dongchedi's content consumption could be significantly affected
- Ambiguity in reported metrics — whether figures such as "500 million average daily consumption instances" include automotive content consumption on the Douyin side affects the assessment of Dongchedi's standalone value

## VII. Content Quality and Credibility Issues

### 7.1 Summary of Major Controversies

**Incident One: December 2023 Winter Testing Controversy**

- **Event**: Dongchedi published its "2023 Winter Test" results, in which the AITO M7 EREV's pure-electric range achievement rate was only 31.6%, ranking last
- **Automaker responses**:
  - Huawei's Richard Yu (Yu Chengdong) angrily denounced it as "a test that hurts people and misleads the public! Science and rigor should be basic ground rules"
  - Great Wall Motor questioned whether the vehicle had its heater running for 71 minutes before testing and had its doors opened multiple times for heat dissipation, which it said deviated significantly from real-world usage
  - Geely Auto likewise raised questions about non-standardized testing procedures
- **Dongchedi's response**: All test items used a unified standard consistent with extreme-cold-weather use cases, applied without discrimination. It announced an open day on December 14, where automakers could observe on-site with a full livestream
- **Aftermath**:
  - There were reports that Huawei and two other platforms had ceased cooperation with Dongchedi (people familiar with the matter later responded that this was simply the expiration of the original cooperation agreement)
  - Multiple automakers expressed doubts about the authority of Dongchedi's reviews

**Incident Two: July 2025 ADAS Safety Testing Controversy**

- **Event**: Dongchedi launched the "Dongchedi Smart-Driving Proving Ground" program, testing 15 high-risk scenarios of assisted-driving systems across 36 mainstream smart vehicle models. The results showed an average pass rate of only 35.74%, with 15 out of nearly 40 vehicles scoring "zero passes" in highway accident scenarios
- **Core controversies**:
  - Tesla's Model X and Model 3 performed best (passing 5 of 6 highway scenarios), sparking accusations of "favoring foreign brands"
  - The use of fabric-covered dummy vehicles instead of real ones in testing may have disadvantaged domestic models that rely on LiDAR (fabric has radar-reflection properties very different from a metal vehicle body)
  - Inconsistent following-distance settings — Tesla's "medium" setting was 60 meters, while some domestic vehicles may have been set at only 30 meters, creating an unfair starting point
- **Reactions from various parties**:
  - Elon Musk reposted the test video, stating: "Given that data export is legally prohibited, Tesla achieved China's top score without any locally trained data"
  - HarmonyOS Intelligent Mobility (Huawei's ecosystem) responded, "We have seen it; no comment"
  - Multiple domestic automakers questioned the testing methodology through official accounts and affiliated media
- **Dongchedi's response**: This was an assisted-driving educational program intended to remind the public to correctly understand the functional boundaries of assisted driving, and it did not constitute any official ranking

### 7.2 Deeper Analysis of Credibility Issues

**Structural conflict: the platform is both referee and player**

Dongchedi faces a fundamental role conflict:
- As a **content platform**, it needs to produce attention-grabbing review content (large-scale programs like winter testing and ADAS testing are traffic-driving tools)
- As a **transaction platform**, its revenue depends on advertising spend and lead purchases from automakers
- As a **reviewer**, it needs to remain neutral and objective

These three identities are inherently in conflict. Automakers are Dongchedi's core customers, and when review results are unfavorable to certain automakers, the platform faces substantial commercial pressure.

**Lack of standardized testing**

The automotive industry lacks a unified third-party review standard. While Dongchedi's proprietary tests fill a market gap, the fact that the standards are self-set and the process is not fully transparent makes them vulnerable to criticism.

**The two sides of controversy**

From a traffic perspective, each review controversy has brought Dongchedi enormous topic attention. Whether this constitutes an incentive mechanism of "controversy equals traffic" is worth observing.

## VIII. Application of AI-Generated Content on Dongchedi

### 8.1 Overview of AI Technology Deployment

Dongchedi's AI deployment spans four layers:

**Data layer**
- Structured data at a scale exceeding 50 million records
- Vehicle-model database covering 76,000+ models
- Real-world testing database covering 4,000+ vehicle models with over 1 million data points

**Model layer**
- Self-developed automotive vertical large language model, registered/filed in December 2024
- Full integration with Volcano Engine and the Doubao large language model
- Officially launched an AI vehicle-selection product in July 2025

**Application layer**
- AI vehicle-selection assistant: supports fuzzy vehicle search, multi-vehicle comparison, information queries, and transaction services
- AI marketing: intelligent marketing plans based on analysis of user voice/sentiment
- AI valuation: used-car pricing and market assessment

**Content generation layer**
- Partnership with Volcano Engine, using the Doubao large language model to help creators generate professional automotive content and educational articles
- Improves content production efficiency

### 8.2 Assessment of the Extent of AI-Generated Content Adoption

**Current stage: an assistive tool, not yet a large-scale replacement for human creation**

- AI is primarily applied at the **tool layer** (vehicle selection, valuation, comparison) rather than the **content creation layer**
- In content generation, AI functions more as an "assistant to creators" than a "replacement for creators"
- The platform continues to emphasize support for human creators (500 million yuan in cash + 150 billion in traffic), indicating that AI has not yet displaced the human-centered content production model

**Future direction:**
- In April 2026, Volcano Engine released a next-generation automotive AI solution, with the Doubao large language model now deployed in more than 7 million vehicles
- The application of AIGC in automotive marketing is accelerating: creative asset generation, marketing matrix distribution, real-time data feedback
- Standardized content such as spec comparisons and configuration explanations on vehicle model pages may gradually be replaced by AI-generated content
- Personalized reviews and experience-sharing creative content will likely still depend on human creators in the near term

### 8.3 Impact on the Content Ecosystem

AI's impact on Dongchedi's content ecosystem cuts both ways:
- **Efficiency gains**: lowers the barrier to content production, which may accelerate content output from mid- and long-tail creators
- **Quality risk**: AI-generated automotive content, if not backed by real-world testing verification, could further dilute the platform's professionalism and credibility
- **Competitive moat**: Dongchedi's real-world testing database (1 million+ data points) is a unique asset for training an automotive vertical AI — one that is difficult for competitors to replicate

## IX. Core Findings and Investment Implications

### 9.1 Strengths of the Content Ecosystem

1. **ByteDance ecosystem traffic dividend**: After integrating with Douyin, its reach — 500 million average daily consumption instances and 310 million users interested in automobiles — is unmatched by any competitor (Autohome, Yiche)
2. **Creator-scale advantage**: 7.5 million creators (across platforms) form a powerful content supply network
3. **Complete range of content formats**: full coverage across short video, livestreaming, and text-and-image, spanning the entire chain from awareness to decision to transaction
4. **Unique OGC assets**: the real-world testing database, winter/summer test programs, and other large-scale review IPs — while controversial, have genuinely established a differentiated brand perception

### 9.2 Risks in the Content Ecosystem

1. **Ongoing damage to credibility**: two major review controversies (the 2023 winter test, the 2025 ADAS test) have exposed the structural flaw of the "platform tests and grades itself" model. If credibility erodes further, the core value of OGC content will be undermined
2. **Dependence on Douyin traffic**: of the 7.5 million creators and 500 million average daily consumption instances, how much is independently contributed by the Dongchedi app itself? This question is directly relevant to assessing Dongchedi's value as a standalone listed entity
3. **Low creator monetization efficiency**: of 7.5 million creators, only 8,000 received help increasing their income — an extremely low monetization coverage rate — and retention of long-tail creators could be a latent risk
4. **Content homogenization**: automotive content is inherently constrained by the finite number of vehicle models, and large numbers of creators produce content around the same limited set of popular models, leading to significant homogenization
5. **AI as a double-edged sword**: while AI lowers the barrier to content creation, it may also lead to a proliferation of low-quality content, diluting the platform's overall content quality

### 9.3 Key Hypotheses to Verify

- The true growth trend of Dongchedi app's standalone DAU (stripped of synergy effects with Douyin)
- The real reason the support-program budget fell from 800 million to 500 million yuan — is it efficiency optimization or a shortfall in return on investment?
- Whether creator-support intensity will contract further after the IPO due to profitability pressure
- Whether review controversies will affect automakers' willingness to spend on advertising — this is a core risk on the revenue side
