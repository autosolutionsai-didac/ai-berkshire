## II. Full Breakdown of Revenue Structure

### 2.1 CPS / Lead-Generation Service Revenue (estimated 40-45% of total, approx. 2.2-3.4 billion yuan)

#### 2.1.1 How the CPS Model Works

In August 2022, Dongchedi was the first in the industry to launch a CPS (Cost Per Sale) transaction solution, rolling it out nationwide starting in 2023 and fundamentally upending the previously dominant CPT (Cost Per Time, i.e., annual-fee-based) model.

**The core logic shift:**
- CPT model: dealers pay a fixed annual membership fee (typically 100,000-250,000 yuan/year), and the platform provides a quota of leads in return. The drawback was severe "lead padding" — to hit the promised lead volume, platforms distributed large numbers of low-quality or even fake leads.
- CPS model: dealers pay only for vehicles actually sold, so platform revenue is directly tied to final sales volume.

**Fee mechanics:**
- CPS is not a simple flat commission but a hybrid of "base membership fee + transaction commission."
- Based on dealer-reported case studies: in 2022 one dealer's membership fee was roughly 132,000 yuan (for 280 units sold); after switching to CPS in 2023, assuming unchanged sales volume, net of a 35% rebate, the fee came to roughly 255,000 yuan.
- From this, the effective commission per vehicle sold is estimated at roughly 500-900 yuan (varying considerably by region, brand, and vehicle price).
- Fees are higher in tier-1/tier-2 cities and lower in tier-3/tier-4 cities.
- Some premium brands may see per-vehicle commissions of 1,000-1,500 yuan, while lower-priced models may fall in the 300-500 yuan range.

**Dealer network scale:**
- Over 28,500 dealers were on the CPS program (2024 data), surpassing 30,000 in 2025.
- Coverage spans more than 110 brands, i.e., 100% coverage of mainstream automotive brands.
- 57% of dealers achieved year-over-year growth in sales volume in 2024.

#### 2.1.2 Revenue Scale Estimate

**Conservative estimate:**
- 30,000 dealers x average annual fee of 80,000-100,000 yuan (including CPS commission) = 2.4-3.0 billion yuan.
- Note: under the CPS model, dealers' actual annual payments vary widely — top-tier 4S dealerships may pay 300,000-600,000 yuan/year, while small dealers may pay only 30,000-50,000 yuan.

**Cross-validation:**
- Autohome's 2025 lead-generation service revenue was 2.71 billion yuan (down 13.6% year-over-year), with both the number of paying dealers and per-dealer pricing declining.
- Dongchedi's dealer count has already surpassed Autohome's (Autohome has roughly 25,000-28,000 paying dealers), but because CPS pricing has considerable elasticity, a reasonable estimate places this segment's revenue for Dongchedi in the 2.2-3.0 billion yuan range.

### 2.2 Brand Advertising Revenue (estimated 25-30% of total, approx. 1.4-2.2 billion yuan)

#### 2.2.1 How the Advertising Business Works

Dongchedi's advertising business has been commercially integrated with Douyin Auto, with "Ju Dongche" serving as the unified operator, coordinating resources across Douyin, Toutiao, Xigua Video, and Dongchedi.

**Three main marketing scenarios:**
1. **Brand voice**: new-model launch announcements, brand image advertising.
2. **Mindshare seeding**: the influencer-marketing product "Dongche Star Picks," enabling joint placements across both Douyin and Dongchedi.
3. **Business operations**: performance advertising, bid-ranking placements, feed advertising.

**Key product portfolio:**
- Brand splash-screen ads, feed ads.
- "Star-Push-Search-Live" product bundle (influencer seeding + search optimization + livestream traffic conversion).
- "Dongche Cloud Store": a full-funnel business-operations platform for dealers.
- Juliangyinqing (Ocean Engine) advertising tools for the auto industry.

#### 2.2.2 Revenue Scale Estimate

**Estimation logic:**
- Autohome's 2025 media services revenue was 1.15 billion yuan (down 24.3% year-over-year), covering only the single Autohome platform.
- Dongchedi's advertising value lies in tapping into the Douyin ecosystem, reaching close to 500 million users interested in autos, with average daily auto-content consumption of 500 million views.
- However, it should be noted there is ambiguity around revenue attribution between advertising on Dongchedi itself versus ByteDance/Ocean Engine's broader auto-industry advertising.
- If only advertising revenue attributable to the Dongchedi legal entity is counted (excluding the ByteDance-retained portion of Douyin auto advertising), a reasonable estimate is 1.4-2.2 billion yuan.

**Reference data:**
- ByteDance's total revenue for the first half of 2024 was roughly USD 73 billion, with China-region advertising accounting for roughly 62% of that.
- Automotive-industry advertising typically accounts for 8-12% of the total internet advertising pie.
- As the core vehicle for ByteDance's auto vertical, Dongchedi can plausibly be allocated roughly 30-40% of ByteDance's total auto-advertising revenue.

### 2.3 Used-Car Transaction Service Revenue (estimated 10-15% of total, approx. 0.6-1.1 billion yuan)

#### 2.3.1 Business Model

Dongchedi's used-car business has merged with Douyin's used-car operation, launching the "Nationwide Purchase" model — "buy online, deliver offline."

**Core data:**
- Used-car listings exceed 300,000 units.
- Roughly 30,000 partner used-car dealers, with 88,000 active dealer accounts.
- Coverage spans 350+ cities, with roughly 40% of transactions completed through "Nationwide Purchase."
- In 2025, China's used-car transaction volume surpassed 20 million units for the first time, at an average price of roughly 64,500 yuan.

**Fee model:**
- Dealers pay a platform service fee (including commission) per vehicle sold; industry case studies show roughly 2,000 yuan per unit.
- Services include: transaction matching, vehicle inspection, title-transfer service, after-sales protection.
- Direct-sale business (fixed-price purchases): the platform earns the buy-sell spread.
- Auction business: owners consign vehicles for auction, and the platform collects a service fee.

#### 2.3.2 Revenue Scale Estimate

**Estimation logic:**
- Assuming Dongchedi's platform facilitates 2-3% of the national 20-million-unit used-car market (roughly 400,000-600,000 units).
- At an average service fee of 2,000-2,500 yuan per unit.
- Used-car transaction service revenue = 400,000-600,000 units x 2,000-2,500 yuan = 0.8-1.5 billion yuan.
- Given that some transactions incur only a lower information-service fee, a conservative estimate is 0.6-1.1 billion yuan.

### 2.4 SaaS / Dealer Digitalization Tools Revenue (estimated 5-8% of total, approx. 0.3-0.6 billion yuan)

#### 2.4.1 Product Portfolio

Dongchedi's digitalization tools for dealers are delivered mainly through the "Maichetong" SaaS system:

- **Smart lead-acquisition system**: big-data-driven identification and intelligent distribution of prospective car buyers.
- **Vehicle pricing management**: online quoting and price-comparison tools.
- **Customer management CRM**: customer follow-up, lead routing, data analytics.
- **Content operations tools**: helping dealers publish short videos and livestreams on Douyin/Dongchedi.
- **Data analytics dashboard**: conversion-rate analysis, competitor data, regional market insights.

#### 2.4.2 Fee Model

- Basic SaaS functionality is typically bundled into the dealer membership fee (packaged with CPS).
- Advanced modules require additional payment: enhanced data analytics, smart marketing tools, etc.
- Separately billed value-added services run roughly 10,000-30,000 yuan/year per dealer.

#### 2.4.3 Revenue Scale Estimate

- 30,000 dealers x average SaaS value-added service of 10,000-20,000 yuan/year = 0.3-0.6 billion yuan.
- Note: this revenue overlaps somewhat with CPS membership fees; only the incremental portion is counted here.

### 2.5 Financing / Insurance Referral Revenue (estimated 3-5% of total, approx. 0.2-0.4 billion yuan)

#### 2.5.1 Current State of the Business

Dongchedi's positioning in auto financing and insurance is relatively conservative, operating mainly as a referral model:

- **Auto installment financing**: the platform displays installment plans from partner financial institutions, and collects a referral commission when users apply.
- **Auto insurance referral**: recommending auto insurance products at the point of new/used-car purchase, collecting insurance commissions on a CPS basis.
- **Extended-warranty services**: sales of extended-warranty products for used cars.

#### 2.5.2 Revenue Estimate

- Benchmarked against the financial-and-insurance portion of Autohome's "online marketplace and other" segment (roughly 10-15% of its 2.59 billion yuan online-marketplace revenue).
- Dongchedi's financing/insurance referral revenue is estimated at roughly 0.2-0.4 billion yuan.

### 2.6 Aftermarket / Car-Care Services Revenue (estimated 1-2% of total, approx. 0.05-0.15 billion yuan)

#### 2.6.1 Current State of the Business

- Launched the offline car-care brand "Dongdong Yangche" at the end of 2023, with its first store opening in Chongqing.
- Operates through licensed-franchise partnerships (not self-owned stores), offering car-wash, maintenance, glass-coating, and similar services.
- The Dongchedi app has a car-owner services channel covering fueling, a parts mall, car washing, minor maintenance, and more.
- Still in a pilot phase, with limited scale.

#### 2.6.2 Revenue Estimate

- The aftermarket business is still in early exploration, contributing minimal revenue.
- Estimated at roughly 50-150 million yuan (mainly from online referral commissions rather than offline store revenue).

## IV. Deep Dive: The Economics of the CPS Model

### 4.1 The Economic Essence of the CPT-to-CPS Shift

| Dimension | CPT model | CPS model |
|------|---------|---------|
| Payment trigger | Time (annual fee) | Transaction (commission) |
| Party bearing risk | Dealer (paid for leads that may not convert) | Platform (provides leads that may not convert) |
| Revenue certainty | High (locked in at year start) | Low (depends on sales volume) |
| Dealer satisfaction | Low (severe lead padding) | High (paying for actual sales is more transparent) |
| Core capability required of platform | Traffic acquisition | Precise matching + conversion efficiency |
| Industry ceiling | Capped by number of dealers | Capped by sales volume — theoretically higher ceiling |

### 4.2 Estimating the CPS Per-Vehicle Commission Rate

**Estimation method:**
- China's new-car sales volume in 2024 was roughly 31.43 million units, with an average new-car price of roughly 160,000-170,000 yuan.
- Estimated new-car sales facilitated by the Dongchedi platform: assuming 30,000 dealers each selling an average of 3-5 units per month, that comes to roughly 1.08-1.8 million units/year.
- Assuming a per-vehicle CPS commission of 600-1,000 yuan, the commission rate works out to roughly 0.35%-0.6% of vehicle price.
- This rate is far below that of real-estate brokerage (1-3%), but given autos' lower average selling price and intense competition, it is a reasonable level.

### 4.3 Implicit Risks in the CPS Model

1. **Accelerating dealer attrition**: China's total number of auto dealers has fallen from a peak of over 40,000 to roughly 30,000 in 2024, as price wars have squeezed margins razor-thin.
2. **The OEM direct-sales trend**: new-energy-vehicle upstarts (Nio, Xpeng, Li Auto, etc.) use a direct-sales model, bypassing dealers and lead-generation platforms.
3. **Pressure on commission rates**: as dealer profits continue to shrink, their capacity to absorb platform commissions is declining.
4. **Competitors following suit**: Autohome and Yiche are also rolling out CPS-like products, which could dilute Dongchedi's differentiation advantage.

## VI. Key Assumptions and Uncertainties

### 6.1 Core Assumptions Underlying the Estimates

1. **Advertising revenue attribution**: assumes only advertising revenue attributable to the Dongchedi legal entity is counted, excluding the ByteDance-retained portion of Douyin/Toutiao auto advertising. If Dongchedi consolidates all of "Ju Dongche's" advertising revenue at IPO, total revenue could be revised upward by 1.5-2.5 billion yuan.
2. **CPS commission rate**: based on a limited set of dealer-reported case studies; actual rates likely vary significantly by brand and region.
3. **Used-car transaction volume**: Dongchedi has never disclosed platform transaction-volume data; the 2-3% market-share figure is an estimate.
4. **Profit margin**: Dongchedi's CPS model requires heavy operating investment (field sales teams, transaction guarantees, after-sales service), so its margin may be well below Autohome's 22%.

### 6.2 Key Uncertainties

1. **IPO disclosure**: once a prospectus is filed, real financial data will be disclosed for the first time, at which point the above estimates can be validated.
2. **ByteDance ecosystem revenue-sharing mechanism**: how is the traffic-acquisition cost from Douyin accounted for at Dongchedi? Are there related-party pricing concerns?
3. **Macro risks**: price wars in the auto industry and a wave of dealer closures could suppress platform revenue growth.
4. **Competitive landscape**: Autohome's post-Haier-acquisition strategic direction, and the competitive strategy of Yiche (a Tencent-affiliated company).
