# DCar (Dongchedi) — In-Depth Investment Research Report

> Research date: 2026-05-30 | Information scarcity: Grade C (not publicly listed, no public financial statements) | Synthesis of 20 parallel sub-reports

---

## One-Line Conclusion

> DCar is the new king of China's vertical automotive space — leveraging ByteDance-ecosystem's 510 million daily active automotive users and a CPS (cost-per-sale) model innovation, it is comprehensively overtaking Autohome. But its "moat is borrowed" (96% of users depend on ByteDance-ecosystem traffic), the industry's underlying customer base is shrinking (dealers are deregistering at a rate of 5,000 per year), and a CPS price hike once triggered BYD's withdrawal from the platform and boycotts across multiple provinces. An IPO is most likely in H1 2027, with a reasonable valuation of $4.0-5.0 billion. Recommend waiting for the prospectus to disclose real financial data before making a decision.

## Core Findings (Highlights from 20 Reports)

### I. Business Model: ByteDance Ecosystem's Automotive Monetization Gateway

**Nature of the business**: Not a traditional automotive media company, but a "vertical monetization platform for ByteDance-ecosystem traffic in the automotive space." 84% of users click through from Douyin to DCar to view vehicle details — DCar is the "checkout counter" for Douyin's automotive content.

**Revenue structure (estimated)**:

| Segment | Amount (RMB billion) | Share | Model |
|------|------------|------|------|
| CPS leads/transaction commission | 2.2-3.0 | 40-45% | Pay-per-transaction; per-vehicle commission of RMB 500-900 |
| Brand advertising | 1.4-2.2 | 25-30% | Joint placement across four endpoints (Douyin + Toutiao + Xigua + DCar) |
| Used-car transaction services | 0.6-1.1 | 10-15% | Nationwide sourcing model, 300,000+ vehicles listed |
| SaaS/digitalization tools | 0.3-0.6 | 5-10% | DCC system, AI livestreaming tools |
| Financial services/insurance/aftermarket | 0.2-0.4 | 3-5% | Deliberately restrained (IPO considerations) |
| **Total** | **5.5-7.5** | 100% | |

**Revenue scale is now on par with Autohome** (Autohome's 2025 revenue: RMB 6.45 billion), but the trends are diametrically opposed: DCar is growing across every segment, while Autohome has posted declines in both revenue and profit for two consecutive years.

### II. Moat: Real, But Not Owned

| Moat dimension | Score | Analysis |
|-----------|------|------|
| Traffic barrier | 8/10 | ByteDance-ecosystem's 510 million daily active automotive users dwarfs every competitor — but this is ByteDance's moat, not DCar's |
| Data barrier | 7/10 | A 76,000-model database, 4,000+ real-world tests, 50 million+ structured data points, a petabyte-scale data warehouse — years of accumulation that is hard to catch up to |
| Technology barrier | 6/10 | Recommendation algorithms + the Doubao large language model + VR viewing, but 70% of the technology is inherited from ByteDance's central platform |
| Network effects | 5/10 | The creator-to-user-to-dealer flywheel has taken shape, but the content "gear" is borrowed |
| Brand recognition | 4/10 | Users are mostly delivered by the algorithm; proactive brand recognition is weaker than Autohome's |
| Switching costs | 3/10 | Switching costs are low on both the consumer side and the dealer side |
| Pricing power | 3/10 | A 93% CPS price increase triggered BYD's withdrawal and boycotts across multiple provinces — pricing power is limited |

**Core judgment: The moat genuinely exists (data + technology + scale), but its largest component (traffic) does not belong to DCar itself. Duan Yongping would say: "A good jockey riding someone else's good horse."**

### III. Competitive Landscape: Taking the Throne, But the Industry Is Deteriorating

| Metric | DCar | Autohome | Trend |
|------|--------|---------|------|
| DAU | 10 million (+57%) | Not disclosed | DCar catching up |
| MAU | 39 million | 65.75 million (+1.2%) | Autohome stagnant |
| Revenue | ~RMB 6.0 billion (estimated) | RMB 6.45 billion (-8.3%) | About to overtake |

**New threats**: Xiaohongshu's automotive lead-generation volume is up 860% year-over-year; NEV makers are building their own direct-sales channels; dealers are deregistering at 5,000/year, weakening the overall market.
