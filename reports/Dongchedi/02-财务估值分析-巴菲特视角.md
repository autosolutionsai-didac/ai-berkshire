## I. Estimating Revenue Scale

### 1.1 Core Difficulty: Dongchedi Has No Public Financial Data

Dongchedi formally spun off from ByteDance into an independent company in 2023, and in 2024 it completed roughly $600 million in financing (with participation from Sequoia China, KKR, General Atlantic, and others), at a valuation of roughly $3 billion. In February 2026, reports surfaced of a planned Hong Kong IPO targeting to raise $1.0-1.5 billion. To date, it has not disclosed any revenue or profit figures.

All revenue figures below are estimates based on industry data and comparable companies, not facts, and are provided solely as investment-analysis reference.

### 1.2 Revenue Structure Comparison Using Autohome as an Anchor

Autohome's 2024/2025 revenue structure (unit: RMB 100 million):

|--------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Media services (brand advertising) | RMB 1.52 billion | RMB 1.15 billion | -24.3% |
| Lead-generation services | RMB 3.14 billion | RMB 2.71 billion | -13.6% |

### 1.3 Estimating Dongchedi's Three Main Revenue Sources

**(1) Advertising revenue (brand advertising + performance advertising)**

- Dongchedi's DAU is roughly 10 million (mid-2025), while Autohome's DAU is roughly 8-9 million — the two are of comparable scale
- But Dongchedi's ad inventory is less mature than Autohome's, so its ad-monetization efficiency is lower
- Backed by the ByteDance ecosystem, Dongchedi has a unique advantage in influencer/creator marketing tied to Douyin, branded "Dongchedi Star Selection"
- Estimate: Autohome's media services revenue is roughly RMB 1.15 billion; Dongchedi's advertising revenue is estimated at 50-70% of that
- **Estimated range: RMB 600-800 million**

**(2) Lead/CPS transaction commission revenue (core revenue)**

This is what sets Dongchedi apart from Autohome — since 2023 it has fully switched to a CPS (cost-per-sale) model:
- Over 28,000 CPS member dealers, covering 110+ brands
- Claims to have facilitated more than 2 million vehicle transactions per year
- The industry cost per closed lead is roughly RMB 1,500-2,000, but the commission Dongchedi collects under the CPS model may be lower than under the traditional lead model
- Assuming a per-vehicle CPS commission of RMB 500-800 (below the traditional lead cost, which is also what makes the CPS model attractive to dealers), on 2 million vehicles sold:
  - Conservative (RMB 500/vehicle): RMB 1.0 billion
  - Neutral (RMB 650/vehicle): RMB 1.3 billion
  - Optimistic (RMB 800/vehicle): RMB 1.6 billion
- **Estimated range: RMB 1.0-1.6 billion**

**(3) Other revenue (data services, financial-product referrals, used cars, etc.)**

- Autohome's online marketplace and other revenue is roughly RMB 2.59 billion, but this includes a large amount of new-retail revenue (offline experience stores, etc.)
- Dongchedi is still at an early stage in used cars, financial-product referrals, and data services
- **Estimated range: RMB 200-400 million**

## II. Estimating Profitability

### 2.1 Typical Industry Profit Margins

| Company | Gross margin | Operating margin | Net margin |
|------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Autohome (2024) | 78.9% | 14.3% | 23.9% |
| Autohome (2025) | 72.4% | 11.9% | 22.4% |
| CarGurus (2025) | ~70% | ~21% | 17.2% |
| Cars.com (2025) | ~50% | ~8% | ~4% |

Gross margins in the automotive vertical-media/lead-generation industry typically run between 50% and 80%, a classically high-margin internet business. But net margins vary widely, depending on traffic-acquisition cost and the intensity of market competition.

### 2.2 Characteristics of Dongchedi's Cost Structure

**Traffic-acquisition cost — the core advantage of being backed by ByteDance:**
- Autohome must spend heavily on search engines like Baidu to acquire traffic, its single largest cost item
- Dongchedi's traffic comes mainly from natural referrals within the Douyin ecosystem, so its marginal customer-acquisition cost is extremely low
- This means Dongchedi's traffic cost may be only one-third to one-half of Autohome's
- But Dongchedi needs to invest heavily in building out its content ecosystem (an RMB 800 million content-support program was announced in February 2026)

**Gross margin under the CPS model:**
- Under the CPS model, Dongchedi bears part of the transaction-completion risk (it is only paid when a user actually buys a car)
- But because the platform itself carries no inventory, actual costs remain very low
- Estimated gross margin: 60-70% (lower than Autohome's traditional CPT model, but still healthy)

### 2.3 Has Dongchedi Already Turned Profitable?

**Key judgment: most likely not yet profitable, or just at breakeven.**

Reasons:
1. Only spun off from ByteDance in 2023, still in a rapid-expansion phase
2. Raised $600 million in financing in 2024 — if it were already generating ample profit, such large-scale fundraising wouldn't be needed
3. Continued heavy investment in content-ecosystem building (RMB 500 million + RMB 800 million support programs)
4. Benchmarked against Yiche (Bitauto) — from 2015 until going private, it never achieved profitability, with annual losses of RMB 300 million to RMB 1.4 billion
5. But Dongchedi's cost structure is better than Yiche's, and the CPS model also reduces ineffective spending

**Estimated profit/loss scenarios:**

| Scenario | Revenue | Estimated net profit | Net margin |
|------|------|-----------|--------|
| Conservative (still loss-making) | RMB 1.8B | -RMB 300M to -RMB 500M | Loss |
| Neutral (at breakeven) | RMB 2.3B | RMB 0 to RMB 200M | 0-8% |
| Optimistic (already profitable) | RMB 2.8B | RMB 300-500M | 10-18% |

If Dongchedi does indeed go public in Hong Kong in 2026, it will typically need to demonstrate profitability or a clear path to it. My inclination is to judge that by 2025 it may have come close to breakeven or achieved modest profitability, in preparation for the IPO.

- Declining revenue (2025 revenue down 8.3%)
- Traditional business under pressure (both media services and lead-generation services declining)
- Intensifying market competition (precisely from share being taken by Dongchedi)

## IV. Estimating Dongchedi's Valuation

### 4.1 Method 1: Revenue-Multiple Method (PS Valuation)

| Scenario | Estimated revenue | Reasonable PS multiple | Valuation |
|------|---------|-----------|------|
| Conservative | $250M | 3x | $750M |
| Neutral | $320M | 4x | $1.28B |
| Optimistic | $390M | 5x | $1.95B |

Logic for selecting the PS multiple:
- Dongchedi's revenue is growing (vs. Autohome's decline), warranting a growth premium
- As a private, unlisted company, it should carry a liquidity discount
- Backed by the ByteDance ecosystem, it commands a platform premium
- Reasonable PS range: 3-5x (above Autohome's 2.2x, approaching CarGurus's 3.3x)

### 4.2 Method 2: User-Value Method

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Dongchedi monthly active users | ~35-40 million |
| Autohome monthly active users | ~30-35 million |
| Autohome market cap | ~$2.0 billion |
| Autohome value per user | ~$57-67/user |
| Dongchedi reasonable value per user | ~$50-80/user |
| **Dongchedi user-value estimate** | **$1.75-3.2 billion** |

Note: Dongchedi's "broad automotive-interest user base" exceeds 500 million, but this is the pool of general-interest users within the Douyin ecosystem and cannot be used directly for valuation. The core app's monthly active users of roughly 35-40 million is the correct, effective user base.

### 4.3 Method 3: Lead/Transaction-Value Method

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Annual facilitated transaction volume | ~2 million vehicles |
| China's annual passenger-vehicle sales (2024) | ~27.56 million vehicles |
| Dongchedi penetration rate | ~7.3% |
| Value per transaction to the platform | ~RMB 500-1,000 |
| Lead/transaction-dimension valuation | RMB 1.0-2.0 billion ($140-280 million) |

This method produces a valuation that runs low, because it captures only the direct value of transaction commissions and does not include advertising, data, or other indirect monetization.

### 4.4 Composite Valuation Judgment

| Valuation method | Conservative | Neutral | Optimistic |
|---------|------|------|------|
| Revenue-multiple method | $750M | $1.28B | $1.95B |
| User-value method | $1.75B | $2.5B | $3.2B |
| Lead/transaction-value method | $700M | $1.0B | $1.5B |
| **Composite judgment** | **~$1.0B** | **~$2.0B** | **~$3.0B** |

**Core conclusion:**
- The $3 billion valuation from the 2024 financing round sits at the upper edge of the optimistic scenario
- If the 2026 Hong Kong IPO raises $1.0-1.5 billion at an estimated dilution ratio of 15-20%, that implies an IPO valuation of roughly $5.0-10.0 billion — which would require significant revenue growth and profit improvement to support
- I believe a reasonable independent valuation lies between $1.5 and $3.0 billion; $3 billion would require proof of sustained high growth and profitability

### 4.5 The IPO Valuation "Story" vs. "Reality"

**The story the IPO can tell (supporting a high valuation):**
1. China's auto market sells more than 30 million vehicles annually, the largest market in the world
2. The CPS model's penetration rate still has enormous room to grow
3. AI-assisted car selection and smart recommendations will improve user experience and conversion rates
4. Ecosystem synergy with Douyin gives it a traffic-cost advantage that is difficult to replicate
5. The shift to new-energy vehicles brings a reallocation of marketing budgets

**Risks in reality (constraining the valuation):**
1. No public track record of profitability
2. Revenue is highly dependent on the ByteDance/Douyin ecosystem, raising doubts about true independence
3. Autohome, as the first mover, has a market cap of only $2.0 billion and is shrinking
4. Chinese auto dealers are broadly unprofitable and highly sensitive to platform fees
5. Fierce price wars in the auto industry are squeezing OEM and dealer marketing budgets

### 5.2 Dongchedi's Share of Value Within the ByteDance System

| Dimension | Dongchedi | Share of ByteDance |
|------|--------|--------------|
| Valuation ($3 billion) | $3.0B | 0.55% |
| Revenue (~RMB 2.3B / $320M) | $320M | 0.2% |
| Users (app MAU ~35 million) | 35M | Extremely small share |

**Dongchedi's financial significance to ByteDance is very small — its revenue contribution is under 0.2%, and its valuation share is under 0.6%.**

### 5.3 Strategic Value vs. Financial Value

Dongchedi's value to ByteDance lies not in its financial contribution, but in:

**Strategic value:**
1. **A monetization channel for automotive-vertical traffic**: automotive content on Douyin is consumed 500 million times per day, and this traffic needs a specialized platform to monetize
2. **A testing ground for the transaction closed-loop**: the CPS model is an important experiment in ByteDance's evolution from an "advertising platform" to a "transaction platform"
3. **An IPO "icebreaker"**: if Dongchedi's IPO succeeds, it will be the first listed company in the ByteDance family, paving the way for further spin-offs
4. **Deep integration with automakers**: through Dongchedi, ByteDance goes deep into the auto industry value chain, in service of its broader smart-driving and connected-car business

**Implications for investors:**
- If you are investing in ByteDance (via secondary-market equity), Dongchedi's IPO has a negligible effect on ByteDance's overall valuation
- If you are considering investing in the Dongchedi IPO itself, you need to evaluate its fundamentals independently, not simply award it a high valuation because it is "part of the ByteDance family"

## VI. Core Conclusions

### 6.1 Revenue and Profitability Assessment

- Dongchedi's 2025 revenue is estimated at RMB 2.0-2.5 billion ($280-350 million), with revenue still growing relatively quickly
- It has most likely not yet achieved stable profitability, or has only just reached breakeven
- Gross margin is roughly 60-70%, below Autohome's but still at a healthy level
- If it goes public, it will need to demonstrate a path to profitability in the prospectus

### 6.2 Valuation Assessment

- The $3 billion valuation from the 2024 financing round is reasonable but leans optimistic
- A reasonable independent-IPO valuation range: $1.5-3.0 billion
- If the IPO is priced above $5.0 billion, it would require an extremely strong growth narrative and profit improvement to justify
- Benchmarked against Autohome's current market cap of only $2.0 billion, for Dongchedi to convince the market that "I'm worth more than $3 billion," the key lies in proving growth speed and profit quality

### 6.3 Key Questions From a Buffett-Style Perspective

1. **Where is the moat?** Dongchedi's core advantage is traffic inflow from the ByteDance/Douyin ecosystem, but this is both an advantage and a risk — if it were ever separated from the ByteDance ecosystem, would the moat still be there?
2. **When will free cash flow appear?** It is currently still in a heavy-investment phase (content support, AI infrastructure), and free cash flow may need another 2-3 years to stabilize in positive territory
3. **Is management honest and reliable?** As a company originating from ByteDance, it's worth watching whether its post-independence management can establish an independent governance culture
4. **What is the relationship between price and value?** If the IPO is priced at $5.0-10.0 billion (implying a PS of 14-28x), the margin of safety would be seriously inadequate for an automotive vertical platform that has yet to prove its profitability

**Final judgment: Dongchedi is a competitive automotive content-plus-transaction platform, but as an investment target, the IPO price is the decisive factor. If the IPO is priced below $3.0 billion (roughly 10x PS), it holds some appeal; if priced above $5.0 billion, it would be borrowing too heavily against future growth and would not satisfy the value-investing principle of margin of safety.**
