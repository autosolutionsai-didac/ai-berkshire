# Dongchedi: In-Depth Analysis of Its Used-Car and Automotive Aftermarket Layout

> Research date: May 30, 2026
> Research scope: Dongchedi's business layout and competitive positioning in used-car transactions, automotive-care aftermarket services, and auto finance

---

## I. Deconstructing the Used-Car Business Model

### 1.1 Core positioning: platform-based matchmaking + light self-operation

Dongchedi's used-car business is neither a pure C2C nor a pure B2C model in the traditional sense, but a hybrid model **led by platform-based matchmaking, supplemented by light self-operation**:

- **Platform matchmaking layer (the core)**: Dongchedi is essentially a used-car listing and transaction-matching platform. About 30,000 dealer partners act as member merchants that list vehicles on the platform, with prices set by the dealers themselves. The platform holds no inventory and bears no vehicle-ownership risk, earning revenue instead through transaction service fees and commissions. Industry estimates put the platform's service fee at roughly 2,000 yuan per vehicle.
- **Direct-operated layer (exploratory)**: Dongchedi has opened 3 directly operated stores offering an "official direct-sale fixed-price" service, which includes official quality inspection, a 7-day no-reason return policy, a 1-year/30,000-km extended warranty on core components, and a lifetime buyback guarantee for accident, flood, or fire-damaged vehicles. This tier functions more as a "showroom" for building brand trust than as a scaled profit driver.
- **Franchise-store network**: About 90 franchise stores cover multiple locations nationwide, operated by partner dealers but required to follow Dongchedi's standardized processes.

### 1.2 The "Nationwide Purchase" model: the core differentiator

Dongchedi's most distinctive used-car offering is its "Nationwide Purchase" model — selecting and ordering a car online, with delivery handled cross-region offline:

- Covers more than 280 cities, with fulfillment/delivery reaching nearly 300 cities
- In 2025, roughly 40% of transactions were completed through the Nationwide Purchase model
- Vehicle inventory exceeds 300,000 units
- Addresses the structural pain point of traditional used-car trading being "strong locally, weak across regions"

### 1.3 Business integration with Douyin: the traffic super-weapon

In July 2023, Dongchedi announced the completion of an upgraded integration with Douyin's used-car business — a key to understanding Dongchedi's competitiveness in used cars:

- After integration, the used-car user base reached **147 million**, with **88,000** active merchant accounts
- The two sides jointly committed **5 billion units of traffic** to support quality content and vehicle listings
- On Douyin: short-video content grew 290%, the number of users going live grew 421%, and vehicle listings grew 575%
- On Dongchedi: video content grew 344%, traffic grew 225%, and content-driven leads grew 1,007%
- An innovative "vehicle listings + content" dual-operating model achieved a combination of "people finding cars" and "cars finding people"

The essence of this integration is **using short video and livestreaming to rebuild the trust chain in used-car sales**. The biggest obstacle in traditional used-car trading is a lack of trust caused by information asymmetry; showcasing vehicle condition via video and negotiating live during a livestream significantly lowers the user's decision-making barrier.

### 1.4 The CPS fee model: aligning interests with dealers

Starting in 2023, Dongchedi fully switched its dealer-partnership model from CPT (pay-per-time) to CPS (pay-per-sale):

- As of the latest data, the number of dealers on the CPS model has surpassed **28,500**, covering more than 110 brands
- 57% of merchants saw transaction volume grow year-over-year versus 2023
- Per-dealer efficiency under CPS is up 20% compared with the CPT model
- Under this model, Dongchedi only earns revenue when a transaction is actually completed, which forces the platform to improve lead quality rather than chase lead quantity

## II. Scale and Financial Performance of the Used-Car Business

### 2.1 Known data

Dongchedi (and ByteDance) has never publicly disclosed specific transaction-volume or revenue figures for its used-car business. However, it can be estimated indirectly:

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Platform vehicle inventory | 300,000+ units |
| Partner dealers | ~30,000 |
| Cities covered | 350+ |
| Nationwide Purchase share | ~40% of transactions |
| Consumption driven by trade-in programs | 380 billion yuan (including new cars, full-year 2025) |
| Douyin auto redemption orders | 670,000 units (2025, including new cars) |
| Dongchedi's overall valuation | $3 billion (~21.8 billion yuan) |

### 2.2 Scale estimation

In 2025, national used-car transaction volume reached 20.1 million units, with a transaction value of 1,289.8 billion yuan. The market is highly fragmented, with a CR5 of only 0.26%. Assuming Dongchedi's used-car platform facilitates 1%-2% of national volume (roughly 200,000-400,000 units), and using an average price of 60,000-70,000 yuan, platform GMV would be in the range of 12-28 billion yuan. At a service fee of roughly 2,000 yuan per vehicle, corresponding revenue would be approximately 400-800 million yuan.

**It should be emphasized** that the above is a rough estimate, and actual figures could deviate significantly. The share of used cars within Dongchedi's overall revenue remains unclear, but it is very likely still much smaller than new-car lead-generation and advertising revenue.

### 2.3 Relationship to Dongchedi's overall valuation

Dongchedi completed roughly a $600 million financing round in 2024, at a valuation of about $3 billion (21.8 billion yuan). Benchmarked against Autohome (full-year 2025 revenue of 6.45 billion yuan), Dongchedi's revenue scale is expected to be in the range of 5-7 billion yuan, of which:
- New-car CPS leads and advertising revenue remain the core (estimated at 70%-80%)
- Used-car transaction-service revenue is an incremental contributor (estimated at 10%-15%)
- Aftermarket-service revenue (e.g., car care) is still at an early stage (estimated share is extremely small)

## III. Competitive Landscape: Dongchedi vs. Guazi vs. Youxin

### 3.1 Comparison of five major platforms

| Platform | Model | Scale characteristics | Differentiation |
|------|------|----------|--------|
| **Guazi Used Cars** | B2B2C, curated stores | Covers 200+ cities, tens of thousands of active dealers | Industry No. 1, strongest brand recognition, high cross-provincial circulation rate |
| **Dongchedi** | Platform matchmaking + light self-operation | 3 directly operated + ~90 franchise stores | Douyin traffic ecosystem, Nationwide Purchase model |
| **Youxin (Uxin)** | Offline superstore | Q4 2025 retail transaction volume of 19,160 units | Asset-heavy expansion, revenue growth of 101% |
| **Youliang Tech** | B2B auction platform | Covers 30+ provinces, 29,000+ auctions | Founded 2023, growth rate of 90%+ |
| **Aoconda** | Offline premium used cars | 4 directly operated large-format retail centers | World's largest used-car showroom, repurchase rate of 60%+ |

### 3.2 The essential difference between Dongchedi and Guazi

- **Guazi**: follows an **asset-heavy operating path**, building its own inspection teams and a curated-store system, requiring 50%-80% of the vehicle price to be prepaid to lock in inventory — creating significant cash-flow pressure, but resulting in a high degree of service standardization.
- **Dongchedi**: follows a **light-platform + traffic path**, does not lock up inventory, and does not bear vehicle risk; its core weapon is the ByteDance-linked traffic from Douyin. Its weakness is weaker control over vehicle condition and the transaction experience compared with Guazi.

In short: Guazi leads on "going heavy," while Dongchedi has the edge on "staying light." The two are currently not in direct head-on competition; it is more that they are dividing the market along different dimensions.

### 3.3 Assessment of the competitive dynamic

The core tension in the used-car market is between **"scaling up" and "building trust."** With market CR5 at just 0.26%, the market is extremely fragmented, indicating that no platform has truly solved the challenge of cross-regional, standardized transactions yet.

Dongchedi's opportunity lies in this: ByteDance's traffic advantage can substantially lower customer-acquisition costs, and Douyin's livestreaming/short-video format is naturally suited to showcasing a "seeing is believing" category like used cars. But the challenges are equally clear: used cars are a classic "non-standardized" product — every vehicle's condition differs — and pure online content cannot fully replace an in-person inspection; the platform's grip on vehicle condition remains a soft spot.

## IV. Automotive Aftermarket Layout: Dongchedi Yangche (Car Care)

### 4.1 Development timeline

| Time | Event |
|------|------|
| December 2023 | Launched the "Dongdong Yangche" brand; first store opened in Chongqing |
| Early 2024 | Chongqing pilot operations, with roughly 27 stores rolled out |
| August 2024 | Brand upgraded to "Dongchedi Yangche," officially opening nationwide franchising |
| Second half of 2024 | First Shanghai store opened, marking expansion beyond the Chongqing testbed |
| 2025-present | Nationwide franchise expansion phase; specific total store count not disclosed |

### 4.2 Franchise model in detail

| Item | Detail |
|------|------|
| Franchise fee | 40,000 yuan/year |
| Security deposit | 10,000 yuan |
| Management service fee | 8,000 yuan/month |
| Store requirements | 200+ square meters, street-facing, at least 2 maintenance/repair bays |
| Business-license requirements | Must include automotive maintenance and repair items |

### 4.3 Traffic sources: four channels

The core selling point of Dongchedi Yangche is directing four streams of traffic to franchise stores:

1. **Dongchedi app installation orders**: users buy parts on the app and designate a store for installation
2. **Douyin local-services orders**: redeemed in-store via Douyin group-buy vouchers
3. **Douyin e-commerce installation orders**: installation services bundled with auto products sold through e-commerce channels
4. **B2B key-account orders**: business from local car-rental companies and insurers' accident-vehicle repairs

The flagship packages are a 198-yuan maintenance package and a 68-yuan deep-clean package, pursuing a value-for-money strategy.

### 4.4 The gap versus the "Cat-Tiger-Dog" trio

| Metric | Tuhu Car Care | Tmall Car Care | JD Car Care | Dongchedi Yangche |
|------|----------|----------|----------|------------|
| Number of stores | 6,311 | 2,500+ | 2,200 | Not disclosed (estimated in the low hundreds) |
| Model | Direct-operated + deep franchising | Deep franchising | Deep franchising | Franchise + certification |
| Supply chain | Self-built warehousing and logistics | Alibaba supply chain | JD supply chain | Still under construction |
| Core moat | Standardized service + brand trust | E-commerce ecosystem | E-commerce + logistics | Douyin traffic |

**The reality gap**: Tuhu Car Care alone has 6,311 workshop stores, putting it one to two orders of magnitude ahead of Dongchedi Yangche in store count. The aftermarket is a "service-heavy, operations-heavy, slow business" — one that cannot be caught up to quickly simply by pouring in traffic.

### 4.5 Car-care features and user engagement

Within the Dongchedi app, "car care"-related features mainly include:
- **Car-care cost calculator**: users can look up estimated annual maintenance, insurance, and fuel/electricity costs by vehicle model
- **Maintenance knowledge content**: large volumes of short-video and image/text content teaching users about maintenance schedules and precautions
- **Online appointment booking**: connects to partner stores offline, offering booking for maintenance, car washes, and similar services
- **Parts mall**: online sales of common maintenance parts such as engine oil and filters

In terms of user engagement, Dongchedi's overall DAU surpassed 10 million (as of August 2025), but the car-care section, as a low-frequency utility feature, sees far less usage than core scenarios like browsing and choosing a car. The car-care section functions mainly to **support user retention and round out the ecosystem** rather than serve as an independent traffic entry point.

## V. Auto-Finance Referral Business

### 5.1 Current status: a restrained layout, not yet rolled out at scale

Contrary to expectations, Dongchedi's layout in auto finance is relatively **restrained and cautious**:

- **No self-built financial license**: Dongchedi has not acquired a microloan or consumer-finance license the way Ant Group has
- **Mainly a referral model**: installment purchases, loans, and similar demand on the platform are mostly completed through the dealer side; Dongchedi itself plays more of an information-display and content-education role
- **Partners undisclosed**: no search results reveal any officially disclosed strategic partnership between Dongchedi and a specific bank or auto-finance company
- **Insurance business**: the B2B orders under Dongchedi Yangche include accident-vehicle repair work for insurance companies, but this is a store-level partnership, not a platform-level insurance-referral business

### 5.2 Why the restraint?

- **Regulatory risk**: since 2021, internet-finance regulation has tightened, and ByteDance has consistently maintained a cautious stance on financial businesses
- **IPO considerations**: Dongchedi is preparing for an IPO, and the regulatory complexity of a finance business could drag down the listing process
- **Core capability mismatch**: Dongchedi's core strength lies in content and traffic, and financial risk control is not its area of expertise

### 5.3 Future possibilities

If Dongchedi successfully goes public and achieves stable profitability, auto-finance referrals (installment-purchase recommendations, insurance price comparison, extended-warranty product sales) would be a natural extension. Referencing Autohome's experience, commission revenue from finance referrals can reach 5%-10% of overall revenue. But in the short term (1-2 years), this will not become a major revenue source.

## VI. Comparison with Autohome's Aftermarket Layout

### 6.1 Autohome's layout

| Business segment | Autohome | Dongchedi |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Used cars** | Runs C2B auctions via "Tiantian Paiche," connecting more than 20,000 buyers, with average daily listings exceeding 8,000 units, covering 300+ city outlets | Platform matchmaking + Nationwide Purchase, 300,000+ vehicles listed, 30,000 dealers |
| **Aftermarket services** | Inspection/appraisal, maintenance/repair, and finance form a closed service loop, in partnership with 9 inspection agencies, having completed 500,000+ standardized inspections | Dongchedi Yangche is in the early franchise-expansion stage, with store count in the low hundreds |
| **Finance and insurance** | Has a mature finance-referral system, one of its important revenue sources | Nearly blank, a restrained layout |
| **Offline events** | Held 5,000+ offline auto shows and group-buy events in 2025 | Primarily online, relatively few offline events |
| **Technology enablement** | AI Car-Inspection Expert, AI Vehicle-Listing Smart Control matrix, etc. | Digital inspection standards, smart recommendations |

### 6.2 Core differences

- **Autohome**: follows a **full-lifecycle service-platform** path, forming a complete loop from browsing and buying a car (new + used) to using it (maintenance, repair, insurance) and trading up. Through its acquisition of Tiantian Paiche, it gained real operating capability in used-car transactions. Full-year 2025 revenue was 6.45 billion yuan; although down year-over-year, its business model is mature.
- **Dongchedi**: follows a **content-and-traffic monetization** path, with its core asset being its traffic advantage from the Douyin ecosystem. Its aftermarket layout is more about "rounding out the ecosystem" than being a "core profit center," and it still lags noticeably behind Autohome's depth of full-lifecycle service.

### 6.3 Each side has its own weak spot

- Autohome's pain point: **traffic growth has peaked**. Media-services revenue share fell from 28.29% in 2022 to 16.24% in 2025, as Dongchedi continues to eat into users' content time.
- Dongchedi's pain point: **insufficient monetization depth**. Traffic is large but the conversion chain is short, and aftermarket-service capability is weak, meaning it cannot monetize repeatedly across the full vehicle-usage lifecycle the way Autohome can.

## VII. Assessing the Possibility of a "Second Growth Curve"

### 7.1 Can used cars become the second growth curve?

**Short term (1-2 years): unlikely to become an independent curve, but growth is sizable**

- Positive factors: in 2025, national used-car transactions surpassed 20 million units for the first time, and the market is still growing; Dongchedi's Nationwide Purchase model has a differentiated advantage; Douyin livestreaming is naturally suited to showcasing used cars
- Constraining factors: the used-car market is extremely fragmented (CR5 of only 0.26%), and online penetration remains low; its non-standardized nature dictates slow scaling; the roughly 2,000-yuan service fee per vehicle leaves limited profit margin
- Forecast: used cars will contribute stable incremental revenue (estimated at 400-800 million yuan), but in the short term this will not be enough to rival new-car lead-generation revenue (estimated at 4 billion+ yuan)

**Medium term (3-5 years): has the potential to become a genuine second curve**

- If Dongchedi can achieve breakthroughs in the following areas, used cars could become a true second curve:
  - Make the Nationwide Purchase delivery experience an industry benchmark
  - Continue expanding the user base through the Douyin ecosystem
  - Build brand trust around inspection standards and after-sales guarantees
  - Layer on high-margin services such as financing installments

### 7.2 Can the aftermarket become the second growth curve?

**Current assessment: too early to tell**

- Dongchedi Yangche's store count lags far behind Tuhu (6,311 stores) and Tmall Car Care (2,500+ stores)
- The aftermarket is a classic "service-heavy, operations-heavy" business, where internet-traffic advantages fade substantially at the offline service-delivery stage
- Under the fee structure of a 40,000-yuan/year franchise fee plus an 8,000-yuan/month management fee, store-level profitability is under pressure, constraining expansion speed
- The car-care business is more likely positioned as an **ecosystem-completion and user-retention tool** rather than an independent profit center

### 7.3 Overall assessment

Dongchedi's growth curve is more likely to be a **combined upgrade of "new-car CPS + used-car transaction services + content-ecosystem monetization"** rather than any single business becoming an independent second curve. Specifically:

1. **Primary engine**: transaction commissions under the new-car CPS model (core cash flow)
2. **Growth engine**: transaction-volume growth driven by used-car Nationwide Purchase + Douyin synergy
3. **Ecosystem completion**: car-care services, finance referrals, etc., raising user LTV
4. **Future optionality**: post-IPO capital operations to accelerate the aftermarket layout

## VIII. Risks and Uncertainties

### 8.1 Macro risks
- Rising new-energy-vehicle penetration and unstable residual values for used new-energy vehicles are affecting confidence in the used-car market
- In 2025, used-car profits "bottomed out," putting operating pressure on dealers, which could affect platform activity

### 8.2 Competitive risks
- Competitors such as Guazi and Youxin continue to invest, and the market has not yet settled into a stable structure
- In the aftermarket space, competitors such as Tuhu, Tmall Car Care, and JD Car Care have been deeply entrenched for years, making catch-up difficult

### 8.3 Model risks
- The light-platform model has limited control; a major after-sales dispute could damage the brand
- Under the CPS model, dealers may bypass the platform to complete "private transactions," leaking away commission
- The car-care franchise model faces an inherent tension between "service standardization" and "franchisee interests"

### 8.4 IPO uncertainty
- Dongchedi's IPO process and valuation will affect its subsequent capital-investment capacity
- Post-listing profitability pressure could limit strategic loss-making investment in used cars and the aftermarket business

---

## IX. Core Conclusions

1. **The used-car business model is clear but limited in scale**: Dongchedi is pursuing a light-model path of "platform matchmaking + Nationwide Purchase," building differentiation on its Douyin traffic advantage, but it still has a long way to go before becoming an industry leader.

2. **The aftermarket layout is at a "trial" stage**: Dongchedi Yangche's store count lags the "Cat-Tiger-Dog" trio by one to two orders of magnitude; the aftermarket is a slow business, and the traffic advantage is heavily diluted in offline service delivery.

3. **Finance referrals are nearly blank**: due to regulatory and IPO considerations, Dongchedi is keeping its finance business restrained, and it will not become a revenue contributor in the short term.

4. **Compared with Autohome, monetization depth falls short**: Dongchedi's user base and traffic have caught up to, or even surpassed, Autohome's, but it still lags noticeably in monetization capability across the full vehicle-usage lifecycle.

5. **Forming a second growth curve will take time**: used cars have potential but need 3-5 years of accumulation, while the aftermarket is even more of a long-term investment direction. In the short term, Dongchedi's growth will continue to depend mainly on deepening the new-car CPS model and the ongoing empowerment of the Douyin ecosystem.

6. **The variable most worth watching**: the effect of Douyin livestreaming in rebuilding the used-car trust chain — if short video and livestreaming can genuinely and significantly reduce information asymmetry in used cars, Dongchedi could carve out a path in the used-car space that is entirely different from Guazi's.
