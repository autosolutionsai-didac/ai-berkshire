# Dongchedi New Energy Vehicle Business Deep Dive

> Research date: May 2026
> Analytical dimensions: new-energy transition opportunity, channel transformation, content ecosystem, competitive landscape

## 1. Share of New Energy Vehicles in Dongchedi's Content and Transaction Mix

### 1.1 Market backdrop: new energy has become the absolute mainstream

China's new-energy-vehicle penetration rate reached 53.9% for full-year 2025, the first time the annual penetration rate has surpassed that of gasoline vehicles. By April 2026, new-energy penetration had historically broken through 60%; in Dongchedi's national passenger-vehicle retail sales Top 10 ranking, only one gasoline model remained, with the other nine spots taken by new-energy vehicles.

The China Association of Automobile Manufacturers (CAAM) forecasts that 2026 new-energy vehicle sales will reach 19 million units, with the share of total vehicle sales continuing to climb.

### 1.2 The new-energy shift in Dongchedi's content structure

According to multiple reports and platform data published by Dongchedi:

- **User intent shift**: Dongchedi's DAU keeps growing, and the platform has amassed a large, high-intensity car-buying user base, within which the share of users with new-energy purchase intent is already high. Female users are more inclined to accept new-energy vehicles (especially battery-electric), and new-energy users are more concentrated in higher-tier cities.
- **Content ecosystem center of gravity shifting**: Interest in new-energy-related content has surged — search interest for specialized terms such as in-car infotainment systems, charging, and driver-assistance rose 27% year over year. Dongchedi has explicitly designated new energy as the core direction for content support.
- **Changing creator mix**: Non-traditional automotive creators from technology, finance, and digital-product backgrounds continue to move into new-energy content, and many new-energy vehicle owners have converted their identity from user to KOC (Key Opinion Consumer).
- **Transaction dimension**: Dongchedi's trade-in services drove nearly 380 billion yuan in auto-related consumption (per the 2025 Corporate Social Responsibility Report), with new energy as the core category driving trade-ins.

**Key judgment**: Although Dongchedi has not publicly disclosed a precise figure for new energy's share of content, user-behavior data, content search intensity, and the structure of the sales ranking all suggest that new-energy content likely already exceeds 50% of the mix and is still rising rapidly — highly consistent with the overall market penetration trend.

### 1.3 User behavior differences: gasoline-vehicle users vs. new-energy users

A user-behavior report Dongchedi published in April 2025 reveals a notable divergence:

| Dimension | Domestic gasoline-vehicle users | New-energy users |
|------|------------|-----------|
| App dependency | Higher (stronger tool utility) | Relatively lower |
| Content consumption | Mainly vehicle selection | More immersive content consumption and car-owner community socializing |
| Price-band concentration | 50,000-150,000 yuan accounts for 82% | 50,000-150,000 yuan accounts for 58.1% (more dispersed) |
| User profile | Skews toward younger, unmarried users | More concentrated in higher-tier cities |

**What this means**: New-energy users are somewhat less dependent on Dongchedi as a "tool-type" platform than gasoline-vehicle users. New-energy users may rely more on official brand channels, social media, and other sources for information. This raises the bar for Dongchedi's content strategy — it needs to upgrade from a purely tool-type platform to an integrated "content + social + transaction" ecosystem.

## 2. New-Energy Automakers' Advertising Spend and Cooperation with Dongchedi

### 2.1 Relationships between major new-energy automakers and Dongchedi

**BYD**: The relationship has been rocky. When Dongchedi pushed the switch to a CPS (cost-per-sale) model in 2023, causing lead-generation fees to rise sharply (dealers reported increases of three to four times), BYD issued an internal memo instructing all its stores to stop purchasing Dongchedi annual contracts. This was a major blow to Dongchedi's business model — BYD is China's largest new-energy automaker, and its stance carries industry bellwether significance. The relationship has since been partly repaired, but the episode exposed Dongchedi's weak bargaining position vis-à-vis leading automakers.

**Huawei/Harmony Intelligent Mobility (HIMA)**: Following the 2023 winter-test controversy, Huawei suspended cooperation with all three major platforms — Dongchedi, Autohome, and Yiche — triggering shockwaves across the industry. In the July 2025 driver-assistance tests, HIMA responded to Dongchedi's test results with "no comment," and the relationship remains sensitive.

**NIO/Xpeng/Li Auto/Xiaomi**: These brands all participated in various Dongchedi test programs (winter testing, driver-assistance testing, etc.), but Xiaomi's SU7 drew controversy for its poor showing in the 2025 driver-assistance test. Overall, the new-force automakers maintain a cooperative stance toward Dongchedi, though they sometimes hold reservations about the test conclusions.

**Tesla**: In Dongchedi's 2025 driver-assistance test, Tesla's Model 3 and Model X took the top two spots in the overall ranking using a pure-vision approach, and Elon Musk personally reposted the results. This gave a boost to the international visibility of Dongchedi's testing.

### 2.2 Dongchedi's cooperation models with automakers

1. **Lead/traffic cooperation**: Automakers and dealers purchase sales leads from Dongchedi — this is Dongchedi's core revenue source.
2. **Brand advertising**: splash-screen ads, feed ads, branded zones, etc.
3. **Content cooperation**: including providing vehicles for Dongchedi's test programs, joint reviews, etc.
4. **Influencer marketing**: through the "Dongchedi Star Selection" product, running influencer marketing campaigns across both Dongchedi and Douyin.
5. **Trade-in policy implementation**: as an officially authorized platform, Dongchedi provides subsidy-application services for automakers.

### 2.3 Key tension: review independence vs. commercial cooperation

Dongchedi faces a structural conflict:

- **As a content platform**: it needs independent, rigorous testing (winter testing, driver-assistance testing) to build credibility and user trust
- **As a commercial platform**: it needs to maintain cooperative relationships with automakers to secure advertising and lead revenue

The 2023 winter test drew challenges from Huawei and other automakers, and the 2025 driver-assistance test sparked controversy again. This shows how difficult it is for Dongchedi to balance "being the referee" and "being in business." Automakers sometimes use suspension of cooperation as a pressure tactic.

## 3. Impact of Direct-Sales vs. Dealer Models on Dongchedi's Lead-Generation Business

### 3.1 Industry channel shift: direct sales cooling off, dealers making a comeback

In Q4 2025, new-energy automakers' channel models underwent a concentrated transformation:

- **Denza**: in core markets such as Hebei, Henan, Zhejiang, and Guangdong, is closing direct-sales stores or transferring them to dealers
- **HIMA**: launched a broad rollback of its direct-sales network, with channel adjustments fully implemented in provinces including Guangdong, Sichuan, and Jiangsu
- **Li Auto, NIO**: previously staunch defenders of direct sales, have also begun exploring more cost-effective channel-optimization paths

**Core driving factor**: annual rent for a direct-sales showroom in a core downtown commercial district of a first-tier city runs into the millions of yuan, and combined with staffing, utilities, and property costs, annual operating cost per store reaches 5-6 million yuan. For an automaker with a 200-store network, just opening and operating stores requires roughly 1 billion yuan in annual investment. In an environment of sustained margin pressure, this burden is unsustainable.

The industry is accelerating its shift toward a hybrid "direct sales + franchise/authorized dealer" model: direct sales retain the brand-showcase function in core first-tier commercial districts, while franchise and authorized channels handle rapid penetration into third- and fourth-tier cities and county markets.

### 3.2 Impact on Dongchedi's lead-generation business

**Positive factors**:

1. **Dealer comeback expands lead demand**: under the direct-sales model, automakers build their own lead-generation systems and rely less on third-party platforms; under the dealer model, dealers naturally have stronger demand for external leads. New-energy automakers returning to the dealer system means more stores will need to purchase Dongchedi leads.
2. **Lower-tier market expansion**: auto sales volume and growth rate in lower-tier towns and cities far exceed those in first- and second-tier cities, and the dealer model is better suited to covering lower-tier markets, which expands Dongchedi's potential pool of lead customers.
3. **CPS model better aligned with dealer interests**: after adjustment and optimization of Dongchedi's CPS (cost-per-sale) model, 2024 data showed that 57% of partner dealers saw positive growth in completed sales, and 94.8% of dealers were willing to continue the partnership.

**Risk factors**:

1. **Leading automakers building their own closed-loop traffic**: BYD, Li Auto, and other leading automakers are building their own customer-acquisition channels through founder IP and branded social media, reducing reliance on Dongchedi. Auto magnates such as Li Bin, Li Xiang, and Lei Jun have built personal IPs, making good use of Douyin, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Channels, and other platforms to acquire customers directly.
2. **Lead-pricing standoff**: the CPS model switch previously triggered resistance from major clients such as BYD. As Dongchedi raises per-lead prices, it faces the risk of client attrition.
3. **Trend toward online purchasing of new-energy vehicles**: some new-energy brands support direct online ordering, reducing reliance on the traditional lead chain of "capture lead - follow up - showroom visit."

**Net-impact assessment**: In the near term, the shift from direct sales back to dealers is a positive for Dongchedi's lead-generation business. But over the medium-to-long term, the trend of leading automakers building their own traffic systems is irreversible; Dongchedi needs to upgrade from "selling leads" to "helping close deals" to sustain the value of its lead-generation business.

## 4. The Influence of Dongchedi's Winter Testing, Range Testing, and Other New-Energy Review Content

### 4.1 Winter testing: an upgrade path from domestic to global

**2023 winter test (a year of controversy)**

- The AITO M7 EV (range-extended) achieved only a 31.6% range-attainment rate, ranking last and prompting Huawei's Yu Chengdong to publicly challenge the results
- Several Geely and Great Wall models achieved range-attainment rates below 40%
- AITO officially pointed out that before testing, the vehicle underwent 71 minutes of heater operation with more than ten instances of doors and windows being opened and closed, a serious departure from real-world usage scenarios
- Huawei subsequently suspended cooperation with the three major auto vertical platforms

**2025 global winter test (a year of upgrading)**

- Upgraded to a global test: conducted in Heihe, China; Alaska, United States; and Umeå, Sweden
- Covered nearly 40 brands and more than 90 vehicles
- Test categories expanded to basic driving, active safety, extreme-range, and timed rally events
- Cumulative mileage of nearly 100,000 kilometers
- Received attention and coverage from CCTV, and the international scope enhanced brand influence

### 4.2 Driver-assistance safety testing: the most influential review event of 2025

In July 2025, Dongchedi partnered with CCTV News to close off 15 kilometers of real highway and urban roads for a simulated driver-assistance safety test covering 36 mainstream models:

- **Highway scenarios**: 6 scenario types, 36 vehicles, 183 tests, 44 passed — **pass rate of only 24%**
- **Urban scenarios**: 9 scenario types, 26 vehicles, 233 tests, 103 passed — **pass rate of 44.2%**
- **Overall ranking**: Tesla Model 3 first, Model X second, followed by Lantu (Voyah) Dreamer, Xpeng G6, and AITO M9 in third through fifth place

This test triggered enormous public response:
- Elon Musk personally reposted it and expressed approval
- HIMA responded with "no comment"
- The Ministry of Public Security's Traffic Management Bureau took the opportunity to clearly state that all current "smart driving" systems are driver-assistance, not autonomous driving
- Some users and automakers questioned how representative the extreme-scenario testing was

### 4.3 Assessment of review influence

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Public attention | Extremely high — trended multiple times, and Musk's repost boosted international visibility |
| Industry influence | High, but also highly controversial, with several automakers challenging the methodology |
| Policy influence | Some effect — helped push the Ministry of Public Security to clarify its position on driver-assistance systems |
| User trust | Divided — some users recognize its independence, while others believe the test standards are unfair |
| Competitive moat | High — winter testing and driver-assistance testing have become signature IP content for Dongchedi |

**Key judgment**: Winter testing and driver-assistance testing have become Dongchedi's most important content IP and brand asset. They generate enormous traffic and buzz, but also strain relationships with automakers. For a pre-IPO Dongchedi, this review content is a "killer feature" for demonstrating platform value, but it also needs continuous improvement in methodological rigor and transparency to maintain credibility.

## 5. Channel Allocation of New-Energy Automakers' Marketing Budgets

### 5.1 Overview of industry marketing spend

In the Q1 2025 ranking of Chinese automakers' selling expenses, Xiaomi surpassed BYD to take first place, reflecting the aggressive brand-building investment of new entrants. Marketing strategies diverge significantly across automakers:

- **Li Auto**: claims the industry's lowest marketing-expense ratio, close to Tesla's level, relying on founder IP and word-of-mouth.
- **Xiaomi**: a high-investment strategy, leveraging Lei Jun's personal influence combined with saturation marketing across multiple channels.
- **NIO/Xpeng**: marketing-expense ratios remain elevated, but show a clear downward trend compared with before.
- **BYD**: large sales volume but relatively restrained marketing spend, relying more on product strength and price competitiveness.

### 5.2 Structural shifts in channel-spend allocation

**The traditional landscape is being upended**:

Traditionally, Autohome, Dongchedi, and Yiche — the three major vertical platforms — were the core channels for automakers' online marketing. But this landscape is being disrupted:

1. **Rise of Douyin/short video**: auto magnates (Li Bin, Li Xiang, Lei Jun, Yu Chengdong) have built personal IPs, acquiring customers directly on Douyin. Automakers' official brand accounts have also become important channels for distribution and customer acquisition.
2. **Xiaohongshu (RedNote) content seeding**: growing influence among female users and younger demographics, making it an important advertising battleground for new-energy automakers (especially mid-to-high-end brands).
3. **WeChat Channels/WeChat ecosystem**: private-domain operations and community-driven referral growth have become important ways for automakers to lower customer-acquisition costs.
4. **Brands' own channels**: increased investment in automakers' own apps, mini-programs, offline events, and other proprietary channels.

**Pressure on vertical platforms**:

Automakers and dealers have already begun shifting budgets from vertical platforms such as Autohome and Dongchedi toward "new-force" channels like Douyin and Xiaohongshu. While reducing reliance on vertical platforms, automakers are achieving large-scale acquisition and rapid conversion of their own sales leads.

### 5.3 How much share can Dongchedi capture?

Dongchedi has not publicly disclosed specific advertising revenue figures. Inferring from valuation and financing information:

- Raised roughly $600 million in financing in 2024, at a valuation of roughly $3 billion
- IPO rumored for 2026, targeting a raise of $1.0-1.5 billion
- As of August 2025, DAU surpassed 10 million, covering more than 30,000 dealers and 110+ brands

**Dongchedi's unique advantage**:

Backed by ByteDance, Dongchedi has a unique capability for cross-linking with Douyin. Data show that after users' interest is sparked on Douyin, about 84% go on to jump to Dongchedi to browse its vehicle database, and 78% search for specific model series and read in-depth reviews. This chain of "interest sparked (Douyin) -> in-depth decision-making (Dongchedi) -> transaction conversion" is something neither Autohome nor Yiche has.

**Share estimate**: Within new-energy automakers' digital marketing budgets, Dongchedi (including its combined placement with Douyin) may capture a 15%-25% share, though this proportion faces ongoing pressure from brands' self-built channels and emerging platforms.

## 6. Whether Intelligent (Driver-Assistance/Cockpit) Content Has Become a New Growth Driver

### 6.1 Driver-assistance reviews: already Dongchedi's biggest content growth driver in 2025

The July 2025 real-world driver-assistance safety test was one of the most influential content events in Dongchedi's history:

- Co-produced with CCTV News, boosting authority
- Testing covered 36 vehicles, 15 high-risk scenario types, and hundreds of simulated collisions
- Elon Musk personally reposted it, drawing global attention
- Enormous exposure across the network

Dongchedi has explicitly stated it will "increase investment in intelligent-feature and safety real-world testing" and has launched the "Million Owners Review Program," making intelligent-feature content a core direction for platform differentiation.

### 6.2 AI-powered platform features: from tool to ecosystem

**2025: AI vehicle-selection launched**

Dongchedi unveiled its AI panorama for the first time, based on the platform's 50-million-plus structured data points, a database of more than 76,000 models, and real-world test data on more than 4,000 models (exceeding 1 million data points), building a proprietary AI large model to power an AI agent spanning selecting, comparing, buying, and pricing vehicles.

**2026 Beijing Auto Show: debut of "AI Xiaodong"**

At the 2026 Beijing Auto Show, Dongchedi formally launched its AI assistant "AI Xiaodong," upgrading from the single scenario of "choosing a car" to five major aspects of auto life: choosing, buying, selling, using, and enjoying. Core capabilities include:
- High-precision intelligent interaction
- A dedicated automotive knowledge base
- Deep multi-turn dialogue
- Integration of massive volumes of genuine user reviews

**Technology collaboration with Volcano Engine**: Dongchedi is partnering with ByteDance's Volcano Engine to build a smarter automotive content ecosystem.

### 6.3 Assessment of the growth driver

Intelligent-feature content is indeed a new growth driver for Dongchedi, but two levels need to be distinguished:

1. **Content dimension**: content such as driver-assistance reviews brings substantial traffic and buzz but is hard to monetize directly, and may alienate advertising clients (automakers)
2. **Product dimension**: intelligent features such as AI vehicle-selection and AI Xiaodong improve user experience and platform stickiness, helping extend user lifetime value

**Potential risk**: the controversy surrounding driver-assistance reviews may cause some automakers to reduce their ad spend. Dongchedi needs to strike a balance between content influence and commercial interests.

## 7. Layout of Charging-Station/Energy-Replenishment Related Services

### 7.1 Dongchedi's charging platform

Dongchedi has launched a public charging service platform, with the following footprint:

- **Chongqing pilot**: through market-based cooperation with the "Yuyi Charge" app for interoperability, covering more than 1,000 charging stations, more than 9,000 charging piles, and nearly 14,000 charging connectors in Chongqing
- **National expansion**: the service has expanded to 324 cities across 31 provinces, covering mainstream charging brands
- **Functionality**: real-time queries for charging-station distance, price, parking fees, and other information, supporting one-scan access and cross-platform use

### 7.2 Positioning and strategy

For energy-replenishment services, Dongchedi has adopted an **asset-light aggregation model** — rather than building its own charging piles, it aggregates third-party charging services to provide users with a one-stop query and payment experience. This is consistent with its positioning as an "auto-life platform."

### 7.3 Assessment of business value

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Strategic value | High. Extends the user service chain from "buying a car" to "using a car," raising user stickiness and LTV |
| Revenue contribution | Currently small. The charging-aggregation business is fiercely competitive (Amap, Baidu Maps, and others are also in this space), with limited margin |
| Competitive moat | Low. Charging aggregation is essentially an information service with limited technical barriers |
| Synergy with core business | Moderate. Can provide an integrated experience for new-energy users, but has limited connection to the core lead-generation/advertising business |

**Judgment**: Charging services are one piece of Dongchedi's "new-energy ecosystem" puzzle, functioning more as a supplementary business that rounds out the user experience, and are unlikely to become a major revenue source in the near term. But as the installed base of new-energy vehicles continues to grow, the strategic value of this business will gradually become apparent.

## 8. Overall Assessment and Key Conclusions

### 8.1 Impact of new energy on Dongchedi: opportunities outweigh challenges, but structural risks remain

**Core opportunities**:
1. New-energy penetration has broken through 60% and is still rising, naturally benefiting Dongchedi's content and transaction ecosystem
2. New-energy automakers shifting from direct sales back to dealer models expands the customer base for the lead-generation business
3. New content IP such as driver-assistance reviews creates differentiated advantages and traffic-growth drivers
4. The Douyin + Dongchedi dual-platform linkage forms a unique closed loop of "interest sparked - in-depth decision-making - transaction conversion"

**Structural risks**:
1. Leading new-energy automakers (BYD, Li Auto, etc.) are building their own traffic systems, reducing reliance on third-party platforms
2. The conflict between review independence and commercial interests could intensify — automakers use suspension of cooperation as leverage
3. New-energy users are less dependent on the Dongchedi app than gasoline-vehicle users, posing a challenge to platform stickiness
4. Marketing budgets continue to shift from vertical platforms toward social media and brands' own channels

### 8.2 Dongchedi's differentiated moat

Compared with Autohome and Yiche, Dongchedi's core moats in the new-energy era include:

1. **ByteDance ecosystem synergy**: Douyin driving traffic -> Dongchedi converting it, with 84% of Douyin users interested in autos going on to Dongchedi
2. **Review content IP**: winter testing and driver-assistance testing have built strong brand recognition, with CCTV endorsement enhancing authority
3. **AI technology capability**: powered by Volcano Engine, products such as AI vehicle-selection and AI Xiaodong lead peers
4. **Data accumulation**: more than 50 million structured data points, with a real-world test database covering more than 4,000 models

### 8.3 Impact on IPO valuation

Dongchedi is preparing for a 2026 Hong Kong IPO, targeting a raise of $1.0-1.5 billion. The performance of its new-energy business will directly shape investors' judgment of its growth prospects:

- **Favorable factors**: continued growth in new-energy penetration provides an industry tailwind; the global winter test and driver-assistance reviews showcase content moats; the AI product suite demonstrates technological capability
- **Unfavorable factors**: volatile relationships with leading automakers add revenue uncertainty; the lead-generation business may face a pricing ceiling; new businesses such as charging contribute little in the near term

### 8.4 Key metrics worth continued tracking

1. The trend in the share of new-energy-intent users within Dongchedi's DAU
2. The state of repair in cooperative relationships between new-energy automakers (especially BYD and Huawei) and Dongchedi
3. Changes in dealer renewal rates and average order value under the CPS model
4. Exploration of monetization paths for content IP such as winter testing/driver-assistance testing
5. User penetration of AI vehicle-selection/AI Xiaodong and their effect on improving conversion efficiency
6. Growth in MAU and transaction volume for charging services
