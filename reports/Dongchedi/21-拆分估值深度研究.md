## I. Spin-off Timeline

### 1.1 Groundwork before the spin-off (2023)

Dongchedi was incubated by ByteDance in 2017, initially operating as the automotive channel within Toutiao. After roughly six years operating inside the ByteDance system, a gradual carve-out process began in 2023.

**July 2023**: Dongchedi announced a full integration of automotive content across Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video, with Dongchedi serving as the overall operator coordinating resources across all platforms. After the integration, the four platforms' automotive-interested daily active users reached 310 million. This step effectively established Dongchedi as the independently operated entity for ByteDance's automotive vertical business.

**December 8, 2023**: Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. was established, with founding shareholders He Jian (60%) and Zhao Jing (40%). This was the key step in the spin-off, setting up the shell company for the subsequent equity restructuring.

**December 21, 2023**: Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (Dongchedi's operating entity) completed a corporate registration change, with its majority shareholder switching from Toutiao Co., Ltd. to Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd., which now holds 100% of the equity. At this point, Toutiao formally exited Dongchedi's direct equity structure.

> **Sources**: [21jingji.com - Dongchedi's Breakthrough and Independent IPO](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20240612/herald/df8fd1fa06f2818bab38dda74ed322ea.html); [sfccn.com - Dongchedi's Breakthrough and Independent IPO](https://www.sfccn.com/2024/6-12/1NMDE0NDlfMTkyNTQ1Ng.html)

### 1.2 Independent operation takes effect (January 2024)

**January 2024**: ByteDance transferred Dongchedi-related employees to the newly established wholly-owned subsidiary, with the legal representative role held by Dongchedi's strategy lead. A small number of employees formerly under ByteDance had their labor relationships transferred to the new company by the end of January 2024. Dongchedi began independent accounting, formally separating from ByteDance's financial system.

> **Source**: [Jiemian News - Spinning Off Dongchedi, ByteDance May Land Its First IPO](https://www.jiemian.com/article/11291287.html)

### 1.3 Pre-IPO financing (June-October 2024)

- **June 2024**: The Information reported that ByteDance planned to raise $700-800 million for Dongchedi.
- **September 2024**: The financing size was adjusted to up to $600 million, with a valuation of nearly $3 billion (approximately 21.8 billion yuan).
- **Around October 2024**: The financing closed, with roughly $600 million raised at a post-money valuation of approximately $3.1 billion.

> **Sources**: [36Kr - ByteDance Spins Off Dongchedi](https://36kr.com/p/2806954496145032); [Sina Finance - Dongchedi Plans to Raise $600 Million](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/hkstock/hkzmt/2024-09-10/doc-incnrnkw9390722.shtml); [Tencent News - Dongchedi Raises $800 Million](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20241009A038XA00); [Caproasia](https://www.caproasia.com/2026/02/28/china-car-platform-beijing-dongchedi-technology-dcar-plans-hong-kong-ipo-in-2026-to-raise-1-5-billion-raised-600-million-at-3-1-billion-valuation-in-2024-founded-in-2017-by-tiktok-parent-byteda/)

### 1.4 IPO preparation (2026)

- **February 27, 2026**: Bloomberg reported that Dongchedi was considering launching a Hong Kong IPO as early as 2026, seeking to raise $1.0-1.5 billion.
- **March 20, 2026**: Bloomberg reported that Dongchedi had selected Citigroup and Goldman Sachs for the IPO.
- **As of June 2026**: No formal prospectus has yet been found filed with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. The IPO plan remains in progress, with the timeline and fundraising size still subject to change.

## II. Ownership Structure and Control Analysis

### 2.1 Corporate architecture after the spin-off

The post-spin-off Dongchedi forms a multi-layered nested VIE structure:

```
He Jian (60%) + Zhao Jing (40%)
    │
    ▼
Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. (established 2023.12.8)
    │ 100% ownership
    ▼
Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (operating entity)
    │ All equity pledged to ↓
    ▼
Beijing Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd.
    │ Look-through ownership ↓
    ▼
Hong Kong Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (formerly "Hong Kong Xingfuli Technology Co., Ltd.", established 2021)
    │ Look-through ↓
    ▼
ByteDance (offshore structure)
```

### 2.2 Analysis of the 60%/40% He Jian / Zhao Jing shareholding arrangement

**He Jian**: President of Dongchedi, previously head of R&D for Baidu's medical division before joining ByteDance; he is a core manager of Dongchedi.

**Zhao Jing**: Head of HR at Dongchedi (though some reports describe her as a veteran media professional — both accounts exist).

> **Speculation**: The shareholding arrangement held by He Jian and Zhao Jing at Xiamen Dongchezu is very likely a nominee-holding arrangement on behalf of ByteDance, rather than genuine personal ownership. Such arrangements are extremely common in the VIE structures of Chinese internet companies, primarily to satisfy Chinese legal restrictions on foreign investment in certain industries. He Jian, as the core member of management, takes on the role of nominal shareholder, while Zhao Jing, as HR lead, participates in the nominee arrangement — consistent with the design logic of ByteDance's other VIE structures (such as Douyin Group's VIE arrangements). The 60/40 split may reflect He Jian's dominant position as the business's top executive.

> **Sources**: [Sina Finance - Dongchedi's Independent IPO, What Is ByteDance Planning?](https://finance.sina.cn/2024-06-12/detail-inaynxwz4756629.d.html); [Rui Finance - ByteDance Reportedly Plans to Spin Off Dongchedi for IPO](https://m.rccaijing.com/news-7206602703454795570.html)

### 2.3 ByteDance's actual control

Through equity pledges and a multi-layer structure, ByteDance retains substantive control over Dongchedi:

1. **Equity pledge**: Xiamen Dongchezu has pledged all of Beijing Dongchedi's equity (registered capital of 10 million yuan) to Beijing Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd.
2. **Beijing Dongchezu look-through**: Beijing Dongchezu is wholly owned by Hong Kong Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd.
3. **Hong Kong entity**: Hong Kong Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. was formerly named "Hong Kong Xingfuli Technology Co., Ltd.", established in 2021, with Wang Xiao (formerly head of Xingfuli under Douyin Group) serving as director.

> **Speculation**: This "nominally independent, substantively controlled" arrangement is a classic VIE structure design. ByteDance achieves substantive control over Dongchedi's operations and finances through the chain of offshore entity (Hong Kong Dongchedi Technology) → Beijing Dongchezu → equity pledge → contractual control → Xiamen Dongchezu/Beijing Dongchedi. At IPO, investors will be purchasing shares of the offshore holding company, enjoying the economic benefits of the onshore operating entity via VIE agreements.

> **Source**: [21jingji.com - Dongchedi's Breakthrough and Independent IPO](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20240613/bd8acc0761b6c53b2685b8907898b972.html)

## III. Valuation Analysis at the Time of the Spin-off

### 3.1 Valuation within the ByteDance system before the spin-off

While operating within the ByteDance system, Dongchedi had no publicly disclosed independent valuation. However, it can be estimated along the following dimensions:

**Share of ByteDance's overall valuation**:

- ByteDance's overall secondary-market valuation (employee buyback price) was approximately $300-350 billion in 2024, and its latest valuation as of 2026 is approximately $550-600 billion.
- Dongchedi's $3 billion valuation represents only about 0.1% of ByteDance's total valuation.
- From ByteDance's perspective, Dongchedi is a non-core business (relative to Douyin/TikTok, Feishu, Volcano Engine, etc.), and the strategic logic of the spin-off is "divesting businesses no longer regarded as core."

> **Speculation**: Within ByteDance, Dongchedi's implied valuation may have been far below the $3 billion it achieved after becoming independent. The reasons: (1) as a vertical business line within ByteDance, Dongchedi's value was largely dependent on ByteDance's traffic ecosystem, and it may not have commanded an independent-valuation premium when accounted for on a standalone basis; (2) ByteDance had never conducted any external financing for Dongchedi before 2023, indicating it had not previously been treated as a standalone valuation subject.

### 3.2 The "zero consideration" arrangement at the time of the spin-off

Based on public information, in the December 2023 equity change, Toutiao transferred 100% of Beijing Dongchedi's equity to Xiamen Dongchezu, but the transaction consideration was not publicly disclosed.

> **Speculation**: Given that the shareholders of Xiamen Dongchezu — He Jian and Zhao Jing — are very likely nominee holders for ByteDance, this is in substance an internal reorganization by ByteDance rather than a genuine third-party transaction, and therefore may not have involved actual cash consideration. This is standard practice in building a VIE structure — establishing an entity controlled by domestic natural persons to satisfy compliance requirements.

## V. Valuation Reasonableness Analysis

### 5.1 PS multiple estimation

Dongchedi has never publicly disclosed specific revenue figures. The following is estimated based on industry benchmarking:

**Benchmark: Autohome (ATHM/2518.HK)**

| Metric | Autohome 2024 | Autohome 2025 |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Total revenue | 7.04 billion yuan | 6.45 billion yuan |
| Net profit | Approximately 2.05 billion yuan | Approximately 1.61 billion yuan (adjusted) |
| Market cap | Approximately $3.19 billion (end of 2024) | Approximately $2.05 billion (mid-2025) |
| PS multiple | Approximately 3.3x | Approximately 2.3x |

**Estimating Dongchedi's revenue**:

- Dongchedi's daily active users are approximately 70% of Autohome's (Q3 2023 average quarterly DAU of 10.04 million vs. Autohome's approximately 14 million).
- However, Dongchedi's user growth rate is faster (DAU up 57% over the past two years), and, backed by ByteDance's traffic ecosystem, it has broader reach.
- After the July 2023 integration, Dongchedi coordinates automotive content across Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video, reaching 310 million automotive-interested users daily.

> **Speculation**: Assuming Dongchedi's 2024 revenue falls in the 3-5 billion yuan range (referencing Autohome's 7 billion-plus scale and Dongchedi's market share), the PS multiple implied by the $3 billion valuation would be approximately:
>
> - Optimistic assumption (revenue of 5 billion yuan): PS ≈ 4.4x
> - Neutral assumption (revenue of 4 billion yuan): PS ≈ 5.5x
> - Conservative assumption (revenue of 3 billion yuan): PS ≈ 7.3x
>
> Compared with Autohome's PS of 3.3x at the end of 2024, Dongchedi's valuation carries a 1.3-2.2x premium, which can be explained by its higher growth rate, younger user base, and ByteDance's traffic ecosystem.

## VI. ByteDance's Economic Interest Arrangements

### 6.1 Control under the VIE structure

As described in the ownership structure section above, ByteDance retains substantive control through the following means:

1. **Equity pledge chain**: Xiamen Dongchezu → Beijing Dongchedi (all equity pledged) → Beijing Dongchezu → Hong Kong Dongchedi Technology → ByteDance's offshore structure
2. **Contractual control**: Contractual control over the onshore operating entity achieved through VIE agreements
3. **Management nominee holdings**: He Jian and Zhao Jing act as nominal shareholders, in practice following ByteDance's directives

### 6.2 Traffic cooperation and related-party transactions

The July 2023 automotive content integration is key to understanding the related-party transactions between Dongchedi and ByteDance:

- **Content integration**: Dongchedi coordinates operation of the automotive channels on Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video
- **Traffic support**: The automotive content on the four platforms reaches 310 million interested users daily, with more than 5.6 billion daily views
- **Creator ecosystem**: Covers more than 6.34 million automotive content creators

> **Speculation**: After becoming independent, Dongchedi very likely has the following related-party transaction arrangements with ByteDance (to be disclosed in the IPO prospectus):
>
> 1. **Traffic procurement agreement**: Dongchedi pays traffic fees to ByteDance to gain access to the automotive channel traffic entry points on Douyin, Toutiao, Xigua, and other platforms
> 2. **Technical services agreement**: ByteDance provides Dongchedi with recommendation algorithms, cloud services, technical infrastructure, and other support
> 3. **Content operations agreement**: As the unified operator of automotive content across the four platforms, Dongchedi has related revenue-sharing arrangements
> 4. **Brand licensing**: May involve licensing of brand or intellectual property usage
>
> The pricing of these related-party transactions will be a key focus of IPO review. If traffic fees are set too low, it is equivalent to ByteDance transferring profit to Dongchedi; if set too high, it would compress Dongchedi's profitability.

> **Sources**: [GMW.cn - Douyin Group's Automotive Content Ecosystem Fully Integrated](https://economy.gmw.cn/2023-07/24/content_36717540.htm); [Jiemian News - Dongchedi Prepares for Listing, ByteDance's Pilot Test](https://www.jiemian.com/article/11293651.html)

### 6.3 ByteDance's stake and returns from the IPO

> **Speculation**:
>
> - **Ownership stake**: Pre-IPO round investors collectively received approximately 19.4% of the shares, meaning ByteDance (through the VIE structure) still holds approximately 80.6% of the shares after the financing. If the IPO dilutes another 15-20% (based on raising $1.0-1.5 billion), ByteDance's final stake may be around 60-65%.
> - **Economic return**: Dongchedi was cultivated from scratch within the ByteDance system, with input costs primarily in labor and traffic; on an internal-cost basis, this may be on the order of several hundred million yuan. After becoming independent, at a $3 billion valuation, ByteDance's paper return could reach 10x or more.
> - **Lock-up period**: As the controlling shareholder, ByteDance's post-IPO lock-up period is typically 6-12 months, and it is unlikely to sell down significantly in the near term.

## VII. Comparison with Other Spin-off Cases

### 7.1 Precedents within the ByteDance system

Dongchedi is the first ByteDance family subsidiary to move toward an independent IPO. Previously, ByteDance's other business lines (such as Nuverse and Dali Education) were either shut down/scaled back or remain operating within the group. Dongchedi's spin-off has a "testing ground" nature — if successful, it could provide a template for the independent listing of other ByteDance businesses (such as Volcano Engine and Feishu).

### 7.2 Comparison with other Chinese internet company spin-off cases

| Case | Spinning-off party | Spun-off entity | Pre-spin-off valuation | IPO valuation | IPO premium |
|------|--------|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Dongchedi | ByteDance | Dongchedi | No independent valuation → $3 billion (Pre-IPO) | TBD (estimated $5-8 billion) | TBD |
| Meituan | Tencent ecosystem | Meituan-Dianping | — | Approximately $53 billion (2018 IPO) | Tencent was a strategic investor, not a direct spin-off |
| Kuaishou | Independent development | Kuaishou | Approximately $50 billion (Pre-IPO) | Approximately $140 billion (first day of 2021 IPO) | Approximately 2.8x |

> Note: The Meituan and Kuaishou cases are not fully comparable to Dongchedi's situation. Meituan was not spun off from Tencent — rather, Tencent participated as a strategic investor; Kuaishou also listed after developing independently. Dongchedi represents a purer "parent company incubation → spin-off → independent listing" path.

### 7.3 More comparable cases

Within the Chinese internet industry, more comparable spin-off cases include:

- **Alibaba group**: Alibaba Health, Alibaba Pictures, and others listed independently from the Alibaba system, typically with Alibaba retaining a controlling position
- **JD group**: JD Logistics, JD Health, Dada Group, and others listed after spinning off from JD
- **NetEase group**: Youdao listed independently after spinning off from NetEase

> **Common characteristics**: The parent company typically retains a controlling position after the IPO (holding more than 50%), maintains business synergy through related-party transactions, and the spun-off business's independent valuation is typically higher than its implied valuation within the group. Dongchedi's spin-off path is highly consistent with these cases.

## IX. Key Risks and Uncertainties

1. **Traffic dependency risk**: Dongchedi's core competitiveness derives largely from traffic support within the ByteDance system (310 million daily active automotive-interested users); if traffic cooperation terms change after the IPO, this would directly affect valuation.
2. **Related-party transaction pricing risk**: The pricing of traffic fees, technical service fees, and other arrangements between ByteDance and Dongchedi will be a focal point of IPO review and investor scrutiny.
3. **Unverified profitability**: Dongchedi has never publicly disclosed profitability data; against the backdrop of Autohome's two consecutive years of declining revenue and net profit, the sustainability of Dongchedi's profitability is questionable.
4. **Regulatory risk**: The compliance of the VIE structure and the reasonableness of related-party transactions could both affect the IPO process.
5. **Market environment risk**: The overall performance of the Hong Kong IPO market in 2026 will affect Dongchedi's offering pricing and market acceptance.

## X. Research Conclusions

### 10.1 Key findings

1. **The spin-off is in substance an internal reorganization**: The December 2023 equity change was part of ByteDance's construction of a VIE structure; He Jian and Zhao Jing are very likely nominee holders, and ByteDance retains substantive control through the multi-layer structure and equity pledges.

2. **The basis for the $3 billion valuation**: Primarily based on (a) the strategic value of ByteDance's traffic ecosystem, (b) the trend of automotive transactions moving online in China, and (c) benchmarking against Autohome's valuation while applying a growth premium. The PS multiple is approximately 5-7x (based on estimated revenue), higher than Autohome's 3.3x, with the premium reflecting growth expectations.

3. **Pre-IPO investors' core bet**: Not a bet on Dongchedi's current profitability, but a bet on the long-term monetization potential of the ByteDance ecosystem and the trend toward platformization of automotive transactions. HongShan's $400-500 million investment reflects high institutional confidence in this sector.

4. **Maximizing ByteDance's economic interest**: Through the path of "internal incubation → spin-off → external financing → IPO," ByteDance has transformed an internal business line into a company independently valued at $3 billion or more, while retaining approximately 80% ownership — realizing the release of asset value.

### 10.2 Valuation assessment

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Pre-IPO round's $3 billion valuation | Relatively reasonable, but already fully prices in growth expectations |
| Comparison with Autohome's valuation | 46% premium, requiring sustained high growth to support it |
| Pre-IPO investors' margin of safety | Moderate-to-low, dependent on realizing returns via an IPO premium |
| ByteDance's return | Extremely high, from zero-cost cultivation to a $3 billion valuation |
