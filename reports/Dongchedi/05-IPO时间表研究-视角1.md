# Dongchedi IPO Timeline: In-Depth Research

> Research date: 2026-05-30 | Sources: Bloomberg, The Paper, 36Kr, IT Home, Sina Finance, Jiemian News, 21st Century Business Herald, Huxiu, HKEX, AIM Group, and other public information
> Confidence level: Medium (not officially confirmed by the company; based mainly on media reports and sources familiar with the matter)

---

## Core Conclusion

Dongchedi's Hong Kong IPO has entered a substantive preparation stage: news of a listing surfaced in February 2026, and by March it had selected Citigroup and Goldman Sachs as underwriters, targeting a raise of USD 1.0-1.5 billion. However, as of the end of May 2026, a prospectus has not yet been formally filed with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, and there is no public record of a filing with the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) for overseas listing either. Based on the typical Hong Kong IPO process, the earliest listing window would be Q4 2026 through the first half of 2027.

### 1.2 Analysis of the Change in Ownership Structure

**Before the spin-off (prior to December 2023):**
```
ByteDance Ltd.
  └── Toutiao Co., Ltd.
        └── Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (100% controlled)
```

**After the spin-off (December 2023 to present):**
```
Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd.
  ├── He Jian 60% (President of Dongchedi)
  └── Zhao Jing 40%
        └── Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (100% controlled)
              └── Chongqing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (app operating entity)
```

**Key question: Does ByteDance still hold actual control?**

Based on the corporate registry, Xiamen Dongchezu's shareholders are just two natural persons, He Jian and Zhao Jing, and ByteDance no longer holds a direct stake. However, multiple industry analysts have pointed out that ByteDance likely retains control through the following mechanisms:

1. **Contractual control / nominee-shareholder arrangements**: He Jian and Zhao Jing may be acting as nominee holders for ByteDance, with shareholder agreements safeguarding ByteDance's actual control.
2. **VIE structure**: if the company lists in Hong Kong, it will almost certainly need to build a VIE structure, at which point ByteDance (or Zhang Yiming) could reappear in the control chain through an offshore entity.
3. **Business dependence**: Dongchedi's core traffic (an estimated >96%) comes from ByteDance's ecosystem (Douyin, Toutiao, etc.); even if it is independent at the equity level, it remains deeply tied at the business level.

> **Note**: The ownership-structure analysis above is based on public corporate-registry information and media speculation. The full ownership structure (including any VIE arrangement) will only be formally disclosed once an IPO prospectus is filed. ByteDance's actual economic interest cannot currently be confirmed.

## III. Analysis of Regulatory Approval Progress

### 3.1 CSRC Overseas-Listing Filing

**Regulatory background:**
- The "Trial Administrative Measures for the Overseas Issuance and Listing of Securities by Domestic Enterprises" have been in effect since March 31, 2023.
- All domestic enterprises listing overseas must file with the CSRC.
- Since 2025, review timelines have compressed to under 60 days (previously over 100 days), with some cases (e.g., CATL) taking as little as 25 days.

**Dongchedi's special considerations:**

1. **Data-security review**: as an internet platform, Dongchedi holds large volumes of user behavioral data. Under current rules, data-intensive enterprises meeting certain criteria must first pass a security review by the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC). This could add to approval time.
2. **VIE-structure review**: in 2024, 19 companies with VIE structures passed CSRC filing, concentrated mainly in software, information services, and internet industries — suggesting a VIE structure alone is not a substantive obstacle.
3. **ByteDance-affiliation review**: given ByteDance's unique profile (the world's largest privately held tech company, the TikTok controversy in the US, etc.), regulators may subject Dongchedi's independence to additional scrutiny.

**Current status:** as of the end of May 2026, Dongchedi does not appear on the CSRC's published list of overseas-listing filings.

### 3.2 Hong Kong Stock Exchange Review

**Key anticipated review points:**

| Review focus | Potential issue for Dongchedi |
|---------|-------------------|
| Independence | Extremely high dependence on ByteDance traffic (estimated >96%); HKEX may question whether the company can operate independently |
| Related-party transactions | Traffic purchases, technical services, and other related-party dealings with ByteDance/Douyin will be closely scrutinized |
| Profit track record | The Hong Kong main board requires cumulative profit of at least HKD 80 million over the most recent three fiscal years (at least HKD 35 million in the most recent year); whether Dongchedi meets this profitability threshold is in question |
| Market-cap / revenue test | If the profit test is not met, the company can apply under the "market capitalization / revenue test": a market cap of at least HKD 4 billion at listing plus revenue of at least HKD 500 million in the most recent audited fiscal year |
| Data compliance | Personal-information protection, cross-border data-transfer compliance, etc. |

> **Note**: In October 2024, HKEX streamlined its new-listing review timeline, shortening the main review period for simple cases to 40 working days. However, given the VIE structure and ByteDance-related-party complexities involved, Dongchedi's actual review is likely to take longer.

## IV. Reference Value of Precedents from ByteDance's Other IPO-Related Businesses

### 4.1 ByteDance's Overall Stance on Going Public

| Factor | Current status |
|------|------|
| Overall listing intent | Very low. CFO Gao Zhun has repeatedly stated there is "no specific listing plan or timetable" |
| Secondary-market valuation | Traded at a secondary-market valuation of roughly USD 550 billion as of February 2026, with potential to rise above USD 600 billion |
| Strategic preference | Favors independent listings of subsidiaries/business lines rather than a listing of the parent as a whole |
| TikTok US | Sold to a group of US investors in early 2026, via an "asset sale" rather than a listing |

### 4.2 The Significance of Dongchedi as a "First Mover"

If Dongchedi successfully lists, it would be the first subsidiary within the ByteDance system to complete an independent IPO. Its significance lies in:

1. **Path validation**: paving the way for the independent listing of other ByteDance businesses (such as Lark or Volcano Engine).
2. **Valuation reference**: helping the market more directly understand the value of ByteDance's various business lines.
3. **Regulatory test case**: gauging regulators' attitude toward listings by ByteDance-affiliated companies.
4. **The logic behind choosing Dongchedi:**
   - Relatively independent business (a vertical segment, not part of the core feed/social business).
   - Moderate scale (a roughly USD 3 billion valuation, keeping the cost of a misstep manageable).
   - Lower regulatory sensitivity (automotive information services vs. short video/social media).
   - Listed competitors already exist (Autohome is listed on the NYSE and Yiche has been taken private, providing valuation references).

## V. Expected Listing Timing Window

### 5.1 Three Scenarios

#### Scenario One: Optimistic (listing in Q4 2026)

**Preconditions:**
- CSRC filing (including CAC review) completed June-July 2026.
- Prospectus submitted to HKEX July-August 2026.
- Hearing passed September-October 2026.
- Public offering and listing November-December 2026.

**Probability estimate: 20-25%**

Rationale: the timeline is extremely tight — from underwriter selection in March to a year-end listing is only about 9 months, which is fast for a case involving a VIE structure and complex related-party transactions. As of end of May there is still no sign of a prospectus filing.

#### Scenario Two: Base case (listing in first half of 2027)

**Preconditions:**
- CSRC filing completed Q3 2026.
- Prospectus submitted to HKEX Q4 2026.
- Hearing passed Q1 2027.
- Public offering and listing Q1-Q2 2027.

**Probability estimate: 40-45%**

Rationale: this is the most reasonable timeframe, allowing sufficient time to complete audits (three years of financials for 2023-2025), build the VIE structure, and clear regulatory approvals.

#### Scenario Three: Delayed or shelved (second half of 2027 or later)

**Preconditions:**
- Unexpected delays in regulatory approval.
- Deteriorating market conditions.
- A strategic shift at ByteDance.
- Financial metrics falling short (e.g., ongoing losses preventing the company from meeting listing requirements).

**Probability estimate: 30-35%**

Rationale: numerous uncertain factors are at play, including — but not limited to — ByteDance's relationship with regulators, Hong Kong market sentiment, Dongchedi's own profitability, and industry headwinds (roughly 5,000 dealers exiting the network annually).

| Reference: Autohome's P/S | ~2x | — | — |

### 6.2 Key Questions to Be Answered Before Listing

1. **Profitability**: has Dongchedi achieved stable profitability? If still loss-making, this will limit which listing standards it can qualify under.
2. **Independence**: how will it demonstrate to HKEX and investors that it can survive independently after separating from the ByteDance system?
3. **Related-party transaction pricing**: how are traffic purchases and technical services with ByteDance priced? Are they conducted on fair terms?
4. **Growth ceiling**: where is the ceiling for China's auto-information-services market? How can growth be sustained amid the trend of dealers exiting the network?
5. **ByteDance's ownership stake**: how much economic interest will ByteDance ultimately retain? Could the company come to be viewed as "ByteDance's shadow stock"?

## VII. Risk Disclosures

1. **Highly uncertain listing timing**: all analysis here is based on media reports and sources familiar with the matter; neither the company nor ByteDance has officially confirmed an IPO plan.
2. **Valuation-anchor controversy**: there is considerable market disagreement over whether the USD 3 billion Pre-IPO valuation is reasonable. Comparable company Autohome has a market cap of only roughly USD 2 billion.
3. **Regulatory policy changes**: internet-platform regulatory policy carries inherent uncertainty.
4. **Market-window risk**: swings in Hong Kong market sentiment could affect the timing of the listing.
5. **Opaque financial data**: Dongchedi has never publicly disclosed any financial data, making it impossible to independently verify its revenue and profitability.
