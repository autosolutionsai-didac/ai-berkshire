## 2. Management assessment (from `IonQ-management-20260705.md`, ★★)

**Who runs it:** **Niccolo de Masi** — Chairman + President + CEO simultaneously. He is the **SPAC sponsor who took IonQ public in 2021**, moved from the board into the CEO chair (Feb 2025), consolidated the chairmanship (Aug 2025), and pushed the six-year operating CEO (Peter Chapman) off the board within ~5 months. Both scientist co-founders (Monroe, Kim) are gone (advisory only). **A capital-markets operator running a deep-physics company.** He does hold a genuine Cambridge physics background — a real mitigant.

**Integrity — the veto item (Li Lu/Buffett weight it first):**
- **Positives (both sides):** revenue/bookings guidance **beaten 5-for-5, 2021-2025**; #AQ milestones hit ahead of schedule; the crudest short attack (Scorpion 2022) was **court-discredited**.
- **Negatives (the pattern that matters at 141× sales):** promotional framing that shades into **technically misleading** (physical-vs-logical qubit conflation, "eliminate Nvidia's Blackwell by 2027," a documented 2020 "32 perfect qubits" overstatement when the machine was ~11 qubits); a **"no equity before 2028" pledge broken within six months**; a **CEO-owned Gulfstream leased back to the company** (textbook related-party); say-on-pay collapsing to ~54% for / ~46% against; and independent complexity theorist **Scott Aaronson accusing IonQ of "wild misrepresentations… to governments."**
- **The de Masi turnover pattern (the governance tell):** founders out (2023-24), CFO/COO Kramer out (2025), CRO turned over, **CTO seat vacant ~1.5 years**, **~5 new directors in 3 years**, dMY co-founder Harry You resigned the board *the day de Masi became CEO*. High churn concentrated around a single consolidating figure.

**Capital allocation:** **★★.** Genuine strength — raises were struck at **premiums to market** (Jul 2025 $1B at ~25% premium; Oct 2025 $2B at ~20% premium) — management sold overvalued stock well. Offsetting weakness — ~$2.5B of mostly-stock M&A with several **undisclosed prices**, two off-thesis deals (Capella satellites, SkyWater foundry), and revenue that is **substantially bought, not earned**.

**Insider behavior (the strongest single signal):** over ~18 months insiders sold **~13.6M shares and bought ~98K** — essentially one-directional. **de Masi cashed out ~$106M** (his entire Isalea stake, ~3.5 months after becoming CEO); **Chapman ~$270.7M cumulative** (14 sales, zero buys). **Contrast, in fairness:** the two *scientist* founders **largely held** (Monroe sold only ~$2.2M, Kim ~$0.5M). **The physicists kept their stock; the promoter-executives cashed out** — a revealing asymmetry.

**Compensation:** de Masi **$89.58M FY2025** (≈69% of revenue; **217:1 pay ratio**) at a company losing ~$510M. Five NEOs ~$207.8M in stock awards in one year.

## 4. Ten-year certainty (Li Lu's core question)

*"What will this business look like in 10 years, and can I be confident?"*

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Will IonQ exist in 10 years? | **Uncertain.** Well-capitalized today (~$3.1B, ~5-yr runway), but the model depends on continuous dilution-funded raises that require a durable narrative. |
| Will it be the quantum winner? | **Unknowable.** Faces IBM, Google, and a directly-comparable, better-funded Quantinuum; the modality race is unsettled. |
| Can I estimate its 2035 earnings? | **No.** No positive earnings today; terminal value hinges on a fault-tolerance event that may arrive 2030, 2040, or never at IonQ's scale. |
| "A business a fool can run"? | **No** — depends on both scarce physics talent (the founders who left) *and* one promoter's capital-markets access. |
| Certainty rating | **Very low.** This sits **outside a circle of competence that demands predictability and margin of safety.** |

**Li Lu would not own this.** Not because quantum is uninteresting, but because the combination of **no margin of safety + unquantifiable downside + integrity "not a clear yes" + 10-year unpredictability** violates every one of his filters. His framework is built to *decline* exactly this profile — a thrilling story where the downside is large and knowable and the upside is a distant maybe.

## Dimension rating: ★★ (2 / 5)

**Risk & management score: ★★ (2/5).** Credit where due: a strong cash cushion (~5-yr runway) removes near-term insolvency risk; single-class voting; premium-priced raises; a genuinely capable, physics-literate operator; and a clean near-term guidance record. But the aggregate risk picture is dominated by **structural, high-severity, largely-uncontrollable risks** (dilution, timeline, valuation, modality) layered on **management whose integrity is "not a clear yes,"** a **churn-and-cash-out pattern**, deteriorating say-on-pay, and **ten-year certainty that is near-zero.** For a downside-first framework this is close to the floor.

> "Risk is not volatility; risk is the permanent loss of capital." — Li Lu (paraphrase). IonQ's base and bear scenarios (−52%, −93%) are permanent-loss shaped. **The management is capable; the integrity gate and the risk shape are the blocks.**
