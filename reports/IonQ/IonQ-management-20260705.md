## 2. Integrity Assessment (the veto item)

### 2.1 Promise-vs-Delivery Tracking

| # | Time | Promise / claim | Where | Delivery | Assessment |
|---|------|-----------------|-------|----------|:----------:|
| 1 | Ahead of each FY | Annual **revenue / bookings guidance** | Earnings calls | **Beaten every year 2021–2025** (5-for-5); 2025 revenue **+20% over guidance** (USD 130M, +202%); 2026 guide raised USD 225–245M → **USD 260–270M** | ✅ Clean near-term record |
| 2 | 2024–2025 | Hit self-defined **#AQ** milestone *dates* | Blogs / earnings | **#AQ 35 (Jan 2024, ~1 yr early)**, #AQ 36 (Dec 2024), **#AQ 64 (Sep 2025, ~3 mo early)** | ✅ on their own yardstick |
| 3 | **Oct 2020** | "**32 perfect qubits**" (Chapman) | Product launch | Ex-executive later stated the real machine was **~11 qubits**; not customer-available at 32 | ❌ **Documented overstatement** |
| 4 | Mar 2021 (SPAC) | dMY III deck revenue ramp (~USD 5M '21 / 15M '22 / 34M '23 / 237M '25) and "**~USD 65B market by 2030**" | SPAC deck | **2021–23 and 2025 revenue all missed the deck** (e.g., 2023 $22M vs ~$34M) | ❌ Original vision badly missed |
| 5 | Jul 24, 2025 | Quantum **supremacy** "in **quarters and very low single-digit years**"; "a **fully fledged quantum internet company**" | CNBC (Cramer) | No commercial quantum advantage delivered to date | ⚠️ Aggressive, unproven |
| 6 | Sep 2025 | "**10,000 qubits by 2027** will **eliminate Nvidia's Blackwell**"; next chip "= **1 billion GPUs**" | de Masi on X | **Blurs physical vs. logical qubits**; conflicts with IonQ's own roadmap (10k *physical* / ~800 logical by 2027) | ❌ Over-promotional / misleading framing |
| 7 | Oct 2025 | "**Four-nines (99.99%) two-qubit fidelity — world first**" | Press release | Real, but a **largely *acquired* capability** (Oxford Ionics EQC) demonstrated on **R&D/prototype**, not shipping systems | ⚠️ Credit is bought, not organic |

**Delivery-rate read:** A genuinely two-sided record. On the **numbers management sets each year** (revenue/bookings guidance) IonQ has **beaten 5-for-5, 2021–2025** — bears understate this. On **#AQ dates** it delivered ahead of schedule — but (a) it **grades itself with a self-defined ruler** peers call gameable, (b) the flagship **#AQ 64 ran on a Tempo development system, not the shipping product** (Forte ships at #AQ 35/36), and (c) the four-nines fidelity "record" came **via acquisition**. On the **original 2021 SPAC vision and the visionary claims** (Blackwell replacement, "quantum internet company," "32 perfect qubits") the record is **missed or promotional-to-misleading**. Net: **near-term commitments land; the headline vision inflates, with one documented outright overstatement (2020).**

**Actual revenue (audited):** 2021 USD 2.1M → 2022 11.1M → 2023 22.0M → 2024 43.1M → **2025 130.0M** (first pure-play quantum firm past USD 100M; ~80% of 2025 growth organic, ~20% acquired). FY2025 **net loss USD 510M is mostly non-cash** (warrant mark-to-market + deal costs); **adjusted-EBITDA loss ≈ USD 187M** is the truer cash-burn figure — a fairness point the raw net-loss headline obscures.

### 5.2 Compensation

| Executive | Total comp | vs. company | Reasonable? |

## 7. CEO-Departure Scenario

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| If de Masi left tomorrow, could IonQ operate? | Operationally yes (CFO Singh is heavyweight; technical bench exists), but the **equity-markets narrative that funds the company is highly de-Masi-dependent** — his promotion and dealmaking *are* the funding engine. |
| Bench depth / successor? | Thin at the very top and **churn-heavy** (founders gone, CFO/CRO/CTO all changed in 2025; 5 new directors in 3 yrs). No obvious internal successor identified. |
| Advantage in the person or the system? | **Two different advantages:** the *science* rests on trapped-ion IP/talent (the founders who left); the *capital access* rests on de Masi personally. Neither is a durable, self-sustaining moat. |
| Past transitions smooth? | Chapman's exit was **abrupt** (off the board within ~5 months, full equity acceleration) — reads as negotiated, not routine. |

**Buffett test ("a business a fool can run"):** IonQ **fails** — it is neither fool-proof nor person-independent; it depends on both scarce physics talent *and* one promoter's capital-markets access.

### Overall management rating: ★★ (2 / 5)

**Integrity read (the crux):** **Concerning but not proven dishonest.** The genuine positives: management **delivers on quantifiable commitments** — revenue/bookings guidance beaten every year 2021–2025, #AQ 64 hit ahead of schedule, and the crudest short attack (Scorpion) was court-discredited. The negatives cluster on **the pattern that matters most for a stock at 141x sales:** promotional framing that shades into the **technically misleading** (physical-vs-logical qubit conflation, "eliminate Blackwell," a documented 2020 "32 perfect qubits" overstatement); **growth that is substantially acquired, not organic**; and a governance record that reads as management-first — **executives cashing out into the run-up** (de Masi ~USD 106M, Chapman ~USD 270.7M; insider group cut to <1%) **while the scientist-founders held**; an **USD 89.6M CEO package (217:1 pay ratio)** at a company losing half a billion a year; a **"no equity before 2028" pledge broken within six months**; a **CEO-owned Gulfstream leased back to the company**; **say-on-pay support collapsing 95% → 64% → 54%**; and a leading complexity theorist accusing IonQ of misrepresentation to governments. None of this is proven fraud, and the short-sellers' sharpest claims are disputed — but the aggregate is the opposite of the understated, shareholder-first conduct the four-master framework prizes. **Honesty is "not a clear yes."**

### Duan Yongping's "Buying People" Test

1. **Is this person honest?** — **Uncertain / not a clear yes.** Capable of straight numbers, but persistently over-promotional and monetizing heavily into the run-up.
2. **Is this person capable?** — **Yes.** Demonstrable operator and dealmaker with a genuine physics background.
3. **Would you hand this person your money to manage for 10 years?** — **No** — at this valuation, this dilution rate, and with honesty unresolved.

> Under Duan's rule ("the first is not a clear 'yes'"), the honesty gate caps this well below investment grade regardless of capability. **Capability is real; the integrity gate is the block.**

**Bottom line (opinion, labeled):** de Masi is intelligent, energetic, and a proven capital-markets operator — but on Buffett's ordering, **integrity comes first, and here it is the unresolved variable.** IonQ's model is a **self-reinforcing dilution-and-acquisition machine**: issue expensive stock, buy revenue and IP, sustain the narrative, repeat. That can work spectacularly while the stock is a currency — but it is **not a "buy people and hold 10 years" profile.** Consistent with the Phase-1 quality-screen exclusion.
