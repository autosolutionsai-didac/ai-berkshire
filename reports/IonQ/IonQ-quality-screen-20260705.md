## Verdict

**EXCLUDED.** IonQ fails 5 of 7 hard metrics (ROE, cumulative FCF, net margin, dilution, earnings-quality/OCF), with interest coverage not meaningful and only gross margin passing. None of the three exemption clauses is satisfied (all require either positive operating cash flow or positive returns, which IonQ does not have).

This is the quality screen behaving exactly as designed: it does not judge whether IonQ is a *good speculation on quantum computing* — it judges whether the company currently clears a first-class-business bar on hard, historical, cash-based metrics. A pre-revenue-scale, deeply cash-burning, heavily-diluting company does not, regardless of narrative.

## Statement of limitations

A bull case for IonQ rests on future scale economics and a technology bet — a venture/thematic framework, not the Buffett-style quality screen.
