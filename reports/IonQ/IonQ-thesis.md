## A1. Core thesis (the 5 sentences — written as the bear / pass case)

We are **NOT** buying IonQ at $49.12, because the five buy-conditions are **not met**:

1. **Business essence (understood, but outcome not forecastable):** IonQ is a trapped-ion quantum-computing hardware/systems, networking and sensing pure-play; the *business* is understandable, but the *technology outcome it is priced on* — useful fault tolerance — is a 2030–2040+ maybe that cannot be forecast.
2. **Moat (narrow, not widening):** only 1 of 5 classic moats holds (technology/IP, and it is *substantially acquired*, e.g. Oxford Ionics); there is no pricing power (gross margin **fell 40.4%→23.8%** as volume rose) and scale economics are *negative* — this is not a widening moat.
3. **Management (capable, integrity "not a clear yes"):** CEO+Chairman+President Niccolo de Masi is a genuinely able capital-markets operator, but his integrity is **"not a clear yes"** (documented overstatements, physical-vs-logical qubit conflation, a broken "no equity before 2028" pledge, a related-party jet, say-on-pay collapsing 95%→64%→54%, insiders cashed out to <1%) — this fails the veto item.
4. **Price vs. value (a premium, not a discount):** at **141× sales / 117× EV-revenue**, with **199%-of-revenue** stock-based-comp dilution, the price embeds near-certain victory; there is **no margin of safety** — the base scenario is **−52%** below today.
5. **Downside (not contained):** the payoff distribution is violently asymmetric — base **−52%** (~$23), bear **−93%** (~$3.5), dilution structural — this is permanent-loss-shaped, not a contained downside.

*(≈195 words. If these five sentences cannot be completed as a buy case — and here they cannot — the thesis itself is telling us the buy decision is not clear enough. It is not.)*

### What would make us reconsider (the reconsideration trigger)

We would only reopen IonQ as a genuine investment candidate (not a speculation) on a **durable margin recovery AND a valuation reset** — concretely: gross margin back toward **40%+** for multiple quarters, a **credible dated path to positive operating cash flow** (assumptions #1 and #2 turning 🟢), **dilution slowing** (SBC below revenue, raises stopping), *and* a valuation reset toward **<30× EV/revenue** (roughly the ~$15–25 base-scenario zone). Absent that combination, the Phase-1 quality-screen **EXCLUSION stands** and the verdict remains **AVOID**.
