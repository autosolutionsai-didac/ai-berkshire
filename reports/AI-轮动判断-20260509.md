**Compiled on**: 2026-05-09

## One-line core judgment

**Don't chase infrastructure & power in the short term; favor a valuation recovery in large model vendors over the medium term; expect monetization to diverge sharply across applications over the long term** — reasoning from the capex-cycle logic of "shovel sellers before shovel users." The biggest variable is whether the model layer levels out (capability becomes commoditized); if it does, the application-layer logic reverses.

### Three key observations

1. **Research coverage is highest for the "models" and "applications" layers; "infrastructure & power" is a blank spot**
   Given the current heat position (infrastructure already at the 80th-95th percentile), this gap can be read as reasonable — it has avoided a sub-sector that is already stretched. But if infrastructure & power pulls back over the next 6-12 months, a research file needs to be built quickly.

2. **Tencent, Alibaba, and NVIDIA are the three most deeply researched names**
   - Tencent: king of the application layer (high probability of winner-take-all)
   - Alibaba: the only publicly listed pure-play at the model layer (high-confidence valuation recovery)
   - NVIDIA: the compute sub-sector leader (caught between defensive and cyclical)

3. **Unlisted model companies are "research, not action"**
   ByteDance / DeepSeek / MiniMax / Zhipu AI are all private-market names; for now the only options are:
   - Wait for an IPO (DeepSeek has already filed on the STAR Market)
   - Gain indirect exposure through holdings (Alibaba holds legacy shares in Zhipu AI, Tencent holds a stake in MiniMax)
   - Track valuation changes as a thermometer for the model sub-sector

## Key tradeoffs (what happens if this call is wrong)

| If wrong on this point | Consequence |
|------------|------|
| AI capex slams the brakes (ROI proves insufficient) | Compute + infrastructure & power pull back 30-50% together; models/applications hold up relatively better |
| Model layer levels out (open source catches up to closed source) | Large-vendor model moats collapse; the application layer benefits instead (lower costs) |
| Application layer never manages to monetize | Capital retreats from the entire AI theme, dragging everything down from infrastructure to models |
| Domestic substitution proceeds faster than expected | US AI stocks face valuation pressure; A-share/Hong Kong AI stocks see valuation recovery |
