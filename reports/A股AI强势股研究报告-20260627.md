## AI Research Bias Awareness

This report studies AI-related companies that have "already rallied sharply," which naturally carries three types of bias:

| Bias | Manifestation | How this report addresses it |
|---|---|---|
| Recent-gain anchoring | Easy to interpret a rally as a fundamental inevitability | First screen by range-of-gain, then separately judge AI-business purity and profit realization |
| Theme-label contamination | "AI concept" stocks mix in traditional hardware, materials, and transitioning businesses | Exclude overly broad concepts; focus on CPO, optical modules, AI server materials, storage, industrial software, and compute businesses |
| Valuation hindsight | Seeing a good company and overlooking that the stock price has already priced in the future | Use 2025 EPS and a three-scenario valuation to back out what the current price implies |

Information-richness rating:

|---|---:|---|
