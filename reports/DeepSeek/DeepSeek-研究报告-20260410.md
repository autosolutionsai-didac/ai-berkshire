## Core Technical Innovations

1. **MoE architecture**: 671B parameters with only 37B activated, cutting compute cost by 90%
2. **MLA attention**: Dramatically reduces KV-cache memory during inference
3. **FP8 mixed-precision training**: The first open-source large language model trained in FP8
4. **R1-Zero**: Pure reinforcement learning with no supervised fine-tuning — the model spontaneously developed chain-of-thought reasoning
5. **Full MIT open source**: Model weights and training tools are all open-sourced

## Business Model

**A deliberately non-commercial "idealistic AI lab."**

- API pricing is extremely low: V4 input priced at $0.30/M tokens (1/10 of GPT-5's price)
- The founder has explicitly stated he isn't pursuing profit, but "research without constraints"
- Cross-subsidized by High-Flyer Quant's annual profit of 5 billion yuan
- More like "the CERN of AI" than the next OpenAI

## Industry Impact: The "DeepSeek Shock"

R1 was released on January 27, 2025, and reached #1 on the US App Store, in what was dubbed AI's "Sputnik moment":
- Nvidia plunged 17% in a single day, wiping out ~$600 billion (the largest single-day market-cap loss in U.S. stock market history)
- The AI sector lost ~$1 trillion in total
- Challenged the "scale is everything" belief, proving that algorithmic innovation can close the compute gap
- But 11 months later, the market fully recovered — the Jevons Paradox took hold (lower cost → elastic demand unlocked → compute demand actually rises)
