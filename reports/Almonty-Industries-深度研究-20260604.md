## I. Company Overview: Global Leader in Non-Chinese Tungsten

Almonty Industries was founded in 2011 and is the world's largest producer of non-Chinese tungsten concentrate. The company operates three mines and is developing a fourth project in the United States:

| Mine | Location | Status | Capacity / Characteristics |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **Sangdong** | South Korea | Phase 1 in production (2026.3.17) | 640,000 tonnes of ore/year → 2,300 tonnes of tungsten concentrate/year |
| **Panasqueira** | Portugal | Stable production for 130 years | 700,000-750,000 tonnes/year, currently the main revenue source |
| **Los Santos** | Spain | Under care and maintenance | Planned restart of operations in 2026 |
| **Gentung Browns Lake** | Montana, USA | Development stage | Expected to start production in H2 2026, 140,000 tonnes of units/year |

**Headquarters** has relocated from Canada to Dillon, Montana, USA — a strategic move placing it close to the Gentung project and the Pentagon procurement chain.

**CEO Lewis Black** founded the company in 2011 and made two open-market purchases of 50,000 shares in March 2025, backing his confidence in the company's outlook with concrete action.

## II. Core Thesis: Tungsten's Bottleneck Status

### 2.1 What Is Tungsten and Why Does It Matter?

Tungsten (W) is the metal with the highest melting point on Earth (3,422°C) and is widely used in:

- **Semiconductor manufacturing**: Tungsten hexafluoride (WF6) is an irreplaceable deposition gas in chip manufacturing
- **Defense**: A key material for armor-piercing rounds, missiles, and drones
- **Hard alloys**: Cutting tools, mining drill bits
- **Aerospace**: High-temperature alloy components

### 2.2 WF6 — An S-Tier Bottleneck

WF6 is a critical precursor in semiconductor manufacturing, used in tungsten chemical vapor deposition (CVD). Japanese WF6 producers (accounting for roughly 25% of global supply) have notified Samsung and SK Hynix that their WF6 inventories could be depleted by June 2026. This bottleneck directly threatens global memory chip production.

### 2.3 China's Monopoly and Geopolitical Risk

- **China controls more than 80% of global tungsten powder production and refining**
- In January 2026, China's "Dual-Use Items Export Control List" further tightened tungsten export controls
- **Only 15 companies are authorized to export tungsten**, with Beijing directly controlling export volumes, timing, and destinations
- **Starting January 2027, US defense contractors must source tungsten from non-Chinese sources** (DFARS regulation)

### 2.4 APT Price Surge

Rotterdam APT (ammonium paratungstate) spot price trend:

| Time point | APT price (US$/MTU) | Change |
|---------|-------------------|------|
| Early 2025 | ~$460 | Baseline |
| January 2026 | ~$920 | +100% |
| April 2026 | ~$3,185 | +591% |
| May 29, 2026 | ~$3,040 | +557% (vs. start of year) |

**Structural drivers:** Tightening Chinese export quotas + rising global defense spending + US strategic reserve purchases + expanding WF6 semiconductor demand. Analysts believe prices could test $30,000/MTU (concentrate), as the supply gap has become structural.

## IV. Growth Engine: The SeAH Molybdenum Offtake Contract

### Core Terms of the Exclusive Offtake Agreement

| Term | Detail |
|------|------|
| Buyer | SeAH M&S (South Korea's largest molybdenum processor, the world's second-largest molybdenum oxide smelter, a SpaceX contractor) |
| Subject | The entirety of Sangdong's molybdenum output, exclusive offtake for the mine's full life |
| Mine life | 60 years (based on South Korean government historical data) |
| Annual output | Approximately 5,600 tonnes of molybdenum |
| Floor price | US$19.00/lb (before processing fee deduction) |
| Minimum guaranteed annual revenue | **~US$234 million** |
| Expected production start | **End of 2026** |

### What Does This Mean?

This is a fortune-changing contract. The molybdenum mine alone can:
- Provide a US$234 million annual revenue floor (vs. current annualized revenue of approximately US$100 million)
- Eliminate downside risk from molybdenum price movements (floor price guarantee)
- Provide extremely long revenue visibility with a 60-year contract term
- Indirectly link to aerospace demand, since SeAH is a SpaceX supplier

## VI. Four Catalysts (June 2026)

Almonty faces a rare period of concentrated catalysts, with four major events occurring within a single month:

### Catalyst 1: Phase 2 Shareholder Vote (June 8-9) ★★★★★

- **Details:** A vote on the Sangdong mine's Phase 2 expansion. If approved, ore processing capacity will double from 640,000 tonnes/year to **1.2 million tonnes/year**, and tungsten concentrate output will double from 2,300 tonnes to ~4,600 tonnes/year
- **Timeline:** Proxy voting deadline 10:00 a.m. ET on June 5; AGM scheduled for June 9
- **Significance:** Phase 2 will come online in 2027, timed to coincide with the Pentagon's ban on non-Chinese tungsten procurement taking effect
- **Management's position:** The board unanimously recommends voting in favor, and the CEO has publicly called on shareholders to vote in support
- **Risk:** If not approved, near-term share price pressure would result, though Phase 1 would remain operational

### Catalyst 2: Inclusion in the Russell 1000 Index (effective June 29) ★★★★

- **Details:** FTSE Russell announced a preliminary list on May 22; Almonty will be added to the large-cap Russell 1000 and Russell 3000 indices
- **Significance:** Approximately US$12.2 trillion in assets are benchmarked to Russell US indices, and passive funds will passively buy ALM
- **Impact:** Increased institutional investor visibility, increased liquidity, reduced volatility

### Catalyst 3: Pentagon Tungsten Procurement Ban (effective January 2027) ★★★★

- US defense contractors will be required to source tungsten from non-Chinese sources
- Almonty has already signed a binding supply agreement with Tungsten Parts Wyoming for at least 40 tonnes of tungsten oxide per month
- Sangdong + Gentung together form a complete US domestic + allied supply chain

### Catalyst 4: Molybdenum Mine Production Start (end of 2026) ★★★★★

- The SeAH offtake contract takes effect, and the US$234 million annual revenue floor begins to materialize
- The key inflection point where the story turns into cash flow

## VIII. Competitive Landscape and Moat

### Non-Chinese Tungsten Supply Landscape

Global tungsten supply is heavily concentrated in China (80%+), with very few non-Chinese suppliers:

| Company/Project | Location | Status |
|----------|------|------|
| **Almonty (Sangdong)** | South Korea | In production, Phase 2 pending approval |
| **Almonty (Panasqueira)** | Portugal | Continuous operation for 130 years |
| **Mactung** | Canada | 2027 or later |
| **Bakuta** | Kazakhstan | In development |

**Sangdong at full production could meet approximately 40% of global non-Chinese tungsten demand** — an extremely scarce market position.

### Moat Assessment

- **Resource scarcity**: High-grade tungsten deposits are extremely rare globally; Sangdong is one of the highest-grade tungsten deposits in the world
- **Customer lock-in**: SeAH's 60-year exclusive offtake contract, and the Tungsten Parts Wyoming supply agreement
- **Policy barriers**: The DFARS ban creates institutional demand assurance
- **Technical barriers**: Sangdong employs proprietary processing technology and a modern water treatment system
- **First-mover advantage**: Capacity was built out before the Pentagon ban takes effect

**Weaknesses:** Tungsten is a commodity, and long-term prices are dominated by Chinese policy; mining assets are highly capital-intensive; there is high dependence on a single commodity (though the molybdenum mine provides some diversification).

## X. Conclusion and Investment Recommendation

### Core Judgment

Almonty Industries is a **high-risk, high-reward "bottleneck investment" target**. Its investment thesis rests on three pillars:

1. **Tungsten's irreplaceability** — WF6 is an S-tier bottleneck in semiconductor manufacturing with no substitute
2. **The flip side of China's monopoly** — Sangdong is the only Western tungsten mine capable of replacing Chinese supply at scale
3. **Contracted revenue lock-in** — SeAH's 60-year molybdenum offtake provides a US$234 million/year revenue floor

If these three pillars hold, Almonty will become a roughly US$500 million-a-year revenue mining company by 2027, and the current US$5.7 billion market cap, corresponding to a forward P/S of about 10-11x, is not particularly expensive against the backdrop of a commodity supercycle.

### But the Following Facts Must Be Confronted:

- **The company has never been profitable**, with EBITDA only turning positive in Q1 2026
- **The trailing valuation (P/S 159x) is at an absurd level**, and its entire justification depends on future execution
- **Sangdong has only been in production for three months**, and whether it can run stably up to full capacity remains unproven
- **Molybdenum revenue is still at least six months away** from beginning to materialize
- **Beta of 2.03** means the share price could swing sharply around catalyst events

### Investment Recommendation: **Conditional Wait-and-See, Awaiting a Better Entry Point**

| Investor type | Recommendation |
|-----------|------|
| **High-risk-tolerance / small speculative position** | Could establish a tentative position (1-3% of total portfolio) ahead of the Phase 2 vote (6/8-9) to capture swing gains from the catalyst event |
| **Value investor** | **Wait and see.** Wait for: (1) 2-3 consecutive quarters of stable production data from Sangdong; (2) confirmation of the molybdenum mine's production start; (3) a valuation pullback after APT prices normalize. An ideal entry point would be in the US$12-16 range |
| **Conservative investor** | **Avoid.** A mining company that has never been profitable trading at a US$5.7 billion market cap — no matter how good the story, the risk/reward profile is unsuitable for conservative investors |

### Key Monitoring Points

- **June 8-9**: Phase 2 shareholder vote result — pass/fail will directly affect the medium-term valuation
- **June 29**: Russell 1000 inclusion takes effect — a window for passive fund inflows
- **Q2 2026 earnings**: Sangdong's first full quarter of production and revenue data — a key test of execution
- **End of 2026**: Molybdenum mine production start — whether the US$234 million/year revenue materializes as scheduled
- **January 2027**: Pentagon's ban on non-Chinese tungsten procurement formally takes effect — a demand-side catalyst
