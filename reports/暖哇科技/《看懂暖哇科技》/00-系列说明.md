# Understanding Nuanwa Technology: Series Overview

> Four articles that peel apart this "first insurance-AI stock" layer by layer to reveal its true structure.
>
> The series takes about 50 minutes to read; reading in order is recommended.

---

## Series positioning

Nuanwa Technology is not a widely discussed company — it is a company that **most people have never heard of, but one that has sparked fierce debate within the insurtech circle**.

- The bulls say: the insurance-AI track has high long-term certainty, revenue has nearly tripled over three years, and the company already covers nine of China's top ten insurers
- The bears say: nearly half of revenue is transfusion from majority shareholder ZhongAn Online, cumulative losses over three years exceed **RMB 660 million**, gross margin has declined year after year, and its insurance brokerage subsidiary is repeatedly the subject of complaints

**Both camps are looking at the same company, yet arrive at completely opposite conclusions.** On one side is the halo of "the largest independent AI technology company in the insurance industry"; on the other is the reality of "ZhongAn dependency syndrome" and persistent losses.

What this series sets out to do is **first lay out clearly "what this company is," and then let you judge for yourself whether it is "worth paying attention to"** — rather than handing you a conclusion.

## Series writing principles

This series follows these hard rules:

1. **Lay out the data first, then the logic, then the conclusion** — no preset bullish or bearish stance
2. **Every core judgment carries a counter-argument** — so readers can weigh it themselves
3. **Unreliable data is dropped rather than force-filled** — Nuanwa Technology has not yet gone public, and much key data (such as gross-margin breakdowns by business line or customer-level revenue detail) has not been fully disclosed. This series uses only the prospectus and credible sources as its baseline and does not force-fill the gaps
4. **Not investment advice** — every article carries a disclaimer at the end
