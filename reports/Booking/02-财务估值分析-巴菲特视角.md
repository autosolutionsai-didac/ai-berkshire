**Key findings:**
- **ROIC has climbed steadily to 43.7%**, far above the industry median of 3.6%, marking an extremely capital-efficient business. That means for every $1 invested, the company earns back nearly 44 cents in profit. Source: [GuruFocus BKNG ROIC](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/roic/BKNG)
- **Free cash flow margin has stayed above 33% for years** and significantly exceeds net income, driven mainly by: (1) the working-capital advantage of the merchant prepayment model; (2) low capital-expenditure requirements
- **Gross margin has risen year after year**, reflecting the platform's growing pricing power and the rising share of merchant revenue

## III. Three-scenario valuation and margin of safety

### Base assumptions
- The valuation is based on expected 2026 free cash flow
- Q1 2026 revenue was $5.53 billion (+16%), and full-year guidance calls for low- to mid-single-digit growth (pressured by geopolitics and currency effects)
- Expected 2026 free cash flow: about $10 billion (extrapolated from Q1 performance and the historical FCF/revenue ratio)
- Expected 2026 EPS: about $3.70 (post-split basis, equivalent to about $92.50 pre-split)

### Conservative scenario (bear case)

| Assumption | Value |
|------|------|
| 2026-2030 revenue CAGR | 5% |
| Steady-state FCF margin | 30% |
| Terminal valuation multiple | 15x FCF |
| **Fair market cap** | **About $105 billion** |
| Upside/downside vs. current | -23% |

Triggers: a global economic recession that reduces travel spending; Google's AI search meaningfully diverting OTA traffic; regulatory pressure on commission rates.

### Base case scenario (neutral)

| Assumption | Value |
|------|------|
| 2026-2030 revenue CAGR | 8-10% |
| Steady-state FCF margin | 33% |
| Terminal valuation multiple | 20x FCF |
| **Fair market cap** | **About $170-190 billion** |
| Upside/downside vs. current | +24% to +39% |

Assumptions: steady growth in global travel; the Connected Trip strategy raises average order value; AI investment expands EBITDA margin by 100-125 basis points per year; ongoing buybacks continue to shrink shares outstanding by 4-5% per year.

### Optimistic scenario (bull case)

| Assumption | Value |
|------|------|
| 2026-2030 revenue CAGR | 12-15% |
| Steady-state FCF margin | 36% |
| Terminal valuation multiple | 25x FCF |
| **Fair market cap** | **About $280-320 billion** |
| Upside/downside vs. current | +104% to +134% |

Triggers: Connected Trip fully succeeds and Booking becomes travel's "super app"; accelerated penetration in Asia-Pacific; AI substantially improves operating efficiency and user conversion rates.

### Margin-of-safety assessment

**At the base case's $170-190 billion, the current market cap of $137 billion implies roughly 24-39% of upside.**

Even in the conservative scenario, a floor valuation of $105 billion implies a maximum downside of about 23% — for a top-tier platform business with ROIC above 40% and a 34% free cash flow margin, this risk/reward ratio is attractive.

In addition, the company shrinks its share count by 4-5% per year through buybacks, so even if the share price doesn't rise, per-share intrinsic value is naturally compounding at a 4-5% rate.
