# AI Full Value-Chain Stock Screen: Candidate Pool of Investment Opportunities

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Method:** 20 parallel research teams scanning the entire AI value chain, covering Hong Kong / A-share / US / Taiwan / Korea markets
**Goal:** Identify all attractive AI investment opportunities across the market and build a candidate pool

---

## Screening Criteria

From a value-investing perspective, a "good opportunity" must satisfy all of the following at once:
1. **Clear moat** — monopoly / oligopoly / high switching costs / network effects
2. **Strong pricing power** — gross margin high and either stable or rising
3. **Confirmed AI growth** — revenue growth above 30%, or high AI purity
4. **Valuation not outlandish** — PE/PS within an acceptable range (a growth-stock premium is allowed, but no bubble)
