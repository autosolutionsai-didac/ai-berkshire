# Dongchedi User Profile and Behavior Deep Dive

> Research date: May 2026
> Data sources: Dongchedi official reports, CAAM white paper, QuestMobile, CTR, third-party competitive analysis

---

## I. Baseline User Profile

### 1.1 Gender distribution

Dongchedi's user base is predominantly male, but female users are growing rapidly:

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Male user share | ~72% (2021 data: 71.72%) |
| Female user share | ~28% (2021), continuing to rise in recent years |
| Female user annual growth rate | 99%-101% (2023-2024 YoY) |
| Growth in verified female car owners | 70% (2022 YoY) |

**Key insight**: Female users still make up a modest absolute share, but their growth rate is roughly 1.7x that of male users. This trend correlates strongly with the spread of new-energy vehicles (NEVs) — the intelligent features, stylish looks, and lower usage barrier of NEVs have lowered the entry threshold for female users. Dongchedi's own research also shows that more than half of users disapprove of automakers' "female-targeted marketing," suggesting female users want equal, professional content rather than being deliberately courted.

### 1.2 Age distribution

| Age band | Share | Characteristics |
|--------|------|------|
| 24 and under | ~18% (post-2000s) | Fastest-growing cohort, favors livestreams and short video |
| 24-30 | ~22% | Mostly first-time buyers, price-sensitive |
| 31-35 | ~34% (largest cohort) | Rising upgrade/replacement demand, quality-focused |
| 36-40 | ~18% | Family car needs, favors SUVs and MPVs |
| Over 40 | ~8% | Small but stable share |

**Key shift**: Over the past year, users under 30 grew 16%. The post-2000s cohort now accounts for 18% of users, an incremental group that can no longer be ignored. The platform's median user age sits at roughly 30-32.

### 1.3 Geographic distribution

- **Tier-1 and tier-2 cities** remain the core user base, with the highest user density in economically developed coastal eastern regions
- **Tier-3 and lower-tier cities** have seen a marked rise in their share of users in recent years, a clear lower-tier expansion trend
- Users in lower-tier markets rely more on online channels for car information, with Dongchedi and Douyin as the top choices
- The compound growth rate of passenger-vehicle sales in lower-tier markets (30.4%) significantly exceeds that of tier-1 cities (24.2%), implying that Dongchedi's user-growth ceiling may be higher than expected

### 1.4 Income and car-purchase budget

- **Mainstream purchase budget range**: RMB 100,000-200,000, the target price point for more than half of users
- **Core user profile**: middle-class consumers form the primary buying group
- **Shift in spending mix**: upgrade/replacement buyers are gradually overtaking first-time buyers as the dominant force in new-car sales
- This structural shift has far-reaching implications for Dongchedi's product strategy — upgrade/replacement buyers need services like used-car valuation and trade-in far more than pure content browsing

### 2.2 Traffic composition

Distribution of Dongchedi's user traffic within the platform:

| Feature module | Traffic share |
|----------|----------|
| Vehicle-model database visits | 35% |
| Content reading | 23% |
| Active search | 12% |
| Other (community, livestream, etc.) | 30% |

- Users under 24 show a stronger preference for livestream and short video

## III. Car-Purchase Decision Path Analysis

### 3.1 The "See-Choose-Buy" three-stage model

Dongchedi breaks the car-purchase decision into three stages:

#### Stage one: "See" (interest)
- **Behavior**: browsing the feed, watching short videos and full vehicle reviews
- **Content preference**: video-format full vehicle reviews, new-model launches, test-drive experiences
- **Dongchedi's role**: reaching potential interested users via algorithmic recommendation and Douyin traffic referral

#### Stage two: "Choose" (intent)
- **Behavior**: entering the vehicle-model database for detailed specs, comparing models, reading word-of-mouth reviews
- **Decision factors**: vehicle class and price are the top considerations (both cited by over 70% of users)
- **Most-used features**: model-database detail pages, photo galleries, trim/version lists
- **Dongchedi's role**: providing structured vehicle data and side-by-side comparison tools

#### Stage three: "Buy" (purchase decision)
- **Behavior**: actively comparing prices, inquiring about discounts, submitting leads
- **Key influencing factor**: brand effect becomes markedly stronger, and the influence of word-of-mouth and social circles grows
- **Dongchedi's role**: connecting dealers, providing quotes and discount information, closing transactions

### 3.2 The stage where Dongchedi has the greatest influence

**Core judgment: Dongchedi has the greatest influence at the "Choose" stage.**

Reasons:
1. 35% of traffic flows to the vehicle-model database, the core scenario for the "Choose" stage
2. "The more content read, the higher the conversion rate" has become a defining consumption trait of the new generation of consumers
3. Among Dongchedi's active users, over 70% are high-intent shoppers — meaning most users arrive at the platform already intending to buy, and the platform's value lies in helping them narrow a vague intention down to a specific model
4. By comparison, the "See" stage is handled mostly by Douyin (sparking interest), and the "Buy" stage is handled mostly by offline dealerships (closing the sale)

### 3.3 Decision-making differences among young users

- Young users' purchase behavior is more strongly influenced by brand and word-of-mouth factors
- The influence of socially-shared word-of-mouth sentiment and "circles" is steadily strengthening
- Young users are more inclined to gather information via livestream and short video rather than traditional text-and-image content

## IV. Dongchedi vs. Autohome: Differences in User Profile

### 4.1 Scale and growth-rate comparison

| Metric | Autohome | Dongchedi |
|------|----------|--------|
| DAU (2023 Q3) | 14.14 million | 10.04 million |
| MAU | ~65.75 million | ~39 million |
| MAU growth rate | 1.23% (near stagnant) | 57% (fastest DAU growth in the past two years) |
| Industry ranking | #1 | #3 (closing in) |

### 4.2 Differences in user attributes

| Dimension | Autohome | Dongchedi |
|------|----------|--------|
| Platform positioning | Full-lifecycle service for auto consumers | Fragmented-scenario service for auto enthusiasts |
| Core user age | Skews more mature (higher share aged 35+) | Skews younger (31-35 is the primary cohort, higher share under 24) |
| Content format | Primarily text-and-image, professionally in-depth | Short video + livestream + personalized recommendation |
| Female users | Lower share | Higher share and faster growth |
| Community atmosphere | Traditional forum model, professional but staid | Points, check-ins, redemptions — more interactive and playful |
| User needs | Full lifecycle: choosing, buying, using, and trading in cars | Fragmented browsing, choosing, and buying + social |
| Content distribution | Editorial recommendation + search | AI-personalized recommendation + search |

### 4.3 Judgment on the competitive landscape

Autohome's advantage lies in brand trust and an accumulated base of professional content, but its user growth has nearly stalled, and it faces a "midlife crisis." Dongchedi is growing faster, but its absolute MAU still trails by a wide margin. The key variable is whether Dongchedi's deep integration with Douyin can keep delivering incremental users. If Douyin's 510 million auto-interested users can be effectively converted into high-frequency Dongchedi users, overtaking Autohome in scale is only a matter of time.

## V. Douyin-Referred Users vs. Self-Opening Users

### 5.1 User flow path

Based on integration data between Dongchedi and Douyin:

- **84% of users** who browse vehicle content on Douyin go on to jump to Dongchedi to check the detailed vehicle-model database
- **78% of users** search for a specific model series or brand keyword on Dongchedi and read professional reviews in depth

### 5.2 Behavioral differences

| Dimension | Douyin-referred users | Self-opening users |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Entry intent | Interest-driven, "planted" by content | Clear purpose, arrives with specific needs |
| First action | Views the recommended vehicle's detail page | Directly searches for the target vehicle |
| Depth of content consumption | Shallow to deep, gradual | Goes straight to in-depth comparison and spec-checking |
| Strength of purchase intent | Low → medium (needs nurturing) | Medium → high (already has a clear direction) |
| Conversion cycle | Longer, needs multiple touchpoints | Shorter, decides faster |

### 5.3 Cross-platform synergy logic

Douyin and Dongchedi merge dual-end data to build a two-dimensional "interest-intent" model:
- On Douyin: analyzing likes, comments, and shares to gauge interest preferences
- On Dongchedi: analyzing search history and the number of model comparisons to assess the strength of purchase intent
- Synergy strategy: use Douyin's creative content to spark interest among low-intent users, and use Dongchedi's professional content to drive conversion among high-intent users

**In essence, Douyin solves "how to get users to see it," while Dongchedi solves "how to get users to trust it."**

## VI. User Retention and Repeat Engagement

### 6.1 Retention data

- New-user retention rate was ~45% (early data, measured one year after the 2018 launch)
- Over 70% of active users are high-intent shoppers
- Specific post-purchase retention data has not been publicly disclosed

### 6.2 Post-purchase user behavior

Dongchedi offers post-purchase users the following retention scenarios:

| Scenario | Feature | Retention driver |
|------|------|-----------|
| Owner-community circle | Posting, Q&A, sharing driving experience | Sense of social belonging |
| Car use and maintenance | Maintenance manuals, after-sales merchant recommendations, service booking | Practical utility value |
| Owner reviews | Publishing and reading genuine owner reviews | Sense of contribution and reference value |
| Used cars | Old-car valuation, used-car transactions | Trade-in/upgrade demand |
| Trade-in | One-stop trade-in service | Closed-loop transaction convenience |

### 6.3 Structural retention challenges

**Core tension: cars are a low-frequency purchase, and users' motivation to keep using the app drops sharply after buying.**

This is a shared challenge across all vertical auto platforms. Dongchedi's response strategy includes:
1. **Community retention**: sustaining post-purchase user activity through the owner-community circle
2. **Car-use services**: extending into maintenance, insurance, and traffic-violation lookups — higher-frequency needs
3. **Content consumption**: auto news as "interest content" to keep drawing in enthusiasts
4. **Trade-in loop**: trade-in services to lock in the next purchase need
5. **Trade-in driving consumption**: Dongchedi reports that its trade-in service has driven RMB 380 billion in auto consumption

But frankly, post-purchase user churn remains the platform's biggest retention challenge. Unlike Douyin, an auto content platform inherently lacks a daily, high-frequency use case.

## VII. User Perceptions of Content Credibility

### 7.1 Positive feedback

- The **owner-review system** is well regarded: Dongchedi uses its self-developed automotive-industry large language model to run semantic analysis over a huge volume of owner reviews, surfacing genuine owner opinions
- **Verified-owner content** is highly trusted: reading volume rose 158%, 4x that of non-owner content
- **Used-car services** are broadly trusted: rigorous inspection and appraisal, plus warranty and a 7-day no-reason return policy

### 7.2 Controversy and doubts

- **Controversy over smart-driving reviews**: discrepancies between Dongchedi's assisted-driving test results and users' actual experience have raised credibility doubts
- **Insufficient credibility of a single institution**: the industry believes Dongchedi should partner with authoritative bodies to establish unified testing rules
- **Winter-test controversy**: some automakers have questioned the testing methodology under extreme conditions
- **Users' core demand**: an increasing number of users are demanding "authenticity" and "objectivity," with declining tolerance for commercialized content

### 7.3 Trust-building measures

Measures Dongchedi has rolled out in response:
- **Million-Owner Testing Program**: increased investment in real-world testing of intelligence and safety features
- **Owner-review product**: a structured evaluation system built on genuine owner input
- **Emphasizing "if you want the truth, go to Dongchedi"**: a brand positioning centered on authenticity
- **Increased investment in professional testing**: real-world testing of range, charging, smart-driving, and other core performance metrics

## VIII. Analysis of Female Users

### 8.1 Growth data

| Period | Change in female users |
|------|-------------|
| 2020 | Post-90s and post-95s women made up over 50% of female users (within the female-user segment), the fastest-growing subgroup |
| 2021 | Female user share ~28% |
| 2022 | Female user share rose; verified female car owners up 70% |
| 2023-2024 | Female user base grew 99%-101% YoY |
| 2024-2025 | Female share among new car buyers doubled |

### 8.2 Characteristics of female users

- **Faster decisions**: female users' purchase decision time is generally shorter than that of male users
- **Different decision path**: they lean more toward browsing sales-ranking charts, and focus on looks, brand, and price
- **Skew younger**: post-90s and post-95s women form the primary female-user cohort
- **Stronger NEV preference**: growth in female users correlates strongly with NEV adoption

### 8.3 Growth drivers

1. **NEVs lower the usage threshold**: features like driver-assist and smart parking attract more female users
2. **More approachable content formats**: short video and livestream are more intuitive than traditional text-and-spec comparisons
3. **Rising consumption independence**: the share of women buying cars independently is rising
4. **Optimized platform content ecosystem**: Dongchedi's interactivity and playfulness exceed those of traditional auto platforms

## IX. Core Findings and Investment Implications

### 9.1 User-profile summary

Dongchedi's typical user profile: **a 25-35-year-old urban male, middle-class, with a car-purchase budget of RMB 100,000-200,000, who gathers car information through short video and search, and places high trust in genuine owner reviews.** But the platform is rapidly expanding along three axes: younger (post-2000s), more female, and more lower-tier.

### 9.2 Core strengths

1. **Fastest-growing young-user base**: compared with Autohome's aging user base, Dongchedi has successfully captured young incremental users
2. **Douyin traffic referral forms a differentiated moat**: the 84% cross-platform jump rate cannot be replicated by other platforms
3. **Content format fits the trend**: the short-video + livestream + AI-recommendation distribution model suits the mobile-internet era better
4. **High concentration of user intent**: 70% of active users are high-intent shoppers, giving higher traffic quality than general-content platforms

### 9.3 Core risks

1. **Post-purchase retention challenge**: the low-frequency nature of car consumption means user activity drops sharply after purchase, requiring ongoing investment to sustain retention
2. **Content-credibility controversy**: the objectivity of its reviews has been questioned, which could undermine the foundation of user trust
3. **Reliance on Douyin traffic**: if Douyin's referral efficiency declines or policy changes occur, Dongchedi's growth engine will take a direct hit
4. **MAU still trails in absolute terms**: compared with Autohome's 65 million+ monthly actives, Dongchedi's 39 million still has ground to make up
5. **Balancing monetization and experience**: deepening commercialization could erode user trust in content objectivity

### 9.4 Key questions

From an investment perspective, the most worthwhile user metrics to track:
- **90-day post-purchase retention rate**: determines whether the platform can evolve from a "transaction tool" into a "car-use ecosystem"
- **Share of new users referred from Douyin**: measures the degree of dependence on ByteDance-system traffic
- **Ceiling on the female-user share**: currently ~28%-30%; whether it can reach 40%+ will determine the incremental headroom
- **User ARPU**: once MAU growth slows, the ability to lift per-user value will determine the sustainability of revenue growth
