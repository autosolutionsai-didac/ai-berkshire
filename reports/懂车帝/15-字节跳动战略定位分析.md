# Dongchedi's Position Within ByteDance's Overall Strategy

> Research date: 2026-05-30 | Analysis type: Parent-company strategic positioning research

---

## Core Conclusion

> Dongchedi is the testing ground and vanguard of ByteDance's "spin-off IPO" strategy. Against a backdrop where a full ByteDance IPO remains nowhere in sight, Dongchedi carries a dual mission: first, to provide an exit channel for long-time shareholders who have waited out a decade; second, to validate a replicable model of "independently operated vertical business + parent-company traffic empowerment." But this positioning itself contains an inherent contradiction — if independence is insufficient, capital markets won't award a high valuation; if independence is too strong, ByteDance's traffic-empowerment logic breaks down.

## II. Dongchedi's Organizational Affiliation and Reporting Line Within ByteDance

### 2.1 Historical Evolution

| Milestone | Organizational relationship | Key change |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 2017 | Toutiao Auto channel → standalone app | Incubated by the commercialization department, driven by Zhang Lidong |
| 2021 | Placed under the "Douyin" business unit | ByteDance's six-BU reorganization placed Dongchedi, as a vertical business, under the Douyin business unit |
| Late 2023 | Equity structure change | Toutiao exited as a shareholder; the controlling shareholder changed to Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. |
| Early 2024 | Established as an independent company | Employee labor relationships moved from ByteDance to the new company, achieving independent financial accounting |
| Mid-2024 | External financing completed | Sequoia China, KKR, General Atlantic, and Banyan Capital participated, at a $3 billion valuation |
| February 2026 | Planning a Hong Kong IPO | Targeting to raise $1.0-1.5 billion |

### 2.2 Current Reporting Structure

**ByteDance (parent company) → Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. (100% owned) → Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd.**

Dongchedi is now highly independent operationally, but ByteDance retains actual control through the equity structure. Dongchedi's strategy lead serves as the legal representative of the new company and no longer reports directly to the Douyin business unit.

**Worth noting**: although the organizational structure is now independent, Dongchedi still depends heavily on ByteDance-family traffic (automotive content on Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video is operated centrally by Dongchedi), meaning "independence" is more a financial and legal arrangement — the operational umbilical cord has not been fully severed.

## III. ByteDance's Strategy for Vertical Industries

### 3.1 Overview of Vertical Businesses

| Vertical | Product/brand | Current status | Outcome/trajectory |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Automotive** | Dongchedi | Independently operated, preparing for IPO | The most successful vertical business, valued at $3 billion |
| **Real estate** | Xingfuli | Retrenching, exited offline | Exited offline transactions in 2023, returned to online content, repositioned as "Douyin's real estate middle platform" |
| **Education** | Dali Education | Major strategic retrenchment | Massive layoffs after the 2021 "double reduction" policy; K-12 business essentially halted |
| **Gaming** | Nuverse | Strategic abandonment | Large-scale layoffs in late 2023, sold off some studios |
| **Healthcare** | Xiaohe Health | Operating quietly | No signal of large-scale investment observed |
| **Smart automotive** | Volcano Engine Auto | Actively expanding | Focused on smart cockpit and autonomous driving, partnered with 40+ automakers |

### 3.2 The Pattern Behind the Fates of Vertical Businesses

Looking at the rise and fall of ByteDance's vertical businesses, a pattern emerges:

**A vertical business survives only if it satisfies two conditions: (1) compatibility with ByteDance's traffic-monetization logic; (2) no dependence on heavy-asset offline operations.**

- **Dongchedi succeeded**: automotive is a major advertising category, and the lead-generation model is naturally compatible with ByteDance's traffic monetization
- **Xingfuli failed**: real-estate transactions depend on heavy, offline operations, and ByteDance's traffic advantage could not be converted directly
- **Dali Education failed**: policy risk compounded with heavy-asset operations, leaving ByteDance's algorithmic capability with no place to be applied
- **Nuverse failed**: gaming requires long-cycle content development capability, which clashes with ByteDance's "rapid iteration" culture

### 3.3 Volcano Engine Auto: ByteDance's Second Leg in the Automotive Sector

ByteDance's automotive footprint is not limited to Dongchedi (a consumer-facing information + transaction platform) — it also includes Volcano Engine's smart automotive business (enterprise-facing technology empowerment):

- **Smart cockpit**: an AI cockpit system built on the Doubao large model, already partnered with 40+ automakers including SAIC, Changan, and GAC Aion
- **Positioning**: benchmarked against Huawei HarmonyOS Intelligent Mobility's software play, but explicitly "not building cars"
- **Synergy with Dongchedi**: Volcano Engine's Seedance 2.0 already supports automatically distributing automotive content to Douyin, Xigua Video, and Dongchedi

This means ByteDance's complete automotive-industry footprint is: **Volcano Engine serving the enterprise side (empowering automakers) + Dongchedi serving the consumer side (connecting users and dealers)** — walking on two legs.

## IV. ByteDance's "Spin-Off IPO" Strategy

### 4.1 Why Was Dongchedi Chosen to Go Public First?

ByteDance's choice of Dongchedi as "the first to eat the crab" reflects clear strategic logic:

**(1) Small scale, controllable risk**

Dongchedi is valued at $3 billion, less than 1% of ByteDance's total valuation (roughly $300-350 billion). Even if the IPO underperforms, the impact on the parent company would be minimal — this is a "low-risk experiment."

**(2) Strong business independence**

Dongchedi is a vertical automotive platform with clear business boundaries, and it doesn't touch Douyin's core advertising and e-commerce revenue, so it won't trigger conflicts of interest after the spin-off. By contrast, spinning off Douyin e-commerce or Ocean Engine would make related-party transactions and revenue allocation extremely complicated.

**(3) Providing an exit channel for long-time shareholders**

ByteDance's early investors (Sequoia, KKR, General Atlantic, and others) have waited on the sidelines for more than a decade without an IPO exit. An independent Dongchedi listing lets these investors realize part of their investment return by holding Dongchedi shares first.

**(4) Lower regulatory resistance**

Dongchedi is an automotive information platform that doesn't touch sensitive issues such as data security or cross-border data flows, so its regulatory approval hurdles are far lower than those facing Douyin or TikTok.

**(5) Validating the spin-off model**

If the Dongchedi IPO succeeds, ByteDance will have a replicable "spin-off IPO" playbook, paving the way for spinning off larger business segments in the future.

### 4.2 What Other Businesses Might Be Spun Off for an IPO?

| Candidate business | Likelihood of spin-off | Analysis |
|---------|-----------|------|
| **Douyin Group** | High | Renamed "Douyin Group" in 2022, appointed a CFO, and has made IPO preparations. But its scale is enormous, it touches data security, and its political sensitivity is high |
| **Feishu (Lark)** | Medium | An independent business unit, but still loss-making and not yet IPO-ready in the near term |
| **Volcano Engine** | Medium | A cloud + AI business, growing fast but not yet profitable. If the AI MaaS business scales up in the future, a spin-off becomes possible |
| **TikTok** | Low | Heavily affected by US-China relations; the uncertainty of forced sale/ban makes an independent IPO nearly impossible |

### 4.3 Obstacles to a Full ByteDance IPO

ByteDance itself has been rumored to be preparing an IPO five times but has yet to file. The core obstacles include:

1. **Dual US-China regulatory pressure**: TikTok's geopolitical risk makes a US listing nearly impossible; Chinese regulators have also grown more cautious about approving listings by large internet platforms
2. **Valuation dynamics**: ByteDance's secondary-market valuation is roughly $300-350 billion, and there is uncertainty over whether public markets can support that valuation
3. **Zhang Yiming's personal preference**: since retiring from operations in 2021, Zhang Yiming has been less involved in management decisions and has consistently been cautious about going public
4. **Disclosure pressure**: going public means disclosing financial data publicly; as a private company, ByteDance currently enjoys an information asymmetry advantage in competition

## V. The Relationship Between Dongchedi and Douyin's Automotive Content

### 5.1 Positioning: Synergy Far Outweighs Competition

In July 2023, Dongchedi announced a full merger of automotive content across Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video, **with Dongchedi serving as the overall operator**, coordinating automotive content resources across all platforms.

**Key data after the merger**:
- Combined daily active automotive users across the four platforms: 310 million
- Automotive content creators: 7.5 million
- Average daily automotive content publishers: 2 million
- Average daily consumption: 500 million views
- Year-over-year user growth: 82%

### 5.2 Traffic Allocation Mechanism: A Funnel-Shaped Collaboration

```
Douyin (broad traffic entry point)
  ├── Lifestyle scenarios, creative short videos → sparking automotive interest, seeding brand awareness
  ├── 84% of users click through to Dongchedi to browse the model library
  └── 78% of users search for specific model series and read in-depth reviews
        │
        ▼
Dongchedi (vertical conversion platform)
  ├── Professional model library, in-depth reviews, user word-of-mouth
  ├── Model comparisons, configuration analysis
  └── Lead distribution → dealers → sale closed
```

**In essence, this is a "Douyin seeds interest, Dongchedi converts it" dual-platform collaboration model**. Douyin supplies massive broad-based traffic, while Dongchedi handles specialized conversion; the two are not competitors but sit at the upstream and downstream ends of the same funnel.

### 5.3 Potential Points of Conflict of Interest

But potential tensions shouldn't be overlooked either:

1. **Douyin's own automotive advertising revenue**: brand advertising placed by automakers on Douyin belongs to Douyin (Ocean Engine), while performance advertising placed on Dongchedi belongs to Dongchedi. There is a gray area of competition between the two for advertising budgets
2. **Divvying up interests after an independent listing**: once Dongchedi lists independently, the pricing of related-party transactions with Douyin will become a sensitive issue. Will traffic support be charged for? How will it be priced? Both will affect Dongchedi's income statement
3. **The sustainability of Dongchedi's unified operating authority**: Dongchedi is currently the "unified operator" of ByteDance-family automotive content, but whether this arrangement can continue after the listing depends on whether ByteDance is willing to keep ceding this authority

## VII. If ByteDance Goes Public as a Whole, Would the Significance of Dongchedi's Separate Listing Diminish?

### 7.1 In the Short Term: No, It Would Not Diminish

A full ByteDance IPO is nearly impossible to achieve in the near term, for the following reasons:

1. TikTok's geopolitical risk has not been resolved, blocking a US listing
2. The pace of Chinese regulatory approval for large platform listings remains cautious
3. ByteDance still has no CFO in place (the status of Gao Zhun, who joined in 2022, is unclear), so signals about IPO preparation are ambiguous
4. ByteDance's 2025 profit has already reached $50 billion, with ample cash flow and no financing pressure

Therefore, the core value of Dongchedi's independent listing — **providing an exit channel for long-time shareholders** — will not be superseded by a full ByteDance IPO within the foreseeable 2-3 years.

### 7.2 In the Long Term: There Is a Risk of Marginalization

Suppose ByteDance achieves a full IPO in 2028-2030:

- As a wholly owned ByteDance subsidiary, Dongchedi's financial data would be consolidated into ByteDance's reported results
- Investors could gain indirect exposure to Dongchedi's value simply by holding ByteDance stock
- Dongchedi's "scarcity value" as an independently listed company would decline substantially
- A dual-listing structure (parent company and subsidiary both listed) would add regulatory complexity around related-party transactions

**But viewed the other way**: if Dongchedi has already gone public independently and built an independent shareholder base and brand recognition, ByteDance may choose to maintain this structure (similar to the relationship between Alibaba and Ant Group) rather than force a merger.

### 7.3 Core Judgment

> Dongchedi's independent listing is not an "ultimate goal" but rather ByteDance's "expedient measure" within the current window. Its strategic significance lies more in: (1) resolving the exit problem for long-time shareholders; (2) validating the spin-off model; (3) letting Dongchedi itself capture independent brand value and capital-market recognition. Whether this arrangement retains meaning after a full ByteDance IPO will depend on the market environment and regulatory posture at that time.

## VIII. Comprehensive Strategic Position Assessment

### 8.1 Dongchedi's Importance to ByteDance

**Financial importance: Low.** Dongchedi is valued at $3 billion, less than 1% of ByteDance's total valuation. Even raising $1.0-1.5 billion in an IPO would be negligible to ByteDance (with annual profit of $50 billion).

**Strategic importance: Moderate.** Dongchedi is the most successful case among ByteDance's vertical businesses and the testing ground for the spin-off-IPO strategy. Its success or failure will influence ByteDance's future capital-operations strategy for other business segments.

**Brand importance: Low.** Most users don't know, or don't care, that Dongchedi is a ByteDance product.

### 8.2 ByteDance's Importance to Dongchedi

**Traffic importance: Extremely high.** 96% of Dongchedi's user reach depends on ByteDance-family platforms — this is its lifeline.

**Technology importance: High.** Recommendation algorithms, big data, and AI capability all come from the ByteDance ecosystem.

**Brand endorsement: Moderate.** The "ByteDance-affiliated" label helps with financing and the IPO, but has little effect on end users.

### 8.3 Implications for Investors

1. **Dongchedi's independence is "limited independence"** — financially independent but highly dependent on the parent company for traffic and technology; this dependency will be scrutinized repeatedly by capital markets at IPO time
2. **ByteDance will not sacrifice its core interests for Dongchedi's sake** — if Douyin's own automotive advertising business conflicts with Dongchedi, ByteDance will most likely prioritize protecting Douyin
3. **The spin-off IPO is a "trial run," not "abandonment"** — ByteDance's continued resource investment in Dongchedi (RMB 500 million+ in annual cash support, RMB 100 billion+ in traffic) shows this isn't about shedding a burden, but hoping Dongchedi grows big and strong enough to earn an independent valuation in capital markets
4. **Watch related-party-transaction disclosures** — in the IPO prospectus, the traffic settlement method and pricing mechanism between Dongchedi and the ByteDance family will be key to judging its true profitability
