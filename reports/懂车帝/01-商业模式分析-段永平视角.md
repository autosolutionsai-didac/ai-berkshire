# Dongchedi Business Model Analysis -- Duan Yongping's Investment Lens

> Analysis date: May 2026
> Analysis dimensions: nature of the business model, user value, moat, business matrix
> Core question: Is this a "good business"? Is there differentiation? Is there pricing power?

---

## I. What business is Dongchedi actually in?

### 1.1 One-sentence definition

At its core, Dongchedi is an **auto-lead trading platform riding on ByteDance's traffic ecosystem**, dressed up as a content platform but really in the business of lead brokerage.

More precisely, Dongchedi is ByteDance's "traffic-monetization pipeline" in the auto vertical. It converts a vast pool of loosely-interested users into car-purchase-intent users through short video and algorithmic recommendation, then sells those "leads" to dealers.

### 1.2 Breaking down the revenue sources

Dongchedi has not yet gone public, and its specific revenue structure has not been publicly disclosed. Based on public information, the following is an inference:

| Revenue type | Estimated share | Notes |
|---------|---------|------|
| **CPS commission (pay-per-transaction)** | 40-50% | Launched in 2022, rolled out broadly in 2023; partner dealers have surpassed 28,500, covering 110+ brands. Dealers pay commission based on actual transaction volume — currently the core revenue model |
| **Brand advertising** | 25-35% | Automakers run brand ads, splash ads, and feed ads on Dongchedi and Douyin's auto channel |
| **Value-added SaaS services** | 10-15% | Paid tools such as the "Maiche Tong" dealer-management system, the "Judongche" integrated operations platform, and "Dongche Cloud Store" |
| **Used cars/car care/finance** | 5-10% | Used-car transaction commissions, "Dongdong Car Care" services, insurance referrals, and other emerging businesses, still at an early stage |

**Revenue estimate**: Dongchedi's 2024 financing round valued it at roughly $3 billion. Applying the typical 5-8x P/S multiple for a tech platform implies annual revenue of roughly $400-600 million (about RMB 3.0-4.0 billion). Given the push toward IPO, it may already be close to breakeven or slightly profitable.

### 1.3 What unique advantages does it get from ByteDance?

This is the key to understanding Dongchedi. ByteDance has given Dongchedi three things, each of which competitors cannot replicate:

**First, traffic.** Douyin's DAU exceeds 700 million, with auto-interested users watching auto content 310 million times a day. After users encounter an auto short video on Douyin, 84% go on to jump to Dongchedi to check the vehicle-model database, and 78% search for a specific model series. This is a steady "highway" pumping traffic straight from Douyin's general-entertainment pool into Dongchedi's professional decision-making arena.

**Second, algorithms.** ByteDance's core technology asset is its recommendation algorithm. Dongchedi has inherited this "content finds the user" distribution logic. Traditional vertical auto media (Autohome, Yiche) operate on a "user finds the content" model, where users arrive with clear needs and search; Dongchedi operates on "content finds the user," starting to shape decisions before the user has even formed a clear purchase intent. This captures users at the very top of the funnel.

**Third, content-production capacity.** 7.5 million auto content creators, generating content consumed 500 million times a day on average. Dongchedi did not build this itself — it is a spillover effect of ByteDance's entire content ecosystem.

**Duan Yongping's lens — the follow-up question**: are these three advantages truly Dongchedi's own, or are they lent to it by ByteDance?

The answer skews toward the latter. This is a fundamental problem — Dongchedi's core competitiveness is, to a large extent, "borrowed." If ByteDance ever shifts strategy and turns down the traffic tap, Dongchedi will feel the impact immediately. Since being spun off from ByteDance in 2023, this dependency has not truly been severed — if anything, it remains deeply intertwined, with Dongchedi continuing to operate the auto content and commercialization for Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua.

## II. User Value Analysis

### 2.1 Value delivered to C-end users (car buyers)

**What does Dongchedi provide?**
- Short-video-format auto review content (more intuitive, easier to consume)
- A professional real-world testing database: covering 4,000+ models, with over 1 million real-test data points
- Large-scale IP events such as NEV winter testing (now an industry benchmark event)
- AI car-selection tools: fuzzy model search, multi-vehicle comparison, smart recommendation
- Genuine word-of-mouth reviews from a community of a million-plus active car owners

**Comparing the differences with Autohome and Yiche**:

| Dimension | Autohome | Yiche | Dongchedi |
|------|---------|------|--------|
| Content format | Primarily text and images, traditional forum | Original programming + text and images | Short video + livestream + text and images |
| User acquisition | Search engines + brand awareness | Tencent-system traffic referral | Douyin ecosystem referral |
| User profile | Skews more mature, already has purchase intent | Intermediate stage | Skews younger, starts from general interest |
| Decision stage | Model-selection, price-comparison stage | Model-selection stage | Full path from "planting the seed" to "decision" |
| Interactivity | Forum-style, weaker | Moderate | Short video + livestream, strongly interactive |

**Core difference**: Autohome serves "people who already want to buy a car," while Dongchedi serves "people who don't yet know what car they want." This difference means Dongchedi establishes influence at the very top of the funnel.

**User-penetration data**: since October 2021, Dongchedi's DAU has ranked first in the industry. The company states that "6 out of every 10 deep-intent car shoppers use Dongchedi."

### 2.2 Value delivered to B-end customers (automakers/dealers)

**Why do dealers choose Dongchedi?**

1. **The appeal of the CPS model**: paying per transaction rather than per lead lowers dealers' psychological barrier. 92.9% of dealers are willing to recommend it to peers, and 94.8% are willing to keep working with it.
2. **Scale advantage in traffic**: Douyin + Dongchedi together cover over 500 million auto-interested users, several times that of Autohome.
3. **SaaS-tool enablement**: "Judongche" provides an integrated platform for lead management, ad placement, and operations across both Douyin and Dongchedi.
4. **Incremental users**: Douyin brings "incremental" users (a broadly interested population), whereas Autohome's users are more "existing" users (those with clearly defined purchase needs already).

**But there is also B-end dissatisfaction**:
- Dealers' customer-acquisition cost has risen 20% over the past four years
- The increase in lead volume has driven up operating costs by 35%
- Lead conversion rate has meanwhile fallen 14%
- Under the CPS model, budgets are hard to control, and some dealers resist it
- There are large numbers of complaints about phone harassment on the Heimao complaint platform

### 2.3 Analysis of traffic sources

**Owned traffic vs. Douyin referral**

This is Dongchedi's biggest "gray area." The company has never publicly disclosed the ratio of owned traffic to referred traffic, but it can be inferred from the following clues:

- Dongchedi app's standalone DAU is about 10 million
- The four properties — Douyin + Dongchedi + Toutiao + Xigua — together reach 310 million auto-interested users a day on average
- 84% of Douyin's auto-interested users go on to jump to Dongchedi

**A conservative estimate is that Dongchedi's owned traffic (users opening the app directly) may account for only 10-15% of the total user base it serves**, with the overwhelming majority of high-value traffic coming from the Douyin ecosystem. This means Dongchedi's ability to survive independently is questionable.

## III. Moat Analysis (Duan Yongping's Standard)

Duan Yongping repeatedly emphasizes the standard for a good business: differentiation, pricing power, and a durable long-term moat. Let's assess each in turn.

### 3.1 Brand moat — moderate-to-weak

**Current state**:
- Has some recognition among young users ("look up cars on Dongchedi")
- Large IP events like winter and summer testing have built a "professional testing" image
- But brand recognition is highly dependent on the Douyin ecosystem; standalone brand strength lags Autohome

**Assessment against Duan's standard**: the essence of brand strength is "users choose you actively over competitors." Dongchedi's users are more often pushed there by the Douyin algorithm than actively downloading the app because they recognize the Dongchedi brand. This is not a genuine brand moat.

**Score: 3/10**

### 3.2 Network effects — nascent but unstable

**Flywheel logic**:
```
More creators produce content → More users watch → More dealers join
→ More transactions occur → More commission revenue → More investment back into the content ecosystem
```

**Current state**:
- 7.5 million auto content creators (largest in the industry)
- DAU of 10 million+ (#1 in the industry)
- 28,500 partner dealers (high coverage)
- 57% of merchants seeing rising transaction volume

**But the flywheel has one fatal weakness**: content creators are mostly active on Douyin, not within the Dongchedi app itself. The "content" gear of the flywheel is effectively borrowed. If creators can post the same content on Douyin, Kuaishou, and Bilibili simultaneously, then Dongchedi's exclusivity on the content side is very weak.

**Score: 4/10**

### 3.3 Switching costs — low

**C-end users**:
- The cost of downloading another app is essentially zero
- The migration cost for accumulated browsing history, favorites, etc. is very low
- Buying a car is a low-frequency decision (roughly once every 3-5 years), so user loyalty is naturally low

**B-end dealers**:
- Under the CPS model, dealers can switch platforms at any time
- Simultaneous multi-platform advertising is the norm, not exclusive partnership
- The "Judongche" SaaS tool has some stickiness, but it is not irreplaceable
- Over 50% of dealers are operating at a loss, making them highly sensitive to platform fees

**Score: 2/10**

### 3.4 Data barrier — moderate

**Unique data assets**:
- Over 50 million structured data points
- A vehicle-model database covering 76,000 models
- A real-world testing database covering 4,000+ models with over 1 million data points
- Genuine word-of-mouth review data from a million-plus active car owners
- User behavior data (browsing, comparison, and price-inquiry paths)

**Assessment**: the data volume is indeed substantial, but the key to a data barrier lies not in volume but in exclusivity and irreplaceability. Vehicle spec data is public, and other platforms can conduct real-world testing too; the truly valuable pieces are user-behavior data and dealer-transaction data. But the value of this data is highly dependent on user scale — if users leave, the data loses value.

**Score: 5/10**

### 3.5 Technology barrier — moderate-to-strong (but sourced from the parent)

**Technical advantages**:
- ByteDance's recommendation-algorithm capability is world-class
- Application of large AI models in scenarios like model selection, comparison, and valuation
- Short-video content distribution and comprehension technology
- "Judongche" integrates smart placement and operations tools across both Douyin and Dongchedi

**But the key question**: how much of this technical capability is Dongchedi's own R&D, versus provided by ByteDance's central platform? After the spin-off, can this technical capability continue to receive ByteDance's support?

**Score: 6/10 (if counting ByteDance's backing) / 3/10 (if counting only its own technology)**

### 3.6 Duan Yongping's three core questions

**Question one: Is this a "good business"?**

**Not entirely.** In Duan Yongping's view, a good business has several traits: stable demand, controllable competition, high margins, and asset-light. Dongchedi meets the "stable demand" criterion (people continually need car-purchase information), but falls notably short on the others:

- Competition is intense: beyond the traditional rivalry with Autohome and Yiche, there is also traffic diversion from Kuaishou Auto, Xiaohongshu Auto, and Douyin's own auto channel
- The business model is not sufficiently "light": the CPS model ties revenue to car-transaction volume, while China's auto market growth is slowing and price wars are fierce, with dealers suffering widespread losses
- Margins are questionable: sustained investment is needed in the content ecosystem (RMB 500 million in cash plus RMB 150 billion in traffic support for creators), and the CPS model's commission rate has a ceiling

**Question two: Is there differentiation?**

**Yes, but the differentiation does not come from Dongchedi itself — it comes from ByteDance.** Dongchedi's differentiation is "ByteDance traffic + algorithmic recommendation," which Autohome and Yiche indeed lack. But this also means Dongchedi's differentiation is "borrowed," not something it created itself.

Duan Yongping often says "don't do things you don't understand" and "the moat has to be your own." Dongchedi's moat is more like "living inside someone else's castle."

**Question three: Is there pricing power?**

**There is some pricing power, but it is weakening.** Vertical auto media were once called "the client of the client," holding absolute leverage over dealers. But the following trends are eroding that pricing power:

- Automakers are building their own direct-sales systems (Huawei/Aito has already suspended cooperation with the three major vertical media)
- Many NEV makers use a direct-sales model that doesn't require traditional lead services
- Over 50% of dealers are operating at a loss, reducing their tolerance for platform fees
- The CPS model essentially hands pricing power over to the market (the commission rate is subject to transaction prices and competition)

## IV. Business Matrix Analysis

### 4.1 Status of end-to-end integration

```
Content (short video/text-image/livestream) → Leads → Transactions → Finance/insurance/car care
     ██████████████          ████████    ████████      ██░░░░░░░░
     Completion: 95%          Completion: 70%  Completion: 60%    Completion: 20%
```

**Content layer (95% complete)**:
- The short-video, livestream, and text-image content ecosystem is already very mature
- 7.5 million creators, 500 million auto-interested users
- Large-scale IP events like winter and summer testing have become industry benchmarks

**Lead layer (70% complete)**:
- The CPS model is operating, with 28,500 partner dealers
- But declining lead quality and falling conversion rates are an industry-wide problem
- User privacy complaints (phone harassment) are hurting the experience

**Transaction layer (60% complete)**:
- The "one-click car purchase" feature for new cars has launched
- The used-car business covers 300,000+ listed vehicles across 350+ cities
- Physical car malls already exist in Wuhan, Chongqing, and Hefei
- But offline transaction-fulfillment capability is still being built out

**Finance/insurance/car care (20% complete)**:
- "Dongdong Car Care" has just begun, piloting in Chongqing
- Insurance referral is embedded in the transaction flow
- No public information on financial products yet
- This layer lags noticeably behind Autohome

### 4.2 NEV vs. gasoline-vehicle business focus

Dongchedi is tilting significantly toward new-energy vehicles:

- The winter- and summer-testing IPs are centered on NEVs
- The "NEV Pilot Program" specifically supports 100 leading NEV creators
- In 2025, Dongchedi's trade-in service drove RMB 380 billion in auto consumption
- NEV makers' content-marketing needs are inherently stronger (they need to educate the market)

**This is the right directional choice.** NEV penetration continues to rise (already over 50% in 2025), and the NEV purchase-decision chain is longer with greater information asymmetry, meaning Dongchedi's content-plus-recommendation model can deliver greater value.

### 4.3 Dongchedi vs. the Douyin auto channel

This is a subtle internal relationship:

| Dimension | Douyin auto channel | Dongchedi |
|------|------------|--------|
| Positioning | General-interest content consumption | Professional purchase decision-making |
| User intent | Casual, entertainment browsing | Purposeful, has a purchase goal |
| Content depth | Shallow (15 seconds-3 minutes) | Deep (reviews, comparisons, quotes) |
| Monetization | Primarily brand advertising | Leads + CPS + SaaS |
| Traffic direction | Feeds out to Dongchedi | Receives Douyin traffic |

**In actual operations**, Dongchedi unifies the operation of auto content and commercialization across Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua. The "Judongche" platform blends traffic across both ends.

**Risk point**: if Douyin's own auto-commercialization capability strengthens (e.g., livestreamed car sales on Douyin), Dongchedi could be "disintermediated." ByteDance is fully capable of completing the loop from content to transaction entirely within Douyin, at which point Dongchedi's independent value would come into question.

## V. Overall Assessment

### 5.1 How would Duan Yongping view this business?

Applying Duan Yongping's own frame of reference:

**"The nature of this business isn't good enough."**

Reasons:

1. **It isn't "doing the right thing"**: Dongchedi's core competitiveness (traffic + algorithms) is not its own — it is given by its parent (ByteDance). Duan Yongping has always emphasized "do things you can control," while Dongchedi's fate largely depends on ByteDance's strategic decisions.

2. **No genuine pricing power**: the CPS model appears advanced (pay for performance), but it also means revenue moves with the auto market's fortunes. In an environment of automaker price wars and widespread dealer losses, it is very difficult to raise commission rates.

3. **The moat is "rented"**: strip away ByteDance's support, and what remains of Dongchedi's moat — its database, app users, brand awareness — does not hold an overwhelming advantage over Autohome.

4. **Unfavorable industry structure**: vertical auto media is essentially an "information intermediary" business, and the value of information intermediaries may be significantly compressed in the AI era. When users can simply ask an AI "what car should I buy" and get a personalized recommendation, the value of the middle-layer platform gets eroded.

5. **Low-frequency consumption + high competition**: buying a car is a low-frequency purchase that happens once every 3-5 years, so users have essentially no loyalty. Meanwhile there are many competitors (Autohome, Yiche, Kuaishou, Xiaohongshu, and automakers' own channels), and no winner-take-all structure exists.

### 5.2 Valuation and investment appeal

- Current valuation: about $3 billion (2024 financing round)
- IPO target: raising $1.0-1.5 billion, implying a possible listed valuation of $5-8 billion
- Comparable reference: Autohome's market cap is about $2.1 billion (US-listed), with both revenue and profit declining

**Risk points**:
- If the IPO is priced above $5 billion, that implies a P/S multiple above 10x relative to the estimated RMB 3.0-4.0 billion in revenue — expensive for an information-intermediary business
- ByteDance's motivation for spinning off and listing the business may be driven more by capital-operation needs than by the business itself having sufficiently large standalone value
- Autohome's decline trajectory (net profit down 13% in 2024, both revenue and profit down in 2025) may be a preview of the industry's direction

### 5.3 One-sentence summary

**Dongchedi is a business where "a good rider rides someone else's good horse."** The rider (the operating team) is indeed excellent, and the horse (ByteDance's traffic and algorithms) is indeed strong, but the horse doesn't belong to the rider. Duan Yongping would not buy this kind of business — what he wants is a company that "owns its own good horse."

For investors, Dongchedi's core bet ultimately comes down to just one thing: whether ByteDance will keep handing the traffic and commercialization rights for the auto vertical to Dongchedi, rather than pulling them back into Douyin itself. The odds on that bet depend on ByteDance's strategic will, not on Dongchedi's own capability.
