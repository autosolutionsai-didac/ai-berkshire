## I. The CSRC's Overseas Listing Filing System

### 1.1 Regulatory framework

Starting March 31, 2023, the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) formally implemented the "Trial Administrative Measures for the Overseas Issuance and Listing of Securities by Domestic Enterprises" and supporting guidelines, replacing the previous approval-based system with a filing-based system. Key points:

- **Filing timing**: Companies must file with the CSRC within 3 business days after submitting a listing application to an overseas securities regulator
- **Review timeline**: If materials are complete, the CSRC completes its review and publicizes the result within 20 business days; if materials are incomplete, a notice requesting supplementation is issued within 5 business days, and the company must supplement within 30 days
- **Scope of application**: Both direct listings and indirect listings (including VIE structures) require filing

### 1.2 Key areas of focus in filing review

Based on published statistics on the CSRC's feedback comments, review focus is concentrated in the following areas:

1. **Data security and personal information protection**: More than half of filing companies are asked about related compliance issues, which is especially critical for the data-intensive Dongchedi
2. **Industry access and foreign investment restrictions**: The TMT sector is a key review area, requiring confirmation of whether the business touches the negative list for foreign investment access
3. **VIE structure compliance**: Requires full disclosure of the VIE arrangement, related risks, and mitigation measures
4. **National security review**: Overseas listing activity that could endanger national security is prohibited

### 1.3 Dongchedi's filing progress

As of now, no specific filing record for Dongchedi with the CSRC has been found through public channels. According to media reports from February to March 2026, Dongchedi has selected Citigroup and Goldman Sachs as IPO underwriters and plans to list on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange as early as 2026, seeking to raise $1.0-1.5 billion. This suggests the formal filing process may already be underway or about to begin.

### 1.4 Reference data on the filing environment

- As of the end of 2025, a total of 179 companies had received CSRC overseas listing filing notices, up 15% from 2024
- In March 2026, 29 companies submitted new applications, all choosing to list in Hong Kong
- In April 2026, 17 companies completed filing, of which 16 were headed to Hong Kong and 1 to Nasdaq
- Hong Kong listings have become the overwhelmingly dominant direction, with regulators taking a relatively friendly stance

## II. Impact of the Data Security Law and Personal Information Protection Law

### 2.1 Analysis of Dongchedi's data sensitivity

As an automotive information and transaction platform, Dongchedi handles the following types of data:

| Data type | Sensitivity level | Notes |
|---------|---------|------|
| User browsing/search behavior | Medium | Reflects consumption preferences; classified as personal information |
| User registration information | Medium-high | Phone numbers, names, and other basic personal information |
| Automotive transaction data | Medium | Purchase intent, pricing information, dealer data |
| Content creator data | Medium | Identity and behavioral data of approximately 7.5 million creators |
| Automotive technical review data | Low | Vehicle performance parameters; classified as business data |
| Location information | High | Involved in features such as nearby dealer recommendations |

**Key data scale**:
- More than 10 million daily active users
- More than 500 million automotive-interested users
- Approximately 7.5 million content creators
- More than 30,000 dealers served

### 2.2 Applicable regulatory matrix

**Data Security Law (effective September 2021)**:
- Requires data to be classified and managed by category and tier
- Cross-border transfer of "important data" requires a security assessment
- Automotive industry data may fall within the "important data" category as determined by the relevant industry regulator

**Personal Information Protection Law (effective November 2021)**:
- Processors handling the personal information of more than 1 million people must undergo a security assessment by the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) before providing personal information overseas
- Dongchedi's user base far exceeds this threshold

**Regulations on Network Data Security Management (effective January 2025)**:
- Elaborates and supplements the relevant systems established by the higher-level laws
- Clarifies the classification obligations of data processors

**Guidelines on the Security of Automotive Data Export (2025 Edition) (draft for comment)**:
- Jointly drafted by eight departments, specifying nine exemption scenarios for automotive data export
- Cumulative provision of personal information of more than 1 million people overseas must be declared for a data export security assessment
- Operators of critical information infrastructure providing personal information overseas must likewise file a declaration

### 2.3 Substantive impact on Dongchedi

A key advantage of Dongchedi choosing to list in Hong Kong: **a Hong Kong listing itself does not directly trigger the data export security assessment obligation**. However, the following scenarios still warrant attention:

1. If, after listing, overseas investors/audit firms gain access to user data, this could trigger data export requirements
2. The prospectus must fully disclose data compliance measures
3. The company must demonstrate it has established a sound data classification and tiering system

## III. Cybersecurity Review Requirements

### 3.1 Core provisions

Key provisions of the Measures for Cybersecurity Review (effective February 15, 2022):

- **Mandatory declaration trigger**: Network platform operators holding the personal information of more than 1 million users that **list overseas** must declare for a cybersecurity review before applying to list with a foreign securities regulator
- **Review focus**: The risk of critical information infrastructure being illegally controlled; the risk of core data, important data, or large volumes of personal information being stolen, leaked, or illegally transferred overseas; and the risk that listing could be influenced, controlled, or maliciously exploited by a foreign government

### 3.2 Does Dongchedi need a cybersecurity review?

**Key distinction: Hong Kong vs. the United States**

The 2022 revision of the Measures for Cybersecurity Review explicitly specifies that the trigger for mandatory cybersecurity review declaration is **"listing abroad" (i.e., outside China)**. During the 2022 revision process, legislators deliberately removed a scenario from the earlier draft Regulations on Network Data that covered "a data processor listing in Hong Kong that affects or may affect national security."

**Conclusion: A Hong Kong listing by Dongchedi does not automatically trigger the mandatory cybersecurity review declaration obligation.**

However, two exceptions warrant attention:
1. If the CAC proactively determines that Dongchedi's products or services "affect or may affect national security," it can still initiate a review on its own authority
2. If Dongchedi were to consider listing or issuing depositary receipts in a market such as the United States in the future, declaration would be mandatory

### 3.3 Compliance steps Dongchedi has already taken

- Dongchedi has passed the National Information Security Level Protection assessment and filing (Level 3)
- In April 2026, Dongchedi's automotive-vertical large language model passed the CAC's generative AI service filing (publicized by the Chongqing Cyberspace Administration)
- These compliance actions indicate the company is proactively clearing regulatory obstacles ahead of the IPO

## IV. ByteDance's VIE Structure and Dongchedi's Ownership Architecture

### 4.1 Spin-off timeline

Dongchedi's path to independence breaks down into several key milestones:

| Time | Event |
|------|------|
| 2017 | The Dongchedi app launched, under ByteDance |
| October 2023 | Chongqing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. was established as the app's operating entity |
| December 2023 | Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. was established, becoming the new controlling shareholder |
| December 21, 2023 | Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. completed a corporate registration change, with Toutiao exiting the equity structure |
| June-October 2024 | Completed its first round of independent financing of approximately $600-800 million, at a $3 billion valuation |
| February-March 2026 | Reportedly selected Citigroup and Goldman Sachs as IPO underwriters |

### 4.2 Current onshore ownership structure

```
Xiamen Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. (100% ownership)
├── He Jian (President of Dongchedi): 60%
└── Zhao Jing: 40%
    │
    └── Beijing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (onshore operating entity)
         │
         ├── Chongqing Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd. (app operating entity, wholly owned subsidiary)
         └── [Pledged to] Beijing Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd.
```

### 4.3 Clues to the offshore structure

Based on public information:
- Beijing Dongchedi's look-through equity shows it is wholly owned by **Hong Kong Dongchedi Technology Co., Ltd.** (formerly "Hong Kong Xingfuli Technology Co., Ltd.", established in 2021)
- This indicates Dongchedi has already built a classic VIE/contractual control structure: **offshore listing vehicle (likely a Cayman company) → Hong Kong intermediate holding company → onshore VIE contractual control → onshore operating entity**

### 4.4 ByteDance's actual control

Although Toutiao has ostensibly exited Dongchedi's direct equity structure, ByteDance retains substantive control through the following mechanisms:

1. **Equity pledge**: Xiamen Dongchezu has pledged Beijing Dongchedi's equity to Beijing Dongchezu Technology Co., Ltd. (likely affiliated with ByteDance)
2. **Management arrangements**: He Jian, as President of Dongchedi, is himself a ByteDance executive
3. **Business dependency**: Dongchedi's content distribution is deeply dependent on the Douyin ecosystem, and ByteDance maintains substantive influence through traffic and technology
4. **Overlapping historical investors**: HongShan, KKR, and General Atlantic are both ByteDance shareholders and Dongchedi investors

### 4.5 Key review points for VIE structures in CSRC filings

Under current regulations, overseas listing filings by VIE-structured companies require particular attention to:

- The completeness and legality of the VIE agreements
- Whether the contractual control arrangement carries a risk of being ruled invalid
- Whether the relevant industry falls under the foreign investment access negative list
- Proof of the issuer's actual control over the VIE entity

**A special issue facing Dongchedi**: Does automotive information service qualify as a value-added telecommunications business? If so, it would be subject to foreign investment access restrictions, and the necessity and compliance of the VIE structure would become a key focus of review.

## V. Analysis of Hong Kong Stock Exchange Listing Requirements

### 5.1 Available listing paths

Dongchedi's Hong Kong listing theoretically has two paths:

**Path One: Standard Main Board listing (Chapter 8)**

Profit test requirements:
- Profit in the most recent year of no less than HK$35 million
- Cumulative profit over the preceding two years of no less than HK$45 million
- Three years of trading record

Market capitalization/revenue test (alternative to the profit test):
- Market capitalization at listing of no less than HK$4 billion
- Revenue in the most recent audited financial year of no less than HK$500 million

**Path Two: Specialist Technology Company listing (Chapter 18C)**

- Commercialized companies: minimum market capitalization of HK$4 billion (after the September 2024 revision, down from HK$6 billion, effective through August 2027)
- Non-commercialized companies: minimum market capitalization of HK$8 billion (down from HK$10 billion)
- R&D spending requirement: for commercialized companies, R&D spending must account for at least 15% of total operating expenses
- Industries covered: including advanced hardware fields such as electric and autonomous vehicles

### 5.2 Dongchedi's most likely choice

**Prediction: Standard Main Board listing (market capitalization/revenue test), not Chapter 18C.**

Rationale:
1. Dongchedi's valuation of approximately $3 billion (approximately HK$23.4 billion) far exceeds the HK$4 billion threshold
2. As an automotive information and transaction platform, Dongchedi's core business is advertising and lead-generation services, making it an internet platform rather than a "specialist technology" company
3. If already profitable, it can use the profit test; if not, it can use the market capitalization/revenue test
4. Autohome (a comparable peer) returned to the Hong Kong market via the standard route in 2021, providing a valid precedent

### 5.3 Hong Kong Stock Exchange's review focus areas

For an internet platform company like Dongchedi, the Hong Kong Stock Exchange is likely to focus on:

1. **Business sustainability**: The degree of dependence on the ByteDance/Douyin ecosystem, and independent operating capability
2. **Related-party transactions**: The pricing fairness of related-party transactions with ByteDance, such as traffic procurement and technical services
3. **Non-compete restrictions**: Whether ByteDance has committed not to engage in competing businesses
4. **Data compliance disclosure**: The impact of China's data security regulations on the business
5. **Control arrangements**: Disclosure of the risks associated with the VIE structure

### 5.4 The new "Technology Enterprises Channel" mechanism

Starting May 6, 2025, the Hong Kong Stock Exchange and the Securities and Futures Commission jointly launched the "Technology Enterprises Channel" mechanism:
- Allows specialist technology companies and biotech companies to receive dedicated guidance before formally applying to list
- Allows listing applications to be submitted confidentially
- Reduces the communication cost between companies and regulators

## VI. The Potential Impact of the TikTok Saga on ByteDance-Affiliated Listings

### 6.1 Recap of the TikTok saga

| Time | Event |
|------|------|
| April 2024 | Biden signed the "divest-or-ban" bill |
| January 17, 2025 | The U.S. Supreme Court ruled the law constitutional |
| January 18-19, 2025 | TikTok briefly suspended U.S. service before resuming |
| January-September 2025 | Trump repeatedly extended the grace period |
| September 2025 | Preliminary agreement reached in Madrid negotiations |
| January 2026 | A U.S.-China joint venture led by Oracle and Silver Lake was established to operate TikTok's U.S. business, with ByteDance holding a 19% stake |

### 6.2 Impact assessment on Dongchedi's IPO

**Direct impact: Limited**

1. The TikTok issue has been preliminarily resolved through the joint-venture arrangement, removing the largest source of uncertainty
2. Dongchedi is a purely domestic Chinese business and does not involve U.S. user data
3. Dongchedi's choice of Hong Kong (rather than U.S.) listing further insulates it from U.S. regulatory risk

**Indirect impact: Warrants ongoing attention**

1. **Brand association risk**: International investors may hold bias or exercise caution toward "ByteDance-affiliated" companies, viewing Chinese tech companies as facing geopolitical risk
2. **Possible valuation discount**: ByteDance's political sensitivity could cause Dongchedi's IPO valuation to bear a certain discount
3. **U.S. investor participation**: Some U.S. institutional investors may face internal compliance restrictions that reduce their investment in ByteDance-affiliated companies
4. **Chain sanctions risk**: Although the probability is extremely low, if U.S.-China relations were to deteriorate further, it cannot be ruled out that the U.S. could extend sanctions to ByteDance's other businesses
5. **Reinforced data security narrative**: The TikTok saga has heightened global regulators' focus on data security at Chinese tech companies, requiring Dongchedi to respond in greater detail to data security questions during roadshows

### 6.3 The signaling significance of ByteDance's overall path to listing

As ByteDance's first independently listed entity, Dongchedi carries "pathfinder" significance:

- If Dongchedi lists successfully, it will validate the path by which ByteDance-affiliated companies can achieve capitalization through spin-offs
- It provides experience and confidence for the subsequent listing of ByteDance's other business lines (such as Feishu)
- Investor sentiment toward Dongchedi's IPO is, to some extent, a test of capital markets' trust in the ByteDance brand

## VIII. Key Questions from an Investor's Perspective

### Pricing regulatory uncertainty

For investors considering participating in Dongchedi's IPO, the following regulatory questions warrant close attention during due diligence:

1. **Has the CSRC filing been accepted/approved?** This is a precondition for listing
2. **What is the scale and pricing mechanism of related-party transactions between Dongchedi and ByteDance?** This affects the authenticity of reported profit
3. **Is ByteDance's actual control arrangement over Dongchedi transparent?** This affects the assessment of governance structure
4. **How large are the data compliance costs?** These could affect future profit margins
5. **Is there a long-term guarantee of Douyin traffic supply?** This determines business independence and valuation sustainability
6. **Is the compliance filing for the automotive-vertical large language model complete?** AI regulation is a new variable

### Benchmark reference

Autohome's 2021 secondary listing in Hong Kong (returning from the U.S. market) saw its Hong Kong market capitalization approach nearly HK$100 billion at one point, and it now stands at approximately HK$24 billion. Dongchedi's Pre-IPO valuation of $3 billion (approximately HK$23.4 billion) is broadly comparable to Autohome's current market cap, but whether it can command a premium at IPO will depend heavily on the completeness of its regulatory compliance and the persuasiveness of its growth story.

## IX. Conclusions and Outlook

### Core judgments

1. **The regulatory path is feasible**: Choosing a Hong Kong listing is currently Dongchedi's optimal choice, avoiding the cybersecurity review that a U.S. listing could trigger, while the CSRC's filing system has also matured and become more predictable
2. **Compliance preparation is substantial**: Based on steps such as passing the Level 3 information security protection certification and the AI large-model filing, the company is proactively advancing its compliance infrastructure
3. **The VIE structure is a key review point**: The arrangement through which ByteDance retains actual control via mechanisms such as equity pledges will be a focal point of CSRC filing review
4. **The TikTok impact is limited but present**: Direct risk has largely been removed, but the "ByteDance-affiliated" political label could bring a latent valuation discount

### Regulatory milestones to track going forward

- Formal acceptance and review progress of the CSRC overseas listing filing
- The timing of the prospectus submission to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange
- The formal release of the Guidelines on the Security of Automotive Data Export (2025 Edition) and its applicability to Dongchedi
- The trajectory of U.S.-China relations and its spillover effects on ByteDance-affiliated companies
