# Dongchedi Internationalization Potential Analysis

> Analysis date: May 2026
> Core question: Does Dongchedi have the capability and opportunity to internationalize? What would internationalization mean for its valuation?
> Data sources: public reporting, industry data, competitor financial filings, search engines

---

## Core Conclusion

**Dongchedi currently has no international footprint, and is unlikely to expand overseas in the near term.** But the explosive growth in Chinese auto exports is creating a structural window of opportunity, and competitor Autohome has already moved first. If Dongchedi fails to seize this window after its IPO, it may miss an important growth narrative.

| Dimension | Assessment |
|------|------|
| Current overseas business | Zero. No overseas app, no overseas operating entity |
| Competitor moves | Autohome launched an overseas English-language version in June 2025, covering 6 countries/regions |
| ByteDance synergy | TikTok has over 1 billion global monthly active users and a fast-growing auto advertising business — large theoretical synergy potential, but not yet realized |
| Chinese auto exports | 8.32 million vehicles exported in 2025, up 30% year-over-year, providing a demand-side foundation for going overseas |
| Valuation impact of internationalization | If overseas expansion succeeds, the valuation multiple could rise from $3 billion to a $5-8 billion range (estimate) |

---

## I. Dongchedi's Current International Status: Nearly Zero

### 1.1 No overseas version

Based on multi-channel search verification, Dongchedi currently has **no**:
- Overseas app (App Store only has a China-region version)
- English-language or other-language website
- Overseas operating team or entity
- Any publicly disclosed overseas expansion plans

Dongchedi has not mentioned an internationalization strategy in its IPO prospectus materials (not yet public) or in public interviews. All public information focuses on deepening its position in the China market: used cars, car-maintenance services, optimization of the CPS (cost-per-sale) transaction model, and so on.

### 1.2 The only "international" move: the Global Winter Test

From late 2024 to early 2025, Dongchedi expanded its annual winter test from Heihe, China, to simultaneous testing in Alaska, USA, and Umeå, Sweden, covering nearly 40 brands and 90+ vehicles, with cumulative test mileage of nearly 100,000 kilometers.

But this is essentially a **content marketing campaign**, not commercial international expansion. Its purpose was to:
- Provide extreme-environment testing endorsement for Chinese automakers going global
- Boost Dongchedi's brand influence in professional vehicle testing
- Add a "global vision" narrative ahead of the IPO

**Objective assessment**: The Global Winter Test is a clever branding move, but it remains far from a genuine international business.

## II. A Competitor Has Already Moved: Autohome Goes Overseas First

### 2.1 Autohome's overseas version (launched July 2025)

Autohome officially launched its international English-language website on June 30, 2025 — the **first** systematic overseas move by a Chinese automotive vertical media company.

| Dimension | Autohome overseas version |
|------|--------------|
| Launch date | June 30, 2025 |
| Markets covered | Phase one covers 6 countries/regions: Hong Kong, Thailand, Saudi Arabia, the UK, Australia, and Brazil |
| Brands covered | 52 Chinese export brands, 894 model series, 1,900+ models |
| Core services | Car selection, price comparison, configuration comparison, model information |
| Offline support | "Autohome Space Stores" overseas physical showrooms, providing localized marketing solutions |
| Strategic positioning | An "information hub" for Chinese automakers going global |

Sources: [Autohome PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/autohomes-international-english-website-launches-on-june-30-building-an-information-hub-for-chinese-automakers-going-global-302496685.html), [AIM Group](https://aimgroup.com/2025/07/06/china-based-autohome-inc-unveils-global-ambitions/)

### 2.2 Implications for Dongchedi

Autohome's overseas logic is clear: **follow Chinese automakers abroad**. The core pain point Chinese auto brands face overseas is "low brand awareness and information opacity," and Autohome is using its 20 years of accumulated vehicle-model databases and evaluation systems to fill this gap.

This same logic applies to Dongchedi — arguably even more so — because Dongchedi has ByteDance's short-video and algorithmic capabilities behind it, which in theory make it better suited to "content-finds-people" distribution in overseas markets.

**But the reality is that Autohome moved first, and Dongchedi has not.** This may reflect several realities:
1. Ahead of its IPO, Dongchedi needs to focus on profitability and has no bandwidth to split resources overseas
2. Within the ByteDance ecosystem, TikTok faces geopolitical risk, making overseas expansion more cautious
3. Dongchedi's CPS model (charging on transaction completion) lacks a dealer-network foundation overseas

## III. ByteDance's Overseas Auto Ecosystem: Potential but Unactivated Synergy

### 3.1 TikTok's automotive content ecosystem has already taken shape

TikTok already has significant influence in the global automotive space:

| Metric | Data |
|------|------|
| Share of users interested in cars | 89% of TikTok users are interested in cars |
| Potential car-buying users | 37% of users are currently considering buying a new or used car |
| #Car hashtag growth | Up 25%+ year-over-year |
| Auto advertising performance | Ad ROI 90% higher than online video, cost-per-inquiry down 41% |
| Dealer leads | One-third of users are actively shopping for a car |

Sources: [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-gb/auto-on-tiktok), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-automotive-marketing-whitepaper-guide/747862/)

### 3.2 TikTok already has an automotive advertising product

TikTok has already launched "Automotive Ads," including:
- Catalog-based vehicle promotion
- Lead-generation forms
- Dealer locating
- Virtual test drives (AR filters)

Actual results:
- Toyota: CPA reduced by 38%
- A multi-brand dealer: leads up 182%, cost per lead down 71%
- Industry-wide: TikTok contributed 9.4% of total digital-media inquiry volume

Sources: [Dealers United](https://www.dealersunited.com/solutions/tiktok-automotive-ads/), [NADA](https://www.nada.org/nada/nada-headlines/driving-success-how-car-dealers-are-leveraging-tiktok-boost-sales)

### 3.3 TikTok Shop has already touched auto accessories

TikTok Shop's US site has already opened an auto-accessories and parts category. While it currently focuses on car-related accessories (car organizers, Bluetooth adapters, dash cams, etc.) rather than whole-vehicle transactions, the e-commerce infrastructure is already in place.

### 3.4 Why hasn't the synergy happened?

In theory, "TikTok automotive content + Dongchedi car-selection decision support + TikTok Shop transaction closure" is a perfect story. But in reality it hasn't happened, likely because:

1. **Geopolitical risk**: TikTok faces sustained regulatory pressure in the US, and ByteDance is reluctant to tie more of its Chinese businesses too closely to TikTok
2. **Corporate separation**: Dongchedi spun off from ByteDance in 2023, and synergy between the two entities now requires new commercial arrangements
3. **Different business logic**: Dongchedi's core model in China is CPS (charging on transaction completion), which requires deep penetration of offline dealer networks; TikTok overseas is still at the advertising-model stage
4. **Market access**: Automotive transaction markets in various countries have strict regulatory requirements (dealer licenses, consumer protection laws, financial services licenses, etc.) — it's not simply a matter of an app going overseas

**Objective assessment**: The TikTok-Dongchedi synergy is a story where "narrative value exceeds present-day reality." It is unlikely to materialize in the short term, but the possibility cannot be ruled out over the long run.

### 4.3 Gaps and opportunities in emerging markets

**Southeast Asia**: Although Carro and Carsome are unicorns, they mainly broker used-car transactions and lack the combined content + leads + new-car-transaction capability that Dongchedi has. The Southeast Asian market is highly fragmented, and Chinese brands (BYD ATTO 3, Great Wall Haval H6, etc.) are penetrating rapidly.

**Middle East**: Local platforms like CarSwitch remain small in scale. Chinese NEVs already hold a 40% share in the Middle East (2024), with markets like Saudi Arabia and the UAE growing rapidly.

**Latin America**: Brazil is home to BYD's first overseas passenger-vehicle plant, but automotive vertical media there is still dominated by traditional local media, with low digitalization.

**These emerging markets happen to be the priority regions for Chinese automakers going global, and also where local competitors are weakest.** In theory, this is the best entry point for Dongchedi's overseas expansion.

### 5.1 Export data overview

|------|--------|--------|---------|
| Total Chinese auto exports | ~6.4 million vehicles | 8.32 million vehicles | +30% |
| BEV exports | ~1.0 million vehicles | 1.646 million vehicles | +66.7% |
| PHEV exports | ~420,000 vehicles | 969,000 vehicles | +130% |
| Chery exports | 1.145 million vehicles | 1.344 million vehicles | +17.4% |
| BYD exports | 434,000 vehicles | 1.054 million vehicles | +142% |

Sources: [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/relnews/cn/2026-02-04/doc-inhkseni3582937.shtml), [Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202601163621774587.html)

### 5.2 2026 outlook

- BYD's 2026 overseas target: 1.5-1.6 million vehicles
- Chery continuing to deepen its presence in Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East
- SAIC building localized KD (knock-down) plants in ASEAN
- Great Wall and Geely establishing production capacity in Russia and the Middle East

### 5.3 Implications for Dongchedi

The core pain points Chinese automakers face going overseas include:
1. **Low brand awareness**: overseas consumers lack understanding of and trust in Chinese auto brands
2. **Difficult dealer management**: building overseas dealer networks is complex, with low digitalization
3. **High cost of lead acquisition**: acquiring car-buying leads overseas costs far more than in China (Google/Facebook advertising costs are high)
4. **Weak after-sales service**: lack of maintenance-information platforms and user communities

These pain points are precisely the problems Dongchedi has solved in the China market. If the "content-driven traffic → lead conversion → CPS transaction" model could be replicated overseas, it would in theory have value.

**But the counter-argument**:
- Dongchedi's advantage in China depends heavily on Douyin traffic, and there is no equivalent traffic gateway overseas
- The CPS model requires deep penetration of local dealer networks — an asset-heavy, operations-heavy undertaking
- Automotive transaction regulations differ by country, raising compliance costs
- Autohome has already moved first

## VI. Tariffs and Trade Barriers: Structural Constraints on Going Overseas

Internationalization analysis cannot ignore the constraints of the geopolitical and trade environment:

| Market | Tariffs/barriers | Impact |
|------|----------|------|
| **EU** | Countervailing duties of 17-35.3% imposed on Chinese EVs (starting 2024); negotiations underway on a minimum-price commitment as an alternative | Chinese automakers forced to build plants in Europe (Hungary, Spain, etc.), increasing the need for localized dealer systems |
| **US** | 100% tariff (starting 2024), effectively excluding Chinese EVs | Direct exports are almost impossible, and third-country routes such as via Mexico are also restricted |
| **Southeast Asia** | Relatively low tariffs, but Thailand requires a local-production ratio | A friendly market, but requires localized operations |
| **Middle East** | Low tariffs, policy-friendly | The most open incremental market |
| **Latin America** | Some tariffs in countries like Brazil, but Chinese automakers have already built plants | Large growth potential, but weak infrastructure |

Source: [MOFCOM WTO/FTA Consultation Portal](https://chinawto.mofcom.gov.cn/article/jsbl/zszc/202410/20241003540296.shtml)

**Implications for Dongchedi going overseas**: Even in markets that are difficult for Chinese automakers to enter (like the US), automotive content platforms can still find opportunities (CarGurus' international business grew 27%, mainly in the UK and Canada). But Dongchedi's CPS model is highly dependent on Chinese automakers' overseas dealer networks, meaning the most likely path overseas is to "follow Chinese automakers," rather than independently developing mature markets.

## VII. Potential Valuation Impact of Internationalization

### 7.1 Current valuation baseline

Dongchedi's current valuation is approximately $3 billion (2024 financing round), with an IPO fundraising target of $1.0-1.5 billion. On this basis, total market cap after the IPO could fall in the $4.0-5.0 billion range.

Comparison with peers:
- Autohome: market cap ~$3.2 billion (purely China business + nascent overseas business)
- CarGurus: market cap ~$3.4 billion (primarily US + UK/Canada international business growing 27%)
- Auto Trader UK: market cap ~$5.2 billion (UK dominance + high margins)
- CAR Group (Carsales): market cap ~A$9.3 billion / approximately $6.0 billion (Australia + South Korea + Brazil internationalization)

### 7.2 Reference cases of an internationalization premium

**Lessons from CAR Group (Carsales)**:

CAR Group started in Australia and achieved internationalization by acquiring platforms such as South Korea's Encar and Brazil's iCarros. Its valuation multiples (EV/Revenue 9.0x, EV/EBITDA 16.2x) are significantly higher than pure domestic players, primarily due to the growth expectations that internationalization brings.

**CarGurus' international valuation effect**:

CarGurus' international revenue has grown from 1% to 6% of total revenue, with international dealer growth (17.5%) far outpacing domestic growth (3.8%). Seeking Alpha analysts note that "a significant portion of CarGurus' valuation is based on the growth potential of international expansion."

Source: [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4872867-cargurus-great-value-as-international-growth-takes-off)

### 7.3 Scenario analysis of Dongchedi's internationalization valuation

The following is a purely speculative analysis, for reference only:

| Scenario | Assumption | Valuation range |
|------|------|---------|
| **Baseline (no internationalization)** | Purely China business, normal growth after IPO | $4.0-5.0 billion |
| **Moderate internationalization** | Launch overseas version, serve Chinese automakers going overseas, overseas revenue reaching 5-10% within 3 years | $5.0-6.5 billion |
| **Aggressive internationalization** | Synergy with TikTok, dealer networks established in 3-5 overseas markets, overseas revenue reaching 15-20% | $6.5-8.0 billion |
| **Radical internationalization** | Acquiring overseas auto platforms, fully replicating the CPS model, overseas revenue reaching 30%+ | $8.0-12.0 billion |

**Derivation logic**:
- Comparing CAR Group's EV/Revenue of 9.0x with CarGurus' 3.7x, internationalization could lift the valuation multiple by 1.5-2x
- But Dongchedi's margins are far below Auto Trader UK's (60%+), so it could not achieve an equivalent multiple
- Reasonable expectation: successful internationalization could lift the valuation 30-60% above the baseline level

**It must be emphasized**: the above figures are a highly speculative scenario analysis. Dongchedi currently has no overseas business whatsoever, and these scenarios may never materialize.

## VIII. Path Projection: If Dongchedi Were to Internationalize

### 8.1 The most likely path: following Chinese automakers overseas

**Step one** (0-1 year): Launch an overseas information version
- Follow Autohome's overseas model, launching multilingual sites in English, Arabic, Thai, etc.
- Focus content, reviews, and pricing information on Chinese-brand export models
- Provide overseas consumers with a gateway to "understand Chinese automobiles"

**Step two** (1-2 years): Tap into TikTok traffic
- Operate an automotive vertical channel on the TikTok platform
- Leverage TikTok Automotive Ads infrastructure to provide lead-generation services to Chinese brands' overseas dealers
- Connect the chain of "watching a car video on TikTok → checking the car on Dongchedi's overseas version → dealer price inquiry"

**Step three** (2-3 years): Roll out the CPS model
- Pilot in markets like Southeast Asia and the Middle East where Chinese-brand dealer networks are relatively mature
- Partner with the overseas dealer systems of leading exporters such as BYD and Chery
- Charge on transaction completion, replicating the business model validated in the China market

**Step four** (3-5 years): Platform-scale expansion
- Expand from serving Chinese brands to serving all brands
- Accelerate via acquisitions of local platforms (following the path of CAR Group's acquisitions of Encar and iCarros)

### 8.2 Possible priority target markets

| Priority tier | Market | Rationale |
|--------|------|------|
| First tier | Thailand, Saudi Arabia, Brazil | High Chinese-brand penetration, weak local competition, tariff-friendly |
| Second tier | Indonesia, Malaysia, UAE | Fast-growing Southeast Asia/Middle East, large TikTok user base |
| Third tier | Australia, UK | Mature markets, Chinese brands entering but facing intense competition |
| Not currently considered | US | 100% tariff, Chinese cars can barely enter |

### 8.3 Core obstacles to going overseas

1. **Lack of a traffic gateway**: China relies on Douyin for traffic, and there is no equivalent gateway overseas. TikTok could partially substitute, but the synergy requires organizational-level arrangements
2. **Building a dealer network from scratch**: the CPS model's foundation is a dealer network, which must be negotiated market by market overseas
3. **High cost of localized operations**: the automotive industry is highly localized, with huge differences in regulations, consumer habits, and financial products across countries
4. **Pre-IPO focus on profitability**: the most urgent task at present is to prove profitability, and internationalization would drag on margins
5. **Spillover of TikTok's geopolitical risk**: deep ties with TikTok could trigger regulatory attention in some markets

## X. Summary and Investment Implications

### 10.1 Core judgments

1. **Short term (0-2 years)**: Internationalization contributes almost nothing to Dongchedi's valuation. The IPO's core narrative is CPS-model growth and improving profitability in the China market, not overseas expansion
2. **Medium term (2-4 years)**: If the IPO successfully raises $1.0-1.5 billion, some portion may go toward overseas pilots. But this will most likely be cautious information-service expansion overseas, not full commercialization
3. **Long term (4+ years)**: If Chinese auto exports continue growing beyond 10 million vehicles and TikTok-Dongchedi synergy materializes, internationalization could become a catalyst for valuation re-rating

### 10.2 Key indicators to monitor

For investors focused on Dongchedi's internationalization potential, we recommend tracking the following signals:

- [ ] Whether the IPO prospectus mentions overseas expansion plans and use of proceeds
- [ ] Whether a multilingual website or app version is launched
- [ ] Whether a cooperation agreement with TikTok on overseas automotive business is signed
- [ ] Whether an operating entity is established in overseas markets
- [ ] Autohome's overseas-version operating data (as an industry reference)
- [ ] Whether Chinese auto export growth remains above 20%
- [ ] Digitalization needs of overseas dealer networks for leading players such as BYD and Chery

### 10.3 Summary of valuation impact

| Factor | Impact on valuation | Probability |
|------|------------|------|
| Successful IPO, purely China business | Baseline: $4.0-5.0 billion | High |
| Launch of overseas information service | +$1.0-1.5 billion | Medium |
| TikTok synergy realized | +$1.5-3.0 billion | Low |
| Full internationalization (acquisitions + organic build) | +$3.0-7.0 billion | Very low |

**Final judgment**: Internationalization is a growth dimension for Dongchedi that is "promising but far from realized." Current investment decisions should not treat internationalization as a primary basis, but should continue monitoring signals in this direction. If Dongchedi shows no overseas movement within a year of its IPO while Autohome's overseas version continues to expand, that would be a negative signal — indicating either that Dongchedi lacks the intent to internationalize, or that it lacks the ability to go overseas independently after separating from ByteDance.

> "Never pay a premium for something that 'might' happen. Wait until it actually happens and shows results." — a Duan Yongping-style conservative judgment
