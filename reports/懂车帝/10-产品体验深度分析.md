# Dongchedi App: An In-Depth Product Experience Analysis

> Analysis date: May 2026
> Current version: v8.9.6 (Android) / synchronized update on iOS
> Positioning: ByteDance's automotive information and transaction platform, currently sprinting toward a Hong Kong IPO

---

## I. Product Positioning and Development Timeline

Dongchedi launched in 2017, evolving out of ByteDance's Toutiao automotive channel, and positions itself as a "trustworthy platform for choosing and buying new and used cars." It was spun off from the ByteDance group into an independent entity in 2023, and in 2024 it completed roughly $600 million in financing (led by Sequoia China, with participation from KKR, Loyal Valley Capital, and General Atlantic), valuing the company at approximately $3 billion. In February 2026, reports emerged of a planned Hong Kong IPO targeting $1.0-1.5 billion in proceeds, with Citi and Goldman Sachs selected as underwriters.

**Key development milestones:**

| Time | Milestone |

## II. Breakdown of Core Feature Modules

### 2.1 Car-Selection Module

**Model library and spec configuration:** Covers more than 400 model series, offering highly granular spec-configuration comparisons and a car-selection PK (head-to-head comparison) tool. Model information is relatively complete, with especially strong coverage of new-energy vehicle models.

**Dongchedi Score:** The platform's proprietary vehicle rating system, aggregating genuine hands-on ratings from professional automotive reviewers. However, the 2023 "winter test" and 2025 "summer test" drew public objections from multiple automakers (Huawei, Great Wall Motor, Li Auto, and others), which dented its credibility to some degree.

**AI Car Selection ("AI Xiaodong"):** The core feature officially launched in July 2025, built on the Doubao 1.6 large language model. Users describe fuzzy needs via text or voice (e.g., "budget around 200,000 yuan, suited to a woman, stylish-looking new-energy vehicle"), and the system precisely matches models and presents them via multimodal formats including text, images, and video. It covers scenarios spanning selection, comparison, purchase, and valuation.

**Photo-based vehicle recognition:** Uses AI image recognition so a single photo can identify the model and pull up detailed information.

### 2.2 Car-Viewing Module

**3D viewing and AR on-site visualization:** Supports 360-degree viewing of interior and exterior details, with the ability to switch body colors. The AR feature can place the vehicle into a real-world setting, simulating cabin space for occupants of different heights, and can even test trunk capacity by loading in luggage of different sizes.

**VR immersive viewing:** Went live on the PICO platform in 2023, covering more than 400 model series. Users can view vehicles from every angle in a virtual setting, check specs, customize colors, and view pricing and sales rankings.

**Simulated test drive:** Supports free driving, performance testing, and driver-assistance simulation, recreating in a virtual environment the experience of features such as adaptive cruise control, lane-departure warning, and lane-change assist.

**Video manuals:** Explains each of a vehicle's functions and operations in video form, lowering the learning cost for users.

### 2.3 Car-Buying Module

**New-car transactions:** Integrates dealer quotes, special-price vehicles, and livestream coupons. Under the CPS model, dealers pay for completed sales rather than for leads; the partner network spans more than 28,500 dealers across more than 110 brands.

**Used-car transactions:** Pursues an "asset-heavy" strategy, operating directly-run stores (roughly 100), franchise stores, and self-operated delivery centers (50), covering more than 280 cities. It launched the "National Purchase" model (select the car online, take delivery offline), through which roughly 40% of used-car transactions are completed. It offers official inspection, one-price direct sales, and multi-layered after-sales protection.

**Government subsidy integration:** In 2026, the platform is handling vehicle trade-in and renewal subsidy applications for 17 provinces and municipalities; in the first half of 2025 it had already processed more than 960,000 subsidy applications, driving nearly 150 billion yuan in new-car spending.

### 2.4 Car-Ownership Module

Covers aftermarket functions such as vehicle maintenance, traffic-violation lookup, and insurance services, but this currently remains a supporting feature rather than a core source of competitive advantage for the platform.

### 2.5 Community/Content Module

**Feed format:** The home screen uses a feed format mixing text, images, and video, following ByteDance's "content finds the user" recommendation-distribution logic — a sharp contrast with Autohome's "user finds the car" search-driven logic.

**Short-video channels:** Includes vertical content categories such as vehicle pickup, test drives, car-showcasing, and stress-testing ("abusing") vehicles, along with curated topic collections.

**Livestreaming:** Covers scenarios such as new-car launches, dealer livestream sales, and livestreams by automotive review influencers.

## III. Content Ecosystem Analysis

### 3.1 Content Scale

Dongchedi has fully merged its automotive content with that of Douyin, Toutiao, and Xigua Video, all operated under unified Dongchedi management:

- Combined daily active automotive users across the four platforms reach 310 million visits
- Average daily automotive content views exceed 5.6 billion
- Operations cover more than 7.5 million automotive content creators
- More than 2 million creators and users share content daily on average
- In 2025, combined automotive content plays across the two platforms (App + Douyin ecosystem) surpassed 8 billion, with content reads up 26% year over year

### 3.2 Content Format Mix

Short video is Dongchedi's dominant content format by far, and this is its most central point of differentiation:

| Content format | Standing | Characteristics |
|----------|------|------|
| Short video | Core mainstay | Built on the Douyin ecosystem; rich UGC content around vehicle pickup, test drives, car-showcasing |
| Livestreaming | Important supplement | Dealer livestream sales, new-car launch livestreams, influencer review livestreams |
| Text-and-image reviews | Professional supplement | Original in-depth reviews, the Dongchedi Score system, winter/summer test special features |

### 3.3 Creator Ecosystem

In 2026 the platform launched the "Mega Dongchedi Creator Support Program": 1 billion units of traffic support, 100 million yuan in cash incentives, and one-stop dedicated services. 2025 data shows:

- Creator monetization revenue up 44% year over year
- The number of creators monetizing content up 38%
- Creators earning more than 1 million yuan annually up 48% year over year
- Creators earning more than 10 million yuan annually up 79% year over year
- Content flagged as "useful" attracts 10 times the traffic of ordinary content

### 3.4 Content Quality Assessment

**Strengths:** Short-video content is highly novel and broad in coverage, the barrier to user-generated creation is low, and time-sensitive content such as auto shows and new-car launches gets covered quickly.

**Concerns:** The 2023 winter-test controversy exposed insufficient standardization in the platform's proprietary reviews. Multiple automakers publicly questioned the scientific rigor and fairness of the testing methodology, and Huawei was at one point reported to have suspended cooperation with Dongchedi. The platform must continually balance being "traffic-driven" against being "professionally authoritative."

## IV. AI Feature Matrix

Dongchedi is the most aggressive platform in the automotive vertical when it comes to AI applications, having built out a complete AI technology stack:

### 4.1 AI Technical Architecture

Building on top of a leading general-purpose large language model (Doubao 1.6), the company has developed:
- **Automotive-domain LLM:** A specialized language model for the automotive field
- **Automotive multimodal VLM:** Supports multimodal understanding across text, images, and video
- **Dongchedi Smart Engine:** Includes an automotive knowledge-extraction graph, an automotive reinforcement-training engine, and an expert-tuning platform
- **Coze Compass:** Used to build and tune AI agents

### 4.2 AI Feature Inventory

| AI feature | Status | Description |
|--------|------|------|
| AI Car Selection ("AI Xiaodong") | Live | Matches fuzzy requirements, compares multiple vehicles, recommends transactions |
| AI Search | Live | Web-connected search and Q&A agent |
| AI Vehicle Recognition | Live | Photo-based model identification |
| VR Virtual Cloud Auto Show | Live | High-fidelity vehicle reproduction, 360-degree panorama plus AI voice narration |
| AI Online Sales | Live | Assists dealers with online selling |
| Smart Recommendation | Core feature | Personalized content and vehicle recommendations for each user |
| AI Content Creation Tools | Launching 2026 | Inspiration tools, highlight-reel covers, smart moderation |
| Dongchedi Smart Targeting | Launching 2026 | Precision outreach to high-potential buyers of comparable vehicles |

### 4.3 User Experience of AI Car Selection

After entering the AI Car Selection interface, users can type their needs in natural language. For example, entering "budget of 150,000 yuan, family SUV, lots of space," the system will:

1. Parse the user's intent and underlying needs
2. Match models in the vehicle database that meet the criteria
3. Present recommendation results in multimodal form — text, images, and video
4. Integrate owner reviews, high-resolution images, and pricing information
5. Support further follow-up questions and comparisons

This feature is positioned as "an automotive buying expert in the user's pocket," designed especially for the more than 70% of platform users who show high purchase intent.

## V. Feature Comparison with Autohome

### 5.1 Core Positioning Differences

| Dimension | Dongchedi | Autohome |
|------|--------|---------|
| Underlying logic | "Content finds the user" — recommendation-driven distribution | "User finds the car" — search-driven |
| Content format | Primarily short video, feed-based | Primarily text and images, forum community |
| User profile | Younger, leaning toward new-energy vehicles | More traditional, deep car enthusiasts |
| Ecosystem backing | ByteDance family (Douyin + Toutiao + Xigua) | Ping An family (finance + insurance + services) |
| Business model | CPS, pay per completed sale | CPT, pay per time (traditional lead generation) |
| Valuation/market cap | ~$3 billion (pre-IPO) | ~$3.19 billion (U.S.-listed) |

### 5.2 Where Dongchedi Does Better

**1. Content consumption experience:** The short-video-plus-feed format better matches contemporary users' fragmented content-consumption habits; scrolling through videos to look at cars carries a far lower barrier than reading long text reviews.

**2. Depth of AI features:** AI Car Selection, AI Vehicle Recognition, VR viewing, and other features clearly lead Autohome, backed by stronger technical foundations from ByteDance's Doubao large model and Volcano Engine.

**3. New-energy-vehicle content:** Coverage and content production around new-energy models is more agile, aligning with the industry's transition trend.

**4. Acquisition of young users:** The share of users born after 2000 grew from 13% to 18%, users under 30 grew 16%, and the overall user age structure has become younger.

**5. Traffic-acquisition cost:** Backed by Douyin's traffic pool with more than 700 million DAU, customer-acquisition cost is far below that of standalone acquisition.

**6. 3D/AR/VR car viewing:** Greater investment in virtual car-viewing experiences, with more complete features, including coverage of VR devices such as PICO.

### 5.3 Where Autohome Does Better

**1. Depth of the model library:** As the most established platform, its model-library completeness and accumulated historical data are richer.

**2. Forum community accumulation:** Built up over more than a decade, Autohome's forums contain long-term real-owner usage feedback and issue-sharing — deep UGC content that Dongchedi's short videos struggle to replace.

**3. Authority of professional reviews:** Even though Dongchedi's winter/summer tests generate a lot of buzz, Autohome's long-standing reputation remains more solid in terms of the rigor and industry recognition of professional testing.

**4. Volume of reviews:** Autohome has 2.63 million reviews on the App Store, far more than Dongchedi's 760,000, reflecting a larger installed user base.

**5. MAU scale:** Autohome's MAU is about 56.36 million, still higher than Dongchedi's 35.72 million (excluding traffic diverted from Douyin).

**6. Finance/insurance synergy:** Backed by Ping An Group, it has a natural advantage in aftermarket services such as auto finance and insurance.

### 5.4 Key Data Comparison

| Metric | Dongchedi | Autohome |
|------|--------|---------|
| App Store rating | 4.8 (760,000 reviews) | 4.8 (2.63 million reviews) |
| Standalone App DAU | ~10 million | ~77 million (including PC) |
| Standalone App MAU | ~35.72 million | ~56.36 million |
| Negative-review share (most recent 50) | 96% | 98% |
| Partner dealers | 28,500+ | 30,000+ |
| CPS model | Rolled out fully | Not yet adopted |

> Note: Both apps' recent negative-review shares are extremely high (96%-98%), indicating that automotive vertical apps generally face user-experience problems, mainly concentrated in privacy leaks (a flood of sales calls), excessive advertising, and overheating/battery drain.

## VI. User Review Analysis

### 6.1 App Store Ratings

| Platform | Rating | Number of reviews |
|------|------|--------|
| iOS App Store | 4.8 | approximately 760,000 |
| Yingyongbao (Tencent app store) | 4.5 | -- |
| 360 App Store | -- | -- |

### 6.2 Main Positive Feedback

- **Comprehensive vehicle information:** Spec configurations, images, and videos are all in one place, making it easy to look up a car
- **3D/VR car viewing:** Get a full view of a vehicle without leaving home — a novel experience
- **Rich content:** Short-video content is entertaining and makes it easy to pick up automotive knowledge
- **AI Car Selection works well:** A vague description is enough to match a suitable model, saving time and effort
- **Price transparency:** Real transaction-price references and owner-reported prices help users judge whether a quote is reasonable

### 6.3 Main Negative Feedback

- **Serious privacy leaks:** After registering or requesting a quote, users receive a flood of sales calls from dealers — "six or seven unsolicited car-sales calls a day"
- **Too many ads on the home page:** "The home page is full of auto-playing ads, the phone overheats badly, and the battery drains fast"
- **Used-car valuations too low:** Some users report that "the app deliberately exaggerates vehicle problems to push down the price"
- **Uneven content quality:** The feed contains a lot of ads and low-quality content, raising the cost of filtering
- **Confusing home-page experience:** The feed layout is not clear enough, and entry points to important features are not obvious

### 6.4 Comparison of Negative Reviews with Competitors

The shared pain point across the three major platforms (Dongchedi, Autohome, and Yiche) is **harassing calls caused by phone-number leaks** — a structural problem stemming from the lead-distribution business model common to automotive vertical platforms. In terms of developer responsiveness, Yiche performs best (replying to 6 of its most recent 10 reviews), Dongchedi is in the middle, and Autohome barely responds at all.

## VIII. Traffic Allocation Between the Douyin Mini-Program/Embedded Entry Points and the Standalone App

### 8.1 Dual-Platform Architecture

Dongchedi's traffic no longer comes solely from its standalone App; it has formed a dual-platform linkage system in which "Douyin sparks interest, Dongchedi completes the decision":

**Douyin side (traffic entry point):**
- Automotive content in the Douyin feed links directly to a Dongchedi mini-program
- Automotive short videos and livestreams can jump directly into Dongchedi
- Used-car dealers can attach a Dongchedi used-car mini-program to their Douyin videos
- The Douyin automotive channel and Dongchedi content are fully merged
- A national mini-program for vehicle scrappage-and-renewal subsidy applications

**Dongchedi side (decision-making arena):**
- In-depth model library, spec comparisons, professional reviews
- Professional tools such as AI Car Selection and 3D/VR viewing
- Transaction functions such as used-car sales and new-car quotes
- Vehicle-ownership services and community interaction

### 8.2 Traffic Conversion Path

After users on the Douyin side have their interest sparked by automotive content:
- **84%** go on to Dongchedi to browse the model library
- **78%** search for a specific model series and read in-depth reviews

This data shows that Douyin is the traffic engine while Dongchedi is the conversion arena, with significant synergy between the two.

### 8.3 Traffic Scale Comparison

| Dimension | Standalone App | Combined across four platforms (including Douyin, etc.) |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| Automotive user DAU | ~10 million | 310 million visits |
| Average daily automotive content views | Not separately disclosed | 5.6 billion+ |
| Automotive content creators | Not separately disclosed | 7.5 million+ |

**Core conclusion:** The Dongchedi standalone App's DAU is only about 3% of the combined total across the four platforms, meaning the vast majority of automotive content consumption happens within ByteDance's broader traffic pool, including Douyin. The standalone App's value lies not in traffic scale but in receiving high-intent users diverted from Douyin and providing them with professional tools and a closed transaction loop. This "broadcast plus narrowcast" dual-layer architecture is the most fundamental model difference setting Dongchedi apart from Autohome.

### 8.4 Support Policies

- **2025:** Invested 500 million yuan in cash, 150 billion units of traffic, and 10,000 vehicle-trips' worth of quality vehicle inventory to support creators
- **2026:** 1 billion units of traffic support and 100 million yuan in cash incentives
- **Used-Car Trailblazer Program:** Jointly with Douyin, invested 5 billion units of traffic to support professional used-car content

## IX. Competitive Landscape Overview

### 9.1 Three-Way Rivalry

| Dimension | Dongchedi | Autohome | Yiche |
|------|--------|---------|------|
| Backing ecosystem | ByteDance (Douyin) | Ping An Group | Tencent (WeChat) |
| Content specialty | Short video + feed | Forums + text/image reviews | Social distribution |
| Business model | CPS, pay per completed sale | CPT, pay per time | Hybrid model |
| MAU | ~35.72 million | ~56.36 million | ~58.88 million |
| Strategic focus | Transaction closed-loop + AI | Owner services + new retail | Synergy with Tencent's ecosystem |
| Growth trajectory | Rapid growth | Slowing growth | Steady, gradual increase |
| Valuation/market cap | ~$3 billion | ~$3.19 billion | Not independently listed |

### 9.2 Dongchedi's Structural Advantages

1. **Extremely low traffic cost:** Douyin's free traffic pool with more than 700 million DAU pushes marginal customer-acquisition cost toward zero
2. **CPS model innovation:** Upgrades automotive vertical platforms from "selling leads" to "driving completed sales," lifting dealer conversion rates by 68%
3. **AI technology leadership:** Backed by ByteDance's large models and Volcano Engine capabilities, its AI feature matrix is the most complete in the industry
4. **Mindshare among young users:** Has built the perception among the newer generation of car buyers of "check Dongchedi first before looking at a car"

### 9.3 Dongchedi's Structural Risks

1. **Standalone App traffic ceiling:** DAU of 10 million already represents a phase-level bottleneck, with heavy reliance on traffic diverted from Douyin
2. **Content credibility crisis:** The winter/summer test controversies expose an inherent contradiction in a "traffic platform doing reviews"
3. **Used-car asset-heavy risk:** The directly-run-store-plus-National-Purchase model requires heavy capital investment — for reference, Uxin's gross margin is only 6.2%
4. **Pronounced privacy problems:** Complaints stemming from phone-number leaks are the single biggest source of negative sentiment
5. **Profitability still unproven:** While the CPS model looks promising, specific revenue and profit figures had not been disclosed ahead of the IPO

## X. Overall Product Experience Assessment

### 10.1 Scorecard

| Dimension | Score (out of 5) | Assessment |
|------|--------------|------|
| Car-selection tools | 4.5 | AI Car Selection plus 3D/VR viewing lead the industry |
| Content consumption | 4.0 | Rich short-video content, but quality is uneven |
| Transaction experience | 3.5 | The CPS model is innovative, but privacy protection is insufficient |
| Community interaction | 3.0 | Less accumulated depth than Autohome's forums |
| AI features | 4.5 | The most complete AI feature matrix in the industry |
| User experience | 3.5 | The feed design is smooth, but ads are excessive |
| Privacy protection | 2.0 | The phone-number leakage problem is serious |

### 10.2 Core Judgment

Dongchedi's product philosophy is "doing automotive the ByteDance way": replacing search with recommendation algorithms, replacing text-and-images with short video, replacing manual work with AI, and replacing CPT with CPS. This playbook is extremely efficient at acquiring users and producing content, but there remains a gap in establishing professional authority and user trust.

For a Dongchedi that is about to go public, the biggest product challenge is not feature innovation (the company is already aggressive enough on that front), but rather finding the balance between "traffic efficiency" and "user trust." The three issues of the winter-test controversy, privacy leaks, and excessive advertising are, at their core, all manifestations of the same tension between the pressure to monetize traffic and the user experience.

From an investment perspective, Dongchedi's product strength already places it in the first tier of the automotive vertical, but its real moat lies not in the product features themselves but in the traffic-distribution advantage created by its deep integration with the Douyin ecosystem. This is simultaneously its greatest strength and its greatest risk — if Douyin's traffic-allocation strategy were to shift, the user base of Dongchedi's standalone App would face a real challenge.
